## 1. CORE PRODUCT SEGMENTS & LAUNCHES

**Note:** This presentation is for a **value fashion retailer**, not a fragrance/cosmetics company. No fragrances, elixirs, or active-ingredient cosmetics are mentioned. The following is the actual product segmentation extracted:

- **Product Categories Offered:**
  - **Apparel** — Men, Women, Kids (87% of revenue in Q1FY27)
  - **General Merchandise** — Footwear, Home/Kitchen Appliances, Household Products, Travel Accessories, Lifestyle Products, Home Furnishings, Home Decor, Bags (13% of revenue in Q1FY27)
- **Apparel Sub-categories:**
  - Men: Men's Wear, Athleisure, Ethnic Wear, Bottom Wear, Night Wear, Wearables & Accessories
  - Women: Western Wear, Ethnic Wear, Athleisure, Night Wear, Bottom Wear, Wearables & Accessories
  - Kids: Ethnic Wear, Athleisure, Night Wear, Bottom Wear, Wearables & Accessories
- **Private Labels:** 11 private labels contributing **62% of overall revenue** (Rs 3,006 Mn in Q1FY27)
  - **Square Up** — highest revenue private label at **Rs 1,207 Mn in Q1FY27**
  - Private Label Revenue CAGR of **~64% over the past 4 years**
  - Strategic focus: attractive pricing → shift to fair pricing once brand recall established
  - Benefits: greater control over sourcing; enhanced customer loyalty with repeat purchases
- **Private Label Revenue Trajectory (Rs Mn):**
  - FY22: 1,363 (25% of revenue)
  - FY23: 2,477 (31%)
  - FY24: 3,687 (38%)
  - FY25: 6,026 (45%)
  - FY26: 9,770 (53%)
  - Q1FY26: 2,289 (61%)
  - Q1FY27: 3,006 (62%)

## 4. FUTURE GROWTH ADJACENCIES

**Note:** The presentation contains **no explicit mention** of:
- Fragrance entry
- Oral beauty/nutrition M&A
- Active-ingredient cosmetics
- Elixirs

The following growth-related data points are extracted:

- **Geographic Expansion:** Cluster-based expansion strategy across **10 states, 198 cities**; focus on underpenetrated **Tier-3 & Tier-4 markets** (69% of presence)
- **Store Expansion:** 276 stores as of 30-Jun-26; 4-year store count CAGR of ~26%
- **Private Label Expansion:** 11 private labels; 62% revenue share; CAGR ~64% over 4 years; strategic shift from attractive pricing to fair pricing once brand recall established
- **Cluster-Based Strategy Benefits:** Supply chain & inventory management efficiencies; enhanced brand visibility in local markets; optimized marketing expenses; efficient human capital utilization
- **Revenue Growth:** 4-year revenue CAGR of ~35%; Q1FY27 revenue up 29% YoY
- **Offline Channel:** Entirely offline retail model; no mention of online/e-commerce channel additions

## MANAGEMENT LETTERS

**Key Themes:**
- Cluster-based expansion model reiterated across all letters — store count grew from 162→214 (32% YoY)
- Private labels positioned as core margin lever: 45% of revenue (up from 38% prior year)
- Technology upgrade narrative heavy: SAP HANA, WMS, Goldratt, Tableau — most in implementation/testing phase
- Fire at Serampore warehouse (May 2024) acknowledged but minimised; business continuity claimed within one month

**Stated Opportunities:** Tier II/III/IV penetration, private label scaling, omnichannel via WhatsApp/CRM, 500-store target over 5 years

**Stated Risks:** Supply chain disruption, inventory management, competition, financial risks (generic disclosure)

**Strategic Priorities:** 300 stores by FY26-27, 40-50 new stores each FY25-26 & FY26-27, deepening North/East India

**Walk-the-Talk Check:**
- MD claimed "strong financial delivery" — PAT fell 33% YoY (₹2,199M→₹1,474M) driven by fire exceptional charge
- EBITDA growth at 33% vs revenue growth of 38% — margins slightly compressed (14.1% vs 14.6%)
- No dividend declared despite profitability — plausibly justified by expansion capex
- **Yellow flag:** Multiple letters make optimistic tone while underlying PAT declined materially

**Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y):**

| Metric | FY23 | FY24 | FY25 |
|--------|------|------|------|
| Revenue (₹M) | 7,879 | 9,729 | 13,437 |
| YoY growth | — | 23% | 38% |
| PAT (₹M) | — | 2,199 | 1,474 |

- Revenue acceleration is genuine (38% YoY) driven by store expansion + 12.7% SSSG
- PAT collapsed due to ₹1,076M exceptional loss (fire); adjusting for that, PBT was ₹2,963M (stable vs ₹2,928M prior year)

**EBITDA Margin Trend:**
- FY23: 12.9% → FY24: 14.6% → FY25: 14.1% — **slight contraction** despite higher scale
- Gross margin stable at 33.7% (FY24: 33.4%)
- Employee costs and other expenses rising faster than revenue growth

**CFO vs PAT:**
- CFO: ₹5,277M (standalone) vs PAT: ₹1,474M — **strong cash generation** on adjusted basis
- Operating profit before WC: ₹1,950M; working capital consumed ₹825M net
- Insurance receivable ₹4,731M inflated "other financial assets" significantly

**Working Capital:**
- Inventory: ₹5,214M (up 20.5% YoY); inventory days improved (38.6→35.5 days)
- Trade payables: ₹3,107M (up 18.8%)
- WC consumed mainly due to inventory build + insurance receivable

**ROCE/ROE:**
- ROCE: 19.6% (FY24: 20.6%) — **slight deterioration** but still healthy for retail
- ROE: 4.8% (FY24: 10.8%) — **sharp decline** due to PAT drop + equity expansion from IPO

**Debt Position:**
- Net debt (ex-lease): ₹1,483M → D/E: 0.37x (improved from 0.76x post-IPO)
- Total lease liabilities: ₹8,297M (massive — this is the real balance sheet risk)
- Interest coverage (ex-lease): 1.26x — **tight**; including lease obligations it would be much lower
- Credit rating: CRISIL A-/Stable

## FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: **AVERAGE**

**Rationale:**
- Genuine business momentum with strong revenue growth and repeat customer base
- However, material lease liabilities (₹8.3B on ₹4B equity) create hidden leverage risk
- Contingent liabilities at ~47% of net worth are alarming — tax dispute exposure is significant
- Extensive promoter-family RPTs (rent, purchases) require independent monitoring
- Fire-related exceptional loss masks underlying PAT trend; insurance claim recovery is uncertain
- Management remuneration growth outpaced employee cost growth disproportionately
- Technology transformation is aspirational but largely unproven (still in implementation)
- CARO flags on bank return mismatches and audit trail gaps need monitoring

## Consensus vs Variant Views

- **Consensus (Bullish):** Company is in aggressive expansion mode with good same-store sales growth; Q3 is seasonally strong (Durga Puja/festivals in East India); revenue and profit growth expected to improve YoY.
  - *Source:* @sumit_tumba (Post #13)
  - **Verified** (based on publicly available SSSG data and seasonal patterns)

- **Variant View (Bearish):** High PE, negative EPS growth, low interest coverage, low ROE, profitability volatility, Q4 loss, and 33% PAT decline make the stock risky if margins miss in FY26.

  - **Bull case:** In-house brands can improve NPM if sales mix shifts; high depreciation (non-cash) is masking operating margins (~14% OPM); NPM should expand once store opening pace slows and depreciation normalizes.

    - **Needs Verification** (depreciation trajectory and sales mix shift not confirmed from filings)
  - **Bear case:** Company lacks pricing power; thin margins + high leverage make earnings growth and PE rerating difficult.
    - *Source:* @AbhijeetSakhare (Post #15)
    - **Verified** (leverage and margin data from filings)

- **Variant View (Peer Comparison — V2 Retail):**
  - V2 Retail has 13% OPM and ~3% NPM; opened 75 stores in 9 months (faster than Baazar Style); V2 only started making profits in 2024 after a decade of losses.
  - *Source:* @AbhijeetSakhare (Post #19), @antson_m (Post #20)

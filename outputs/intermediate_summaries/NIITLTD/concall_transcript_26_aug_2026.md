### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements from CEO, CFO, Leadership)

**Vijay Thadani (Vice Chairman & MD):**
- “We are starting off FY27 on a strong note, definitely stronger than same period last year.” (Pg 3)
- Both enterprise and consumer go-to-market engines showing growth in Q1; technology grew from AI-led investments, BFSI and others also grew. (Pg 3)
- Order intake remained strong; EBITDA moving right direction with operating costs growing slower than revenue; certain business parts in investment cycle, Q1 investments normally higher. (Pg 3)
- Post-call summary: portfolio becoming more resilient; strengthening product portfolio for working professionals, rollout of new AI programs, go-to-market expansion, iamneo initiative showing early results. (Pg 6)
- “We remain confident of the longer-term structural growth opportunity and are committed to making the investments needed to capture that opportunity.” (Pg 6)
- Disciplined approach to evaluating inorganic opportunities that enhance capabilities, channels, IP with clear path to returns. (Pg 6-7)
- On macros (later Q&A): “the situation remains the same… current days are not pointing in the right direction” but AI opportunity exists irrespective of macros. (Pg 13)

**Pankaj Jathar (CEO):**
- Q1 FY27 revenue INR 957 million, +14% YoY; Enterprise INR 618m (+8% YoY), Consumer INR 339m (+27% YoY). (Pg 3-4)
- Enterprise tech training INR 498m (+16% YoY); Consumer tech INR 182m (+15% YoY); direct-to-college strategy creating pipeline. (Pg 3-4)
- Mix shift: enterprise-consumer from 68-32 same period last year to 65-35 in Q1. (Pg 4)
- AI programs revenue 9% of total; launched training for forward deployed engineers, site reliability engineers, AI auditors, AI Prism. (Pg 5-6)
- Activated 4 new solution lines (insurance, NBFC, wealth, Gen AI) with >15 clients outside traditional bank induction. (Pg 5)
- Merger of RPS Consulting and IFBI into NIIT strengthened offerings. (Pg 5)
- Guidance Q2 FY27: double-digit revenue growth YoY, near breakeven EBITDA, positive margins in second half; FY27 stronger than FY26. (Pg 6)
- Integrated RPS + StackRoute into “enterprise technology learning solutions”; banking/TPaaS into BIPS – both performed well. (Pg 8, 11)

**Sanjeev Bansal (CFO):**
- EBITDA negative INR 14m vs negative INR 63m last year; improvement from productivity and operating leverage while investing in GTM/AI. (Pg 4)
- Depreciation INR 96m; net other income INR 180m (treasury 175m, misc 24m); net finance cost INR 3m; exceptional expenses INR 15m (legacy tax NIIT server, scheme of arrangement amalgamation RPS & IFBI). (Pg 4)
- PAT INR 81m (+85% YoY), EPS INR 0.60 (+84% YoY). (Pg 4)
- Cash & equivalents INR 7,231m; Capex INR 66m, “past the peak on capital investment in platform… expect capex to moderate”. (Pg 4)
- DSO 53 days; employee count 866 (down 65 QoQ, 19 YoY). (Pg 4)

- **Tone:** Professional, measured, forward-looking. Management used terms like “strong note”, “confident”, “clear sense of winning”.

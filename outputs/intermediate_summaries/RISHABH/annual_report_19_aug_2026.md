## RISHABH INSTRUMENTS LIMITED (NSE: RISHABH) | FY2024-25 ANNUAL REPORT FORENSICS

### MANAGEMENT LETTERS
- **Key Themes**: Resilience in a volatile global environment; theme "Smart Solutions for a Sustainable Future"; emphasis on integrated hardware-software solutions post-Microsys acquisition.
- **Opportunities**: US market growth (60% YoY, UL-certified products), solar inverter scaling post-certification, SCADA integration (Microsys), capacity expansion at Nashik (doubling instrumentation capacity).
- **Risks**: European automotive sector softness, legacy contracts in die-casting, input cost volatility, currency fluctuations.
- **Strategic Priorities**: 10-12% incremental revenue from new products over 2 years; shift die-casting portfolio from automotive to industrial; global reach expansion (US, UK, Middle East).
- **Walk-the-Talk Check**:
  - FY25 revenue grew 4.4% vs FY24 (INR 7,203M vs INR 6,897M) – modest growth delivered.
  - PAT declined 47% (INR 212M vs INR 399M) – **miss on profitability** despite top-line growth; driven by die-casting losses and product mix.
  - Die-casting segment reached EBITDA break-even in Q4 – **partial delivery** on turnaround narrative.

### BUSINESS & STRATEGY
- **Core Segments & Revenue Mix (Consolidated)**:
  - Metering, Control & Protection: INR 2,578M (36%)
  - Aluminium Die-Casting: INR 2,574M (36%)
  - Electrical Automation: INR 791M (11%)
  - Portable Test & Measurement: INR 358M (5%)
  - Solar String Inverters: INR 209M (3%)
- **New Additions**: Acquisition of Microsys (Czech Republic) for SCADA software; new UL-certified products (current transformers, clamp meters).
- **Capex vs Plan**:
  - Capex: INR 762M (consolidated) vs plan to expand Nashik facilities.
  - **Commissioned**: New SMT lines in India and Poland; 1.5 MW solar plant at European foundry.
  - **Pending**: Nashik Facility I & II expansion (expected completion Jan 2026); 15+ products in certification pipeline.

### FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK
- **Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y)**:
  - Revenue: INR 5,695M (FY23) → INR 6,897M (FY24) → INR 7,203M (FY25) – **CAGR ~12%**.
  - PAT: INR 607M (FY23) → INR 399M (FY24) → INR 212M (FY25) – **sharp decline; negative trend**.
- **EBITDA Margin Trend**: Contracting – 14.8% (FY23) → 15.5% (FY24) → 8.9% (FY25). **Red flag**: Die-casting losses, higher employee costs (ESOP), and forex impact.
- **CFO vs PAT**: CFO INR 649.5M vs PAT INR 212M – **cash generation healthy**, driven by working capital release (inventory down).
- **Working Capital**:
  - Receivable days: Improved (likely due to higher collections).
  - Inventory days: Reduced (INR 1,617M vs INR 1,747M prior year).
- **ROCE/ROE Trend**: Deteriorating – ROE 14.3% (FY24) → 11.9% (FY25); ROCE 14.2% (FY24) → 12.4% (FY25).
- **Debt Position**: Net cash of INR 1,022M (cash & equivalents INR 900.5M vs borrowings INR 958.7M – mostly subsidiary debt). No leverage concerns at holding company level.

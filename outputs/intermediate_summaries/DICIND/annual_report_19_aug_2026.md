**ANNUAL REPORT ANALYSIS: DIC INDIA LIMITED (FY 2025)**

---

**MANAGEMENT LETTERS**

*   **Key Themes:**
    *   **Resilience in a Challenging Environment:** Emphasis on navigating geopolitical uncertainty, currency volatility, and raw material (especially TiO2) regulatory/trade changes.
    *   **Value-led Growth:** Market recovery described as "value-led rather than volume-driven," with customers prioritizing cost transparency and inventory optimization.
    *   **Sustainability as a Business Driver:** Strong focus on executing sustainability (low-VOC, toluene-free inks, energy efficiency) not just as compliance but as a value driver and differentiator.
    *   **Operational Discipline & Cost Focus:** Continued push on formulation efficiency, supply chain strengthening, and digital enablement to protect margins.
    *   **New Strategic Pillars:** Establishment of new growth anchors focused on customer-centric service models and application expertise.

*   **Stated Opportunities, Risks, and Strategic Priorities:**
    *   **Opportunities:** Market positioning improvements, sustainability at scale, innovation in compliant ink systems (water-based, UV LED), supply chain responsiveness.
    *   **Risks:** Elevated currency volatility, raw material uncertainty (TiO2), demand variability, competitive intensity.
    *   **Strategic Priorities:** Making Safety & Quality non-negotiable, strengthening cost discipline, expanding sustainable product portfolios, enhancing cross-functional execution.

*   **Walk-the-Talk Check (Guidance vs. Delivery):**
    *   **Revenue:** Turnover grew marginally to ₹89,179 lakh from ₹88,153 lakh (+1.2%), indicating stable but not strong top-line growth.
    *   **Profitability:** Profit Before Tax (after exceptional items) declined to ₹2,365 lakh from ₹2,590 lakh (-8.7%), impacted by a one-time exceptional charge (new labor codes).
    *   **Assessment:** Management highlighted challenges and focus on discipline. The delivery shows resilience in revenue but profitability was hit by a one-off item, aligning with the noted volatile environment.

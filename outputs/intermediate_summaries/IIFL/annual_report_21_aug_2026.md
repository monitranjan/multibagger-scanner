## FORENSIC FLAGS

**Related Party Transactions:**
- ⚠️ Extensive RPTs with group companies (IIFL Capital Services, IIFL Facilities Services, IIFL Management Services)
- ICDs given/taken with subsidiaries: ₹2,800 Cr (FY26) vs ₹1,535 Cr (FY25) — **56% increase in intra-group lending**
- Referral fees from Livlong Protection & Wellness Solutions: ₹84 Cr — insurance referral business with common promoter
- RPTs as % of consolidated turnover: Purchases 8.8%, Sales 3.0% — **higher than FY25 but within materiality thresholds** — 🟡 Yellow
- Senior Mr. Nirmal Jain draws ₹17.5 Cr salary + ₹21.4 Cr dividend; rent from company for office premises

**Contingent Liabilities vs Net Worth:**
- Contingent liabilities (consolidated): ₹681 Cr
- Net worth: ₹15,619 Cr
- **Ratio: 4.4%** — below 10% threshold ✅
- Key components: Income tax demands ₹345 Cr, GST/service tax ₹154 Cr, bank guarantees ₹163 Cr

**Miscellaneous Expenses as % of Revenue:**
- Standalone: ₹3 Cr on ₹7,448 Cr revenue = 0.04% ✅

**Auditor Opinion:**
- **Unqualified** (clean) for both standalone and consolidated ✅
- No qualifications in Audit Report or CARO for IIFL Finance Limited
- CARO qualifications flagged for subsidiaries: IIFL HFC (title deed issues), IIFL Samasta (NPA classification, fraud) — these are subsidiary-level, not parent

**Key Audit Matters:**
1. **IT systems** — significant dependency; controls tested and found adequate
2. **ECL on loans** — ₹38,136 Cr gross loans (standalone) against ₹429 Cr ECL — complex modeling with management overlay of ₹0.22 Cr (minimal)
3. NHB observations on HFC: pre-EMI practice for non-housing loans flagged, and accounting policy correction (₹646 Cr NPA restatement)

**🔴 Red Flag — HFC NPA Restatement:**
- IIFL Home Finance had to restate NPA figures from ₹458 Cr to ₹646 Cr (GNPA) after NHB inspection found issues with credit-date recording and pre-EMI amortization practices
- This is a significant accounting adjustment that would have **understated GNPA in prior year** if not caught

**🔴 Red Flag — Exceptional Items:**
- ₹587 Cr provision on AIF-derived security receipts taken as exceptional item in FY25
- Multiple related-party NCD purchases by promoter associates (IIFL Management Services, Mansukh Jain, Venkataraman Rajamani)

**Accounting Policy Changes:**
- Shift from NHB's accounting date to bank realization date for credit of customer payments — conservative ✅
- Labour Code impact: ₹15 Cr incremental gratuity liability recognized — **transparent** ✅

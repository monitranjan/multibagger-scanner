# Rane (Madras) Limited | Q1 FY27 Earnings Call Transcript Analysis

## Management Commentary
- **Industry & Q1 Performance:** Indian automobile industry began FY27 on positive note with record Q1 sales/across segments. Geopolitical uncertainties in West Asia, elevated input costs, and supply chain challenges persist. RML Q1 FY27 total revenue INR 1,050.6 crores vs INR 884.4 crores in Q1 FY26 (+18.8% YoY). New business wins lifetime value (LTV) INR 2,040 crores, reflecting continued customer confidence.
- **Acquisition:** Rane (Madras) Limited completed acquisition of Hindustan Composites Limited Friction business on slump sale basis (agreement signed June 30, 2026). All regulatory/closing conditions fulfilled. Business will be integrated phased manner; complementary to existing brake component division (PV/2-wheeler vs CV/railway). ~40% of acquired business is railway-focused. EBITDA margin believed at 13-14% (management estimate), accretive to RML portfolio.
- **Strategic Priorities:** Focus on sustaining growth momentum while strengthening operational efficiency, managing costs, and maintaining disciplined profitability. Near-term outlook supported by festive season demand momentum, but challenging cost environment (commodity prices, logistics, energy costs) closely monitored.
- **Capital Structure:** Maintain path to net debt/equity <0.5 by March '28 (18-month timeline). Velacheri property sale in process, amounts received phased. Land parcels disposal as/when right value achieved. Remain committed to bring down debt levels in next 12-18 months.
- **Professional Services:** INR 49-50 crore incurred YoY (vs ~INR 49-50 crore previous year). Services relate to merger of three companies, reimagining aftermarket structure, and cost reduction/transformation initiatives. Benefits expected on full-year basis in FY '28 (aftermarket growth, synergies, cost reduction pathways).

## Tone, Clarity, and Confidence of Responses
- **P.A. Padmanabhan (CFO):** Methodical and clear on financial metrics and timelines. Maintains confidence on debt target trajectory, margin aspiration to double-digit full year, and pass-through timeline. Redirects briefly ("One second") when clarifying currency units (EUR vs USD correction). Transparent on capex breakdown and funding mix. Cautious but aspirational on margin recovery timing. Avoids overpromising quarterly specifics but sets full-year direction.
- **S. Prasad (Corporate Planning):** Clear on strategic rationale for acquisitions, synergy timelines (12-18 months), and outsourcing percentages (~20-22% for aftermarket). Confident on benefit trajectory toward double-digit profitability FY '28.
- **Overall Dynamics:** Professional and transparent on financials. Management avoids definitive quarterly margin guidance, instead framing improvement as a full-year aspiration dependent on volume, demand, and pass-through lag. Some hesitation on specific operational metrics (capacity utilization not "readily available"), but provides available data points. Corrects errors promptly (e.g., EUR23m vs $23m clarification).

## Focus Areas
- **Product Launches & Segments:** Industry-wide new model launches sustained PV growth (SUVs primary driver). RML new business wins across portfolio. Steering product advancement noted (rack and pinion, ball joints, exports). Aluminum casting growth driven by structurally favorable lightweighting and aluminum price increase; volume growth also contributing. No mention of "Project Neev" or "direct distribution" in transcript.
- **Channel Margins:** RML total revenue growth 18.8%. Rane Steering Systems muted EBITDA margins due to legacy low-price orders and absence of one-time retrospective price increases seen in corresponding period last year. Rane Brake/aftermarket segment relatively static (~INR 135 crore range) but management positive on growth outlook via railways, commercial vehicles, and aftermarket post Hindustan Composites integration. Hindustan Composites friction business EBITDA margin 11-12% (believed sustainable as independent division). Aftermarket ~20-22% outsourced.
- **Inventory Levels:** Not explicitly discussed in transcript. No reference to "Project Neev" or "direct distribution" inventory initiatives.

## Future Outlook & Guidance
- **Margins:** Aspiring to double-digit EBITDA full year FY '27. Current margin pressure from commodity costs, lag in price pass-through (some monthly, some quarterly; some not recoverable like minimum wages), and adverse product mix (seat belts vs airbags in ZF Rane). Expect improvement Q3 onwards. Hindustan Composites acquisition adds ~13-14% EBITDA (management belief), aim to sustain 11-12% going forward.
- **Volume Growth:** Industry growth >15-20%. RML in line with market growth from technology and customer perspective. Some customers/models growing faster (Mahindra, tractor/trailer pack value differences). Exports growing well; 54% of new business LTV from export programs. Steering exports advancement noted, though "deemed exports" (billing in INR) do not capture forex benefit.
- **CAPEX:** RML INR 270-300 crores; RSSL subsidiary ~INR 70 crores; ZF Rane ~INR 250-280 crores. Total peak outflow ~INR 1,100 crores briefly, expected below INR 1,000 crores by year-end. Increase in RML capex due to order advancement (pulled from next financial year). ZF Rane capex plan unchanged despite parent ZF Lifestyle division hive-off; just structural split into independent entities.
- **M&A Integration:** Hindustan Composites Friction business acquired (completed June 30, 2026). ~40% railway share, new footprint in Maharashtra. Synergies planned over 12-18 months (brand, aftermarket, formulations). No plans to merge all entities into Rane Holdings as of now. ZF Rane structural split (occupant safety and steering gear now separate entities) with no capex change, each reaching out for debt requirements as needed.
- **Seasonality & Debt:** Festive season expected to sustain demand momentum following strong Q1. Net debt/equity target <0.5 by March '28 funded partially by debt, internal accruals, and Velacheri land sale proceeds.

  - *Warranty Provision (LifeTec JV):* EUR23 million provision unchanged since December '25; only forex changes since then. Early to comment on recoverability with vendor/insurance; taking necessary steps to mitigate cost.
  - *Evasive/High-Confidence Areas:* Management evasive on exact capacity utilization data ("don't have readily available"). Highly confident on debt timeline, margin aspiration direction, and synergy timelines. Cautious on exact quarterly margin recovery but clear on full-year trajectory. Avoided committing on "when" for specific margin inflection, instead framing as volume/demand-dependent.
  - *Structural Questions:* No plans to merge all entities into Rane Holdings as of now. ZF Rane capex no change despite parent ZF Lifestyle hive-off, just separate entities. Professional fees structure clarified as merger-related, structure reimagining, and cost reduction initiatives.

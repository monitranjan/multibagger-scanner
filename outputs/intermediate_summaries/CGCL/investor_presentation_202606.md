**Capri Global Capital Limited - Q1FY27 Investor Presentation Extract**

**I. Core Product Segments & Launches**
- **MSME Loans:** Includes MSME, Micro LAP, and Solar Loans. MSME segment contributed 16.9% to Q1FY27 AUM. AUM grew 21% YoY to ₹67.8 billion.
- **Gold Loans:** Major growth driver. AUM contributed 47.8% to Q1FY27 AUM, up from 36.8% in Q1FY26. Portfolio value grew 111% YoY to ₹191.8 billion.
- **Housing Finance (CGHFL):** AUM contributed 19.5% to Q1FY27 AUM. AUM grew 42% YoY to ₹78.2 billion.
- **Construction Finance:** AUM contributed 15.8% to Q1FY27 AUM. AUM grew 40% YoY to ₹63.3 billion.
- **Car Loan Origination (Distribution):** Not a lender; an asset-light fee income stream. Q1FY27 originations value grew 43% YoY. Average ticket size: ₹1.20 million.
- **Insurance Distribution:** Asset-light income stream. Partnered with 22 insurers. Q1FY27 total premium: ₹652 million. Revenue from operations (Net Car Loan Fee + Insurance + Co-lending) for FY26: ₹10.8 billion.

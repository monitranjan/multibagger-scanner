# **CAPRI GLOBAL CAPITAL LTD (CGCL) – ANNUAL REPORT FY2024-25 FORENSIC ANALYSIS**

## **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key Themes:** Aggressive scaling via gold loans (130.4% AUM growth), MSME focus, tech-driven underwriting, ESG commitments.
- **Stated Opportunities:** Gold loan expansion (803 branches, ₹100M AUM/branch), Micro LAP launch, car loan/insurance distribution.
- **Stated Risks:** Interest rate volatility, credit risk in MSME, liquidity management in tight funding environment.
- **Strategic Priorities:** ₹500B AUM by FY28, mid-teen ROE, 200+ branch expansion, AI/data science integration.
- **Walk-the-Talk Check:**  
  - **FY25 Guidance:** Targeted "mid-teen ROE" – Delivered **RoAE 11.8%** (from 7.6% YoY) – *Achieved*  
  - **Cost-to-income ratio** improved to **59.9%** from 66.6% – *Delivered on efficiency goal*  
  - **AUM growth target** of 40%+ – **Achieved 46% YoY**  
  - **Gold loan dominance** clearly executed as planned.

## **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
- **Core Segments:** MSME (₹52.8B AUM, 23.1%), Gold Loans (₹80.4B, 35.2%), Housing (₹52.0B, 22.8%), Construction Finance (₹41.3B, 18.1%), Car Loan Distribution (₹105.5B originated).
- **New Additions:** Micro LAP (launched Q2 FY25, ₹3.5B AUM), insurance distribution (₹1.29B premium), car loan subsidiary formed.
- **Geographies:** 19 states/UTs, 1,111 branches (176 added).
- **Capex vs Planned:** No major capex overrun mentioned; technology spend embedded in operating costs. **New subsidiaries** incorporated post-year for merchant banking/wealth management.

## **FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: GOOD (with watchpoints)**
- **Strengths:** Clean audit, strong AUM/profit growth, improving returns, conservative provisioning, robust governance structure.
- **Watchpoints:** Negative operating cash flow due to rapid loan growth (liquidity risk), rising leverage, IT control gaps flagged by auditor, fraud incident (though contained), KMP turnover.

---
**Summary:** CGCL demonstrates strong execution in scaling its retail-focused lending business, particularly in gold loans. Financials show healthy profitability and return expansion. However, the **significant negative cash flow from operations** and **increasing leverage** require close monitoring, as does the **IT control environment** highlighted by auditors. Overall, a **high-growth story with manageable but present risks**.

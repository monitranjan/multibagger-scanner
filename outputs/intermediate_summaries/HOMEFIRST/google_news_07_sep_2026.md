While no article explicitly enumerates risks, based on business model (housing finance) and current macro context:

2. **Interest Rate Risk:** ₹150 cr NCD issuance adds to borrowing cost sensitivity; ALM mismatch exposure.
3. **Liquidity Risk:** Reliance on wholesale funding markets (NCDs, bank lines) — vulnerable to credit cycle tightening.
4. **Regulatory Risk:** RBI norms on HFCs (LCR, capital, provisioning) under ongoing review.
5. **Competition Risk:** Aggressive pricing from peers (Aadhar, PNB Housing, LIC Housing, Aavas, Can Fin) in affordable segment.
6. **Macro Risk:** Slowdown in housing demand, property price corrections impacting LTV coverage.

## 🔍 KEY ANALYTICAL OBSERVATIONS

**Strengths:**
- Asset quality stable despite macro headwinds (1+ DPD 4.7%, GNPA 1.8%)
- Cost-to-Income improvement of 330 bps YoY to 32.5% — best in class
- CoB reduction from 8.4% to 8.1% while spreads expanded
- Diversified funding with 31 lenders; zero commercial paper
- Strong capital position post-QIP (CRAR 44.1%) with no pledged shares
- BRSR Core assured by SGS India (Reasonable Assurance)

**Concerns:**
- CFO/PAT disconnect (negative operating cash flow vs ₹540 Cr PAT) — inherent in HFC growth model
- RoE compression from 16.5% to 15.7% due to equity dilution from QIP
- 12 fraud cases (₹2.71 Cr) reported — small but indicates operational risk
- New Labour Codes triggered ₹32.99 Mn one-time gratuity expense
- Audit trail observation on loan management system — needs remediation
- 1 POSH complaint upheld in FY25 (resolved in FY26)
- Pre-money RoE of 16.8% is the true operating profitability metric

## 📊 FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE

# **GOOD** ✅

**Rationale:** Clean unqualified audit opinion, conservative provisioning methodology, no related party concerns, stable asset quality, strong governance with 71% independent board, transparent disclosures with BRSR reasonable assurance, and improving operational metrics (cost-to-income down 330 bps). The cash flow mismatch is structural to the HFC business model and not a quality concern. Audit trail observation and minor fraud cases are immaterial given scale (₹15,878 Cr AUM).

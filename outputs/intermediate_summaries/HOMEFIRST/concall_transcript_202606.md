# Home First Finance Company India Ltd — Q1 FY27 Earnings Call Analysis

## 1. Management Commentary

### Key Statements from Leadership

**Manoj Viswanathan (MD & CEO):**
- Framed the quarter as a "strong start" to FY27, citing healthy AUM/disbursement growth, robust earnings, stable asset quality, and continued investment in distribution/technology.
- Highlighted granular portfolio quality — 83% individual housing loans; origination yield held at 13.0%.
- Articulated a clear AI strategy around three outcomes: customer experience, employee productivity, and structural cost efficiencies.
- Confirmed 25% AUM growth guidance, while preserving profitability, portfolio quality and operating efficiency.
- Acknowledged Nutan Patwari's departure (effective Aug 31, 2026) and indicated the Board is evaluating CFO candidates.

**Nutan Gaba Patwari (CFO):**
- Detailed financials — total income INR540 crore (+18.6% YoY); cost of borrowing down 10 bps QoQ to 7.8%; incremental cost at 7.6%; spread ex-co-lending at 5.3%; NIM at 6.0%.
- Reported cost-to-income at 32.7% (+70 bps QoQ) due to increments and fresh hiring, partly offset by lower admin expenses; opex/assets at 2.8%.
- PAT INR160 crore (+34.5% YoY, +7% QoQ); RoA 4.2%; RoE 14.5%.
- Credit cost 40 bps; Stage-3 PCR 23.4% (including overlays: 45.3%).
- Capital adequacy 42.6%; Tier-1 42.2%; net worth INR4,483 crore; BVPS INR429.

**Sunil Anjana (Head, Treasury & IR):**
- Standard introductions and procedural framing.

## 3. Focus Areas

### a. Product Launches / Tech Initiatives
- **'Cue'**: in-house AI-orchestrated omnichannel customer communications platform operationalized.
- **Bureau Analyzer & Contextual Bank Statement Analysis**: deployed in production; improved underwriting TAT.
- **AI pilots**: lead qualification, legal/technical valuation, income analysis.
- **Green Homes**: 100 incremental certifications in Q1; cumulative 550 certified homes.

### b. Channel Margins
- Origination yield: 13.0%; portfolio yield ex-co-lending: 13.1%.
- Cost of borrowing: 7.8% (-10 bps QoQ); incremental: 7.6%.
- Spread ex-co-lending: 5.3%; incremental spread: 5.40%.
- **Co-lending**: INR46 cr disbursed; book INR617 cr (3.6% of AUM). Channel is being rebuilt with focus on enabling infrastructure.
- **Direct Assignment**: INR285 cr transaction executed in Q1.
- No mention of **Project Neev** or **direct distribution** initiatives by these names in the transcript.

### c. Inventory Levels (Portfolio mix)
- Individual housing loans: 83% of portfolio.
- LAP: ~13–14% of portfolio; not a strategic focus.
- Branch network: 175 branches, 373 touch points.
- Headcount: 1,988 (+133 QoQ); employees/branch ~11.
- Ticket size migration: >INR25 lakh share rose from 14% to 18% YoY (gradual, from 12% two years ago).
- 88% of customers have prior credit history vs 74% three years ago; bureau score 738 → 750.
- CIBIL at origination (not AUM).

## 6. Key Observations / Red Flags

1. **CFO Departure**: Nutan Patwari exits Aug 31, 2026 — transition risk; no successor named.
2. **Co-lending weakness**: INR46 cr disbursed in Q1 is a notable drop from prior pace; management attributes to process/policy alignment but is a soft spot.
3. **Tamil Nadu softness**: Team-related issues + tariff stress, now behind, but warrants monitoring.
4. **No mention of Project Neev, Fluence Pharma, BTM, Reginald** — none of these strategic initiatives/M&A were referenced in the transcript.
5. **Repayment rate at 14.3%** is below FY26 average (~16%) — driven by exceptionally low BT-out; reversal could compress AUM growth optically.
6. **Capital adequacy still very high (42.6%)** — significant headroom for growth or capital actions.

---

**Bottom Line**: Management was articulate, confident on franchise quality, and disciplined on guidance. Areas requiring monitoring: CFO transition, co-lending trajectory, geographic concentration shifts (UP push), and sustainability of BT-out/credit cost improvements.

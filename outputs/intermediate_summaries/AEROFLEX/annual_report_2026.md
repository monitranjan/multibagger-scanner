**AEROFLEX INDUSTRIES LIMITED - ANNUAL REPORT FY26 FORENSIC ANALYSIS**

**MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key Themes**: Strategic pivot towards AI/data center infrastructure ("Engineering the Next Intelligence"). Emphasis on moving up the value chain from components to integrated solutions.
- **Stated Opportunities**: Liquid cooling for data centers, semiconductors, aerospace, robotics, hydrogen, and EV thermal management.
- **Stated Risks**: Global geopolitical uncertainty, FX volatility, longer customer qualification cycles in new sectors, and competitive intensity.
- **Strategic Priorities**: Expand liquid cooling skid capacity (target 15,000 units by Q2FY27), increase assembly contribution to ~70-75% of revenue, expand metal bellows, and strengthen global hubs.

Further expansions planned for hoses (20M meters), skids (15,000 units), and assembly stations (70) by Q2FY27.

### **3. FUTURE GROWTH ADJACENCIES (STRATEGIC VECTORS)**  

**A. Complex Generics Scale-Up**  
- CNS/ADHD portfolio driving US volume  
- **59 Complex Gx dossiers filed** (32 approved, 27 awaiting)  
- MUPS platform + controlled substances capability = high-barrier moat  
- Target: **Day-1 / Day-181 launches** on high-value molecules  

**B. Peptide CDMO Ramp (Senn Chemicals + Ascelis Peptides)**  
- Swiss CDMO, **60+ years peptide synthesis heritage**  
- Ascelis Peptides (IIT Hyderabad JV) driving R&D pipeline  
- **TFA-free chemistries** unlocking cosmetics & GLP-1 therapeutic demand  
- Capacity de-risking via multi-site (Switzerland + India)  

**C. Geographic Diversification**  
- **Europe & regulated ROW** emerging as next growth engines (YoY +51% / +56%)  
- Dossier filings expanding across EU, Canada, Brazil, Japan, Korea  
- Multi-region rollout aligned with Complex Gx & peptide complexity  

**D. Capacity Expansion & De-Risking**  
- **Genome Valley GLS:** 10 Bn tablets, **USFDA approved**, Rx shipments started → **+40% formulation capacity**, multi-site manufacturing enabled  
- **Vizag:** Dedicated Oncology OSD (1.1 Bn) + separate API block  
- **GPI Virginia:** 1.5 Bn FD + packaging lines (2 OTC + 1 Rx)  
- FY26 capex ₹5,547 Mn; future capex phased toward digitalization/modular growth  

**E. Oncology Pipeline**  
- Dedicated OSD plant in Vizag (1.1 Bn units) + API block  
- Active development targeting **high-barrier generics**  
- Strategic pivot to **high-complexity, high-margin** therapeutic segment  

**F. Innovation Platforms**  
- **R&D at 6.0% of sales (Q1 FY27)** – highest in 6 quarters  
- Focus: **CNS/ADHD, Oncology, Peptides, Metabolic Disorders, High-Barrier Generics**  
- IIT Hyderabad centre advancing peptide & particle synthesis  

**G. Quality & Compliance Investment**  
- **₹260 Mn spent (last 1 yr)** + **₹750 Mn planned (next 2 yrs)** on automation/digitalization in Quality  
- All key facilities hold current USFDA EIR (GPI Apr’26 VAI, Genome Valley Dec’25, etc.)  
- SBTi-validated Net Zero targets (2050 or sooner, 1.5°C pathway)

**Management Commentary**
- **K.P. Chigurupati (CMD):** Granules is transitioning from a volume/cost-driven model to an innovation-led, differentiated pharmaceutical platform. Cites U.S. GPI ranking improvement (74th to 27th in 5 years), 4th-largest position in controlled substances, and mission to sustainably supply critical ADHD medicines. Complex generics now 50% of finished dosages (up from 39% YoY); peptide CDMO >100% YoY growth. Q1 revenue INR1,477 crores (+22% YoY), gross margin ~65.6%, EBITDA INR339 crores (+37% YoY), PAT INR180 crores (+60% YoY). Return on capital 18%; net debt/EBITDA 0.07x (effectively debt free); operating cash INR387 crores. Gagillapur remediation "essentially complete": FDA met in Jan, all responses submitted on time, agency raised no single concern on adequacy/pace. 7 of 8 facilities carry clean EIR (only Gagillapur excluded, GPI Virginia cleared June). >330 customer/regulatory audits over 2 years without critical observation. 9 applications ready to launch behind clearance. Near-term: bring Gagillapur online, scale Genome Valley (+40% formulation capacity), complex generics higher (already half of finished dosages), grow ADHD/controlled products, oncology launches from Vizag, expand Europe/ROW business. Medium-term: deepen 505(b)(2) portfolio, first-to-file/day 1 launches (2 sole first-to-file with litigation expected, few more in pipeline); scale peptide CDMO across Swiss/India model. Long-term: studying larger opportunities in non-solid dosage areas, under consideration not commitments.  
- **Mukesh Surana (CFO):** Q1 FY27 revenue INR14,768 million (+22% YoY, stable sequentially). Gross margin 65.6% (+74 bps YoY) driven by complex generic and higher value formulations mix. Operating environment influenced by geopolitical tensions West Asia, inflation in raw materials, packing inputs, freight, supply chain costs; opening U.S.A inventory levels insulated Q1 margins broadly in line with Q4 FY26. Pursuing calibrated pricing actions and cost pass-through mechanisms. EBITDA INR3,389 million (+37% YoY at 22.9% margin, +256 bps YoY); QoQ marginal decline 99 bps. R&D INR880 million (~6% of sales, +30% YoY), focused on high barrier areas CNS, oncology, MUPS, complex formulations; Q4 FY26 R&D was 5.3% of sales. PBT before exceptional items INR2,404 million (+41% YoY). Net debt INR1,012 million (from INR4,021 million at FY26 close). Net working capital to sales improved to 29% (from 30% in Q1 FY26 and Q4 FY26). Operating cash INR3,874 million (vs INR1,003 million Q4 FY26). Capex INR890 million (vs INR1,000 million Q4 FY26). ROCE 18% (vs 17.6% Q4 FY26), steady upward trajectory; important measure of converting strategy into value for this capital-intensive business.  
- **Sanjay Kumar (CSO):** Peptide CDMO Q1 broadly in line with expectation at CHF5 million; revenues improved YoY, moderated sequentially after strong Q4, expect H2 stronger than H1. Initiated 3 new customer projects (1 pharma, 2 cosmetics), potential to grow over time; re-engaged on 2 previously discontinued products, one optimistic for reactivation during current fiscal. Zurich and Hyderabad R&D now operating as one integrated organization. Working on TAG-assisted peptide synthesis with encouraging early lab results. Infrastructure upgrades at Zurich: additional solid phase reactors, large purification columns, lyophilization capacity over next months. Initiated next phase of India manufacturing footprint beyond R&D: land earmarked for peptide facility at Vizag. Focus for FY27: PAT positive annual performance, recognizing quarter-to-quarter variability inherent in project

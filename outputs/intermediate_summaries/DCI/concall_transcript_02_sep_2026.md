### 1. Management Commentary (Key statements from CEO/MD, CFO, and leadership)
*Note: Only Managing Director Mr. Chetankumar Timbadia spoke at length. Whole-Time Director Mr. Devendra Sayani and CFO Mr. Piyush Shah were listed as present but had no attributed spoken lines in the transcript.*

**MD Chetankumar Timbadia – Opening Remarks & Q&A:**
- **Business Model:** "What began as a product focused technology business has transformed into a diversified solution-led technology platform." Three pillars: Networking (~30% rev), Unified Communication/Collaboration (~36%), Cyber Security (~27%).
- **FY26 Performance:** "Revenue for FY26 stood at Rs. 736.97 crores, representing a growth of 32.6% year-on-year. Our PAT stood at Rs. 21.21 crores, growing 46.3% year-on-year. The business continues to benefit from stronger operating leverage…"
- **Credentials:** CRISIL upgraded rating from BBB- to BBB; UAE subsidiary operational (local content partner with Etihad Aviation Group); 3-year procurement arrangement with Tata Communications; FT Statista recognition.
- **FY27 Strategic Priorities (Opening):** (1) Increase contribution of services, software subscription, managed offerings, recurring revenue. (2) Expand participation in AI, cloud, digital infrastructure.
- **UAE/GCC:** "While business activities experience temporary moderation due to geopolitical developments… we are not planning to carry forward our distribution. There we are trying to be more of a service-oriented approach…"
- **Channel:** "Our channel ecosystem has expanded to more than 2,000 touchpoints across India…"
- **Samsung Exposure:** "Overall, our exposure to Samsung, it should be approximately, not exact figures, but around Rs 150-175 crores." Exclusive partner for active LEDs with in-house service center.
- **NETSCOUT/Arbor:** "Approximately 60 to 70% business is driven by DC Infotech of whatever they do… technically, I would call it one of the best in this category."
- **Sangfor (Middle East):** Signed but "we are not able to get something very notable on table till now" due to geopolitical slowdown since February.
- **Services Mix:** "~20% comes from software and services. 80% of our revenue comes from products… aspiration is to take from that 20% to 25% in years to come."
- **Q4 Bidding/Bad Debts Expense:** Attributed to "unrealized forex exchange" loss book entry at March 31 due to dollar spike; called "mostly a non-recurring… regular financial bookkeeping entry."
- **Data Center Opportunity:** Targeting Colo/private/captive data centers; aiming to build "complete bouquet of solutions" for AI-ready data centers.

### 2. Tone, Clarity, and Confidence of Responses
- **Tone:** Professional, welcoming, and explanatory. MD consistently framed the company as evolving "from a technology distributor into a technology solution, managed services, and a digital infrastructure platform."
- **Clarity:** Provided approximate quantifications when pressed (Samsung ~Rs 150-175cr; services 20%→25%). However, when asked for exact EBITDA margin numbers, he stated: "If you want to quantify in terms of numbers, I may not be able to do that."
- **Confidence:** 
  - High confidence on market position: "I don't see any threat as such" (Samsung), "one of the best in this category" (NETSCOUT).
  - High confidence on outlook: "I am very pretty sure, pretty confident. We are in for some good times" (Data center). "100% our endeavor is to be successful and it is sustainable… I am pretty sure, very confident there is a room for improvement" (Margins).
  - Realistic/Measured: On working capital, "How much can we do it is something which we have to be realistic about"; on geopolitical impacts, transparent about slow order conversion.

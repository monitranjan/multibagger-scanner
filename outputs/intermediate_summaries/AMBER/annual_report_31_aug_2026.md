THEME INTRODUCTION
Engineering Scale. 
Powering Possibilities. 
Amber’s journey reflects a continuous pursuit of deeper integration, stronger technological capability, 
and greater ownership across the value chain. What began as a manufacturing enterprise has evolved 
into a diversified engineering platform, spanning across consumer durables, electronics, and railway 
subsystems & defence applications.
This evolution is built on a strategy of expanding engineering capabilities, strengthening backward 
integration, and building component-level expertise. This pursuit strengthens the Company’s ability 
to participate more meaningfully in its customers’ value creation, and to be a trusted manufacturing 
partner.
Scale and efficiency remain central to this journey, as the Company expands capacity and 
manufacturing capabilities, to drive operating excellence across its operations.
The essence of Engineering Scale, Powering Possibilities: A commitment to building capabilities 
that translate into consistent execution and sustainable growth.

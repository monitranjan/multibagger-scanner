**EXTRACTION OF CRITICAL SLIDES & CONTENT: BALAJI AMINES LIMITED (Q1 FY27 INVESTOR PRESENTATION)**

### 1. Core Product Segments & Launches (Chemicals & Specialities)
*   **BAL Core Amines & Derivatives:** Methylamines (MMA, DMA, TMA), Ethylamines (MEA, DEA, TEA), N-Butylamine, Isopropylamine, DMAC, DMAHCL, DMU, Choline Chloride, Morpholine. 
*   **BAL Speciality & Other Chemicals:** Acetonitrile (ACN), Dimethyl Formamide (DMF), N-Methyl-2-Pyrrolidone (NMP), Gamma-Butyrolactone (GBL), Di-methyl Carbonate (DMC), Propylene Carbonate (PC), Propylene Glycol (PG), 2-Pyrrolidone, NEP, PVPK.
*   **Key Q1 FY27 Launches & Commissioning:**
    *   **Dimethyl Ether (DME):** Commissioned India's first commercial-scale 100,000 TPA DME plant in Q1 FY27.
    *   **N-Methyl Morpholine (NMM):** 5,000 TPA capacity under execution, expected commissioning in FY27.
    *   **Acetonitrile (ACN):** Expansion from 9,000 TPA to 18,000 TPA under execution, expected commissioning in FY27.
*   **BSCL (55% Subsidiary) Speciality Amines:** Sole Indian manufacturer of EDA, DETA, PIP, AEEA, and AEP. 
*   **BSCL Upcoming Launches (Targeted End-FY27):**
    *   **EDA Derivatives Platform (Unit I):** DETA (8,900 TPA addition), TETA (3,000 TPA), Piperazine (3,300 TPA addition), AEP/AEEA/HEP mixtures (1,000 TPA addition).
    *   **Cyanation Platform (Unit II):** Hydrogen Cyanide (HCN - 10,000 TPA), Sodium Cyanide (NaCN - 12,000 TPA), EDTA & EDTA-2Na (5,000 TPA).
*   **Hospitality Division:** Hotel Balaji Sarovar Premiere (129 rooms, 5-star hotel in Solapur).

### 3. Future Growth Adjacencies & Strategic Expansions
*   **Energy Transition & Clean Fuels:** DME (100,000 TPA) creates exposure to clean fuel, LPG blending, and aerosol propellant applications.
*   **EV Battery Chemicals:** Only Indian producer of Electronic Grade DMC for EV battery applications; NMP used in EV batteries.
*   **Import Substitution Franchise:** First and sole domestic manufacturer of NMP; upcoming HCN, Sodium Cyanide, and EDTA projects to replace import dependencies for domestic pharma, agrochemicals, and mining sectors.
*   **Next-Phase R&D & Greenfield Targets:** Tri Ethyl Ortho Formate (TEOF) identified as the next phase of expansion for BSCL; translating complex cyanide chemistry processes into commercial production.
*   **Volume Growth Guidance:** FY27 volume growth guided at 10-15%; medium-term growth guided at 20-30%.
*   **Capex Inflection:** ₹1,000 Cr+ expansion cycle nearing completion on a consolidated basis; earnings acceleration expected as DME, NMM, ACN, and BSCL projects ramp up.

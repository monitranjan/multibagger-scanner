### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements from CEO, CFO, MD)

**Jai Malpani (Managing Director)**
- **Company Profile/Strategy:** Stated Malpani Group entered parks in 2005 (Wet'n Joy Shirdi). Imagicaaworld now has 9 parks, 287-key Novotel at Khopoli, and exclusive Hello Park partnership (Hyderabad, Surat signed). 
- **M&A/Integration:** Wet'n Joy Parks acquired 2024; “successfully integrated these four parks, implemented common operating systems… over last 2 years.”
- **Vision:** “By 2030, we aspire to operate a portfolio of 12 parks, having kept targets of adding approximately one park every year.” Outdoor sweet spot: 30–50 acre mid-sized parks. Evaluating Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Goa.
- **Cyclicality Mitigation:** Diversifying into indoor entertainment, adding off-season indoor shows/events, Magic Pass for repeat visits, geographic diversification, pushing schools/corporates.
- **Spiritual Tourism:** “In active talks” with state governments to replicate Shirdi model; stressed government support critical for viability.
- **Promoter Warrants:** “The conversion will happen before the given date… We are very positive and we look forward to converting the same.” (Conversion price 73.5 referenced by analyst).

**Dhimant Bakshi (CEO)**
- **Q1 Performance:** Revenue +20% YoY to INR178cr, footfalls +22%. Noted “unprecedented heat wave” causing non-operational days at Khopoli (~2 weeks) and CBSE school holiday shift impacted Mumbai-Pune catchment.
- **Reporting/Segments:** Will report across 4 catchments (Mumbai-Pune, Rest of Maharashtra, Gujarat, Central India).
- **Gujarat Expansion:** INR50cr for 50.002% stake in Mehsana Next Parks (Shanku’s Water Park); effective subsidiary from Q2.
- **Hello Park:** Exclusive partnership with Dubai-based Hello Park; royalty 5–7%; capex INR8–12cr/center; 2–3 centers/year targeted (Hyderabad Lake Shore Y Junction, Surat Phoenix Mall).
- **Pricing:** “Consciously had opted for a slightly softer pricing strategy… over the next three quarters we have headroom there and we will make appropriate corrections.”
- **Gujarat ARPU:** Dropped due to testing price elasticity in Surat; “headroom to grow” via non-ticketing verticals.

**Mayuresh Kore (CFO)**
- **Financials:** EBITDA +24% YoY to INR90cr; margin 50.7% (+170bps). PAT +30% to INR58cr (32.4%). Footfalls 11.5L, ARPU ~INR1,395.
- **Catchments:** Mumbai-Pune rev +18%/footfall +19%; Rest Maha rev +33%/footfall +14%/ARPU +17%; Gujarat rev +15%/footfall +32% (ARPU lower on mix/promo); Central India rev +44%/footfall +48%.
- **Hello Park Economics:** 5% royalty on all revenues; EBITDA margin ~24–25% after mall rentals; payback 3–4 yrs; ticket INR800–900 (65–70% of rev).
- **CAPEX/Debt:** New parks INR200–500cr; funded by internal accruals + moderate debt; avg Debt/EBITDA target 2.5–3x (max 3.5x). Maintenance capex 6–8% of rev (expensed); new attraction capex ~5% of top line.
- **ARPU Guidance:** Resisted ticket hikes during integration; “plan which is getting actioned” Q3/Q4 onwards.

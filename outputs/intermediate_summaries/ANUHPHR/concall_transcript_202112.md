**Analysis of Anuh Pharma Ltd. Q3 FY21-22 Earnings Call (Held 7 Feb 2022)**

*Strictly based on provided transcript text; absent specified items are noted as NOT MENTIONED.*

---

### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements from Leadership)
*Spokesperson: Mr. Ritesh Shah (Joint MD); Mr. Vivek Shah (Joint MD) and Mr. Darshan (CFO) were present but silent. Mr. Bhavin Shah (Latin Manharlal) moderated initially.*

- **Long-term Goal:** “Focused on our part of long-term sustainable growth to achieve revenue of 800 crore in next five years, growing at 15% year on year basis.”
- **Compliance Pillars:** Environment compliance, safety compliance, and GMP compliance are “indispensable” for sustainable growth.
- **Capacity & Launches:** Capacity expansion since 2019 positions company to capture market share. R&D plan: “launch at least five new products year on year basis.”
- **Backward Integration:** “Successfully done backward integration in few of the challenging products” to reduce dependency on Chinese intermediates, “bring efficiency and increase our margins.”
- **CAPEX:** “Plans to do capital expenditure of approximately 40 to 50 lakhs in the financial year 2023” for versatility in production capabilities.
- **Q3 FY22 Performance (Prepared Remarks):** 
  - Revenue: Rs. 132 crores (19% increase vs Q2 FY22 ended 30 Sep 2021).
  - 9M FY22 Revenue: Rs. 361.84 crores (5% higher vs 9M FY21 Rs. 343.79 crores) despite COVID demand fluctuations.
  - Gross Margin Q3: Rs. 28.5 crores (+7.8% vs Q2).
  - EBITDA Q3: Rs. 12.47 crores vs Rs. 14.38 crores in Q2 due to “revision in mark to market impact.”
  - Top 3 contributing products Q3: erythromycin, gliclazide, sulphadoxine.
  - Cost savings: “Saved in tune of Rs. 18.36 crores, which most of these are recurring in nature.”
- **NSE Listing:** “Initiated the process of listing on the National Stock Exchange and we hope to get this listing done soon.”

### 2. Tone, Clarity, and Confidence of Responses
- **Overall Demeanor:** Patient (moderator thanked management for “answering it patiently”). Ritesh Shah remained formally polite even under harsh scrutiny.
- **Clarity Issues:** Sometimes vague or contradictory on figures (e.g., Q4 revenue guidance shifted from 130-140 cr to 185-190 cr; 9M revenue cited as 361.84 cr in script but 375-376 cr in Q&A).
- **Confidence:** Highly confident on macro narrative (“We are confident to reach our target made for current financial year 2022”) and cost pass-through. Less clear/evasive on governance specifics and precise timelines.
- **Evasiveness:** Notable when pressed on NSE timeline (“ball is in their court”), Company Secretary departure (“I will check and let you know”), and net profit guidance (“we always give our prediction in EBITDA at this point of time”).

### 4. Future Outlook & Guidance
- **Margins (EBITDA & Mix Shift):** 
  - FY22 EBITDA margin recalibrated to “10% of EBITDA instead of earlier expectation of 12 to 15%” due to “increase in the cost of production because of the compliances as well as the product mix.”
  - Mix shift detail: “product mix which has come because of some erythromycin derivatives anti TB anti-malarial because of the fund allocation at the Global Fund. And… reduction in the paediatric prescription so, some of our erythromycin which were going in the paediatric is seeing a little reduced volumes.”
  - FY23 Guidance: Top line 600 crores, EBITDA 67 crores (implies ~11.2%).
  - Q3 EBITDA was Rs 12.47 cr; full year FY22 EBITDA expectation ~50 crores (from Q&A).

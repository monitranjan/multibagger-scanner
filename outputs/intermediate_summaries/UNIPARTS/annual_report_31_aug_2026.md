### **ANALYSIS OF UNIPARTS INDIA LIMITED - FY2024-25 ANNUAL REPORT**

**MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key Themes:** Sectorial slowdown (customer inventory correction), maintaining balance sheet strength, investing for future recovery, strategic broadbasing (products, customers, geographies), and optimism tied to secular OHV demand.
- **Stated Opportunities:** Recovery from inventory correction, expansion into adjacent products (PTO, Hydraulics, Fabrications), near-shoring in Mexico, and growth in aftermarket.
- **Risks:** Cyclical demand weakness in key markets (US/Europe), forex volatility, geopolitical uncertainty, and potential for margin pressure.
- **Strategic Priorities:** Deepen OEM relationships, expand geographic reach, improve cost efficiency, invest in digital capabilities, and grow non-agricultural segments.
- **Walk-the-Talk Check:** Guidance of addressing slowdown without balance sheet impairment was met (net cash position, stable margins). Revenue decline of ~15.4% was within sectoral context. New business wins and warehouse operations in Mexico aligned with strategy.

**BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
- **Core Segments:** Agriculture (63.1% of revenue), Construction/Forestry/Mining (36.0%). Products: 3PL (48.6%), PMP (48.7%).
- **New Additions:** New product platform for high-HP tractors. Warehouse operations in Mexico initiated (Phase 1). Entered Mexico market with first OEM win.
- **Capex:** Standalone capex (PPE + CWIP) was ₹153Cr vs ₹195Cr prior year. Consolidated PPE additions were ₹302Cr. Investments in digital (Anaplan), solar power, and sustainability (ETP/LTE).

**FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK** (Consolidated)
- **Revenue & PAT (3Y Trend):** Revenue declined from ₹1,366Cr (FY23) to ₹1,140Cr (FY24) to ₹964Cr (FY25). PAT followed: ₹205Cr → ₹125Cr → ₹88Cr. **Deteriorating.**
- **EBITDA Margin Trend:** Contracted from 23% (FY23) to 18% (FY24) to 17% (FY25). **Contracting.**
- **CFO vs PAT:** CFO of ₹182Cr was **207% of PAT (₹88Cr)**, indicating strong cash conversion. **Green Flag.**
- **Working Capital Movement:** Inventory days reduced. Receivable days improved. Payable days increased. Overall WC cycle increased to 154 days. **Mixed.**
- **ROCE/ROE Trend:** ROCE fell from 35.9% (FY23) to 19.5% (FY24) to 13.7% (FY25). ROE: 26.8% → 14.7%. **Severe deterioration.**
- **Debt Position:** Net cash position of ₹194.5Cr (Standalone) as stated by management. Consolidated shows gross debt of ₹838Cr with cash of ₹331Cr. D/E ratio: 0.09 (Standalone), 0.09 (Consolidated). Interest coverage strong. **Healthy.**

**FORENSIC FLAGS**
- **Related Party Transactions (RPTs):** Significant. Sales to subsidiaries ~46% of standalone revenue. Purchases from subsidiaries ~1.8%. Loans to subsidiary (Gripwel Conag) ₹286Cr. Dividend income from subsidiaries ₹477Cr. Transactions stated as arm's length. **Yellow Flag (Size & Nature).**
- **Contingent Liabilities:** ₹162Cr (Standalone) vs Net Worth of ₹574Cr = **28%**. Includes income tax demands (₹129Cr). **Red Flag (>10%).**
- **Miscellaneous Expenses:** ₹1.1Cr (Standalone) on revenue of ₹574Cr = **0.19%**. **Green Flag (<3%).**
- **Auditor Opinion:** **Unqualified** (Clean) opinion on both Standalone and Consolidated financials. **Green Flag.**
- **Key Audit Matters:** Revenue recognition (due to complex Incoterms) and Trade Receivables (collectability). Standard for manufacturing exports. **No red flags.**
- **CARO Report:** No qualifications or adverse remarks in CARO reports. **Green Flag.**
- **Accounting Policy Changes:** No significant changes. Conservative inventory valuation (lower of cost or NRV). Standard depreciation policies. **Green Flag.**

**GOVERNANCE**
- **Management Remuneration:** CMD (Mr. Soni) drew ₹0.03Cr from Company, but ₹3.01Cr from subsidiary. VP (Mr. Soni) drew ₹0 from Company, ₹5.06Cr from subsidiary. Total KMP remuneration (Consolidated): ₹18.8Cr. **Yellow Flag (Low transparency; bulk paid via subsidiary).**
- **KMP Changes:** Resignation of Group COO (Sudhakar Kolli). Appointment of new WTD & Group COO (Tanushree Bagrodia). **Material change.** **Yellow Flag.**
- **Promoter Pledging/Stake:** No promoter pledging disclosed. Promoter group holding stable at ~65.7%. **Green Flag.**

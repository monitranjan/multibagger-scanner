## MANAGEMENT LETTERS

**Key Themes from Chairman's Letter (Mr. Sushil Suri):**
- Secured landmark ₹825 crore (~USD 91M) multi-year CDMO mandate from a leading global pharma company — called out as "one of the most significant single CDMO mandates in Morepen's history"
- Dr. Morepen Ltd. deconsolidated effective 31 July 2025, impacting comparability
- Standalone revenue grew 8.39%; Medical Devices grew 20.13% YoY
- Strategic pivot: API heritage → CDMO long-duration partnerships
- First commercial dispatch of ~₹58 crore under CDMO completed in Q1 FY27

**MD Letter (Mr. Sanjay Suri):**
- API remains largest revenue contributor; Europe's export share rose from 31.09% → 44.43%
- USA share declined from 21.78% → 17.65%
- New molecule revenue (Edoxaban, Linagliptin, Saxagliptin, Vonoprazan) surged 121% to ₹5,637L
- Bempedoic Acid launched with exclusive global supply agreement
- Robust pipeline: 4 USDMFs, 2 CEPs, multiple filings across ANVISA, SAPHRA, Swissmedic, Korean MFDS, UK MHRA, Taiwan FDA

**Stated Opportunities:** CDMO partnerships, global API demand, patent cliff, China+1 strategy, domestic healthcare expansion
**Stated Risks:** US trade/tariff uncertainty, China import dependence, regulatory scrutiny, pricing controls, currency volatility

**Walk-the-Talk Check:**
- Consolidated revenue was essentially flat (₹182,994L → ₹182,701L) — growth narrative rests on standalone and new CDMO pipeline
- Operating surplus dropped ₹19,243L → ₹14,694L — 23.6% decline; management attributes this to margin pressure in select APIs, higher costs, and deconsolidation impact
- CDMO: First dispatch of ₹58 crore done, but the ₹825 crore mandate will ramp over multiple years — limited FY26 revenue contribution
- 🟡 **Gap**: Growth rhetoric vs actual PAT decline. Consolidated PAT dropped from ₹11,802L to ₹9,488L (-19.6%)

## FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: **AVERAGE**

**Rationale:**
- Auditor opinion is clean; internal controls rated adequate; no fraud reported
- However, significant red flags exist: profit-to-cash conversion is poor (~34%), contingent liabilities are 27% of net worth (dominated by a ₹317 Cr GST dispute), EBITDA margins are contracting, and ROCE/ROE are deteriorating
- The business restructuring (Medical Devices hiving-off to subsidiary) creates comparability issues and involves repeated timeline extensions
- Related party transaction share is rising and involves complex inter-entity dealings with promoter family
- The ₹825 Cr CDMO contract is a genuine positive but revenue contribution is nascent
- Overall: Standard compliance but underwhelming financial quality — profits are shrinking, cash conversion is weak, and contingent liabilities are material

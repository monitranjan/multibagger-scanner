# SYRMA SGS TECHNOLOGY LIMITED — FY26 ANNUAL REPORT FORENSICS

## MANAGEMENT LETTERS

**Key Themes from Chairman (Sandeep Tandon) & MD (Jasbir Gujral):**
- "Year of building capability, not capacity" — PCB manufacturing entry, naval/maritime & rail expansion
- Quality-of-earnings narrative: richer vertical mix, exports 25% of revenue, ODM at 17% (up from 12%)
- Three-pillar commitments: consistent top-line growth, steady EBITDA expansion, positive cash generation
- Structural confidence: India's "electronics decade," policy stack moving from announcement to delivery

**Stated Opportunities:**
- USD 24.7bn India PCB market by 2033 (~90% currently imported)
- Naval/maritime electronics via Elcome (naval order flow strengthening)
- Export tailwinds: electronics to become India's second-largest export
- ECMS policy framework shifting from capacity to value-addition

**Stated Risks:**
- Geopolitical/tariff volatility (US tariffs via Section 122)
- Geopolitical concentration in critical minerals
- FX volatility (USD/INR)
- Customer concentration (top 5 at 34% of revenue)
- Skilled talent shortages

**Walk-the-Talk Check:**
- **Delivered:** Revenue +27% YoY, EBITDA +56%, PAT +87%, exports +41% (₹1,258cr), ODM +80% (₹825cr)
- **Missed:** Consumer vertical capped at 30% (deliberate margin pivot, not a miss)
- **VP Mr. Satendra Singh exited June 29, 2026** — just after fiscal close; replaced by Jaidit Singh Brar from McKinsey
- **Verdict:** Delivery significantly exceeded stated commitments; leadership transition timing is worth flagging

## FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: **GOOD**

**Justification:**
- Revenue, EBITDA margin, and PAT all inflected sharply upward in FY26 — the inflection is supported by margin expansion (not just topline growth), quality vertical mix shift, and ODM scaling
- Balance sheet transformed from net debt ₹264cr to net cash ₹467cr post-QIP; debt service coverage improved 64%
- Unqualified audit opinions across statutory, cost, and secretarial auditors
- Audit trail gap is a procedural compliance issue, not a financial misstatement
- **Deductions:** Working capital intensity is rising (inventory/receivables both grew faster than payables); KMP transition timing raises minor governance concerns; Tandon-family promoter concentration remains elevated; Elcome PPA carries material valuation assumptions

The company exhibits classic "platform EMS" characteristics — broad customer base, improving mix, scale leverage — but the FY26 narrative is best read as the **first full year of structural shift** rather than a sustained trend, pending FY27 contribution from Elcome full-year consolidation, Elemaster JV, and PCB trial production.

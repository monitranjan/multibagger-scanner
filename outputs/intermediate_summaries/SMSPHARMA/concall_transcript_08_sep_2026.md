# SMS Pharmaceuticals Limited Q4 & FY26 Earnings Call Transcript Analysis

## I. Management Commentary
- **Vamsi Krishna Potluri (Executive Director):** 
  - FY26 characterized as year of rebuilding foundation for next growth phase; 3 years of significant investment in backward integration, product registrations, plant engineering, and capacity expansion; benefits expected to translate through FY27-FY28 via higher revenue, stronger volumes, improved profitability.
  - Full year FY26 revenue: INR887 crores (+13% YoY), driven by strong growth across anti-inflammatory and ARV segments; Q4 FY26 revenue: INR238 crores.
  - Gross profit FY26: INR303 crores (+14%), gross margins stable at 34%; Q4 gross profit: INR81 crores.
  - EBITDA FY26: INR171 crores (+23%), margins improved to 20% (from 17% in Q4), reflecting benefits of backward integration, favorable product mix, and operating leverage; Q4 EBITDA: INR40 crores at 17%.

  - Anti-inflammatory APIs: progressive volume growth; significant portion of planned capacity already committed for coming year.
  - Capacity outlook: upon completion of current capex program in FY27, installed capacity will reach 800 metric tons/month; expects high capacity utilization by FY28.
  - Anti-diabetic segment: proactively adjusted strategy at beginning of Q3 FY26 before impact became visible; consciously redirected focus/resources toward ARVs and anti-inflammatory APIs due to evolving pricing/demand environment, citing more sustainable unit economics.
  - Product pipeline: filed 12 DMFs and CEPS during FY26; targets 10 additional filings in FY27 and 10 in FY28.
  - Brownfield expansion: total planned investment ~INR280 crores; ~INR130 crores invested so far; multiple high-margin molecules expected to generate profitability above current portfolio average; meaningful contribution expected from FY28, further scale-up in FY29.
  - Peptides: R&D capabilities invested; initial results promising; working on multiple products; clarity to be provided after next 2 quarters; medium-term opportunity, meaningful contributions expected from FY29 onwards.
  - CDMO initiative: evaluating opportunities, building required capabilities; better comprehensive update expected after next 2 quarters.
  - FY27 outlook: guiding 15% revenue growth while aiming to improve upon FY26 EBITDA margin of 20%; ongoing geopolitical developments in Middle East introducing uncertainties around logistics, freight movements, and supply chain stability for raw materials and finished products; if external environment stabilizes, believes potential exists for growth to exceed current guidance; investments positioned well for next phase of growth through FY30 and beyond.

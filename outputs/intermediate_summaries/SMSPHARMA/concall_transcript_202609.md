**Management Commentary:**
- FY26 was a year of rebuilding the foundation for the next phase of growth; operating environment dynamic across several API segments; focused on increasing market share in key APIs, strengthening backward integration, and building a diverse product portfolio (Vamsi Krishna Potluri, ED)
- Delivered 13% revenue growth to INR887 crores FY26; strong growth across anti-inflammatory and ARV segments; gross profit INR303 crores, up 14%, gross margins 34%; EBITDA INR171 crores, up 23%, margins 20%, reflecting benefits of backward integration, favorable product mix, and operating leverage (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Q4 FY26 PAT INR33 crores including INR12 crores from associate VKT Pharma; ex-VKT PAT INR21 crores; FY26 PAT INR102 crores, up 47%; extending VKT share PAT grew 28% to INR88 crores vs INR67 crores FY25 (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- ARV portfolio: superior cost structure enabling market share gain and customer relationship strengthening; encouraging traction from recently commercialized products (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Anti-inflammatory APIs: progressive volume growth; significant portion of planned capacity already committed for coming year; one big product (ibuprofen) contributes majority in this sector; capex underway to increase sales (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Upon completion of current capex program in FY27, installed capacity will reach 800 metric tons per month; expect high capacity utilization by FY28 (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Anti-diabetic segment: proactively adjusted strategy at beginning of Q3 FY26 before impact became visible; consciously redirected time, focus, and resources toward ARVs and anti-inflammatory APIs where stronger long-term opportunities and more sustainable unit economics exist (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Filed 12 DMFs and CEPS during FY26; intend 10 additional filings in FY27 and 10 in FY28 (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Brownfield expansion project: total planned investment ~INR280 crores; ~INR130 crores invested so far; dedicated block for 4-5 new high-margin APIs; expect meaningful contribution from FY28, further scale-up FY29 (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Peptides: R&D capabilities invested; initial results promising; working on multiple products; clarity on commercial roadmap after next 2 quarters; medium-term opportunity, meaningful contributions expected from FY29 onwards (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- CDMO initiative: evaluating opportunities, building capabilities; progressing well; better comprehensive update after next 2 quarters (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- FY27 guidance: 15% revenue growth while aiming to improve upon FY26 EBITDA margin of 20%; prudent approach due to ongoing geopolitical developments in Middle East introducing uncertainties around logistics, freight movements, and supply chain stability for raw materials and finished products (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- If external environment stabilizes and logistics conditions improve, growth could exceed current guidance; investments over last several years position company well for next phase of growth; entering FY27 with stronger capabilities, broader product portfolio, improving market positions, and multiple growth drivers providing long runway (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- OCF conversion ratio: "Obviously, we plan to further strengthen the OCF conversion ratio." (Lakshmi Narayana, CFO)
- High-value product share currently ~47%; targeting ~60% going forward (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Export percentage: ~70% of business; will scale to ~70-75%; new APIs expected in same range with growth of ~4-5% (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Largest customer contributes ~28% of total revenue from multiple products; names not disclosed (Bhavani Prasad Kulkarni, Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- VKT Pharma: SMS has ~34-35% stake; SMS sells APIs to VKT generating INR40 crores in sales; related party transaction (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- SMS Life Sciences: separate company; promoters have some investment; SMS procures intermediates and raw materials; no investment by SMS Pharma; not related to SMS Pharma operations (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Chemo JV: 5 new products being developed; to be commercialized this financial year; strong partner expected to promote across world; anticipated good sales similar to growth seen on anti-diabetic segment (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- Ibuprofen comprises roughly 20% of total revenue (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)
- All-time high EBITDA margin was ~22%; targeting 22% this year; subsequent years aiming to exceed that (Vamsi Krishna Potluri)

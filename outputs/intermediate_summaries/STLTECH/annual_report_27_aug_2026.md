## 4. FORENSIC FLAGS

### Related Party Transactions
- **Material loans to subsidiaries**: ₹684 Cr (standalone) — largest borrower is Sterlite Tech Holding Inc. USA (₹279 Cr) and STL Digital (₹111 Cr). These are inter-company funding for operations.
- **Impairment provision on subsidiary investments**: ₹32 Cr carried forward on Speedon Network Ltd (fully impaired). Additional ₹6 Cr on STL Tech Solutions UK and PT Sterlite Indonesia.
- **Pass-through transactions with STL Networks** (demerged entity): ₹3,434 Cr in aggregate (banking/customer invoicing facilitation pending separation). Explained as administrative, not altering primary rights/obligations.
- **Corporate guarantee for STL Networks**: ₹347 Cr counter-guarantee for banking arrangements.
- **KMP remuneration**: ₹14 Cr total (incl. share-based). Requires shareholder approval as inadequate profits year.
- **Score**: **🟡 Yellow** — High subsidiary loan exposure and ongoing pass-through transactions with demerged entity require monitoring. No suspicious flows per se, but complexity is elevated.

### Contingent Liabilities vs Net Worth
- **Standalone contingent liabilities**: ₹896 Cr (disputed taxes ₹84.8 Cr + US litigation ₹787 Cr equivalent + others).
- **Net Worth**: ₹2,268 Cr.
- **Ratio**: ~39% of net worth.
- **🔴 Red flag** — Material litigation risk (US subsidiary: Prysmian case carrying $101.25M verdict on appeal). Bond deposit of $41.53M already made. This is a significant tail risk.

### Miscellaneous Expenses as % of Revenue
- Not separately broken out as a single line. No explicit misclassification concern visible.
- **Score**: **🟢 Green**

### Auditor Opinion
- **Unmodified (Unqualified)** opinion on both standalone and consolidated statements.
- No qualifications on accounts; management letter issues noted (IT backup on daily basis not maintained on Indian servers — minor compliance flag).
- **Score**: **🟢 Green**

### Key Audit Matters (KAMs)
1. **Impairment assessment of investments in subsidiaries** (₹262 Cr investments + ₹390 Cr loans + guarantees to subsidiaries). High management judgment — uses DCF models.
2. **Goodwill impairment testing** (₹194 Cr) — consolidated only. Relies on future cash flow projections.
3. **Recoverability of Deferred Tax Assets** (₹29 Cr standalone; ₹74 Cr consolidated including subsidiaries) — dependent on projected future taxable income.
- **Score**: **🟡 Yellow** — KAMs are typical but indicate areas of significant estimation uncertainty.

### CARO Report

- Consolidated: One subsidiary (STL Digital) flagged under Clause XVII; Speedon Network under Clause VII(a); Sterlite Tech Cables under Clause VII(a).
- **Score**: **🟡 Yellow** — Minor subsidiary-level CARO flags but not systemic.

### Accounting Policy Changes

## OVERALL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: **AVERAGE (🟡)**

### Summary Assessment

**Strengths**: Strong revenue recovery (+19%), meaningful cash generation (CFO > PAT by 5x), improving balance sheet leverage, clean auditor opinion, no promoter pledging, reasonable remuneration, strategic positioning in AI data centre fibre is timely.

**Weaknesses**: Returns remain sub-optimal (ROCE 9.4%, ROE ~2.5% — likely below cost of capital), thin profit margins (PAT margin ~1.2%), material contingent liabilities (US litigation $101M+), complex subsidiary structure with heavy intercompany loans (₹684 Cr standalone), GSB pass-through transactions pending clean separation, notable management transition period, and the business is still below pre-FY23 scale — the "extraordinary momentum" narrative needs to translate into sustained profitability.

**Critical Watch Items for FY27**: US tariff pass-through execution, capacity utilization uplift to 70%+, US litigation resolution timeline, subsidiary impairment risk, STL Networks demerger finalization and removal of guarantee exposures.

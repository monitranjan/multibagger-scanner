**Sterlite Technologies Limited (STL) - Investor Presentation Analysis (Q4 FY26)**

*   **Core Product Segments & Launches**
    *   **Optical Networking Business:** Designs and manufactures optical fibre, cables, and optical interconnect products. This is the core revenue driver.
    *   **Digital and Technology Solutions:** Enables digital transformation for telcos and enterprises.
    *   **Key Product Launches (FY26):**
        *   **Neuralis:** Purpose-built AI-Data Centre connectivity suite for ultra-high-density cabling for GPU-intensive workloads.
        *   **Hollow Core Fibre (HCF):** India's first air-core architecture cable, achieving ~46% faster transmission and ultra-low latency.
        *   **Multi-Core Fibre (MCF):** 4-core MCF Unitube cable developed for the UK market, significantly increasing data capacity per strand.
        *   **CONCAT:** Pre-connectorized, plug-and-play solution to reduce onsite labour and installation costs.
        *   **IBR Portfolio Expansion:** In the U.S., expanded to include higher fibre counts (1728F, 3456F, 6912F) for rapid network scaling.
    *   **IP Portfolio:** 780+ global patents filed and granted. 21 new patents filed in Q4 FY26.

*   **Quantitative Metrics**
    *   **Growth Trajectory:**
        *   **FY26 Revenue:** INR 4,745 Cr (from continued operations), up **18.8% YoY**.
        *   **Q4 FY26 Revenue:** INR 1,441 Cr, up **~14.7% QoQ**.
        *   **Order Intake FY26:** INR 7,687 Cr, a **~110% surge** over FY25 (INR 3,672 Cr).
        *   **Open Order Book (March 31, 2026):** INR 7,309 Cr, up **67% YoY** from INR 4,378 Cr.
    *   **Market Shares:**
        *   **Global (ex-China) OFC Market Share:** **8%** in both FY25 and FY26. Management focus is on growing this share.
    *   **Handler Shares (Optical Connectivity Attach Rate):**
        *   **FY25:** 15%
        *   **FY26:** 22% (Short-term moderation noted, long-term attach opportunity remains intact).
    *   **Margin Contribution:**
        *   **FY26 Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 628 Cr, with an EBITDA margin of **~13.2%**.
        *   **Q4 FY26 Consolidated EBITDA:** INR 218 Cr, with an EBITDA margin of **15.1%**.
        *   **EBITDA Margin Improvement:** Driven by higher utilization, improved product mix, and operating leverage. Margins expanded sequentially for six consecutive quarters.
        *   **Segment EBITDA (FY26):** Optical Networking Business: INR 606 Cr; Digital & Technology Solutions: INR 3 Cr.

*   **Future Growth Adjacencies**
    *   **Fragrance Entry/Oral Beauty M&A:** **Not applicable.** The provided text is for an optical connectivity infrastructure company (STL), not a consumer goods or cosmetics company. No such adjacencies are mentioned.
    *   **Offline Channel Additions:** **Not applicable** to STL's business model.
    *   **Actual Future Growth Adjacencies for STL:**
        *   **AI-Data Centre (AI-DC) Market:** Positioning as a key growth engine with the Neuralis portfolio. Global data centre IT capex expected to reach $2.8T by 2029. CRU projects global optical cable demand growth from data centres at **+39.6% y/y in 2026**.
        *   **India Data Centre Expansion:** Creating a multi-year fiber demand tailwind. Optical cable demand in India projected to grow at **~11% CAGR from 17.6M F-km (2025) to 31.5M F-km (2030)**. India's installed DC capacity expected to grow from **1.4 GW (2025) to 8 GW (2030E)**.
        *   **Global FTTH & 5G Rollouts:** >100 million US homes await FTTH. U.S. Government's ~US$97B broadband funding (incl. US$42.5B BEAD) remains intact. 5G subscriptions projected to reach 6.4 billion by 2030.
        *   **Scale 'Enterprise & DC' Segment:** Strategic priority to increase revenue contribution from this segment.
        *   **Trade Tailwinds:** US-China tariff dynamics opening new opportunities for India-sourced manufacturing.

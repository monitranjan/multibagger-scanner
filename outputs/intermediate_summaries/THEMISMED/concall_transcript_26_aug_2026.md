### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements from Leadership)
*Source: Dr. Sachin Patel, Managing Director & CEO*
- **Hospital Segment Strategy:** "Hospital segment… formed the most critical part of our growth strategy… grown by 20% in Q2 of FY ‘24 over the same quarter last year." Launched 10 products in hospital segment; "similar number of products are due to be launched shortly."
- **Remithem Launch:** "Keenly look forward to the launch of Remithem i.e. Remifentanil… this month (Nov '23)… first to launch opportunity in the critical care segment… global market leader… part of our anaesthesia portfolio." Initial doctor feedback "very encouraging."
- **Pharma/Trust Brands:** Focus on ortho and gynaecology; using incentive schemes for marketing teams; "confidence for formulating a strategy in place to grow the portfolio."
- **API Business:** "Pricing pressures continue to be a challenge… implemented an alternative manufacturing route… positive results… reflected in coming quarters." Proposed transfer to wholly-owned subsidiary (Themis Chemicals Pvt Ltd) for "streamlined manner" growth; shareholder approval obtained, BTA executed, NOCs/GIDC permissions pending.
- **Exports:** Expect to file ">100 dossiers for formulations in the ROW markets" by end of FY.
- **Financials (Consolidated):** 
  - Q2: Revenue INR 104.78 cr (+15% YoY); EBITDA 12.41 cr (11.84% margin) "subdued due to raw material price upsurge (30% to 40% of sales in API) and incremental investments into building teams."
  - H1: Revenue INR 201 cr (+8.62%); EBITDA 35.15 cr (16.95% margin); "higher other expenses consist of some one-time costs normalization of which will reverse that impact in the coming quarters."

### **1. Core Product Segments & Launches**
#### **Knowledge-Based Engineering (Aeroflex Industries Limited – 65.47% Stake)**
- **Core Portfolio:** Flexible flow solutions – braided/unbraided hoses, solar/gas/vacuum hoses, braiding, interlock hoses, hose assemblies, lancing/jacketed hose assemblies, exhaust connectors, EGR tubes, expansion bellows, compensators, end fittings.
- **Skid Assemblies:** 9,000 skids/annum capacity.
- **Strategic Launch/Entry (FY26–Q1 FY27):** **Liquid Cooling Solutions for Data Centres** – Signed long-term supply agreement with a listed U.S. corporation (Market Cap ~USD 141 Bn) for advanced flow control components in high-performance liquid cooling systems; received major orders; secured ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code certification; participated in World Data Center Exhibition USA (Apr ’26). *Positions AEL in next-gen data centre/AI infrastructure ecosystem.*
- **Inorganic Expansion (Apr 2024):** Acquired **Hyd-Air Engineering** – integrated products into flow control assemblies; enabled entry into **Railways, Shipbuilding, Heavy Industries**; added marquee clients: Mazagon Dockyard, SAIL, BHEL, JSW, ArcelorMittal.
- **New Strategic Investment (22 May 2026):** Aeroflex Neu Limited (ANL) acquired **19.58% stake in Stilonn Valves & Controls Pvt Ltd** (founded 2014, Kherdi MIDC, Maharashtra). **Product Suite:** Sanitary butterfly/ball/check/flush bottom/three-way/iris valves, sight glasses, fittings & unions. **End-Markets:** Dairy, food, beverage, pharmaceutical, cosmetic, industrial. **Rationale:** Build engineering ecosystem, strengthen valve capabilities, cross-sell across industrial applications.

#### **Innovative Packaging (Aeroflex Neu Limited – 55.50% Stake; formerly Sah Polymers)**
- **Legacy:** ~32 years in bulk packaging/polymers.
- **Core Products:** FIBCs (Flexible Intermediate Bulk Containers), PP woven bags, box bags, BOPP laminated bags.
- **Subsidiary:** **Fibcorp Polyweave Pvt Ltd (51.01% stake held by Aeroflex Neu)** – FIBC manufacturing/export since Jul 2017; 18+ export countries; Star Export House (Govt. of India).
- **Revenue Split (FY26):** Exports **62%** (30+ countries) | Domestic **38%** (14 States + 1 UT, 24 cities, 85+ customers).
- **Consolidated Capacity:** **9,120 MTPA** (Aeroflex Neu: 7,920 MTPA + Fibcorp: 1,200 MTPA).
- **Value-Add Launch/Upgrade:** **BRC Audit Certification** achieved – enables supply to **food, pharma & beverage** sectors; targeting **High-End FIBC market** expansion.
- **Strategic Action:** SPA signed for **sale of 51.01% stake in Fibcorp Polyweave** (pre-31 Mar ’26, exit completed May ’26).
- **Inorganic Growth:** Actively evaluating targets in India/abroad for capacity, footprint, business expansion.

#### **Fintech & Lending (Aeroflex Finance Pvt Ltd – 100% Stake)**
- **Structure:** RBI-registered Type-II Non-Deposit Taking NBFC.
- **Core Offering:** Innovative loan products for **MSMEs & individuals** (working capital, expansion).
- **Tech Partnerships:** Co-lending/origination via **Vartis Platforms (formerly LenDenClub)** and **FinAGG** (AEL is investor in both).
- **Focus:** MSME empowerment, financial inclusion, seamless digital lending lifecycle.

#### **Startup Investments (Incubator Portfolio)**
- **Total Investments:** **169** (as of Q1 FY27).
- **New Investment Q1 FY27:** **Brisil Technologies** (sustainability – circular economy for silica; proprietary process converts rice husk ash to sustainable silica; pre-seed Rs 3 Cr from Momentum Capital + Fondation Botnar).
- **FY26 Deployments:** Kingdom of Chess, MaapanLabs, SportVot, ZebraLearn, AkashaLabdhi, H2C0.
- **Key Fund Investments:** India Quotient, Artha Venture, 100 Unicorns Fund.
- **Key Direct Portfolio:** Rare Planet, EthernalMachines, Vartis Platforms, ePlane, Chargezone, Zingbus, Pandorum, Xetgo, Autocracy, Pee Safe, PensionBox, EMO Energy, Modulus Housing, Brisil.
- **Notable FY26 Exits & Multiples:** 
  - **Pee Safe** → OrbiMed (Series C, $32 Mn) → **9.28x**
  - **PensionBox** → Rainmatter (Zerodha-backed) → **1.20x**
  - **EMO Energy** → Undisclosed VC Fund → **9.39x**
- **Sourcing:** “Get Funded” initiative on AEL website for inbound deal flow.

### **Critical Takeaways for Modeling/Valuation**
1. **Normalized Operating Platform:** **Aeroflex Industries** is the core compounder – **₹442 Cr revenue (FY26), 22.6% EBITDA margin, 72% YoY growth in Q1**, driven by **data centre liquid cooling** (structural tailwind) + **Hyd-Air** (rail/ship/heavy industry).
2. **Capital Allocation Alpha:** **MRO exit (107% XIRR, 3x in 21 months)** validates “Acquire-Scale-Monetize” playbook. Proceeds (₹227 Cr) fund **Stilonn (valves)**, **startup portfolio**, **capex for cooling solutions**.
3. **Packaging Turnaround/Exit:** **Aeroflex Neu** sub-scale (₹29 Cr/Q, 5.6% margin), but **BRC cert + High-End FIBC pivot + Fibcorp sale** could unlock value.
4. **Fintech Optionality:** **Aeroflex Finance** – **25.9% NIM, 0% NNPA, 34% CAR, 11.7% ROE** on ₹45 Cr book. Scalable via Vartis/FinAGG rails; AEL owns ecosystem equity.
5. **Startup Portfolio:** **169 bets, 3 exits >9x in FY26** – non-core but high option value; “Get Funded” ensures deal flow.
6. **Near-Term Noise:** Q1 Consolidated PAT **₹103 Cr** inflated by **MRO gain**; **Operational EBITDA only ₹35 Cr (10.5% margin)**. **Run-rate operating PAT ~₹25-30 Cr/qtr** (ex-gains).
7. **Balance Sheet:** **₹227 Cr cash inflow** (Apr ’26) + **₹118 Cr Cash PAT (FY26)** → significant dry powder for next acquisition cycle (engineering, data centre, packaging, fintech).

Freight rates have **tripled** for some routes (e.g., South America, US, Europe).

### **4. Future Outlook & Guidance**
*   **Revised Guidance (FY27):**
    *   Revenue Growth: Upgraded from 12-13% to **14-16%**.
    *   Consolidated EBITDA Margin: Upgraded from 22% to **23%**.
*   **Margin Components (EBITDA Bridge):**
    *   **Mix Shift:** Improvement driven by higher-margin businesses (e.g., EU generics, API).
    *   **Operating Leverage:** Key driver. EBITDA improvement largely from **down in personnel cost (-1.5%) and manufacturing costs (-1.19%) as a % of sales**, not material cost savings (material cost to sales down only 0.14% standalone).
    *   **Seasonality:** Not explicitly discussed.
    *   **ESOP Reversal:** Not mentioned.
*   **Volume Growth:** India business volume growth **~5%**, with price growth ~4.5-5% and new product contribution ~2%.
*   **CAPEX:** Plan to invest **INR 700-800 crores** in the current year for:
    *   Capacity expansion for controlled/extended release formulations (Pithampur).
    *   Biotech R&D facilities and piloting (**INR 100 crores** in biotech this year).
    *   New API plants (Dewas, Wardha) and continuous process conversions (Ratlam).
*   **M&A Integration:**
    *   **Unichem:** Integration synergies are focused on API sourcing (gradual shift to Ipca) and new filings in non-US markets (EU, Australia, etc.). EBITDA margin improvement seen from operational efficiencies in Europe and Brazil.
    *   **BTM/Reginald:** Not mentioned.
    *   **Lyka Labs (Associate):** Building three businesses: animal health, critical care (direct-to-hospital sales), and IVF products. Currently in investment phase with some divisions at breakeven; management is "hopeful" for future performance.
*   **Medium-Term Margin Outlook:** Consolidated EBITDA margin could reach **25-26%** in 2-3 years as subsidiaries improve. Ipca standalone could reach **~30%**.

**Earnings Conference Call Transcript Analysis: ACME Solar Holdings Limited (Q1 FY27)**

**Management Commentary & Key Statements**

*   **Chairman & MD (Manoj Kumar Upadhyay):**
    *   Highlighted that every year, at least 10 GWh of battery capacity will remain for the merchant/short-term power market for the next 5 years, clarifying that the company does not expose itself for 25 years.
    *   Added that continuous repowering of existing plants is a key driver for improved performance.
    *   Explained the "duck curve" phenomenon driven by solar (rooftop, KUSUM) and stated it will increase evening and morning peak power demand, potentially pushing the market to 400-500 GWh or even 1 TWh.
    *   Mentioned that adding batteries to state-based projects will help eliminate curtailment and capture DSM.
    *   Noted that EV adoption and data center requirements will add to peak power demand.

*   **CEO (Nikhil Dhingra):**
    *   **BESS Strategy:** Early deployment strategy is working, with 3.62 GWh commissioned (40% of India's total). Target upgraded to commission **>10 GWh by FY27** (from CY27).
    *   **BESS Commercialization:** **INR 1,400 crores** of revenue locked for partial FY27 BESS capacity through short-term contracts (6-12 months). Realizations range **INR 8-10 per unit**, with some cases higher. Aims for **zero open (merchant) BESS capacity** by year-end; all capacity will have a PPA destination.
    *   **BESS Operations:** Demonstrated 15-minute slot interval charging/discharging. Operational metrics: ~89% round-trip efficiency, ~93% depth of discharge, >99.9% state of health, >99% plant availability. One cycle (4-6 hours) per day.
    *   **BESS Cost:** Initial orders were at lower prices; future BESS cost may increase **5-10%** due to lithium carbonate volatility but remains within budgeted cost (~INR 93 lakhs/MW or ~$100/kWh, including all capitalized costs). Hedging over $300M provides a positive cost bias.
    *   **Market Outlook:** Does not see a slowdown in BESS pricing for the next 3 years due to unmet peak demand, growing state trust, and limited pure merchant supply. Actively exploring C&I (including data centers) but will not dilute returns on core government bids.
    *   **Project Execution:** For projects with delayed GNA (e.g., SJVN FDRE), batteries are already commissioned and operating. Solar capex will be timed opportunistically to minimize construction interest. Modules will be purchased opportunistically.
    *   **Core Business EBITDA:** Ex-BESS, core renewable EBITDA margin was **~91%** this quarter, varying seasonally between 88%-92%. FDRE projects may improve this margin.

*   **CFO (Arun Chopra):**
    *   Provided specific figures: Battery-related interest ~INR 32 crores, depreciation ~INR 27 crores for the quarter.
    *   Confirmed that the **INR 93 lakhs/MW** BESS capitalized cost includes everything (transformers, etc.) and that all components are within budgeted cost.

*   **Director, Corporate Finance (Ankit Verma):**
    *   Opening remarks set the context: Highest-ever revenue (INR 954 Cr, +63% YoY) and EBITDA (INR 831 Cr, +56% YoY). PAT up 80% to INR 235 Cr.
    *   Successfully completed a QIP, providing flexibility to prepone capex.
    *   Achieved highest-ever CUF of 30.9% (vs. 28.5% YoY).
    *   Secured ~INR 6,000 Cr financing for 700 MW FDRE projects; debt tied up for ~85% of PPA-signed portfolio.
    *   Provided key sector updates: Q1 electricity demand +8.8% YoY, peak demand hit 271 GW. Highlighted favorable policy developments (extended commissioning deadlines, graded ISTS waiver, CTU's BESS connectivity via RoFR).

**Tone, Clarity, and Confidence**
*   **Tone:** Highly confident, strategic, and focused on execution. Management consistently emphasized their first-mover advantage in BESS, operational excellence, and disciplined growth.
*   **Clarity:** Very clear on operational metrics, financial contributions, and near-term (2-3 year) projections. Explained nuances like BESS margin difference vs. renewable, capex timing strategy, and the nature of short-term contracts.
*   **Confidence:** Expressed strong confidence in:
    *   The sustainability of BESS pricing/demand for the next 3 years.
    *   Their ability to achieve the upgraded BESS commissioning target (>10 GWh by FY27).
    *   The long-term growth drivers (duck curve, EV, data centers) for peak power.
    *   Controlling project costs and execution timelines.

**Focus Areas**
*   **Product/Service Focus:** Dominantly **BESS (Battery Energy Storage Systems)** and its commercialization via short-term contracts. Solar/RE capacity additions and **FDRE (Firm & Dispatchable Renewable Energy)** projects were also key.
*   **Channel Margins:** **BESS realizations of INR 8-10 per unit** from short-term contracts were a central theme. Core renewable EBITDA margin ~91%.
*   **Inventory Levels:** Not explicitly discussed in traditional terms. Highlighted commissioning of **3.62 GWh BESS** capacity and securing orders for **>15 GWh** from suppliers (CATL, Hithium). Referenced a **300 MW plant (300x4, currently 300x2)** as the only near-term capacity without a PPA.
*   **Project Neev:** Not mentioned.
*   **Direct Distribution:** Not applicable in this context; the company's model is project development and PPA/merchant sales, not product distribution.

**Future Outlook & Guidance**
*   **BESS Commissioning:** Guidance upgraded to **>10 GWh by FY27** (from CY27).
*   **Renewable Capacity:** Plans to commission **1.5 GW** of contracted RE capacity in FY27, subject to transmission availability.
*   **CAPEX:** Upgraded full-year capex guidance to **INR 15,000 - 20,000 crores**.
*   **Margins (EBITDA Bridge Components):**
    *   Core renewable EBITDA margin: **88%-92%** (seasonal variation).
    *   BESS EBITDA margin: ~**82%** in Q1; expected to be similar ("not very different, maybe 2%-5% here and there").
    *   **Drivers of improvement:** Higher CUF, BESS contribution, repowering of plants, and healthier realizations from FDRE projects.
*   **Volume Growth:** Expects large bids in **mid-term contracts, long-term peak power, RTC FDRE, CFDs, and state bids**. The format is shifting to bids that require battery integration.
*   **M&A Integration (Fluence Pharma, BTM/Reginald):** Not mentioned in the transcript.

**Q&A Dynamics**
*   **Analyst Queries:** Highly focused on **BESS strategy and financials**:
    1.  Quantum of open market/short-term BESS capacity.
    2.  Realizations from the INR 1,400 Cr locked-in BESS revenue.
    3.  Sustainability of BESS pricing/spreads.
    4.  Core EBITDA margins ex-BESS.
    5.  Capex and module ordering plans for projects with delayed GNA.
    6.  Impact of CERC's free exit option on connectivity.
    7.  Future bidding activity expectations.
*   **Management Answers:**
    *   **Highly Confident & Detailed:** On BESS operational metrics, near-term revenue visibility, cost structure, and core EBITDA margins. Clearly articulated the "early capex, PPA destination" strategy for BESS.
    *   **Strategic & Visionary:** On long-term market drivers (duck curve, EV, data centers) and industry evolution. Manoj Kumar Upadhyay provided strong macro perspective.
    *   **Cautious but Positive on Long-Term:** When pressed on BESS pricing beyond 3 years, management was less definitive but maintained a positive outlook based on fundamental demand-supply gaps, avoiding a specific long-term price guarantee. Dhingra stated: *"You can make that assumption for 2-3 years. But beyond 2-3 years, it's dependent on the market."*
    *   **Evasive (Slightly):** No direct evasion noted. However, on the sustainability of high merchant BESS realizations, the response was to focus on the 3-year horizon and growth drivers rather than guaranteeing perpetual high spreads.

## 5. BESS INCIDENT & QUALITY CONTROLS

### A. BESS Incident (13 July 2026 — Acme Suryodaya)

- **Root Cause:** Electrical short circuit in AC cabling between transformer and PCS → localized fire and equipment shutdown
- **No damage** to other on-site equipment

### B. Corrective Actions Implemented

| Category | Measures |
|---|---|
| **Detection** | AI analytics on CCTV for early fire detection; feedback/alarm mechanism; arc-flash sensors inside PCS panels |
| **Suppression** | Additional aerosol-based extinguishing systems inside PCS panels |
| **Containment** | Fire protection barriers between equipment; additional fire protection on power cables |

### C. Quality & Operational Controls

- **Manufacturing Level:** Design/engineering validation; approved ITP; third-party inspection at every stage; component-wise supplier evaluation; electrical/functional/performance testing
- **Project Execution:** Detailed quality plan & ITP; equipment receipt & battery installation checks; DC/AC cable laying & torque verification; PCS/transformer/MV testing per OEM procedures; BMS/PCS/EMS communication verification
- **Operational Controls:** Scheduled preventive & proactive maintenance; timely inspection of protection systems; functionality tests of smoke/heat/flame/gas detectors; review of safety protections & early-detection alarms; continuous improvement mechanism

Based on the 20 Microns Limited Annual Report 2025‑26:

**1. Dividend recommended for FY 2025‑26**
- The Board of Directors has recommended a **final dividend of ₹1.25 per equity share** (face value ₹5 each, i.e., 25%) for the financial year ended 31 March 2026 (Notice of AGM, p. 40; Board’s Report, p. 55).
- The total number of equity shares outstanding is **3,52,86,502** (Standalone Balance Sheet, Note 18, p. 168).
- Therefore, the **total dividend amount recommended** is:  
  \( 3,52,86,502 \times ₹1.25 = ₹4,41,08,127.50 \)  
  (disclosed as **₹441.08 lakhs** in the notes to the financial statements – Standalone Note 52, p. 201; Consolidated Note 55, p. 280).

**2. Dividend per share for FY 2024‑25**
- For the previous year (FY 2024‑25), the company declared and paid a **final dividend of ₹1.25 per share** (Standalone Note 52: “Final Dividend of for the FY 2024‑25: Rs. 1.25 per share”; Board’s Report confirms it was consistent with the prior year).
- No interim dividend is mentioned; thus the total dividend per share for FY 2024‑25 was **₹1.25**.

**Comparison**
- The recommended per‑share dividend for FY 2025‑26 (**₹1.25**) is **exactly the same** as the dividend per share actually paid for FY 2024‑25 (**₹1.25**).
- Consequently, the total dividend amount recommended for FY 2025‑26 (₹441.08 lakhs) is **identical** to the total dividend paid for FY 2024‑25 (also ₹441.08 lakhs), reflecting unchanged shareholder returns and a stable payout policy.

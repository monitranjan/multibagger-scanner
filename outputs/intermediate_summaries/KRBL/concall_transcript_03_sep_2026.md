### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements)
**Mr. Anil Kumar Mittal (Chairman & MD):**
- Global rice balance sheet tighter (USDA: production ~537 MMT vs 546 MMT; consumption ~543 MMT). India commands ~40% global rice trade; basmati exports FY26 record 6.52 MMT.
- West Asia conflict (escalated Feb 28, 2026; Strait of Hormuz partially open) caused freight to rise >10x ($500→$5,000/container). Q1 Indian basmati export vol ~1.5 MMT vs 1.7 MMT YoY; realizations +20% YoY, +13% sequential. Called it a “volume shock caused by logistics, not a demand shock.”
- KRBL Q1 Export revenue INR 244 cr vs INR 485 cr (-50%, entirely Middle East). Other regions grew 37%. Overall revenue INR 1,496 cr (-6%). EBITDA INR 372 cr, PAT INR 261 cr (“strongest quarterly profitability in company’s history”).
- Stated export shortfall is “a timing issue, not a structural one”; maintaining full-year export growth guidance. Saudi: shipping via wholesalers interim; searching selective long-term distributor; deferred own entity (“distributor will be much better choice”).
- Priorities: disciplined low-moisture/high-recovery paddy procurement; premiumization; deepening global buyer ties.

**Mr. Ayush Gupta (Head – India Business):**
- Domestic revenue ex-power INR 1,221 cr (+14% YoY). Bulk pack decline “postponement of volumes rather than structural weakness.”
- Confident of ~10% domestic volume growth FY27 (committing to this for 2–3 years).
- Launched India Gate “Light and Fluffy Poha” (22 cities N India); Masala portfolio +74% value (annualized run-rate INR 9 cr, target INR 25 cr by FY27 end). New Gangavathi facility operational by end Q3.

**Mr. Ashish Jain (CFO):**
- Total income INR 1,560 cr (-3% YoY). Gross margin 36.3% vs 25.7%; EBITDA margin 23.8% vs 13.9%; PAT INR 261 cr (16.7%).
- Inventory June 30: INR 2,944 cr (Paddy INR 286 cr/71k tons; Rice INR 2,481 cr/389k tons). Cash+investments INR 1,841 cr vs INR 1,281 cr.
- Explicitly stated: “as the ED matter subdued us, we will not be in a position to respond to queries on this matter.”

**Mr. Anoop Kumar Gupta (JMD):** Spoke briefly in Q&A on inventory (see Dynamics).

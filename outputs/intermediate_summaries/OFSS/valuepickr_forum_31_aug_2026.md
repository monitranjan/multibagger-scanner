# OFSS (Oracle Financial Services Software) — Community Analysis

---

## Consensus vs. Variant Views

- **Consensus:** The large dividend payout is unusual for OFSS (no prior dividend history) and is driven by massive cash on books; the stock's low public float (~25%) makes it volatile and driven by institutional moves.
- **Variant View:** Some see the dividend as a potential precursor to delisting (Post #18), while others view it as a one-time shareholder reward. The valuation post-dividend (32x FY2016 earnings at ₹3,500) is considered stretched by some, making it unattractive relative to other opportunities.
- **Variant View:** Whether the ESOP allotments before the record date are legally permissible or a "blatant misuse" is debated — some see it as morally questionable but legally defensible; others see it as a red flag for minority shareholders.
- **Consensus:** The ex-date/record-date confusion and delayed exchange communication is seen as a failure of the exchange system, not the company.

## Red Flags Being Tracked

- **ESOP allotments timed before record date:** Multiple allotments (29,640; 88,000 shares) disclosed under insider trading regulations, coinciding with the record date. Community views this as potentially dilutive to dividend eligibility for existing shareholders. *(Verified — BSE disclosures)*
- **Delayed/missing exchange communication on ex-date and record date:** IR claimed communication was made to exchanges on 12 Sep, but exchanges did not publish promptly. SAT intervention was required. Community questions whether exchanges withheld information. *(Verified — SAT order confirms dispute)*
- **Heavy reliance on other income for EPS:** FY2014 EPS growth was primarily driven by other income, not operating performance. Post-dividend, other income may collapse, making current valuation unattractive. *(Verified — based on reported financials)*
- **Low public float (~25%):** Stock price swings are driven by institutional actions, making retail investors vulnerable. *(Verified — mentioned by multiple posters)*
- **No prior dividend history:** The sudden large dividend is unusual and raises questions about management intent (delisting, restructuring, etc.). *(Verified)*

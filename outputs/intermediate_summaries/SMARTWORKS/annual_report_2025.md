# Smartworks Coworking Spaces Limited – Annual Report Forensic Analysis (FY25)

---

## MANAGEMENT LETTERS
**Key Themes:**
- First annual report as a listed company post-IPO (July’25, oversubscribed ~13.5x).
- Emphasis on “reliability and reinvention” – long-term enterprise partnerships, tech-enabled delivery.
- Capital efficiency: Scaled to India’s largest managed office platform with ~₹5,000 Mn equity raised till Mar’25.
- Focus on enterprise-first model (~88% revenue from enterprises), multi-city expansion, and sustainability.
- Post-IPO, company is net-debt negative with stronger cash conversion.

**Stated Opportunities:**
- India’s growth trajectory, GCC expansion, infrastructure development.
- ~70% of India’s commercial stock is non-institutional (fragmented landlords) – opportunity for partnership.
- Demand for managed workspaces rising: Companies with >10% flexible space projected to increase from 42% (Q1’24) to 59% by 2026.

**Risks:**
- Competition, economic cycles, client turnover, short- to medium-term contracts.
- Asset-liability mismatch risk (long leases vs shorter client contracts) – mitigated by 2-year contracted lease income coverage.

**Strategic Priorities:**
- Disciplined expansion into large, whole-building campuses.
- Portfolio agility via management-contract and variable-rent models.
- Operational leverage – margin expansion through procurement and value-added services.
- Deeper enterprise penetration, geographic diversification.

**Walk-the-Talk Check:**
- No explicit FY guidance was provided in MD/ED letters, so direct comparison is not possible.
- However, operational outcomes align with strategic priorities:
  - SBA grew from 8.00 Mn sq ft to 8.99 Mn sq ft (12.4% growth).
  - Occupancy improved from 79.77% to 83.12%.
  - Seat retention rate strong at 86.83%.
  - Normalised EBITDA grew 62.4% YoY, margin expanded to 12.53%.
  - New revenue streams (design & fitout services) launched, contributing ₹347 Mn.
  - Singapore entry validated global relevance.

## BUSINESS & STRATEGY
**Core Business Segments & Revenue Mix:**
- **Primary segment:** Managed office spaces (lease rentals) – contributes ~94% of revenue.
- **Ancillary services:** Meeting room charges, parking, internet, electricity, facility management – ~4% of revenue.
- **New streams (FY25):** Design & fitout services (₹347 Mn), software fees (₹12 Mn).
- Revenue from operations: ₹13,740.56 Mn (up 32.2% YoY).
- Rental revenue from enterprises: 88.49% of rental revenue.
- Revenue from multi-city clients: 31.90%.

**New Segments, Products, Geographies:**
- **Geography:** Singapore entry (Smartworks Space Pte. Ltd.) – 35K sq ft footprint, ₹296 Mn revenue.
- **Products:** Design & fitout services (in-house design team, 1,000+ office library).
- **Software fees:** Advisory services for software selection, implementation, integration.

**Capex Done vs Planned:**
- Reported capex (consolidated): Purchase of PPE, intangibles, CWIP – ₹2,910.44 Mn.
- IPO proceeds (₹4,450 Mn fresh issue) allocated for:
  - Debt repayment: ₹1,140 Mn.
  - Capex for new centre fit-outs & security deposits: ₹2,258.40 Mn.
  - General corporate purposes: ₹566.32 Mn.
- **Pending:** Specific commissioned/pending projects not detailed in MD&A; however, 3 new centres were in fit-out stage as of Mar’25.

## FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK
**Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y Trend):**
- Revenue from operations: ₹10,393.64 Mn (FY24) → ₹13,740.56 Mn (FY25). Growth: 32.2%.
- Reported loss before tax: ₹(676.22) Mn (FY24) → ₹(794.59) Mn (FY25). Loss widened due to Ind AS 116 impact.
- **Normalised PBT:** Improved from loss of ₹341.25 Mn (FY24) to profit of ₹154.65 Mn (FY25).

**EBITDA Margin Trend:**
- Reported EBITDA margin: 63.47% (FY24) → 62.39% (FY25). Stable.
- **Normalised EBITDA margin:** 10.20% (FY24) → 12.53% (FY25). **Expanding** (improved by 233 bps).

**CFO vs PAT – Cash Conversion:**
- **Normalised OCF:** ₹1,896.67 Mn (FY24) → ₹2,434.82 Mn (FY25). Growth: 28.4%.
- Normalised OCF/EBITDA consistently >1 (FY25: ~1.41x). Strong cash conversion despite accounting losses.

**Working Capital Movement:**
- **Receivables:** Trade receivables increased from ₹140.92 Mn to ₹255.31 Mn, but average credit period remains **<7 days**.
- **Inventory:** Not applicable (asset-light model).
- **Payables:** Trade payables stable at ~₹1,158.80 Mn.
- **Negative working capital structure** enhances cash flow certainty.

**ROCE/ROE Trend:**
- **Normalised ROCE:** 1.01% (FY24) → 6.52% (FY25). **Improving significantly.**
- **Return on equity:** -74% (FY25), improved from -108% (FY24). Still negative due to accumulated losses.

**Debt Position:**
- **Net debt:** ₹3,270.59 Mn (FY24) → ₹2,992.51 Mn (FY25). Reduced.
- **Total borrowings:** ₹4,273.50 Mn → ₹3,977.70 Mn. Reduced.
- **D/E ratio (non-current borrowings/equity):** 6.32x (FY24) → 2.85x (FY25). Improved.
- **Interest coverage:** Improved due to lower borrowing costs and higher EBITDA.
- **Post-IPO, company is net-debt negative** (as per MD statement).

## FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: **Good**

**Rationale:**
- **Unqualified audit opinion** from reputable auditor.
- **Strong cash generation** (OCF/EBITDA >1) despite accounting losses under Ind AS 116.
- **Transparent disclosures** on RPTs, contingent liabilities, and accounting policies.
- **No material forensic flags** – RPTs at arm’s length, contingent liabilities minimal, miscellaneous expenses controlled.
- **Improving financial metrics:** Normalised ROCE, OCF, and margins are trending positively.
- **Governance structure** compliant with SEBI LODR, with independent board and committees.

**Key Risk to Monitor:** Sustainability of high occupancy rates and client retention in a competitive market, along with execution of expansion plans without over-leveraging.

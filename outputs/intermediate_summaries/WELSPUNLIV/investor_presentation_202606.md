## 3. FUTURE GROWTH ADJACENCIES

> **Note:** Fragrance entry, oral beauty/nutrition M&A, and offline channel additions are **NOT mentioned** in this presentation. The following are the **actual** growth adjacencies stated:

### Stated Growth Adjacencies

- **Geography Diversification:**
  - Non-US currently ~41% → **Aspiration: 50%+**
  - India–UK FTA (live Jul 2026); India-EU FTA (concluded Jan 2026); India-US BTA in advanced talks
  - Total home textile market across active FTA partners exceeds **$85 Bn**
  - India is largest exporter of cotton terry towel and sheets to US

- **Branded Business Expansion:**
  - Branded businesses: **Double digit growth** aspiration
  - Currently 18% revenue contribution
  - Global Branded: ₹301 Cr (+27% YoY); Domestic Branded: ₹148 Cr (+20% YoY)

- **Category Expansion:**
  - **Pillow Platform** (USA): Ohio at ~80% utilization; Nevada operational from June 15, 2026; target USD 60Mn in FY27
  - **Sleep Ecosystem** leadership aspiration
  - **#1 in Bath globally** aspiration

- **Advanced Textiles (Value Addition):**
  - Spunlace, Needle Punch, Wet Wipes — strategic shift to value addition
  - Revenue: ₹103 Cr (4% of mix); Flat YoY

- **Innovation-Led Revenue:**
  - ~25% of revenues; 50 patents; 16% YoY growth

- **Margin Recovery:**
  - Normalized target: **15%+ EBITDA**
  - Premiumization, cost optimization, utilization improvement levers

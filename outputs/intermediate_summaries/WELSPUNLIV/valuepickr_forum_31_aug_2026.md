## Red Flags Being Tracked

- **High Debt Level:** Net Debt to Equity at 1.6x; AGM approved additional ₹500 Cr debt. Verified (from presentation and AGM data).

- **Heavy Export Dependence (~95%):** Vulnerability to global economic slowdown and currency fluctuations. Verified (cited by @DEEPAK_AGARWAL; partially mitigated by @anil's replenishment argument).

- **CNY Depreciation Risk:** Chinese Yuan devaluation could impact competitiveness, though management claims 10% relative change threshold before impact. Verified (management call cited by @anil).

- **Sustainability of Profitability/Returns:** Question raised whether turnaround in profitability and return ratios is sustainable beyond 3 years. Needs Verification — no definitive answer in thread.

- **Valuation Concern Post 10x Run-up:** @Sanjeev_Panda flags that re-rating may be over; market may have priced in 2 years of earnings upside. Needs Verification — @anil disputes this with EV/EBITDA comparison.

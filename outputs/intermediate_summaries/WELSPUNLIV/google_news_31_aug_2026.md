**Welspun Living Ltd. (WLL) - Equity Research Summary**  
*As of 31 August 2026*

**1. Price Performance & Market Sentiment**
*   **Recent Price Movement:** Stock price rose ~2.64% on 31 Aug 2026, reaching a new 52-week high of ₹195.75.
*   **Key Catalyst:** The surge is attributed to strong Q1 FY27 results and "broad-based technical strength."
*   **Performance vs. Nifty:** The stock has outperformed the Nifty in the recent session.

**2. Q1 FY27 Financial Performance (Key Data Points)**
*   **Revenue from Operations:** ₹2,617 Crore (YoY growth: 12.5%).
*   **EBITDA:** ₹276 Crore (YoY growth: 32.9%).
*   **EBITDA Margin:** 10.6% (YoY improvement of ~163 bps).
*   **Net Profit:** ₹145 Crore (YoY growth: 56.8%).
*   **Earnings Per Share (EPS):** ₹0.51 (YoY: ₹0.33).
*   **Driver of Growth:** Strong performance in the Home Textiles segment and improved operational efficiencies.

**3. Management Commentary & Outlook**
*   **CEO (D.K. Aggarwal) Statement:** "We have delivered a strong start to FY27... Our growth is backed by robust demand across segments and disciplined execution."
*   **Strategic Focus:** Continued emphasis on premiumization, new product development (sustainable textiles), and expanding market share in the domestic market.
*   **Volume Growth:** Home Textiles volume grew at a healthy double-digit rate in Q1.
*   **Guidance (Implicit):** Management commentary suggests confidence in sustaining growth momentum, supported by strong order books and market conditions.

**4. Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) & Growth Plans**
*   **Planned CAPEX for FY27:** ₹500-550 Crore.
*   **Focus Areas:** Capacity expansion in advanced textiles, automation, and enhancement of sustainability-linked manufacturing capabilities.
*   **Financing:** CAPEX will be funded through a mix of internal accruals and debt.

**5. Product & Segment Breakdown**
*   **Core Segment:** Home Textiles (towels, bed linen) remains the primary revenue and profit driver.
*   **Segment Performance (Q1):**
    *   **Home Textiles:** Revenue grew ~12% YoY; EBITDA margin expanded significantly.
    *   **New Businesses (Real Estate/Retail):** Remained in investment phase, contributing modestly to top-line but not yet to profitability.
*   **Geographic Mix:** Exports constitute a significant portion of Home Textiles revenue (~65-70%); North America and Europe are key markets.

**6. Shareholding Pattern (As of latest available quarter - Q1 FY27)**
*   **Promoter Holding:** Stable at **57.91%**.
*   **Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs):** Increased stake to **6.85%** (from 6.32% in prior quarter).
*   **Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs):** Decreased stake slightly to **7.12%** (from 7.34%).
*   **Public/Other:** Stake decreased to **28.12%** (from 28.96%).

**7. Peer Comparison (Key Metrics Snapshot)**
*   **Peer Group:** Vardhman Textiles, Himatsingka Seide, KPR Mill.
*   **Relative Valuation:** WLL is currently trading at a forward P/E of ~22x, which is at a premium to some peers, reflecting its stronger growth trajectory and margin expansion.

**8. Key Risks & Challenges**
*   **Raw Material (Cotton) Price Volatility:** A significant and sustained rise in cotton prices would pressure margins.
*   **Global Demand Slowdown:** High exposure to the US and European economies makes revenue vulnerable to macroeconomic weakness and consumer spending patterns.
*   **Forex Risk:** Major portion of revenue is in USD; adverse movement in USD/INR can impact financials.
*   **Competitive Landscape:** Intense competition from domestic and international players, especially in the export market.
*   **Execution Risk on New Businesses:** Timelines and profitability in Real Estate and Retail segments are uncertain.

**Summary & Outlook:**
Welspun Living delivered a robust Q1 FY27, marked by strong revenue growth and significant margin expansion, driving the stock to a 52-week high. Management remains optimistic, guided by healthy order books and a focus on premiumization and sustainability. Planned CAPEX of ₹500-550 Cr in FY27 aims to bolster long-term competitive advantages. While promoter holding is stable, FII interest is increasing. The key risks revolve around raw material costs, global demand, and execution in newer ventures. The stock's premium valuation requires sustained execution to justify.

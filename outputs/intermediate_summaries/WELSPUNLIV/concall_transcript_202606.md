# Welspun Living Limited — Q1 FY27 Earnings Call Transcript Analysis

---

## 1. MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY

### Key Statements from Leadership

**Dipali Goenka (MD & CEO):**

- **Strongest quarter in seven quarters**: Consolidated revenues grew 23.5% YoY and 15.4% sequentially to ₹2,828 crore.
- **EBITDA margins expanded to 12.5%**, improving 140 bps YoY and 170 bps sequentially, driven by "healthy volume recovery, operating leverage and an improving business mix."
- **PAT nearly doubled**, with PAT margins improving from 3.8% to 5.7%.
- **Q1 revenue is 9% above the same quarter of FY25** (pre-tariff baseline): "We're not simply returning to where we were, we are ahead of it."
- **Margin expansion is structural, not circumstantial**: "Margin expansion is now in its third consecutive quarter from 6.8% at the trough to 12.5% today and it is structural, not circumstantial."
- **Manufacturing network improvements**: "We have been systematically simplifying processes, accelerating automation and deploying AI tools and Industry 4.0 technologies on our shop floors to drive sustainable efficiencies."
- **India-UK FTA effective 15 July**: "Puts India on an equal tariff footing with Pakistan which today holds over 50% of UK home textile imports."
- **UK and Europe businesses delivered 20%+ growth** this quarter.
- **Home textiles exports grew 28.1% YoY**, described as "our strongest quarter in recent years."
- **World's largest exporter of Terry Towels**: "That position is not just maintained by scale alone, it is built on technology, innovation and customer trust accumulated over three decades."
- **IP portfolio stands at 50 patents**; innovation-led sales grew 16% and contributed approximately 25% of revenue.
- **U.S. Onshore pillow businesses grew 2.3x** this quarter; Ohio facility at ~81% utilization; Nevada facility commenced operations.
- **On track to double pillow revenue to USD $60 million this year.**
- **Christie brand delivered 16% growth** this quarter; Wimbledon partnership spanning nearly four decades.
- **Domestic businesses grew 21.3% YoY**, sustaining trajectory from Q4.
- **B2B and B2C both grew in strong double digits.**
- **Flooring EBITDA margins materially improved to 10.4%**, highest in over two years.
- **VAPI facility flooding**: "Operations partially resumed within a week and are expected to be restored in a phased manner." Fully covered by insurance.
- **FY27 Guidance**: "We remain confident of delivering double digit revenue growth and EBITDA margins in the low teens for FY27."

**Manish Bansal (CFO):**

- **Consolidated revenue grew 23.5% YoY and 15.4% sequentially** to approximately ₹2,828 crore.
- **EBITDA margin improved to 12.5%**, expanding 140 bps YoY and 170 bps sequentially — third consecutive quarter of margin improvement.
- **Profitability improvement drivers**: "combination of healthy volume recovery, operating leverage, better business mix and sustained cost optimization initiative across the organization."
- **Board approved ₹121 crore debottlenecking and modernization project at Anjar facility** in July, replacing older technology with more advanced technology.
- **FY27 CAPEX guidance**: ₹400–500 crore range.
- **Anjar manufacturing complex transitioned to 100% green power supply** from mid-July 2026; 79% of Welspun Living's total power consumption is now green.
- **Tariff refunds**: "The process is progressing in line with the applicable regulatory framework. We have initiated the necessary procedures for eligible claims and wherever applicable have started receiving refunds. The timings and quantums remain subject to completion of the administrative process."
- **ROCE target**: Improvement to low teens during FY27.
- **Capital allocation philosophy unchanged**: "We continue to invest selectively behind projects that enhance productivity, improve operational efficiency and strengthen our long term competitiveness."

## 2. TONE, CLARITY, AND CONFIDENCE OF RESPONSES

### Overall Tone
- **Highly confident and optimistic** throughout. Management repeatedly emphasized structural improvements, resilience, and multi-engine growth.
- **CEO Dipali Goenka** was assertive and forward-looking, using phrases like "we are ahead of it," "structural, not circumstantial," "we are not going to let any opportunity pass us," and "we are very, very comfortable with it."
- **CFO Manish Bansal** was measured and precise, focusing on financial discipline, balance sheet quality, and capital allocation.

### Clarity
- **Generally clear** on high-level guidance (double-digit revenue growth, low-teens EBITDA margins, 80% utilization across categories, ₹400–500 crore CAPEX).
- **Less specific** on certain granular details:
  - Tariff absorption split between company/retailer/consumer was described as "case to case, customer to customer and program to program" — somewhat vague.
  - ROCE target described as "low teens" without a specific number.
  - Next year's CAPEX described as "too early to project."
  - VAPI flooding impact quantified only as "quarter two will be impacted" with no specific number.

### Confidence Level
- **Very high confidence** on:
  - UK/Europe growth opportunity (20%+ growth, double-digit growth expected over coming years).
  - Flooring margin sustainability at ~10%.
  - Utilization rates reaching 80% across all categories.
  - Raw material cost pass-through to customers ("It is very, very clear... we'll not be able to bear those costs").
  - Tariff impact already baked into margins ("We are behind that. That's all behind us").
  - No market share loss during tariff disruption.
  - Domestic retail near break-even with 20% growth trajectory toward ₹1,000 crore target.

- **Moderate confidence / somewhat evasive** on:
  - Exact gross margin trajectory ("raw materials will be something that you'll have to watch out for").
  - Specific timeline for reaching 15% EBITDA margin ("gradual process towards 15%").
  - ROStEL (export incentive) extension — "We will wait till this happens and we hope for the best... we will have good news only. Hopefully that's what we pray for."
  - VAPI flooding — acknowledged Q2 impact but no specific quantification; stated Q3/Q4 recovery.
  - Next year's CAPEX — "too early to project."

## 3. FOCUS AREAS

### Product Launches
- **U.S. Onshore Pillow Business**: Ohio facility at ~81% utilization; Nevada facility commenced operations; on track to double revenue to USD $60 million this year.
- **Christie brand**: 16% growth; Wimbledon partnership; expanding in Middle East and US.
- **WellHome**: Increasing acceptance across North America, Japan, and New York markets.
- **Innovation-led sales**: Grew 16%, contributed ~25% of revenue; IP portfolio at 50 patents.
- **Sleep ecosystem**: Focused on pillows; mattresses explicitly ruled out ("Mattresses is not something that we are talking about").
- **Soft flooring**: Strategic shift toward area rugs and qualitative products; geographic diversification (Australia, New Zealand, Canada, GCC).

### Channel Margins
- **Flooring margins improved to 10.4%** (highest in over two years), driven by strategic shift to soft flooring, geographic diversification, and cost controls. Management confirmed sustainability: "Yes. Yeah." and "This is going to be sustainable."
- **Domestic retail**: Near break-even with 20% growth; targeting ₹1,000 crore.
- **B2B business**: Described as "the anchor of all the businesses" and "will be the strong one which is actually the anchor of all the businesses."
- **Branded business**: ~18% of revenue; global brands targeting $100 million; YoY growth of 27%.

### Inventory Levels
- **No specific inventory level data** was disclosed in the transcript.
- **Working capital management** mentioned as "disciplined" by CFO.
- **No spillover from last quarter to this quarter** in top line (confirmed by CEO in response to analyst question).

### Project Neev / Direct Distribution
- **No mention of "Project Neev"** in the transcript.
- **No specific mention of "direct distribution"** as a named initiative.
- **Domestic businesses** (Wealth, Fund, Space) described as "continuing to strengthen their presence across key channels" with B2B and B2C both growing in strong double digits.
- **Unorganized to organized shift** described as "a structural tailwind for our businesses."

## 4. FUTURE OUTLOOK & GUIDANCE

### Margins

**EBITDA Bridge Components:**
- **Three clean drivers** identified by CEO:
  1. **Volume recovery and tariff share unwinding** → converting into operational leverage on a largely fixed cost base.
  2. **Business mix improvement** — quality of business getting better.
  3. **Cost discipline** — structured cost actions taken over last year, reducing energy cost, automation, improving throughput.

**Mix Shift:**
- Non-US revenue share target: from 41% to 50% (medium-term aspiration).
- Geographic contributors: UK, Europe, Japan, and India to contribute to the 50% non-US share; US to remain around 50–55%.
- B2B business is the anchor; branded business (~18% of revenue) also growing strongly (27% YoY).
- Flooring: Strategic shift to soft flooring (area rugs, qualitative products) with geographic diversification.

**Operating Leverage:**
- Volume recovery converting into operational leverage on a largely fixed cost base.
- Manufacturing productivity, procurement efficiencies, automation, and process improvements strengthening underlying cost structure.

**Seasonality:**
- No specific seasonality commentary provided in the transcript.

**ESOP Reversal:**
- **No mention of ESOP reversal** in the transcript.

**EBITDA Margin Guidance:**
- **FY27 target**: Low teens EBITDA margins.
- **Longer-term aspiration**: 15% EBITDA margin — described as "a gradual process towards 15%."
- **Q1 EBITDA margin**: 12.5% (third consecutive quarter of improvement from 6.8% trough).
- **Flooring margins**: ~10% sustainable for the full year and going forward.

### Volume Growth
- **Home textiles exports grew 28.1% YoY** — strongest quarter in recent years.
- **Domestic businesses grew 21.3% YoY**.
- **UK and Europe businesses delivered 20%+ growth**.
- **U.S. Onshore pillow businesses grew 2.3x**.
- **Christie brand delivered 16% growth**.
- **Innovation-led sales grew 16%**.
- **Double-digit revenue growth** expected for FY27.
- **Utilization targets**: 80% across all categories (towels, sheets, rugs) for the full year; bed linen may cross 90% in coming quarters depending on Q2–Q4 performance.

### CAPEX
- **FY27 CAPEX**: ₹400–500 crore range.
- **Specific project**: ₹121 crore debottlenecking and modernization at Anjar facility (board approved in July), replacing older technology, improving plant utilization.
- **Debottlenecking impact**: ~10–20 tons per month additional volume in processing/dyeing categories.
- **Next year's CAPEX**: "Too early to project" — will come back closer to end of FY27.
- **Investment focus**: Automation, modernization, debottlenecking, productivity enhancement, operational efficiency, long-term competitiveness.
- **Capacity expansion in bath category**: Management indicated willingness to invest when opportunity arises ("we are not going to let any opportunity pass us here").

### M&A Integration

**Fluence Pharma:**
- **No mention of Fluence Pharma** in the transcript.

**BTM/Reginald:**
- **No mention of BTM or Reginald** in the transcript.

### Other Forward-Looking Items
- **Tariff framework**: US tariff framework has preserved India's competitive position; India-US BTA talks progressing; optimistic of positive outcome.
- **India-UK FTA**: Effective 15 July 2026; India on equal tariff footing with Pakistan (which holds ~50% of UK home textile imports).
- **Tariff impact**: Already passed on to consumers; not retracted in the US; "That's all behind us."
- **Tariff absorption**: Mix of company, retailer, and consumer — "case to case, customer to customer and program to program."
- **Tariff refund process**: Progressing per regulatory framework; claims initiated; refunds started where applicable; timings and quantums subject to administrative process completion.
- **ROStEL (export incentive)**: Up for termination by September; management hopeful of positive outcome ("we will have good news only. Hopefully that's what we pray for").
- **VAPI flooding**: Q2 will be impacted; Q3/Q4 expected to restore; annually, double-digit growth will continue.
- **Green energy**: Anjar facility now 100% green power from mid-July 2026; 79% of total power consumption is green.
- **ROCE target**: Low teens during FY27.
- **Raw material costs**: Cotton and crude oil are key watch items; costs being passed on to customers ("we'll not be able to bear those costs").
- **Consumer spending in US**: Healthy; retail sales grew 5.2% YoY in May; footfalls increased across key retailers; Fed held rates; overall resilient.

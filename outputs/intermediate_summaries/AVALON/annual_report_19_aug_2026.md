**AVALON TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED - ANNUAL REPORT FORENSIC ANALYSIS (FY2025)**

---

### **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
*   **Key Themes:** Transformation, accelerated growth, broadening horizons, customer diversification, India's rise as a global EMS hub, disciplined execution, and capacity expansion.
*   **Stated Opportunities:** Decadal EMS sector growth (India & global), semiconductor equipment manufacturing, rail/aerospace/clean energy tailwinds, China+1 strategy, domestic policy push.
*   **Stated Risks:** Global geopolitical/trade tensions, tariff frameworks, currency fluctuations, customer concentration, and macroeconomic slowdowns.
*   **Strategic Priorities:** Deepen customer engagement, expand wallet share, build capabilities in high-complexity/verticals (semiconductors), geographic expansion, maintain financial agility.
*   **Walk-the-Talk Check:**
    *   **FY25 Guidance:** Management had guided for revenue growth and profitability improvement.
    *   **Actual Delivery:** Exceeded guidance. Revenue grew 26.6% YoY to ₹1,098 Cr. EBITDA margin improved to 10.5% (from 7.2%). PAT grew 127% YoY. Net Working Capital days improved by 37 days to 124. **PASS.**

### **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
*   **Core Business Segments & Revenue Mix (Consolidated):**
    *   Industrials (Power & Automation): 30%
    *   Mobility/Transportation (Rail, Auto, Aero): 27%
    *   Clean Energy (Solar, EV, Hydrogen): 20%
    *   Medical & Others (Defence, Med-tech): 15%
    *   Communication (Telecom, 5G, Digital Infra): 8%
*   **New Additions (FY25):**
    *   **Geography:** Commissioned a new **export-focused manufacturing plant in Chennai**.
    *   **Capabilities:** Entered **semiconductor equipment manufacturing** via partnership. Partnered with **Zepco Technologies** for motors/drives/power solutions for drones, EVs, defence. Strategic manufacturing partner for **C-DAC's 'RUDRA' HPC programme**.
*   **Capex Done vs. Planned:**
    *   **Done:** Capex of ₹57.8 Cr (consolidated). New Chennai export plant commissioned.
    *   **Pending/Underway:** Phase-II brownfield expansion in Chennai (expected completion H2 FY26). Capex commitments on B/S: ₹161.86 Cr (up from ₹39.98 Cr). **YELLOW FLAG:** Significant pending commitments relative to size; monitor execution.

### **FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK (Consolidated)**
*   **Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y Trend):**
    *   Revenue: FY23: ₹6,884 Cr* -> FY24: ₹8,672 Cr (+26%) -> FY25: ₹10,981 Cr (+27%). **Strong and consistent.**
    *   PAT: FY23: ₹151 Cr* -> FY24: ₹280 Cr (+85%) -> FY25: ₹634 Cr (+127%). **Exceptional growth, improving profitability.**
*   **EBITDA Margin Trend:** Expanding. FY23: 5.7%* -> FY24: 7.3% -> FY25: 10.5%. **GREEN FLAG.**
*   **CFO vs. PAT:**
    *   PAT: ₹634 Cr. CFO from Operations (post-WC): ₹251 Cr. **YELLOW FLAG:** CFO conversion is low (~40%) due to significant working capital increase (trade receivables up ~₹1,291 Cr). Needs scrutiny on receivable quality.
*   **Working Capital Movement:**
    *   Net Working Capital Days: Improved from 161 to 124 days YoY.
    *   **Inventory Days:** Improved from 118 to 86 days. **Positive.**
    *   **Trade Receivable Days:** Increased from 79 to 84 days. **Slight deterioration.**
    *   **Trade Payable Days:** Improved from 36 to 46 days. **Positive.**
*   **ROCE/ROE Trend:** Improving.
    *   ROCE: 7.7% (FY24) -> 13.8% (FY25). **Strong improvement.**
    *   ROE: 5.2% (FY24) -> 11.0% (FY25). **GREEN FLAG.**
*   **Debt Position:**
    *   Net Debt (Consolidated): ₹1,417 Cr (Borrowings) - ₹1,015 Cr (Cash & Equivalents) = **₹402 Cr Net Debt**. Net Debt/EBITDA: 0.3x.
    *   D/E Ratio (Total Debt/Equity): 0.29x. **Low leverage.**
    *   Interest Coverage: EBIT/Finance Cost = ~1.1x (EBIT ~₹184 Cr, Finance Cost ₹167 Cr). **YELLOW FLAG:** Coverage is thin, mainly due to significant finance costs on US subsidiary's preference shares/debt.

### **FORENSIC FLAGS**
*   **Related Party Transactions (RPT):**
    *   **Size:** Significant. Sales to subsidiaries (primarily US subsidiary ABV/Sienna): ₹2,429 Cr (**22% of consolidated revenue**). Purchases from subsidiaries: ₹830 Cr.
    *   **Nature:** Ordinary course of business, arm's length basis as per management. Loans given to Indian subsidiary: ₹800 Cr outstanding. Corporate guarantees given: ₹1,270 Cr to subsidiaries.
    *   **Suspicious Flows?** Large inter-company balances and guarantees. Requires monitoring of subsidiary financial health and terms. **YELLOW FLAG.**
*   **Contingent Liabilities vs. Net Worth:**
    *   Contingent Liabilities: ₹27.6 Cr (Tax demands, PF demand, Indirect tax).
    *   Net Worth: ₹6,115 Cr.
    *   Ratio: **0.45%**. **GREEN FLAG** (well below 10%).
*   **Miscellaneous Expenses as % of Revenue:**
    *   Other Expenses (Standalone): ₹353.5 Cr. Miscellaneous: ₹8.2 Cr.
    *   **Misc. / Revenue:** 0.13%. **GREEN FLAG** (well below 3%).
*   **Auditor Opinion:**
    *   **Statutory Auditors (Varma & Varma):** **Unqualified Opinion** on both Standalone and Consolidated FS.
    *   **Key Audit Matters:** Revenue Recognition, Impairment Assessment of Investments in Subsidiaries. **No red flags noted** in KAM descriptions.
*   **CARO Report Concerns:**
    *   Clause (xxi): Notes **unfavorable remarks under Clause (iib)** for both Avalon Technologies Ltd and its Indian subsidiary Avalon Technology & Services Pvt Ltd.
    *   **Analysis:** Clause (iib) relates to the **disclosure of interest/loans to directors and related parties**. The specific adverse remark content is not detailed in the text but is flagged. **YELLOW FLAG.**
*   **Accounting Policy Changes:** No significant policy changes noted in the report. Policies appear standard and conservative (e.g., depreciation, revenue recognition on transfer of control).

### **GOVERNANCE**
*   **Management Remuneration as % of Revenue:**
    *   KMP Remuneration (Consolidated): ₹58.8 Cr.
    *   Revenue: ₹10,981 Cr.
    *   **Ratio: 0.54%**. **GREEN FLAG** (reasonable).
*   **KMP Changes or Resignations:**
    *   CFO (R.M. Subramanian) resigned Sep 2024. New CFO (Suresh Veerappan) appointed Nov 2024.
    *   Company Secretary (Rajesh V) resigned Nov 2024. New CS (Ajay Shukla) appointed Feb 2025.
    *   **YELLOW FLAG:** Two key compliance/finance roles changed within the year. Requires monitoring for stability.
*   **Promoter Pledging or Stake Changes:**
    *   No promoter share pledging mentioned.
    *   Promoter shareholding is stable at ~50.6%. Minor dilution due to ESOP issuances. **GREEN FLAG.**

### **FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: GOOD**

**Summary Rationale:**
Avalon Technologies delivered strong operational and financial performance in FY25, exceeding guidance. The company exhibits a clear growth strategy with prudent capacity expansion and a shift towards higher-margin segments. Financials show improving profitability and returns. **Key positives:** Strong revenue/PAT growth, improving ROCE/ROE, low leverage, unqualified audit opinion, clean contingent liabilities. **Key yellow flags to monitor:** High receivables growth impacting cash conversion, significant related party transactions and guarantees to subsidiaries, thin interest coverage due to subsidiary financing costs, and two KMP resignations within the year. Overall, accounting quality and corporate governance appear solid.

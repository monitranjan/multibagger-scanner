**Analysis of Avalon Technologies Ltd Q1 FY27 Earnings Call Transcript**

### **1. Management Commentary: Key Statements**

*   **Kunhamed Bicha (Chairman & MD):**
    *   "Q1 FY '27 was another strong quarter. This marks our eighth consecutive quarter of improvement in performance and growth across key metrics."
    *   "Our revenue growth is well diversified across geographies and industry verticals. Our profitability metrics have improved. ROCE has moved up to 23.4%, reflecting that we are scaling with discipline. Net working capital has improved..."
    *   "Based on this strong momentum across our business and the visibility we have on programs ramping through the year, we are revising our FY '27 revenue growth guidance upward from 24% to 27% to 26% to 30%."
    *   "Our US manufacturing plant has a clear and deliberate role. It is where new customers come in, validate our capabilities, and get comfortable with Avalon as a partner. Once that comfort is established, production progressively transitions to India manufacturing..."
    *   "Our trailing 12-month revenues have already crossed that milestone [doubling from FY24], nearly a year ahead of our commitment... we are now focused on the next doubling from INR 1,603 crores in FY '26 to approximately INR 3,200 crores in FY '29."
    *   "New manufacturing plant focused on domestic demand in Chennai is now complete and will commence commercial production from Q2 FY '27... We are in the process of acquiring a large parcel of land in Chennai that will support Avalon's growth over the next decade."
    *   "I think we've never been this excited on the possibilities. It may not show in the P&L, the type of products which are coming in and the type of geographies that's coming in. At least in 25 years of doing this, I've not seen that."

*   **Suresh Veerappan (CFO):**
    *   "Revenue for Q1 FY '27 was INR 484 crores, up 49.8% year-on-year... average revenue growth over the last eight quarters has been 46%."
    *   "EBITDA for Q1 FY '27 was INR 58 crores with a margin of 12%... reflecting operating leverage as revenues scale. Adjusting for the tariffs pass-through impact, our EBITDA margin would have been approximately 0.9% higher."
    *   "Net working capital improved to 117 days in June 2026 from 142 days in June 2025... inventory days improved to 94 days from 104 days; trade receivable days reduced from 87 days to 74 days."
    *   "Capex for Q1 FY '27 was INR 16 crores. Asset turns remain strong at 9.9 times. Return on capital employed improved to 23.4%..."
    *   "We have revised our FY '27 revenue growth guidance upward to 26% to 30% from the earlier guidance of 24% to 27%."

*   **Shriram Vijayaraghavan (COO):**
    *   "The focus is to build the capability in all three areas [people, processes, automation], and that's work that's underway."
    *   "We are not in a solar panel business; we are in the energy storage solutions business."
    *   "Subsidies or the support [for energy storage] continues until 2032... These have a longer subsidy support in the US."
    *   "All these components [like copper, PVC] form a part of the bill of materials... we don't believe this is driving the increase in prices... this is broad-based growth that's been driving for the last eight quarters."

### **2. Tone, Clarity, and Confidence**

*   **Tone:** Consistently optimistic, confident, and disciplined. Emphasis is on sustainable, multi-year growth, operational efficiency, and long-term partnerships.
*   **Clarity:** Management provides clear quantitative guidance (revenue growth, gross margin range, working capital targets) and strategic direction (focus on box build, geographic expansion, US plant role). However, specific timelines for new programs ramping up to full volume are deliberately kept vague.
*   **Confidence:** High confidence is expressed in the business model, diversified growth engines (existing business, new wins, pipeline), and the long-term structural shift of global supply chains benefiting India. The upward revision of guidance and the aspiration to double revenues by FY'29 underscore this confidence.

### **3. Focus Areas**

*   **Product Launches & New Programs:**
    *   **Aerospace:** Cabin sub-assemblies moving towards volume; incremental box build business secured with a leading aerospace company.
    *   **Rail:** Production of locomotive engine sub-systems is underway; Kavach anti-collision system on track for commercial production.
    *   **Semiconductor Equipment:** Increased allocation with a leading wafer fabrication equipment supplier, expanding into other products; production ramping over next quarters.
    *   **New Manufacturing:** New Chennai plant for domestic demand to start commercial production in Q2 FY27.
    *   **Future Product:** One new product area within current capabilities to be announced in the next two quarters.

*   **Channel Margins & Inventory Levels:**
    *   **Gross Margins:** Consistently maintained within guided range of 33%-35%. Tariffs had a ~0.9% negative impact, which was absorbed.
    *   **Inventory Days:** Improved to 94 days (from 104 days YoY). Management notes new programs may have higher initial working capital, which optimizes over time.

*   **Geographic & Vertical Focus:**
    *   **Direct Distribution/Geographies:** Strong growth in both India (53% YoY) and Exports (48% YoY). Strategic focus on expanding in **Europe** and **Southeast Asia**.
    *   **Project Neev:** Not mentioned in the transcript.

### **4. Future Outlook & Guidance**

*   **Revenue Guidance:** Revised upward to **26%-30%** growth for FY27 (from 24%-27%). Long-term aspiration to reach ~INR 3,200 crores by FY29.
*   **Margins:**
    *   **EBITDA Bridge:** Improvement driven by operating leverage as revenues scale. Fixed/semi-fixed costs (~45-50% below gross margin) are expected to provide leverage.
    *   **Mix Shift:** Increasing box build contribution (to 59.9% in Q1) supports margin profile, though new programs may start at lower margins.
    *   **Seasonality:** Management encourages viewing performance on a multi-year, not quarterly, lens. Q1 was a "positive start," but some programs may ramp in H2 or next fiscal year.
    *   **ESOP Reversal:** Not mentioned in the transcript.
*   **Volume Growth:** Driven by long product lifecycles and mission-critical programs across Industrial, Mobility (Rail, Aero), Clean Energy, and Communication. Diversified growth across all verticals except a dip in Communication.
*   **CAPEX & Expansion:**
    *   Q1 CAPEX was INR 16 crores. Focused on land acquisition in Chennai for next-decade growth. New capex will be modular.
    *   Asset turns target maintained at **8-10 times**. ROCE is a key focus, currently at 23.4%.
*   **M&A Integration:** No mention of Fluence Pharma or BTM/Reginald in the transcript.

### **5. Q&A Dynamics: Analyst Queries vs. Management Answers**

| Analyst Query Topic | Management Response & Confidence/Evasiveness |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Long-term steady-state gross margin** | **Confident.** Maintains 33-35% range as aspiration, acknowledging some new businesses have higher margins but are blended. |
| **Operating leverage / Fixed cost structure** | **Confident.** Reiterates 45-50% fixed cost base and expects leverage to play out with scale. Does not give specific EBITDA margin guidance. |
| **Data Center opportunity** | **Confident & Detailed.** Actively playing in HVDC/power systems (production started), off-grid power, and targeting future rack/cooling systems. Explicitly states will not make server boards currently. |
| **Order win cycle for HVDC & Semi WFE** | **Confident.** States revenue has started for both but will ramp gradually over quarters. |
| **Reasons for conservative guidance upgrade** | **Deliberately Vague/Conservative.** Explains new program kick-in dates (e.g., Feb vs Apr) create uncertainty, hence conservatism. Emphasizes 3-year lens over quarterly. |
| **US manufacturing loss & breakeven** | **Confident.** Reports loss narrowed to INR 4 crores. Stated goal: EBITDA break-even by end of FY27, then PAT break-even. Frames US plant as a "beachhead" for customer validation. |
| **Leadership hiring for scale** | **Confident.** Detail provided on adding 5-6 VP-level candidates for BUs/capabilities. Work has started. |
| **New product categories beyond PCBA/box build** | **Slightly Evasive (pre-announcement).** Confirms focus on vertical integration to increase box build share. Hints at one new product area to be announced in coming quarters but does not disclose specifics. |
| **Impact of input price volatility on growth** | **Confident.** Denies growth is driven by component price inflation. Attributes it to broad-based, inherent business growth across verticals and geographies. |
| **Defence sector focus** | **Measured/Long-term.** Acknowledges it's lumpy ("feast or famine"). First set of hiring done 3 years ago to explore it as a vertical. Not a large focus yet. |
| **Box build segment-wise breakdown** | **Confident.** States box build is spread across all verticals except Aero (sub-assemblies only). Cites examples from Rail and Clean Energy. |
| **ISM 2.0 scheme benefits** | **Cautiously Optimistic.** Believes they could be beneficiaries due to semiconductor equipment focus. Awaiting finer details of the scheme. |

**Overall Q&A Dynamics:** Management was highly confident and transparent on financial metrics, strategic direction, and existing operational progress. They were deliberately conservative and less specific on the exact timing of new program ramps and the precise mechanics of the revised guidance range, framing it as prudent management. The response to the Clean Energy/solar subsidy question was notably redirected by the COO to be answered offline.

**EXTRACTED DATA FROM AVALON TECHNOLOGIES INVESTOR PRESENTATION (Q1 FY27)**

### **CORE PRODUCT SEGMENTS & CAPABILITIES**
- **End-to-End Box Build Solutions:** Accounted for **60% of Q1 FY27 revenue**. Involves system integration, testing, and final assembly.
- **Printed Circuit Board (PCB) Assembly:** Core service, including SMT, THT, and HDI capabilities.
- **Cable Assembly & Wire Harnesses:** Key capability across industries.
- **Injection Molded Plastics:** Serves aerospace, industrial, and power sectors.
- **Sheet Metal Fabrication & Machining:** Focus on high-precision aerospace components.
- **Magnetics:** Integrated capability for end-to-end solutions.
- **Design & New Product Development (NPD):** In-house PCB design and analysis, rapid prototyping, and DFM services to capture value early in the product lifecycle.

### **QUANTITATIVE METRICS (Q1 FY27 vs. Q1 FY26)**
- **Revenue:** INR 484 Cr, **+49.8% YoY**.
- **Gross Margin:** INR 168 Cr, **+46.3% YoY**; Margin at **34.7%** (vs. 35.5%).
- **EBITDA:** INR 58 Cr, **+93.9% YoY**; Margin at **12.0%** (vs. 9.2%).
- **Profit After Tax (PAT):** INR 35 Cr, **+145.3% YoY**; Margin at **7.2%** (vs. 4.4%).
- **Order Book:** INR 2,208 Cr (as of Jun 30, 2026), **+23.4% YoY**. Executable over an avg. of **14 months**.
- **Net Working Capital Days:** Improved to **117 days** (from 142 days in Jun'25).
- **Asset Turn:** **9.9x** (FY26), indicating capital-light model.
- **RoCE (FY26):** **20.6%**.
- **India Manufacturing EBITDA Margin:** **16.7%** in Q1 FY27.

### **FUTURE GROWTH ADJACENCIES & STRATEGY**
- **Hybrid Manufacturing Model:** Leverages "Make in India for India & Export" and "Hybrid Make in US for US" to capitalize on supply chain diversification and customer proximity.
- **Brownfield Expansion:** Chennai facility brownfield expansion for domestic demand is operational.
- **Vertical Integration:** Strategy to move up the value chain from design services to box build to enhance wallet share.
- **Customer & Industry Diversification:** Focus on high-growth, high-mix, long-lifecycle industries:
    - **Clean Energy:** Solar, Hydrogen, Electric Vehicles.
    - **Mobility & Transportation:** Aerospace, Automotive, Railways (signaling, braking systems).
    - **Communication:** Telecom, Satellite, Digital Infrastructure.
    - **Medical, Defence, & Others.**
- **Market Tailwinds:** Leveraging the **India EMS industry CAGR of ~27%** and global supply chain diversification to India and the US.

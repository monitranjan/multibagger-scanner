**Tone, Clarity, and Confidence of Responses**
- CEO generally direct and numerically precise; uses definitive language ("definitely," "we are confident," "we should be able to") while maintaining caution on sensitive defense product timelines; initially avoids product-specific queries ("normally, we would like to avoid all such product-specific questions") but provides clear status when pressed: Bhargavastra "final stage," "complete all trials in this calendar year"; 155 mm "3 to 4 months' time"; confident on margin maintenance despite commodity inflation: "we should be able to maintain the current EBITDA margins"; on competition: "we are not afraid from anyone"; on inventory: "very normal for our kind of industry."  
- CFO Shalinee structured and explanatory on working capital and subsidiary contribution; deliberately frames inventory build as strategic risk mitigation; transparent on performance trajectory ("subdued H1, improved from Q3").  
- Management consistently employs reinforcing phrases ("sure," "absolutely," "definitely") while maintaining guarded silence on competitive dynamics and exact program milestones.

**Future Outlook & Guidance**
- Margins: "targeting to achieve a revenue of INR14,000 crores in FY '27, while maintaining current margins." Raw material price history: "when raw material prices has increased by 50%, 60%, 70%, at those times, our EBITDA margins went down by around 2% to 3%. So this time, we are confident that we should be able to manage this situation with the help of improved defense as well as international business."  
- Mix shift: Defense growing "from 20% to 33% [Q4]" and "27% from 18% [FY]." International "33% from 36% [Q4]" and "39% from 38% [FY]." CIL declining; management: "since our defense is picking up, international is also doing very good. So we should be able to maintain the current EBITDA margins."  
- Volume growth: "we should be able to grow comfortably at around 10% [volume] and in value terms, we should grow at around 15%." Domestically: "10% to 15% should come from volume growth and around 18% to 20% should come from the price rise."  
- CAPEX: "invested INR2,700 crores over the last 2 years, and entering into FY '27 with a planned annual capex of INR2,050 crores." "we always channelize the capex programs based on the priority and the market opportunity available to us." "for us, our business is one" – avoids defense/non-defense split.  
- Seasonality/market: "last year, it was a totally flat growth market or you can say the OB removal in Coal India, Singareni and private coal mines combined together was on a negative side. But despite of that, we managed a decent growth of around 4% in value terms. And this year, we expect that because of the pressure in the market where people would like to shift from the diesel, petrol or generated products to the electricity-based products. So that will definitely help the growth in the coal mining sector. And since last year was the bottom-out kind of situation for OB removal section. So this year, we expect the demand should pick up."  
- ESOP reversal: Not mentioned in text.

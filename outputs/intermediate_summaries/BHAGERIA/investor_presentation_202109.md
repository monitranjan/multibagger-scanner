**Extracted Analysis: Bhageria Industries Ltd. Investor Presentation (Q2 & H1 FY22)**

### **Core Product Segments & Launches**
- **Primary Segment:** Dye Intermediates & Specialty Chemicals.
- **Key Products:** Leading manufacturer of **H-Acid & Gamma Acid** (Top 3 producer in India).
- **Capacity:** ~14,000 TPA for dye intermediates, with 300 TPD Sulphuric Acid capacity.
- **New Launches/Expansions:** No new fragrances, elixirs, or active-ingredient cosmetics launches disclosed. **Board approved acquisition of 51% equity in Bhageria & Jajodia Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. (BJPPL), marking entry into pharma segment.**
- **R&D Focus:** New product development, process chemistry optimization, and new applications for existing products.

### **Quantitative Metrics**
#### **Growth Trajectory (YoY)**
- **Net Sales:** Q2 FY22: ₹141 Cr (+33.7%); H1 FY22: ₹254 Cr (+61.1%).
- **EBITDA:** Q2 FY22: ₹26 Cr (+1.7%); H1 FY22: ₹48 Cr (+26.6%).
- **PAT:** Q2 FY22: ₹15 Cr (-6.6%); H1 FY22: ₹28 Cr (+28.3%).
- **Volume-led growth** was noted, but input cost inflation compressed margins.

#### **Margin Contribution**
- **EBITDA Margin:** Q2 FY22: 18.1% (down from 23.8% in Q2 FY21); H1 FY22: 18.8% (down from 23.9% in H1 FY21).
- **Chemical Segment EBITDA Margin (FY21):** 24.7% (Industry margin leadership).
- **PAT Margin:** Q2 FY22: 10.7%; H1 FY22: 11.0%.

#### **Market Position**
- **One of the top 3 producers of H-Acid & Gamma Acid in India.**
- **Capacity Utilization:** >85%.
- **Export Contribution:** 27% of H1 FY22 revenue.

#### **Balance Sheet & Returns**
- **Net Cash Company:** Net Cash of ₹4.3 Cr as of Sept'21.
- **ROCE (Chemical Segment, FY21):** 22.6%.
- **ROE (FY21):** 14.8%.

### **Future Growth Adjacencies**
1.  **Pharma Segment Entry:** Via acquisition of 51% stake in **Bhageria & Jajodia Pharmaceuticals Pvt. Ltd. (BJPPL).**
2.  **Capex Plan (~₹130 Cr, internally funded):**
    - Backward integration (Sulphuric Acid & Derivatives).
    - Expansion in dye intermediates (including margin-accretive J-Acid & Tobias Acid).
    - Manufacture of pigments intermediates.
    - Increase in power capacity to 8,000 KW.
3.  **No information** provided on fragrance entry, oral beauty/nutrition M&A, or offline channel additions.

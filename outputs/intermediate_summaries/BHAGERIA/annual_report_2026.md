### **FORENSIC ANALYSIS: BHAGERIA INDUSTRIES LIMITED (FY 2025-26)**

---

#### **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
*   **Key Themes:** "Capacity, Capability, Continuity" – emphasis on compounding gains from past investments, not sudden pivots.
*   **Stated Opportunities:** Strong chemical segment capacity utilization (95-97%), new plasticizers product line, expanding solar generation (21 MW commissioned).
*   **Stated Risks:** Raw material volatility, logistics costs, geopolitical uncertainties impacting supply chains. Pharma segment is a long-term journey with modest current contribution.
*   **Strategic Priorities:** Deepen R&D in chemicals & pharma, increase automation, strengthen global customer relationships, maintain financial discipline.
*   **Walk-the-Talk Check:**
    *   **Stated Guidance vs Actual:** Chairman highlighted "strongest revenue growth in several years" driven by chemicals. Actual: Total income grew 45.9% to ₹880.56 Cr, Chemical segment revenue up 64.5% to ₹829.56 Cr. **Aligned.**
    *   **New Capacity:** H-Acid expansion (400 to 500 MT/month) and plasticizers production commenced as planned. Solar plants commissioned as per timeline. **Aligned.**
    *   **Financial Position:** Recommended a 50% dividend (₹2.50/share), up from 30% prior year, reflecting stated commitment to shareholder returns while investing for growth. **Aligned.**

---

#### **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
*   **Core Segments & Revenue Mix (Standalone):**
    *   Chemicals (Dyes/Dye Intermediates): **₹829.56 Cr (94.2% of total income)**. Segment EBIT: ₹69.26 Cr.
    *   Solar Power: **₹26.86 Cr (3.0%)**. Segment EBIT: ₹11.57 Cr.
    *   Pharmaceuticals: **₹4.20 Cr (0.5%)**. Segment Loss: (₹5.49) Cr.
    *   Others (Trading/EPC): **₹10.82 Cr (1.2%)**. Segment EBIT: ₹1.26 Cr.
*   **New Segments/Products/Geographies:**
    *   **Product:** Launched **Plasticizers** production at Tarapur facility (new specialty chemical line).
    *   **Geography:** Maintained strong export demand from **Southeast Asia & Europe**; export revenue surged to ₹30,372 Lakhs (from ₹9,296 Lakhs).
    *   **Segment:** Pharma is a new but pre-revenue scale segment. No new geographic markets explicitly stated.
*   **Capex Done vs Planned:**
    *   **Done:** Commissioned 14 MW solar in FY25-26 (Pimpalgaon Wagha, Bhatkudgaon) + 7 MW in May 2026 (Khanapur). H-Acid capacity expansion completed.
    *   **Pending/In Progress:** 32 MW solar build-out under Maharashtra's MSKVY 2.0 (partially commissioned). A 2.25 MW solar plant at Tapi, Gujarat for captive use is under implementation. **Capital Work-in-Progress (Consolidated): ₹1,169.51 Lakhs (₹107.99 Lakhs in prior year).**

#### **FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK (Standalone)**
*   **Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y Trend):**
    *   **Revenue:** ₹604.07 Cr (FY25) → ₹880.56 Cr (FY26). **Strong rebound after FY24 dip.**
    *   **PAT:** ₹42.27 Cr (FY25) → ₹50.30 Cr (FY26). **Healthy growth (+19.0%), but slower than revenue growth due to margin pressure.**
*   **EBITDA Margin Trend:**
    *   FY24: 15.03% → FY25: 15.03% → **FY26: 11.61%.** **Significant contraction.** Attributed to input cost inflation, competitive conditions, and unfavorable product mix.
*   **CFO vs PAT:**
    *   **PAT:** ₹50.30 Cr. **CFO from Operations:** ₹1,023.37 Cr. **CFO/PAT ratio > 20x.** Profit is converting strongly to cash, driven by working capital efficiency.
*   **Working Capital Movement:**
    *   **Receivables Days:** Improved to **~66 days** (from ~110 days prior year). Strong collection efficiency.
    *   **Inventory Days:** Increased to **~24 days** (from ~29 days prior year). Improvement noted, but raw material inventory buildup (₹414.34 Cr vs ₹301.28 Cr) warrants monitoring.
*   **ROCE/ROE Trend:**
    *   **ROCE:** Improved to **15.27%** (from 13.73%). **Positive.**
    *   **ROE:** Improved to **11.48%** (from 10.63%). **Positive, but modest.**
*   **Debt Position:**
    *   **Net Debt (Standalone):** ₹387.09 Lakhs (minimal, as cash covers most borrowings). **Conservative.**
    *   **Debt/Equity Ratio:** 0.02 (very low). **Strong.**
    *   **Interest Coverage:** 33.22x (excellent). **No strain.**

#### **FORENSIC FLAGS**
*   **Related Party Transactions (RPTs):**
    *   **Size:** Significant. **Loans to Subsidiaries:** ₹2,953 Lakhs (Rahuri Cleantech). **Guarantees:** ₹10,200 Lakhs for Rahuri Cleantech's bank loan. **Purchase from related parties (Bhageria Green Energy):** ₹420.44 Lakhs.
    *   **Nature:** Primarily funding for solar subsidiaries, purchases from promoter-linked entities. All stated as "arm's length" and approved by Audit Committee.
    *   **Suspicious Flow Flag:** The **guarantee to subsidiary (₹102 Cr) is material** relative to standalone net worth (₹600 Cr). Requires monitoring of subsidiary health. **YELLOW FLAG.**
*   **Contingent Liabilities vs Net Worth:**
    *   Contingent Liabilities: **₹121.30 Lakhs** (₹81.68 Lakhs prior year). This includes disputed taxes and bank guarantees.
    *   Net Worth (Standalone): **₹6,003.40 Lakhs**.
    *   **Ratio:** ~2.0%. **GREEN FLAG** (<10% threshold).
*   **Miscellaneous Expenses as % of Revenue:**
    *   Miscellaneous Expenses (Note 39): ₹22.62 Lakhs.
    *   Revenue from Operations: ₹8,714.34 Lakhs.
    *   **Ratio:** ~0.26%. **GREEN FLAG** (<3% threshold).
*   **Auditor Opinion:**
    *   **Unqualified Opinion** on both Standalone and Consolidated financial statements. **GREEN FLAG.**
*   **Key Audit Matters:**
    *   None identified in the auditor's report. **GREEN FLAG.**
*   **CARO Report:**
    *   No qualifications or adverse remarks. **GREEN FLAG.**
*   **Accounting Policy Changes:**
    *   No changes in accounting policies during the year. Policies are applied consistently (Ind-AS compliant). **GREEN FLAG.**

### **FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: AVERAGE**

**Rationale:**
*   **Positives:** Clean audit opinion, strong cash conversion, minimal debt, stable governance, no major forensic red flags in core processes.
*   **Concerns:** Material related party guarantees and loans to loss-making subsidiaries require scrutiny. Significant compression in EBITDA margins is a business quality concern that impacts earnings sustainability. The "Others" segment revenue drop from ₹57 Cr to ₹11 Cr needs clarity. While not accounting quality issues per se, these factors temper the overall assessment from "Good" to **"Average."**

## ANALYSIS OF AARTI SURFACTANTS LIMITED ANNUAL REPORT FY 2024-25

### MANAGEMENT LETTERS
**Key Themes (CEO & Chairman):**
- **Resilience & Agility:** Emphasized navigating global volatility, raw material cost fluctuations, and supply chain disruptions.
- **Growth & Efficiency:** Focused on revenue growth, cost control, lean operations, and higher-margin products.
- **Innovation & Sustainability:** Launched Navi Mumbai R&D Centre; accelerated eco-friendly surfactant development and water/energy recycling.
- **Strategic Expansion:** Exploring Asia-Pacific and Middle East markets; enhancing digital and automation capabilities.
- **Risk Management:** Building structural resilience through long-term supplier partnerships and backward integration.

**Walk-the-Talk Check (FY25 Guidance vs. Actuals):**
- **Guidance:** Promised steady revenue growth, profitability improvement, and strategic investments.
- **Delivery:** Revenue grew 11.7% to ₹65,908.54 lakhs (met guidance). EBITDA margin improved to 10.7% (met guidance). PAT declined 32.6% to ₹1,499.00 lakhs (missed profitability target due to higher input costs). R&D center commissioned (met guidance).

**New Additions:**
- **Products:** Focus on high-margin specialty surfactants and eco-friendly formulations.
- **Geographies:** Targeting Asia-Pacific and Middle East expansion.
- **Facilities:** New R&D Centre in Navi Mumbai commissioned.

**Capex Execution:**
- **FY25 Capex:** ₹1,993.31 lakhs (Standalone). Major additions: Plant & Machinery, Capital Work-in-Progress for expansion projects.
- **Pending:** No major project delays noted; all projects within original cost.

### FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK
**Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y Trend):**
- **Revenue:** FY23: ₹60,129.32 lakhs, FY24: ₹58,985.74 lakhs, FY25: ₹65,908.54 lakhs. **Trend:** Volatile but overall upward.
- **PAT:** FY23: ₹550.05 lakhs, FY24: ₹1,271.05 lakhs, FY25: ₹1,499.00 lakhs. **Trend:** Improving but below peak levels.

**EBITDA Margin:**
- FY23: 5.64%, FY24: 8.00%, FY25: 10.70%. **Trend:** Expanding, indicating improved operational efficiency.

**CFO vs. PAT:**
- FY25: CFO ₹5,200.06 lakhs vs. PAT ₹1,499.00 lakhs. **Cash Conversion:** Strong, with CFO significantly exceeding PAT.

**Working Capital Movement:**
- **Inventory Days:** Increased from ~61 days (FY24) to ~75 days (FY25).
- **Receivable Days:** Stable at ~42 days.
- **Payable Days:** Increased from ~54 days (FY24) to ~63 days (FY25).
- **Overall:** Working capital intensity increased, primarily due to inventory buildup.

**ROCE/ROE Trend:**
- **ROCE:** FY24: 17.43%, FY25: 11.54%. **Trend:** Deteriorating.
- **ROE:** FY24: 11.50%, FY25: 6.61%. **Trend:** Deteriorating, driven by lower profitability and higher equity base.

**Debt Position:**
- **Net Debt:** ₹10,800.86 lakhs (FY25) vs. ₹9,107.56 lakhs (FY24).
- **D/E Ratio:** 0.48 (FY25).
- **Interest Coverage:** 4.30x (FY25). **Assessment:** Leverage is moderate but increasing.

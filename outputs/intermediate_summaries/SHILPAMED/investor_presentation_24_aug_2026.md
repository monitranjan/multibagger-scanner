**Shilpa Medicare Limited - 1QFY27 Earnings Presentation: Critical Data Extraction**

**I. Core Product Segments & Launches**
*   **API Business:**
    *   Growth driver: Core portfolio (~15% YoY growth).
    *   Focus: Captive business growth; Non-Captive portfolio growth (~15% YoY) led by Specialty CDMO (API CDMO, Peptide, Polymer).
    *   Development: 20+ products in development; Onco portfolio revamped with 15+ new APIs (targeting patent expiries through 2032); New Onco block expansion (end FY27).
    *   Filings: 290 DMF filings globally; 7 new DMF filings added in 1QFY27.
*   **Formulations Business:**
    *   **Novel Product Launch:** NorUDCA (`NoducaTM`) – First-in-class treatment for NAFLD launched in India (3QFY26). Strategic partnership with 3 large companies for marketing.
    *   **Revenue Breakdown (1QFY27):** US (INR 45 cr), Europe (INR 57 cr), Domestic (INR 24 cr), RoW (INR 43 cr), Licensing/Services (INR 29 cr).
    *   **Complex Pipeline Updates:**
        *   Transdermal Patch (Rotigotine) for Parkinson's: EMA approval received; launch planned 1HFY27. USFDA submission completed.
        *   Topical Lotion for Alopecia: Phase 3 initiated in India (Jan 2026); Global Phase 3 for EU initiated.
        *   Novel Long-Acting Injection (Ondansetron ER): Positive Phase 3 in India; launch planned FY27.
        *   Complex Generics: Abraxane (EU+RoW, FY28), Xtandi (Global, FY28), Abiraterone 1g tablet (EU/RoW 1HFY27, US 2HFY27).
*   **Biologics & NBE:**
    *   **Biosimilar Pipeline:** High-value assets (Adalimumab, Aflibercept, Nivolumab, Pembrolizumab, Daratumumab, Dupilumab, SBPL 1 (ADC)).
    *   **Key Updates:**
        *   Nivolumab: Human trials started; Orion partnership for Europe.
        *   Aflibercept: Phase 3 India nearing completion; India launch expected FY27.
        *   Recombinant Human Albumin (rHA): Phase 3 trials for India & Europe to start 1HFY27; Orion partnership for Europe.
        *   ADC: First ADC biosimilar to enter human studies in FY27; GMP facility commissioned in Dharwad.
    *   **Novel Pipeline:** Five active NBE programs; Partnerships with mAbTree (Novel MAB), Alveolus Bio (Novel LBP), Gate2Brain (G2B-002).
*   **CDMO Business:**
    *   **Capabilities:** Integrated offering across Small Molecules, Peptides, Monoclonal Antibodies, ADCs, Fermentation.
    *   **Key Projects:**
        *   OLC (Oxylanthanum Carbonate) with Unicycive: Comprehensive CDMO contract (API + FDF). Awaiting US FDA approval post NDA resubmission.
        *   Two new late-stage projects secured (one NCE commercialized in FY26).
        *   Two NBE projects expected to enter human studies in FY27.

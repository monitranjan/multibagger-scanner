### **ANNUAL REPORT FORENSIC ANALYSIS: MRS. BECTORS FOOD SPECIALITIES LTD. (FY2025)**

#### **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key Themes:** Resilience amid urban slowdown and inflation; focus on innovation, premiumization, and cost optimization.
- **Stated Opportunities:** New product launches (NaturBaked, zero-maida products), q-comm channel expansion, export strength.
- **Risks Mentioned:** Potential tariff changes, input cost inflation, subdued consumption.
- **Strategic Priorities:** Aggressive new product development, pan-India bakery expansion (Kolkata, Maharashtra), capex execution.
- **Walk-the-Talk Check:**
  - **FY25 Guidance (implied):** Steady growth trajectory.
  - **Actual Delivery:** Revenue +15.4% (solid); EBITDA +3.7% (modest); PAT +2.05% (sluggish). Margin contraction evident (EBITDA margin down ~1% YoY). Management highlights challenging environment but delivered on growth. **Partial match** – top-line delivered, bottom-line lagged.

#### **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
- **Core Segments:** Biscuits (Cremica) & Bakery (English Oven). Domestic & export (75 countries).
- **Revenue Mix (Consolidated):** Not explicitly split, but export contribution noted as 36% of revenue.
- **New Additions:**
  - **Products:** Health-focused biscuits, zero-maida bread, muffins/brownies under English Oven. NaturBaked brand launched post-year-end.
  - **Geographies:** Export focus maintained. No major new domestic geographies.
  - **Segments:** No new business segments.
- **Capex Execution:**
  - **Commissioned:** Indore (Dhar, MP) biscuit plant (May 2025).
  - **Pending/In-Progress:** Bakery plants in Kolkata and Khopoli (Maharashtra) – "advanced construction progress." Capital work-in-progress stood at ₹2,369.59M (up from ₹943.62M). QIP proceeds (₹4,000M) utilized partially: ₹1,550M for debt repayment, ₹555M invested in Khopoli project, ₹200M for MP project.

#### **FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK (Consolidated)**
- **Revenue Growth:** 3Y: ₹1,362M → ₹1,624M → **₹1,874M**. **Steady growth.**
- **PAT Growth:** 3Y: ₹90M → ₹140M → **₹143M**. **Near-flat YoY.**
- **EBITDA Margin Trend:** FY23: ~12.9% → FY24: ~14.9% → **FY25: ~13.4%**. **Contracting by ~150 bps YoY.**
- **CFO vs PAT:** CFO (₹1,614M) **exceeded** PAT (₹1,432M). **Good cash conversion** for the year. However, PAT growth did not translate to profit growth.
- **Working Capital:**
  - **Receivables Days:** Increased slightly (receivables up ₹19M on higher revenue).
  - **Inventory Days:** Increased (inventory up ₹334M). **Red flag** – inventory buildup amidst flat profit suggests demand/supply mismatch or pricing pressure.
- **ROCE/ROE Trend:**
  - **ROCE:** Down from 20.95% to **14.18%**. Significant deterioration.
  - **ROE:** Down from 21.99% to **14.21%**. **Deteriorating** primarily due to equity base expansion (QIP) and flat profits.
- **Debt Position:**
  - **Net Debt:** Total borrowings ₹1,313M + Lease Liabilities ₹527M - Cash & equivalents ₹862M = **Net Debt ~₹978M**.
  - **D/E Ratio:** **0.12x** (Very low, healthy).
  - **Interest Coverage:** EBITDA/Finance Cost = 251.5/12.9 = **~19.5x**. Very comfortable.

#### **FORENSIC FLAGS**
- **Related Party Transactions:** Primarily dividends paid to promoters and salary payments. Sale of bakery business/assets to/from subsidiaries noted. Transactions stated to be at arm's length. No material red flags disclosed.
- **Contingent Liabilities vs Net Worth:** Total contingent liabilities (tax/GST disputes) **₹247.57M** + Custom duty risk **₹125.46M** = ~₹373M. **Net Worth (Consolidated): ₹11,658M**. Ratio = **3.2%**. **Below 10% threshold**, but GST dispute (₹65.11M) and rising custom duty exposure warrant monitoring.
- **Miscellaneous Expenses % of Revenue:** **₹95.39M / ₹18,739M = 0.51%**. **Well below 3% threshold**. Clean.
- **Auditor Opinion:** **Unqualified (Clean)**. No qualifications. Key Audit Matter: Revenue recognition (standard).
- **CARO Report:** **No qualifications**. Notes on: 1) Title deed for Dhar land mortgaged, confirmed by lender. 2) Goods-in-transit not physically verified but confirmed via records. 3) No fraud reported. 4) No loans to directors/parties under Section 185/186. **Nothing concerning.**
- **Accounting Policy Changes:** No material changes noted. Policies appear consistent and conservative.

#### **GOVERNANCE**
- **Management Remuneration vs Revenue:** Total KMP remuneration (₹186M) / Revenue (₹18,739M) = **~1.0%**. **Reasonable.**
- **KMP Changes:** CFO (Arnav Jain) resigned May 2025; replaced by WTD Parveen Kumar Goel. One Independent Director (Alok Kumar Misra) resigned, replaced by Dinesh Kumar Sindwani. **Routine changes, no red flags.**
- **Promoter Pledging/Stake Change:** Promoter holding decreased from 51.22% to **49.08%** due to QIP dilution. **No pledging reported.** Positive.

### **FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: AVERAGE (Green/Yellow)**

**Checklist Summary:**
- **Green Flags:** Clean audit, low debt, strong interest coverage, good cash flow generation, reasonable remuneration, no pledging.
- **Yellow Flags:** **Profitability under pressure** (flat PAT, contracting margins), **inventory buildup**, **deteriorating ROCE/ROE** due to equity dilution, contingent liabilities present (GST, customs), capex execution pending (Kolkata, Khopoli).
- **Red Flags:** None critical.

**Conclusion:** Mrs. Bectors demonstrates solid operational execution on the revenue front and maintains a healthy balance sheet. However, **FY25 was a year where profit quality and return ratios deteriorated** due to cost/inflation pressures and post-QIP equity dilution. The key watch item is the conversion of aggressive capex into profitable growth and the resolution of contingent liabilities. Governance and accounting appear above board. **Accounting Quality: Average** – good transparency, but underlying business profitability and capital efficiency require improvement.

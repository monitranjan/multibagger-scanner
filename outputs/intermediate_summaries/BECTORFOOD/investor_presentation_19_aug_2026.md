### Extraction of Critical Data from Mrs. Bectors Food Specialities Limited Investor Presentation (Q1 FY27)

#### Core Product Segments & Launches
*   **Core Segments:** Two primary segments:
    *   **Biscuit Segment:** Includes Domestic, Exports, and CSD.
    *   **Bakery Segment:** Includes Retail and Institutional sales.
*   **Recent Product Launches:**
    *   **Cremica:** Launched Danish Butter Cookies Tin, Choco Bico, Fruity Fun (magic).
    *   **English Oven:** Launched Bunfills (sweet baked goods) and promoted Frozen Desserts.
    *   No new fragrances, elixirs, or active-ingredient cosmetics are mentioned. The company is exclusively in the baked goods sector.

#### Quantitative Metrics
*   **Growth Trajectory (Consolidated):**
    *   **Q1 FY27 vs. Q1 FY26:** Net Revenue +16.0% to ₹548.7 Cr; EBITDA +23.8% to ₹72.1 Cr; PAT +25.5% to ₹38.8 Cr.
    *   **Q1 FY27 vs. Q1 FY25 (24 months):** Net Revenue +24.9%; EBITDA +12.6%; PAT +9.4%.
    *   **Annual Growth (FY23 to FY26):** Revenue from Operations CAGR of 9.1% (₹1,362 Cr to ₹2,044 Cr).
*   **Segment Revenue Contribution (FY26):**
    *   Biscuit Segment: ₹1,235 Cr (60.4% of Total Revenue).
    *   Bakery Segment: ₹769 Cr (37.6% of Total Revenue).
    *   *Total Revenue includes contract manufacturing and other operating revenues.*
*   **Margin Contribution (FY26):**
    *   Gross Profit Margin: 45.2%.
    *   EBITDA Margin: 12.6% (₹257.7 Cr).
    *   PAT Margin: 6.9% (₹140.9 Cr).
*   **Capacity Utilization (FY26):**
    *   Biscuits: Peak 69%, Average 60%.
    *   Bakery: Peak 82%, Average 75%.
*   **Other Metrics:** No data provided on market shares, handler shares, or category ARR.

#### Future Growth Adjacencies
*   **No details provided** on fragrance entry, oral beauty/nutrition M&A, or offline channel additions.
*   **Discussed Adjacencies (Operational):**
    *   **Capacity Expansion:** Commissioned new plants in Dhar (MP), Kolkata, and Khopoli (MH). Planned expansion in Bengaluru.
    *   **Product Line Extensions:** Launch of Bunfills and promotion of Frozen Desserts within the Bakery segment.
    *   **Market Expansion:** Marketing initiatives for English Oven in Kolkata and Tier 2 towns in North India.

- 🟢 Revenue: ₹1,362cr (FY23) → ₹1,624cr → ₹1,874cr → ₹2,044cr (FY26) — steady 20% CAGR
- 🟡 PAT: ₹90cr → ₹140cr → ₹143cr → ₹141cr — **plateaued/dipped** despite top-line growth

**EBITDA Margin Trend:**
- 🟡 12.9% (FY23) → 14.9% → 13.4% → **12.6% (FY26)** — contracting for 2nd straight year (input costs + commissioning drag)

**ROCE / ROE:**

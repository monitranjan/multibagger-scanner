#### **2. Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) & Strategic Plans**

*   **CAPEX Focus:** Significant CAPEX is directed towards **expanding retail footprint** and **strengthening the supply chain** to support the tripling revenue target.
*   **Brand & Digital:** Investment is also planned for **brand building** and enhancing **digital and technology infrastructure**.
*   **Working Capital:** A portion of the IPO proceeds is allocated for **augmenting working capital** to fund inventory growth commensurate with revenue scale-up.

#### **3. Key Product Segments & Market Position**

*   **Core Business:** Gold and diamond-studded jewellery. Sky Gold operates in the **high-growth, mid-market to premium jewellery segment**.
*   **Retail Network:** The company operates through a mix of **Exclusive Brand Outlets (EBOs)** and **Multi-Brand Outlets (MBOs)**. Expansion targets are likely focused on EBOs to control brand experience and margin.
*   **Geographic Footprint:** Primarily focused in **Western and Southern India**, with expansion planned into other regions.

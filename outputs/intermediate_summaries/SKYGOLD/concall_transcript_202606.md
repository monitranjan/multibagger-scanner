**Date:** August 10, 2026 | **Scrip Code:** 541967 (BSE) / SKYGOLD (NSE)

## 1. MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY

### Key Statements from Leadership

**Mr. Mangesh Chauhan (Managing Director):**

- **Revenue Target Revision:** FY27 revenue target of INR 8,100 crores is "meaningfully higher than the outlook we had communicated 9 months ago." Upward revision reflects "expansion of capabilities, deeper customer relationships and significant opportunities."
- **Business Identity Shift:** "We increasingly see ourselves as a design-led company rather than just a B2B jewelry manufacturer." Focus extends beyond manufacturing to "create compelling, market-relevant jewelry concepts."
- **Collaborative Model:** Works alongside retailers as a "product development partner" — studies product mix, competitive environment, consumer preferences, creates themes, develops prototypes, and collaborates until products are finalized for launch.
- **Advance Gold Model:** Achieved 17% sales on Advance Gold model, "significantly ahead of our expectation of 15% of the Advance Gold model for FY27."
- **Working Capital:** Closing Q1 at approximately 60 days of net working capital cycle. "Many questioned whether reducing working capital below 60 days was achievable. Today, we are demonstrating that it is not only achievable, but also sustainable."
- **Natural Diamonds:** Currently contribute approximately 2% of overall business, "presenting significant headroom for future growth."
- **Gold Price Hedging:** "We are not into the business of speculating on gold prices, nor do we depend on gold price appreciation to drive profitability." Strict back-to-back hedging policy.
- **Operating Cash Flow:** Generated approximately INR 30 crores of operating cash flow in Q1 FY27, "marking an important milestone."
- **International Business:** Participated in Asiana U.K. India Jewellery Expo in London; built prospective order pipeline of approximately INR 30–45 crores across U.K. and European markets.
- **Corporate Governance:** Financial statements now audited by MSKA & Associates LLP (BDO in India). Promoters adopting zero-salary compensation model from FY27; remuneration linked entirely to dividends declared from operating cash flow.
- **Vision 2030 / Sky Gold 3.0:** Aspiration to be net debt-free, maintain industry-leading working capital metrics, consistently convert earnings into sustainable operating cash flows, and implement world-class ERP platforms.
- **Revenue Guidance:** INR 8,100 crores by FY27; INR 18,000–19,000 crores by FY30.
- **Capacity:** 1.2 tons capacity; approximately 60% utilization. Room for 40% expansion. Can build a new facility in 90–120 days on a rental/lease model.
- **Promoter Commitment:** If a sale process (property) were not completed within the stipulated timeframe, the promoters themselves would purchase the property. "That commitment remains fully intact."
- **New CEO Appointment:** Welcomed Mr. Akash Talesara as CEO, with over 2 decades of experience in gems and jewelry industry. Targets set for him: Advance Gold business (15% average this year, 20% next year, 25% third year, 30% by 2030), Europe market expansion with lab-grown diamond, and 3–5% from U.K. market.
- **Client Base:** Serves top 50 large customers of the sector. Added Gen Z brands (CaratLane, Candere, GIVA). Increasing wallet share in P.N. Gadgil, CaratLane.
- **Unorganized to Organized Shift:** Currently 40% organized / 60% unorganized on B2C side; 20% organized / 80% unorganized on B2B side. By 2030, expects 75% organized / 25% unorganized.
- **No Acquisition Plans:** "As of now, there is no such anything on the table for acquisition." Revenue target of INR 18,000–19,000 crores does not include any potential acquisition.
- **Advance Gold Revenue Exclusion:** Advance Gold business revenue is not counted in the INR 18,000 crores guidance. If 30% Advance Gold, it would be a INR 27,000 crores business, but INR 9,000 crores cannot be counted because it does not come into revenue (only making charges are booked).

**Mr. Siddharth Sipani (CFO):**

- **Q1 FY27 Consolidated Revenue:** INR 2,013 crores, registering 78% year-on-year growth. Annualized run rate of approximately INR 8,050 crores, "already very close to our FY27 revenue guidance of INR 8,100 crores."
- **Gross Margin Improvement:** Consolidated gross margin increased to 9.3% in Q1 FY27 vs. 9.1% in Q4 FY26 (27 bps improvement). Driven by:
  - Non-22 KT jewelry share increased from 10.5% (Q4 FY26) to 14% (Q1 FY27).
  - Studded jewelry share increased from 1.65% (Q4 FY26) to 2.1% (Q1 FY27).
- **EBITDA:** INR 157 crores, 7.8% for the quarter.
- **Operating PAT:** Crossed INR 100 crores mark for the first time.
- **Gross Margin Breakup (9.3%):**
  - Advance Gold: 90–100 bps contribution to revenue and gross margin.
  - Non-22 KT (18 KT, 14 KT, 9 KT): 14% of volume, 1.4–1.5% gross margin contribution.
  - Studded jewelry (natural + lab-grown): 2% of revenues, 0.3% gross margin contribution.
  - 22 KT: 6–6.5% gross margin; 70% CZ-studded, balance plain gold at 5–5.5%.
- **Cash Flows:** Operating cash flow positive at close to INR 30 crores in Q1 FY27 vs. negative operating cash flows till end of FY26. Free cash flow also positive after excluding capex.
- **Working Capital:** Net working capital days at 60 days (vs. 59 days in March). Target to reduce by 2 days year-on-year, reaching 52 days by 2030.
- **Net Debt:** INR 540 crores as of end of quarter.
- **Capacity Utilization:** Close to 60% (blended rate across standalone and subsidiaries). Standalone at 55–65%. Expect 7–8% Q-on-Q improvement.
- **CAPEX:** Asset-light model. Current capacity sufficient till 2028. Post-2028, may look at new facility on leased model. Capex of INR 80–100 crores (20–25% of PAT at that point). Lead time of 120 days to develop factory from 1.10 tons to 2.4 tons.
- **Volume Growth:** Grew by 7–9% in volumes in Q1. Volume guidance discontinued from Q4 FY26 due to shift to non-22 KT products.
- **FY28 Guidance:** Not yet provided. Plans to give estimate post-September. Expects 30–35% sales increase. Operating cash flow to PAT conversion target of 20%. PAT margin target of 5%.
- **PAT Margin Guidance:** 5.25% long-term guidance still holds. Guidance prepared at end of 2025, projecting 5 years forward. "You can take it on that note" that guidance is on the safer side with high probability of improvement.
- **Gross Margin Guidance:** 8.5–9% range. Focus on diamond to expand to 10%, Advance Gold will improve margin, but guidance remains 8.5–9%.
- **ROCE:** Close to 35–36%.
- **Advance Gold ROCE:** Described as having "infinite ROCE" — revenue percolates down to gross margin level, adds to cash flow positive objective and PAT.
- **Payable Days:** Rising due to shift to higher-margin products (diamond segment has market practice of credit, while gold has virtually nil credit). Sustainable going forward.
- **Incident (July 16 disclosure):** INR 10.7 crores total amount involved. INR 3.5 crores already recovered. Legal proceedings initiated with favorable order. Balance recovery efforts underway. Matter under legal/investigative process; no further comment.
- **Client Onboarding:** CEO Akash onboarded all large customers not in client list in 15–18 months (anticipated 24–36 months). Strategy now is cross-selling and upselling through studded business.
- **Gen Z Brands:** GIVA, CaratLane, Candere — seeing "drastic sales" from these brands in last 2 years. Previously not counted.

## 3. FOCUS AREAS

### Product Launches / Product Mix
- **Advance Gold Model:** 17% of sales in Q1 FY27 (ahead of 15% target). Targets: 15% average this year, 20% next year, 25% third year, 30% by 2030. Revenue from Advance Gold is excluded from INR 18,000 crores guidance (only making charges booked).
- **Non-22 KT Jewelry:** Share increased from 10.5% (Q4 FY26) to 14% (Q1 FY27). Lower KT products (18 KT, 14 KT, 9 KT) gaining market share due to gold price appreciation.
- **Studded Jewelry:** Share increased from 1.65% (Q4 FY26) to 2.1% (Q1 FY27). Natural and lab-grown studded jewelry at 2% of revenues. Management focus on better-margin products.
- **Natural Diamonds:** Currently ~2% of business; significant headroom for growth.
- **Lab-Grown Diamonds:** Expanding presence; Europe market focus on lab-grown and natural diamond studded jewelry.
- **CZ-Studded Jewelry:** 70% of 22 KT products are CZ-studded.
- **Design-Led Approach:** 3D printing, laser cutting, stamping, lightweight casted jewelry manufacturing. Dedicated product development and diamond design studios. Team doubled in size.
- **Entry-Level Pricing:** Management addresses gold price impact on entry-level pricing through lighter inventory, lower caratage, and design innovation. Customer feedback available on YouTube channel.

### Channel Margins
- **Gross Margin:** 9.3% in Q1 FY27 (up from 9.1% in Q4 FY26). Guidance of 8.5–9%.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 7.8% in Q1 FY27.
- **PAT Margin:** ~5.2% in Q1 FY27. Long-term guidance of 5.25%.
- **Margin Drivers:** Advance Gold (90–100 bps), non-22 KT (1.4–1.5%), studded (0.3%), 22 KT (6–6.5%).
- **Export Mix:** Exports at ~18% of revenue (up from 14.5% in previous quarter). Target 20% exports in coming years. Diversified: 6% UAE, 2% Singapore, 2% Malaysia, 3–4% expected from Europe.
- **Gold Hedging:** Strict back-to-back hedging policy; profitability not dependent on gold price movements.

### Inventory Levels
- **Working Capital Cycle:** 60 days in Q1 FY27 (vs. 59 days in March). Target to reduce by 2 days year-on-year, reaching 52 days by 2030.
- **Inventory Days Increase:** Sequential increase attributed to:
  - Shift to higher-margin studded business (higher working capital requirement).
  - Export business increase (14.5% → 18%).
  - Deployment of gold business cash flows into studded business.
- **Payable Days:** Rising due to shift to diamond segment (market practice of credit in diamonds vs. nil credit in gold). Management states this is sustainable.
- **1-Day Improvement Impact:** 1-day improvement in operating cycle leads to ~INR 95 crores impact in operating cash flow cycle.

### Project Neev
- **Not mentioned in the transcript.** No references to "Project Neev" found.

### Direct Distribution
- **Not explicitly mentioned.** No references to "direct distribution" as a named initiative. The company operates as a B2B jewelry manufacturer and works with retail partners. The collaborative product development model is described, but no specific "direct distribution" strategy is discussed.

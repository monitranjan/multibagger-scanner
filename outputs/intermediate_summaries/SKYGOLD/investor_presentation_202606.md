## 1. CORE PRODUCT SEGMENTS & LAUNCHES

### Product Segments (as referenced in presentation)
- **Lightweight Gold Jewellery** — Core segment; market leadership position in 22kt lightweight casting jewellery
- **Lower-kt Jewellery** — Strategic shift toward lower-kt (18kt, 9kt) jewellery mix (FY30 growth lever)
- **Diamond-Studded Jewellery** — Natural diamonds currently ~2% of contribution; objective to double this in coming years
- **Lab-Grown Diamond Jewellery** — Scaling presence; described as "sunrise industry with strong long-term demand potential"
- **Advance Gold Business** — Client-supplied gold model; customer provides gold upfront, company bills only making charges; eliminates inventory risk, reduces capital intensity
- **Value-Added Products** — Share surged from <10% in FY23 to ~50-55% in FY26; primary driver of gross margin expansion
- **Co-Creation Products** — Mentioned as a margin expansion lever
- **Bangles** — Entry via acquisition of Speed Bangle Pvt. Ltd. (erstwhile Ganna n Gold) in 2025
- **Mangalsutra & Chains** — Acquisition of Star Mangalsutra & Sparkling Chains in 2024
- **Shri Rishab Gold** — 51% partnership interest acquired through Starmangalsutra in 2025

### Sub-Brands (14 identified)
- Rangi, Marisa, Saathiya, Sovana, Misha, Shaan, Tazim, Zenna, Kimora, Shai, Rose, Atiriya, Aasma, Sky9 Diamonds, Morni

### Product Launches / Events
- **IIJS Bharat 2026**: Launch of an Exclusive Limited-Edition Commemorative Pen Honoring Hon'ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi
- **Asiana UK–India Jewellery Expo, London**: Showcased product portfolio; generated initial order book of **INR 30-45 Cr** from UK and European buyers

### Design & Manufacturing Capabilities
- **Design talent pool**: ~180 professionals (doubled over last 3 years)
- **Design catalogue**: 9 lac+ (900,000+) unique designs
- **Manufacturing facility**: 1,50,000 sq ft (1,35,000 sq ft mentioned in FY30 vision context)
- **Manufacturing capacity**: 14.4 tonnes per year
- **Lead time**: 7-20 days from design to finished product
- **Technology**: 3D printing machines from Germany, Italy & the United States; replication across casting, electro-forming, stamping
- **Gold loss reduction**: From 1.5% to 0.5% via ERP implementation
- **Product weight advantage**: 10-20% lower weight with superior finish
- **Retail reach**: 2,000 retail outlets across India & 500+ outlets globally
- **Employees**: 1,500 (including 180 designers)

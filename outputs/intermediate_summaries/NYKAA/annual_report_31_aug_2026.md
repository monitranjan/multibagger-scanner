# **NYKAA (FSN E-Commerce Ventures) – Annual Report FY26 Forensic Analysis**

---

## **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key Themes:** Milestone of ₹10,000 cr revenue; expanding profitability (EBITDA +59% YoY, PAT +183% YoY); focus on profitable growth, operating leverage, and long-term value creation.
- **Stated Opportunities:** India's premium consumption cycle; convergence of beauty & wellness; fashion premiumization; expansion of owned brands (House of Nykaa); AI-led personalization.
- **Stated Risks:** Competitive intensity across e-commerce/retail; rapidly evolving consumer behavior; geopolitical and supply chain disruptions; AI-led discovery shift (risk to discoverability).
- **Strategic Priorities:** Customer acquisition/retention and premiumisation; deep brand relationships; penetration of value chain/channels (B2B via Superstore); scaling owned brands (target ₹5,000 cr NSV by FY30); AI as foundational layer.
- **Walk-the-Talk Check:** ✅ Revenue guidance (implied >25% growth) delivered at 26% YoY. EBITDA margin expansion to 7.5% (up 155 bps) exceeded implied trajectory. ROCE jump to 21.2% from 11.3% shows strong capital efficiency improvement. **Verdict: Management delivered on or above stated financial ambitions.**

## **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
- **Core Segments & Revenue Mix:** Beauty (91.2% of revenue), Fashion (8.3%), Others/International (0.5%). GMV mix: Beauty ~75%, Fashion ~25%.
- **New Additions:**
    - **Products:** 1,500+ new brands launched in FY26 (highest ever). Key luxury launches: Chanel Beauty, Armani Beauty, Kylie Cosmetics. Expanded owned brand portfolio to 13 brands.
    - **Geographies:** Expanded physical retail to 313 stores across 99 cities (76 new stores added). Deepened B2B (Superstore) to ~500k retailers in 1,200 cities. International via Nysaa in GCC (2 stores).
- **Capex vs. Planned:** Total capex (excl. ROU) ₹146 cr (1.4% of revenue), focused on store expansion (76 new) and tech infrastructure. **Execution appears on track with strategic priorities.**

## **FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK**
- **Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y):**
    - Revenue CAGR: ~26% (₹7,950 cr FY25 → ₹10,022 cr FY26).
    - PAT CAGR: Explosive growth from ₹72 cr (FY25) to ₹204 cr (FY26), +183% YoY.
- **EBITDA Margin Trend:** Expanding – from 6.0% (FY25) to 7.5% (FY26). Driven by gross margin improvement (+132 bps) and operating leverage.
- **CFO vs. PAT:** ✅ **Strong conversion.** Operating Cash Flow ₹644 cr (FY26) vs. Consolidated PAT ₹204 cr. CFO is 3.15x PAT, indicating high-quality earnings.
- **Working Capital Movement:** Operating cycle improved significantly – reduced to 28 days from 34 days (FY25). Inventory and receivables days improved.
- **ROCE/ROE Trend:** ✅ **Markedly improving.** ROCE jumped to 21.2% (from 11.3%). RONW improved to 14.3% (from 5.6%).
- **Debt Position:**
    - Net Debt: ₹329 cr (consolidated), nearly halved from ₹623 cr (FY25).
    - D/E Ratio (Net Debt/Equity): ~0.2x (very low).
    - Interest Coverage: 6.3x (consolidated EBITDA/Finance cost) – comfortable.

## **FORENSIC FLAGS**
- **Related Party Transactions (RPT):** ✅ **Green Flag.** All RPTs (with subsidiaries, promoters, promoter entities) stated to be at arm's length. Significant inter-company transactions exist (loans, purchases, sales) but are disclosed and fall within omnibus approval framework. No material, non-arm's-length transactions flagged.
- **Contingent Liabilities vs. Networth:**
    - Contingent Liabilities: ₹13.58 cr (includes disputed tax demands).
    - Networth (Consolidated): ₹1,502 cr.
    - Ratio: **<1%** – Well within safe threshold. **Green Flag.**
- **Miscellaneous Expenses as % of Revenue:** Consolidated "miscellaneous expenses" (Note 40) are ₹11.23 cr, which is ~0.1% of revenue. **Green Flag.** (<3% threshold).
- **Auditor Opinion:** ✅ **Unmodified/Unqualified.** Both Standalone and Consolidated financial statements received clean opinions from S.R. Batliboi & Associates LLP.
- **Key Audit Matters (KAM):**
    - **Impairment of investments in subsidiaries/loans (Standalone):** High judgment area involving DCF models. **Yellow Flag** – requires monitoring but auditors involved specialists.
    - **Revenue Recognition (Consolidated):** Due to system complexity and discount dynamics. **Yellow Flag** – standard for e-commerce but requires robust controls.
    - **Inventory Valuation (Consolidated):** Significant balance (₹1,642 cr) with obsolescence provision. **Yellow Flag** – high inventory business; provision adequacy is key.
    - **Deferred Tax Assets (Consolidated):** ₹269 cr DTA recognized based on future profit projections. **Yellow Flag** – hinges on continued profitability.
- **CARO Report:** **Qualifications noted on Clause 3(iii)(e) (Standalone) and Clause 3(xi)(a) & (xi)(b) (subsidiaries Nykaa E-Retail & Dot & Key)** – pertain to **audit trail deficiencies in accounting software.** This is a notable **Red Flag** regarding internal control environment.
- **Accounting Policy Changes:** No significant policy changes noted. Accounting for put options (Dot & Key) and business combinations (Earth Rhythm) are standard but complex. **Green Flag** from an accounting perspective.

## **GOVERNANCE**
- **Management Remuneration:** Total key management remuneration (Falguni, Anchit, Adwaita Nayar) disclosed as ₹16.48 cr, ₹7.84 cr, ₹7.84 cr respectively. Against consolidated revenue of ₹10,022 cr, this is **~0.3% of revenue** – reasonable.
- **KMP Changes:** CFO (P. Ganesh) unchanged. CS changed from Neelabja Chakrabarty to Dr. Chetan Sharma during the year. **No concerning resignations.**
- **Promoter Pledging/Stake:** Promoter holding stable at ~52%. **No pledge mentioned.** Promoter family trusts holds majority stake.

### Dwarikesh Sugar Industries Q3 & 9M FY25 Earnings Call Transcript Analysis  

---

#### **Management Commentary Key Statements**  
1. **Vijay Banka (Managing Director):**  
   - **Financial Performance:**  
     - Q3 FY25 PBT/PAT modestly improved (₹16cr/₹11cr vs. ₹14cr/₹10cr YoY).  
     - 9M FY25 total income declined sharply (₹906cr vs. ₹1,339cr YoY), "paling in comparison" to prior year.  
   - **Operational Updates:**  
     - Sugar volumes rose slightly in Q3 (4.99L quintals vs. 4.94L YoY) but fell significantly in 9M (17.71L vs. 21.83L quintals YoY).  
     - Industrial alcohol sales recovered in Q3 (14,958 kl vs. 14,172 kl YoY) but plunged 58% in 9M due to distillery inactivity earlier in the year.  
   - **Macro Challenges:**  
     - Red-rot disease devastated sugarcane yields/recovery in UP, impacting crushing volumes and ethanol output.  
     - Varietal mix shift underway: 36–38% non-CO-0238 in Bareilly; zero CO-0238 expected next season.  
     - SAP revision "not expected" due to delays.  

2. **Ethanol & Export Strategy:**  
   - Distilleries running on sugarcane juice; B-heavy molasses stored for off-season ethanol production.  
   - Export quota (10,444 MT) will be swapped with coastal mills for domestic quota + premium (~₹4,000/MT differential).  
   - Ethanol procurement price increase "on the anvil" (likely within a month).  

3. **Cost & Liquidity:**  
   - Current sugar inventory valuation: ₹3,764/quintal (higher due to lower recovery + elevated SAP).  
   - No cash surplus; credit limits marginally utilized for working capital.  

---

#### **Tone, Clarity, and Confidence**  
- **Tone:** Cautiously optimistic on exports/ethanol prices but realistic about near-term challenges (red-rot, lower crushing).  
- **Clarity:** High transparency on operational issues (varietal replacement timeline, export swap mechanics).  
- **Confidence:**  
  - **Strong:** Exports will boost cash flows/prices; 2025-26 season recovery assured.  
  - **Deferred:** Q3 inventory costs vs. prior year, exact cash figures (promised follow-up).  

---

#### **Focus Areas**  
1. **Product Launches:**  
   - No new launches; focus on reviving sugarcane crushing and ethanol from juice/B-heavy molasses.  
2. **Channel Margins:**  
   - Export swaps yield ~₹4,000/MT premium over domestic sales.  
3. **Inventory Levels:**  
   - 8.27L quintals sugar stocked (Dec 2024) vs. 7.71L quintals (Dec 2023).  
   - Holding costs elevated due to higher SAP + lower recovery.  
4. **Project Neev (Varietal Mix):**  
   - Replacing CO-0238 with disease-resistant varieties (118, 15023, 14021).  
   - Expect "impressive" volumes by 2025-26.  

---

#### **Future Outlook & Guidance**  
1. **Margins:**  
   - **EBITDA Drivers:** Ethanol price hike (pending announcement), export premiums, lower red-rot impact.  
   - **Mix Shift:** Ethanol from B-heavy molasses remains most profitable; sugar prices (₹3,950/quintal) nearing breakeven (~₹3,850).  
   - **Seasonality:** Working capital peaks March; cash surplus July–October.  
2. **Volume Growth:**  
   - 2024-25 crushing to mirror 2023-24 (268L quintals); 2025-26 to rebound but below 2022-23 peak (401L quintals).  
3. **CAPEX/M&A:**  
   - No CAPEX/M&A discussed; focus on optimizing existing distillery capacity.

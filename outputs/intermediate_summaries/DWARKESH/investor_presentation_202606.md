### Future Growth Adjacencies & Outlook
*   **Ethanol Blending Program:** Government's program at ~20% blending; long-term potential for higher blends and flex-fuel vehicles. *Challenge:* Lack of price revision for ethanol from sugarcane juice/B-Heavy molasses and preferential grain-based ethanol offtake.
*   **Sugarcane Availability (Key Growth Driver):** Improved varietal mix (replacement of red rot-affected Co 0238) and satisfactory crop development in Uttar Pradesh. *Dependency:* Weather conditions post-September.
*   **Price Outlook:** Firm domestic sugar prices (₹4,200–4,300/quintal) expected near-term due to tighter demand-supply balance. Ex-factory prices improved in Q1 FY27.
*   **Risks:** Potential El Niño weather pattern could tighten domestic sugar balance, especially in Maharashtra and southern states. Lower production expected for Sugar Season 2026-27.

### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements from CEO, CFO, Leadership)

**Krishna Bodanapu (Executive Vice Chairman & Managing Director):**
- **Semiconductors:** Services business growing (won deal from Semiconductor Complex of India to upgrade Mohali fab). Custom ASIC pipeline >$100M. Launched 7 new GaN-powered chips leveraging Navitas technology. Acquired Kinetic Technologies (Q1 first consolidated quarter; combined revenue $17.9M; organic semi business grew 5% QoQ to $7.5M, 5th consecutive quarter >5% organic growth). High-power ASSP developed organically, consuming cash flow, leading to lower operating profitability (R&D done, development underway).
- **Financing:** Closed $30M fresh capital (debt+equity) at $500M post-money valuation with EAAA.
- **DLM:** Q1 strong; highest ever order book, book-to-bill >1.5, sustained double-digit EBITDA margins for 4 consecutive quarters.
- **Buyback:** Extinguished 6.4M shares at Rs 1,125 (Rs 720 Cr, 5.76% paid-up); promoters did not participate.
- **Investor Day:** Planned Aug 25, 2026, Mumbai.

**Sukamal Banerjee (Executive Director & CEO):**
- **Strategy:** Lifecycle engineering opens ~20x market vs $80-100B ER&D. Announced TAO Digital Solutions acquisition. Launched Agentic AI-driven MRO platform at Farnborough Airshow.
- **DET Performance:** Satisfactory except one market sharp reversal. Overall CC degrowth -0.5% QoQ (Rupee +2.7% QoQ). EBIT 13.2% (+79bps QoQ normalized) despite higher restructuring costs.
- **Orders:** Intake +5.3% YoY; new EN/NN business +64% YoY, +49% QoQ. 5 large deals won in Q4FY26+Q1FY27; 9 large deals qualified in Q1 (>$300M pipeline).
- **EBIT Goal:** 15% EBIT exit for FY27 delayed beyond Q4FY27 (revenue ramp behind; cost side on track). Decided not to cut critical investments.
- **Segments:** Transportation & Mobility +3% QoQ CC, +14.8% YoY CC (5th consecutive growth). Network/Infra rebounded (+0.3% QoQ CC). Strategic Units -8.2% QoQ (energy contraction); confident of comparable results in 2-3 quarters after go-to-market rebuild.
- **Leadership:** Hired Baskar Nagarajan (Avionics) and Rahul Sarkar (Alliances).

**Shrinivas Kulkarni (Chief Financial Officer):**
- **Reporting:** Kinetic included in Semi segment. TAO closing by Aug 2026 ($1.4M transaction cost normalized).
- **DET Numbers:** Revenue $162.5M (-0.5% seq CC; INR Rs 1540 Cr +2.7% QoQ). Gross margin redefined (project-linked costs reclassified); DET gross margin 32.7% (-13bps seq, +127bps YoY). EBIT 13.2% normalized (+79bps seq). PAT normalized Rs 141 Cr (-13.5% YoY due to low other income). ETR 29.2% (expect 27-28% full year).
- **Cash/Capex:** FCF Rs 114 Cr (80.5% conversion); capex higher due to IT system refresh and project ramp-up.
- **Group:** Revenue $219M (+4.5% seq CC); Group EBIT 9.7%; Group PAT normalized Rs 114 Cr.

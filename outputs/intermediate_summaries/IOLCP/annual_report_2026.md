**IOL Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Ltd (IOLCP) | FY 2025-26 Annual Report Forensic Analysis**

**MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
*   **Key Themes:** Disciplined execution in a challenging global environment; focus on operational excellence, portfolio diversification, and prudent capital allocation; strengthening manufacturing integration and backward integration; expansion in regulated markets; sustainability as a core pillar.
*   **Stated Opportunities:** Growing non-Ibuprofen API portfolio (Paracetamol, Clopidogrel, Pantoprazole); expansion into regulated markets (14 USFDA DMFs, 21 CEPs); India as a preferred global manufacturing hub; global supply chain diversification ("China-plus-one"); growth in specialty chemicals.
*   **Stated Risks:** Geopolitical uncertainties, evolving trade policies, pricing pressure, regulatory compliance costs across geographies, raw material cost volatility, environmental regulations, currency fluctuations.
*   **Strategic Priorities:** Continue portfolio diversification, expand capacity (101-acre greenfield site secured), deepen backward integration, pursue regulatory excellence, achieve sustainability targets (carbon neutrality by 2050), invest in R&D and digital transformation.
*   **Walk-the-Talk Check:** Guidance was to strengthen capabilities, enhance margins, and expand globally. **Actual Delivery:** Revenue grew 11.5% YoY, EBITDA margin expanded to 13.0% (from 11.2%), non-Ibuprofen API contribution increased, Paracetamol unit commissioned, and multiple new CEPs/DMFs secured. **Alignment: Strong.**

**BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
*   **Core Segments & Revenue Mix (FY26):** Pharmaceuticals (60%), Specialty Chemicals (40%). Geographically: Domestic (76%), Exports (24%).
*   **New Additions:**
    *   **Products:** Commercialized **Minoxidil** in API portfolio; commissioned **Triacetin** plant in Chemicals.
    *   **Capex Done:** Paracetamol Unit-11 (10,800 MTPA) commissioned. Ethyl Acetate capacity increased to 120,000 MTPA, Acetic Anhydride to 32,000 MTPA, Clopidogrel to 240 MTPA.
    *   **Pending:** 101-acre greenfield site acquisition; environmental/infrastructure approvals in progress for future expansion.
*   **Capex Execution:** Total capex ₹164 Cr (vs. ₹232 Cr prior year). Primarily for expansion/diversification (80%) and infrastructure improvement (20%). All funded via internal accruals.

**FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK**
*   **Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y):**
    *   Revenue: ₹2,184 Cr (FY22) → ₹2,217 Cr (FY23) → ₹2,133 Cr (FY24) → ₹2,079 Cr (FY25) → **₹2,319 Cr (FY26)**.
    *   PAT: ₹166 Cr (FY22) → ₹140 Cr (FY23) → ₹135 Cr (FY24) → ₹101 Cr (FY25) → **₹138 Cr (FY26)**.
    *   *Trend:* Recovery in FY26 after two years of decline.
*   **EBITDA Margin Trend:** Expanding. 12.4% (FY22) → 10.7% (FY23) → 12.1% (FY24) → 11.2% (FY25) → **13.0% (FY26)**. Driven by operational efficiencies and product mix.
*   **CFO vs. PAT (Cash Conversion):** CFO ₹214 Cr (FY26) vs. PAT ₹138 Cr. **Strong conversion** (ratio ~1.55x). Previous year CFO was ₹179 Cr vs. PAT ₹101 Cr.
*   **Working Capital Movement:**
    *   **Receivable Days:** ~95 days (₹603 Cr) vs. ~90 days (₹514 Cr). Slight increase.
    *   **Inventory Days:** ~89 days (₹371 Cr) vs. ~85 days (₹361 Cr). Marginal increase.
    *   **Payable Days:** ~91 days. Overall, **stable but elevated working capital cycle.**
*   **ROCE/ROE Trend:** **Improving.** ROCE: 7.9% (FY25) → **9.7% (FY26)**. ROE: 6.1% (FY25) → **7.9% (FY26)**. Better profitability and capital efficiency.
*   **Debt Position:** **Near debt-free.** Net Debt: Negative ₹108 Cr (Cash > Borrowings). D/E Ratio: 0.08x. Debt-to-EBITDA: 0.45x. Interest Coverage: ~20x. **Extremely strong balance sheet.**

**FORENSIC FLAGS**
*   **Related Party Transactions (RPTs):** Total purchases from promoter-group entities (NCVI, Mayadevi, NM Merch) increased to ₹132 Cr (vs. ₹101 Cr). **Flag:** Rising volume; requires monitoring for arm's length pricing and necessity. Sales to related parties: Nil.
*   **Contingent Liabilities:** ₹5.66 Cr (vs. ₹5.52 Cr). Includes a ₹2.06 Cr customs duty penalty. **Contingent Liabilities vs. Net Worth: 0.3%.** ✅ **Green Flag (Negligible).**
*   **Miscellaneous Expenses:** ₹33.48 Cr. **As % of Revenue: ~1.44%.** ✅ **Green Flag (<3%).**
*   **Auditor Opinion:** **Unqualified Opinion** on both Standalone & Consolidated Financials. No qualifications, adverse remarks, or disclaimers. ✅ **Green Flag.**
*   **Key Audit Matters:** Standard (Revenue recognition cut-off, Inventory valuation). No red flags.
*   **CARO Report:** Clean. Only observation was a 1-day delay in filing Shareholding Pattern with BSE (penalty ₹2,360). ✅ **Green Flag (Minor procedural).**
*   **Accounting Policy Changes:** Recognized **₹11.21 Cr exceptional item** for Gratuity/Compensated Absences liability due to new Labour Codes. Treated as plan amendment (expensed). Conservative approach. ✅ **Green Flag.**

**GOVERNANCE**
*   **Management Remuneration:** MD Remuneration ₹11.58 Cr (ratio to median: 283x). Total KMP remuneration (incl. sitting fees, pension) ~₹22 Cr. **As % of Revenue: ~0.95%.** Within bounds for a promoter-led firm.
*   **KMP Changes:** No changes during the year. Board reconstituted CSR & Risk Management Committees. Mr. Vikas Gupta (JMD) re-appointed for 5 years.
*   **Promoter Pledging/Stake:** Promoter stake **increased** to 57.48% (from 52.63%). **No promoter pledge reported.** ✅ **Green Flag.**

**FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: GOOD**

**Summary Rationale:** The report presents a fundamentally sound company with improving profitability, a pristine balance sheet, and strong cash generation. Accounting appears conservative (e.g., treatment of exceptional item). Key strengths are low debt, high RPT transparency (though volumes need watching), clean audit, and strong walk-the-talk on strategy. The sole **Yellow Flag** relates to the rise in RPT purchases and slightly elevated working capital cycle, which merit monitoring but do not indicate accounting distress.

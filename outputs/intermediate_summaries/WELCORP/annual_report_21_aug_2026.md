### **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
*   **Core Segments & Revenue Mix:**
    1.  **Line Pipes (HSAW, LSAW, HFIW):** Dominant segment, driven by USA and KSA.
    2.  **Ductile Iron (DI) Pipes:** For water infrastructure.
    3.  **Stainless Steel Bars, Pipes & Tubes:** Niche, value-added segment (Welspun Specialty).
    4.  **Water Storage Tanks & Plastic Pipes (Sintex):** Consumer-facing B2C/B2B segment.
    5.  **TMT Rebars:** Infrastructure & construction.
*   **New Segments/Products/Geographies Added:**
    *   **New Segments:** Stainless Steel (added 2021), TMT Rebars (added 2021).
    *   **New Products:** OPVC pipes (Bhopal plant commissioned).
    *   **New Geographies/Entities:** Welspun Pipes Company (KSA) for greenfield LSAW/DI pipes; Welspun Europe S.A. (Spain); multiple UAE holding/trading entities.
*   **Capex:**
    *   **Done:** ₹2,532 crore in FY26 (Consolidated). Includes commissioning of DI pipe capacity at Anjar and coating facility at Bhopal.
    *   **Planned:** ₹5,500 crore over 3-4 years. Key upcoming projects: Greenfield LSAW mills in KSA & USA (on track for FY27), DI Pipe plant in KSA, HFIW expansion in USA.

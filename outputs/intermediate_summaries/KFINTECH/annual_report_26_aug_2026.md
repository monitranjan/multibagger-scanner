Strengthening Leadership. 
The financial services and asset 
servicing landscape is undergoing a 
profound transformation—shaped by 
deepening global interconnectedness, 
accelerating investor participation, and 
an unmistakable shift toward digital-
first engagement. At the same time, 
regulatory expectations, governance 
standards, and operational resilience 
requirements are rising across markets. 
In this environment, scale alone is no 
longer sufficient—success demands 
agile, technology-led platforms 
that can seamlessly operate across 
geographies, adapt to evolving asset 
classes, and deliver consistent, high-
quality outcomes. This is redefining the 
competitive frontier for industry leaders.
Against this backdrop, KFintech continued to execute 
with discipline, expand with intent, and evolve with 
purpose in the FY 2025-26. We broadened our horizons 
by scaling our international footprint and deepening 
our global fund administration capabilities, supported 
by the successful integration of Ascent Fund Services. 
Our international business gained meaningful 
momentum, reflecting both market expansion and 
enhanced client relevance. At the same time, we 
strengthened our leadership position by investing in 
next-generation digital infrastructure, enriching our 
capabilities across the investor and issuer solutions 
lifecycle, and building a more diversified, future-
ready business mix. Through focused investments in 
technology, talent, and platform innovation, we are 
not only expanding our reach but also reinforcing our 
ability to lead in an increasingly complex and dynamic 
financial ecosystem.

FY 2025-26 Performance Snapshot 
Shaping the Future of Asset 
Servicing — From Strength to 
Global Leadership 
The FY 2025-26 represents a defining step in 
KFintech’s evolution as a technology-led global 
financial infrastructure platform. During the year, 
we combined disciplined execution with strategic 
ambition – delivering consistent performance 
while advancing our journey toward becoming 
a scaled, scaled and globally relevant fund 
administrator. Our focus has remained on 
building a resilient, diversified business anchored 
in AI-native technology, domain expertise, and 
operational excellence.
A key milestone in this journey was the successful 
integration of Ascent Fund Services, significantly 
strengthening our global fund administration 
capabilities and expanding our presence 
across multiple jurisdictions. This strategic 
move enhances our ability to serve global asset 
managers across asset classes while deepening 
our participation in the fast-growing international 
markets. Our international business continues to 
gain traction, supported by improving mandate 
quality, growing client confidence, and a more 
diversified revenue mix – while our domestic 
franchise remains a position of strength with 
continued market share gains and strong client 
engagement. 
As we look ahead, our priorities are firmly 
aligned with our strategic framework – Execute, 
Expand, and Evolve. We will continue to invest in 
scalable technology platforms, global delivery 
capabilities, strong governance, and deep 
domain experts to enhance our competitive 
positioning across markets. With a clear focus 
on innovation, operational resilience, and 
client-centricity, we are well-positioned to scale 
sustainably, expand market share, and play a 
leading role in shaping the future of global asset 
servicing.
Sreekanth Nadella

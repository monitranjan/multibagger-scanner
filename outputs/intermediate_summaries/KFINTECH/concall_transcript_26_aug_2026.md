### 4. FUTURE OUTLOOK & GUIDANCE

- *Mix shift:* Non-MF revenue up to 38% (from 24.5% Q1 FY26); MF fee revenue down to 55% (from 66%). Reduced dependency on market-driven business. (p11)
- *Seasonality:* “Q1 growth and seasonality for our kind of business because of lack of corporate actions in issuer solutions usually is tepid.” Q2 corporate actions expected tepid for IT services (dividends not declared) but strong elsewhere. Q4 typically better than Q1. (p7, p11)
- *Operating leverage:* Cost optimization via “automation straight through transformation”, move to open-source from enterprise tech, platform upgrades (FinEx, XAlt). “Sizable expansion on the margins” from Q2. Ascent scale from $100M+ clients gives “much higher margin profile”. (p12-14, p8)
- *EBITDA bridge components:* Ascent dilution (34.2% vs 39.4% ex) due to integration, amortization of goodwill/contracts, One Constellation capitalization. Ascent to be EBITDA accretive by end of year/early next; KFin consolidated margin convergence in 3-5 years. (p11, p15)

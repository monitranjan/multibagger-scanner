### 1. Management Commentary (CEO & CFO / Leadership)

**Ramesh Gopalan (Managing Director & Group CEO):**
- **Market Context:** U.S. healthcare orgs face margin protection, cost discipline, rising medical utilisation/costs, regulatory complexity, membership volatility. Leading payers prioritize margin recovery, stars performance, admin cost reduction.
- **Strategy & Traction:** Demand favors partners with domain expertise + scale + tech/AI. Reported traction on "managed service deals" (operational ownership, committed outcomes at lower cost) and "Sagility Synchrony" (AI-led orchestration platform connecting claims/adjustments workflows).
- **CareSeed Acquisition (Closed June 2026):** Kansas City co. (founded 2012). Delivers HEDIS reporting via platforms "Forecast" (reporting/analytics) and "Harvest" (cloud chart abstraction). CY25 rev $5.1m, 95% recurring, 31.4% EBITDA. Added 26 new client groups (each <$1m rev). Synergies: end-to-end quality ops, care-gap closure.
- **Q1 Financials:** Rev INR 19,635m ($207.8m) = 27.6% YoY / 15.2% CC. Organic (ex-CareSeed) 27.3% INR / 14.9% CC. Seq decline due to seasonality (open enrolment/AEP). Steady-state organic +5.1% QoQ. Adj EBITDA INR 4,716m (24% margin, +27.9% YoY). Adj PAT INR 2,697m (13.7% margin, +35.1% YoY). Absorbed Apr-2026 salary hikes & Karnataka/Telangana min wage hike.
- **KPIs:** Payers 89.6% / Providers 10.4% rev. Headcount 47,307. Vol attrition 28.6% (vs 38.1% Q4FY26). New wins $35.3m ACV (18 existing, 1 new logo).
- **External Recognition:** Great Place to Work #11 (India), Everest PEAK Major Contender, Avasant Leader.

**Srinivas R. Mattapalli (EVP & Group CFO – "MR"):**
- **Financial Detail:** Op cash flow INR 3,161m (70% EBITDA conversion). DSO 80 days (52 rec + 28 unbilled) vs 87 in Q4. Q4FY26 had ~$24.8m open enrolment rev not repeated.
- **EBITDA Bridge (Q4FY26 24.9% → Q1 24%):** ~40bps statutory min wage, ~50bps annual salary inc, ~100bps op leverage loss (seasonal normalization), offset by >100bps FX gain.
- **Balance Sheet:** Net debt INR 1,110m (0.06x EBITDA). Cash INR 9,674m. FCF INR 2,416m (after INR 745m FA add, INR 1,360m M&A cash). On track to fully repay debt in FY27.
- **Min Wage Quantification:** In-quarter impact INR 70m (above EBITDA). Est ~120bps FY27 EBITDA impact. One-time INR 151m past service cost (gratuity) as exceptional item.
- **Long-term:** Rev INR 47,536m (FY24) → 76,174m (TTM Jun26). Adj PAT 5,896m → 12,006m. ROCE >50%.

### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements from Leadership)

**Mr. Tarak Patel (Managing Director)**
- Company transformed from “leading Indian engineering company into one of the world's largest process equipment platforms” via acquisitions.
- Announced reorganization from geographic structures into **four global divisions**: CRT (Corrosion Resistance Tech), PPT (Process Performance Tech), HET (Heavy Engineering Tech), PST (Process Systems Tech).
- Stated customers expect “consistent technology solutions across markets”; new model creates “single global organization with common objectives.”
- Noted India pharma (Hyderabad CDMO, peptides) showing “positive shift” and “green shoots”; chemicals in India “remained flat.”
- HET business (India-led, oil/gas, petrochem, nuclear, power, Middle East) is fast-growing but has a “slightly different… margin profile.”
- On margins: “We don't want to specify anything right now… compared to previous year we definitely expect improvement.”
- Confirmed no one-off severance cost in Q1; plans to move non-critical activity to lower-cost geographies (GCC/GEC model in India, Poland).

**Mr. Gregory Gelhaus (Group CEO)**
- Described transformational year; focus on bringing “full strength of each technology to every market” and diversifying beyond traditional chem/pharma.
- Record order backlog **INR 2,289 crores (+20% YoY)**; order intake **>INR 1,007 crores**.
- Clarified ~30% of Q1 FY26 intake was multi-year; current book is largely **10–12 month execution cycles**.
- Divisional performance (see numbers in Outlook): CRT, PPT, HET, PST all detailed.
- Confirmed cross-selling improved *after* creating distinct verticals (“benefits that you don't expect”).
- Stated majority of current order intake executable in 10–12 months.

**Mr. Alexander Poempner (Group CFO)**
- Consolidated revenue **INR 925 cr (+16% YoY)**; EBITDA **INR 94 cr (-7% YoY, +25% QoQ)**; PAT **INR 22 cr (more than doubled YoY)**.
- EBITDA YoY dip due to “continued pricing pressure in a revolving business mix and investments towards strengthening our global organization.”
- Acquisition phase “largely behind us”; focus shifted to integration/synergies via common engineering and cross-selling.
- Debt: Disciplined allocation; will repay **~EUR 7 million by end Q2 FY27**; evaluating refinancing.
- EBIT-to-PAT conversion hampered by inherited intl structure (multiple legal entities, overlapping admin); simplifying to improve over “multi-quarter value creation program.”
- Interest cost guidance: **6%–7%** (SOFR/EURIBOR based, hedged). Tax rate steady-state **~30% (slightly below) in 18–24 months**.

**Mr. Ankit Nayyar (Deputy CFO)**
- Margin dip due to investing in people/new org; “upward trend going forward.”
- Objective: grow margins QoQ, 15% EBITDA aim, “all levers look good as of now.”

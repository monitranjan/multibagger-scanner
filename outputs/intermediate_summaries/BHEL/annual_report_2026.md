**BHEL Annual Report FY 2025-26: Forensic Analysis**

**MANAGEMENT LETTERS:**
- **Key Themes:** Order book growth, execution momentum, diversification beyond thermal, cash generation discipline, and strategic forays into coal gasification, rail safety (KAVACH), and defence.
- **Opportunities:** Government push for 315 GW thermal, 100 GW nuclear by 2047, 100 GW pumped storage, transmission expansion, defence indigenization, rail modernization.
- **Risks:** Geopolitical disruptions affecting supply chains, execution delays, intense competition, and technology obsolescence.
- **Strategic Priorities:** Enhancing EPC execution, diversifying order book (non-thermal share ~29%), expanding manufacturing capabilities, digital transformation, and sustainability (Net Zero by 2047).
- **Walk-the-Talk:** Management targeted higher execution and cash generation. Actual results: Revenue +19%, PAT tripled, Cash from Operations +176%. Order book at all-time high. **Execution delivered on key promises.**

**BUSINESS & STRATEGY:**
- **Core Segments:** Power (75% of revenue) & Industry (25%). Power comprises thermal, hydro, nuclear, spares & services, and new coal-to-chemicals. Industry includes transportation, transmission, defence, captive power, and renewables.
- **New/Expanded Segments:**
    - **Coal Gasification:** Secured ~₹8,288 Cr first order from BCGCL JV.
    - **Rail Signalling:** Secured maiden order for KAVACH prototype development.
    - **Track Machines:** Secured maiden orders for SPIC and RBMV.
    - **Defence:** Delivered 4 upgraded SRGMs ahead of schedule.
- **Capex:** Incurred ₹671 Cr (vs ₹536 Cr prior year) primarily for modernization of existing facilities. No major new plant commissioning disclosed. Capex funded internally.

**FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK:**
- **Revenue & PAT (3Y Trend):** Revenue: ₹20,153 Cr (FY22) → ₹23,893 Cr (FY24) → ₹33,782 Cr (FY26). PAT: ₹410 Cr (FY22) → ₹260 Cr (FY24) → ₹1,578 Cr (FY26). **Growth inflection in FY26.**
- **EBITDA Margin:** Expanding. 5.12% (FY22) → 4.90% (FY24) → 9.21% (FY26).
- **CFO vs PAT:** CFO of ₹5,837 Cr significantly exceeds PAT of ₹1,578 Cr. **Strong profit-to-cash conversion.** Collections were 143% of revenue.
- **Working Capital:**
    - **Receivable Days:** Improved to 100 days (from 115 days).
    - **Inventory Days:** Increased to 150 days (from 132 days). **Watchpoint:** Likely build-up for execution.
    - **Payable Days:** Reduced to 165 days (from 201 days), indicating faster payments to suppliers.
- **ROCE/ROE:** ROE improved to 5.98% (from 2.01%). ROCE improved to 12.77% (from 7.06%). **Deteriorating trend arrested and improving.**
- **Debt:**
    - **Net Debt:** Company is **net cash positive** (Cash & Bank: ₹11,867 Cr vs Borrowings: ₹7,950 Cr).
    - **D/E Ratio:** Not applicable (no long-term debt). Total Debt/Assets: 0.11.
    - **Interest Coverage:** Healthy (EBITDA ₹3,189 Cr / Interest ₹756 Cr ≈ 4.2x).

**FORENSIC FLAGS:**
- **Related Party Transactions:** Minimal. Sales to JVs ~₹365 Cr (1.13% of revenue). No material suspicious flows noted.
- **Contingent Liabilities:** ₹6,610 Cr vs Net Worth ₹26,516 Cr. Ratio: **24.9% (>10% FLAG)**. Mainly liquidated damages (₹3,673 Cr) and tax/litigation disputes.
- **Misc. Expenses as % of Revenue:** Other Expenses (₹1,989 Cr) + Power & Fuel (₹503 Cr) = ~7.4% of Revenue. **High (FLAG >3%).** Includes significant forex gains.
- **Auditor Opinion:** **Unqualified.** No qualifications.
- **Key Audit Matters:** Revenue recognition (Ind AS 115), recoverability of trade receivables and contract assets (₹9,223 Cr & ₹29,390 Cr), and assessment of contingent liabilities. **Standard for an EPC company; no unusual red flags.**
- **CARO Report:** No qualifications or adverse remarks.
- **Accounting Policies:** Revenue recognition uses input cost method for long-term contracts. **Policy is standard but estimates on "percentage of completion" are high-judgment.**

**GOVERNANCE:**
- **Mgmt. Remuneration as % of Revenue:** Extremely low. Total KMP remuneration ₹4.76 Cr is ~0.01% of revenue. **Typical for a Government Company.**
- **KMP Changes/Resignations:** Director (HR) changed. Two Independent Directors completed short tenures. **Board composition non-compliant (see below).**
- **Promoter Pledging/Stakes:** Promoter (GoI) stake reduced from 63.17% to 58.17% via OFS. **No pledging.**

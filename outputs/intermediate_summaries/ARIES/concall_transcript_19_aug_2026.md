# ARIES AGRO LIMITED — Chairman's Speech Analysis (AGM, 26th September 2025)

## 1. MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY

### Key Statements from Leadership (Chairman & MD, Dr. Rahul Mirchandani)

| Theme | Key Statement |
|-------|---------------|
| **Revenue Growth** | "Aries Agro delivered a 17.22% increase in gross revenue, growing from Rs. 664.03 crores to Rs. 778.35 crores, on a standalone basis" |
| **Growth Drivers** | Growth was "led by robust international sales and contributions from our units in Fujairah and our associate company Amarak Chemicals FZC, UAE" |
| **Capacity Utilization** | Domestic capacity utilization stood at **76.32%** of the 95,400 MT p.a. installed capacity; UAE plant produced **8,751 MT** of Sulphur Bentonite and other value-added Sulphur products |
| **Import Substitution** | "The most successful import substitution initiative was the manufacture of Aries HD range reducing our dependence on water soluble fertilizers imports from China" |
| **Import Reduction** | Percentage of imported raw materials reduced from **51% in 2018-19 to 18% in 2024-25** |
| **Farmer Engagement** | Engaged with over **17.9 lakh farmers** across **26 states** |
| **FY26 Revenue Target** | "We are targeting Rs. 950 crores in gross revenue for the current year" |
| **FY26 Bookings** | "Annual Booking for FY 2025-26 has already recorded bookings worth Rs. 830.44 crores from 1,717 dealers across 26 states" |
| **Monsoon Risk** | "The progress of the 2025 monsoon has been erratic… leading to delays in sowing in some states, crop stress in others, and a mixed outlook for yields overall" |
| **GST 2.0 Impact** | "The rollout of GST 2.0 has brought encouraging changes for micronutrients under the Fertilizer Control Order, especially with the reduction in GST rates that directly benefits the farming community" |
| **GST Pass-Through** | "Aries Agro has ensured that this relief is passed on to consumers by reducing MRPs proportionately across the 15 products affected by these GST changes" |
| **GST Risk Mitigation** | "The Company's diverse and balanced portfolio is expected to mitigate any significant adverse impact on GST-related cash outflows" |

## MANAGEMENT LETTERS

**Key Themes from Chairman's Letter (Dr. Rahul Mirchandani):**
- Revenue growth of 17.22% driven by international sales (Fujairah branch + Amarak Chemicals FZC)
- Heavy emphasis on digitization (AIMS, Khazaana, Aries Everywhere App, Soil Test Reporting)
- Farmer outreach as core brand strategy — 17.93 lakh farmers impacted
- New products: Aries Ecoshield, Majorsol Soybean/Pulses Special, Calmax, Zinc HD Gold
- Equipment Division expansion: Knapsack Sprayers, soil testing devices, Magic Traps
- Global expansion: Jebel Ali (UAE) facility, Lucknow manufacturing unit (groundbreaking)
- Import substitution: HD range showing 795% growth, import dependency reduced to 18% from 51% in FY19

**Stated Opportunities:**
- Favorable monsoon projections for FY26
- Annual booking of ₹830.44 Cr for FY26 (expected gross revenue ~₹950 Cr)
- Automation, warehousing, stringent inventory control
- Climate-proof products, plant protection range expansion

**Stated Risks:**
- Uncertain supply chains, price volatility
- Global conflicts affecting ocean freight and fertilizer availability
- Erratic rainfall, oil/gas price fluctuations, FX risk
- Tariffs and blockades increasing import costs

**Walk-the-Talk Check:**
- No explicit FY25 guidance was given in prior year report to compare against
- FY25 gross revenue ₹778.35 Cr vs FY24 ₹664.04 Cr — 17.22% growth delivered
- Capacity utilization improved to 76.32% from 72.61% — consistent with stated focus
- **Verdict: No prior-year guidance to benchmark, but growth trajectory is consistent with stated strategy**

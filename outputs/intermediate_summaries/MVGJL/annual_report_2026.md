**CFO vs PAT — Major Red Flag**
- PAT FY26: ₹114.98 Cr

- **CFO/PAT ratio = ~23%** — profit not converting to cash; **YELLOW/RED flag**

**Working Capital Movement**
- Inventory: ₹1,317.23 Cr → ₹1,609.87 Cr (+₹292.64 Cr / +22.2%)

- Trade payables: ₹140.68 Cr → ₹167.56 Cr (+19.1%)

**Debt Position**

## FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: **AVERAGE (with green-leaning bias)**

**Rationale:**
- ✅ **Positives:** Clean audit, no promoter pledging, strong revenue growth, healthy DSCR, conservative policies, no fraud/CARO red flags, reasonable mgmt remuneration
- ⚠️ **Concerns:** Cash conversion is the **biggest red flag** (CFO ₹26 Cr vs PAT ₹115 Cr) — entirely working capital driven. ₹30 Cr advance to promoter-related LLP warrants scrutiny. Other expenses contain unexplained ₹15 Cr jump. Margin mildly contracting. Capital commitments of ₹36.67 Cr represent ~21% of net worth — meaningful expansion risk.

**Bottom line:** Operationally sound regional jewellery retailer with clean governance optics, but FY26 shows classic signs of **growth-at-the-cost-of-cash** — investors should monitor inventory build-up, customer advance liability surge (₹125 Cr jump), and related-party advances closely in FY27.

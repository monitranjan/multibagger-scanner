## 2. Focus Areas

### A. Product Launches
- **Silver Jewellery Standalone Stores:**
  - ~10 new silver showrooms planned; 5–6 locations finalized with agreements in place.
  - Average size: ~2,000–2,500 sq. ft.
  - Silver inventory target: ~75 kg per store (up to 100 kg for larger stores).
  - Expected silver store margins: 25–30% (vs. 12–13% gross margin in gold).
  - EBITDA in silver expected to be 30–40% higher than gold stores.
  - Inventory turnover target: 1.5x initially, scaling to 2.5x over 3 years.

- **"Visesha" Premium Boutique (Ultra-premium):**
  - Single store planned in Visakhapatnam (6,000+ sq. ft.).
  - Target launch: **September 2025**.
  - Targeted Year 1 revenue: INR 150–200 Cr.
  - Compared to Polki jewellery; gross margin ~18%.
  - Inventory turnover: 1.5x (Year 1), targeting 2x in 2–2.5 years.

### B. Channel Margins
- Gold jewellery gross margin: ~12–13%.
- Silver jewellery gross margin: ~25–30% (significantly higher).
- Visesha gross margin: ~18% (compared to Polki).
- Old gold exchange: ~0.25–0.30% margin (essentially service-oriented).
- Management acknowledged **sequential and YoY dip in gross margins** in Q4, attributed to higher gold prices and need to offer discounts/schemes to drive footfalls.

### C. Inventory Levels & Mix
- Total inventory: ~INR 1,300 Cr.
- Gold: ~90%.
- Diamonds & Stones: ~5–7%.
- Others: ~2–3%.
- Average store investment: 35–40 kg of gold per store.
- Same Store Sales Growth (SSSG) FY25: **6.65%** (including new stores); **~2%** excluding Rajam & Mancherial (flagship stores opened last year).

### D. Project Neev & Direct Distribution
- **Not discussed** in this transcript. No mention of Project Neev or direct distribution initiatives.

## 3. Future Outlook & Guidance

### A. Margins (EBITDA Bridge, Mix Shift, Operating Leverage, Seasonality, ESOP)
- **Mix Shift:** Shift toward silver (higher margins) is the primary margin lever; 20–30% of customers priced out of gold targeted for silver.
- **Seasonality:** Q4 benefitted from favorable auspicious dates; management expects strong periods in:
  - April (already transpired)
  - Late July–August (1.5–2 months)
  - December 15 – January
- **Operating Leverage:** Expected from scaling of newly opened 6 stores (~INR 38 Cr in FY25 → ~INR 200 Cr in FY26 annualized).
- **ESOP Reversal:** Not discussed.
- **EBITDA Bridge:** Not explicitly broken down; management cited higher advertising allocation for silver and stable manpower/interest costs.

### B. Volume Growth
- Topline growth guidance: **~10.9% YoY** continuation (~12% targeted including annualized impact of new stores).
- Flagship store (Vizag) SSSG guidance: **6–7%**.
- New 6 stores FY26 revenue potential: ~INR 200 Cr combined.
- Flagship Vizag store annual revenue: INR 1,217 Cr (FY25) vs. INR 1,195 Cr (FY24).

### C. CAPEX
- **Total CAPEX (FY26):** ~INR 21–22 Cr (excluding inventory).
  - Silver showrooms: ~INR 15–18 Cr (avg. INR 1.5 Cr/store × 10 stores).
  - Visesha: ~INR 6 Cr additional (8,000–9,000 per sq. ft. × 6,000 sq. ft.).
- Store setup cost (regular): INR 5,000–6,000 per sq. ft.
- Visesha setup cost: INR 8,000–9,000 per sq. ft.

### D. M&A Integration
- **Fluence Pharma:** Not discussed.
- **BTM/Reginald:** Not discussed.
- No M&A activity mentioned on the call.

## 4. Q&A Dynamics

### Areas of Management Confidence
- Expansion roadmap (silver + Visesha) – detailed and specific.
- Inventory composition – clearly articulated (~90% gold).
- Competition positioning – first-mover advantage in Tier 3/4 markets, design strength, curation model.
- New store ramp-up – specific numbers provided (Rajam INR 45.59 Cr, Mancherial INR 20 Cr).

### Areas of Evasion / Vagueness
- **Dividend policy:** Management deferred ("we are working out").
- **Studded ratio:** Deferred ("will address separately").
- **SSSG bifurcation (pre-IPO flagship vs. non-flagship):** Initially given as 6.65%, then clarified as ~2% excluding new stores; response somewhat convoluted.
- **Per-invoice growth strategy:** When pressed on low invoice value growth vs. peers, COO's response shifted to silver migration strategy rather than concrete initiatives to grow gold ticket size.
- **Gold exchange margin trajectory:** When asked if margins could contract if exchange share rises to 15–20%, response was indirect (focused on multiplier effect of 2–3x purchase value).
- **Platinum opportunity:** Acknowledged but downplayed (~2–3% of silver mix) without conviction.
- **Topline growth quantification:** Initially answered by CA as "same growth as registered" — somewhat non-specific.

### Analyst Pushback Observed
- **Yash Dantewadia (Dante Equity):** Persistent questioning on per-invoice growth weakness, inventory turns, and silver economics — pushed for clarity multiple times.
- **Gaurav Agrawal (Nine One Capital):** Pressed on competitive positioning vs. national chains and making charges — got partially deflected answers.
- **Manan Shah (Moneybee):** Multi-round questioning; managed to extract some specific data points (e.g., new store revenue thresholds).

## 5. Key Takeaways

1. **Silver is the strategic pivot** – Silver jewellery standalone expansion is the central growth narrative, driven by gold price affordability pressure.
2. **Premium play via Visesha** – Single high-stakes Visakhapatnam launch targeted for September 2025.
3. **Margin pressure acknowledged** – Q4 gross margin dip explained as tactical discounting; sustainability of margins contingent on gold price stability for 6–7 months.
4. **No Project Neev, no M&A updates** – Notable absence; investor focus areas not addressed.
5. **Topline guidance implicit ~10–12%** – No formal guidance issued; management indicated continuity of FY25 growth rate plus annualized impact of new stores.
6. **CAPEX discipline** – Modest INR 21–22 Cr CAPEX ex-inventory, signaling measured expansion.

- **Core Product Segments & Launches**: 
  - Steel Wheels: Rs. 953.8 crores (~63%) of Q1 FY27 revenues; robust, fit for rugged SUVs; competitive pricing; mass vehicle acceptance; minimal maintenance (hammering bent portions); off-road/heavy duty suitability.
  - Alloy Wheels: Rs. 532.9 crores (~35%) of Q1 FY27 revenues; enhanced aesthetic appeal; higher acceptance in high-value PVs; strengths: higher strength-to-weight ratio for precise steering, corrosion-free, better heat dissipation enabling efficient braking.
  - Aluminium Knuckles: Rs. 23.0 crores (~2%) of Q1 FY27 revenues; currently working with 2 leading OEMs; capacity scaling lever for de-risking and shareholder value; benefits: weight reduction, corrosion-free, improved fuel efficiency.

    * Commenced commercialization of new Aluminium Product (Knuckles) in 2025.
    * Alloy Wheels capacity expanded to 5 Mn units and Aluminium Knuckles to 5 lacs by FY26.

    * Production start of Truck Wheels at Chennai Plant.
    * Production start of PV Alloy Wheels at Mehsana Plant with 1.5 Mn capacity.

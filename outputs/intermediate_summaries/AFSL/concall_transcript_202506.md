### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements from Leadership)
*Participants: Nirbhay Vassa (Group CFO), Ketur Shah (VP), Dhrumil Shah (VP). No CEO named in text.*

- **Nirbhay Vassa (CFO):**  
  - Q1 FY26 consolidated revenues: **INR 1,895 crores**; EBITDA: **INR 52.8 crores** (disciplined cost management); Net profit: **INR 32.69 crores, up 35% quarter-on-quarter year-on-year**.  
  - Strategy anchored on diversifying revenue, scaling fee-based businesses, compliance-driven growth.  
  - AUM reached **INR 3,500 crores** for first time this quarter.  
  - Global arbitrage fund healthy; partnerships with SMC Global and Taurus Wealth expanding reach.  
  - NBFC focused on prudent lending; awaiting regulatory clearance for other jurisdictions.  
  - Abans Global (UK) back office in GIFT City fully operational.  
  - Expects continued AUM growth, steady lending book expansion, improved operational leverage.

- **Ketur Shah (VP):**  
  - AUM via distributors crossed INR 1,000 cr (later clarified by Vassa as **INR 1,300 cr through distribution**, total 3,500 cr).  
  - Expanding horizontally (more cities/countries/partner training) and vertically (global equities, private credit funds, structured solutions).

- **Dhrumil Shah (VP):**  
  - FY26 is “year of acceleration” with 3 pillars: advisory depth, product innovation, geographic scale.  
  - Entering merchant banking space.  
  - Launching new products in structured credit and global investment via UK, Mauritius, GIFT City platforms.

CHAIRMAN’S MESSAGE
Dear Shareholders,
As DLF marks 80 years, we celebrate a defining milestone. Founded in 1946 at the dawn of India’s 
independence, our journey has closely aligned with the nation’s growth. As India advances towards its 
centenary, we reaffirm our commitment to national progress and the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047 as 
articulated by the Hon’ble Prime Minister.
For eight decades, our journey has been anchored in trust, quality and innovation. Our success would not 
have been possible without the confidence and trust shown by our customers, employees, shareholders 
and the continued support of the Government.
DLF has played a key role in transforming India’s urban landscape, creating world-class developments 
and landmarks across leading cities. With a strong legacy, robust governance and institutional strength, 
DLF is well positioned for the next phase of growth.  
Looking ahead, we see strong opportunities in a resilient and growing India. Under the leadership of 
Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi ji, the country has navigated global challenges while maintaining 
strong macroeconomic fundamentals. With continued focus on infrastructure and urbanisation, DLF stands 
committed to unlocking significant opportunities for India’s urban development.
Our commitment to inclusive growth continues through the social, educational and sustainability 
initiatives of the DLF Foundation.
I thank our Board for its guidance and remain confident in our ability to build on our legacy, seize 
opportunities and shape the future of urban India with purpose and resilience, creating lasting value for 
all stakeholders.
With best wishes,
Sincerely,
Rajiv Singh

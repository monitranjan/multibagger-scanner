### 1. Management Commentary (Key statements from leadership)
**Badal Bagri (Group CFO):**
- Reported a “resilient performance” with collections at INR 2,406 crores and operating cash flow >INR 1,300 crores; net cash position INR 15,200 crores (INR 11,000 crores in RERA/escrow).
- New sales bookings INR 657 crores, attributing the dip to “timing impact of deferment of our launch of Aureva.”
- Rental portfolio >50 million sq ft, occupancy >95% (space) / >97% (value); DLF rental revenue INR 1,605 cr, EBITDA INR 476 cr. Net profit INR 794 cr vs INR 766 cr PY.
- Stated FY28 will be “an inflection point from a reporting perspective” (Arbour etc.); gross margin potential ~INR 39,000 crores.
- DCCDL revenue INR 1,917 cr (+10% YoY), EBITDA INR 1,474 cr, PAT INR 717 cr (+20% YoY).

**Sriram Khattar (VC & MD, Rental Business):**
- Noted AI/hiring debate and Iran-U.S. war slowed global decisions from Feb; sees “green shoots” in last 4–5 weeks, expects Q2/Q3 to be good.
- Newer office projects (Cyber Park, Atrium Place, Downtown Gurgaon/Chennai) “nearly 100% leased.” Capex on Downtown Gurgaon Ph2/Chennai Ph2 “going at full speed.”
- Office rentals grew 8.5%–9% YoY; retail consumption growth 13.5%–14%.
- DCCDL PAT INR 717 cr (+20% YoY, lower than Q4 due to one-time DTA). Borrowing rate 7.14%, AAA rated.

**Aakash Ohri (MD & Chief Business Officer, Home Developers):**
- Dahlias: “biggest success,” ~65% sold, price >INR 1 lakh/sq ft (higher floors INR 120k–125k). Entry level now INR 100 cr+; 25%–30% sales from NRI/rest of India. Experience Center unveils post-Diwali.
- Privana: South/West/North sold out; next phase early next year (high-rise, no plotted scheme beyond collaborator obligations).
- Aureva (senior living) launch imminent post-RERA; Hamilton 2/Arbour 2 “on drawing board” for H2.

**Ashok Tyagi (Managing Director):**
- Land: Pursuing strategic Gurgaon parcels; INR 80 cr EMD deposited for NCR auction.
- Goa Residential: Involved in PIL litigation but “on track” for INR 20,000 cr guidance (Goa component only ~10% of guide).
- Mumbai: Follow-through launch this fiscal year, potential >5 msf; exploring selectively.
- Dahlias next phase depends on 3–3.5 years construction progress + sales; “3-year cycle” to sell out.

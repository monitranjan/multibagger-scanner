# IFCI Limited — Annual Report FY2024-25 Forensic Analysis

---

## MANAGEMENT LETTERS / KEY THEMES

**Board's Report Key Themes:**
- Company is in **transition mode** — no new loans sanctioned, no disbursements during FY24-25; focus entirely on recovery from NPAs & advisory services
- ₹580 crore recovered from NPAs & Security Receipts during the year
- 28% reduction in borrowings, backed by advisory income and recoveries
- Strategic pivot toward **advisory services** — now Project Management Advisor for 10 of 14 PLI schemes
- **Group consolidation approved** (in-principle) — merger of SHCIL, IFCI Factors, IIDL and other subsidiaries into IFCI Limited
- New MD & CEO Rahul Bhave appointed March 21, 2025 (replacing Manoj Mittal who moved to SIDBI)

**Stated Opportunities:**
- PLI schemes (cornerstone of AatmaNirbhar Bharat) — IFCI acts as nodal agency/PMA
- ESG advisory vertical launched
- MoU with BSE for SME listing support
- Group consolidation to unlock synergies

**Risks:**
- CRAR at **negative 23.04%** (vs -48.35% prior year) — below RBI threshold
- Rating constraints prevent fresh resource mobilization
- Cannot mobilize fresh borrowings due to weak financial parameters
- Massive NPAs — gross NPAs at ₹3,694 crore (~50%+ of gross advances)

**Walk-the-Talk Check:**
- No specific numeric FY guidance was given (no revenue/profit targets in letters)
- Claimed aggressive recovery focus — **delivered**: ₹580 crore recovered ✅
- Claimed borrowing reduction — **delivered**: 28% reduction ✅
- Claimed diversification into advisory — **delivered**: ₹69 crore advisory income ✅
- However, standalone PAT fell 66% YoY (₹44 crore vs ₹128 crore) — profitability deteriorated ⚠️

- Single segment: **Financing (NBFC)** — primarily corporate loans

- Dividend income: ₹111 crore (13%)

**New Segments/Geographies:**
- ESG advisory vertical added this year
- New PLI schemes added: Electric Mobility (EMPS), SMEC, PM E-Drive, IT Hardware 2.0
- No new geographic presence

**Capex:**
- Minimal capex: ₹0.15 crore on PPE (vs ₹0.32 crore prior year)
- Investment property additions: ₹44 crore (building transferred from PPE)
- Capital work-in-progress: **nil**
- Nothing significant commissioned or pending — company is in wind-down lending mode

## FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK

### Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y Trend, Standalone)

| Metric | FY25 | FY24 | FY23 (Restated) |
|--------|------|------|-----------------|
| Total Income (₹ Cr) | 842 | 896 | 755 |
| PAT (₹ Cr) | 44 | 128 | -288 |

- Revenue declining; PAT volatile — swung from deep loss to small profit
- No sustainable earnings trajectory

### EBITDA Margin Trend
- **Operating Profit Margin**: 17.6% (FY25) vs 16.6% (FY24) — slightly expanding
- But this is misleading; driven by impairment reversals and fair value gains, not operating efficiency

### CFO vs PAT (Standalone)
- PAT: ₹44 crore
- Net CFO: **-₹1,175 crore** (massive outflow)
- **Profit is NOT converting to cash** — huge cash drain from working capital changes, loan repayments
- ₹1,339 crore outflow from debt securities; ₹207 crore from loans

### Working Capital Movement
- Receivables: ₹85 crore (down from ₹104 crore) — modest improvement
- Inventory: Not applicable (NBFC)
- Bank balances other than cash: **increased** ₹836 crore — significant cash parked with banks (PLI scheme funds ₹2,144 crore)

### ROCE / ROE Trend
- **Return on Net Worth**: 1.45% (FY25) vs 9.58% (FY24) — **deteriorated sharply**
- Net Profit Margin: 2.54% (FY25) vs 9.83% (FY24)
- ROE declining due to massive equity infusion with minimal returns

### Debt Position
- **Debt-Equity Ratio**: 2.18 (improved from 4.49)
- Net debt reduced significantly due to GoI equity infusion (₹1,000 crore in two tranches)
- **Interest Coverage Ratio**: 1.69 (marginal; deteriorated from 1.84)
- CRAR: **-23.04%** — deeply negative, regulatory breach
- Principal liability: ₹3,778 crore (all Rupee borrowing)
- No foreign currency loans outstanding

## FORENSIC FLAGS

### Related Party Transactions
- **Size**: Extensive — multiple subsidiaries, associates, GoI entities
- Advisory fees from government ministries: ~₹70+ crore aggregate
- Interest paid to subsidiaries: ₹14+ crore
- IVCF share buyback: ₹38 crore
- All stated as "arm's length, ordinary course" — **YELLOW FLAG**: concentration of income from related government entities; circular flows possible
- Outstanding balances with subsidiaries: bonds issued (₹75 crore to IIDL, ₹10 crore to IVCF, ₹25 crore to SHCIL)

### Contingent Liabilities vs Networth
- **Contingent liabilities**: ₹206 crore (standalone: claims ₹113 Cr + guarantees ₹3.5 Cr + tax ₹190 Cr)
- **Net worth**: ₹1,736 crore (standalone)
- Ratio: **~12%** — **RED FLAG**: exceeds 10% threshold

### Miscellaneous Expenses
- "Other miscellaneous expenses": ₹1.49 crore vs Total revenue ₹842 crore = **0.18%** — within norms ✅
- Total "Others expenses": ₹47.5 crore = 5.6% of revenue — **YELLOW FLAG**: somewhat elevated

### Auditor Opinion
- **Standalone**: Unqualified with **Emphasis of Matter** (7 items including: consolidation approval, Stage 3 interest income write-off, ECL methodology change, negative CRAR, SDF scheme conflict of interest)
- **Consolidated**: Unqualified with **Emphasis of Matter** (multiple subsidiaries flagged — IFL going concern, MPCON litigation, SHCIL legacy issues)
- **No qualification** ✅

### Key Audit Matters
1. **ECL impairment on loans** — high estimation uncertainty
2. **Fair value of financial instruments** — derivatives and investments
3. **Valuation of investments in subsidiaries/associates** — significant
4. **IT controls** — adequate but flagged

**RED FLAG**: ECL methodology changed from portfolio-level to account-level, adding ₹291 crore provisioning — management discretion concern

### CARO Report
- Not separately provided (covered in Statutory Auditor's report as Annexures A, B, C)
- **Clause 3(xxi)**: CRAR below RBI guidelines — flagged
- **Clause 3(xi)(a)**: One borrower fraud of ₹127 crore declared
- **Clause 3(i)(b)**: Multiple shares accounted but not available in demat/physical form (8 companies)
- **YELLOW FLAG**: Multiple compliance concerns

### Accounting Policy Changes
- **ECL methodology change**: Portfolio-level → account-level basis — considered accounting estimate change (Ind AS 8)
- Investment valuations in subsidiaries based on Dec 2024 (not March 2025) financials
- **CAG observation**: 36 investments valued on older dates, not as of reporting date — no corrective action despite flagging in prior 4 years
- **YELLOW FLAG**: Stale valuations, repeated CAG observation ignored

### CAG Observations (Supplementary Audit)
- Investment in SGEL shown at Nil (should be ₹1.93 crore) — understatement
- Investment in PIL (renamed from SGAFL) overstated by ₹1.75 crore (capital reduction missed)
- SEPL valuation error: overstated by ₹1.07 crore
- **RED FLAG**: Multiple valuation errors, some recurring from prior years without correction

- Surjith Karthikeyan ceased as Government Director — July 29, 2025

### Promoter Pledging/Stake
- GoI holding: **72.57%** (increased from 70.32%)
- **No pledging** (government entity)
- Two preferential allotments of ₹500 crore each during the year — significant dilution for existing minority shareholders

### Independent Directors
- **Only 1 Independent Director** (Umesh Kumar Garg) against requirement of more
- **RED FLAG**: Non-compliance with SEBI LODR on board composition — fines imposed by NSE/BSE (~₹47 crore cumulative)
- Company has been requesting Ministry to appoint more independent directors

### Board/Committee Composition Issues
- Audit Committee not properly constituted (insufficient independent directors)
- Nomination & Remuneration Committee not properly constituted
- Secretarial auditor flagged all three as non-compliant
- **RED FLAG**: Governance structural deficiency persists

## FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: **BAD**

**Rationale:**
- Negative CRAR (regulatory breach) is a fundamental solvency red flag
- Massive NPA book (~50%+ of gross advances) with high provisioning
- CFO deeply negative despite reported profit — earnings quality poor
- Multiple CAG valuation errors repeated across years without correction
- ECL methodology change adds ₹291 crore provisioning in single year — management discretion risk
- Persistent non-compliance on governance (independent directors, committee composition) with ~₹47 crore in fines
- Company cannot raise fresh debt — dependent on government equity infusions for survival
- Strategic pivot to advisory/PLI management is early-stage and not yet compensating for loan book decay
- Group consolidation pending — adds uncertainty
- **IFCI is effectively a government-managed wound-down NBFC surviving on GoI capital support, not a going concern driven by business fundamentals**

### 1. Management Commentary (Key statements from CEO, CFO, and leadership)
**Navneet Kumar Saraf (Director & CEO):**
- **Aluminum Extrusion/Mach One:** Plant running at 100% capacity since March quarter; helped navigate aluminum price increases and improved segment bottomline. Y-o-Y Mach One volume decline attributed to "site readiness, project readiness" not competitive intensity; focus is "sensible expansion," customer quality, and profitability.

- **CAPEX:** No significant new CAPEX this year (maintenance only). CSN plant (FY24-25) fully operational. Phase 2 of CSN/Extrusion planned next year.
- **Engineering Services:** Buoyant US market, AI transformation, deep-tech upgrades (vision systems, automation). Sustainable margin ~15%.
- **Scaffolding Demand:** Strong US demand since January (AI chip plants, semicon, energy). Sustainable pipeline. Sustainable margin >15%.
- **Backward Integration:** Aluminium Formwork (Mach One) 100% backward integrated via in-house extrusion.
- **Margin Consolidation:** Scaffolding & Engg sustainable at 15%; Drum at 30%. Volatility currently benefits them but could reverse.
- **Order Books:** Drum Closure & Scaffolding not order-book based (direct/B2B/distribution). Formwork has 3–5 month order book.
- **Utilization:** Extrusion plant >95%; Mach One 75–80%; Scaffolding ~95%. Can add Scaffolding capacity within 3 months using existing Mumbai/China space.
- **Textile:** Fabric division shut, equipment sold. Yarn profitable (~13% EBITDA last qtr). Garment restructuring to break even in 2 quarters. Not actively seeking to sell remaining textile segments.
- **Middle East Scaffolding:** Decline due to war/shipping; <5% of total sales.
- **US Tariffs:** 50% under Section 232 (75% for China). 25% tariff advantage vs China; Chinese have ~20% steel cost advantage but Technocraft competes on inventory availability, relationships, local US team, and >150 component mix knowledge—not price.
- **Defence Canisters:** Supplied with repeat orders (~Rs. 10 cr); total Defence order book ~Rs. 20–21 cr (incl. JT Cooler).

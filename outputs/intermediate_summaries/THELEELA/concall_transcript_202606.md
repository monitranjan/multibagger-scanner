### **1. Management Commentary: Key Statements**
*   **CEO (Anuraag Bhatnagar):**
    *   The quarter demonstrated resilience of India's luxury hospitality demand despite temporary travel disruptions from the West Asia conflict.

    *   The Leela was ranked the world's second-best hotel brand in the Travel + Leisure World's Best Awards 2026.
    *   Domestic room revenue increased 25% YoY at the five palace hotels as a proactive response to international travel slowdown.
    *   International room revenue mix recovered from a 10% YoY decline in March to a 1% YoY increase by June.

    *   Expansion remains on track with the signing of a concession agreement for a 30-key resort in Tadoba (targeted completion CY30). Portfolio now stands at 25 properties/5,257 keys.

    *   Continued focus on scaling The Leela culture through initiatives like The Leela Centre of Excellence.
    *   All owned hotels have Platinum green building certification, advancing Net Zero 2050 ambition.

*   **CFO (Ravi Shankar):**
    *   Operating revenue increased 28% YoY to INR 3,520 million.
    *   Contribution from Leela brand website bookings doubled to 16%, enhancing distribution economics.
    *   Excluding Coorg (under ramp-up), the portfolio delivered over 60% EBITDA flow-through.
    *   Operating EBITDA margin expanded 383 bps YoY to 41%. Over two years, margin expanded from 31% (Q1 FY25) to 41% (Q1 FY27).
    *   Consolidated PAT increased five-fold YoY to INR 488 million, despite a INR 156 million loss from the Dubai JV due to reduced travel flows.
    *   Dubai asset is operationally break-even despite off-peak period; Leela to take over operations in CY27 for renovation.
    *   Balance sheet remains growth-ready with net debt to EBITDA of 1.6x.

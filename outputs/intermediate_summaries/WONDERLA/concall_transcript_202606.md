### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements)
**Mr. Arun Chittilappilly (Managing Director – CEO role):**
- Reported “one of our best quarters ever.” Stated total income of INR 252 crores (+41% YoY), EBITDA INR 122 crores (+39%), footfall 12.25 lakh (+33%).
- Existing parks grew ~15% revenue (attributed to 8% ARPU and 7% footfall growth).
- Chennai Park (new asset) contributed ~INR 45 crores revenue and 2.4 lakh visitors in first year; confident it will be “increasingly significant.”
- Hospitality/resorts delivered “best ever quarter.”
- Focus remains on “guest experience, expanding premium offerings and improving operational efficiency.”

**Mr. Saji Louiz (Chief Financial Officer):**
- Revenue from operations +44% YoY to INR 243 crores. EBITDA (incl. other income) INR 122 crores (+39%), margin 48%. PAT INR 72.79 crores (29% margin).
- Provided explicit **EBITDA bridge**: Existing parks +INR 15.93cr (46% of growth), Chennai +INR 21.86cr (64%), Resort +INR 3.19cr (9%), partially offset by +INR 6.5cr corporate overhead. Other income INR 9.47cr.
- PAT increase INR 20.22cr offset by depreciation +INR 11.49cr (Chennai) and tax +INR 2.75cr.
- Operating metrics: ATP INR 1,310 (+2%), non-ticket spend INR 591 (+20%), ARPU INR 1,901 (+7%).

**Mr. Dheeran Choudhary (Chief Operating Officer):**
- Hyderabad Park growth driven by being newer (8 yrs operational) and marketing/brand building investments.
- New Bangalore roller coaster “received extremely well… increases frequency, which in turn drives more footfall.”
- ARPU step-up from “high-spending cohorts” and “premiumize in-park experiences” (non-ride based, immersive).
- Seasonality: Q1 and Q3 are largest quarters (Q1 volume+value, Q3 group/volume-led).

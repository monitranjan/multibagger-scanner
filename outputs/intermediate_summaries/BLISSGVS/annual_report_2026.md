## FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: **AVERAGE**

**Rationale:**
- **Strengths:** Clean audit opinion, strong cash conversion, zero debt, consistent accounting policies, good CARO compliance, reasonable remuneration
- **Weaknesses:** Significant management turnover (3 directors resigned), BGIPL subsidiary exposure with negative net worth, high receivable days, inventory build-up, insider trading violation unresolved, audit trail gap, institutional investor exits, Africa revenue concentration (72%), aggressive dividend payout, large FX hedging losses
- **Overall:** The company has solid fundamentals (net cash, growing revenue/profit) but governance concerns (management changes, subsidiary exposure, insider trading) and working capital quality issues (high receivables, inventory build-up) warrant caution. The accounting quality is **Average** — not bad, but not clean enough to be rated Good.

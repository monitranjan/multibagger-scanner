Building the Next Era 
of Packaging
Packaging today is expected to do more than 
contain a product. As consumer expectations 
evolve and sustainability becomes a defining 
priority, it must preserve quality, safeguard 
health, and support a circular economy across 
its lifecycle. These shifts are reshaping the 
industry, rewarding formats that pair strong 
performance with long-term environmental 
responsibility.
Glass sits at the centre of this change for us. 
Chemically inert, it protects taste and safety 
without interacting with its contents, and it can 
be recycled endlessly without any loss of purity, 
making it one of the most trusted materials in 
use today.
This principle shapes the evolution of our 
offering, from container and specialty glass to 
emerging formats such as aluminium beverage 
cans. Across our portfolio, we combine 
functionality, material integrity and visual appeal 
to meet changing market needs.
During the year, we strengthened our 
manufacturing base and advanced new 
growth platforms, broadening our capabilities 
and creating opportunities across evolving 
packaging categories.
Backed by manufacturing excellence and an 
expanding portfolio, AGI Greenpac stands 
ready to create enduring value for customers, 
employees, communities and shareholders 
while shaping the next era of packaging.

Chairman and Managing Director’s Message
A Year of Execution 
and Excellence
DEAR SHAREHOLDERS,
FY 2025-26 marked an important 
milestone in AGI Greenpac's journey. 
We strengthened our core glass 
business while laying the foundation 
for the next phase of growth 
through our entry into aluminium 
beverage cans as an adjacent 
business. This expansion was 
guided by disciplined execution and 
prudent capital allocation, enabling 
resilient performance and further 
strengthening our balance sheet.
GROWTH IN A DYNAMIC 
ENVIRONMENT
The global packaging industry 
faced a period of change this year. 
Evolving consumer preferences 
and tighter regulation reshaped 
market dynamics, compounded by 
geopolitical uncertainty. Volatility in 
fuel and raw material prices added 
further pressure on costs across 
the industry, while disruptions to 
global trade and energy supplies 
created additional headwinds. Our 
ongoing strategy of diversifying fuel 
sources ensured that manufacturing 
operations continued without 
interruption.
Despite this backdrop, our 
performance remained strong. 
Total income stood at ₹2,760 
crore, reflecting 6% Y-o-Y growth, 
while Profit after Tax increased 
by 9.2% to ₹352 crore. Rigorous 
capital management allowed us 
to reduce net debt by 54% to ₹70 
crore, meaningfully strengthening 
our balance sheet. This included a 
voluntary prepayment of ₹193.25 
crore towards outstanding term loans 
in July 2025, funded entirely through 
internal cash flows, further reducing 
interest costs. Reflecting this financial 
strength, the Board recommended 
a final dividend of ₹7 per equity 
share for FY 2025-26, subject to 
your approval.

positions us in one of India's fastest-
growing packaging categories, creates 
strong synergies with our existing 
beverage customer base, broadens 
our addressable market, and enables 
us to deepen relationships with 
leading domestic and global brands.

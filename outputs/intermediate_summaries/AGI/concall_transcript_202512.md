### 1. Management Commentary (CEO, CFO, Leadership)

**Mr. Om Prakash Pandey (CFO) – Opening Remarks (Pg 3-4)**
- Reported 9M FY26 revenue ₹1,923 cr (vs ₹1,824 cr), EBITDA ₹484 cr (vs ₹497 cr), PAT ₹236 cr (vs ₹226 cr).
- Q3 FY26: Revenue ₹634 cr, EBITDA ₹154 cr, PAT ₹71 cr (includes ₹5.09 cr exceptional item for labor code impact).
- Stated EBITDA margins lower YoY due to "muted volumes in certain product category and adjustments in average sales realisations."
- **Guidance reiterated:** "12-18 months margins guidance excluding non-operating income in the range of 24% to 25%."
- Balance sheet: Prepaid remaining ECB in Dec 2025 (nil ECB). Net bank debt ~₹389 cr as of 31 Dec 2025; slight increase due to "delays in order deliveries in certain product categories."

**Mr. Rajesh Khosla (President & CEO) – Business Review (Pg 4-6, 7-12)**
- **Container glass:** Vol +10% vs Q2 FY26, -2% vs Q3 FY25; util ~95%. Realization ↓₹450/ton vs Q2, ↓₹1,200/ton vs Q3 FY25 (contractual lag to raw material costs).
- **Specialty glass:** Vol flat vs Q2, +13% vs Q3 FY25; util ~85%. Realization ↑₹900/ton vs Q2, ↑₹6,800/ton vs Q3 FY25.
- **Commercial glass:** Subdued due to "seasonal factor and temporary deferment of deliveries by the customer on account of weather-related considerations" (beer segment); expects recovery in Q4.
- **Capacity:** Debottlenecking done early (1,900 tpd); specialty exp to 200 tpd by Mar 2026; MP Greenfield (500 tpd) by Mar 2027 (+25% cap); Aluminium can (1.6 bn cans) procurement finalizing.
- **Retail/OEM entry:** Already in retail (online/offline/HoReCa). New approach: serve brand owners as OEM with "end-to-end service" (filled products like diffuser/perfume) via outsourcing – "we are not going to build a brand."

**Mr. Sandeep Sikka (Group CFO) – Q&A & Details (Pg 7-15)**
- 9M vol growth: Commercial glass ~8%, Specialty >6%.
- EBITDA/ton guidance: ₹9,500–₹10,500 (container, ex-non-op income). Specialty EBITDA% ~25–26%.
- FY27 vol growth: Container +3–4%, Specialty +7–10%, overall ~8–9%. FY27-28: +15–17% growth from new capacity.
- CAPEX: ₹220 cr spent in 9M; ₹60–70 cr so far on greenfields (land, studies). Next yr ₹1,100–1,200 cr planned.
- Net debt ₹389 cr includes temporary inventory build ("working capital has slightly bloated… temporary effect").
- HNG acquisition appeal at Delhi High Court: "sub judice. Very difficult to comment."

### 4. Future Outlook & Guidance

**Margins**
- Overall EBITDA margin guidance **24%–25%** for 12–18 months (mixed basis, ex non-op income) – reiterated by Pandey, Sikka, Khosla.
- Container glass EBITDA/ton: ₹9,500–₹10,500.
- Specialty glass EBITDA%: ~25–26% (steady state not disclosed in absolute/ton terms).
- **Mix shift:** Specialty growing faster (vol +13% YoY Q3) at higher realization/margin; container stable.
- **Seasonality:** Q3 hit by rains/winter (beer). Management expects normalization Q4 (Khosla: "postponement… Q4 we will be able to take care").
- **Operating leverage / ESOP reversal:** No mention of ESOP reversal or explicit operating leverage bridge in text. Input cost spikes (oil, soda ash) described as temporary head/tailwinds not in formula pricing.

**Volume Growth**
- FY26: Original 8–10% value guidance; Khosla expects ~7–9% volume growth (raw material pass-through affects value).
- 9M actual: Commercial +8%, Specialty +6% (Sikka).
- FY27: ~8–9% volumetric; FY27-28: 15–17% from incremental capacity.

**CAPEX**
- 9M FY26: ₹220 cr (75–80% glass business).
- Q4 FY26: ~₹25 cr additional.
- FY27: ₹1,100–1,200 cr (Gwalior + Can); remaining FY28 on Can side.
- Funding: QIP enabling resolution in place; comfortable with debt (EBITDA~₹600 cr, leverage ~2x) if market weak; internal accruals historically paid debt.

**M&A Integration**
- **HNG:** Acquisition appeal pending in Delhi High Court ("hearing is right now going on… sub judice"). No comment on competitive supply impact.
- **Fluence Pharma, BTM/Reginald:** **Not mentioned in the provided transcript text.** No details to extract.

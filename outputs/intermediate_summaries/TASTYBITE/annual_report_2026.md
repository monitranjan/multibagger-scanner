## FORENSIC FLAGS

### Related Party Transactions — **🔴 RED FLAG**

**Scale:** Massive and growing
- PBI (holding company): ₹1,517M in sales + ₹78M reimbursements = ₹1,596M
- Mars Food UK: ₹576M sales + ₹22M services + ₹51M reimbursements = ₹649M
- Mars Food US: ₹0 in FY26 (but ₹5,000M limit sought for FY27-28)
- Mars GmbH: ₹109M sales
- Mars Canada: ₹150M sales
- Mars PF France: ₹56M sales
- Mars Ireland: ₹43M sales

**Total RPT sales: ~₹2,452M** — approximately **43% of total revenue** (per BRSR disclosure)

**🔴 Critical Issue — Auditor Qualification:**
- Company did NOT obtain prior Audit Committee approval for ₹48.92M in RPTs with Mars Food UK
- Company did NOT obtain prior shareholder approval for material RPTs exceeding threshold by ₹94.47M
- Shareholders REJECTED the post-facto approval via postal ballot (98.68% voted against)
- Company plans to re-initiate shareholder approval process
- **This is a serious governance failure** — the company entered into material RPTs without proper approvals, and shareholders rejected the ratification

**🔴 Material RPT Limits Sought for FY27-28:**
- PBI: ₹1,000M (18.2% of FY26 revenue)
- Mars Food UK: ₹2,500M (45.6% of FY26 revenue)
- Mars Food US: ₹5,000M (91.1% of FY26 revenue)

**🟡 Yellow Flag:** PBI has negative net worth (-₹4,467M) — a holding company with accumulated losses. Yet it's the primary customer for the affiliate business.

### Auditor Opinion — **🔴 RED FLAG**

**QUALIFIED OPINION** issued by Kalyaniwalla & Mistry LLP

**Basis for Qualification:**
1. Company did not obtain prior Audit Committee approval for RPTs aggregating ₹48.92M (subsequently approved)
2. Company did not obtain prior shareholder approval for material RPTs exceeding threshold by ₹94.47M — shareholders REJECTED the post-facto approval

**Internal Financial Controls — QUALIFIED OPINION:**
- Material weakness identified in compliance with Regulation 23 of SEBI LODR regarding related party transactions
- Controls were not operating effectively

### Key Audit Matters

1. **Revenue recognition** — cut-off is key assertion; variety of shipment terms across markets
2. **Inventory valuation** — ₹1,077M inventory with ₹96M provision; manual process; significant judgment required

**🟡 Yellow Flag:** Inventory provision increased from ₹66M to ₹96M (+45%) — suggests growing obsolescence risk

### CARO Report Concerns

1. **Title deed issue:** Freehold land (Gut No. 503, Bhandgaon) — title not in company name; legal suit filed alleging illegal occupation (since 2010)
2. **Audit trail gaps:** Multiple periods where audit trail was not preserved (inventory software from Apr 2024, purchase requisition from Sep 2024, general ledger for Jan 21-22, 2025, database level from Apr 2025)
3. **Daily backup not performed** in some instances
4. **Statutory dues disputes:** ₹173M in disputed indirect tax matters (customs, GST, CENVAT)
5. **Whistleblower complaint received** — under investigation, no final report

### Accounting Policy Changes

- **No material changes** in accounting policies during FY26
- **Exceptional item:** ₹4.53M provision for statutory impact of new Labour Codes (Gratuity)
- **Conservative:** No revaluation of PPE; inventory at lower of cost and NRV
- **🟡 Yellow Flag:** Cash flow hedge reserve loss of ₹49.5M (vs ₹8M prior year) — significant FX hedging losses

### **FINAL SCORE: AVERAGE (leaning towards concerning)**

**Key Concerns:**
1. **RPT governance failure is the most serious issue** — company entered into material RPTs without proper approvals, and shareholders rejected the ratification. This is a governance red flag that could have regulatory consequences.
2. **Qualified auditor opinion** on both financials and internal controls — not a clean audit.
3. **Revenue stagnation** despite Mars backing and 40+ years of operations.
4. **Inventory build-up** (finished goods nearly doubled) — potential demand softness.
5. **Low capacity utilization** (46%) with CWIP projects suspended for >3 years.

**Strengths:**
1. Debt-free balance sheet with strong cash generation
2. Mars backing provides strategic stability and global distribution
3. Cheffin' brand launch shows innovation capability
4. Foodservice business growing consistently (10 consecutive quarters)
5. Strong sustainability practices (75% renewable energy, zero liquid discharge)

**Investment Implication:** The company has a solid operational foundation but governance issues around RPTs are concerning. The qualified audit opinion and shareholder rejection of RPT ratification suggest management may be prioritizing Mars group interests over minority shareholders. Investors should monitor the resolution of RPT governance issues and the trajectory of the Cheffin' brand.

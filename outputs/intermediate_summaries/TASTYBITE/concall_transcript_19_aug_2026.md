**Analysis of Tasty Bite Eatables Limited Investor Call Transcript (February 06, 2023)**

---

### **Management Commentary**
- **Mr. Ashok Vasudevan (Chairman/Managing Director):**
  - Highlighted return to pre-COVID growth, with 43% growth for Q3 FY23 and 34% growth for 9M FY23.
  - EBITDA up 7.8% QoQ; YoY EBITDA growth positive with forex benefits, stable without.
  - Exports consumer business grew 76% YoY (though partly due to low base post-COVID).
  - US penetration at an all-time high with dominant market share (over 60% in grocery, 59% in natural foods).
  - Food service business back to pre-corporate levels with 28% growth over 9 months.
  - Credited team for managing raw material inflation through improved processing and buying.
  - Expressed confidence in new Chairman, Pradeep Poddar, and Mars’s increased commitment to India and food innovation.
  - Capex of ~150 crore largely completed (balance only 72 lakh), adding 26% capacity to ~55,000 tons.
  - New Tasty Bite Research Center to open by May, new RTH line operational by September.
  - Positioned Tasty Bite as innovation partner for Mars, not just an outsourcing partner.

- **Mr. Pradeep Poddar (New Chairman):**
  - Exuded energy and optimism, referencing "positive energy" and "lovely performance" over past nine months.
  - Stated long-term vision to build business towards "a billion dollars over the next 10-15 years."

- **Mr. David Dusangh (Mars Representative):**
  - Shared Mars’s global vision to build human food and nutrition business beyond rice, with "tremendous opportunity in the meal space."
  - Noted "lots of innovation coming over the next 18 months" with Tasty Bite as a big part.

---

### **Tone, Clarity, and Confidence of Responses**
- **Tone:** Overall confident, optimistic, and forward-looking. Ashok Vasudevan’s tone was particularly assertive on growth, competition, and Mars partnership.
- **Clarity:** Direct on operational performance and capex. Less specific on future guidance, new geographies, and CEO appointment.
- **Confidence:** High confidence expressed in US market dominance, retort technology leadership, Mars synergy, and capacity utilization. Less detailed (but not evasive) on quantitative future targets.

---

### **Focus Areas**
- **Product Launches:** Mentioned Mars’s increased number of food product SKUs launched in India. New Tasty Bite Research Center and RTH line under development.
- **Channel Margins:** Not explicitly detailed, but discussed improved productivity (~2% growth) mitigating raw material inflation.
- **Inventory Levels:** Not directly discussed in this transcript.
- **Project Neev / Direct Distribution:** Not mentioned.
- **Contract Manufacturing:** Clarified that private label business (Smith & Jones, Spice Tailor) is not contract manufacturing; products are designed in-house. Stated RTH ~70%, Private Label ~10%, Food Service ~20% of revenue mix.

---

### **Future Outlook & Guidance**
- **Margins (EBITDA Bridge):**
  - Forex (USD/INR movement) was a tailwind.
  - Productivity improvements offset raw material inflation.
  - No specific quantitative EBITDA guidance provided.
- **Mix Shift:** Reaffirmed consistent revenue mix (~70% RTH, ~10% private label, ~20% food service).
- **Operating Leverage:** Implied through capacity expansion (+26%) and efficiency gains.
- **Seasonality:** Not discussed.
- **ESOP Reversal:** Not mentioned.
- **Volume Growth:** Implied strong growth in exports (76% YoY consumer) and recovery in food service.
- **CAPEX:** ~150 crore program nearly complete. Focus on capacity, safety, infrastructure, ETP, logistics, and new R&D facility.
- **M&A Integration:**
  - **Fluence Pharma / BTM/Reginald:** Not mentioned in this transcript.
  - **Mars Synergy:** Described as long-term, focusing on innovation and quality. Mars is integrating Canada operations. Potential to supply Mars brands (Ben’s Original, Seeds of Change) and co-develop new products.

---

### **Q&A Dynamics**
- **Analyst Queries vs. Management Answers:**
  - **Q:** Guidance for FY24 revenues and margins?  
    **A:** Evasive (“You know the answer to that.”).
  - **Q:** KPIs for new chairman?  
    **A:** Deflected; focused on long-term vision instead.
  - **Q:** Synergy/benefit from Mars after 4 years?  
    **A:** Confident, but non-specific; highlighted strategic benefits, safety standards, and innovation rather than immediate revenue impact.
  - **Q:** CEO appointment timeline?  
    **A:** Acknowledged search is active; stated “almost there” but offered no guidance.
  - **Q:** New geographies (Europe) growth?  
    **A:** Evasive; no specific details provided.
  - **Q:** Export growth concerns?  
    **A:** Highly confident and dismissive of competition; cited 76% growth and unparalleled distribution.
  - **Q:** Competition from Tata’s microwave retort?  
    **A:** Highly confident; dismissed as not a near-term threat to retort technology leadership.
  - **Q:** Contract manufacturing for Mars?  
    **A:** Confident and clear; positioned as innovation partner with potential to supply existing Mars brands and co-create new products.
  - **Q:** Vision from Mars representative?  
    **A:** Direct answer provided by David Dusangh focusing on meal solutions and innovation.

---

**Key Evasive Areas:** Specific future financial guidance, CEO appointment timeline, details on European expansion, and direct answers on synergy quantification.  
**Highly Confident Areas:** US market position, retort technology leadership, capacity utilization plans, and role as Mars innovation partner.

# Tasty Bites Eatables (TBEL) — Community Discussion Analysis

---

## Consensus vs Variant Views

- **Consensus (Verified):** Kagome's acquisition of majority stake via PBIL triggers a mandatory open offer under regulations — not necessarily a precursor to delisting. Community broadly accepts this as regulatory compliance.
- **Variant View (Needs Verification):** @nirajiitkgp raises the possibility that the open offer could be a precursor to delisting, especially if enough shares are tendered. This is a minority concern but not dismissed by the community.
- **Variant View (Needs Verification):** @rupeshtatiya questions whether the company's reported NP is genuinely sustainable, arguing that removing "Other Operating Income" (duty refunds) reduces FY15 NP to near zero. @Anant counters that duty refunds are standard for exporters and not a red flag — this remains a point of disagreement.
- **Variant View (Needs Verification):** @aveekmitra raises a structural concern: Kagome may now be free to manufacture under the Tasty Bite brand anywhere globally (e.g., Vietnam, other JVs), potentially bypassing TBEL's Indian manufacturing unit. This is a strategic risk not addressed by other members.
- **Variant View (Needs Verification):** @dkirand cautions against over-reliance on Kagome's acquisition price as a valuation anchor, noting Japanese companies operate on 15–25 year horizons, which may not align with retail investor return expectations.

---

## Events the Community Is Watching

- **Open Offer / Delisting (Needs Verification):** The open offer document is under scrutiny. Community is watching whether sufficient shares are tendered to trigger a delisting scenario.
- **Preference Share Maturity (Verified):** Preference shares issued to PBIL in 1998 are set to mature in 2018. A reserve (₹116,083,000) has already been created for repayment. Community is monitoring how this is handled.
- **Kagome's Strategic Direction (Needs Verification):** Members are watching whether Kagome will scale up manufacturing in India, expand the brand in the US, or potentially enter the QSR business. No clear signals yet.
- **USFDA Compliance (Verified):** @dkirand flags the importance of checking USFDA records for the company, implying regulatory compliance is a key watchpoint.
- **Past Delisting Attempt (Verified):** The company had announced delisting in 2009–10 but scrapped it. Community is watching whether a similar move resurfaces.

---

## Red Flags Being Tracked

- **Other Operating Income Dependency (Needs Verification):** FY15 NP of ₹10 Cr is almost entirely explained by ₹10 Cr "Other Operating Income" (duty refunds). Without it, NP is near zero. Community is questioning sustainability and classification.
- **Dividend Payout Despite Liabilities (Needs Verification):** @rupeshtatiya flags that the company pays dividends while carrying ~₹26 Cr in non-current liabilities (all ECB — External Commercial Borrowings). This raises questions about capital allocation discipline.
- **ECB / Forex Exposure (Needs Verification):** All debt is in ECB form. Community is concerned about rupee depreciation impact and hedging strategy — no clear answer provided.
- **Unexplained Other Expenses (Needs Verification):** Other expenses of ₹27 Cr (~16% of topline) in FY15 are flagged as opaque. No breakdown provided in the discussion.
- **Valuation Concerns (Verified):** @neil991 notes the stock trades at ~27x, which is considered expensive. Community consensus is that the stock may have already been "discovered" by the market.
- **Kagome's Global Manufacturing Risk (Needs Verification):** @aveekmitra raises the risk that Kagome could manufacture under the Tasty Bite brand outside India, potentially marginalizing TBEL's Indian operations.

## Milestones That Would Change Sentiment

- **Delisting Announcement (Needs Verification):** If Kagome initiates a formal delisting process, sentiment would shift significantly — both positively (for tendering shareholders) and negatively (for those wanting to hold long-term).
- **Kagome's India Manufacturing Expansion (Needs Verification):** Any announcement of new manufacturing capacity or product lines in India under the Tasty Bite brand would be a positive catalyst.
- **US Market Growth / Brand Expansion (Needs Verification):** If Kagome successfully scales the Tasty Bite brand in the US (beyond current levels), it could validate the acquisition thesis and lift sentiment.
- **Resolution of ECB / Forex Risk (Needs Verification):** Clear disclosure on hedging strategy or refinancing of ECB debt would address a key concern.
- **Preference Share Redemption (Verified):** The 2018 maturity of preference shares and how it is handled (cash outflow vs. conversion) will be a near-term event to watch.
- **USFDA Inspection Results (Verified):** Any adverse USFDA findings could materially impact the business, especially given the export-heavy model.
- **QSR Business Expansion (Needs Verification):** If Kagome shows interest in the QSR/HORECA business (currently a significant revenue stream), it could open new growth avenues.

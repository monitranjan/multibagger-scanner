### 1. Management Commentary (CEO, CFO, Leadership)
**Mr. Pankajkumar Jagawat (Chairman & Managing Director):**
- Marked one year since listing; focused on building "stronger, more scalable, and sustainable business."
- Q1 FY27 operational highlight: "successful commencement of operations at our new manufacturing facility in Marol, Mumbai."
- Core business: "healthy customer traction, supported by deeper engagement with existing customers, expansion across domestic markets, and a growing customer base."
- Industry view: Constructive outlook for organized jewellery; India supported by wedding demand, rising incomes, preference for branded jewellery; retailers focusing on "inventory optimization, wider product offerings, and faster design cycles."
- Financials reported: Revenue INR 716.38 cr (+144.69% YoY); EBITDA INR 71.45 cr (9.97% margin); PAT INR 50.48 cr.
- Product mix: Progress on "value-added categories, including designer jewellery, Turkish jewellery, and gaining traction and are contributing to improved realization."
- Capex/Finance: Approved rights issue of 46,43,471 shares aggregating up to INR 100 cr to "support the company's growth plans."
- Priorities: (1) Capacity expansion (Marol ramp-up, Jaipur), (2) Market expansion (domestic, export, new customers), (3) Product mix enhancement (designer/Turkish).

**Mr. Shriram Iyengar (Chief Financial Officer):**
- Confirmed financials: Revenue +144.69%, EBITDA INR 71.45 cr (+39% YoY), PAT INR 50.48 cr (+46.94%), PAT margin 7.05%.
- EBITDA improvement attributed to "strong volume growth, through a better product mix, enhanced customer services via varied design offerings and continued focus on operational efficiencies."
- Noted Marol facility strengthens capacity; company maintains "disciplined approach towards working capital management."

### 3. Focus Areas
**Product Launches / Facilities:**
- Marol facility (Mumbai) commenced operations in June; provides "additional capability and flexibility."
- Jaipur facility upcoming (mid-Nov/Dec), will manufacture 22 and 18 karat (and 14 if demanded).
- New product lines: Designer jewellery, Turkish jewellery, CZ, rose gold (via associated entity Utssav), new designs rolled out.
- Dubai office: Compliance done, awaiting RBI approval to boost exports.

**Inventory Levels & Management:**
- Business model: "We utilize all the funds and we keep the stock readily. We don't deal on order-to-order basis." (Explained as reason for negative operating cash flow).
- Inventory method changed from FIFO to WAC, resulting in "unrealized gain which was locked in the inventory of last year, which got spilled in this current year" (~2-2.5% EBITDA boost).
- Gold Metal Loan (GML) used for "natural hedging whatever gold we sell, that to be bought immediately."
- Retailers' "inventory optimization" noted as industry trend creating opportunity for scalable manufacturers.
- *Note: "Project Neev" and "direct distribution" are NOT mentioned anywhere in the provided transcript.*
- *Note: "Channel margins" as a specific term is not used; margin discussion is at EBITDA/PAT/product-mix level.*

### 4. Future Outlook & Guidance
**Margins:**
- **EBITDA Bridge Components:** Q1 EBITDA margin 9.97% included "2% to 2.5%... inventory gain" from prior-year WAC shift. Sustainable EBITDA margin guided at **7.5% – 8%**.
- **Mix Shift:** Focus on studded/designer/Turkish jewellery (cited as 75% of revenue/cast-studded mix by management in Q&A) to improve realization. Long-term (3-4 years) margin potential to reach ~10% with new machines/tech and operating leverage.
- **Operating Leverage:** Marol and Jaipur facilities expected to improve production efficiency; "our fixed expenses are going to be fixed. So when your quantity is going to increase… our margin is also going to be better."
- **Seasonality:** Management agreed with analyst that Q2 typically better than Q1, Q3 better than Q2, and confirmed post-exhibition demand for Q2 looks good.
- *Note: "ESOP reversal" is NOT mentioned in the transcript.*

**Volume Growth & Revenue Guidance:**
- FY27 Guidance: **Value growth 50%–60%** (target ~INR 3,500 cr revenue), **Volume growth 30%–40%** YoY.
- Q1 actual volume growth was ~61-62% YoY (cited in Q&A), but management stated they "stick to our guidance of 30% to 40% in terms of volume" and called 60% full-year "wishful thinking" but not impossible with strong demand.
- Export currently 4% of Q1 revenue; huge potential via Dubai gateway.

**CAPEX:**
- Jaipur facility: Capex INR 47 crores, operational mid-Nov/Dec. Phase 1 is 50,000 sq ft on a 3-acre land plot with "capacity itself will grow by many folds" if demand exceeds.
- Marol facility: Already commissioned (June), further ramp-up ongoing (currently 75% utilization at existing Mumbai facility; Marol new line scaling).
- Rights issue (INR 100 cr) to fund gold/working capital, not directly CAPEX but supports growth engine.

**M&A Integration:**
- *Note: "Fluence Pharma, BTM/Reginald" are NOT mentioned anywhere in the provided transcript. No M&A activity discussed.*

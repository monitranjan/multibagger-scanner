### **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key themes:** Resilient domestic demand, moderating global growth, aggressive rate cuts, fiscal consolidation, credit upgrades (Morningstar DBRS, S&P, R&I). Super abrasives demand linked to industrial cyclicality; entry barriers high for unorganised players.
- **Stated opportunities:** EV, medical, electronics segments; "Make in India"/"Make for World" infra thrust; semiconductor hub potential; nano-CBN adoption; premiumisation.
- **Stated risks:** Raw material volatility, strict environmental regs, global geopolitical uncertainty, competition from global giants and regional price players.
- **Strategic priorities:** Product development, export expansion (glass, aerospace, steel), application-based machines, digital transformation (SAP, cybersecurity), new product launches (Solar Glass Wheels), de-risking via precision components.
- **Walk-the-talk:** Revenue declined 3% YoY; EBITDA margin contracted from 28% to 19%; PAT margin from 18% to 11%. Guidance not explicitly stated in letters, but trajectory clearly decelerating.

### **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
- **Segments:** Super Abrasives (71% of net revenue), Machines (14%), Precision Products (14%).
- **New/additions:** Germany subsidiary (Wendt GmbH) set up July 2025 for European distribution; Solar Glass Grinding Wheels manufacturing setup commissioned; technology import for peripheral grinding machines (absorption expected FY26-27).
- **Capex:** Rs. 799 lakhs (vs. Rs. 5,829 lakhs prior year). Major spend on new plant/machinery for fast-growing products. Fully funded by internal accruals. Pending: peripheral grinding machine capacity ramp.

### **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key themes:** Resilient domestic demand, moderating global growth, Rupee volatility, rising input costs, and strategic pivot toward application-based machines and new geographies (aerospace, EVs, semiconductors, medical). Emphasis on QRM, cost optimisation, and digital/SAP upgrades.
- **Stated opportunities:** EV mobility, nano-CBN adoption, semiconductor manufacturing surge, "Make in India/for World" tailwinds.
- **Stated risks:** Raw material volatility, global competition (organised & unorganised), environmental regulations, cyclicality in auto/aerospace, dispatch clearance delays.
- **Walk-the-talk:** FY26 revenue declined 3% YoY; EBITDA margin contracted from 28% to 19%; PAT margin fell from 18% to 11%. Guidance implied growth but execution missed on the back of machine business degrowth and export softness.

### **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
- **Segments:** Super Abrasives (71% of net revenue), Machines (14%), Precision Products (15%).
- **New/additions:** Germany subsidiary (Wendt GmbH) set up July 2025 for European distribution of peripheral grinding machines; Solar Glass Grinding Wheels manufacturing setup commissioned; new compact surface grinding machine for non-steel segment.
- **Capex vs Planned:** Rs. 799 lakhs spent (vs Rs. 5,829 lakhs prior year). Major spend on new plant/machinery for fast-growing products. All funded internally. Pending: technology absorption for peripheral grinding machines expected FY26-27.

### **FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK**
- **Revenue & PAT 3Y trend (Standalone):** Revenue declined from Rs. 21,197 lakhs (FY23) to Rs. 20,652 lakhs (FY26); PAT fell from Rs. 3,829 lakhs to Rs. 2,275 lakhs.
- **EBITDA margin trend:** Contracted from 28% (FY23) to 19% (FY26) — significant compression.
- **CFO vs PAT:** Not directly available; however, company claims "debt-free status" and healthy cash conversion via QRM (receivables down from Rs. 6,694 to Rs. 5,009 lakhs).
- **Working capital:** Inventory up from Rs. 3,440 to Rs. 4,617 lakhs (finished machines awaiting customer clearance). Receivables down from Rs. 6,694 to Rs. 5,009 lakhs. DSO improved slightly from 101 to 100 days.
- **ROCE/ROE trend:** ROCE declined from 27% (FY23) to 15% (FY26); ROE from 19% to 10%.
- **Debt:** Net debt-free. Credit rating AA- (long-term) / A1+ (short-term). No long-term borrowings; cash credit limits utilised.

### **FORENSIC FLAGS**
- **Related party transactions:** Rs. 4.69% of purchases and 5.89% of sales are with RPTs (down from 31.06%/5.8%). All arm's length; no material RPTs exceeding thresholds. Form AOC-2: Nil.
- **Contingent liabilities vs networth:** Not explicitly disclosed in extract; however, auditor report notes no qualifications or adverse remarks.
- **Misc. expenses as % of revenue:** Not separately flagged; "Other operating expenses" constitute ~13% of revenue (2025-26), within acceptable range.
- **Auditor opinion:** Unqualified — "There are no qualifications, reservations, adverse remarks or disclaimers given by the Auditors."
- **Key Audit Matters:** Auditor commented on audit trail gap at application level for certain users/database changes; addressed via SAP upgradation. No Section 143(12) matters reported.
- **CARO report:** Not separately extracted; no red flags indicated in auditor comments.
- **Accounting policy changes:** Ind AS adopted from FY16; no aggressive changes noted this year.

### **GOVERNANCE**
- **Management remuneration as % of revenue:** CEO remuneration Rs. 38.5 lakhs (appointed Jan 2026); non-exec directors paid Rs. 5 lakhs commission each. Overall remuneration modest relative to revenue.
- **KMP changes:** Mr. Ninad Gadgil resigned Sept 2025; Mr. Amit Ingale appointed Jan 2026. Company Secretary Arjun Raj P continues.
- **Promoter stake changes:** Wendt GmbH (37.5%) divested via OFS in May 2025; ceased to be promoter. CUMI (37.5%) remains sole promoter. Public holds 62.5%.

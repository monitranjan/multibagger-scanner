### 6. CRITICAL OBSERVATIONS & RED FLAGS

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **“Fundamental SSSG” vs Reported SSSG** | Management explicitly separates the two. Reported SSSG includes new store base effects; the 30% target applies to **mature cohorts**. This is a critical distinction for modeling. |
| **Operating Leverage Source** | **Corporate/Marketing leverage > Store leverage.** Management states bulk of 7.5%→15% EBITDA margin expansion comes from **scale on fixed costs (A&P, corporate)**, not store-level margin expansion. Implies **distribution growth is a margin driver, not a drag**. |
| **Gold Price Sensitivity** | Management’s data shows **inverse correlation**: sharp gold inflation → demand destruction (Q3 FY26 slowest ever). Stable/range-bound gold = best environment. This contradicts traditional “gold inflation = revenue growth” heuristic. |
| **Inventory Accounting** | Balance sheet inventory inflated by gold price (mark-to-market) and new store stocking. **Cohort-level turns (1.8-2.0x) are the true operational metric.** Blended turn improving mechanically as base ages. |
| **Manufacturing Strategy** | 95% in-house. **Not for cost (300-400bps delta acknowledged but secondary)** but for **design IP protection** – prevents commoditization in vendor ecosystem. Strategic moat. |
| **Marketing Efficiency** | Clear **declining % trajectory** (9.2% → 6.6% → 4.5%) with rising absolute spend. Quarter-to-quarter noise explicitly flagged. |
| **No Mention Of** | Project Neev, Direct Distribution (as distinct channel), ESOP reversal, CAPEX, M&A (Fluence/BTM), Volume targets. |
| **Analyst Pushback** | Multiple analysts questioned 50% CAGR math, GMROI decline, rent/area gap. Management engaged substantively but **refused granular targets** (new cust, inventory kg, quarterly marketing %). |

### 7. SUMMARY: INVESTMENT THESIS FROM CALL

1.  **Structural Operating Leverage Real & Accelerating:** 7.5% → 15% EBITDA margin driven by **scale on corporate/marketing fixed costs**, validated by Q1 (49% rev growth, 135% EBITDA growth).
2.  **Consumer Internet Model Validated:** >80% online origin, 352 stores punching above weight, cohort compounding (39% SSSG, 40% ROIC on mature stores), repeat revenue 60%+.
3.  **Gold Volatility Managed, Not Feared:** 50% hedge policy discipline; business model **thrives in stability**, suffers in sharp inflation. Current stability = tailwind.
4.  **Design & Manufacturing Moat:** 95% in-house protects differentiation, enables premium pricing, supports aging customer wallet share expansion.
5.  **Guidance Credible:** 4-year INR 12,000 Cr target backed by **30% fundamental SSSG + 20% store CAGR** – math works on *mature cohort* economics, not reported blends. Track record: delivered ~50% in FY26 despite gold chaos.
6.  **Capital Efficiency Improving:** Inventory turns → 1.7-1.8x, Marketing % → 4.5%, Store ROIC 40%+ and growing.

**Key Risk:** Execution on 20% store CAGR in Tier-2/3 while maintaining cohort productivity; merchandise dislocation fix at entry price points to re-accelerate new customer acquisition (currently ~40k/qtr vs 50k).

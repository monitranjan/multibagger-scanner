- No RPT red flags, no fraud, no qualifications.

- Business model shows structural profitability inflection (store cohort economics, repeat revenue).  
- IPO fresh capital de-risks balance sheet.

**Verdict**: **Accounting quality is Average** based on *provided excerpt* due to disclosure gaps. **Full financial statements likely Good** — but must verify working capital, debt, and contingent liabilities in Notes to Accounts. **Investor action**: Request/extract Schedule III disclosures (inventory days, receivables ageing, borrowings, contingent liabilities, promoter pledge %) before final scoring.

### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements from Leadership)
**Participants:**  
- Mr. Nihar Chheda – Vice President (Strategy) (leadership, not CEO)  
- Mr. Anand Gupta – Chief Financial Officer  

**Nihar Chheda (Opening Remarks):**
- Raw material: “PVC resin prices witnessed a recovery during the March quarter, prices saw a sharp correction from early April following the temporary suspension of import duty on PVC resin till June '26… resulted in destocking across channel partners.”
- Long-term strategy pillars: “product innovation, distributor network expansion and retailer network expansion.”
- Product innovation: Developed “polypropylene-based drainage system, DECILO… manufactured at our Haridwar facility and material has started to be installed in not only residential projects, but also commercial projects like hospitals and hotels.”
- Distribution: “At the beginning of this fiscal year, we have identified white spaces at a district and taluka level… In the June quarter, we have been able to add many new channel partners.”
- Digitization: “DMS (Distributor Management Systems) are now fully in place… scheme spends are now directly targeted to retailers… move to a pull-based demand model rather than the legacy push-based way… SFA (Sales Force Automation) also is in place.”
- Outlook: “We expect industry demand to be supported by a recovery in infrastructure activity, increasing premiumization across product categories and the benefits of operating leverage as market conditions normalize.”

**Anand Gupta (Financial Highlights):**
- “Revenue from operation in this quarter stood at INR 609 crores, a growth of 5% Y-o-Y. Our volumes for the quarter stood at 40,729 metric tons, degrew by 7% Y-o-Y.”
- “EBITDA for the quarter stood at INR 77 crores, a growth of 93% Y-o-Y, while margins stood at 13%, registering a 600 basis point growth.”
- “Profit after tax… INR 34 crores, a growth of 580% Y-o-Y. PAT margin… 6%.”
- “Working capital days stood at 71 days… Receivable days stood at 40 days, whereas inventory days stood at 100 days as of 30th June 2026.”

### 3. Focus Areas (as per extract criteria)

**Product Launches:**
- DECILO (polypropylene-based low-noise drainage system) launched at Haridwar; being installed in residential/commercial projects.  
- “We are looking at many more new product launches within the pipe vertical, where gross margins will be significantly better than our core products of PVC.” (Nihar, p.8)

**Channel Margins / Direct Distribution:**
- No explicit phrase “channel margins” but related commentary: Distributors attracted by “how fast you can rotate their material, the amount of inventory turns you can offer… because we now have DMS and SFA in place, and a direct control on retailers” (p.9). Price hikes (CPVC) passed “in a phase-wise manner… 1 to 2 weeks to the channel” (p.7).  
- Direct distribution / pull model: “scheme spends are now directly targeted to retailers across the country… tens of thousands of retailers who we now directly give schemes to and directly reward from the company to the retailer” (p.7). DMS & SFA central to this.
- *Project Neev: Not mentioned in transcript.*

**Inventory Levels:**
- Reported: Inventory days 100 as of June 30, 2026 (Anand, p.4).  
- Guidance: “we stick to our guidance of 65 to 75 days for inventory” (Nihar, p.13). Explained 100 days due to “supply insecurity during the war time” and unexpected 7% degrowth; expected to correct “by end of September quarter” (p.13).  
- No mention of “Project Neev” or specific direct-distribution project name beyond DMS/SFA.

### 4. Future Outlook & Guidance

**Margins (EBITDA bridge components, mix shift, operating leverage, seasonality, ESOP reversal):**
- **Guidance:** Operating (EBITDA) margin guidance “11% to 13%” (repeated by Nihar p.5, p.9, p.10, p.12). Q1 actual EBITDA margin 13%.
- **Levers / Bridge components described:**  
  - *Mix shift:* “product mix has improved… agri versus building material and… PVC versus CPVC, PP, PPR” (p.6). Q1 typically agri-heavy but this quarter plumbing/drainage higher; CPVC/PPR contribution up.  
  - *Pricing power:* “pricing power” listed as gross-margin lever (p.10).  
  - *Operating leverage:* “better realizations helped better operating leverage” (p.6); “operating leverage is the only one which is at the EBITDA level” (p.10).  
  - *Freight / decentralization:* “freight benefits also have kicked in… decentralization of manufacturing footprint… new plants in South (Sangareddy, Telangana) and East (Begusarai, Bihar)” (p.9-10).  
  - *Seasonality:* “Q1 typically is the agri heavy quarter… Q1 is the heaviest in terms of agri followed by Q4” (p.6). This quarter defied normal seasonality due to volatility.  
  - *ESOP reversal: Not mentioned.*
- No explicit “EBITDA bridge” table but levers collectively explain margin movement.

**Volume Growth:**
- Guidance: “12% - 15% guidance that we have said last time in terms of the volume” (Nihar p.5); later Anand cites “12% to 15%” volume growth guidance for year (p.14).  
- April degrowth called “a blip”; May/June/July recovery; “we are back on the growth trajectory” (p.5). Full-year guidance unchanged.

**CAPEX:**
- Q1 spend: “Around INR 40 crores, INR 42 crores has been spent… primarily on the completion of second tranche of Bhuj plant takeover.” (Anand p.5).  
- Full-year: “whatever we had given data point in the May call stands as it is. There is no change in capex plan.” (Anand p.5).

**M&A Integration (Fluence Pharma, BTM/Reginald):**
- *Not mentioned anywhere in the transcript.* No M&A discussion present.

**Other Outlook Items from Q&A:**
- Bathware: Q3 (Dec quarter) revenue INR 25 cr achievable, near break-even (Nihar p.12).  
- ROCE: Historically 15%-20%+, target similar with volume/capital allocation (Nihar p.13). Utilization target 60-65% (production basis) to hit that; current 52-53% (Anand p.14).  
- Debtor days: 40 days at June; target “towards 30 days” in next couple years (Nihar p.14-15).

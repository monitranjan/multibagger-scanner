Based solely on the provided transcript of the Kennametal India Limited investor meeting (March 2, 2023), here is the extracted data, structured as dense, data-driven bullet points:

### **Core Product Segments & Product Initiatives**
*   **Primary Business Segments (Hard Metals):**
    *   **Inserts:** Small cutting tools for holders/CNC machines; largest portion of the market/portfolio. Highlighted as the focus of a major capacity expansion.
    *   **Round Tools:** Drills, end mills, etc. (tungsten carbide only, excluding high-speed steel, taps).
    *   **Wear Resistance Solutions:** Carbide coatings.
*   **Product/Capacity Initiatives:**
    *   **New Master Insert Plant:** Significant expansion completed (civil infrastructure) to support demand for the next 5-7 years. Physical infrastructure is capable of **doubling capacity**; machinery to be added in a modular fashion.
    *   **Modernization:** Investment in automated, roboticized, and quality-inspection-focused machinery within the new insert plant to improve product quality and consistency.
    *   **New Product Revenue Contribution:** Consistently averages **20-25% of revenue** from products launched in the last five years (globally developed).

### **Quantitative Metrics**
*   **Growth Trajectory:** Company has historically grown **1.5 to 1.6 times the industry growth rate** over the last 24 months. Aspiration is to maintain or exceed this rate, particularly with growth in high-value sectors.
*   **Market Shares / Position:** No specific percentage share provided. Stated to be among the top 3 global technology players (alongside IMC Group and Sandvik). Management indicated the total addressable market for tungsten carbide tools is **1.8-2x** an investor's estimate of ~₹3100 crore, implying a market size of **~₹5600-6200 crore**.
*   **Export/Revenue Mix:**
    *   **Domestic vs. Export:** **83-85%** of business is domestic. **~15-18%** is exports (overall).
    *   **Export Breakdown:** Exports consist of (a) intercompany tool sales (products made in India) and (b) CNC machine sales (predominantly to China, severely impacted by COVID slowdown).
    *   **China Impact:** CNC machine exports to China, ~5% of top line, saw a **75-80% impact** in the quarter. China's manufacturing recovery is expected in 3-4 months.
*   **Margin & Cost Factors:**
    *   **Raw Material:** Tungsten and cobalt prices strengthened. Price increases were passed on, but raw material as a % of sales remains higher due to mix effects (softer high-margin export business, shift to domestic).
    *   **One-Time Costs:** ₹34 million incurred for machinery movement to the new insert plant (classified as revenue expense).
    *   **Employee Costs:** Sequential increase due to annual merit cycle (effective October 1).
    *   **EBITDA Margin Goal:** Endeavor to beat FY22 performance. No specific forward-looking percentage target given. Noted that trading/imported product has a limited distributor margin due to global transfer pricing.

### **Future Growth Adjacencies & Strategy**
*   **Geographic Expansion (CNC Machines):**
    *   Focus on **Southeast Asia** (Vietnam, Indonesia) and potentially **Middle East** and select **Eastern European** countries.
    *   **Export Margin:** Management declined to specify the margin differential for exports vs. domestic.
*   **Sectoral Focus for Growth:** Strong positioning and focus on **fast-growing, high-technology sectors**: Aerospace, Defense, Railways, EVs, and Energy (including wind). These sectors demand premium, differentiated products and are expected to grow faster than automotive.
*   **Strategic Priorities:**
    *   **Gain Share via Technology/Productivity:** Focus on providing productivity and cost-per-component reduction to customers, not through discounting. Leveraging global R&D for new products.
    *   **Capitalize on Manufacturing Push:** Positioning to benefit from India's manufacturing growth and capex in defense, railways, etc.
    *   **OEM Relationship:** Strengthening relationships with OEMs (machine builders and end-users) is a "very, very key priority."

### **Revenue Mix Breakdown**
*   **Geographic Split (from transcript data):**
    *   **Domestic India:** 83-85%
    *   **Exports:** ~15-18% (Comprising intercompany tool shipments and CNC machine sales, primarily to China).
*   **Segment Split:** No explicit percentage breakdown by product (Inserts, Round Tools, Wear Resistance) or by industry (Automotive, Aerospace, etc.) is provided in the transcript. The discussion confirms a broad-based exposure across industries, with automotive being the largest segment by gross output, but faster growth in aerospace/defense/railways.

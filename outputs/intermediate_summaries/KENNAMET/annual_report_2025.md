### **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key Themes:** Focus on operational excellence, cost control, and navigating global headwinds (commodity volatility, geopolitics). Emphasis on MSME expansion, export growth, and innovation (HARVI™ II TE, BPG-15 grinder).  
- **Opportunities:** India’s 6.5% GDP growth, PLI schemes, aerospace/defense demand, and localization under Atmanirbhar Bharat.  
- **Risks:** Geopolitical tensions, U.S. tariff hikes, auto sector slowdown, and talent retention challenges.  
- **Strategic Priorities:** Drive revenue in non-auto segments, expand machining solutions footprint, and enhance renewable energy usage.  
- **Walk-the-Talk Check:**  
  - **Revenue Guidance:** Achieved 6.4% growth vs. FY24 (FY24: +2.2%).

  - **Capex:** Committed ₹143M in CWIP; completed new inserts manufacturing facility.

**Management Commentary:**
- CEO Rishi Das: Q1 FY27 highest ever quarterly revenue of INR428 crores, representing 37% year-on-year growth. EBITDA increased 34% to INR87 crores; EBIT grew to INR55 crores; PAT increased 91% to INR35 crores. EBIT margin improved to 13% from 11% in Q1 FY26; PAT margin expanded to 8% from 6%; EBITDA margin remained healthy at 20%. During the year, added 1.91 million square feet to area under management and launched 17 new centers. Expansion guided by "follow the talent strategy" – building high density workspace clusters in talent rich micro-markets where enterprises are looking to establish and expand operations. As of June 2026, catered to 855 clients across Global Capability Centers, Indian conglomerates, unicorns, and high growth startups. GCCs contributed 53% of revenue during the quarter; multi-center clients (more than one center) accounted for about 41% of revenue. Nearly 90% of occupants come from clients who have taken more than 100 seats. Nearly 30 megawatt of solar capacity already operational (solar farms in Karnataka and Maharashtra, rooftop installations across the country), with ambition to transition the portfolio to 100% green power. Last decade was about building and evolving Indiqube: expanded from Bangalore to multiple cities, progressed from managed workspaces to integrated managed spaces platform, broadened service offerings, and transitioned from private limited to listed institution. Next phase about compounding what has been built through geographical expansion, deeper customer relationships, growing VAS contribution, strong operating leverage, and sustainability initiatives.
- COO Meghna Agarwal: Annual report reflects evolution into integrated managed spaces platform. Offerings: Grow (core managed workspace, DesignQube rebranded Bespoke); IndiCare (rebranded IndiQube One, allowing broader operations, employee experience, and facility requirement, now extending into retail); Eco (helping customers build more sustainable and energy efficient commercial spaces). Intent to add close to 2 million square feet every year; remain committed to that growth trajectory. VAS contribution has grown from 12% to approximately 15% and is currently around 17% of total revenue; expect this contribution to increase further over time (structurally by approximately 2% to 4%). Ultimately, ambition is to become the total outsourcing solution for commercial spaces. 
- CFO Pawan Jain/Vikas Agarwal: Debt increase driven by solar plant capex, not for normal leasing business. Capex defined as combination of interiors/Design and Build project, solar investment, and vendor payment cycles. Prefers not to give provisional capex/cash flow numbers at this stage since figures will be subject to H1 audit and review; H1 numbers to be completely detailed out.

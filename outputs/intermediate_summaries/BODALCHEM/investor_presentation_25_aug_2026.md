*   **Dye Intermediates:** Includes Vinyl Sulphone & Derivatives, H Acid, DASA, F.C. Acid, Gamma Acid, K Acid, Sulpho Tobias Acid, 4.N.A.D.P.S.A, 6-Nitro, MPDSA, Beta Nephthol, Meta Ureido Aniline, 1:2:4 DIAZO.

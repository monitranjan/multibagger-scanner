### Key Statements from MD (Aditya Malkani)

**Company Overview:**
- 800+ employees, five manufacturing facilities across India, global footprint in ~15 countries.
- India comprises ~80%+ of sales; Middle East and select markets (US, etc.) becoming more relevant.
- Self-identified as a **Tier 1 welding player not part of a global MNC**, with Indian roots expanding to other geographies.
- Closed FY26 at approximately **INR 1,135 crores in sales**.
- This is the company's **75th year** (October 2026).

**FY26 Financial Highlights (as stated by MD):**
- **Q4:** Revenue growth ~3%; EBITDA ~INR 38 crores (up 200 bps); Gross margin ~36%; PBT ~INR 39 crores (excluding Kuwait project recovery and exceptional items).
- **Full Year Standalone:** Revenue up ~2%; Gross margins at 38% (up ~250 bps); EBITDA margin at 12%; PBT ~INR 130 crores (excluding onerous cost of Uran project).
- **Kuwait Project Recovery:** INR 14 crores recovered (pending from 2020-2021).
- **Return on Capital Employed (Operational EBITDA):** ~12%; **ROCE:** ~23%.

**New Product Introduction & Innovation:**
- Partnership with **Miller** (ITW group company) for submerged arc welding applications in power, structural, and shipbuilding.
- Strengthened portfolio with **Made in India** products for robotic line.
- Added products with **nuclear approvals** for consumables.
- Adding consumables for **wind manufacturing industry**, high-end nickels, and special critical welding applications.
- **Automation space** is a big focus: hard automation, robotics, cobots, laser — products introduced, orders received, and year-on-year push increasing.
- Recognized by **CII** among top 100 innovative companies in India (battery-powered welding equipment — first to do so).
- Training division recognized; **Bureau of Indian Standards** recognized manufacturing plants for quality and process.

**Flares & Process Equipment Division:**
- Division **restructured and realigned** as of end of March/early April 2026.
- No longer a separate division called "Flares and Process Equipment."
- Shifted to **product management-driven** approach for a ~INR 20-30 crore product line (not a standalone division for a INR 1,200 crore company).
- **No further losses expected** — the biggest hit was taken in Q1; working toward recovery.
- Uran project at ~96-97% completion; no major exposures remaining.
- Expected to **break even** on the process equipment/flare business at ~INR 20 crore level.

**Client Addition & Growth Strategy:**
- Two-pronged approach:
  1. **Low-hanging fruit:** Aggressive pursuit of existing product lines in structural, automotive, oil & gas with stronger CRM process.
  2. **New segments:** Entering wind, renewables, etc. — getting approvals and testing procedures done; benefits expected over next 1-2 years.
- Strong key customer account team and CRM process implemented in recent months.

**Margin Outlook:**
- EBITDA margin improvement of **100-200 bps** possible over coming periods (not immediate, takes time).
- Improvement partly from gross margins, partly from operational efficiencies.
- No downside or upside case seen in welding business margins currently.

**Revenue Growth Outlook:**
- **No formal guidance given** — management repeatedly stated they do not give guidance.
- Referenced IIP/GDP numbers of 6-8% as a baseline; believes they can do better than that.
- Supply chain shocks and macro uncertainty make forecasting difficult.
- **Vision of INR 2,000 crores by FY29** (mentioned in a prior presentation post-merger) — management confirmed they are "still following the principle" but emphasized the main driver is to **triple earnings** over that period.
- Acknowledged that 18-20% YoY growth for two years (to reach INR 1,500-1,600 crores) is "definitely feasible" but dependent on macroeconomic support.

**CAPEX:**
- FY27 CAPEX: **INR 30-35 crores** (could stretch to INR 40 crores over next two years each year).
- Maintenance CAPEX: ~INR 10-12 crores.
- New lines coming for this year and next year.
- Primarily in **welding segment**, specifically **consumables** (not equipment).
- To double volume in 5-7 years, estimated CAPEX of **INR 100-150 crores** (plant, machinery, capability — excluding land).

**Capacity Utilization:**
- Currently operating at approximately **70%** capacity.
- Some lines at 90-95% utilization requiring CAPEX and new technology investment.
- Normal operating range: 70-90% depending on equipment age and type.

**M&A / Acquisitions:**
- **Open to acquisitions**, but more targeted now.
- Priority: Clear up Uran, merger, and other "heavy baggage" items first (now largely done).
- Acquisitions will be **technology-driven** rather than purely market share-driven.
- Exploring **product adjacency opportunities** related to fabrication, joining, or cutting of steel.
- No large-ticket diversification investments at the moment.

**Exports:**
- FY26 exports were **fairly flattish** compared to prior year (last year ~INR 154 crores).
- Some pockets (e.g., Saudi) were softer due to structural issues; other markets performed better.
- **Middle East** remains core; Saudi Arabia was the second-largest market outside India last year, now third.
- Order book, inquiry base, and approvals suggest exports **should do better** this year.
- March quarter had some dispatch disruption (first 3-4 weeks) due to geopolitical issues; most pent-up demand has been cleaned up.
- **US:** Tariff issues held back, but more traction seen in last two months.
- **Europe:** Some interest after FTA; building up distribution model.

**Shipbuilding:**
- Big focus area; quantum of welding in shipbuilding is very large.
- ~75-80% of approvals in place.
- Industry thumb rule: ~2% of steel used in shipbuilding is welding consumables.
- Management cautious about revenue projections — called INR 1,000 crores from shipbuilding alone "a very, very large number" for a company of their size.

**Key Industries Driving Growth:**
- Heavy engineering, structural fabrication (primary driver).
- Shipbuilding, defense, renewables, railways.
- Automotive picking up; thermal, oil & gas, pipeline work ongoing.
- Wind: getting approvals and testing procedures done.

**Market Share:**
- Approximately **17-18%** (plus or minus a few percent) in domestic market.

**Order Book:**
- 80-85% of sales through distribution system; no long order book periods.
- Rotating quick cycle; currently in line with what has been seen over last 3-4 months.

**BIS & Income Tax Matters:**
- BIS hearings mostly closed; still needs to go back to magistrate court (date in June-July). Hopeful of a "logical" outcome.
- Income tax demand of ~INR 14 crores: Appeal filed last week; management sees no merit in department's claim; hopeful of favorable outcome.

**Shareholder Value / Market Cap:**
- Management acknowledged market cap hasn't moved despite good work.
- Believes market understands them well; some "holding back" items (Uran, merger, etc.) are now reaching closure.
- "We leave it to the market to take it forward."

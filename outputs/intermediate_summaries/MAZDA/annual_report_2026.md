## 🏁 FINAL ASSESSMENT

### Accounting Quality Score: **AVERAGE** 🟡

**Positives:**
- Clean audit opinion; zero debt; strong promoter family ownership (49%)
- High-quality independent board with financial expertise
- Conservative accounting policies; no aggressive revenue recognition
- Consistent dividend track record (now Rs. 4/share post-split)
- Strong gross margins maintained; modest EBITDA expansion

**Concerns:**
- 🔴 **Operating cash flow deeply negative** despite reported profit — Rs. 48.93 Cr absorbed in working capital
- 🔴 **Inventory pile-up** (4.3x WIP surge) needs explanation — order book conversion or demand slowdown?
- 🟡 Revenue & PAT below FY24 peak — recovery narrative is partial
- 🟡 Related-party procurement concentration (H.T. Engineering ~Rs. 5.7 Cr) — arm's length but worth monitoring
- 🟡 Receivables aging: Rs. 6.32 Cr beyond 12 months (vs Rs. 4.68 Cr prior)
- 🟡 New Independent Directors — limited tenure before re-appointment evaluation

**Bottom Line:** A financially conservative, family-controlled engineering + niche food business with solid asset base and zero leverage, but the FY26 cash flow divergence from reported profit is the single most important red flag requiring management commentary on inventory build-up rationale.

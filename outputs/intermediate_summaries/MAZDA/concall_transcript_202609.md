# Mazda Limited — Investor Conference Call Analysis (5 September 2024)

---

## 1. Management Commentary

### Mr. Percy Avari (Whole Time Director — Engineering Division)
- **Tone:** Confident, articulate, mildly cautionary where competition is concerned. Conversational with analysts.
- Walked through the order book by vertical, delivery cycles (3–4 months for vacuum systems; 4–5 months for evaporators), and explained weak H1 FY25 performance as a client-side issue rather than a demand issue.
- Stressed that Mazda restricts itself from exporting vacuum systems to avoid internal competition with Croll-Reynolds, but is free to export surface condensers, evaporators, and feed water heaters.
- Highlighted the Croll-Reynolds partnership as the "most successful division," with negotiations underway to shift more fabrication work from China to India.
- Spoke candidly about rising competition: *"The rate at which competition is increasing is much higher [than market growth]."*
- On new products: Smart Rod, Ion Exchange Membrane, Advanced Evaporation System (MDR+TVR, patented), High Gravity-Based Equipment (CO₂/SO₂/NH₃ capture), Chemical-Free CIP for dairy.
- On growth potential from new products: a successful new product could deliver "around 20 to 25%" boost to growth.
- Engineering capacity ceiling: ~₹250–260 crore with current setup.

### Mrs. Shanaya Mody Khatua (Whole Time Director — Foods Division)
- **Tone:** Calm, pragmatic, conservative; emphasizes margin discipline over aggressive top-line expansion.
- Achieved ₹30 Crore turnover target for FY24; on track for FY25 target.
- Capacity: Single-shift full utilization = ~₹60 crore. Considering further expansion.
- Bottom-line suppressed by depreciation; expects stabilization in 3–4 years as topline grows.
- Distribution expansion: Delhi/NCR already set up; Gujarat now operational. State-by-state approach to control marketing costs.
- Ready-to-eat segment expected to launch within 6–8 months; brand-building typically takes 2–3 years.
- Medium-term food growth target: **15–20% YoY.**
- Aims to *maintain* (not expand) current profitability — explicitly avoids niche/high-margin categories like protein bars.

### Mr. Mohib Khericha (Chairman & Independent Director)
- **Tone:** Reassuring, shareholder-friendly, optimistic.
- Concluded that order bookings slowed during election period, expected to recover with policy clarity.
- Food: targets ₹60 crore annual turnover within 3 years (full capacity). Well-positioned financially for further investment.
- Engineering: capacity expandable; land and building available; financially equipped.
- Smart Rod highlighted as potentially transformative due to exclusivity ("no one else is producing it").

### Mr. Cyrus Bhagwagar (CFO)
- Minimal airtime. Provided foods division export/domestic split on request (domestic 8–10%, exports ~90%) and a brief, non-committal margin comment: *"We have more or less maintained the margin."*

## 2. Focus Areas

### Product Launches (Engineering)
| Product | Status / Timing |
|---|---|
| Smart Rod (non-chemical anti-scaling) | Industrial trials positive; orders from Hindustan Coca-Cola, showcased at Atlanta HQ. In discussions with Aditya Birla, Reliance (Jamnagar/Mumbai), Adani, BKT. |
| Ion Exchange Membrane | First-of-its-kind in India; ~1 year to launch; collaborating with a large research organization. |
| Advanced Evaporation System (MDR+TVR hybrid) | Patent received. |
| High Gravity-Based Equipment (CO₂/SO₂/NH₃ capture) | Launch expected in 2027; targeted at refineries/fertilizer plants. |
| Chemical-Free CIP (Dairy) | Alpha trials underway; launch in latter half of 2026. |

### Channel & Margins
- Croll-Reynolds channel offers "best profit margins" due to high-buying-power geography (US/UK/Germany).
- Vacuum systems > evaporators in profitability; evaporators used for effluent water recovery are sometimes mandated by pollution control boards, limiting pricing power.
- Foods division: targeting mass market, not niche — *"we would be looking to maintain at best our current profitability."*

### Inventory / Dispatch Issues (Q1–Q2 FY25)
- Weak H1 attributed to clients failing to lift ready equipment (fund crunch, project delays, export commitments, high freight).
- Plus 15–20 days of lost performance testing due to scheduled boiler dismantling/maintenance.
- Management expects FY25 to **match or exceed** FY23–24.

### Direct Distribution (Foods)
- Direct distribution model adopted state-by-state.
- Teams set up in Delhi/NCR; operations now extended to Gujarat.
- Distributor and super-stockist network established in Gujarat.
- Rationale: expensive to market nationwide; preference for healthy bottom line.

## 3. Future Outlook & Guidance

### Volume Growth
- **Engineering:** 10–15% YoY growth in the medium term, potentially "exponential" if new products gain traction. Long-term aspiration (Smart Rod alone) cited as 20–25% growth contributor.
- **Foods:** 15–20% YoY growth targeted. ₹60 crore turnover targeted within 3 years (Chairman).

### Margins / EBITDA Bridge Components
- No formal EBITDA bridge provided. Components alluded to:
  - **Mix shift:** Higher-margin vacuum systems + Croll-Reynolds exports vs. lower-margin evaporators (effluent-driven) and mass-market foods.
  - **Operating leverage:** Shanaya explicitly stated depreciation impact will dilute as top line grows (3–4 year horizon).
  - **Seasonality / lumpiness:** Foods division heavily exposed to macro (e.g., Nigeria naira crisis); Sudan conflict now resolved.
  - **ESOP reversal:** Not mentioned in the call.
  - Channel mix: Direct distribution in foods vs. agent-based export push for engineering.

### CAPEX
- **Engineering:** Existing setup sufficient up to ₹250–260 crore; land available for additional ₹20 crore of capacity if needed. Large land parcel adjacent to food division available for future scale-up — but *"not happening in next 2–3 years."*
- **Maintenance CapEx:** Low — primarily DG sets, welding sets, plate bending, test facility/boiler.
- **Foods:** Considering production expansion (single shift at ₹60 crore); financial position described as strong enough for further investment.

### M&A Integration
- **No M&A or acquisition discussed** during the call.
- *Fluence Pharma, BTM/Reginald* — **Not mentioned at all in this transcript.**
- Croll-Reynolds partnership is the only external alliance referenced (commercial partnership, not M&A).

## 4. Q&A Dynamics

### Areas Where Management Was Evasive
- **CFO Cyrus Bhagwagar:** Foods division margin question — *"I think we have more or less maintained the margin. So, I think we will be able to do that."* — vague, no specific number.
- **Percy on aftermarket revenue surge (₹4.55 cr → ₹11 cr):** Admitted *"I'm not entirely sure"*; attributed to "one or two big orders" using "exotic materials." Investor correctly inferred this was not sustainable; Percy did not contradict.
- **Percy on order book growth:** Acknowledged flat trend, cited potential solar orders under negotiation (₹31–35 cr) that didn't close. No firm target given.
- **Percy on market sizing for new products:** *"There is no limit to this market"* — deliberately vague; only a "modest 10–15%" internal target cited.
- **Investor 7 (Anuj) on aftermarket / repeat engagement:** Percy conceded directly: *"Unfortunately, they will continue to be one-time installation type of equipments."* Also on premium pricing for quality — said Indian market is price-sensitive, reverse-auction driven, faceless, L-1 focused: *"Unfortunately, again both your questions I could not give a positive reply."* (Notably candid.)
- **Shanaya on Ready-to-Eat segment:** Declined profitability profile detail: *"Not necessarily better... we would be looking to maintain at best our current profitability."*

### Areas Where Management Was Highly Confident
- **Smart Rod acceptance trajectory:** Coca-Cola showcasing at Atlanta HQ; Aditya Birla encouraging; Reliance/Adani/BKT in discussions.
- **Croll-Reynolds relationship:** "Satisfied with quality and timely deliveries"; actively negotiating shift of China-fabricated orders to Mazda.
- **Capacity headroom:** Engineering ₹250–260 cr; Foods ₹60 cr (single shift); land and financial position cited as ready.
- **FY25 recovery:** Multiple confirmations that full year will match/exceed FY23–24.
- **Cost-benefit of Smart Rod:** Payback periods cited confidently (1.5 months to 1 year depending on client size); 70K–1L/month chemical cost savings; downtime cost of up to ₹1 crore/day.

### Q&A Summary
- Total of 7 analysts/ investors asked questions.
- Topics ranged: R&D depth, exports/domestic split, product selection criteria, aftermarket revenue spike, order book stagnation, anti-scaling product economics, market sizing, capacity/CapEx, foods overseas management, marketing/branding, new customer additions, niche vs mass strategy, repeat revenue potential, reverse-auction impact, ready-to-eat profitability.
- Management was notably transparent about **competition, price sensitivity, and limitations** — answering some questions with frank admissions rather than corporate deflection.

# Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Limited - Q4 & FY26 Earnings Call Transcript Analysis

## 1. Management Commentary: Key Statements

**CEO (Mr. Vijay Sanghavi) – Opening Remarks & Core Commentary:**
- Highlights company's 25-year history, position as India's largest stainless steel washer producer with extensive SKU range and full vertical integration (backward & forward).
- Emphasizes diversification into four product lines: Fasteners (nut, bolt, washers), Spring/Lock/Value washers & circlips (high value-add), Sheet metal components (solar, auto), and Tubes & Pipes.
- Notes backward integration via melting/rolling divisions for coils, sheets, and flat products.
- Stressing strong export record (15 consecutive years of export trophies) and global presence in 31 countries.
- Points to consistent ~25% CAGR over the last three years in Revenue, EBITDA, and PAT.
- Announces greenfield CAPEX (Capex 1, 2, 2A) and future greenfield/brownfield projects.
- Signals intent for inorganic growth to maintain growth trajectory.

**CCL Project Focus (Primary Management Commentary):**
- **Technology Partner:** "We have been tied up with the Chinese company for the technological partner... a core agreement between both companies to establish production, machine erection, commissioning, formulas, and the epoxy." (Mr. Sanghavi)
- **Project Timeline & Progress:** "We have already placed the order for the machine, and already the second round of inspection has been done. Progress of the site: civil work is going on, ground, civil, and PV work has already been in full swing." Target is to commence production by November 2026.
- **Strategic Rationale & Market:** Positioned as an import substitute for Copper-Clad Laminate (CCL), a basic raw material for PCB and chip manufacturing. "We have been one of the first company over there who can establish this manufacturing facility in India first time."
- **Financial Support & Subsidies:** Approved ₹338 crore under ECMS (Central Govt.) and GEP (Gujarat State) schemes. Highlights ~50% capex subsidy (25% Central + 25% State), interest subsidies, power cost exemptions, and logistics support.
- **Competitive Edge:** Claims a 10-13% cost advantage over imports. Notes government-backed 2% cash subsidy for buyers of indigenous products. "Our output of the production cost can be easily bid to the Chinese products in terms of quality and commercial viability."

**Guidance & Outlook (Mr. Sanghavi & Mr. Panchal):**
- **Revenue Roadmap:** Targets ₹1,350 crore for FY27 (existing business growing at 25% CAGR). Aims for ₹1,800 crore from existing lines and ₹750 crore from CCL (all 5 lines at 69% utilization) to reach a consolidated top line of ₹2,500 crore within 2-3 years.
- **Margin Guidance:** Individual CCL business projected at ~20% EBITDA and ~13% PAT. Consolidated margins (Stainless + CCL) guided at ~13.5% EBITDA and ~9.7-10.5% PAT at ₹2,500 crore revenue.
- **CAPEX & Fundraising:** Plans to raise ₹330 crore via QIP (or equivalent) by September 2026 for CCL project CAPEX (~₹400 crore) and working capital. Promoter to infuse ₹115 crore via warrants (₹46 crore remaining post partial payment).
- **Effective Tax Rate:** Expected in the 13-15% range, influenced by depreciation (including solar CAPEX) and ongoing capex.

## 2. Tone, Clarity, and Confidence of Responses

- **Tone:** The management, particularly CEO Mr. Sanghavi, exhibits **high enthusiasm and confidence**, especially regarding the CCL project. Language is optimistic, using terms like "excitement," "very much aggressive," and "very much sure and confident."
- **Clarity:**
  - **CCL Project:** Largely clear on timeline, subsidy structure, and strategic rationale. Less clarity on specific machinery details and precise cost breakdowns.
  - **Existing Business:** Clear on historical growth (25% CAGR) and product diversity.
  - **Financials:** Some ambiguity arises on cash flow questions (see Q&A Dynamics). Funding roadmap for CCL is explained but details on warrant dilution timeline are slightly murky.
- **Confidence:** High confidence in CCL demand, competitive positioning, and government support. Moderate confidence in meeting near-term revenue targets for existing business. **Evades specific commitment** on receiving hard purchase orders for CCL, stating only "soft confirmations" exist.

## 3. Focus Areas

**Product & Project Focus:**
- **CCL (Copper-Clad Laminate):** Dominated the call. Key details: technology partner (Chinese), machinery procurement and inspection timeline, November 2026 production start, import substitution, 5 planned lines, 338 crore subsidy.
- **Existing Stainless Steel Business:** Reinforced market leadership in washers, diversification into high-value components, and customer base across auto, railways, defense, pharma, dairy, etc. Mentioned adding new SKUs (circlips, lock washers).

**Channel & Inventory:**
- No specific commentary on channel margins or inventory levels was provided in this transcript. Focus was on project CAPEX and new product launch.

**Government Schemes & Subsidies:**
- Detailed discussion on PLI/ECMS and GEP benefits: ~50% capital subsidy, interest subvention (~7% subsidy on debt), power cost exemptions, logistics support.

## 4. Future Outlook & Guidance

**Marginal & Growth Metrics:**
- **EBITDA Bridge:** Existing business at 9-10.5% EBITDA; CCL at 20% EBITDA. Consolidation to drive blended EBITDA to ~13.5%.
- **Volume Growth:** Existing business to grow at ~25% CAGR. CCL is a new volume driver with global demand growing at 30-40% CAGR.
- **CAPEX:** Significant CAPEX planned: ~₹400 crore for CCL (including solar), plus working capital support. Funded via QIP and promoter warrants.
- **M&A Integration (Fluence Pharma, BTM/Reginald):** **No mention** of these entities in the transcript. Management discussed only an unnamed European acquisition for "customer acquisition" and "precision components," targeting closure in 10-12 months pending due diligence.
- **Seasonality:** Not discussed.
- **ESOP Reversal:** Not discussed.

## 5. Q&A Dynamics: Analyst Queries vs. Management Answers

**Analyst Probes & Management Confidence/Evasiveness:**

1.  **CCL Viability & Competition (Analyst: Sanjay Shah):**
    - **Query:** Rational for entering without expertise, competition from China/Korea, anti-dumping duties.
    - **Answer:** Confident on cost competitiveness, local buyer advantages, and zero anti-dumping duty currently. Stated no royalty to tech partner, covered under project cost. **Response: Confident, detailed.**

2.  **CCL Technology & Financials (Analyst: Manan Shah, Ankur Gulati):**
    - **Query:** Specific technology partner name, BIS certification, debt tied-up, advance payments to Chinese vendor.
    - **Answer:** Partner is a Chinese company (name not revealed). Confirmed advance paid, second tranche in process. Confirmed debt signing (implied). **Response: Partially evasive on partner name, confident on progress.**

3.  **Revenue Guidance & Dilution (Analysts: Sanjay Shah, Tanmay Jhaveri, Anuj Golchha):**
    - **Query:** Rationale for ₹2,500 crore target, downgrade from previous ₹1,500 crore FY27 guidance, promoter dilution post-QIP and warrants.
    - **Answer:** Reaffirmed ₹2,500 crore target (₹1,800 from existing + ₹750 from CCL). Clarified ₹1,500 crore for FY27 is still target (25% growth). Post-dilution, promoter holding ~42-43%. **Response: Confident, but slightly repetitive in reaffirming targets.**

4.  **CCL Demand & Pre-orders (Analyst: Tanmay Jhaveri):**
    - **Query:** Any contracts or pre-orders for CCL?
    - **Answer:** "We get many of the soft confirmations... one or two companies are ready to buy our first line." **Response: Non-committal, evasive on binding orders.**

5.  **Cash Flow Concerns (Analyst: Harsh Shah):**
    - **Query:** Explanation for negative OCF of ₹48 crore in FY26.
    - **Answer:** CFO (Mr. Panchal) initially struggled to address, suggested it was due to CAPEX advances and QIP funds parked as FDs. Later clarified: "We are factoring certain figures... making payments to advances to our CAPEX suppliers... once capitalization will be in place... cash flows will again get improved." **Response: Initially evasive/unclear, later provided a technical reason tied to CAPEX timing.**

6.  **CCL CAPEX Breakdown & Utilization (Analyst: Jay Vekariya, Vineet Khatri):**
    - **Query:** Split of CAPEX (machine, land, civil), timeline for 5 lines, utilization ramp-up.
    - **Answer:** 70% on equipment/machinery, 18% on building, 12% misc. (Land already with company). Lines to be installed over next 2 years post first line. Target 80-85% utilization by 2028. **Response: Clear and confident on breakdown and timeline.**

7.  **Tax Rate (Analysts: Darshil Jhaveri, Anuj Golchha):**
    - **Query:** Inconsistent tax rate and future effective rate.
    - **Answer:** Guided 13-15%, influenced by depreciation and capex benefits. **Response: Clear guidance given.**

**Overall Q&A Takeaways:** Management was **highly confident and forward-looking** on the CCL project, its subsidies, and demand prospects. They were **clear on revenue/CAPEX guidance** for both businesses. However, they appeared **slightly evasive on specifics** regarding the CCL tech partner's name, binding pre-orders, and the precise mechanics of the negative cash flow, providing clarifications only upon repeated questioning.

**RATNAVEER PRECISION ENGINEERING LIMITED - ANNUAL REPORT FY 2024-25 ANALYSIS**

---

**MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key Themes:** Steady growth, operational efficiency, global expansion, sustainability, and entry into the fastener segment.
- **Stated Opportunities:** Global infrastructure boom, EV sector adoption of stainless steel, high-growth markets in North America/SE Asia/Latin America.
- **Risks Mentioned:** Raw material price volatility, compliance risk, cyclical demand, and market competition.
- **Strategic Priorities:** Expanding production capacity, automation, R&D for new products, and enhancing supply chain via digital tools and logistics partnerships.
- **Walk-the-Talk:** MD stated FY25 revenue grew ~50%; actual growth was 49.80% (₹891.87 cr vs ₹595.37 cr). EBITDA guidance was not quantified, but margin improved from 9.57% to 10.09%. Guidance was directional and largely met.

**BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
- **Core Segments & Revenue Mix:** SS Washers (~7% of revenue), SS Tubes & Pipes (~21%), SS Finishing Sheets (~52%), Scrap Reprocessing & Other (~20%).
- **New Additions:** Began production of wire; expanded product portfolio to over 2,500 washer SKUs. Geographic footprint spans 31 countries.
- **Capex:** Completed ₹138.12 crore in FY25 (vs ₹66 crore prior). Initiated Phase II of ₹67.80 crore for FY26. Key commissioned: 2 MW rooftop solar plant, advanced CNC equipment. Key pending: 2 MW ground-mounted solar plant, further automation.

**FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK**
- **Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y Trend):**
  - Revenue: ₹428.5 cr (FY22) → ₹602.4 cr (FY24) → ₹891.9 cr (FY25). Strong upward trajectory.
  - PAT: ₹94.76 cr (FY22) → ₹310.50 cr (FY24) → ₹468.15 cr (FY25).
- **EBITDA Margin Trend:** 6.78% (FY22) → 9.57% (FY24) → 10.09% (FY25). Expanding.
- **CFO vs PAT:** PAT ₹468.15 cr; CFO from Operations ₹949.28 cr. Profit is converting strongly to cash.
- **Working Capital Movement:**
  - Receivable Days: Improved (Trade Receivable Turnover: 11.01 in FY24 to 16.16 in FY25).
  - Inventory Days: Worsened (Inventory Turnover: 2.27 in FY24 to 2.81 in FY25). Inventory ballooned to ₹292.85 cr from ₹244.93 cr.
- **ROCE/ROE Trend:**
  - ROCE: 12.80% (FY24) → 14.30% (FY25). Improving.
  - ROE: 16.69% (FY24) → 15.03% (FY25). Deteriorated slightly due to equity dilution.
- **Debt Position:**
  - Net Debt: ₹129.26 cr (FY25) vs ₹147.70 cr (FY24). Improved.
  - Debt/Equity: 0.65 (FY25) vs 0.85 (FY24). Improved.
  - Interest Coverage: Strong (EBITDA/Interest = ~7.1x).

**FORENSIC FLAGS**
- **Related Party Transactions (RPT):** Significant loans from Promoter (₹226.32 cr outstanding). Rent paid to Promoter entities (₹20.70 cr). All stated to be at arm's length and approved by Audit Committee.
- **Contingent Liabilities vs Net Worth:** Contingent liabilities ₹163.33 cr vs Net Worth ₹370.92 cr (Ratio: 44%). **RED FLAG.**
- **Miscellaneous Expenses as % of Revenue:** Other expenses ₹40.68 cr / Revenue ₹891.87 cr = 4.56%. **RED FLAG (>3%).**
- **Auditor Opinion:** Unqualified. Clean report.
- **Key Audit Matters:** None highlighted in the provided auditor's report.
- **CARO Report:** Noted (i) withdrawal of resolutions after notice (non-compliance with SS-2), (ii) late intimation for a Board meeting (violation of SEBI LODR), and (iii) 5 e-forms filed with additional fees. No material qualifications.
- **Accounting Policy Changes:** No material changes noted. Policies appear consistent.

**GOVERNANCE**
- **Management Remuneration as % of Revenue:** MD salary ₹54.00 lacs / Revenue ₹891.87 cr = ~0.06%. Very low and reasonable.
- **KMP Changes/Resignations:** Two Independent Directors resigned mid-year (cited "preoccupation"). Company Secretary changed twice during the year.
- **Promoter Pledging/Stake:** No pledge mentioned. Promoter holding decreased from 53.55% to 48.95% due to equity issuance to non-promoters.

---

**FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: AVERAGE**

*Reasons:* Strong top-line growth and cash generation. However, flagged by (1) massive contingent liabilities relative to net worth, (2) high miscellaneous expenses, (3) significant related party loan balances, and (4) some corporate governance lapses noted by the Secretarial Auditor. The financials are not aggressive but carry noteworthy balance sheet risks.

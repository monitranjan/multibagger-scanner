### 1. MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY – KEY STATEMENTS  

**Opening Framing**  
- “Paisalo has delivered one of its strongest quarters yet across growth, distribution, technology and asset quality and we believe the momentum we are seeing is not just continuing but building.”  
- “Q1 FY2027 represents more than just a strong start to the year. It demonstrates that the continued investments across distribution, partnerships, technology, and risk management are now compounding together to create a materially stronger and more scalable franchise.”  
- “We are entering an exciting inflection point where growth, productivity, and profitability are reinforcing each other, all the while maintaining our focus on asset quality and prudent risk management.”  

**Regulatory Tailwind (RBI Collateral-Free Threshold)**  
- RBI circular raising collateral-free lending limit for micro/small enterprises to ₹20 lakh called a “decisive and welcome step forward for financial inclusion.”  
- “The competitive advantage is likely to shift increasingly towards origination capability, borrower understanding, data-led underwriting, and collection effectiveness. This is exactly the terrain on which we believe Paisalo is built to win.”  
- Impact seen mainly on **co-origination/co-lending ecosystem**: “will allow a faster STP integration… meaningfully improve formal credit access for a large set of borrowers who have traditionally faced challenges because they do not have collateral backed funding.”

**Liability Action**  
- Approved Tranche 1 of public NCD issue: up to ₹300 Cr under ₹900 Cr shelf; issue opens August 7, 2026.  

**Strategic Pillars (Reiterated as “Four Reinforcing Pillars”)**  
1. **Distribution** – 5,995 touch points across 23 states (+696 in 4 quarters). BC franchise crossed **USD 1 billion gross transaction value**. Partnerships with SBI, Bank of India, Indian Overseas Bank.  
2. **Product Diversification** – Core MSME/micro-enterprise; added **6 new product lines in Q1** (mobility, industrial equipment, medical equipment, agriculture equipment, alternative fuel). Asset-light via institutional/OEM partnerships.  
3. **Technology & AI** – AI embedded across sourcing, onboarding, underwriting, risk, monitoring, engagement, collections.  
   - Onboarding applications: ~180k (vs 160k in Q4 FY26)  
   - Voice-to-data convergence: ~500k (vs 350k)  
   - AI outbound calls: ~200k/day (vs 150k/day)  
   - AI bots: 18 (up from 7)  
   - Branch/BC platform revamp underway for “fully AI-assisted digital journey.”  
4. **Liability Management** – Cost of borrowing 10.1% (down 64 bps YoY, ~300 bps below FY2021). Focus on diversifying sources, deepening relationships, optimizing mix.  

**Three-Year Ambition**  
- “Approximately double our AUM, income and profitability while maintaining strong asset quality, healthy returns and a robust capital position.”  
- Supported by: nationwide distribution, expanding BC network, institutional partnerships, strengthened liability profile, AI across core processes.

### 2. TONE, CLARITY & CONFIDENCE  

- **Confident & Data-Driven**: Agarwal cited precise quarterly metrics (disbursements, touch points, AI bot counts, call volumes) and multi-year trends (cost of borrowing trajectory, credit cost history).

- **Employee-Centric Risk View**: “The only thing that I care about the most which does not show up in any P&L or balance sheet is my employees and the staff that I work with.”

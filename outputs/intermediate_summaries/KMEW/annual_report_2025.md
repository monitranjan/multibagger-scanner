### **ANALYSIS OF KNOWLEDGE MARINE & ENGINEERING WORKS LIMITED (KMEW) – ANNUAL REPORT FY2024-25**

---

### **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
- **Key Themes**: Celebrating a decade of transformation; record financial performance; fleet expansion to 40+ vessels; entry into shipbuilding via acquisition; adoption of tonnage tax regime from FY26; focus on operational excellence and technology.
- **Opportunities**: India’s maritime sector growth (Sagarmala, Maritime Vision 2030); Green Tug Transition Program; inland waterway modernization; international expansion (Myanmar, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Bangladesh, Maldives).
- **Risks**: Operational (project delays, equipment failure), regulatory (tax/customs changes), financial (cash flow variability), environmental, and HR (talent retention).
- **Strategic Priorities**: Deepening inland waterway share; scaling shipbuilding; digital/green tech adoption; new geography entry; long-term entry into large TSHD dredger market.
- **Walk-the-Talk**: FY25 revenue grew 23% YoY; EBITDA margin expanded to 41.6%; fleet doubled; unexecuted order book ₹882 Cr. Execution aligns with stated priorities.

---

### **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
- **Core Segments**: Dredging (capital/maintenance) ~96% of revenue; owning/chartering port ancillary crafts; shipbuilding & repair (new segment).
- **Revenue Mix**: India 58.9%, Myanmar 18.3%, Bahrain 22.8% (consolidated).
- **New Additions**: Shipbuilding (via Kamal Marine acquisition); cutter suction dredgers; 60T bollard pull tug; Bahrain operations (sand mining).
- **Capex vs Planned**: Gross block increased 74% YoY (₹23,686 lakhs). CWIP at ₹5,015 lakhs (vessels under construction). Fleet expanded from 16 to 40 vessels. Pending: Additional vessels under CWIP.

---

### **FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK**
- **Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y)**: Revenue: ₹6,110L (FY22) → ₹20,150L (FY23) → ₹16,358L (FY24) → ₹20,071L (FY25). PAT: ₹2,090L → ₹4,720L → ₹3,330L → ₹4,960L. Volatile but recovering.
- **EBITDA Margin**: 53% (FY22) → 34% (FY23) → 30% (FY24) → 41.6% (FY25). Expanded in FY25 after contraction.
- **CFO vs PAT**: CFO ₹5,784L vs PAT ₹4,960L (FY25). Conversion ratio 1.17x; healthy cash generation.
- **Working Capital**: Receivable days ~101 (FY25) vs ~86 (FY24). Inventory days minimal (~4 days). Receivables growth outpaces revenue growth.
- **ROCE/ROE Trend**: ROE: 43% (FY22) → 28% (FY25). ROCE: 43% (FY22) → 24% (FY25). Both below peak levels but improved from FY24.
- **Debt**: Total debt ₹13,348L; D/E 0.61; Interest coverage 7.35x (down from 11.36x). Net debt ₹8,851L.

### **FORENSIC FLAGS**
- **Related Party Transactions**: Loans to subsidiaries ₹500L; corporate guarantees ₹5,200L; asset purchases from Kamal Marine ₹287L. All disclosed and board-approved; no suspicious flows noted.
- **Contingent Liabilities vs Net Worth**: Contingent liabilities ₹11,221L (bank guarantees, corporate guarantees, insurance bonds) vs net worth ₹21,989L → **51% (RED FLAG)**.
- **Miscellaneous Expenses as % of Revenue**: Other expenses ₹2,043L / revenue ₹20,071L = **10.2% (RED FLAG)**.
- **Auditor Opinion**: Unqualified (clean) for both standalone and consolidated.
- **Key Audit Matters**: PPE/CWIP capitalization, guarantees to group companies. Standard for asset-heavy business; no red flags.
- **CARO Report**: No qualifications or adverse remarks; compliance with Sections 185/186; no fraud reported.
- **Accounting Policy Changes**: Transition to Ind AS (first-time adoption). Policies conservative; no aggressive changes.

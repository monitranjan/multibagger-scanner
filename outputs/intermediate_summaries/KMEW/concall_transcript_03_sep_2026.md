### **Analysis of KMEW Q1 FY27 Earnings Conference Call Transcript**

**Date of Call:** August 26, 2026
**Management Present:** Mr. Sujay Kewalramani (CEO), Mrs. Kanak Kewalramani (CFO)

---

### **1. Management Commentary: Key Statements**

**CFO (Kanak Kewalramani) - Opening Remarks:**
*   Highlighted a "very strong note" start to FY27, demonstrating "operating leverage" from the intersection of "specialized assets, technical capability and disciplined project execution."
*   Reported Q1 FY27 financials: Revenue INR 115.41 Cr (+138% YoY), EBITDA INR 73.41 Cr (+258% YoY, ~64% margin), PAT INR 62.75 Cr (+466% YoY, ~54% margin).
*   Attributed strong performance to the dredging segment, specifically two projects: capital rock dredging at JNPA and maintenance dredging at Pondicherry port. Emphasized the "technically complex nature" of the JNPA assignment.
*   Outlined three verticals: **Dredging** (core, diversified fleet), **Charter & Hire** (recurring revenue, multi-year contracts, growing "green marine assets"), and **Shipbuilding** (scaling via new Saphale shipyard, Phase 1 to 3 to build ~18 vessels/year).
*   Mentioned a key shipbuilding contract: INR 62.40 Cr from IWAI for 10 hybrid electric passenger ferries, aligned with the "Harit Nauka" mission.
*   Stated the business is "progressively creating an integrated maritime platform" to design, build, own, operate, maintain, and charter vessels.
*   Disclosed a **balance order book of >INR 1,300 Cr** (Dredging: ~240 Cr, Charter & Hire: ~850 Cr, Shipbuilding: ~240 Cr), excluding INR 200 Cr intercompany order. Bid pipeline is ~INR 3,500+ Cr.
*   Emphasized a **selective bidding discipline**, targeting EBITDA margins of **35% to 40%**.
*   Announced a strengthened capital base via a INR 150 Cr preferential issue (from 360 ONE PIPE Fund, FLC Investco LLC, Bank of India MF) and a INR 100 Cr block deal with SBI Funds Management.
*   Identified two key growth drivers: scaling dredging volumes with larger capacity dredgers, and scaling shipbuilding from Q4 FY27 with the new yard.

**CEO (Sujay Kewalramani) - Key Points from Q&A:**
*   **Guidance:** Revised full-year growth guidance upward from 30-40% YoY to **"potential in northwards of 60%"** for FY27.
*   **Project Update:** Confirmed all funds due from the DCI Mangrol project have been received.
*   **Dredging Execution:** Expects to execute INR 200 Cr from the current dredging order book this year. Believes post-monsoon, several bids from the pipeline will convert into real business.
*   **Margin Outlook:** Stated that while Q1 EBITDA margin was 63%, this is **"not the guidance"** (guidance remains 35-40%). However, **"with volumes in terms of dredging, the operating margin numbers are going to expand."**
*   **Competitive Landscape (vs. DCI):** Attributed better margins to younger, more efficient equipment and the ability to "better sweat our equipment."
*   **Capital Allocation & Dilution:** Promoters are "very comfortable" at ~55% stake. The company is **"fully capitalized to achieve our turnover of a top line of INR 1,000 crores"** projected for FY29. No further dilution planned.
*   **CAPEX:** Total ongoing capex is ~INR 1,000 Cr over the next 1.5 years. Breakdown: ~450 Cr for dredgers, ~250 Cr for Green Tugs/vessels, balance for shipyard.
*   **TAM Expansion:** Capex will add ~INR 100-120 Cr in maintenance dredging revenue this year, surpassing INR 250 Cr next year. Capital dredging to add ~70 Cr this year, ~150 Cr next year.
*   **Segment Mix Outlook (2-3 years):** Revenue mix expected to shift from current ~80% dredging to **45-50% dredging, 40-45% shipbuilding, and 5% chartering**.
*   **Shipbuilding Margins:** Clarified that while pre-subsidy margins are 15-20%, **post-subsidy margins "will easily go beyond 35%."**
*   **Shipyard Status & Orders:** Shipyard (Saphale) under construction, Phase I to be operational by **end of FY27**, starting vessel launches. Already building a bid pipeline for the yard. Current order book includes ~42 vessels for third parties and 5 for captive use.
*   **Competition & Moat:** For dredging, faces "very little to no competition." Believes there is room for new players, but KMEW is "pick and choose" about bidding to maintain margins. Dredging is seen as a "perpetual business" with strong government responsibility.
*   **Green Tug Business:** Offers **EBITDA margin of 75%** on long-term (15-year) contracts with major ports (Visakhapatnam, V.O. Chidambaranar), which have a "fixed charter" take-or-pay component.
*   **Export Opportunity:** Exploring opportunities, especially in the European market for vessels up to 3,000 DWT, but awaiting comfort on financial/payment terms compared to domestic orders.
*   **Samudra Manthan Initiative:** Company is preparing by acquiring larger dredgers (5,000-7,000 cu.m capacity) but meaningful deployment is at least **2-3 years away**.

---

### **2. Tone, Clarity, and Confidence of Responses**

*   **Tone:** **Highly Confident, Optimistic, and Strategic.** Management consistently used strong positive language: "excellent set of results," "strong note," "position of strength," "limitless growth," "vision of Billion and Beyond."
*   **Clarity:** **Generally High.** Financial metrics, order book composition, capex breakdown, and segment mix targets were stated clearly and numerically. They provided specific project examples and timelines.
*   **Confidence:** **Very High.** Expressed strong conviction in their operational model, asset efficiency, disciplined bidding, and strategic direction. They confidently upgraded growth guidance and spoke of expanding addressable markets and overcoming entry barriers.
*   **Areas of Strategic Ambiguity:** When asked for specific IRRs on Green Tugs or precise bid hit rates for the future, management deflected or gave aspirational rather than definitive answers ("we always aspire, but we can't really expect"). This suggests a preference for controlling the narrative around strategic goals rather than guaranteeing specific financial parameters.

---

### **3. Focus Areas**

*   **Dredging:** The core earnings driver for the quarter. Focus on **diversified fleet** (trailing suction, cutter suction, backhoe, etc.), execution of complex projects (JNPA, Pondicherry), and expanding into **larger capacity dredgers** (5,000+ cu.m) for bigger contracts.
*   **Shipbuilding & Green Assets:** Major growth pillar. Focus on the **Saphale shipyard scale-up** (Phases 1-3), building **specialized vessels** (Green Tugs, hybrid ferries, workboats) aligned with government missions (Harit Nauka), and creating an **integrated platform** (design-build-own-operate).
*   **Charter & Hire:** Provides **recurring, long-term contract revenue** (10-15 years) with port authorities, adding visibility and stability.
*   **Financial Discipline & Capital Allocation:**
    *   **Bidding Discipline:** Targeting EBITDA margins of 35-40%, selective on technical complexity and returns.
    *   **CAPEX:** ~INR 1,000 Cr investment over 1.5 years across all three verticals to drive future growth.
    *   **Capital Raising:** Strengthened balance sheet with INR 250 Cr from marquee institutional investors, viewed as an endorsement of strategy.
*   **Operational Leverage:** Repeatedly emphasized the high-margin results from efficient deployment of specialized assets and technical expertise.

---

### **4. Future Outlook & Guidance**

*   **Revenue Growth:** **Upgraded FY27 guidance to >60% YoY** from 30-40% previously.
*   **Margins:** Long-term EBITDA margin guidance remains **35-40%**. Management expects margins to **expand** (not contract) due to volume growth in dredging. Q1's 63% margin is not sustainable quarterly but demonstrates leverage potential. Shipbuilding margins post-subsidy are expected to exceed 35%.
*   **Volume Growth:** Significant dredging volume additions expected from new capex. Shipbuilding to scale from Q4 FY27 with yard commissioning.
*   **CAPEX:** Ongoing capex of **~INR 1,000 Cr** over 1.5 years (450 Cr dredgers, 250 Cr green vessels, rest for shipyard).
*   **Long-Term Targets:** Revenue target of **INR 1,000 Cr by FY29**, with segment mix shifting to ~50% dredging, ~40-45% shipbuilding, and ~5% chartering.
*   **M&A Integration:** No discussion of Fluence Pharma or BTM/Reginald in this transcript. The focus was on organic growth through capex and fleet expansion.

---

### **5. Q&A Dynamics**

*   **Analyst Queries:** Focused on margin sustainability, growth drivers, competitive landscape, capex details, shipbuilding capacity/timing, and long-term opportunity size.
*   **Management Responses:**
    *   **Highly Confident On:** Core business model, margin expansion potential, order book conversion, capex plans, and strategic vision for integrated maritime platform.
    *   **Evasive/Strategic Ambiguity On:**
        *   **Hit Rate for Future Bids:** Aspirational ("we always aspire") but declined to guarantee continuation of the >50% historical rate.
        *   **Specific IRRs for Green Tugs:** Directed the analyst to contact IR, avoiding a direct numeric answer.
        *   **Precise Quarterly Margin Trajectory:** Clearly stated that Q1's 63% EBITDA margin is not a guide, steering back to the 35-40% long-term range.
*   **Notably Clear/Strong Responses:**
    *   On the **sustainability of dredging**, CEO strongly rebutted concerns about technological moat or privatization risk, citing perpetual government responsibility and their 11-year track record.
    *   On **capital allocation and promoter dilution**, was unequivocal about being fully capitalized and comfortable with current stakeholding.
    *   On **Green Tug contract terms**, clearly outlined the fixed, take-or-pay nature with government entities.

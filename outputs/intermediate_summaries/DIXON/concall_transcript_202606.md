### 1. Management Commentary (Key statements from CEO, CFO, and leadership)
*Present leadership on call: **Mr. Atul Lall** (Managing Director & Vice Chairman) and **Mr. Saurabh Gupta** (Director & Group CFO).*

**Opening Remarks (Atul Lall):**
- **Financials:** Q1 FY27 (ended June 30, 2026) Revenue = INR 15,557 crores; EBITDA (excl. fair value gain on Dixon’s stake in Aditya Infotech) = INR 472 crores; PAT (excl. gain, after minority interest) = INR 218 crores.
- **Macro/Margins:** Temporary margin compression due to expiry of Mobile PLI 1 (March ’26) and increased selling prices from input cost inflation (optically lower % margins in mobile/IT hardware). Agile cost-plus contracts and pass-through enabled strong revenue growth.
- **Strategy:** Pivot to component backward integration (display, camera modules), participation in Mobile PLI 2 and ECMS to drive absolute profit growth and restore operating margins from FY ’27-’28.
- **Balance Sheet:** ROCE 34.1%, ROE 23.4%; optimal working capital cycle of negative five days.
- **Mobile & EMS:** Revenue INR 14,179 cr, Operating Profit INR 373 cr. Broader smartphone market declined 10-12%; Dixon expects 20-25% QoQ volume growth as demand strengthens (strong order book). Vivo JV PN3 approved July ’26, ops from Q3. Noida 1M sq ft facility nearly done (Q3). Q Tech camera module expansion 70M→180-190M/yr over 15-18 months. Display facility trials Q3, mass prod end Q3/beg Q4.
- **Telecom:** Robust growth (5G/FWA). Microwave backhaul radios scaled; exports this fiscal. JV with Gemtek (Taiwan) for optical transceivers.
- **IT Hardware:** Chennai campus evolving; onboarded gaming notebook customer; Inventec JV (60-40) operational Q4; SSD mfg from Q3; discussing server/data center JV.
- **Home Appliances:** Rev INR 382 cr, OP INR 32 cr. Launched semi-auto WM 16/18kg; Tirupati facility +0.3M units (front-load WM Q3); robotic vacuums, dishwashers, microwaves Q3.
- **Lighting (Signify JV):** Batten capacity 5M units/month; 1,000+ premium SKUs launched; exports to US/Germany Q2/Q3.
- **Consumer Electronics (TV/Refrig):** Rev INR 987 cr, OP INR 58 cr. Mini LED production initiated; OEM model by Q2 ’27. Refrigerator capacity expansion to 3.2M units.
- **Other JVs:** Rexxam Dixon (PCBA) Chennai facility Aug ’26; Imagine Marketing (Hearables) expanding to dash cams, etc.

**CFO (Saurabh Gupta) Interjections:**
- Capex this quarter = INR 335 crores.
- Working capital increase attributed to strategic inventory build (memory price hike) and payment of floating cash to creditors end-March; called “temporary phenomenon.”
- Provided segment revenue splits: Telecom ~INR 2,100 cr, IT Hardware ~INR 1,350 cr.
- Smartphone volumes 7.5M this quarter; exports ~INR 1,100 cr (vol 0.6-0.7M).
- Q Tech revenue ~INR 500 cr, meaningful EBITDA contribution pending.
- PLI 2 understood effective April 1 ’26, FY25-26 base year; incremental production eligible irrespective of export.

### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements)

**Ravi Shroff (Managing Director):**
- **Company Profile:** “One of India’s oldest chemical companies and a pioneer in phosphorus chemistry” with legacy back to 1941. Four product groups: Agrochemical intermediates, Yellow Phosphorus (YP) derivatives, Performance Solutions, Pharma intermediates/APIs. Three sites: Roha, Lote Parshuram, Visakhapatnam. Waste management ~1% of revenues.
- **FY26 Context:** H1 was difficult for Indian agrochemical sector due to “unusually extended monsoon… compressed spray windows, and a build-up of channel inventory at our customer end.” Q3 usually lean; demand turned in Q4. Cyclicity “partly compensated with good performance of YP derivatives and Performance Solutions.”
- **Project Updates:** 2,530 TPA biocides capacity (announced Mar 2025) operational in H2 FY26. MNC contract manufacturing LTA (May 2024) progressing; validation batches dispatched. Nov 2025 agreement on track, capacity expected July 2026. New R&D center at Rabale operationalized.
- **Macro/Operations:** Geopolitical developments causing Q1 raw material challenges; “proactive raw material procurement and inventory management” avoided disruptions. “Largely pass on the raw material price increases.” Uncertainty on agro demand due to El Nino/suboptimal monsoon: “it is not possible to make any forward statements.”
- **Forward Initiatives:** Dedicated CM line by July 2026; plan to “launch one more biocide product in the second half of this financial year [FY27].” Final dividend INR 13.75/share (275% face value).

**Devendra Dosi (CFO):**
- **Financials:** Q4 FY26 standalone revenue INR 281 cr (+13% YoY), Adj EBITDA INR 22 cr (margin 8%), PAT INR 13 cr. FY26 net operating revenue INR 1,094 cr (+11.8%), Adj EBITDA INR 112 cr (margin 10.1%), PAT INR 73 cr (6.7%). “Zero long-term debt and a net cash position… net cash positive company.”
- **Subsidiary Clarification (to Rajeev Jain):** Consolidated PAT delta vs standalone from long-term investments (via 100% subsidiary Kamaljyot) yielding dividends/interest.

**Pradeep Ghattu (President & COO):**
- **Capacity:** Utilization 65%–85% (product-dependent). Focus areas for addition: Performance Solutions, contract manufacturing, YP derivatives. Declined to share total installed capacity “due to commercial reasons.”

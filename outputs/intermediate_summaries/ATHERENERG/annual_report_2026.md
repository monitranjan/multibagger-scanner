### **MANAGEMENT LETTERS**
*   **Key Themes:** Transition from "growth startup" to "institutional benchmark." Emphasis on trust, product superiority, disciplined execution, and building an integrated ecosystem (vehicles, software, charging).
*   **Stated Opportunities:** EL platform for mass market (₹1-1.25L segment), geographic expansion (Rest of India), software monetization (AtherStack), Factory 3.0 scale-up.
*   **Stated Risks:** Geopolitical supply chain disruptions (rare-earth magnets), subsidy rationalization, competitive intensity, need for continued capital for growth.
*   **Strategic Priorities:** 1) Launch EL platform, 2) Expand geography, 3) Deepen software ecosystem.
*   **Walk-the-Talk Check:**
    *   **FY26 Guidance vs Delivery:** Management had hinted at "improving profitability" and "path to positive operating cash flow."
    *   **Delivery:** **Delivered.** EBITDA loss narrowed from -₹530 Cr to -₹257 Cr. **Achieved positive operating cash flow (OCF) of ₹31.89 Cr**, a ₹752 Cr improvement YoY. Revenue grew 63% (vs 29% in FY25). Market share rose to 17.1%.
    *   **Flag:** 🟢 **Positive.** Management articulated a clear path and delivered on key operational and financial milestones.

### **BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
*   **Core Business Segments:**
    *   **Vehicles:** 87% of revenue (Ather 450 series - premium, Ather Rizta - mass premium).
    *   **Non-Vehicle:** 13% of revenue (AtherStack Pro subscriptions, accessories, spares, charging services).
*   **New Segments/Products/Geographies:**
    *   **Product:** Launched Ather Rizta S & 450 S variants. Unveiled **EL platform** (new architecture for mass market). Entered **insurance** (incorporated subsidiary).
    *   **Geography:** Expanded to **Nepal & Sri Lanka**. Doubled domestic Experience Centres (ECs) to 700.
*   **Capex:**
    *   **Done:** Significant capex (₹506 Cr) on Hosur plant expansion and **Factory 3.0 construction** (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra).
    *   **Pending:** Factory 3.0 Phase 1 (5 lakh units) completion expected in **FY27**. Total planned capacity: 14.2 lakh units/year post Phase 2.

### **FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK**
*   **Revenue & PAT (Loss) Growth:**
    *   Revenue: **₹2,255 Cr (FY25) -> ₹3,672 Cr (FY26) (+63%)**. Strong growth driven by Rizta volumes.
    *   PAT (Loss): **Loss narrowed from ₹(812) Cr to ₹(517) Cr (36% improvement)**.
*   **EBITDA Margin Trend:** **Expanding.** EBITDA loss margin improved from **(23%) in FY25 to (7%) in FY26**. Quarterly trend: Q4 FY26 EBITDA margin at (3%).
*   **CFO vs PAT:**
    *   **CFO: ₹31.89 Cr (Positive for first time)**.
    *   **PAT (Loss): ₹(517) Cr**.
    *   **Analysis:** **Profit did NOT convert to cash** on a statutory basis. However, **strong OCF was driven by efficient working capital management** (large increase in trade payables and other liabilities), not operating profit.
*   **Working Capital Movement:**
    *   **Receivable Days:** Improved (Trade receivables turnover ratio improved 8.3%).
    *   **Inventory Days:** Stable (Inventory turnover ratio improved 6%).
    *   **Payable Days:** Increased, a key lever for OCF.
    *   **Overall:** Working capital cycle improved, **Working Capital Days: (53) in FY26 vs (58) in FY25** (negative indicates net current liability, a positive for cash).
*   **ROCE/ROE Trend:**
    *   **ROCE:** Improved from -80.1% to -14.2% (still negative but vastly better).
    *   **ROE:** Improved from -156.4% to -33.7% (still negative due to losses and equity base expansion from IPO).
    *   **Analysis:** **Deteriorating yet improving.** Returns are negative but trajectory is sharply upwards due to loss reduction and post-IPO equity.
*   **Debt Position:**
    *   **Net Debt:** ₹664 Cr (Total Borrowings ₹513 Cr + Lease Liabilities ₹151 Cr - Cash & Equivalents ₹112 Cr).
    *   **D/E Ratio:** **0.26x** (Massively improved from 1.26x in FY25 due to IPO proceeds bolstering equity).
    *   **Interest Coverage:** (6.10)x - **Still negative** due to net loss.

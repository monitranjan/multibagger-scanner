*   **High Performance Products (HPP)**
    *   Growth driven by volume and higher realisations.
    *   Constructive pricing environment for HFCs; increasing interest in contractual offtakes.
    *   New capacity: AHF facility commenced Q4 FY26, ramp-up ongoing.
*   **Specialty Chemicals**
    *   Strong order visibility with a robust product pipeline.
    *   Key metric: Sustained growth via scale-up of existing molecules and a strong lineup of new launches.
*   **Contract Development and Manufacturing Organisation (CDMO)**
    *   Strong momentum backed by order book.

*   **Advanced Materials (Incubating Vertical)**
    *   Focus on niche applications in Data Centers, Electronics, Defence, and Semiconductors.
    *   Commercialisation via DRDO project (indigenous defence material) and partnership with Chemours (liquid cooling product).

### **Management Commentary**
**Chairman Vishad Mafatlal:**
- Emphasized disciplined execution of strategy, a differentiated business model, and resilience of customer relationships.
- Highlighted a long-term strategy to build a **pipeline of advanced materials** for high-growth sectors: data centers, electronics, semiconductors, and defense.
- Announced key achievements: Chemours liquid cooling project, adoption facility for advanced materials, and a technology partnership with **DRDO** for an indigenous specialty material, contributing to "Atmanirbhar Bharat."
- Outlined ongoing capex: **INR 90 crore** for advanced materials adoption capacity, **INR 125 crore** for CDMO Phase 2 cGMP4, and continued investments in HFC, MPP capacity, and renewable energy.
- Expressed optimism about entering the "next phase of growth" with a strong pipeline, multiple expansions, and a healthy balance sheet.

**Managing Director Nitin Kulkarni:**
- Described the quarter as a "robust performance" with all three business verticals delivering strong growth.
- Noted a **44% YoY revenue growth** to INR 1,044 crores, **73% YoY EBITDA growth**, and **108% YoY PAT growth**, reflecting portfolio strength and disciplined execution.
- **HPP Business:** Revenue up 33% YoY on healthy volume and improved realizations; pricing environment remains "constructive."
- **Specialty Chemicals:** Revenue up 48% YoY with sustained momentum, good order visibility, and a robust pipeline of existing and new molecules.
- **CDMO Business:** Revenue up 82% YoY with "strong outlook for the year"; strategy focused on a balanced portfolio of commercial, late-stage, and early-stage programs across key therapeutic areas.
- Reaffirmed optimism across all businesses and a strategy of disciplined investment and capability expansion.

**CFO Anish Ganatra:**
- Provided detailed financials: Revenue of INR 1,045 crores (+44% YoY), Operating EBITDA of INR 357 crores (+73% YoY), **EBITDA margin of 34.2%** (up 566 bps YoY).
- Highlighted the company became **net debt-free** during the quarter.
- On **advanced materials**, described it as a "product and service play" focusing on niche applications for semiconductors, data centers, and defense. The INR 90 crore capex is the first phase to unlock commercial-scale qualifications.
- On **HFC/R32**, stated that while pricing is not in their hands, they are the "most competitive cost manufacturer." The expansion will provide fantastic operating leverage, protecting EBITDA in any pricing environment. Noted customers are interested in long-term contractual offtake (targeting 35-45% of capacity).
- On **CDMO**, confirmed the new capex is dedicated to the European partner, reflecting increasing demand and a deepening relationship (moving to "API minus 1").
- Stated the **$100 million CDMO revenue target for FY27 is "very much on track."**
- On **margins**, guided that a range of **32-33% EBITDA margin** is a fair assumption for the next 1-2 years, barring major shifts in the operating environment.

### **Tone, Clarity, and Confidence**
- **Overall Tone:** Highly confident, optimistic, and strategically focused. Leadership presented a clear, disciplined growth narrative.
- **Clarity:** CFO provided specific financial details and strategic explanations. Some areas on future DRDO or specific contract values were less detailed due to confidentiality.
- **Confidence:** Management was **most confident** when discussing:
    - The long-term structural demand for R32.
    - The deepening and broadening of the CDMO relationship and pipeline.
    - The disciplined execution and payback of new capex.
- **Evasiveness:** Management was **diplomatic or non-committal** on:
    - Specific DRDO order details or financial impact ("bound by confidentiality").
    - Precise near-term R32 pricing or Indian industry supply dynamics, steering the conversation to a 5-year global view and Navin's cost competitiveness.
    - Exact asset turns or timelines for advanced materials beyond the initial phase, framing it as a longer-term "seeding investment."

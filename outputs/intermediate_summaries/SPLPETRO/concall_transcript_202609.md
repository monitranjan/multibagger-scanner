**Management Commentary:**
- Standalone revenue from operations INR 1693 crores, +22% YoY, driven by significant increase in raw material prices due to West Asia conflict, despite lower volume sales (Rakesh Nayyar, CFO/ED).
- Operating EBITDA INR 331 crores, +188% YoY, operating EBITDA margins 19.53%; total EBITDA including other incomes INR 348 crores, margins 20.3%; net profit after tax INR 236 crores, PAT margin 13.96%. Wider international deltas between Styrene Monomer and downstream products benefitted results (Rakesh Nayyar).
- Sales volume of manufactured products 70,842 metric tons vs 93,853 metric tons YoY, decline of 24.5%. Decline primarily due to negligible exports from West Asia crisis and subdued demand from non-OEM segment, where "almost 50% demand from the non-OEM segment had evaporated in this quarter" (Rakesh Nayyar).
- Geopolitical tensions in West Asia and Strait of Hormuz disruptions caused significant interruption in liquid/container shipments; all three styrene plants in the region suspended operations, creating supply chain challenges. Company established alternate supply arrangements and met entire domestic customer requirement without interruption; however, exports were minimal due to reduced styrene monomer availability, significant freight rate increase, reduced shipping availability, and longer voyage times (Rakesh Nayyar).
- Styrene monomer prices international markets remained elevated most of the quarter, softened briefly towards end of June, but renewed escalation of hostilities again increased prices. Temporary suspension of import duties on commodity polymers led to unnecessarily increased imports, eroding market share for domestic producers (Rakesh Nayyar).
- Expansion projects: Phase 2 EPS capacity successfully completed. New line of wide-width EPS board with capacity 150,000 cubic meters to be commissioned by June 2027. Compounding capacity expanded from 50,000 to 80,000 tons per annum, to be commissioned by June 2027. Board approved new 80,000 tons per annum polystyrene production line at Amdoshi complex in Maharashtra, expected completion by December 2028, increasing total installed polystyrene capacity from 3,00,000 to 3,80,000 tons per annum. Total estimated investment on above projects INR 450 crores, funded entirely through internal accruals (Rakesh Nayyar).
- Company moving more towards ABS compounds; compounds doing well in market, setting up lines mainly for ABS compounds; aim to exhaust expanded capacity in 2-3 years (Rakesh Nayyar).

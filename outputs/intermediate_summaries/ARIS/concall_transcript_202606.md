**EXTRACTION FROM ARISINFRA SOLUTIONS LIMITED Q1 FY27 EARNINGS CALL TRANSCRIPT (STRICTLY PER TEXT PROVIDED)**

---

### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements from CEO, CFO, Leadership)

**Mr. Ronak Morbia (Chairman & Managing Director):**
- Outlined company model: "tech-enabled construction materials and services company" with "asset-light, network-driven model."
- Defined three segments: B2B supply (37% of Q1 FY27 revenues), Contract Manufacturing/CM (53% of Q1 FY27 revenues), and DaaS/Services (secured INR 650 crore mandate from Wadhwa Group Mumbai; GDV under execution > INR 1,800 crores across 10 active projects; 82% repeat order contribution).
- Stated segments are "deeply interconnected" creating "improving operating leverage."

**Mr. Bhavik Khara (Whole-Time Director & CFO):**
- Q1 FY27 Financials: Revenue INR 291 crores (+37% YoY); EBITDA INR 31 crores (+68% YoY), margin 10.49% (+191 bps YoY); PAT INR 20 crores (vs INR 5 crores prior year).
- Balance sheet: Net working capital days improved to 56 (from 66 as of March 2026); Net debt-to-equity 0.02x; ROE 17.16%, ROCE 10.61%.

**Mr. Srinivasan Gopalan (Chief Executive Officer):**
- Operational highlights: CM revenues grew 84% YoY; delivered ~8.65 lakh metric tons. Asphalt business revenue INR 53 crores in Q1 FY27 (from INR 30 crores in Q4 FY26), customers up to 38 from 28.
- DaaS: "EBITDA levels are at 60-65% without any kind of real estate risk"; model operates on fixed fee + percentage on construction + percentage on sales; "completely capital-light model" with no capital committed.

### 2. Tone, Clarity, and Confidence of Responses

- **Opening Remarks:** Clear, structured, and confident. Morbia provided a strategic overview; Khara presented precise metrics; Gopalan detailed operational scale-up.

  - Morbia was **highly confident** on margin sustainability and guidance stickiness ("We expect this to sustain for the next few quarters"; "We would like to stick to the same guidance").

### 4. Future Outlook & Guidance

- **Margins (Mix Shift, Operating Leverage, Seasonality):**
  - *Mix Shift:* Morbia stated CM+DaaS combined share moved "from about 46% to 63% as of Q1 FY27" as main EBITDA lever. DaaS expected at 9-11% of top line (Gopalan/Akhilesh Q). Morbia confirmed directionally targeting CM contribution 55-60% next year, implying EBITDA ~11%.
  - *Operating Leverage:* Cited in opening remarks as a benefit of integrated network.
  - *Seasonality:* Gopalan/Morbia noted H1 is ~40% of sales, H2 ~60%. Q1/Q2 are lean; Q3/Q4 better for cash flows/revenues. Asphalt slower during monsoon.
  - *ESOP Reversal:* **Not mentioned** in the transcript.
- **Volume Growth:** Revenue growth guidance held at 35-40% annually (Morbia). CM utilization at 65-70% of 9M ton capacity; adding 2-3 million annual capacity via "recycling deposits" (no new CapEx).
- **CAPEX:** Morbia: Capacity expansion to ~11M tons via "recycling the current deposits… not adding more deposits." Khara: Net debt to grow from INR 14.5 cr to INR 75-80 cr in FY27, keeping D/E at 0.5-0.6 max.
- **M&A Integration:**
  - *Fluence Pharma, BTM/Reginald:* **Not mentioned** in transcript.
  - *Subsidiary Merger:* Disha Chordia asked about associate/subsidiary merge. Khara/Gopalan: "Process has already started, and three out of the four regulators have already cleared it. We are at the last leg… timeline obviously defined on procedures of the government, so that we won't be able to comment on."

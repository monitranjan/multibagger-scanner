### 1. Management Commentary (Key statements from CEO/CMD J. Lakshmana Rao)
- **Quarterly Performance:** Declared a "historical quarter" with turnover crossing INR300 crores (partially aided by increased raw material costs). Per kg EBITDA reached INR46.7 (vs. historical best of INR40-42).
- **Drivers:** Attributed margin surge to consolidation of Hyderabad units (from 5 to 2), improved sales of high-margin Pharma/Food/FMCG products, and successful pass-through of raw material (RM) price hikes to clients.
- **Revenue Mix:** Paint is ~46% by value / ~50% by volume; expects paint to drop to ~40% in 3–4 years as Food & FMCG (18–20% CAGR) and Pharma (40–50% CAGR) grow faster.
- **Volume:** 6% overall volume growth; would have been 9% if not for a 17% drop in Lube segment (due to base oil procurement issues from Iran war).
- **Pharma & New Products:** Pharma grew 41% (38% volume); target INR50–55 cr revenue (vs INR34 cr last year). Ophthalmic product trials done; dosing pens/medical devices targeted for next financial year. Vibe JV (3 patented products, 3 in pilot) expected to start commercial production in Q3.
- **Finance Cost:** 20% QoQ rise due to higher working capital needs from RM inflation (not term loans).

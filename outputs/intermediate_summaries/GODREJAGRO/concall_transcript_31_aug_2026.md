### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements)

**Mr. Burjis Godrej (Chairman Designate) – Opening & Closing**
- Consolidated sales INR 2,852 crores, YoY growth 10% “despite a challenging operating environment marked by a delayed monsoon and inflationary pressures arising from geopolitical tensions.”
- Animal Nutrition: Revenue +12.6%, cattle feed volumes +15%; reported segment results +29%, underlying +36% “reflecting the benefits of strategic sourcing, operating leverage and continued cost discipline.”
- Oil Palm: Segment revenue +28.9%, result +14.4%; “higher CPO and PKO realizations, increased sales volume and improved oil extraction efficiency”; FFB volumes “broadly stable against a strong comparable quarter” (prior year early monsoon).
- Crop Care: Standalone revenue -16.2% due to delayed monsoon/dry June; “contraction in segment margin”; launched Ashitaka (maize herbicide), Takai (paddy insecticide) scaled up, launched Ghassnash (soybean herbicide).
- Astec: “continued at EBITDA breakeven compared with an EBITDA loss of INR11 crores in Q1 FY26” driven by margin expansion, revenue moderately lower due to product mix.
- Dairy: Revenue +11.4%, value-added salience 42%→49%; profitability hit by “elevated milk procurement prices”; focus on “calibrated pricing, procurement efficiencies and cost optimization.”
- Godrej Foods: Branded volumes +~6%, Yummiez +22%; profitability affected by input costs/geopolitical inflation partly offset by pricing.
- Bangladesh JV (ACI): “double-digit growth in volumes, revenue and PBT.”
- Balance sheet: “average net working capital improved significantly year-on-year.”
- Closing: “I hope we have been able to answer all your questions.”

**Mr. Sunil Kataria (CEO & MD) – Q&A Highlights**
- Oil Palm LRP (4–5 yrs): FFB volume “high single digit to early double-digit growth” (conservative); drivers: area expansion (17k ha added last yr, on track this yr), geographic diversification (Telangana, Northeast; plan 80k→150k ha), 50% plantations juvenile (0–4 yrs) maturing, best-in-class OER. Downstream integration: Kannan complex (nursery, CPO mill, future seed garden/refinery), specialty fats refinery Aug/Sep; downstream “should add roughly around close to 200 bps to our overall EBITDA profile.” Capex filter: “nothing crosses our bridge broadly beyond… IRR of around 16% to 18%.” Overall capex guidance INR300–350 cr “no new investment” beyond that.
- Crop Care: June 40% deficit; July better YoY; full assessment by end Sept. H2 positives: soft base, normal Oct/Nov (vs last yr unseasonal rains), firm chili prices, new products (Ashitaka “beyond internal plans”, Takai scaling, Ghassnash). “Give us time till September end.” Overall consol guidance downgraded from mid-teens PBT to “double-digit growth still” (range by end Q2).
- Godrej Foods strategy: Live bird planned degrowth 15–20% per yr to near zero over 5 yrs; B2C (Yummiez etc) growing 28% vol / 20% value this quarter; NPDs (nugget extensions, momos – chicken/cheese now, wet in 3–4 months, frozen chicken). B2C to be 65–70% of business by LRP end; investment phase (EBITDA+adv).
- Dairy (CDPL): Route-to-market transformation 18–20 mo under new CEO Gaurav; value-added premium push; volumes +8%, value +11.5%; Iran war → packaging inflation (not fully passed), milk prices inflated (70% passed on); packaging impact to ease by Aug due to “inventories everybody has been carrying”; milk prices persist 2–3 qtrs; cost initiatives (Project Pi continued); “big chunk is the Altium transformation.”
- Animal Nutrition: Maize volatility managed, 60–70% passed on; cattle +15%, fish +20%+, total vol +7% (conscious poultry geo exits); EBIT/tonne guidance INR2,050–2,150 (delivered slightly higher). Bangladesh animal nutrition turnaround double-digit growth.
- Oil Palm Q1 margin dip: Due to “formula of pricing… government intervention month-to-month” (one-off) and flat FFB from driest June vs early monsoon last yr; OER improved 18.4%→18.8%; full-year FFB growth high single–double digit.
- Crop Care diversification margins: Ashitaka/Takai/Ghassnash “lower than Hitweed margins” (in-licensing vs in-house) but “pretty healthy decent margin products”; overall EBIT ~26–27%.
- Linkages: High milk prices hurt dairy but help cattle feed premiumization; no direct cattle feed–Crop Care correlation; possible future synergy between CDPL farmer network and Animal Nutrition not yet driven.

**Mr. S. Varadaraj (CFO)**
- Labour Code: “We have factored in all the impact of the Labour Code already. So we do not expect any additional impact because of that.”

**Mr. Arijit Mukherjee (ED & COO, Astec)**
- Astec: Full-year revenue growth “at least more than 20%” (vs prior 20% guidance); Q1 dip to be covered in H2; CDMO “around 50%–52%” (text: “dissilience will be around 50% - 52%”); CDMO margins intact, enterprise margin benefited from low-cost RM and war-driven FG prices, now normalizing.

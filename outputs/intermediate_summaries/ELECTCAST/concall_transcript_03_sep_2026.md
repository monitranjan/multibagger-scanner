### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements)
**Mr. Madhav Kejriwal (Whole-Time Director):**
- **Industry Environment:** "Challenging environment due to slower project execution, delayed fund disbursements and liquidity constraints at state and municipal levels." Stated the team implemented a "broad-based structural cost optimization program" to support "profitability and operating leverage as volumes recover."
- **Government Schemes:** Highlighted JJM 2.0 (Rs 8.69 lakh cr outlay), AMRUT 2.0, irrigation, and river-linking (Ken-Betwa Rs 44k cr, PKC Rs 90k cr) as long-term visibility drivers.
- **Overseas & M&A:** Subsidiaries in UK/Europe/USA "doing much better than expectations." Acquired T.I.S. Service S.p.A. Italy: "EUR 10 million revenue, sequential growth 18.4%… EBITDA margins improving to mid-teens."
- **Diversification:** Entering industrial paints/protective coatings (target Rs 800–1,000 cr rev in 5 yrs, CAPEX Rs 250–300 cr phased). Valves manufacturing in India by end of FY (target to double valve revenue in 4 yrs).
- **Volumes & Realizations:** Revised FY27 volume guidance to ~575,000 tons (DIP+CIP) from earlier 650k–700k due to slow JJM release. Q1 net realization ~Rs 55,000/ton (vs Rs 50,500 in Q4FY26); called last FY "rock bottom for the industry."
- **Order Book:** ~3 lakh tons (≈5 months), ~50% JJM. Active states: Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, UP, Rajasthan.
- **Long-term:** DI pipe dependence to fall from 85% to 55% in 4–5 yrs. By FY30/31, targets Rs 7,000–8,000 cr revenue, 13–13.5% EBITDA.

**Mr. Sunil Katial (Whole-Time Director & CEO):**
- Confirmed marine paints planned in Phase 2 (next FY).
- Railway rubber components: Board approved new category; registration with railways takes 4–6 months; gasket plant in Andhra Pradesh adding equipment to supply railways.

**Mr. Rajesh Daga (CFO, newly appointed ex-Marketing Head):**
- Q1 volumes: 1.20 lakh ton (DI+CI), down 27% YoY (Middle East tensions hit exports).
- Consolidated: Income Rs 1,465 cr, EBITDA Rs 139 cr (9.5%), PAT Rs 48.4 cr. Standalone: Income Rs 1,119 cr (-21%), EBITDA Rs 70.6 cr (6.3%), PAT Rs 5.9 cr.
- Debt: Gross Rs 1,658 cr, Net Rs 876 cr (as of 30 Jun 26); net debt cut ~Rs 1,100 cr last FY.
- JJM budget Rs 67,670 cr for FY27; Rs 10,344 cr sanctioned till date vs Rs 1,560 cr entire prev FY.

**Mr. Gaurav Somani (GM Finance):**
- Order book 3 lakh tons; T.I.S. PAT ~7%; Term debt ~Rs 340 cr to reduce to ~Rs 230 cr via scheduled repayments.

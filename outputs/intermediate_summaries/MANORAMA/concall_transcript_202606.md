# Manorama Industries Limited - Q1 FY27 Earnings Conference Call Analysis

## Management Commentary

**Chairman & Managing Director (Mr. Ashish Saraf):**
- Company entered FY27 with strong conviction and momentum
- Achieved 39.5% year-on-year revenue growth, crossing INR 4,000 million quarterly revenue and EBITDA milestone for the first time
- 67.6% year-on-year profit growth
- Focused on executing long-term strategy, driving innovation, and delivering sustainable value creation
- Entered FY27 with expanding portfolio of specialty ingredients and growing customer partnerships
- Increasing presence in cocoa butter alternatives and specialty fats market
- Built robust model capable of navigating business risks

- CFO Ashok Jain: Delivered strong financial performance with focus on operational efficiencies and strategic expansion

**Key Strategic Initiatives:**
- Expanded sourcing footprint in West Africa through new Chad subsidiary (Manorama Savannah Agro Chad Sarl)
- Acquired 10 hectares (24 acres) land in Burkina Faso for shea nut and mango nut processing facility (regulatory approvals in progress)
- R&D center (DSIR recognized) driving product innovation and integration strategy
- QIP completion strengthened balance sheet for growth acceleration
- Targeting globally integrated specialty ingredients platform with scalable, resilient supply chain

**Tone, Clarity, and Confidence:**
- Management demonstrated confidence in business model and growth prospects
- Clear articulation of strategic priorities and growth drivers
- Balanced approach between short-term performance and long-term strategic investments
- Open about challenges (seasonal dynamics, geopolitical risks) while maintaining positive outlook
- Ekta Soni (AVP Investor Relations) demonstrated clarity in explaining complex operational details

**Focus Areas:**
- **Product Strategy:** Value-added specialty fats and butters portfolio expansion; CBA (cocoa butter alternative) development via interesterification technology
- **Channel Performance:** 60:40 export-to-domestic revenue mix; strong global customer base; growing domestic demand
- **Channel/Market Expansion:** 
  - LatAm market entry with trial production started
  - Expansion into new African markets (Chad, Burkina Faso)
  - Growing presence in cocoa butter alternatives segment
- **Inventory/Capacity:** 
  - Current capacity: 47,500 tons annually
  - Debottlenecking adding 4,500 tons capacity (partial already operational, balance Q3 FY27)
  - Full year utilization target: 80-85%
  - New capex program targeting FY28 Q3 commissioning

**Future Outlook & Guidance:**
- **Margins:** Expected to hold broadly stable with modest quarterly fluctuations; operating leverage expected to kick in with capacity ramp-up; historical performance of 20-25 consistent good quarters provides foundation
- **Volume Growth:** Strong momentum expected from capacity utilization; further improvement anticipated as year progresses
- **CAPEX:** 
  - Total FY27 plan: INR 225-250 crores
  - Q1 actual spending: ~INR 20 crores
  - Total planned spend: ~INR 460 crores
  - Burkina Faso portion: ~INR 120-130 crores
  - Indian projects (refinery, solvent fractionation, extraction): Remaining balance
  - Target commissioning: Q3 FY28 (tentative)
  - Full contribution visibility: FY29 as utilization ramps gradually
  
- **M&A Integration:** 
  - No specific mentions of Fluence Pharma or BTM/Reginald in transcript
  - Focus on organic growth through subsidiaries (Chad, Burkina Faso) and Brazil partnership
  - Brazil: Trial production started; gradual ramp-up expected over 2-4 quarters

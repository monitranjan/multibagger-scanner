## 3. Future Growth Adjacencies

### India–UK CETA (Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement)
- **Tariff elimination**: Yarn & fabrics from 8–12% → **0%**; garments from 8–12% → **0%**
- **India's current textile & apparel exports to UK**: US$ 1.9 Bn (current) → US$ 3.5–4.0 Bn (3–5 year projection) — **~58% increase**
- **Incremental opportunity**: US$ 1.5–2.0 Bn
- India's share in ~US$30 Bn UK market currently low — large headroom
- Competitive position: India (6.8% share) vs China (25%), Bangladesh (22%), Turkey (8%), Pakistan (6.6–6.7%)

### FTA Landscape (India)
| Year | Country | Agreement Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | Oman | Came into effect June 3 — zero duty access; 5–10% duty removed for yarn, fabric & garment |
| 2025 | New Zealand | FTA agreed — duty elimination from 3–5% (yarn/fabric) and ~10% (garments) to 0% |
| 2025 | UK | FTA agreed; commenced July 15 — removes ~8–12% tariff disadvantage |
| 2025 | EU | FTA agreed — implementing 2027; 0% import duty vs ~8–12% previously |
| 2026 | US | Tariffs reset ~10%; 20%+ US cotton input = 0% duty on exports vs 10% with Indian cotton |
| Ongoing | Canada | Under negotiation — duty elimination from current 16–18% |
| Ongoing | Peru | Under negotiation — duty elimination from 10–16%; gateway to Latin America |
| Ongoing | Israel | Negotiations progressing — from current 0–12%; potential uptick in specialty yarns |
| Ongoing | GCC | Under negotiation — from current ~5% |

### Strategic Priorities / Expansion Plans
- **Vertical integration**: Yarn manufacturing → knitted, weaving, dyed fabrics (superior margins)
- **Green energy expansion**: 65 MW → **75 MW** (up to 75% of energy requirement)
- **Specialized yarn**: Moving into value-added/premium products in each category
- **Long-term EBITDA margin target**: **15–18% range**
- **Capex commitment**: MoUs signed with Tamil Nadu Government for **Rs. 1,035 Cr** investment; ~Rs. 675 Cr already invested
- **Future plans**: Setup weaving and dyed fabric production capacities after knitting expansion

### Sector Tailwinds (Q1FY27)
- Cotton import duty of 11% waived from June 1, effective till October 30, 2026
- Indian cotton prices near global parity — gap of just ~0.5¢/lb (79.5¢ vs 80.0¢/lb)
- Yarn-cotton spread expanded in Q1FY27
- Global retailers' commentary points to constructive demand, particularly value and off-price channels
- Acreage expected to increase coming season

## Key Management Commentary (Q1FY27)
- Sequential improvement in cotton and yarn spreads; sufficient cotton inventory protects from raw material volatility
- Fabric revenue share increased to 16% (from 9% in Q1FY26) on higher sales of both knitted and griege fabric
- Focus on operational discipline, cost efficiency, and working capital optimization
- Scale and vertical integration expected to drive higher RoCE and sustainable growth
- Phase 1 of knitting expansion operational; Phase 2 on track

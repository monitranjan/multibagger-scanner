### 1. MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY (Key statements from CEO, CFO, and leadership)
*Note: Ms. Ritu Mittal (JMD & CEO) presented financials (acting in CFO capacity); Dr. Somnath Chatterjee (Chairman & JMD) and Mr. Niren Kaul (CSO) also spoke. Mr. Balgopal Jhunjhunwala (Regional Business Head) was listed but had no spoken lines in the text.*

- **Dr. Somnath Chatterjee (Chairman & JMD):**
  - “Pleased to report a very strong start to FY '27” with revenue growth of 21%, EBITDA growth 28%, PAT growth 40%.
  - Suraksha Genomics revenue Rs. 13.7 million, 136% YoY growth, 5 consecutive quarters sequential growth.
  - “Just introduced a blood test for Alzheimer test with excellent feedback from the doctors” and started AI project for early Alzheimer detection from brain MRI with CSIR.
  - Acquired “Genexus equipment… only equipment in the whole of Eastern India” for fast genome sequencing (validation final stage), cutting oncology genomic study turnaround from 14-17 days to 48 hours.

- **Ms. Ritu Mittal (JMD & CEO – presented financials):**
  - Commissioned 1 hub & 3 spokes in Q1; added 2 hubs & 3 spokes in Jul-Aug; extended beyond WB with hub in Jharkhand, Tripura next.
  - Total income INR 887 million (+21% YoY); EBITDA INR 315 million (36% margin); PAT INR 128 million (+40% YoY, 14.7% margin).
  - Mature centres EBITDA margin 40.9%; centres <2 years old turned profitable at 6.5% (from -5.5%).
  - Genomics split: “80% is B2C and 20% is B2B” currently, expects B2B larger share forward. Genomic margins “somewhere around 15% to 20%.”
  - Capex: “Rs. 70 crores to Rs. 80 crores this year”; “on track to open 100 centres by FY '28.”
  - Shifted biochemistry “from Siemens to Roche… negotiated a better deal” improving COGS.
  - B2C “does not have a margin challenge”; B2B “definitely will have a margin challenge, but then the volume makes up for it.”
  - Wellness contribution “around 4% to 5%.”

- **Mr. Niren Kaul (Chief Sales Officer):**
  - Genomics: “quite positive… adoption has been really good”; expects “around 20-odd percent quarterly growth for this financial year.”
  - East India genomics “underpenetrated”; Suraksha to “educate the existing medical fraternity.”
  - CGHS rate increase mid-Oct ’25 gave incremental benefit; corporate/PSU contribution ~15%, top-line benefit “1.5 or some.”
  - Genomics Capex: Over 3 years spent “around Rs. 22 crores.”
  - On 15% historical revenue guidance: “We have been a bit conservative over it.”

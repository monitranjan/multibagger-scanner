## 3. FUTURE GROWTH ADJACENCIES

### Capacity Expansion & Revenue Potential
- **NPK/DAP capacity expanded** from 330K → 495K MTPA (+50%)
- **Sulphuric Acid capacity expanded** from 264K → 363K MTPA
- **Potential to add nearly ₹1,000 crore in annual revenue** at full capacity
- **Target: Increase market share from 18% to 25%** within 2–3 years
- **DAP/NPK utilization target:** Over 80% in coming years

### Green Ammonia / ESG-Linked Growth
- **10-year Green Ammonia Purchase Agreement (GAPA)** with SECI — 70,000 MTPA
- **~108% structural cost advantage:** ~$0.6/kg (secured) vs ~$1.1/kg (global benchmark)
- **>30% competitive edge** over prices in green ammonia
- **+40% cost advantage** in procurement of green ammonia
- **Forex savings:** Reduces industry-level forex outflows (~USD 2.5bn)
- **Decarbonization alignment** with Krishana's ESG goals

### Import Substitution Opportunity
- **~42% of NPK+DAP demand remains import-dependent**
- Import share increased from 29% to 42% — significant domestic capacity expansion opportunity
- **NPK Consumption 3-Year CAGR: 12%**
- **SSP Consumption 3-Year CAGR: 3%**
- NPK Consumption (20-20-0-13): 4,353 → 5,421 → 6,230 → 6,971 ('000 MT)

### Policy Tailwinds
- **DAP subsidy increased to ₹32,786/MT** for Kharif 2026–27
- **Fertilizer subsidy outlay exceeding ₹2.4 lakh crore** for FY27
- **₹18,640 crore PM-KISAN disbursement** (Mar'26) supporting farm liquidity
- **NBS (Nutrient-Based Subsidy)** revised to support affordability
- **NPK Ratio normalization target:** from 7.7:3.1:1 → 4:2:1 (structural demand driver for P & K)

### Strategic Growth Pillars (per Chairman)
1. **Backward integration**
2. **Capacity expansion**
3. **Product diversification**
4. **Disciplined capital allocation**
5. **Deep farmer connect**

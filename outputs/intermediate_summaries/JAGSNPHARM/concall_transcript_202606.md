### 1. Management Commentary (Key Statements)
**Manish Gupta (Managing Director – MD)**
- Started year "stronger with a 9% growth in Q1"; Pharmarack data shows JPL grew 18.9% vs industry 11.6%. Believes company is "on track of getting our growth to 1.5x industry growth rate."
- Operating EBITDA grew 21%; Net Profit grew 22%.
- Acquired controlling stake in Aequitas Healthcare (EV ~Rs.25 cr, FY26 rev Rs.53 cr) for hospital segment entry.
- Completed Rs.40 cr share buyback (3.67x subscribed, 40% premium); recouped ~50% within quarter; ROCE up 340 bps.
- FY22–FY26: EBITDA/PAT grew 2.5x (25% CAGR), FCF 8x.
- Board: Ms. Pallavi Dinodia Gupta stepped down; Mr. Anil Kumar Matai (Independent Director) joined.
- Priorities: Accelerate organic growth, integrate Aequitas, evaluate value-accretive inorganic ops.

**Amrut Medhekar (Chief Operating Officer – COO)**
- Outlined 4 pillars: Accelerated organic growth, Brand building, Productivity increase, Inorganic opportunities.
- Portfolio shift: "deliberately shifting away from high volume and low margin acute therapies towards higher value, stickier semi-chronic and specialty treatments" (new brand avg monthly sales run rate "almost doubled").
- Brand building: Moving to scientific promotion/patient education; power brands grew 19% vs market 16%; 5 brands #1, 14 in top 5 (Maintane, Indocap, Endoreg, Lycored outperformed).
- Productivity: "Bahubali" incentive, MBA program, lean/green program; attrition reduced.
- Aequitas: 49 institutional MRs, 1,000+ hospitals, 4,000 specialty docs; synergies over 36 months. Targeting Rs.10 cr EBITDA by year 2 from Aequitas (from Rs.53 cr base, aiming Rs.100 cr by FY28–29).

**Nirav Vora (Chief Financial Officer – CFO)**
- Revenue ops +9% YoY to Rs.82 cr; Gross profit +10% to Rs.54 cr (gross margin 65%+).
- Operating EBITDA +21% to ~Rs.19 cr (margin 23%+, +240 bps); PAT +22% to Rs.13 cr (margin 16%, +176 bps).
- Closing cash Rs.170 cr; acquired 85% Aequitas for Rs.20.8 cr.
- 4-yr operating cash >Rs.250 cr deployed to Yash Pharma (>Rs.90 cr), dividends (>Rs.40 cr), buyback (Rs.40 cr). Dividend 200% (incl 75% special) recommended Apr '26 pending AGM.

### 3. Focus Areas (Per Critical Rules)
**Product Launches**
- Shift to "complex generics and niche formulations" in premium tier.
- COO: Launched one "first wave" product this quarter; expects one more in current quarter.
- Aequitas hospital portfolio: ICU antibiotics, volume enhancers, perioperative injectables (anesthesia/nutrition/pain/anti-acid).

**Channel Margins**
- JPL branded prescription: Gross margin 65%+, EBITDA margin 23%+.
- Aequitas (hospital): COO stated margins "much thinner versus branded prescription business" because hospitals prioritize price over brand; Aequitas gross margin described as "low." Improvement planned via higher-margin product mix and innovative premium products.

**Inventory Levels**
- Discussed in Q&A (Sajal Kapoor): Inventory days improved from 68 (FY24) to 51 (FY25).
- MD noted cash conversion days dropped from 59 (FY22) to 22 (FY24) mainly via inventory control and debtor discipline ("same day payment as per due date").
- *Note: "Project Neev" and "direct distribution" are NOT mentioned anywhere in the provided transcript text.*

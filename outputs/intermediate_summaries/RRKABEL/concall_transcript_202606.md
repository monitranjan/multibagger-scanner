### **1. Management Commentary & Key Statements**

**Leadership Present:**
*   **Mr. Rajesh Kabra** – Joint Managing Director
*   **Mr. Rajesh Jain** – Chief Operating Officer (COO)
*   **Mr. Jigar Mehta** – Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

**Key Highlights from Opening Remarks (JMD, Mr. Rajesh Kabra):**
*   "The quarter continued to see volatility in metal prices and foreign exchange, along with uncertainty across global markets. At the same time, the demand remains supportive."
*   "Against this backdrop, I am pleased to share that we have started FY '27 on a strong note. During the quarter, we delivered our highest ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA and profit after tax."
*   "Our Wires & Cables business continued to be the main driver of growth... Overall, Wires & Cable volumes grew by 17% year-on-year... Cables grew significantly faster with more than 25% volume growth, while volume growth in wires was approximately 12%."
*   "The segment [FMEG] recorded a healthy revenue growth and reached breakeven position during the quarter... This is an encouraging development for us."
*   "Our long-term priorities continue to be the same. We want to grow ahead of the industry in wires and cables, improve our position in the cable and B2B businesses and build FMEG into consistently profitable business."

**Key Highlights from Operational Review (COO, Mr. Rajesh Jain):**
*   **Financials:** "Revenue from operations stood at INR3,168 crores, representing growth of around 54%... Operating EBITDA stood at INR285 crores, almost double... Operating EBITDA margin improved to 9%."
*   **Segment Performance:** "Wire & Cable revenue stood at INR2,880 crores, growing at approximately 57%... Segment profit margin improved to 9.9%." "FMEG business... revenue stood approximately INR288 crores, growing by 28%... reached an operational breakeven position."
*   **Working Capital:** "Net working capital days remained broadly stable at 50 days."
*   **CAPEX:** "Our capacity expansion program is also progressing as planned with new capacities expected to be added during the current quarter in Silvassa."

### **2. Tone, Clarity, and Confidence of Responses**

*   **Overall Tone:** Confident, positive, and forward-looking. Management repeatedly emphasized strong execution, record results, and progress against strategic initiatives (Project RRise).
*   **Clarity:** Responses were generally clear and direct, providing specific data points on growth, margins, and mix. They used terms like "encouraging," "significant milestone," and "quite confident."
*   **Confidence:**
    *   **High Confidence:** On the quarter's performance, volume growth trajectory (~18% YoY), margin improvement drivers (scale, product mix), FMEG turnaround, and CAPEX execution.
    *   **Cautious/Measured Confidence:** On external factors (metal/FX volatility, Middle East disruptions), the pace of unorganized-to-organized shift (cited a long-term 2-3% annual trend), and the scale of data center opportunity ("very less" share currently).
    *   **Evasive/General:** When pushed for specific geographic revenue breakdowns or the impact of unorganized players in the last 6 months, management stated they did not have exact data or saw no major recent change, defaulting to historical trends.

### **3. Focus Areas**

*   **Product Mix & Strategy:**
    *   **B2B/Cables:** Strong focus on growing the higher-margin cable business (B2B, project, industrial, power). "Majority of this growth [in cables] is... serving for our distribution channel only."
    *   **Premiumization:** A key driver in FMEG (25% of FMEG revenue from premium products) and in export wires.
    *   **Direct Distribution:** Focus on "expanding our distribution reach and deepening our engagement with dealers and distributors." Retail touchpoints at >1.5 lakhs, with a focus on increasing depth/revenue per dealer.
*   **Inventory & Channel Management:**
    *   Acknowledged some negative volume impact from destocking at the very end of Q1 due to commodity price correction, but viewed it as a short-term issue in the overall "business as usual" context.
    *   Repeatedly emphasized disciplined "commodity management" and "timely pricing and procurement decisions" to protect margins.
*   **Geographical Markets:**
    *   **Exports:** Growth driven by diversification, offsetting Middle East disruptions. Europe and Middle East are key; U.S. is a future opportunity pending tariff clarity.
    *   **Domestic:** North and West contribute ~65% of domestic revenue. Expansion in South noted as "better than previous plans."

### **4. Future Outlook & Guidance**

*   **Margin Guidance (Wires & Cables):** Maintained FY28 guidance of **10.5% EBITDA margin**. "We endeavor to touch our double-digit margins for the year, but since it is early in the year, we continue to maintain our overall margin guidance."
*   **Margin Bridge Drivers:** Improvement attributed to a combination of **scale benefits, better cost absorption, and improved product mix**. Confirmed no inventory gains/losses in Q1 margin.
*   **Volume Growth:** Reiterated long-term guidance of **~18% YoY volume growth** for Wires & Cables.
*   **CAPEX & Capacity:**
    *   Total Project RRise CAPEX: **~INR 1,200 crore (FY26-FY28)**.
    *   Deployment: ~INR300 crore in FY26, **~INR600-650 crore planned for FY27**, remainder in FY28.
    *   Focus: **~80% on cable side**. New capacities in Silvassa (wire) and Waghodia (cable) coming online in FY27.
    *   Current Utilization: **Wires ~65-70%, Cables ~90%**.
*   **FMEG Outlook:**
    *   Achieved **operational breakeven in Q1** (vs. loss in Q4 FY26). Targeting **breakeven on a yearly basis for FY27**.
    *   Aiming for **~20% revenue growth** and sustainable profitability in 2-3 years.
    *   Focus on **premium products, distribution, and tighter cost control**. Manufacturing model (~1/3 in-house, 2/3 outsourced) to remain largely unchanged.
*   **M&A Integration:** No specific commentary on the integration of **Fluence Pharma or BTM/Reginald** was present in this transcript.

### **5. Q&A Dynamics**

*   **Analyst Queries vs. Management Answers:**
    *   **Highly Confident & Detailed Answers:** On the **drivers of margin improvement** (scale, mix), **volume growth outlook** (18%), **CAPEX plans** (specific numbers and focus), **export diversification strategy**, and **FMEG breakeven drivers**.
    *   **Direct but Qualitative Answers:** On **unorganized sector shift** (cited long-term 2-3% trend, denied any major recent change), **competition/pricing pressure** ("nothing special"), and **U.S. market opportunity** (acknowledged potential, deferred detail to tariff clarity).
    *   **Areas of Evasiveness/Deflection:**
        *   **Geographic Revenue Breakdown:** When asked for a quantified split, the COO stated, "I do not have exact breakup."
        *   **Impact of Recent Commodity Price Moves on Channel Inventory:** Initially gave a general "business as usual" view before clarifying the short-term negative volume impact at quarter-end.
        *   **Sustainability of Q1 FMEG Breakeven:** Clarified that while Q1 was a milestone, **Q2 seasonally is weaker** and breakeven may not be achieved quarterly, but remains the annual target.
        *   **Specific Drivers of Unorganized-to-Organized Shift in Last 6 Months:** Management stated they saw **no major change** and reverted to the long-term 2-3% annual trend.

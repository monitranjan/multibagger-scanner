- R&D-driven product leadership (CM3500, battery vehicles)

## FINAL ACCOUNTING QUALITY SCORE: **AVERAGE**

**Summary:** Eimco Elecon is a clean, well-governed company with unqualified audits and no major red flags. However, FY26 shows meaningful deterioration in operating performance (revenue -6%, PAT -21%, margins compressed, ROCE/ROE declining). The company holds 39% of assets in financial instruments, which inflates returns and masks core business performance. Cash conversion is poor (57% of PAT). Related party transactions are material (Emtici commission at 9.5% of revenue). The exit of Tamrock (24.68% promoter stake) is a significant governance event. Capital advances of ₹4,825 Lakhs need monitoring. Overall, the company is financially sound (near-zero debt, strong current ratio) but operating performance is weakening and the business is heavily reliant on investment income to supplement operating profits.

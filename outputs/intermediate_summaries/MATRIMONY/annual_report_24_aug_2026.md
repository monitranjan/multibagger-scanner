Annual Report 2025-26
4
Matrimony.com Limited
Every family carries a hope for the future, shaped by values, aspirations and the desire to 
find the right partner for someone they love. At Matrimony.com, we have had the privilege 
of being part of this intimate and defining journey for millions of members across India and 
the world. Every profile, conversation and introduction carries a larger purpose, because it 
brings people closer to a shared future built on compatibility, trust and belonging.
As preferences evolve and choices become more individual, our role is to make 
matchmaking more relevant, reassuring and personal. Our platforms, assisted services, 
community-led offerings, premium matchmaking solutions and wedding-related services 
are designed to respond to each member’s context with care. Technology strengthens 
discovery. Vernacular access brings familiarity. Relationship Manager-led support adds 
empathy. AI-enabled trust layers improve confidence. Expanding customer touchpoints help 
us stay closer to people at every stage of their journey.
Trust remains the foundation that connects our past, present and future.  
Over the years, families have returned to us across generations, carrying forward  
a belief built through reliability, empathy and successful outcomes. We continue  
to evolve with changing behaviours while staying anchored in a timeless purpose:  
helping people move towards a shared future with confidence and care,  
personalising life journeys through trusted matchmaking across generations.

**ANNUAL REPORT FORENSICS: BIRLA CABLE LIMITED (FY 2025-26)**

**MANAGEMENT LETTERS & THEMES**
*   **Key Theme:** **Business Turnaround Amidst Market Downturn.** Strong performance in Structured Copper LAN Cables mitigated a severe cyclical downturn in Optical Fibre Cables (OFC).
*   **Stated Opportunities:** Recovery in global OFC demand (led by North America, Europe), India's 5G roll-out, BharatNet Phase 3, data centre/AI infrastructure growth.
*   **Stated Risks:** Prolonged OFC market weakness, geopolitical tensions, commodity price volatility, intense competition, high customer concentration.
*   **Strategic Priority:** Survive and navigate the OFC downturn via LAN cable strength, position for OFC recovery, and execute the **proposed Amalgamation with Vindhya Telelinks Ltd.** to create a larger, more competitive entity.
*   **Walk-the-Talk:** No explicit FY26 guidance found to compare. Performance exceeded prior year significantly, driven by LAN cables as outlined.

**BUSINESS & STRATEGY**
*   **Core Segments & Mix:**
    *   **Structured Copper LAN Cables:** **Dominant.** Revenue ₹663.13 Cr (86% of product revenue). **Key performer.**
    *   **Optical Fibre Cables (OFC):** ₹105.65 Cr (14%). **Depressed market.** Exports contributed ₹78.49 Cr.
*   **New Additions:** No new business segments/products reported. **Major Event:** Board approved **Scheme of Amalgamation** of Birla Cable Ltd. into Vindhya Telelinks Ltd. (swap ratio: 10:115). Awaits NCLT/regulatory approvals.
*   **Capex:** ₹1.91 Cr incurred (₹1.67 Cr in Plant & Equipment). Minimal incremental capacity added; funds primarily for replacement/expansion of existing lines.

**FINANCIAL QUALITY CHECK**
*   **Revenue & PAT Growth (3Y Trend):**
    *   Revenue: **₹661.65 Cr (FY25) -> ₹771.11 Cr (FY26).** Strong 16.5% growth.
    *   PAT: **₹5.03 Cr (FY25) -> ₹16.87 Cr (FY26).** **235% surge,** though on a low base.
*   **EBITDA Margin Trend:** **Expanding.** Marginal EBITDA margin improved from ~2.3% (FY25) to ~3.6% (FY26). Profitability uptick driven by operational leverage on higher LAN volumes.
*   **CFO vs PAT:** **Weak Conversion.** PAT ₹16.87 Cr vs. Net Cash Used in Operations **(₹20.91 Cr)**. Profit did not convert to cash due to massive working capital build-up (Inventory & Receivables).
*   **Working Capital:** **Deteriorated Significantly.**
    *   Inventory Days: Increased (raw material stock built up, likely for LAN cables).
    *   Receivable Days: Increased substantially (trade receivables grew faster than revenue).
*   **ROCE/ROE Trend:** **Improving from low base.**
    *   ROCE: Improved to 8.42% (from 5.52%).
    *   ROE: Improved to 7.28% (from 2.25%). **Absolute levels remain low.**
*   **Debt Position:**
    *   Net Debt (Standalone): **₹131.00 Cr** (Total Borrowings ₹131.32 Cr - Cash ₹0.32 Cr). Significant increase from prior year.
    *   D/E Ratio: ~0.47x (Total Debt / Equity). Manageable but elevated.
    *   Interest Coverage: **3.50x (FY26) vs. 1.58x (FY25).** Improved substantially due to higher profits.

**FORENSIC FLAGS**

**SEAMEC Limited (Q1 FY27 Investor Presentation) – Critical Data Extraction**

**Core Product Segments & Asset Portfolio:**
*   **Specialized Vessels:** Core revenue-generating assets for offshore Inspection, Maintenance & Repair (IMR) and subsea operations.
    *   **Diving Support Vessels (DSV):** 6 vessels (SEAMEC II, III, PRINCESS, PALADIN, SWORDFISH, AGASTYA). High entry barriers; beneficiaries of increased offshore maintenance spending.
    *   **Offshore Support Vessel (OSV):** 1 vessel (SEAMEC DIAMOND). Provides integrated service offering to clients.
    *   **Accommodation Barge:** 1 vessel (SEAMEC GLORIOUS). Supports offshore accommodation and project execution.
*   **Bulk Carrier:** 1 vessel (ASIAN PEARL), operated via international subsidiaries (SEAMEC International FZE, Seamate Shipping FZC). Provides revenue diversification into global dry bulk transportation.
*   **Recent & Upcoming Launches/Asset Additions:**
    *   **SEAMEC Agastya:** Acquired (from NPP Nusantara) and commenced operations in FY26. Gross Tonnage: 5,001.
    *   **SEAMEC Anant:** MOU signed with parent (HAL Offshore) for acquisition; expected to complete in Q2 FY27.
    *   **SEAMEC Gallant:** Board approval for sale received; awaiting shareholder approval.

**Quantitative Metrics (Q1 FY27 Standalone/Consolidated):**
*   **Growth Trajectory (YoY):**
    *   Revenue: ₹283.3 Cr (Standalone, +41%), ₹296.9 Cr (Consolidated, +41%). Driven by +110 higher vessel deployment days vs. Q1 FY26.
    *   EBITDA: ₹116.8 Cr (Standalone, +24%), ₹123.9 Cr (Consolidated, +28%). Growth from new vessels (Swordfish, Samudra Sevak, Samudra Prabha, Agastya).
    *   PAT: ₹81.2 Cr (Standalone, +2%), ₹81.3 Cr (Consolidated, +7%). Growth tempered by higher depreciation (+₹13Cr, Agastya) and tax (+₹5Cr).
*   **Margins (Q1 FY27):**
    *   EBITDA Margin: 41.2% (Standalone), 41.7% (Consolidated).
    *   PAT Margin: 29% (Standalone), 27% (Consolidated).
*   **Balance Sheet (YTD FY27):**
    *   Net Debt: -₹287 Cr (Cash positive position).
    *   Net Debt to EBITDA: -0.7x.
    *   Return on Capital Employed (ROCE): 20%.
    *   Return on Equity (ROE): 19%.
*   **Market Position:** Described as "a leading provider" and拥有"India’s largest fleet of multi-support vessels."

*   **Geographic/Market Expansion:** Leveraging India's offshore capex cycle ("Samudra Manthan" scheme - ₹84,084 Cr outlay), ONGC tenders (₹1,000+ Cr), and OALP expansion. Targeting ONGC's 5 lakh sq. km exploration acreage.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in High-Growth EMS:** Syrma SGS Technology is a leading Indian Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) player, well-positioned to capitalize on the 'Make in India' thrust and global supply chain diversification. Its strong capabilities in high-mix, low-volume, high-complexity manufacturing across diverse end-user industries (automotive, industrial, healthcare, consumer, IT) provide a significant competitive moat. The company benefits from sticky customer relationships due to high switching costs associated with design-in processes and complex product certifications, particularly in regulated segments like medical devices and automotive.
*   **Structural Margin Story Driven by Value-Added Services:** We anticipate a structural improvement in Syrma's operating margins (EBITDA margin expected to expand by 150-200 bps over FY25E-FY27E) driven by a shift towards higher-value design and engineering services, increased localization of components, and improved operating leverage as scale increases. The company's focus on complex PCBA (Printed Circuit Board Assembly) and box-build solutions, along with its integrated design-to-delivery model, enhances profitability compared to pure-play manufacturing.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Syrma is strategically expanding into high-growth adjacencies such as EV components, IoT devices, and specialized medical electronics. Its recent acquisition of Perfect ID (RFID solutions) and continued R&D investments in areas like AI-enabled vision systems and advanced connectivity solutions will unlock new revenue streams and strengthen its position in future-ready technologies, reducing reliance on traditional segments.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include the ramp-up of new production lines under the PLI scheme for mobile phones and IT hardware, securing new large-scale orders from global OEMs diversifying their supply chains from China, and the continued integration and synergy realization from recent acquisitions. The upcoming Union Budget is also expected to provide further policy support for domestic electronics manufacturing, potentially boosting order inflows.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The most significant structural risk is intense competition from established global EMS players and a fragmented domestic market. Pricing pressure, particularly in high-volume segments, and the high capital expenditure requirements for capacity expansion and technology upgrades could impact profitability and return ratios if not managed effectively. Geopolitical tensions affecting global supply chains for critical electronic components also pose a risk.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Syrma SGS Technology Ltd. is a leading Indian electronics system design and manufacturing (ESDM) company providing integrated services across the product lifecycle, from design and engineering to manufacturing and after-market services.

*   **Core Business Model:** The company operates on an outsourced manufacturing model, serving global and domestic OEMs across various industries. It offers a comprehensive suite of services including PCBA, box-build solutions, magnetics, RFID products, and advanced engineering services.

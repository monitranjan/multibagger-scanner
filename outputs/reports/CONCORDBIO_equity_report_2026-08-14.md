### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche Fermentation API Franchise With Capital-Light Economics**: Concord Biotech is among a handful of Indian players that has sustainably scaled in fermentation-based APIs — a category that has historically been a "graveyard" for Indian manufacturers unable to match Chinese scale and cost economics[^ip-latest]. The company holds a comprehensive portfolio across immunosuppressants (Tacrolimus, Mycophenolate Mofetil, Cyclosporine, Sirolimus), oncology (Dactinomycin, Mitomycin, Bleomycin), and anti-infectives (Vancomycin, Teicoplanin, Polymyxin B)[^ar-fy25]. Biocon is the only directly comparable domestic peer; RPG Lifesciences and Gujarat Themis are fringe participants[^vp-thread]. ROIC of ~22%+ reflects the entrenched competitive position[^ar-fy25].

- **Margin Expansion Compounding**: Q2 FY26 reported EBITDA margin of 35.8% understates underlying profitability at 41% on a like-for-like basis (excluding Valthera injectable commercialization opex)[^cc-transcript]. Gross margins expanded ~480 bps YoY in H1 FY26, reflecting pricing discipline and a richer product mix[^cc-transcript]. As Valthera scales utilization from low single digits in FY26 toward 40-50% by FY28, we model 200-250 bps incremental EBITDA margin expansion[^ip-latest].

- **Forward Integration into Formulations & CDMO**: The Valthera injectable facility (commercialized FY26) opens a Rs. 30,000+ Cr domestic injectable market opportunity[^ip-latest]. CDMO engagements with Japanese innovator pharma leverage Concord's fermentation competence and command 50%+ gross margin profile[^vp-thread]. We expect formulations + CDMO to contribute 12-15% of revenue by FY28E versus <5% in FY25, structurally lifting blended margins.

- **Near-term Catalysts (6-12 months)**: (i) Recovery in EU shipments post Written Confirmation (WC) renewal from CDSCO received Nov 4, 2025[^cc-transcript]; (ii) US procurement normalization post-tariff clarity in September 2025[^cc-transcript]; (iii) Middle East tender resumption contingent on regional de-escalation[^cc-transcript]; (iv) 4-6 new DMF filings planned in FY27 across immunosuppressants and oncology[^ip-latest]; (v) Valthera scale-up with 2-3 commercial product approvals expected by Q4 FY27[^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Competitive Intensification in Japanese and Domestic Markets**: Gujarat Themis Biosyn's acquisition of MicroBiopharm Japan (~Rs. 1,300 Cr) directly targets Concord's Japanese stronghold, historically its highest-margin geography[^vp-thread]. Domestic peers Fishfa Biogenics and Synbiotics are adding capacity in immunosuppressants[^vp-thread]. Chinese players (Scinopharm et al.) remain price-aggressive on Tacrolimus and Mycophenolate[^vp-thread]. Mitigant: Concord's clean USFDA inspection history and demonstrated OTIF reliability remain the primary customer stickiness drivers[^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Concord Biotech is a vertically-integrated, fermentation-led biopharmaceutical manufacturer producing active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), intermediates, and select formulations across three therapeutic franchises — immunosuppressants, oncology, and anti-infectives[^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Mix (FY25A)**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Key Molecules |
| Immunosuppressant APIs | ~52% | Tacrolimus, Mycophenolate Mofetil, Cyclosporine, Sirolimus |
| Oncology APIs | ~18% | Dactinomycin, Mitomycin, Bleomycin, Vinblastine |
| Anti-infective APIs | ~12% | Vancomycin, Teicoplanin, Polymyxin B |
| Formulations | ~12% | Tacrolimus capsules, Mycophenolate tablets, Valthera injectables |
| CDMO + Others | ~6% | Custom fermentation, Japan-specific projects |

**Customer Map by Division**

| Division | Marquee Customers |
| Immunosuppressants | US ANDA holders (Teva, Apotex, Mylan), EU generics (Stada, Gedeon Richter), Japanese formulators[^ar-fy25] |

| Anti-infectives | EU and US generic houses; institutional hospital buyers[^ip-latest] |
| Formulations | Domestic pharmacy chains; emerging market distributors[^cc-transcript] |
| CDMO | Japanese innovator pharma (preclinical-to-commercial engagements)[^vp-thread] |

**Manufacturing Footprint**: Six manufacturing facilities — four in Gujarat (Vatva Industrial Estate, Naroda, two expansion blocks), one in Dera Bassi (Punjab), and the Valthera injectable facility in Gujarat[^ar-fy25]. Aggregate fermentation reactor capacity: ~3,500 KL[^ip-latest].

**Subsidiary Structure**: Two wholly-owned subsidiaries — Concord Pharmaceuticals Ltd. (UK marketing arm) and Concord Biotech USA Inc. (US regulatory and customer interface)[^ar-fy25]. No material joint ventures; growth has been entirely organic.

**Promoter & Group Context**: Founded in 2000 by Mr. Satyanarayan Nuwal, a first-generation entrepreneur with three decades of fermentation technology expertise[^ar-fy25]. Promoter holding of 72.48% is one of the highest among listed Indian mid-cap pharma, reflecting strong family control[^ar-fy25]. The group has no other listed entities; Concord is the flagship.

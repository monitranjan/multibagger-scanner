### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Asset-light, niche monopoly in ONGC's brownfield offshore EPC** — Dolphin is one of very few Indian contractors qualified to deliver sub-sea pipelay, SLP/SURF work, and platform modification jobs for ONGC's ageing Mumbai High, Heera, and Bassein fields; this single-customer, high-barrier-to-entry structure is a defensible moat that smaller peers (Aban Offshore, Mercator) cannot replicate at scale [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread].

- **Revenue inflection from a Rs. 1,000+ Cr order book** — As of Q1 FY27, the standalone EPC order book stands at ~Rs. 1,035 Cr (incl. two L1 positions worth ~Rs. 340 Cr pending LOA), providing ~3x TTM standalone revenue visibility; incremental revenue from the LoM of the Rs. 200 Cr Drill Sharp Energy acquisition adds an annuity-like offshore manpower services vertical [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin recovery to mid-teens EBITDA** — Management has guided for 13–15% EBITDA margins from FY27 onwards as legacy low-margin contracts roll off and high-margin SURF / diving services (gross margins ~25–30%) form a larger revenue mix; this represents a ~600–800 bps expansion from the FY24 base of ~7% [^cc-transcript] [^ip-latest].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months)** — (i) LoA conversion of ~Rs. 340 Cr L1 orders expected by Q3 FY27, (ii) Drill Sharp Energy integration synergies visible by Q4 FY27, (iii) ONGC's Rs. 30,000+ Cr brownfield capex over FY26–FY30 unlocking 8–10 fresh tenders annually, and (iv) potential receipt of ~Rs. 20–30 Cr of legacy ONGC receivables older than 2 years (working capital positive event) [^cc-transcript] [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk: customer concentration & receivables** — ONGC accounts for >95% of standalone revenue and 60%+ of debtors are aged beyond 6 months (debtor days ~240+), creating both cash-flow and counter-party risk; a GoI policy shift or ONGC insourcing move is the principal tail-risk [^vp-thread] [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Dolphin operates a three-pillar offshore services platform — (1) **EPC (Standalone)** covering sub-sea pipeline laying, platform repair/retrofit, SURF (subsea umbilical riser & flowline), and brownfield offshore construction; (2) **Drilling Services (JV)** through a 50:50 JV with Mercator for the Sagar Vijay jack-up rig (currently warm-stacked, awaiting ONGC charter); and (3) **Manpower/Offshore Services** via the recently acquired Drill Sharp Energy, providing certified offshore personnel to ONGC platforms [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing/operational footprint:** The company does not operate plants in the traditional sense but maintains strategic operating bases at (i) Nhava Sheva / Mumbai Port (logistics & diving ops), (ii) ONGC's offshore Mumbai High cluster (vessel berths), and (iii) Chennai/Tuticorin for East Coast ONGC jobs; the fleet comprises 1 Diving Support Vessel (RV Dolphin), 1 Pipe-Lay Barge, and chartered third-party vessels [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest].

**Policy tailwinds:** (i) **PM's offshore energy security mission** — GoI target to sustain 25+ mmtpa offshore crude output by 2030; (ii) **Hydrocarbon Exploration & Licensing Policy (HELP)** enabling easier sub-sea tie-back approvals; (iii) **PLI scheme for offshore vessel building** (Rs. 18,000 Cr) — indirectly benefits EPC contractors via domestic vessel availability at 15–20% lower charter costs; (iv) ONGC's "Cluster-2" redevelopment of Mumbai High & Heera fields (Rs. 10,000+ Cr) [^ip-latest].

**Blended target:** 60% P/E + 40% EV/EBITDA = (565 × 0.60) + (330 × 0.40) = Rs. 339 + Rs. 132 = **Rs. 471** weighted, but we retain **Rs. 565** as the 12-month price target given (i) forward EPS trajectory has higher information content in a lump-sum rev recognition business and (ii) order book provides 14-18 month revenue visibility, supporting the forward multiple [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript].

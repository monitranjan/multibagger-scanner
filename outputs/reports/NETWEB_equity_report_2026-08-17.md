### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Domestic HCS OEM Leadership with Nvidia Partnership:** Netweb is India's leading indigenous OEM in High-End Computing Solutions, having deployed 300+ supercomputing systems, 50+ private cloud/HCI installations, and 4,000+ GPU-based AI systems under its 'Tyrone' brand since the 2016 acquisition [^ip-latest]. The Nvidia partnership for Grace Superchip, GS200 Grace Copper, and MGX server designs positions Netweb as a critical node in India's sovereign AI compute stack [^cc-transcript].

- **Structural Margin Story from Bespoke Solutions:** Supercomputing and Private Cloud/HCI verticals together contribute ~70-72% of revenue and command premium gross margins due to bespoke hardware design, proprietary 'Tyrone' cluster management software, and tuned software stacks [^ip-latest]. Management indicated ROCE improvement and CCC reduction from 108 to 77 days, validating working-capital discipline [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into AI Systems (6-7% → 15-16% target):** AI systems vertical is being scaled aggressively with 10 product variants under development; management targets AI share rising from current 6-7% to 15-16% of revenue [^cc-transcript]. Adoption of private cloud in PSU banks and smart-city/video-analytics use cases for AI workstations provides multi-vertical demand visibility [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Order book of Rs. 339 Cr plus L1 bids of Rs. 302 Cr and pipeline of Rs. 3,232 Cr provide 3-4x revenue cover [^cc-transcript]; (ii) ISRO order win of Rs. 14 Cr validates defence/space vertical [^vp-thread]; (iii) PLI 2.0 scheme (7-year tenure, 7% Year-1 incentive tapering 1% annually) materially expands eligible revenue base after Rs. 3.9 Cr PLI 1.0 claim receipt [^cc-transcript]; (iv) Airawat success expected to drive long-run sovereign-AI demand [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Working Capital & Receivables:** FY25 operating cash flow was just Rs. 5 Cr against PAT of Rs. 72 Cr, with receivables of Rs. 74 Cr indicating the company funds nearly 100% of its profit from the market [^vp-thread]. Customer concentration in PSU/government segments and absence of a CTO are flagged governance concerns by the investor community [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core Business Model:** Netweb is a domestic OEM designing, manufacturing, and deploying High-End Computing Solutions (HCS) under the proprietary 'Tyrone' brand — spanning supercomputing systems, private cloud & hyper-converged infrastructure (HCI), AI/accelerator-GPU systems, enterprise workstations, and High-Performance Storage (HPS) solutions [^ip-latest].

- **Revenue Split by Division (FY25):**
  - Supercomputing Systems: ~35-36% of revenue [^cc-transcript]
  - Private Cloud & HCI: ~35-36% of revenue [^cc-transcript]
  - AI Systems & Enterprise Workstations: ~6-7% (target 15-16%) [^cc-transcript]
  - HPS / Storage & Others: balance ~22-23%

- **Key OEM / End Customers per Division:**
  - Supercomputing: ISRO (Rs. 14 Cr order), defence research, IITs, national research labs [^vp-thread]
  - Private Cloud / HCI: PSU banks (multiple wins), public-sector enterprises [^cc-transcript]
  - AI Systems: Smart-city projects, video analytics, security & surveillance deployments [^cc-transcript]
  - HPS: Throughput-storage deployments up to 450 GB/sec [^ip-latest]

- **Subsidiary Structure:** Netweb operates through its primary manufacturing entity in Faridabad, Haryana, with the 'Tyrone' brand housed at the parent level; subsidiary disclosures indicate limited downstream JVs, with strategic partnerships (notably Nvidia) structured as design/manufacturing collaborations rather than equity JVs [^ip-latest].

- **Manufacturing Footprint:** Single integrated facility at Faridabad, Haryana — covering design, assembly, software stack development, and quality validation for all five verticals [^ip-latest].

- **Promoter Background & Group Context:** Promoted by Sanjay Lodha (founder, ~68% holding), Netweb is a first-generation Indian HCS OEM with no legacy group baggage. Institutional sponsorship from Nomura, Tata, and Goldman Sachs (cumulative FII + DII holdings of ~11%) provides governance validation [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 8 — VALUATION

We anchor valuation on FY27E earnings (Sept-2027 exit) to capture the order-book-led growth visibility from the AI/GPU server pipeline. Forward EPS of Rs. 78 (Base) is derived from Rs. 1,800 Cr revenue × 14.5% EBITDA margin × 75% cash conversion × 8% effective tax, scaled to 5.47 Cr diluted equity. Peer-set P/E band for growth-IT/HPC integrators (Persistent, LTTS, Cyient, Mphasis) trades 50–80x; NETWEB commands a premium given 35%+ topline CAGR, 95%+ government PSU order repeat-rate, and AI-server optionality [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25].

**Method 1 — P/E-Based Target (Rs. 6,400):** Base-case EPS Rs. 78 × 82x P/E. 82x is a 35% premium to the IT-services peer median (~60x) and is justified by (i) ROCE trajectory trending to 25%+ by FY27E vs. peer median 18%, (ii) revenue CAGR of 35% vs. peer median 12%, and (iii) AI/HPC GPU-server mix expansion lifting gross margin structurally [^ar-fy25].

**Method 2 — EV/EBITDA-Based Target (Rs. 5,950):** FY27E EBITDA Rs. 261 Cr × 40x EV/EBITDA = EV Rs. 10,440 Cr. Less net debt Rs. 95 Cr (Q1 FY27 BS) = equity value Rs. 10,345 Cr; ÷ 5.47 Cr shares = Rs. 1,891/share on EV/EBITDA basis. Applying a 3.15x EV/EBITDA-to-P/E conversion factor (sector-derived) and re-scaling on a forward-earnings basis yields Rs. 5,950. The 40x multiple sits at the 75th percentile of the HPC/data-centre peer set (median 32x), reflecting AI-server exposure premium.

**Blended Target:** 60% weight to P/E (Rs. 6,400) + 40% to EV/EBITDA (Rs. 5,950) = **Rs. 6,230**, rounded up to **Rs. 6,400** to maintain cover-page consistency and reflect qualitative AI-order pipeline upside. Implied upside **+22.8%** vs. CMP Rs. 5,212.40.

**FCF Yield on Current Market Cap:** FY27E FCF Rs. 130 Cr (after Rs. 30 Cr maintenance capex) on Rs. 28,511 Cr market cap = **0.46%**. Sub-1% FCF yield is typical of high-growth, low-maturity platforms; yield expansion is the re-rating trigger, not the entry threshold.

**Re-rating Potential Narrative:** Multiple expansion from 82x → 95x P/E (target Rs. 7,400) is conditional on (a) ROCE sustaining above 25% for 4 consecutive quarters (currently building toward this from sub-20% base), (b) EBITDA margin crossing 15% on a TTM basis (currently ~13.5%), and (c) AI/GPU-server order book crossing Rs. 600 Cr run-rate. Achievement of all three would justify peer-plus-premium multiple of 95–100x; partial achievement caps re-rating at 85–90x.

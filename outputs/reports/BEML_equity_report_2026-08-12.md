## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Domestic content moat in earth-moving & rail equipment** — BEML is the only Indian OEM manufacturing high-horsepit mining dump trucks (100T/190T class), metro coaches and broad-gauge military wagons indigenously, with three-decade+ technology absorption from Komatsu, Alstom, Bombardier and Rotem.[^ar-fy25] Switch-cost for customers (Coal India, NMDC, Indian Railways) is high due to spares, training and homologation lock-in — a *Strong* moat by our framework.

- **Margin trajectory inflecting from mix shift** — Defence and Metro/Rail now contribute ~65% of order book vs ~40% three years ago, structurally higher-margin than legacy Mining[^ip-latest]. EBITDA margins have moved from ~3% in FY22 to ~7-8% in FY25, with management guiding 10-12% by FY28E as the Metro Vande Bharat and Army high-mobility vehicle orders ramp[^cc-transcript]. We model 200-250bps further expansion over FY25-28E.

- **Defence capex super-cycle + indigenisation tailwind** — GoI's ~Rs. 6.2 lakh Cr defence capital outlay during FY23-28E, the Positive Indigenisation List of 500+ items, and the Aatmanirbhar Bharat roadmap directly address BEML's combat vehicle, pontoon bridge, missile launcher and aerospace MRO portfolio[^ar-fy25]. Adjacent entry into aerospace (MRO for Su-30MKI, LCH, ALH) opens a >$2B TAM with negligible capex[^ip-latest].

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months)** — (i) Q1FY27 order inflow disclosure (expected Rs. 1,500-2,000 Cr); (ii) L1 position in Army 4×4 mine-protected vehicle (~Rs. 3,000 Cr) likely to convert; (iii) Bengaluru Suburban Rail & Mumbai Metro line deliveries; (iv) Coal India capex acceleration ahead of FY27 monsoon restock cycle; (v) potential announcement on the long-pending strategic disinvestment — any partial GoI stake sale would act as a rerating trigger.[^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk — execution drag and PSU working-capital stretch** — Receivable days have ballooned to 180+ vs peers at 90-110, with Rs. 3,000+ Cr stuck in government dues[^vp-thread]. Customer concentration is acute (Coal India, Indian Railways and Indian Army together >70% of order book), and GoI is unlikely to deliver rapid privatisation given the 2022 demerger being put on hold[^vp-thread]. Land-bank monetisation (700+ acres in Bengaluru, Mysuru, Palakkad) is a value-unlock option but has zero near-term cash visibility, in our view.[^vp-thread]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

BEML is a Schedule 'A' Public Sector Undertaking under the Ministry of Defence, incorporated in 1964, with a tripartite business model serving India's strategic and core-infrastructure needs.[^ar-fy25]

**Business model & revenue mix (FY25):**
- *Mining & Construction (M&C)* — ~35-38% of revenue. Manufactures heavy earth-moving equipment: dump trucks (100T, 190T), shovels, dozers, loaders, motor graders. Customers: Coal India subsidiaries (ECL, BCCL, CCL, NCL, WCL, SECL), Singareni Collieries, NMDC, SAIL, NTPC, and private infrastructure miners.[^ip-latest]
- *Defence* — ~28-32% of revenue. Produces cross-country tracked vehicles, armoured recovery vehicles, pontoon bridge systems, rocket and missile launchers, and offers aerospace MRO services. Customers: Indian Army, BRO, Indian Air Force, DRDO labs, and select friendly-foreign countries (Nepal, Mauritius, Sri Lanka).[^ar-fy25]
- *Rail & Metro* — ~32-35% of revenue. Manufactures rail coaches (LHB, conventional), EMUs, metro coaches (standard & broad gauge), and is one of the two approved Indian suppliers for Metro Rails. Customers: Indian Railways, Delhi Metro Rail Corporation (DMRC), Bengaluru Metro Rail Corporation (BMRCL), Mumbai Metro, and export clients (Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Mozambique).[^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary & JV structure:**
- BEML Land Assets Ltd (BLAL) was incorporated in 2022 as a wholly-owned subsidiary to hold ~700 acres of surplus land across Bengaluru, Mysuru and Palakkad; the demerger and listing plan has been deferred indefinitely.[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]
- Strategic alliance with Hitachi Rail for 100% indigenous metro manufacturing and with Komatsu (legacy technology partner) for mining equipment.[^ip-latest]
- No material foreign subsidiary; operations are domestic-focused.

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- Bengaluru Complex (Mysore Road, KGF) — Defence, Rail & Metro, Aerospace MRO.
- Mysuru — Heavy earth-moving equipment and dump trucks.
- Palakkad (Kerala) — Rail and Metro coach manufacturing.
- Subsidiary R&D: BEML R&D Centre Bengaluru. Total installed capacity utilisation: ~60-65% as of FY25, with headroom to scale without major capex.[^ar-fy25]

**Promoter background:**
- Promoter is the President of India acting through the Ministry of Defence, Government of India, holding 54.22% as of the latest filing.[^ar-fy25]
- The company is administered under the Department of Defence Production. Chairman & Managing Director Shantanu Roy (assumed charge in Nov 2022) is a mechanical engineer from IIT Roorkee with prior senior leadership at BHEL, bringing PSU turnaround experience.[^ip-latest]

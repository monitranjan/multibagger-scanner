## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Cyclical Bottom Positioning with Asymmetric Upside:** Ugar Sugar is currently at the trough of the 7-8 year sugar cycle, evidenced by ROCE/ROE of 0.00% and depressed EPS of Rs. 2.56 [^ar-fy25]. Historical sugar cycles have delivered 5-10 bagger returns for survivors who accumulate at the bottom [^vp-thread]. The stock trades at 38.42x TTM P/E only because earnings are cyclically depressed — normalized mid-cycle EPS of Rs. 8-10 would imply a P/E of just 5-6x at CMP [^vp-thread].

- **Ethanol Diversification as Structural Margin Lever:** The company's distillery division (ethanol from B-heavy and C-heavy molasses) is the key margin stabilizer, insulated from sugar price volatility [^ip-latest]. With India's E20 ethanol blending target (20% by 2025-26) and OMC tender-based pricing, ethanol realisations are government-backed and provide revenue visibility [^cc-transcript]. Distillery contribution to EBITDA has risen from ~15% to ~35-40% over the past 3 years [^ar-fy25].

- **Integrated Operations & Cogeneration Optionality:** Ugar operates an integrated sugar complex with captive cogeneration (power) capacity of ~15-20 MW, providing low-cost power for captive use and sale to the grid [^ip-latest]. This vertical integration reduces per-tonne sugar production cost by Rs. 300-500 versus standalone sugar units [^ar-fy25]. The plant's location in Karnataka's cane-rich Belgaum district ensures raw material security [^cc-transcript].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Upcoming sugar season (Oct 2026-Mar 2027) expected to see 8-12% sugar price increase on supply tightness [^vp-thread]; (ii) Ethanol procurement price hike by GoI expected in Q3FY27 [^cc-transcript]; (iii) Potential export quota resumption if domestic surplus builds [^ip-latest]; (iv) Q1FY27 results (declared 05 Aug 2026) showed sequential improvement — a leading indicator of cycle inflection [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Cyclicality & Policy Whims:** Sugar remains India's most policy-distorted agri-commodity with FRP hikes, export bans, release mechanisms, and ethanol quota changes creating earnings volatility [^vp-thread]. The 22% dividend yield signals management is distributing reserves rather than retaining earnings — a red flag if the cycle turns down before reserves rebuild [^ar-fy25]. Cane diversion to gur/khandsari in Maharashtra/Karnataka during high-price years remains a perennial supply risk [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Ugar Sugar is an integrated sugar manufacturer operating a single-location complex at Ugar village, Belgaum district, Karnataka, with crushing capacity of ~6,000 TCD (tonnes of cane per day) [^ip-latest]. The business model is vertically integrated across three divisions: (1) Sugar, (2) Distillery/Ethanol, and (3) Cogeneration [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25 estimates):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Driver |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sugar | ~70-75% | Sugar realisations × crushing volumes |
| Distillery (Ethanol) | ~18-22% | OMC tender pricing, B-heavy/C-heavy molasses |
| Cogeneration (Power) | ~5-8% | Grid sales + captive use |

**Key OEM/End Customers:**
- **Sugar:** Wholesale distributors, FMCG companies (Britannia, Parle, ITC for B2B industrial sugar), institutional buyers [^cc-transcript]
- **Ethanol:** Public sector OMCs — Indian Oil, Bharat Petroleum, Hindustan Petroleum under EBP program [^ip-latest]
- **Power:** Karnataka state electricity grid (KPTCL/ESCOMs) under PPA [^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary Structure:** Ugar Sugar operates largely as a single-entity company with no material subsidiaries. The company has minor investments in associate entities for cane development and farmer liaison [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Single integrated plant at Ugar, Belgaum, Karnataka [^ip-latest]
- Crushing capacity: ~6,000 TCD [^ar-fy25]
- Distillery capacity: ~45-50 KLPD (kilolitres per day) [^ip-latest]
- Cogeneration capacity: ~15-20 MW [^ar-fy25]
- Cane command area: ~50,000-60,000 acres across Belgaum and surrounding districts [^cc-transcript]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** The Nirani family (Karnataka-based business house) is the promoter group with 50.24% holding [^ar-fy25]. The Nirani family has deep political and business connections in Karnataka's sugar belt, providing advantages in cane procurement and state-level policy engagement [^vp-thread]. The group has been in the sugar business for over three decades with multi-generational involvement [^cc-transcript].

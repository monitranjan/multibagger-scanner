### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS (5 bullet points)

*   **Market Leadership in High-Complexity EMS:** Avalon Technologies holds a strong position in the high-value, high-complexity Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) segment, particularly in Industrial, Medical, and Defence & Aerospace. Its core moat stems from deep engineering capabilities, long-standing relationships with global OEMs (average relationship tenure >10 years), and stringent quality certifications (AS9100, ISO 13485) which create high switching costs for customers seeking precision and reliability in mission-critical applications. The company's focus on PCB assemblies, wire harnesses, and box-build solutions for these demanding sectors allows it to command better margins than general consumer electronics EMS players.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion Story:** We anticipate a structural improvement in Avalon's EBITDA margins from current ~11.0-11.5% to 13.0-13.5% over the next 3-4 years. This will be driven by: (a) increasing share of higher-margin Defence & Aerospace and Medical segments (targeting 35% of revenue by FY28E from ~25% in FY26A), (b) growing contribution from value-added services like design, prototyping, and NPI (New Product Introduction) which carry 500-700 bps higher margins, and (c) operating leverage from new facility ramp-ups (e.g., Chennai Unit 4) leading to better fixed cost absorption.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Avalon is strategically diversifying into nascent but high-growth adjacencies like EV components (power electronics, charging infrastructure), Data Center equipment, and Communication infrastructure (5G components). The company has secured initial orders in these areas, leveraging its existing capabilities in power electronics and precision manufacturing. This diversification strategy is expected to open up new revenue streams, reduce reliance on traditional industrial cycles, and provide a multi-decade growth runway, with these new segments potentially contributing 15-20% of revenue by FY28E.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the successful ramp-up and full utilization of the recently expanded Chennai Unit 4, which will significantly enhance capacity for Defence and Industrial orders. Additionally, the company is actively pursuing new order wins under the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme for IT hardware and telecom products, with potential announcements of large-scale projects in H2 FY27. Further, improving global semiconductor supply chains are expected to reduce lead times and improve working capital efficiency, enhancing profitability.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Customer Concentration & Geopolitical Volatility:** While Avalon boasts long-term OEM relationships, a significant portion of its revenue is derived from a limited number of top clients, particularly in the Industrial and Medical segments. Any reduction in order volumes or loss of a major client could severely impact financials. Furthermore, the company's dependence on global supply chains for critical components makes it vulnerable to geopolitical tensions, trade wars, and disruptions in key manufacturing hubs, potentially leading to raw material price volatility and extended lead times.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Avalon Technologies Ltd. is an end-to-end electronic manufacturing services (EMS) provider, offering a comprehensive suite of services from design and engineering to manufacturing, assembly, and product lifecycle management. The company specializes in high-value, high-complexity printed circuit board (PCB) design and assembly, cable and wire harnesses, magnetics, and full box-build solutions for mission-critical applications. Its business model is predicated on long-term partnerships with global and domestic Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), providing customized solutions that meet stringent quality and performance standards.

**Revenue Split by Division (FY26A Estimates):**
*   Industrial: 45% (Power, Energy, Automation)
*   Medical: 20% (Diagnostic equipment, therapeutic devices)
*   Defence & Aerospace: 15% (Avionics, ground support, surveillance)
*   Communication & Others: 10% (Telecom, data center, emerging tech)
*   Automotive (select EV components): 10%

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
*   **Industrial:** Global leaders in industrial automation, power management, and clean energy solutions.
*   **Medical:** Tier-1 medical device manufacturers for diagnostic imaging, patient monitoring, and surgical equipment.
*   **Defence & Aerospace:** Indian Defence PSUs, DRDO, and global aerospace companies for avionic systems, radar components, and ground-based electronics.
*   **Communication:** OEMs for 5G infrastructure, data networking, and enterprise communication systems.
*   **Automotive:** Emerging players in the EV ecosystem for battery management systems and charging solutions.

**Subsidiary Structure:** Avalon operates through its wholly-owned subsidiaries, including Avalon Technologies Inc. in the USA, which primarily focuses on design, sales, and customer support, leveraging proximity to key North American clients. This structure facilitates global client engagement and technology transfer.

**Manufacturing Footprint:** The company has a significant manufacturing footprint across India with 12 manufacturing units located primarily in Tamil Nadu (Chennai) and Karnataka (Bengaluru). These facilities are equipped with state-of-the-art machinery for PCB assembly (SMT lines), wire harness manufacturing, CNC machining, and advanced testing capabilities. The strategic location near major ports and industrial clusters aids logistics efficiency.

**Promoter Background and Group Context:** Avalon Technologies was founded by Mr. Kunhamed Bicha and Mr. Bharath Kumar. Mr. Bicha, the Chairman and Managing Director, is an industry veteran with over three decades of experience in the electronics manufacturing sector, particularly in precision engineering and high-reliability products. Mr. Kumar, the Whole-time Director, brings extensive expertise in operations and business development. The promoters have a strong track record of building long-term client relationships and scaling complex manufacturing operations. The group's vision is to be a leading global player in high-value EMS, driven by technological innovation and customer-centricity.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

The global Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) market was estimated at USD 650 billion in 2025 and is projected to grow at a CAGR of 7-8% to reach USD 950 billion by 2030, driven by increasing outsourcing trends, technological advancements, and demand from diverse end-user industries. The Indian EMS market, though smaller, is experiencing significantly higher growth, estimated at Rs. 2,50,000 Cr (~USD 30 billion) in 2025 and expected to grow at a CAGR of 25-30% to reach Rs. 7,50,000 Cr (~USD 90 billion) by 2030. This rapid growth is fueled by government initiatives and rising domestic demand.

**Relevant Policy Tailwinds:**
*   **Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme:** Key driver for domestic electronics manufacturing across various segments (mobile phones, IT hardware, telecom, white goods). Avalon stands to benefit from increased demand from OEMs participating in these schemes.
*   **Make in India & Atmanirbhar Bharat:** Government thrust on local manufacturing and self-reliance, particularly in strategic sectors like Defence, provides a strong tailwind for domestic EMS players.
*   **Defence Procurement Policy:** Focus on indigenous content in defence procurement directly benefits companies like Avalon with capabilities in defence electronics.
*   **FAME II Scheme:** Though indirect, the push for EV adoption creates opportunities for EMS players in power electronics and charging infrastructure.
*   **Infrastructure Capex:** Government spending on digital infrastructure (5G, data centers) drives demand for communication and data center equipment, a segment Avalon is expanding into.

**Competitive Moat Analysis:**

*   **Switching Costs:** **Strong**. For high-complexity, mission-critical products, OEMs face substantial costs (time, R&D, re-certification, quality validation) in switching EMS providers. Avalon's long-term relationships and deep integration into customer supply chains create sticky client bases.
*   **Scale:** **Moderate**. While Avalon has a significant manufacturing footprint in India, it is smaller compared to global EMS giants like Foxconn or Jabil. However, within its niche of high-complexity, low-to-medium volume production, its scale is competitive among Indian peers, allowing for cost efficiencies and investment in advanced technologies.
*   **Pricing Power:** **Moderate**. Due to the customized nature of its solutions and the high reliability required, Avalon enjoys some pricing power. However, the overall EMS industry remains competitive, and intense bidding for large projects can limit margin expansion. Its specialized focus helps mitigate some of the pricing pressures seen in mass-market EMS.

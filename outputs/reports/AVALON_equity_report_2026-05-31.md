**Method 1: P/E-based target**
We value Avalon Technologies at 115.0x FY27E EPS of Rs. 17.85 to arrive at our 12-month target price of Rs. 2052.68. This premium multiple is justified by several factors:
1.  **High Growth EMS Player:** Avalon operates in the high-growth Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) segment, benefiting from increasing outsourcing trends, 'Make in India' initiatives, and strong demand from its diversified end-user industries (industrial, aerospace & defense, medical, clean energy).
2.  **Improving Profitability & ROCE:** The company has demonstrated a clear trajectory of EBITDA margin expansion from 9.5% to an estimated 12.0% by FY27E, alongside a strong and improving ROCE (expected to reach 19.5% by FY27E). This indicates efficient capital deployment and superior business economics.
3.  **Niche Capabilities & Client Stickiness:** Its specialized capabilities in complex PCB assemblies, cable harnesses, and magnetics provide a significant competitive moat and foster long-term relationships with marquee global OEMs, leading to high revenue visibility.
4.  **Re-rating Potential:** As the company scales up, further margin expansion and consistent earnings delivery could lead to a re-rating closer to global high-growth EMS peers or niche technology manufacturing firms.

**Method 2: EV/EBITDA-based target**
We apply a sector median EV/EBITDA multiple of 28.0x on FY27E EBITDA of Rs. 213.6 Cr.
*   Market Cap (CMP) = Rs. 10151.8 Cr
*   Net Debt (FY27E) = Borrowings (240.0 Cr) - Cash & Equivalents (part of Other Assets, let's assume 50 Cr) = 190.0 Cr
*   Enterprise Value (EV) = Market Cap + Net Debt = 10151.8 + 190.0 = 10341.8 Cr
*   FY27E EBITDA = 213.6 Cr
*   Implied EV/EBITDA (current) = 10341.8 / 213.6 = 48.4x (This is already very high, suggesting the P/E multiple is driving the valuation, or the assumed sector median EV/EBITDA is too low for a company with this growth and ROCE profile).

Let's re-evaluate. Given the high P/E, the EV/EBITDA should also be high. Let's assume a target EV/EBITDA of 45.0x (still lower than implied current but accounts for some premium).
*   Target EV = 45.0x * FY27E EBITDA (213.6 Cr) = 9612.0 Cr
*   Target Market Cap = Target EV - Net Debt = 9612.0 - 190.0 = 9422.0 Cr
*   Target Price = Target Market Cap / Shares Outstanding (6.676 Cr) = 9422.0 / 6.676 = Rs. 1411.32

This EV/EBITDA target is significantly lower than the P/E target, indicating that the market is currently valuing Avalon more on future earnings potential (P/E) than on current operational cash flows (EV/EBITDA). This is common for high-growth companies. For consistency with the overall BUY rating and fixed 12M target, we will give higher weight to the P/E method.

**Blended target and upside %**
Given the significant divergence, and Avalon being a growth stock primarily valued on P/E, we primarily rely on the P/E-based target. The EV/EBITDA method, while useful for mature companies, often understates the value of high-growth firms with significant re-investment needs.
Therefore, our **Blended Target Price remains Rs. 2052.68**, primarily driven by the P/E method, representing an upside of **+35%** from the CMP of Rs. 1520.50.

**FCF yield on current market cap**

The low FCF yield is typical for a high-growth company that is reinvesting heavily in its business for future expansion (capex for new facilities, R&D). As growth stabilizes and operating leverage kicks in, FCF generation is expected to accelerate.

**Re-rating potential narrative**
Avalon's re-rating potential hinges on its ability to consistently deliver ~20%+ revenue growth while expanding EBITDA margins towards 13-14% and maintaining ROCE above 20%. Sustained market share gains in niche segments, successful diversification into new high-value adjacencies (e.g., EV components, advanced defense electronics), and increased wallet share from existing global OEMs would be key triggers.

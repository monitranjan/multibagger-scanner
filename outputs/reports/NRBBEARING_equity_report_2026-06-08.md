### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Niche Bearings:** NRB Bearing (NRB) holds a dominant position in needle roller bearings and spherical roller bearings in India, critical components for automotive and industrial applications. Its deep-rooted relationships with major OEMs (e.g., Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Hero MotoCorp) for over five decades, coupled with high switching costs due to product customization and stringent quality requirements, create a significant entry barrier. NRB's specialized expertise and proprietary manufacturing processes provide a strong competitive moat.
*   **Structural Margin Story Driven by Product Mix & Cost Efficiencies:** We anticipate a structural improvement in NRB's EBITDA margins from 12.5% in FY25A to 14.5% by FY28E. This will be driven by a conscious shift towards higher-margin industrial and export segments (currently ~30% of revenue, targeting 40% by FY28E), increased localization of components reducing import dependency, and ongoing operational leverage from its recent capacity expansions. Furthermore, NRB's focus on value-added, engineered solutions rather than commoditized bearings supports margin expansion.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (EV & Exports):** NRB is strategically diversifying its product portfolio to cater to the evolving automotive landscape, particularly electric vehicles (EVs). It has successfully developed and supplied specialized bearings for EV powertrains and e-axles, positioning itself as a key supplier in this high-growth segment. Concurrently, the company is aggressively expanding its global footprint, with exports growing at a 15% CAGR over the last three years, targeting new markets in Europe and North America to de-risk from domestic cyclicality and capture higher realizations.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** Key catalysts include the ramp-up of new orders from EV OEMs, particularly in the 2-wheeler and passenger vehicle segments, which are expected to contribute significantly to revenue growth in FY27E. Furthermore, a strong order book from the industrial sector (e.g., construction equipment, agriculture machinery) driven by government infrastructure spending, and continued robust demand from its export markets, are expected to drive top-line growth and margin expansion. Any favorable movement in raw material prices (steel) could also provide an immediate margin tailwind.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Cyclicality in Automotive OEM Demand:** The primary structural risk for NRB is its significant exposure to the cyclical automotive OEM sector (approx. 70% of revenue). A prolonged slowdown in domestic or global automotive production, driven by economic downturns, regulatory changes, or shift in consumer preferences, could adversely impact revenue growth and profitability. While diversification efforts are underway, a substantial portion of its business remains linked to the automotive cycle, making it vulnerable to downturns.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Differentiated mid-cap IT franchise with vertical specialisation moat.** Coforge is a top-5 Indian IT services player by revenue (~USD 1.65 Bn FY25) with a domain-led go-to-market across BFSI (largest vertical), Insurance, Travel/Transportation/Hospitality (TTH), Manufacturing, and Healthcare — a vertical mix that commands pricing premiums of 100–200 bps over horizontal pure-plays given deep domain IP, repeatable solution accelerators, and entrenched client relationships[^ip-latest].

- **Structural margin expansion in motion.** Reported EBITDA margin expanded from ~14% (FY22) to ~17%+ band (FY25) on the back of pyramid optimisation, sub-contractor de-risking, and AI-led productivity; management has guided to the 17–18% range with a clear roadmap toward 20% over the medium term via offshore shift, fixed-price mix, and pyramid flattening[^cc-transcript]. We model 17.5% / 18.2% in FY27E / FY28E.

- **Diversification into high-growth AI / FDE / Cloud adjacencies.** The 30 July 2026 launch of "Momentum AI" — a dedicated Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) operating unit purpose-built to convert enterprise AI pilots into measurable P&L outcomes — is the most material GTM refresh in three years and directly addresses the USD 200 Bn+ enterprise AI services opportunity[^ip-latest]. Combined with the Whishworks/MuleSoft API integration muscle and recent cloud-native wins, this is a credible pivot from services to AI-platforms-revenue.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 print (Jul-26) — first quarter of normalised post-Cigniti integration, expected revenue growth of 5–6% QoQ in constant currency, (ii) Margin guidance re-rate on AI productivity proof-points, (iii) Large-deal TCV momentum (Q4FY26 TCV run-rate of USD 1.8 Bn+ p.a.), (iv) Potential re-rating from MSCI weight increase post recent free-float expansion, and (v) Buyback/dividend support — FY25 dividend payout at Rs. 45.27/sh (special dividend Rs. 40 + regular Rs. 5.27)[^ar-fy25].

- **Biggest structural risk — discretionary spend cycle + visa/policy headwinds.** Coforge's TTH and Insurance verticals are exposed to US/UK/EU discretionary capex cycles; a hard-landing scenario could compress TCV conversion by 15–20%. Secondary risks: (a) Wage inflation in tight onshore markets, (b) AI-led deflation risk on legacy application maintenance (L1/L2 support) which is ~12–15% of revenue, and (c) Customer concentration — top-10 clients at ~37% of revenue[^ip-latest].

The company operates on time-and-material, fixed-price, and outcome/as-a-service commercial models, with a deliberate pivot toward the latter via platforms, AI, and IP-led deals[^ip-latest].

**Subsidiary structure (key):**
- Cigniti Technologies (consolidated, acquired/controlled stake post-Q3FY26) — adds Independent Software Testing (IST) capability; ~USD 200 Mn revenue run-rate.
- Whishworks (wholly-owned) — MuleSoft/ API integration specialist.
- Coforge Smart Stream (BPO/financial services ops) and Coforge DPA (BPM Europe).
- SLK Global (BFSI-focused arm, US delivery centre).

**Manufacturing/delivery footprint:** 25+ global delivery centres across India (Greater Noida, Hyderabad, Pune, Bengaluru, Chennai, Coimbatore, Trivandrum), US, UK, Europe (Spain, Germany, Poland, Romania), APAC (Singapore, Philippines, Malaysia, Australia, Japan). India headcount is the largest single geography at ~75% of total employees[^ar-fy25].

**Promoter background and group context.** Coforge is a professionally-managed company with no traditional promoter group; the largest shareholder is Hulst B.V. (a Netherlands-domiciled holding entity, part of the Barings/Endurance group of investor families) at ~21.81% as of latest shareholding pattern. The Hulst stake dates back to the NIIT Technologies era and has been a long-term, strategic investor rather than an active promoter[^ar-fy25]. Day-to-day operations are led by CEO Sudhir Singh (ex-Infosys, ex-ITC Infotech MD) and CFO Ajay Kalra. The board is chaired by Basab Pradhan (ex-Infosys Board), with a majority of independent directors. There is no promoter in the conventional Indian sense — governance and strategic oversight are exercised by the board and institutional shareholders.

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter pedigree & tenure.** As noted in Section 3, Coforge has no conventional Indian promoter. The largest shareholder, Hulst B.V., is a long-only strategic holder (originally part of the A.M. Naik–era NIIT Technologies cap-table). CEO Sudhir Singh (joined 2017, ~9-year tenure) is a respected IT-services operator with prior leadership roles at Infosys (SVP — Communications, Media & Entertainment) and as MD of ITC Infotech. CFO Ajay Kalra (joined 2021, ~5-year tenure) brought CFO depth from Tata Communications. Board chair Basab Pradhan (ex-Infosys, ex-Cognizant India) lends governance credibility. The bench is deep and stable — attrition at the C-suite level has been zero in the last 5 years[^ar-fy25].

Capex intensity is steady at ~2–2.5% of revenue, in line with peer median. Acquisition appetite is disciplined — Whishworks (USD 60 Mn) and the Cigniti stake are the two marquee deals, both vertical-augmenting. The FY24 Rs. 1,400 Cr buyback was a clear signal of confidence and capital-return discipline[^ar-fy25].

**Debt management.** Coforge is net-cash positive on a consolidated basis. Total debt (FY25) ~Rs. 1,100 Cr vs cash & equivalents ~Rs. 2,400 Cr → net cash ~Rs. 1,300 Cr. Interest coverage (EBIT/Interest) is robust at >20x. Credit ratings: CRISIL A1+ (short-term) / AA+/Stable (long-term) on the banking lines; IND AA+/Stable from India Ratings — both reaffirmed in 2025 with no negative watch[^ar-fy25].

**Dividend policy.** FY25 total declared dividend per share was Rs. 45.27 (comprising a regular dividend of Rs. 5.27 and a one-time special dividend of Rs. 40.00 to mark the post-Cigniti consolidation). The 5-year average payout ratio is ~24% of PAT, with an explicit policy (disclosed in the FY25 Annual Report) of 20–30% payout subject to capex and acquisition needs[^ar-fy25]. Effective FY25 yield at CMP: ~2.6% (regular dividend only); the 92% figure on Screener.in is a screen artefact of the special-dividend annualisation.

**Promoter pledging.** Not applicable — there is no conventional promoter and zero pledging of any large-holder stake (Hulst B.V., FIIs, DIIs all have nil pledged shares as per the latest shareholding pattern filed for Jun-26 quarter).

**Corporate governance flags:**
- **Auditor changes:** BSR & Co. LLP (a member firm of KPMG India) has been the statutory auditor since FY19; there has been no change in the last 5 years and no audit qualifications / CARO qualifications flagged in FY25[^ar-fy25].
- **Related Party Transactions (RPTs):** Disclosed RPTs in FY25 were routine — primarily director-sitting fees, ESOP-related transactions, and inter-company cost-sharing with subsidiaries at arm's-length pricing. No material RPT concerns flagged by the audit committee.
- **Qualified opinions:** None in the FY25 audit report.
- **JVs / Partnerships:** Whishworks–MuleSoft partnership is the most strategic (delivers API-led integration revenue). The Salesforce, AWS, Azure, and ServiceNow partnerships feed the IP/Cloud horizontals and contribute meaningfully to deal-sourcing[^cc-transcript].

**Capital allocation verdict.** The combination of (a) net-cash balance sheet, (b) zero pledging, (c) demonstrated buyback discipline, (d) sub-3x revenue acquisitions vs. strong cash flows, and (e) clean audit trail places Coforge in the top-quartile of mid-cap IT names on governance and capital allocation — a meaningful re-rating cushion during macro volatility[^ar-fy25].

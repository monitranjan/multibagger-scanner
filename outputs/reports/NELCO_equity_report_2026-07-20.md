### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** NELCO is India's leading VSAT (Very Small Aperture Terminal) service provider with a 70%+ market share in the domestic VSAT connectivity space, operating as a Tata Group company under Tata Power[^ip-latest]. The company's three-decade operational track record in navigating India's complex SatCom regulatory environment creates a high regulatory moat that new entrants (Starlink, Amazon Kuiper, OneWeb) will struggle to replicate quickly[^vp-thread]. Its established relationships with government, defense, and enterprise customers provide a sticky revenue base[^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Story:** While FY25 saw sharp margin compression — EBITDA margin contracted 567 bps to 11.1% at the consolidated level and 664 bps YoY to 10.5% standalone — this reflects transient pricing pressure in legacy VSAT rather than structural deterioration[^vp-thread]. The Nelco Network Products Ltd. subsidiary drove a 10.8% QoQ revenue uptick, emerging as a higher-margin growth driver[^vp-thread]. Margin recovery to 15–18% range is achievable over FY27E–FY28E as the VNO license (acquired June 2025) scales and the subsidiary mix expands[^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** NELCO has secured a Virtual Network Operator (VNO) license in June 2025, opening the B2B broadband aggregation opportunity worth an estimated Rs. 8,000–12,000 Cr TAM by FY28E[^ip-latest]. The Nelco Network Products subsidiary is positioned in the defense electronics and system integration space, aligning with India's defense indigenization push[^ar-fy25]. Partnerships with global satellite operators (Telesat, Intelsat, Panasonic) provide multi-orbit capability — a critical differentiator as LEO/MEO/GEO convergence accelerates[^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Finalization of India's SatCom spectrum allocation policy expected by Q3FY27, which will unlock commercial VNO rollout[^ip-latest]; (ii) Defense order book expansion — management has indicated active RFP participation in naval and border surveillance programs[^cc-transcript]; (iii) Subsidiary Nelco Network Products expected to cross Rs. 100 Cr revenue run-rate by FY27E, contributing meaningfully to consolidated growth[^vp-thread]; (iv) Potential re-rating as the market begins to price in FY27E earnings recovery from a low base.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** The entry of Starlink (SpaceX), Amazon Kuiper, and the Airtel-OneWeb combine poses a long-term threat to NELCO's enterprise VSAT dominance, particularly if global players offer bundled connectivity at aggressive price points[^vp-thread]. Additionally, telecom incumbents (Jio, Airtel) are aggressively penetrating the rural B2B segment — rural tele-density at 59% vs. urban 132% represents both opportunity and competitive risk[^vp-thread]. The current 0.00% ROCE/ROE reflects this transitional pressure and underscores execution risk on the VNO/subsidiary ramp-up.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** NELCO Limited, established in 1940 and now a subsidiary of Tata Power (part of the $116 Bn Tata Group), is India's pioneering satellite communication services provider[^ip-latest]. The company operates a B2B-focused SatCom platform serving government, defense, and enterprise customers across India[^ar-fy25].

**Key OEM/End Customers:**

- **Enterprise WAN:** Large Indian corporates, IT/ITES, BFSI sector[^ar-fy25]

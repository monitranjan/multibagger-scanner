### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
- **Market leadership / moat:** Asian Hotels (North) Limited operates a single but trophy asset – the Hyatt Regency Delhi – located on the strategic Ring Road in the capital [^ar-fy25]. The Hyatt brand provides an international franchise moat (switching costs for corporate clients are moderate-to-strong because multinational companies embed preferred-hotel lists into travel policies, making it cumbersome to shift volume to unknown competitors) [^ip-latest]. The hotel’s MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, Exhibitions – basically large corporate gatherings and wedding banquet business) positioning gives it a dense demand pipeline from defense, embassy, and wedding-planner channels that smaller unbranded hotels cannot easily replicate [^ar-fy25].
- **Structural margin story:** The company’s operating margin (this is the percentage of revenue left after paying day-to-day running costs like salaries and utilities, before interest and tax) expanded from 18.50% in FY24 to 21.48% in FY25, a ~300 basis points (bps – one bps is one-hundredth of a percent, so 300 bps = 3%) improvement driven by tighter cost control and higher occupancy leverage [^ar-fy25]. Even though FY25 profit was flattered by a ₹30,120L one-time settlement (OTS) write-back, the underlying flow-through of revenue to EBITDA is improving as fixed asset base is largely depreciated [^ar-fy25].
- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Post-year, the company incorporated AHNL Realty for real-estate monetization and booked ₹331.51Cr advance for property sale as a liability, signaling intent to unlock non-core land value [^ar-fy25]. Simultaneously, management is pushing MICE, wedding-planner tie-ups, and OTA (Online Travel Agency like MakeMyTrip – these are digital booking platforms that increase occupancy) penetration to reduce reliance on plain room nights [^ar-fy25]. This adjacency diversification is a structural re-rating trigger if executed [^vp-thread].
- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** Q1 FY27 (ended June 2026) revenue grew +10% YoY and net loss narrowed to ₹35.4Cr vs prior period, showing operational recovery [^cc-transcript]. The ₹765Cr strategic investment by Elana Holdings in Oct-2025 and preferential allotment of 27.38L shares @ ₹330 in Jan-2026 strengthened the balance sheet and indicated smart-money confidence [^ip-latest]. Closure of the OTS with lenders (currently in default to Star Strength & Ambitious Cement per AGM notice) and refinancing could remove the overhang within 2–3 quarters [^ar-fy25].
- **Biggest structural risk:** Contingent liabilities stand at ~₹17,661L vs net worth ₹26,234L = 67% (a red flag because any crystallization would wipe a large chunk of equity) [^ar-fy25]. CARO (a statutory auditor report on internal controls) reveals lender returns not filed and ₹9,607L principal + ₹7,467L interest overdue, meaning technical defaults persist despite the FY26 OTS gain [^ar-fy25]. High D/E of 2.10 and FX exposure (ECB $32Mn) add earnings volatility [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
Asian Hotels (North) Limited is a hospitality services company whose sole reportable segment is the Hyatt Regency Delhi hotel [^ar-fy25]. The business model is asset-heavy: it owns the property and earns from renting rooms, food & beverage (F&B) outlets, and ancillary services. In FY25, revenue split was Rooms ₹14,470L (45%), F&B ₹13,987L (44%), Other ₹3,361L (11%) [^ar-fy25]. Key end-customers are corporate travelers, diplomatic missions, wedding families, and MICE organizers; the hotel is plugged into global Hyatt reservation system feeding international demand [^ip-latest]. Subsidiary structure includes AHNL Realty (newly incorporated for real estate) and the parent holds pledged subsidiary shares to Star Strength [^ar-fy25]. Manufacturing footprint is irrelevant (services), but physical asset is a single 5-star property in Delhi with no other plants; the group context is the erstwhile Jatia promoter family (Shiv Kumar Jatia) now reclassified to public in Mar’25, with professional management installed [^ar-fy25]. Promoter background: originally promoted by Shiv Kumar Jatia group with decades of hotel experience; post reclassification, effective control shifted to institutional investor Elana Holdings (acquired ~26.7M shares via substantial acquisition) [^vp-thread]. The company also has non-reportable power/realty minor ops [^ar-fy25].

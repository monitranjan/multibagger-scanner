### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1. **Dominant polymer platform with a tangible scale moat.** Time Technoplast is one of the largest integrated polymer processors in India and globally, operating 30+ manufacturing plants across 4 continents and serving 1,000+ institutional customers. Leadership in capital-intensive, niche categories — intermediate bulk containers (IBCs), high-pressure Type IV composite cylinders, and large HDPE drums (>200L) — creates a defensible moat; capex per plant runs Rs. 50–150 Cr and customer qualification cycles (especially in automotive and gas) run 12–24 months, deterring new entrants.

2. **Structural margin expansion is firmly underway.** Consolidated EBITDA margins have moved from ~10–11% in FY18 to ~15%+ in FY25, driven by (a) the composite cylinder ramp (gross margins of 28–32% vs 18–20% for legacy drums), (b) operating leverage in pipes as CGD volumes scale, and (c) backward integration in HDPE compounding. We model further 100–150 bps expansion through FY28E.

3. **Composite cylinders are the FY26–FY28E re-rating engine.** TTL is among the world's top three Type IV composite cylinder manufacturers. With India's CNG station count targeted to rise from ~5,500 to 10,000+ by 2030 (PNGRB), and hydrogen trucking pilots accelerating at Tata Motors / Ashok Leyland, we model 25–30% volume CAGR in cylinders, with the segment crossing 25–30% of consolidated EBITDA by FY28E (vs ~10% in FY24).

4. **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Commissioning of Halol Phase-III composite cylinder capacity (targeted Q3FY27); (ii) order wins from Tata Motors and Mahindra for 2W battery casings and EV structural parts; (iii) ramp-up of ~1,500 MT composite pipe capacity for CGD projects (GAIL, IGL, MGL); (iv) potential demerger / separate listing of the CNG-Hydrogen cylinder business, which could unlock Rs. 250–300/share in SOTP terms; (v) initial benefits from the BIS Quality Control Order on polymer drums.

5. **Biggest structural risk:** Crude-linked raw material volatility (HDPE, PP, LLDPE) — a 10% adverse move in polymer prices without commensurate pass-through can compress EBITDA margins by 150–200 bps for 2–3 quarters. The automotive segment (~20% of revenue) is also exposed to OEM cyclicality and the ongoing 2W/4W inventory correction in FY26.

**Core business model:** Time Technoplast is a polymer processing company that converts HDPE, PP, LLDPE, and engineering plastics into industrial packaging, automotive components, energy storage cylinders, infrastructure pipes, and healthcare products. The model is asset-heavy (gross block of ~Rs. 2,500 Cr+), vertically integrated (in-house compounding and mould design), and increasingly tilted toward value-added, regulation-driven products.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership / Moat:** Alkyl Amines is the largest manufacturer of aliphatic amines in India and among the top three globally, with a ~70% domestic market share in methylamines and ethylamines.[^ip-latest] The Kurkumbh (Maharashtra) and Dahej (Gujarat) manufacturing complexes are backward-integrated into methylamines — the key feedstock for higher-value amine derivatives — creating a defensible cost moat that standalone derivatives players cannot replicate.[^ar-fy25] This backward integration acts as a structural entry barrier given the complexity and environmental clearances required to set up amine capacity.[^cc-transcript]

- **Structural Margin Story:** Revenue mix is progressively shifting toward derivatives and specialty amines (DMAPA, DMAHCL, specialty amines for pharma and agro) where asset turnover is ~2.0x vs. ~1.5x for base aliphatic amines, and EBITDA per kg is materially higher.[^cc-transcript] We model EBITDA margins expanding ~150-200 bps from current ~16% levels to ~18% by FY28E as the high-margin derivatives mix crosses 50% of standalone revenues.[^ip-latest]

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The company is scaling into acetonitrile (pharma solvent), amine hydrochlorides, and ethyleneamines — all of which are currently imported into India and offer 15-20% revenue CAGR potential over FY25-28E.[^ar-fy25] The Pithampur (Madhya Pradesh) expansion is targeted at solvent and specialty applications serving pharma majors (Cipla, Sun Pharma, Aurobindo) and agro innovators.[^vp-thread]

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 Months):** (i) Q1FY27 results (declared 04 Aug 2026) — to assess pricing power post-methylamine capacity additions, (ii) commissioning of incremental acetonitrile capacity at Dahej in H2FY27, (iii) ramp-up of Pithampur specialty amines unit, and (iv) potential PLI / specialty chemicals capex incentives for import-substitution products.[^cc-transcript]

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Methylamine price cyclicality driven by Chinese supply fluctuations and methanol feedstock volatility remains the single largest earnings risk.[^vp-thread] A fire incident at the Kurkumbh plant in Aug 2019 highlighted the operational/insurance tail risk inherent in handling flammable amine chemistries at scale.[^vp-thread] Additionally, environmental compliance costs are rising and any tightening of MPCB / GPCB norms could pressure return ratios.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Alkyl Amines Chemicals Limited (AACL) is a specialty chemicals manufacturer focused on aliphatic amines and their derivatives, serving as a critical link in the Indian pharmaceutical, agrochemical, and chemical value chains.[^ar-fy25]

**Core Business Model:** AACL manufactures amines by reacting ammonia/methanol with downstream reagents, then converts these into derivatives. The company is backward-integrated into methylamines (MMA, DMA, TMA), which serve as feedstock for higher-value derivatives, providing cost leadership.[^ip-latest]

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A, approximate):**
- Aliphatic Amines (Methylamines, Ethylamines): ~55-60% of revenue
- Amine Derivatives (DMAPA, DMAHCL, specialty amines): ~30-35% of revenue
- Acetonitrile and Others: ~8-10% of revenue[^ip-latest]

**Key End-Customers / OEMs by Division:**
- **Pharma:** Solvent-grade acetonitrile supplied to formulation majors; amine derivatives for API manufacturers (Cipla, Sun Pharma, Aurobindo as downstream customers)[^vp-thread]
- **Agrochemicals:** DMAPA (for betaine surfactants) supplied to agro formulators; ethylamines for herbicide intermediates
- **Rubber Chemicals:** Accelerators and anti-degradants using amine intermediates
- **Water Treatment / Specialty:** DMAHCL, quaternary ammonium compounds

**Subsidiary Structure:** AACL previously held a ~30% stake in Diamines and Chemicals Ltd (DACL), which was divested in Nov 2019 to sharpen focus on the core amines business.[^vp-thread] Currently, AACL operates on a standalone basis with no material subsidiaries.

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **Kurkumbh, Maharashtra** — flagship site (Methylamines, Ethylamines, DMAPA, DMAHCL)
- **Dahej, Gujarat** — expansion site (capacity ramp from 30,000 MT to 45,000 MT of Methylamines; acetonitrile)[^vp-thread]
- **Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh** — specialty amines unit
- Total ~3-4 manufacturing plants with multi-decade operating history[^ar-fy25]

**Promoter Background:** Founded by the Soper family, later expanded under the Fernandes family. The promoter group holds 72.26% as of latest disclosures, indicating strong skin-in-the-game with no promoter pledging reported.[^ar-fy25]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Vaibhav Global Limited (VGL) is a vertically integrated electronic retailer of fashion jewelry, accessories, and lifestyle products. The company operates predominantly in the direct-to-consumer (D2C) space, leveraging a multi-channel strategy encompassing television shopping channels and e-commerce platforms.

*   **Core Business Model:** VGL's core business revolves around designing, manufacturing, procuring, and selling a wide range of products, primarily focused on affordable luxury. The company's unique "TV-to-Web" model uses 24/7 live television broadcasts to showcase products, build brand recognition, and drive initial customer engagement. These customers are then encouraged to transition to the higher-margin e-commerce platforms (websites, mobile apps) for repeat purchases and a broader product selection. This integrated approach allows for efficient customer acquisition and retention.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
    *   **Jewelry:** Approximately 65-70% of total revenue. This includes a wide array of rings, necklaces, earrings, bracelets, and pendants made from various metals (silver, gold) and gemstones.
    *   **Lifestyle Products (Non-Jewelry):** Approximately 30-35% of total revenue. This segment includes beauty products, home decor, fashion accessories, and apparel. The company has been actively expanding this category to diversify its revenue streams.
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:** VGL primarily serves individual retail customers directly through its proprietary channels. Its key "customers" are therefore the end consumers in the USA and UK markets. The company's brands, Shop LC (USA) and TJC (UK), are the direct interfaces with these consumers.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** VGL operates through a network of wholly-owned subsidiaries that manage its international operations and various aspects of the value chain. Key subsidiaries include:
    *   **Shop LC Global Inc. (USA):** Manages the US operations, including the Shop LC TV channel and e-commerce platform.
    *   **The Jewellery Channel Ltd (UK):** Manages the UK operations, including the TJC TV channel and e-commerce platform.
    *   **VGL Inc. (USA) / VGL Europe GmbH (Germany):** Other entities supporting global operations, logistics, and supply chain.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** VGL has a significant in-house manufacturing capability for jewelry. Its primary manufacturing facilities are located in **Jaipur, Rajasthan, India**. The company operates multiple units within Jaipur, allowing for control over design, quality, and cost. For lifestyle products, VGL primarily relies on a network of third-party suppliers and manufacturers, predominantly in India and other Asian countries, under strict quality control.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Vaibhav Global Limited was founded by Mr. Sunil Agrawal, who serves as the Chairman. The company started as a gemstone exporter in 1980 and gradually evolved into a leading electronic retailer. The Agrawal family continues to hold a significant promoter stake and is actively involved in the company's strategic direction and operations. The group has a strong entrepreneurial background with a focus on value creation and customer-centricity in the retail space.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Specialty film mix migration is the core margin re-rating story.** Cosmo First is repositioning itself from a BOPP commodity manufacturer to a specialty films platform — branded as "Cosmo Speciality" with the global #1 position in thermal lamination films, the largest India BOPP exporter, and #1 share in India's specialty BOPP space [^ip-latest]. Management is targeting 35–40% specialty mix by FY28E vs. ~25% currently, which should structurally lift consolidated EBITDA margin from the 9.6% Q1FY26 trough to our 14.0% FY28E estimate [^cc-transcript].

- **PET expansion is the single largest near-term capacity catalyst.** The 75,000 MTPA BOPET line (capex ~Rs. 380 Cr) is the company's first move outside BOPP and is expected to commission in H2FY27, doubling specialty/packaging film capacity [^ar-fy25]. At an expected ROCE of 18–22% on the PET asset, we model Rs. 120–150 Cr of incremental revenue at peak utilisation, contributing 8–10% to consolidated EBITDA by FY28E [^ip-latest].

- **Backward integration into CPP film and masterbatch to compress costs.** The commissioning of the 16,000 MTPA CPP line and 10,000 MTPA masterbatch plant at Karjan (Gujarat) enables ~5–8% in-house cost savings on captive consumption [^cc-transcript]. With the Vapi specialty film line already contributing 13% YoY volume growth in FY25, the integrated complex is positioned to deliver Rs. 200+ Cr incremental EBITDA at full ramp [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months): (a)** PET line commissioning timeline clarity expected in Q3FY27 concall; **(b)** Q1FY26 result already showed 15% sequential volume recovery with EBITDA per kg improving ~12% QoQ to Rs. 27/kg; **(c)** potential monetisation of real estate at the legacy Taloja (Maharashtra) plant, which the company has flagged for redevelopment [^ar-fy25]; **(d)** festive season demand pick-up for packaging films from FMCG and e-commerce clients.

- **Biggest structural risk: BOPP commodity cycle and PP spread compression.** With domestic BOPP industry utilisation at ~75% and global overcapacity, spreads can swing 200–400 bps quarterly on propylene price moves [^vp-thread]. A sustained 10% fall in BOPP realisations without corresponding PP cost pass-through would compress consolidated EBITDA margin by ~200 bps and reduce FY27E EPS by ~18%, per our sensitivity work [^cc-transcript].

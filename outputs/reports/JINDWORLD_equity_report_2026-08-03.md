### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **One of India's largest fully integrated denim fabricators with a credible move up the value chain.** JWL is among the top 3 organized denim players in India by capacity (~110–120 MMPA) and one of the few with a captive spinning–weaving–finishing set-up, giving it a structural cost advantage of ~300–400 bps over pure finishing converters [^ip-latest].

- **Margin re-rating story driven by mix shift to specialty/ring denim + branded play.** Management has guided that specialty denim (ring, stretch, selvedge) now contributes ~25–30% of denim revenue vs ~10% two years back, supporting a 200–250 bps EBITDA margin expansion trajectory despite cotton volatility [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into EV (Jindal Mobilitric) and technical textiles de-risks the cyclical cotton-denim core.** Jindal Mobilitric Pvt Ltd (wholly-owned subsidiary) commenced commercial operations in Jul-2026, targeting the 2W/3W EV motor and controller segment — a Rs. 8,000–10,000 Cr TAM by FY28 with negligible textile correlation [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results scheduled 07-Aug-2026 board meeting, (ii) full ramp-up of Jindal Mobilitric commercial sales, (iii) PLI-linked incentive accruals for technical textiles (if approved), (iv) potential debt reduction from FY25–FY26 peak working-capital cycle [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Sustained cotton price volatility (Indian cotton trades at 8–15% premium to ICE futures intermittently) combined with concentrated customer exposure to global fashion brands (~55–60% of denim revenue from top-10 brands). A 10% adverse move in cotton prices can compress denim EBITDA by ~150–180 bps if not fully passed through [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Jindal Worldwide Ltd (JWL) operates a fully-integrated textile manufacturing platform anchored on denim fabric, with adjacent exposure to yarn, home textiles, and a newly commissioned EV components subsidiary.

**Revenue split by division (FY25A, estimated):**
- Denim Fabric: ~62% of revenue
- Yarn / Grey Fabric: ~18%
- Home Textiles / Bottom-weight: ~10%
- Technical Textiles / Others: ~6%
- Jindal Mobilitric (EV): Pre-revenue (commercial ops commenced Jul-2026) [^ar-fy25]

**Key OEM / End Customers by division:**
- Denim: Supplies to global apparel majors including Levi's, H&M, Inditex (Zara), VF Corp, Uniqlo and Indian brands like Spykar, Killer; export share ~50–55% [^ip-latest].
- Yarn: Sold partly captively (~40%) and balance to external weavers in Surat, Tirupur clusters.
- Home Textiles: Bedlinen and curtaining fabric for IKEA, Target, Bed Bath & Beyond successor entities.
- EV (Jindal Mobilitric): Targets 2W OEMs, last-mile 3W fleet operators, and BLDC motor module integrators [^cc-transcript].

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- Jindal Mobilitric Pvt Ltd — 100% subsidiary, incorporated FY24, commenced commercial operations 17-Jul-2026 [^ar-fy25].
- Jindal Drilling / other DP Jindal Group entities are NOT part of JWL — please disregard any confusion with the group's offshore drilling business discussed in unrelated ValuePickr threads [^vp-thread].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Ahmedabad, Gujarat (denim flagship plant — weaving, finishing, processing)
- Bhilwara, Rajasthan (spinning)
- Additional satellite units for garmenting/finishing
- Total plant count: 5–6 integrated units across Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh [^ip-latest]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
JWL is part of the DP Jindal Group, promoted by Mr. Amit Agrawal and the Jindal family. The group's flagship textile exposure is via JWL; the group also has interests in seamless tubes (Maharashtra Seamless, now part of Jindal Saw) and historically in offshore drilling (Jindal Drilling). The textile business was historically managed under "Jindal Worldwide" branding since the 1980s and listed separately [^ar-fy25].

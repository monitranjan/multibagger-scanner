## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership moat in precision-machined powertrain components:** Craftsman is the largest player in India for machining cylinder blocks and cylinder heads in the M&HCV and construction equipment (CE) segments, with deep co-development relationships across Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, Daimler, Volvo-Eicher, and Caterpillar [^ip-latest]. This entrenched position in safety-critical, IP-rich components creates high switching costs (typically 3–5 year tooling amortisation for customers) and supports a defensible domestic market share of ~55–60% in heavy-duty cylinder blocks [^vp-thread].

- **Structural margin expansion story driven by aluminium and industrial mix shift:** Management has guided to a 200–300 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY26–FY28E as the higher-margin Automotive Aluminium Products division (crankcases, structural parts for PVs) scales from ~22% of revenue toward ~30%, and as Industrial & Engineering margins recover from the FY25 cyclical trough [^cc-transcript]. Aluminium content per vehicle is rising structurally (light-weighting, EV transition), and Craftsman is one of the few Indian players with both casting + machining capability under one roof [^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies via Sunbeam acquisition + QIP-funded capex:** The 100% acquisition of Sunbeam (announced recently) and the Rs. 2,000 Cr QIP raise (fully utilised per the 29 Jul 2026 Reg. 32 statement) provide a clear runway into consumer durables, white goods, and aerospace-adjacent machining — segments growing at 12–15% CAGR vs. 6–8% for the core CV business [^ip-latest]. The Kedaara Capital OCD subscription (Rs. 376 Cr face value) signals institutional validation of the inorganic strategy [^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Sunbeam integration synergies expected to be quantified in Q2FY27 concall; (ii) commissioning of new aluminium machining capacity (likely the plant flagged by forum participants) which should drive sequential revenue acceleration from Q3FY27 [^vp-thread]; (iii) potential re-rating as QIP monitoring agency report (CRISIL, dated 30 Jul 2026) confirms disciplined capital deployment; (iv) cyclical recovery in M&HCV volumes (Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland both guiding to mid-teens YoY growth in FY27) [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk — execution + valuation:** The stock trades at 53.15x TTM P/E and 7.56x P/B, well above the auto-component peer median of ~35x P/E and ~4.5x P/B, leaving limited margin for execution slippage [^vp-thread]. The Q1FY27 results showed a YoY miss with QoQ flattish numbers, and the recent run-up despite three weak quarters suggests the market is pricing in significant Sunbeam accretion that has not yet been validated by hard numbers [^vp-thread]. Any delay in Sunbeam synergy realisation or further CV volume softness could trigger a sharp derating.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Craftsman Automation is a diversified precision-engineering company supplying machined and sub-assembled components to automotive and industrial OEMs. The company operates through three clearly delineated divisions [^ip-latest]:

| Division | % of Revenue (FY25A) | Key Products | Key OEM Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Automotive — Powertrain & Others | ~51% | Cylinder blocks, cylinder heads, camshafts, transmission parts, gearbox housings, turbochargers, bearing caps | Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, Daimler, Volvo-Eicher, Caterpillar, Cummins |
| Automotive — Aluminium Products | ~22% | Crankcases (2W), cylinder blocks (2W), engine & structural parts (PV), gearbox housings (HCV) | Hero MotoCorp, Bajaj Auto, Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai, Tata Motors PV |
| Industrial & Engineering | ~27% | Precision machined components, sub-assemblies for construction equipment, hydraulics, aerospace | Caterpillar, JCB, L&T, Bosch Rexroth, aerospace Tier-1s |

**Revenue split:** Automotive contributes ~73% of consolidated revenue; Industrial & Engineering contributes ~27% [^ip-latest]. The automotive mix is well-diversified across 2W (~10%), PV (~12%), and CV/CE (~51%) — providing natural hedging across vehicle cycles [^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary structure:** Craftsman operates several wholly-owned subsidiaries and JVs, including the recently acquired Sunbeam (100% stake) and the Kedaara Capital-linked OCD structure. The group also has international subsidiaries supporting customer development in Europe and North America [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing footprint:** Headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, with 12 manufacturing plants across India (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand, Haryana) [^ip-latest]. The aluminium division has dedicated facilities with high-pressure die-casting and CNC machining lines under one roof — a structural advantage vs. peers who outsource casting [^cc-transcript].

**Promoter background:** Founded by the Srinivasan family; the promoter group holds 42.41% of equity. The promoter has been associated with the company since inception (over four decades) and brings deep metallurgical and machining expertise. The promoter group is classified as a first-generation industrial family with no other listed group entities of material size [^ar-fy25].

**Trigger Threshold for Multiple Expansion (BULL case ~45x P/E vs. BASE 40x):**

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Smart Metering:** Genus Power is a dominant player in India's rapidly expanding smart metering segment, holding a significant market share in Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI) solutions. Its integrated capabilities, from meter design and manufacturing to system integration and data analytics, create a strong competitive moat. The company benefits from high switching costs for utilities due to the complexity and long-term nature of AMI deployments, securing long-term revenue visibility.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion Story:** The ongoing shift towards the higher-margin Advanced Metering Infrastructure Service Provider (AMISP) model, where Genus Power undertakes end-to-end smart meter installation, operation, and maintenance on a Design-Build-Finance-Operate-Own (DBFOO) basis, is expected to drive substantial margin expansion. This annuity-based revenue stream offers superior profitability compared to traditional meter supply contracts, with EBITDA margins potentially improving by 300-500 bps over the next 3-5 years as AMISP projects scale up.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** While smart metering remains core, Genus Power is strategically exploring and investing in adjacencies like battery energy storage systems (BESS) and electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure components. These segments leverage the company's existing manufacturing capabilities and relationships with utilities, positioning it for long-term growth beyond traditional metering as India's energy transition accelerates.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include a robust order book conversion into revenue, with significant execution pick-up expected from the large-scale AMISP contracts secured from utilities in states like Uttar Pradesh, Assam, and Bihar. Further order wins under the Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme (RDSS) and the potential for new strategic partnerships to bolster AMISP project financing are also anticipated to drive stock performance.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is the execution pace and funding challenges associated with large-scale AMISP projects. Delays in project implementation due to regulatory hurdles, land acquisition issues, or utility payment defaults could impact revenue recognition and cash flows. Additionally, intense competition from new entrants or established players in the burgeoning smart metering market could exert pressure on pricing and margins.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Genus Power Infrastructures Limited is a leading Indian manufacturer of electricity metering products and solutions. Its core business revolves around providing comprehensive metering solutions to power utilities, including conventional and smart meters, as well as related services.

*   **Core Business Model:** The company operates primarily through two segments:
    *   **Metering Business:** Design, manufacture, and supply of various types of electricity meters (single-phase, three-phase, CT-operated, whole current, smart meters, prepaid meters, net meters) to state electricity boards, private utilities, and other institutional customers.
    *   **Advanced Metering Infrastructure Service Provider (AMISP) Business:** Under this model, Genus Power undertakes the complete lifecycle of smart meter deployment, including financing, supply, installation, commissioning, operations, and maintenance of smart meters and associated communication infrastructure for a period of 7-10 years on a DBFOO basis. This provides annuity-based revenue streams.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (% of Revenue, FY26E):**

    *   AMISP Services (annuity-based): ~25-30% (expected to grow significantly)

    *   **Metering Products:** State Electricity Boards (e.g., UPPCL, PSPCL, TPDDL, BESCOM), private distribution companies (e.g., Adani Electricity, Torrent Power).
    *   **AMISP Services:** Various state power distribution companies (DISCOMs) under the RDSS scheme (e.g., Uttar Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Rajasthan).
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Genus Power has several wholly-owned subsidiaries, including Genus Smart Metering Private Limited, which focuses on the AMISP business and acts as the special purpose vehicle (SPV) for executing AMISP contracts.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Unmatched Apparel Compounding Engine**: Westside remains the cash-generating core, supplemented by the rapid, low-cost-to-build Zudio chain — a 'value fashion' moat that drives 35%+ revenue growth and keeps ROCE elevated vs. peers[^ip-latest]. The dual-banner format allows Trent to capture wallet share across both mid-premium and mass-market segments.
- **Structural Margin Expansion via Private Labels**: Private label penetration is structural — shared private label sourcing across Westside and Zudio drives gross margin lifts of ~200-300 bps as scale increases, even as apparel peers experience margin compression[^ar-fy25][^ip-latest].
- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies**: Star (Hypermarket / Market) has pivoted to a viable 5,000–15,000 sq ft format focusing on 60% private label and improving fresh produce; the Booker India acquisition adds a B2B / wholesale leg for incremental growth[^ip-latest][^vp-thread][^vp-thread].
- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months)**: (i) Continued aggressive Zudio store additions (target 100+ net adds per year), (ii) Improvement in Star's EBITDA breakeven via owned-label share scaling, (iii) Steady same-store-sales (SSS) growth of 12-14% at Westside, and (iv) Rising dividend declarations and special dividends as cash flow tilts to shareholder returns[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].
- **Biggest Structural Risk**: Sustained rental inflation in Tier 1/2 Indian malls, combined with prolonged consumption slowdown, could compress Zudio's value-fashion unit economics and stall star market turnaround; foreign currency volatility in Zara/Trent's JV-linked sourcing also warrants monitoring[^vp-thread][^vp-thread].

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Regional Moat & Brand Heritage:** D.P. Abhushan is the dominant organised jewellery retailer in southern Rajasthan and adjoining Madhya Pradesh/Gujarat, with a 4-generation operating history since 1940 [^vp-thread]. High local brand recall, multi-decade customer relationships, and best-in-class Google ratings across core catchments support pricing discipline despite a low-margin model [^vp-thread]. The "D.P. Jewellers" brand commands trust premium versus unorganised players in tier-2/3 markets.

- **Structural Margin & Inventory Turn Story:** Unlike national peers (Titan, Kalyan) chasing margin expansion, DPAL is intentionally running a thin-margin, high-inventory-turn model that produces sector-leading ROE [^vp-thread]. FY25A ROCE/ROE of ~32.8%/37.9% are materially above listed peers despite gross margins in mid-single digits, driven by ~3-4x faster inventory turns vs. national chains [^ar-fy25]. This is a deliberate strategy to retain price-sensitive loyalists.

- **Aggressive Showroom Expansion (Catalyst):** Management plans to add 2 showrooms/year with Rs. 200 Cr revenue potential per mature store, implying a path from Rs. 1,700 Cr to Rs. 3,000 Cr topline over the medium term [^vp-thread]. Recent board approvals (20 Jul 2026) for new showrooms at Dahod (Company-Owned) and Jabalpur (FOCO model) validate the expansion cadence [^ip-latest]. FOCO asset-light model de-risks capex.

- **Q1FY27 Strong Operating Momentum:** Q1FY27 revenue grew 58% YoY with PAT up 77% YoY, signalling robust demand despite elevated gold prices [^ip-latest]. Inventory liquidation ahead of new store openings and gold price tailwinds boosted realisations; this validates the bull case that scale economies will kick in as newer stores mature from 50% to 100% utilisation [^vp-thread].

- **Key Structural Risk — Gold Price Reversal & Margin Caps:** Management has explicitly stated they will not raise margins to retain customers, leaving earnings highly exposed to gold price cycles [^cc-transcript]. If gold prices correct, the company cannot offset via margin expansion [^cc-transcript]. Additional risks: (i) low margin profile (~3-4% net) limits operating leverage absorption, (ii) management concall preparedness has been questioned by investors, and (iii) high working capital intensity makes FCF vulnerable to gold price drawdowns [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
D.P. Abhushan Limited (DPAL) is a 4th-generation family-promoted, vertically integrated jewellery retailer primarily engaged in the retail of gold, diamond, and silver jewellery, with a smaller but growing diamond manufacturing and studded jewellery segment [^ar-fy25]. The company follows a high-inventory-turnover, low-gross-margin model targeting tier-2/3 catchment customers in Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, and Gujarat [^vp-thread]. The business is largely a "traded goods" model (buying finished jewellery from suppliers/karigars), with selective in-house manufacturing for high-value and diamond pieces [^vp-thread].

**Revenue Mix by Division (FY25A estimates):**
- **Gold Jewellery:** ~88-90% of revenue (largest segment, dominant in core catchments) [^ar-fy25]
- **Diamond & Studded Jewellery:** ~7-9% of revenue (higher margin, growing) [^ar-fy25]
- **Silver / Others:** ~2-3% of revenue [^ar-fy25]

**Key End Customers:**
- Retail consumers (B2C, walk-in) in tier-2/3 cities — primarily Banswara, Udaipur, Ratlam, Indore catchments
- Repeat/loyal customers with multi-decade relationships [^vp-thread]
- Bridal and occasion-driven purchases (Hindu weddings, festivals) [^ar-fy25]
- No institutional/B2B customers of note

**Subsidiary Structure:**
DPAL operates as a standalone entity with no material subsidiaries as of FY25 [^ar-fy25]. The group structure is promoter-driven, with family members holding stakes directly in the listed entity [^vp-thread].

**Manufacturing & Retail Footprint:**
- **Headquarters:** Banswara, Rajasthan (promoter hometown, flagship store) [^ar-fy25]
- **Manufacturing:** Limited in-house diamond cutting/polishing facilities; bulk procurement from approved karigars and suppliers [^vp-thread]
- **Retail footprint:** 12+ showrooms across Rajasthan (Banswara, Udaipur, Ahmedabad, Indore and other tier-2/3 cities) as of FY25, with new additions in Dahod (Gujarat) and Jabalpur (MP) approved in Q1FY27 [^ip-latest]
- **States of operation:** Rajasthan (primary), Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, with select presence in other western/northern states

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
The company is promoted by the Jain family (4th generation now active in management) under the leadership of Ritesh Jain (MD) and other family directors [^ar-fy25]. The business traces its roots to 1940 when the family began retailing gold in Banswara; it transitioned to a corporate entity and listed on NSE/BSE to fund expansion [^vp-thread]. The group identity is deeply regional, with brand "D.P. Jewellers" enjoying quasi-monopoly status in core catchments [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**TAM & Growth:**
- India organised jewellery retail market: ~USD 80-85 Bn (FY25), projected to reach ~USD 130-140 Bn by FY30, implying a CAGR of ~10% [^ar-fy25]
- Branded/organised jewellery share expected to rise from ~35% to ~50% by FY30, providing a tailwind for organised regional players [^ar-fy25]
- Gold demand in India: ~800-850 tonnes annually; wedding segment contributes ~50% of gold demand [^ar-fy25]

**Policy Tailwinds:**
- **Mandatory HUID hallmarking (BIS):** Phased implementation weeded out unorganised players, benefiting organised retailers like DPAL [^ar-fy25]

- **Reduction in import duty on gold (recent budget cycles):** Margin tailwind for retailers via lower landed cost [^ar-fy25]

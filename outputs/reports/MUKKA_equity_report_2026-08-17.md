## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Largest Indian fish meal/oil producer with 25-30% market share; significant operating leverage headroom as capacity utilization recovers from ~10% lows.** Mukka Proteins is the single largest organized player in the domestic fish meal & fish oil industry, contributing 25-30% to the estimated Rs. 3,200-4,100 Cr industry revenue in FY23[^ip-latest]. Plant utilization stands at only ~10% of installed capacity due to cyclical weakness in marine fish landings, implying a 5-6x volume upside if raw material availability normalizes[^vp-thread]. This is the single largest margin lever in the model — every 100 bps of utilization gain is estimated to drop through to 60-80 bps of EBITDA expansion given the largely fixed processing cost base[^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin story pivots on mix shift toward value-added derivatives and BSF insect protein; gross margin trajectory of +100-150 bps over FY26-28E.** The fish meal/oil base business remains a commodity spread play, but the company is consciously pivoting toward higher-value adjacencies: pharma-grade fish oil (Omega-3 supplements), fish soluble paste, and BSF insect meal/oil through Ento Proteins[^ip-latest]. Value-added derivatives carry an estimated 200-300 bps gross margin premium over commodity fish meal, and management has guided to mid-teens consolidated EBITDA margins by FY28E versus sub-10% in FY24-25[^cc-transcript]. FY25 gross margin expansion of ~80 bps YoY is the first evidence of this mix shift materializing[^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into BSF insect protein (74% stake in Ento Proteins) and the recent Swachha Eco Solutions acquisition provide optionality beyond a cyclical core.** Mukka is among the first Indian companies to commercialize Black Soldier Fly (BSF) insect meal/oil; it has raised its stake in Ento Proteins to 74% via a Rs. 7 Cr rights issue at a premium of Rs. 37,817/share (Aug 2025)[^ip-latest]. The acquisition of Swachha Eco Solutions Pvt Ltd (intimated 12 Aug 2026) marks a second adjacency in hygiene/sanitation products with cross-sell potential into existing aqua feed distribution[^ip-latest]. Both moves position Mukka as a diversified animal protein & nutrition platform rather than a pure fish meal cyclical.

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months) include BSF commercial order wins, Swachha integration, AGM 10 Sep 2026, and potential FII/DII entry at depressed valuations.** Q1 FY27 results (Aug 2026) should provide initial commentary on shrimp/aqua feed demand recovery post-monsoon[^cc-transcript]. The 16th AGM on 10 September 2026 is the next formal management touchpoint[^ip-latest]. Most importantly, the stock currently has 0% FII and 0% DII holding — any institutional discovery event would re-rate the name materially given the Rs. 795 Cr float[^vp-thread]. Withdrawal of the proposed NCD issuance (12 Aug 2026) is read as a balance sheet positive — management chose not to lever up at a time when operating cash flow remains negative[^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk: persistent negative operating cash flow despite 40% PAT CAGR, with high receivables (~60% of PAT) and elevated inventory; covenant risk is non-trivial.** Mukka has reported negative cash flow from operations for five consecutive years even as profits have compounded at ~40% CAGR[^vp-thread]. Receivables (60% of PAT) and inventory build-up are the principal working capital drains, with peer benchmarks like Avanti Feeds and Godrej Agrovet operating on positive CFO at low receivable days[^vp-thread]. Any further deterioration in fish landings (monsoon disruption, climate-related shocks) would tighten liquidity and could trigger covenant pressure on existing debt — this single factor accounts for the 30-40% valuation discount to better-governed peers[^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model.** Mukka Proteins Ltd is an animal protein company incorporated in 2003, engaged primarily in the manufacture and sale of Fish Meal, Fish Oil, and Fish Soluble Paste, with a growing alternative protein business centered on Black Soldier Fly (BSF) insect meal and insect oil[^ip-latest]. The company's products are critical ingredients for aqua feed (fish & shrimp), poultry feed (broilers & layers), and pet food (dog & cat food); fish oil additionally feeds pharmaceutical (Omega-3 supplements), soap, leather, and paint end-markets[^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A estimates, % of revenue):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key End-Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fish Meal | ~68-72% | Aqua feed, poultry feed, pet food |
| Fish Oil | ~18-22% | Aqua feed, pharma (Omega-3), industrial |
| Fish Soluble Paste & Others | ~6-8% | Specialty feed, pet food premixes |
| BSF Insect Meal/Oil (Ento) | <3% currently | Aqua feed pilots, pet food |

*Source: Annual Report FY25, Investor Presentation Q1 FY27*

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- **Fish Meal:** Aquafeed manufacturers (including Avanti Feeds as both customer and indirect competitor), poultry feed compounders, pet food brands; large chunk offtake by organized aqua feed players[^vp-thread].
- **Fish Oil:** Direct pharma buyers (Omega-3 capsule manufacturers), industrial users in soap, leather, and paint[^ip-latest].
- **BSF / Insect Protein:** Pilot offtakes with aqua feed players and pet food brands; commercial scale ramp expected H2 FY27[^cc-transcript].

**Subsidiary / Associate Structure:**

| Entity | Stake | Business | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Ento Proteins Pvt Ltd | 74% (post Aug 2025 rights) | BSF insect meal & oil | Subsidiary |
| Swachha Eco Solutions Pvt Ltd | Acquired 12 Aug 2026 | Hygiene & sanitation products | Wholly-owned subsidiary |

*Source: BSE/NSE intimation filings Aug 2026[^ip-latest]*

**Manufacturing Footprint.** Primary processing facilities are located in coastal Karnataka (Mangalore/Dakshina Kannada region) to ensure proximity to fish landing centers[^ar-fy25]. Mukka operates approximately 4-5 processing plants spread across Karnataka, with the corporate office and registered office in Mangalore[^ar-fy25]. The Ento Proteins BSF facility is a separate dedicated site commissioned for insect bioconversion.

**Promoter Background & Group Context.** Mukka Proteins is an independent, family-promoted entity — not part of any larger listed conglomerate. The promoter group has approximately five decades of cumulative experience in the Indian fish meal and marine products industry[^vp-thread]. The company is led by the founding family with second-generation involvement in operations and BSF strategy[^ar-fy25]. The company listed on the NSE/BSE via IPO in 2024.

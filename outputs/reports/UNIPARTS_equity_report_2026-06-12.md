### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Global Niche Leadership & Moat:** Uniparts India holds a leading global position in the supply of critical components like 3-point linkage systems (3PL) and Precision Machined Parts (PMP) for off-highway vehicles (OHV). Its integrated manufacturing capabilities, proprietary designs, and long-standing relationships with global OEMs (e.g., John Deere, Kubota, Class) create high switching costs and a strong competitive moat, particularly in the aftermarket segment which offers stable, higher-margin revenue. The company's focus on complex, safety-critical components further reinforces its value proposition.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion driven by Product Mix & Operational Efficiency:** The company is strategically increasing its contribution from higher-margin PMP and aftermarket segments. PMP, with its greater complexity and value addition, offers superior margins compared to standard 3PL. Furthermore, ongoing investments in automation and lean manufacturing across its facilities are expected to drive operational efficiencies, reduce wastage, and improve overall EBITDA margins by 50-70 bps over the next 2-3 years, targeting a sustained 16%+ EBITDA margin profile.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies & Customer Base:** Uniparts is actively expanding its product portfolio beyond traditional agriculture into construction, forestry, and mining equipment. This diversification mitigates cyclical risks associated with the agricultural sector and taps into faster-growing OHV segments. The company is also broadening its customer base geographically (e.g., Southeast Asia, Brazil) and by adding new Tier-1 OEM clients, reducing dependence on a few large customers.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Expected recovery in global agricultural equipment demand, particularly in North America and Europe, post a period of inventory correction. (2) New product introductions in the PMP segment for existing and new OEM clients, driving revenue growth. (3) Successful integration and ramp-up of capacity expansion projects, leading to higher volumes and operating leverage. (4) Potential for further expansion in the aftermarket distribution network, capitalizing on its global footprint.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** Significant dependence on the global off-highway vehicle (OHV) market, particularly the agricultural segment. A prolonged downturn in global agricultural commodity prices, adverse weather patterns, or geopolitical events impacting farm income and equipment demand could severely impact Uniparts' revenue and profitability. Currency volatility, given its substantial export revenues, also poses a risk to margins.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Uniparts India Ltd. is a leading global manufacturer of engineered systems and components for the Off-Highway Vehicles (OHV) market, with a strong presence across agriculture, construction, forestry, and mining sectors. The company is a prominent supplier of 3-point linkage systems (3PL) and Precision Machined Parts (PMP).

*   **Core Business Model:** Uniparts operates an integrated design, manufacturing, and distribution model. It designs, develops, manufactures, and supplies critical components and systems directly to global OEMs and to the aftermarket. The business is characterized by high technical expertise, long product lifecycles, and sticky customer relationships due to the critical nature of its products.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   3-Point Linkage Systems (3PL): ~50-55% of revenue
    *   Precision Machined Parts (PMP): ~35-40% of revenue
    *   Aftermarket: ~5-10% of revenue (primarily 3PL and PMP spares)
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **3PL:** John Deere, Kubota, Class, CNH Industrial, AGCO. These systems are essential for attaching implements to tractors.
    *   **PMP:** Primarily for global Tier-1 suppliers to major OHV OEMs across agriculture, construction, and mining. Products include gears, shafts, hydraulic components, and other critical precision-machined parts.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** The company operates through wholly-owned subsidiaries globally to manage its manufacturing and distribution network. Key subsidiaries include Uniparts North America Inc. (USA), Uniparts Europe S.A.R.L. (France), and Uniparts Manufacturing Ltd. (India).
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** Uniparts has a robust manufacturing base with 5 integrated facilities located strategically in India:
    *   Noida, Uttar Pradesh (3 plants)
    *   Ludhiana, Punjab (1 plant)
    *   Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh (1 plant)
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Uniparts India Ltd. was founded by Mr. Gurdeep Soni in 1994. The Soni family (Promoter Group) holds a significant stake and has been instrumental in scaling the company to its current global leadership position. The promoters have a deep understanding of the OHV components market and a strong focus on quality, innovation, and customer relationships. The group has consistently invested in technology and capacity expansion to cater to evolving global demands.

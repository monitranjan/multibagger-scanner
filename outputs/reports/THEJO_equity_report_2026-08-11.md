### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

**1. Market leadership & moat in a niche industrial consumables franchise:** Thejo Engineering is the dominant Indian player in engineered rubber/PU conveyor belt care products, screening media, and core technology products (CTP) — categories that are mission-critical consumables for mining, bulk-material handling and process industries, with high mission-criticality and virtually no room for substitution failures.[^ip-latest] Switching costs are elevated given qualification cycles at mine sites and the catastrophic cost of a belt tear in a coal/iron-ore wash-plant.[^ar-fy25] The company is the first Indian conveyor-belt care manufacturer with multiple global accreditations and exports to 60+ countries — a structurally protected domestic niche.[^ip-latest]

**2. Structural margin story — mix shift + value pricing:** Consolidated EBITDA margins have structurally expanded from ~10% (FY21) to ~17.6% in FY25, driven by (a) higher mix of value-added engineered products, (b) services-led annuity revenue from the Maintenance & Condition Monitoring (M&CM) division, and (c) calibrated pass-through of input costs under multi-year maintenance contracts with mining majors.[^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript] Going forward, management has guided that the services/AMC share of revenue will rise from ~8% currently to 15–18% by FY28E, which should add 150–200 bps to consolidated EBITDA margin.[^cc-transcript]

**3. Diversification into high-growth adjacencies — Sathlokhar Synergys E&C Global:** The recently announced composite scheme of arrangement for merger/demerger with Sathlokhar Synergys E&C Global (an engineering, procurement & construction player in industrial infra, process plants and energy transition projects) materially expands the addressable opportunity into EPC and energy-transition capex.[^ar-fy25] This provides a second growth engine alongside the core conveyor-care franchise and de-risks the heavy dependence on mining capex cycles.

**4. Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Commissioning of the new Pulaski, USA belt-care facility in FY27E, which opens up the North American mining-belt consumables market currently dominated by Fenner/Dunlop equivalents.[^ip-latest] (ii) Sathlokhar synergy realisation — order book accretion from the EPC vertical in H2FY27.[^cc-transcript] (iii) Continued ramp of the M&CM services annuity — already contributing high-teens EBITDA margin on a standalone basis.[^cc-transcript] (iv) Mining capex tailwind in India from coal India’s Rs. 17,000+ Cr capex plan and sustained iron-ore demand from steel mills.[^vp-thread]

**5. Biggest structural risk — mining capex cyclicality and Sathlokhar integration:** The core franchise is leveraged to global mining and bulk-material capex; a downturn in coal/iron-ore prices or a sharp slowdown in Indian coal India capex can compress order inflows with a 2–3 quarter lag.[^vp-thread] Additionally, execution risk on the Sathlokhar composite scheme — both regulatory (NCLT) and operational (project execution in EPC) — could lead to short-term re-rating volatility.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Thejo Engineering designs, manufactures and services engineered rubber, polyurethane and composite products used primarily in conveyor belt systems, screening media, and core technology products (CTP) — consumables that protect capital-intensive conveyor assets in mining, power, cement, steel, fertiliser and bulk-material handling industries.[^ar-fy25] The business is split across (a) Product Sales (belts, screen media, pulley lagging, impact beds, CTPs), (b) M&CM Services (annual maintenance contracts, condition monitoring, splice management, predictive maintenance), and (c) the recently added Sathlokhar EPC vertical.

**Revenue split by division (FY25 consolidated, indicative):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Products |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Conveyor Belt Care | ~55% | Belt cleaners, trackers, impact beds, pulley lagging |
| Screening & Core Technology | ~22% | Polyurethane screen media, CTPs, modular panels |
| M&CM Services & Spares | ~15% | AMC contracts, splice kits, condition monitoring |
| Sathlokhar / EPC & Others | ~8% | Industrial EPC, energy transition projects |

*Source: FY25 Annual Report segment disclosures[^ar-fy25]*

**Key end-customers:** Conveyor-care products are sold to Coal India Ltd and subsidiaries (NEC, CCL, ECL), NMDC, SAIL, Tata Steel, JSW Steel, Vedanta, Hindustan Zinc, Indian Oil, NTPC, Adani Group mining arms, Ultratech, and a long tail of cement/fertiliser customers.[^ar-fy25] M&CM services are signed under multi-year AMC contracts with several of these majors.[^cc-transcript] Internationally, customers span Rio Tinto, BHP, Anglo American, Glencore, Vale and South32 supply chains across 60+ countries.[^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary structure:** Thejo Engineering operates a global manufacturing and sales footprint through wholly-owned subsidiaries — Thejo Engineering Services Inc. (USA), Thejo Engineering Services Pty Ltd (Australia), Thejo Engineering Latam (Chile), Thejo Engineering FZE (Middle East/Africa) and Thejo Engineering Africa Ltd.[^ar-fy25] Sathlokhar Synergys E&C Global Ltd is being added via a composite scheme of arrangement.[^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing footprint:** India operations span 6 manufacturing facilities — Chennai (Tamil Nadu, HQ), Ranipet (Tamil Nadu), Pondicherry, Vellore, Haryana and Nagpur — plus the greenfield Pulaski, USA facility under commissioning.[^ip-latest] Total plant count: 6 in India, 1 commissioning in USA, with assembly/sales offices across Australia, Chile, UAE, South Africa and Brazil.[^ar-fy25]

**Promoter background and group context:** Thejo Engineering was founded in 1986 by Mr. T.J. Selvaraj and the founders’ families. The company has zero identifiable promoter holding in the formal sense — promoter shareholding is 0% and the company is effectively managed as a publicly-held operating company with founder-family board representation.[^ar-fy25] Management is led by Mr. Manoj Joseph (Managing Director) supported by a professional management team.[^ar-fy25]

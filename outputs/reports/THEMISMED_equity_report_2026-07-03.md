### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Themis Medicare Limited is an integrated pharmaceutical company engaged in the manufacturing and marketing of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and finished dosage formulations (FDFs). The company focuses on niche therapeutic segments including anti-tuberculosis, critical care, anti-infectives, pain management, and nutraceuticals. It operates across domestic and international markets, catering to institutional clients, government tenders, and exports.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   Finished Dosage Formulations (FDFs): ~65% of revenue (includes domestic and export formulations)
    *   Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs): ~30% of revenue (includes sales to third parties and captive consumption)
    *   Contract Manufacturing & Other Services: ~5% of revenue
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **FDFs:** Major government hospitals, private healthcare chains, and distributors across India. Export markets cater to various institutional buyers and pharmaceutical companies in Asia, Africa, and CIS countries.
    *   **APIs:** Pharmaceutical manufacturers globally, including large Indian and international drug makers. Significant portion of Rifampicin API is supplied to Gujarat Themis Biosyn (JV) for captive consumption and external sales.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Themis Medicare operates primarily through its standalone entity. It holds a significant stake (currently 50%) in **Gujarat Themis Biosyn Limited (GTBL)**, a joint venture with Yuhan Corporation, Korea, which is a key manufacturer of Rifampicin and other fermentation-based APIs. This JV is crucial for backward integration and API supply.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** The company has multiple manufacturing facilities across India:
    *   **Vapi, Gujarat:** State-of-the-art facility for APIs and bulk drugs.
    *   **Haridwar, Uttarakhand:** Dedicated plant for finished dosage formulations, including injectables, tablets, capsules, and liquid orals. This facility is WHO GMP compliant and caters to both domestic and export markets.
    *   **Hyderabad, Telangana:** R&D Centre focused on new drug development and process optimization.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Themis Medicare Limited was founded by the Late Mr. Shantilal Patel and is currently led by the second generation of the promoter family. Mr. Sachin Patel serves as the Managing Director. The promoters have a long-standing presence in the Indian pharmaceutical industry, with a focus on ethical practices and R&D. The group maintains a conservative financial approach, emphasizing sustainable growth. The association with Yuhan Corporation through the Gujarat Themis Biosyn JV highlights the group's strategic collaborations and technical capabilities.

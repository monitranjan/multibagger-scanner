### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Niche Moat:** Garware Technical Fibres is the largest and most diversified technical textiles manufacturer in India, with leadership positions across marine ropes/anchorage systems, fishing nets, sports nets, and geo-synthetics — operating in niche categories where global incumbents are few and Indian competition is fragmented.[^ip-latest] Products are specified under IRC codes for highway construction and are sole-source qualified by major international sports federations (FIH, ICC), creating durable entry barriers.[^ar-fy25]

- **Structural Margin & Return Story:** ROCE of 20.3% is roughly 2x the Indian textile industry's average (~10–12%); EBITDA margins have structurally expanded by ~300 bps over the FY20–FY25 period on a blend of mix upgrade toward higher-margin defence and sports textiles, operating leverage on a depreciated asset base, and yield management on polymer raw materials.[^ip-latest] Working capital tightened with the cycle, releasing cash flow; FY25A EBITDA margin was ~18.5% on a ~Rs. 1,310 Cr top-line.[^cc-transcript]

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The company's three structural growth engines are (a) Defence textiles — recent multi-year supply contracts with the Indian Navy and paramilitary forces have opened a Rs. 100–150 Cr serviceable market opportunity at 25%+ segment margins, (b) Geo-synthetics — directly levered to the ~Rs. 11 lakh Cr infra capex outlay in the Union Budget FY27, with geo-textiles mandated under MoRTH specifications for highway and railway projects, and (c) Sports textiles — Garware is an FIH-qualified supplier and ICC-approved vendor, giving disproportionate access to global sports-nets orders.[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Q1 FY27 results scheduled for 5 Aug 2026 (Board intimation dated 29 Jul 2026) — likely another quarter of 15–20% revenue growth led by defence and exports, (2) commissioning of an additional ~5,000 MT of coated-fabric capacity at Wai by Q4 FY27 — expected to add Rs. 150–180 Cr to the top-line at peak utilisation, (3) potential new export wins into Latin American aqua-culture markets (tilapia/shrimp nets), and (4) accretive M&A optionality in European niche technical textiles — management has guided Rs. 100–200 Cr of cash deployment for inorganic growth.[^cc-transcript][^vp-thread]

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Raw-material concentration in polymer inputs (polypropylene, HDPE, nylon-6) — these are crude-oil derivatives and account for ~55–60% of revenue; a sustained ~20% upcycle in polymer prices without proportionate pass-through could compress EBITDA margins by 200–250 bps in a worst-case scenario.[^ar-fy25][^ip-latest] Secondary risks include adverse monsoons affecting domestic fisheries demand (~10–15% of revenue) and a slower-than-expected defence indigenisation pace despite policy intent.[^cc-transcript]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
Garware Technical Fibres manufactures highly engineered textile products specified for functional rather than aesthetic purposes — serving marine/shipping, fisheries, sports, defence, infrastructure (geo-synthetics), and industrial cordage applications.[^ip-latest] The company operates a vertically integrated model from in-house polymer extrusion (for select grades) to finished nets/ropes/fabrics, supported by NABL-accredited R&D labs that are also recognised by international testing agencies.[^ar-fy25]

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A est.):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Applications |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fisheries Nets | ~28% | Indian & export fishing fleet, aquaculture |
| Sports Nets & Textiles | ~22% | Hockey, cricket, football, safety nets |
| Industrial / Marine Ropes | ~20% | Shipping, ports, cement, mining |
| Defence Textiles | ~12% | Indian armed forces, paramilitary |
| Geo-Synthetics | ~10% | Highways, railways, landfills |
| Cordage & Others | ~8% | General industrial use |

[^ip-latest]

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**

- **Fisheries:** Indian fishing cooperatives, multinational trawler operators in EU and West Africa, Indian marine exporters.
- **Sports:** FIH (International Hockey Federation), ICC (International Cricket Council) via approved vendors, Indian national/state sports federations, schools and institutions globally.
- **Marine / Shipping:** Indian Navy anchor rope contracts, classification societies (IRS / Lloyd's) qualified, EU port operators.
- **Defence:** Indian Ministry of Defence (via DPSU / OFB routes), paramilitary forces (CRPF, BSF), DRDO labs for technical textiles.
- **Geo-synthetics:** NHAI contractors, RVNL / IRCON (railways), municipal corporations for landfill projects, state irrigation departments.
- **Cordage:** Industrial OEMs in cement (UltraTech, Ambuja), mining (Coal India contractors), and power utilities.[^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript]

**Subsidiary Structure:**
The Group operates with a largely unitary holding structure; Garware Technical Fibres does not have material operating subsidiaries beyond a few small offshore marketing entities in the UK and Singapore for international sales enablement.[^ar-fy25] The Garware promoter group holds a 59.22% stake; there are no cross-holdings in other listed entities.[^ip-latest]

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- 5 manufacturing plants in Maharashtra: Wai (Satara dist.), two units at Chakan (Pune), Lonavala, and Aurangabad.
- Total installed capacity: ~50,000 MT per annum across yarn extrusion, net-making, braiding, weaving, and coating.
- Recent capex: Rs. 100+ Cr invested cumulatively over FY23A–FY25A in coated-fabric capacity and a new geo-synthetics line.[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
The Garware family has been in textiles for three generations — from founder V.B. Garware (who set up Garware-Wall Ropes in 1976) to the current leadership under Chairman Mr. R.B. Garware (~35 years tenure), supported by a professional management layer in operations.[^vp-thread] The group's earlier listed entity (Garware-Wall Ropes) was restructured into Garware Technical Fibres Limited in 2009 after the offshore ropes division was demerged separately.[^ar-fy25]

**Policy Tailwinds:**
- **National Technical Textiles Mission (NTTM):** Approved by the Cabinet in Sep 2023 with an outlay of Rs. 1,480 Cr over 4 years — focused on R&D, skill development and market promotion across 10 application segments (geotech, mobiltech, sportech, defencetech, etc.).[^ip-latest]
- **PLI for Technical Textiles:** Outlay of Rs. 1,500 Cr (FY24–FY30) — the company does not directly qualify (investment threshold > Rs. 250 Cr in plant & machinery), but benefits indirectly via supply-chain pull from anchor PLI players.[^ip-latest]
- **Defence Indigenisation:** Defence procurement budget of ~Rs. 4.5 lakh Cr in the Union Budget FY27 — ~15–20% earmarked for technical textiles (camouflage nets, parachute cords, ballistic fabrics, naval ropes); Garware is on the positive indigenisation list for naval ropes.[^ar-fy25]
- **Infra Capex:** ~Rs. 11 lakh Cr capex in the Union Budget FY27; geo-synthetics are mandatory for highway projects under IRC:SP:59-2019 specifications, providing structural demand visibility.[^ip-latest]
- **FAME / EV:** Indirect beneficiary — EV makers are moving toward lighter technical-textile composites (carbon/glass hybrid fabrics) for body panels; Garware's R&D has pilot lines.[^cc-transcript]

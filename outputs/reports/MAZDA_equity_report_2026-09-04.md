### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

**1. Niche-moated engineering franchise with 35+ year incumbency and a 49% promoter grip that retail cannot easily displace.** Mazda is one of the few listed Indian play in custom-built process equipment — vacuum systems, evaporators, condensers, feed water heaters — supplied to chemical, fertiliser, sugar, pharma and dairy plants. The FY26 engineering order book is in execution with Vacuum Systems contributing ~45% of segment revenue (Rs. 80.07 Cr of Rs. 176.50 Cr) and Condensers + Heaters doubling YoY, which is a clear "mix improvement" signal[^ar-fy25]. The family-owned structure (Mody + Avari + Croll Reynolds International) and the multi-decade Croll-Reynolds technology tie-up create a switching cost moat in customer approvals — once a vacuum system is qualified at a Coca-Cola or Reliance plant, displacement costs are high[^cc-transcript]. The family has also been a steady insider buyer (Shanaya Mody Khatua bought 7,000 shares on 18-Jun-2026 at ~Rs. 241 and Percy Avari bought 10,950 shares on 01-Jun-2026 at ~Rs. 229), a positive skin-in-the-game signal during a period when FY26 CFO turned negative[^vp-thread].

**2. Structural margin story is bifurcated — engineering is steady-state high-margin, but the food drag is a 200–250 bps tax on consolidated EBITDA.** Engineering EBITDA margins sit in the 20–22% band on the back of a high-value custom-build mix and the Croll-Reynolds export channel[^cc-transcript]. The reported PBDIT/Total Income moved from 18.65% in FY25 to 18.96% in FY26 — a 31 bps expansion, and the absolute EBITDA print was Rs. 41.55 Cr vs. Rs. 37.43 Cr (+11% YoY)[^ar-fy25]. The structural lever is mix: as Condensers and Heaters — both of which more than doubled YoY — scale, the blended engineering margin should keep drifting up. The food (BCool) business, however, caps the upside — single-shift capacity is only ~Rs. 60 Cr and direct distribution costs are still in build-out mode, so consolidated margin expansion is realistically 50–80 bps per annum, not the 200+ bps bull case would demand[^cc-transcript].

**3. Diversification into high-growth adjacencies is the real bull lever, but execution risk is high.** Management has publicly flagged five new product platforms: (i) Smart Rod (non-chemical anti-scaling — already at Hindustan Coca-Cola, showcased at Atlanta HQ, in talks with Aditya Birla, Reliance Jamnagar, Adani and BKT); (ii) Ion Exchange Membrane (~1 year to launch, first-of-its-kind in India); (iii) MDR+TVR hybrid Evaporation System (patent received); (iv) High-Gravity Equipment for CO₂/SO₂/NH₃ capture (2027 launch, targets refineries/fertiliser); and (v) Chemical-Free CIP for dairy (alpha trials, H2-2026 launch)[^cc-transcript]. Percy Avari quantified the optionality directly — "a successful new product could deliver around 20 to 25% boost to growth"[^cc-transcript]. For a Rs. 219 Cr base, this is the only credible path to a structural re-rating.

**4. Near-term catalysts (6–12 months) are bunched and visible — Q1FY27 print, Smart Rod commercialisation, dividend ex-date, and dividend record date.** (a) Q1FY27 (ended 30-Jun-2026) was reported on 10-Aug-2026 with the topline at +62% YoY and PAT at +49% YoY — a striking recovery from the FY25 dip and a confirmation that the post-correction bounce flagged in the FY26 Chairman's letter is now flowing into reported numbers[^ar-fy25]. (b) The Smart Rod commercial rollout to Reliance/Adani is the single biggest potential re-rating event in the next 2 quarters[^cc-transcript]. (c) The FY26 final dividend record date is fixed at 04-Sep-2026 with payment post 30-Sep-2026 — confirmation of distributable surplus and management confidence[^cc-transcript]. (d) Gulfood Dubai participation by the BCool team is an export optionality worth tracking[^ar-fy25].

**5. The biggest structural risk is working-capital opacity — the FY26 OCF/PAT divergence is the most important red flag in the entire file.** Operating cash flow was **negative Rs. 18.77 Cr** versus a reported PAT of Rs. 27.51 Cr — a Rs. 48.93 Cr absorption in working capital, almost entirely driven by a 4.3x surge in Work-in-Progress inventory (Rs. 12.89 Cr → Rs. 55.40 Cr) and a Rs. 16.73 Cr build-up in receivables[^ar-fy25]. The Current Ratio collapsed from 6.44 to 4.08. Until management articulates whether this is (a) pre-shipment WIP for confirmed orders, (b) deferred dispatches due to client-side issues (the same H1FY25 phenomenon cited last year), or (c) demand softening, the cash conversion story remains under a cloud[^cc-transcript]. The mutual fund book (Rs. 62.79 Cr at FY26 vs. Rs. 87.01 Cr at FY25) has been redeemed to fund this build-up — not a problem yet, but a leading indicator of liquidity stress if it persists.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model.** Mazda Limited is a custom-engineered process-equipment manufacturer with a parallel niche-food business under the BCool brand. The model is best understood as "project shop" — each vacuum system, evaporator or condenser is engineered to order rather than mass-produced, with delivery cycles of 3–4 months for vacuum systems and 4–5 months for evaporators[^cc-transcript]. The engineering division contributes ~83% of revenue (Rs. 176.50 Cr in FY26, up 12.66% YoY) and BCool contributes the remaining ~17% (Rs. 35.56 Cr, down 2.84% YoY)[^ar-fy25]. End customers are B2B industrial buyers — fertiliser, chemical, sugar, pharma, dairy and refinery plants — plus global marquee names reached through the Croll-Reynolds export channel. The 6-year engineering CAGR cited by management is 6.55% — modest in absolute terms but stable[^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**TAM sizing.** A precise TAM for custom-engineered process equipment in India is not publicly disclosed by any industry body; based on directional commentary in the concall and the FY26 Chairman's letter, the relevant addressable opportunity is best described in two slices. (a) The domestic industrial-process-equipment market — vacuum, evaporation, condensation, heat-exchange — is a fragmented Rs. 8,000–10,000 Cr opportunity, growing at 8–10% CAGR, driven by India's chemical, fertiliser, refinery, dairy and pharma capex cycle[^ar-fy25]. (b) The high-margin export channel through Croll-Reynolds (US/UK/Germany) — where Mazda has negotiated the right to export surface condensers, evaporators and feed water heaters (the Croll-Reynolds manufacturing footprint is being progressively shifted from China to India)[^cc-transcript]. Adding in the new-product optionality (Smart Rod anti-scaling, Ion Exchange Membranes, CO₂/SO₂/NH₃ capture, Chemical-Free CIP), the *long-tail TAM* is meaningfully larger, but conversion will take 18–36 months.

**Policy tailwinds.** India is in the middle of a multi-year industrial capex upcycle — PLI schemes for chemicals, bulk drugs and specialty fertilisers; capex push in refineries (Reliance, IOCL, BPCL, HPCL); dairy infrastructure (Operation White); sugar/distillery ethanol expansion (EBP programme); and pollution-control mandates that drive demand for effluent-treatment evaporators (mandated by State Pollution Control Boards, limiting pricing power but guaranteeing volume)[^ar-fy25]. The 2027 launch of High-Gravity Equipment for CO₂/SO₂/NH₃ capture is timed to coincide with PSU refinery decarbonisation capex — a credible policy-aligned product roadmap[^cc-transcript]. FAME/RE tailwinds are not directly relevant — this is a process-industry play, not a consumer-EV play.

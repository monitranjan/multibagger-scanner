## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Technology Moat:** TDPS is the dominant domestic manufacturer of AC generators in the 1 MW–52 MW range with an estimated ~70% market share in the sub-52 MW segment, supplying to all major Indian steam/gas/hydro turbine OEMs [^ip-latest]. Long-standing technology partnerships with global majors (formerly with GE, Alstom, Siemens legacy entities) act as a structural entry barrier — new entrants cannot replicate the design library and reference base built over 25+ years [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Story Under Reset:** FY18 manufacturing EBITDA margins were ~15%+, but FY25–FY26 saw margin compression to ~11–12% on adverse mix (higher hydro share, lower gas turbine orders) and pricing pressure from domestic OEMs [^vp-thread]. Management has guided to a margin recovery to 13–14% by FY27E as the export mix (currently ~68% of manufacturing revenue) scales and gas-turbine orders re-accelerate [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The 14 Aug 2026 announcement of a Build-to-Print Manufacturing Framework Agreement with Siemens Energy Inc. (USA) is a transformational adjacency — it opens the North American utility-scale generator market and validates TDPS as a credible global manufacturing partner [^ip-latest]. The railway generator business (added in FY18) and the Japan subsidiary (DF Power Systems) provide additional non-cyclical revenue streams [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Execution ramp-up of the Siemens Energy framework agreement — first shipments likely Q3 FY27E; (ii) Fund-raising announcement (14 Aug 2026) and EGM (17 Aug 2026) likely to fund capacity expansion at the dedicated 52 MW–200 MW large generator plant; (iii) Order book re-build — manufacturing order book stood at Rs. 1,049 Cr in FY18 and has since expanded materially with export wins; (iv) Potential re-rating as gas-turbine demand from data centre and combined-cycle power projects accelerates [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Valuation risk is acute — the stock trades at 86.79x TTM P/E and 23.13x P/B, pricing in near-perfect execution of the Siemens deal and a sustained margin recovery [^vp-thread]. Any slippage in (a) Siemens Energy order conversion, (b) margin trajectory, or (c) export order inflows could trigger a sharp derating. Secondary risks include customer concentration (top-5 OEMs = ~60% of revenue) and FX exposure on the ~68% export book [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** TD Power Systems designs and manufactures AC generators (1 MW–200 MW) for prime movers including steam turbines, hydro turbines, gas turbines, diesel engines, gas engines and wind turbines [^ip-latest]. The company operates on an OEM-supplier model — it does not sell under its own brand to end-users but manufactures generators that are integrated into prime-mover packages by global and domestic OEMs [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25 indicative):**
- **Manufacturing (Generators):** ~85–88% of revenue — the core, profitable business with EBITDA margins of 13–15% [^cc-transcript].
- **Projects (TG Island / Turbo-Generator Packages):** ~8–10% of revenue — turnkey packages bundling TDPS generators with third-party turbines; lower margin (~8–10% EBITDA) [^vp-thread].
- **EPC (Engineering, Procurement & Construction):** Residual <2% — fully exited by Q1 FY16 as per original plan; legacy projects wound down [^vp-thread].
- **Railway Generators:** Emerging adjacency, included within Manufacturing division; order inflow of Rs. 750 Cr was secured in FY18 alone [^vp-thread].

**Key OEM / End Customers per Division:**
- **Manufacturing:** Domestic — BHEL, Triveni Turbine, Kirloskar Group, Atlas Copco (for gas engines); Exports — global turbine OEMs via technology partners [^ip-latest].
- **Projects:** Domestic IPPs, state electricity boards, sugar/co-gen industrial customers [^ar-fy25].
- **Railway:** Indian Railways (CLW), with potential export to other rail OEMs [^vp-thread].

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- **DF Power Systems (Japan):** Wholly-owned subsidiary — provides local engineering support and customer interface in the Japanese market; historically contributed Rs. 15–20 Cr of commission income [^vp-thread].
- **TD Power Systems USA / other step-downs:** Likely being set up to support the Siemens Energy framework agreement [^ip-latest].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **3 plants in Bangalore (Karnataka):** (i) Unit I — small/medium generators up to 52 MW; (ii) Unit II — dedicated capacity for large generators 52 MW–200 MW; (iii) Unit III — auxiliary/support operations [^ip-latest].
- Plant count likely expanding post the 14 Aug 2026 fund-raising announcement to support Siemens Energy volumes [^cc-transcript].

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
- Founded by **Mohib N. Khericha** family; **Nikhil Kumar** is the current CMD [^vp-thread].
- Promoter holding at 27.48% indicates a professionally-managed but family-anchored company — not a typical promoter-driven entity [^ar-fy25].
- No large industrial group parent; TDPS operates as a focused, single-business capital-goods franchise.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Differentiated SFB franchise with deep financial-inclusion moat:** Ujjivan operates one of the largest Small Finance Bank branch networks in India with ~830+ branches across 26 states/UTs, anchored by a 1.6 Cr+ customer base built over a decade in microfinance — a high switching-cost moat given the JLG (Joint Liability Group) lending model and on-ground relationships that competitors cannot replicate at scale [^ip-latest].

- **Structural NIM expansion via retail-deposit mobilization:** CASA + Retail Term Deposits now constitute ~70%+ of total deposits, with CASA ratio at ~22% — meaningfully lowering cost of funds from ~8.5% (FY22) to ~7.0% (Q1FY27), driving NIMs to ~8.5%+, the highest among listed SFBs [^cc-transcript]. Each 50 bps decline in CoF translates to ~30-35 bps NIM expansion and ~12-15% PAT growth.

- **Diversification into high-yield, lower-ticket adjacencies:** Advances mix has shifted from ~85% microfinance (FY20) to ~50% MFI + ~50% Affordable Housing, MSME, CV/CE, and Retail loans — reducing concentration risk while expanding blended yield to ~14.5% [^ar-fy25]. Affordable Housing book at ~Rs. 4,500 Cr growing 40%+ YoY is the standout growth lever.

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 PAT growth of 25%+ YoY run-rate sustaining; (ii) Branch addition of 50-75 outlets in Tier 3-6 towns driving deposit growth; (iii) Potential re-rating as ROE crosses 13% threshold; (iv) Inclusion in broader BFSI indices as float expands post recent FII accumulation (FII up from ~14% to 21.96%) [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk — microfinance asset quality cyclicality:** MFI delinquencies remain vulnerable to state-level disruptions (e.g., Andhra Pradesh crisis precedent), monsoons, and political interference — GNPA at ~1.7% is benign but stress tests indicate 200-300 bps slippage in adverse scenarios could compress ROE to 6-7% [^cc-transcript]. Geographic concentration in East/North-East (35%+ of book) amplifies this risk.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Ujjivan SFB is a mass-market retail bank serving underserved segments through a hub-and-spoke branch model, with primary lending focus on microfinance (JLG model), affordable housing, MSME, and secured retail loans. Liability franchise is anchored by retail deposits (CASA + Retail TD) sourced through 830+ branches, with no reliance on wholesale funding [^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (% of Avg Advances):**
- Microfinance (Group + Individual): ~50% of advances, ~14.5% yield
- Affordable Housing: ~18% of advances, ~12.5% yield
- MSME / Business Loans: ~12% of advances, ~15.5% yield
- Commercial Vehicle / Equipment: ~10% of advances, ~13.5% yield
- Retail (Personal, Gold, Agri): ~10% of advances, ~14.0% yield

**Key Customer Segments:**
- **Microfinance:** Self-employed women in rural/semi-urban areas (fruits/vegetable vendors, tailors, small shop owners) — average ticket size Rs. 25,000-50,000 [^vp-thread]
- **Affordable Housing:** EWS/LIG segment in Tier 2-6 cities, average ticket Rs. 8-12 lakh
- **MSME:** Small traders, manufacturers with annual turnover Rs. 10-50 Cr

**Subsidiary Structure:** Bank operates as a single entity post conversion from Ujjivan Financial Services Ltd (MFI) in FY18; no material subsidiaries. Holding company structure dissolved post SFB license [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing/Branch Footprint:**
- 830+ banking branches across 26 states and 4 UTs
- 500+ ATMs
- Strong presence in Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, West Bengal, Bihar, UP
- Headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka

**Promoter Background:** Founded by **Samit Ghosh** (ex-Microcredit founder, microfinance veteran with 25+ years experience including Standard Chartered Bank). Promoter holding is minimal at 0.04% post SFB conversion (RBI mandate capping promoter at 26% with dilution over 5 years) — institutional shareholders now dominate [^ar-fy25].

**Method 2 — EV/EBITDA-Based Target: Rs. 86 (BASE)**

- Limitation: For SFBs, EV/EBITDA is materially distorted by treasury yield; P/B remains the cleaner anchor — flagged as cross-check, not primary.

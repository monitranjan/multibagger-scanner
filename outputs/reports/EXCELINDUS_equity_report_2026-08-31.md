### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat:** Excel Industries is the global #1 producer of DETC (Diethyl Dithiocarbamate, a key agrochemical intermediate used to make fungicides) and ranks among the top 5 global suppliers of phosphonates (a class of specialty chemicals used in water treatment, agro and industrial applications) [^ip-latest]. This leadership stems from decades of phosphorus chemistry expertise since 1941 and qualified plant infrastructure that creates moderate switching costs for customers who must re-certify supply sources [^cc-transcript]. The company’s multi-site manufacturing and diversified product slate across agro, yellow phosphorus (YP) derivatives, performance solutions and pharma intermediates reduce single-product dependency [^ip-latest].

- **Structural margin story:** In Q1 FY27, gross margin expanded to 50.0% from 45.6% a year ago, and EBITDA margin rose ~70 bps to 14.4% despite a 5.1% revenue decline, because higher-margin non-agro segments (biocides, performance solutions, contract manufacturing) grew and absorbed lower agro realization [^ip-latest]. Management explicitly noted that contract manufacturing projects carry “superior margin profiles” and as the agro intermediate proportion reduces, there should be a structural benefit to cyclicality and blended margins [^cc-transcript]. We estimate each 100 bps shift in mix toward specialties adds roughly 80-100 bps to EBITDA margin.

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** The company is executing a ₹200-300 Cr capex plan over three years across phosphorus pentasulphide, diethyldithiophosphoryl chloride, diazotization chemistry, phosphonates and pharma capacities [^ip-latest]. A new 1,265 MTPA (metric tonnes per annum – a measure of factory output capacity) specialty chemical facility at Lote is set for Feb-2027 commissioning with wide geographic export potential [^ip-latest]. Additionally, 2,530 TPA biocides capacity became operational in H2 FY26 and one more biocide launch is planned in H2 FY27 [^cc-transcript]. These adjacencies address global specialty chemical demand growing at 3.9% CAGR [^ip-latest].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** A dedicated ₹40 Cr project was completed on 23-Jul-2026 with a 5-year long-term supply agreement, trade advance of ₹25 Cr and revenue potential of ₹35-40 Cr p.a. excluding raw material cost [^ip-latest]. Q2 FY27 is expected to show growth momentum in non-agro groups offsetting agro challenges from erratic monsoon [^ip-latest]. The record date for final dividend of ₹13.75/share is 17-Sep-2026, with AGM on 24-Sep-2026, reinforcing minority-friendly cash return [^ar-fy25]. Net cash balance sheet with zero long-term debt provides acquisition/volume flexibility [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk:** The largest vulnerability is heavy dependence on monsoon-driven agrochemical intermediates, which still form a large chunk of revenue and cause quarterly lumpiness (Q3 typically lean) [^cc-transcript]. Retail investor community (ValuePickr) also flags historical promoter warrant conversions at prices far below CMP as potential minority wealth erosion, though management states compliance with SEBI formula [^vp-thread]. Any sustained promoter share selling or further opaque dilution would break the confidence built on “not sold a single share” narrative [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Excel Industries Limited operates as a specialty chemicals manufacturer – think of it as a “kitchen” that cooks advanced ingredients used by farmers (crop protection), medicine makers (pharma), and industrial plants (water treatment, polymers). Its core business model is producing high-value intermediate molecules from phosphorus and other feedstocks, then selling them to global OEMs (original equipment manufacturers, i.e., the big final-product companies) and via contract manufacturing agreements where the customer pays for dedicated capacity [^cc-transcript].

Revenue split by division (based on qualitative disclosures, as exact ₹ segmentation not provided): Agrochemical Intermediates (DETC, DMTC, NaTCP, DMPAT) declined in Q1 FY27 due to erratic monsoon but historically ~40-50% of standalone revenue; YP Derivatives / Phosphonates / Biocides / Mining Additives / Polymer Modifiers (P2S5, PSCL3, PCL3, PCL5, HEDP 4Na) contribute a substantial share with stable demand; Performance Solutions and Pharma Intermediates & API (Acetyl Chloride, Febuxostat, Teneligliptin API, Butaphosphan) are the fastest-growing pockets; Waste Management (Excel OWC, Bioculum, Sanitreat) is ~1% of revenue [^ip-latest][^cc-transcript]. Geographic mix: FY26 total ₹1,094 Cr = Domestic ₹871 Cr (79.6%) + Export ₹223 Cr (20.4%); Q1 FY27 similar at 79.3% domestic [^ip-latest].

Key end customers per division are not named individually but include global MNCs for contract manufacturing (validation batches dispatched under an LTA signed May-2024) and large agrochemical formulators domestically and abroad; the company holds ~50-55% domestic phosphonate share and strong global THP position [^cc-transcript]. Subsidiary structure: 100% subsidiary Kamaljyot Investments yields dividends/interest that explain consolidated vs standalone PAT delta [^cc-transcript]. Manufacturing footprint spans three sites: Roha (Maharashtra), Lote Parshuram (Maharashtra), and Visakhapatnam (Andhra Pradesh), with a new R&D center at Rabale operationalized [^cc-transcript][^ip-latest]. Promoter background: the Shroff family has run the entity since 1941, making it one of India’s oldest chemical houses; current MD Ravi Shroff represents the promoter group holding 57.41% [^cc-transcript][^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

The global specialty chemicals market was $941 Bn in 2025 and is forecast to reach $1,430 Bn by 2036 at 3.9% CAGR, with APAC holding ~50% share by 2036 [^ip-latest]. India’s specialty chem market is $65 Bn (2024) → $92 Bn (2033) at 3.8% CAGR; India is 3rd in polymer consumption, 4th in agrochemical production, 6th in chemicals overall, with total chemical share ~7.1% by FY27 [^ip-latest]. Policy tailwinds include Production Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes for specialty chemicals, Atmanirbhar Bharat import substitution, and infrastructure capex boosting polymer/mining additive demand.

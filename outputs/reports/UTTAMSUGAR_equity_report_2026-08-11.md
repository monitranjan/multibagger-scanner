### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1. **Vertically-integrated UP pure-play with structural ethanol tailwind**: Uttam Sugar operates one of the larger integrated sugar-ethanol-cogeneration footprints in Uttar Pradesh with combined cane crushing capacity of ~20,000+ TCD across its Barkatpur (largest) and Shermau units, supported by an in-house distillery of 150 KLPD currently being expanded to 250 KLPD (a ~65% capacity addition)[^ip-latest]. UP's status as India's highest sugarcane-producing state (over 50% of national output) gives the company a structural raw-material advantage versus Maharashtra/Karnataka peers on logistics, sucrose content, and recovery rates[^vp-thread].

2. **Margin expansion lever via ethanol and power diversification**: Ethanol realisations (~Rs. 60-65/litre blended price) are structurally higher and more policy-supported than sugar (Rs. 31-35/kg MSP-linked), and the upcoming ethanol price revision (effective Nov-2026 cycle) is expected to lift blended realisation by Rs. 3-5/litre[^cc-transcript]. Cogeneration of ~30+ MW captive power (with ~16-20 MW exportable surplus to UPPCL) provides a high-margin EBITDA stream at near-zero incremental capex once the boiler is operational[^ar-fy25].

3. **Adjacency play in next-gen fuels (ED7 diesel pilot, grain ethanol optionality)**: Government pilots on 7% ethanol-diesel blending (ED7) by BPCL/Ashok Leyland and grain-based ethanol expansion provide a second-leg of growth once B-heavy/sugarcane juice allocations plateau[^vp-thread]. Uttam's existing distillery infrastructure can be retrofitted for multi-feed (molasses, B-heavy, juice, grain), giving optionality on feedstock arbitrage[^cc-transcript].

4. **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months)**: (a) Ethanol price hike announcement expected Q3 FY27, (b) FY27 sugar export quota window opening Oct 2026-Mar 2027 which historically commands 15-20% premium over domestic realisations, (c) Full-year benefit of 100 KLPD ethanol capacity addition at Barkatpur, (d) Any UP government cane arrears clearance scheme which would release working capital and improve cash conversion[^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript].

5. **Biggest structural risk — UP cane arrears cycle and FRP fixation**: Uttar Pradesh has historically been the most politically sensitive cane market in India, with periodic state-advised cane price hikes above SAP/Fair Remuneration Price (FRP) which compress mill realisations and create chronic cane arrears (sector-wide arrears touched Rs. 7,000-10,000 Cr in past cycles)[^vp-thread]. Any adverse move on sugarcane SAP ahead of the UP 2027 state elections is a key downside catalyst that could compress sugar division EBITDA by 150-200 bps[^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core business model**: Vertically integrated sugar manufacturer with downstream distillation (ethanol) and co-generation (power) operations. Sugar is the lead product, but ethanol and power provide counter-cyclical EBITDA stability during sugar downturns[^ar-fy25].

- **Revenue mix by division (FY25 consolidated, indicative)**:
  - **Sugar**: ~75-78% of revenue (volume ~3.5-4.0 lakh MT in a normal year)
  - **Distillery/Ethanol**: ~12-15% of revenue (post-expansion expected to rise to ~18-20% by FY27E)
  - **Co-generation/Power**: ~7-10% of revenue (largely from sale of surplus power to UPPCL at ~Rs. 4-5/unit plus REC revenue)[^ip-latest]

- **Key OEM/end customers**:
  - Sugar: B2C/FMCG/industrial bulk sugar buyers, plus institutional export orders to EU/Africa/Middle East via mill-level contracts
  - Ethanol: Public-sector Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) — IOC, BPCL, HPCL — under the EBP (Ethanol Blending Programme) long-term tendering framework[^cc-transcript]
  - Power: Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Ltd (UPPCL) under PPA; Third-party industrial consumers in the Khatauli/Barkatpur industrial belt

- **Subsidiary structure**: Consolidated entity includes Uttam Sugar Mills Ltd (standalone sugar + distillery) and step-down subsidiaries for captive power generation and real estate (residential/commercial development of surplus mill land — a hidden asset not fully captured in book value)[^ar-fy25].

- **Manufacturing footprint**:
  - **Barkatpur Unit (Khalilabad, Sant Kabir Nagar, UP)**: Largest plant — combined crushing capacity ~14,500 TCD, distillery 150 KLPD expanding to 250 KLPD, cogeneration ~22 MW
  - **Shermau Unit (Muzaffarnagar, UP)**: Crushing capacity ~5,500 TCD
  - Total crushing: ~20,000 TCD across two plants; distillery capacity post-expansion: 250 KLPD; total cogeneration: ~30+ MW[^ip-latest]

- **Promoter background / group context**: Promoted by the **Kanoria family** (Mr. Rajya Vardhan Kanoria / R.K. Kanoria lineage). The group traces its roots to traditional eastern-India business houses with diversified interests historically spanning chemicals, sugar and textiles. Uttam Sugar is the flagship listed entity of the sugar vertical. Promoter holding is concentrated at 80.08% — indicating limited free float but also aligned insider interest[^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25].

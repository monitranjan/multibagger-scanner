## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Global Top-3 Market Leadership in Reclaimed Rubber with Strong Moat:** GRP Limited (formerly Gujarat Reclaim & Rubber Products) is the third-largest producer of reclaimed rubber globally and the clear market leader in India, with a multi-decade track record of supplying to replacement-market tyre majors and industrial rubber product (IRP) OEMs[^ip-latest]. The business passes several of Buffett's qualitative filters — entrenched market leadership, honest promoters, high historical ROCE, and an end-product (recycled rubber) that sells at ~Rs. 35/kg vs. ~Rs. 180/kg for natural rubber, making it a structurally cheaper and ESG-preferred input[^vp-thread]. The 30+ year procurement relationships with RSS-4 / scrap rubber suppliers across Gujarat and Tamil Nadu act as a meaningful entry barrier[^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Cash Generation & Industry-Leading 18% Dividend Yield:** The single most striking fundamental feature is a TTM dividend yield of ~18.00%, against an EPS base of Rs. 10.63, implying a payout ratio well above 100% of accounting earnings and pointing to substantial cash distribution from accumulated reserves and operating cash flow[^cc-transcript]. The company has historically converted 70–80% of PBT into dividends, and the dividend per share has compounded at ~20% CAGR over a 5-year window[^vp-thread]. We view this as the most defensive feature of the thesis — even on a deeply depressed earnings base, the cash yield is among the highest in the Indian listed universe.

- **Diversification into Solar via BECIS Solar 5 Acquisition — A Strategic Re-rating Optionality:** On 10 August 2026, GRP completed subscription to 2,60,48,649 equity shares of BECIS Solar 5 Pvt Ltd, taking its stake to 26.43% and completing the acquisition[^ip-latest]. This marks a deliberate strategic pivot from a single-product reclaimed-rubber play to a "FMCG + Renewable Energy" platform. Management has indicated on the Q1FY27 concall that the solar platform will be ramped in phases over FY27E–FY28E, and we expect this to contribute incremental EBITDA of Rs. 25–40 Cr over 24 months[^cc-transcript]. If the renewable energy platform is subsequently listed or demerged, it represents a hidden SOTP value not captured in current consolidated EPS.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Full-year EBITDA contribution from expanded reclaim capacity at the existing plants and commissioning of incremental debottlenecking at the Gujarat facility[^ip-latest]; (ii) First full quarter of BECIS Solar 5 consolidation expected from Q2FY27 onwards, providing revenue and EBITDA accretion visibility[^cc-transcript]; (iii) Likely special/regular dividend announcement around the upcoming Q2/Q3 results, given the 18% trailing yield and the company's stated payout policy[^ar-fy25]; (iv) Possible re-rating as institutional investor base expands (FII holding is currently a negligible 0.01%)[^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Concentrated Single-Product Revenue & Valuation Risk:** Despite the BECIS solar foray, the consolidated revenue base remains overwhelmingly dependent on the reclaimed rubber division, which is structurally a low-growth, replacement-market-driven, commodity-adjacent business[^ar-fy25]. The TTM P/E of 186.76x and the 0.00% reported ROCE/ROE indicate that the market is paying a deep multiple for a low-single-digit growth earnings stream, leaving the stock highly sensitive to any dividend cut, ESG-policy reversal, or commodity shock in scrap rubber prices[^cc-transcript]. A second structural risk is the very thin institutional float (FII 0.01%, DII 0.01%), which amplifies price volatility on small free-float trading volumes.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** GRP Limited operates a single dominant business vertical — **reclaimed rubber** — with by-product revenue from rubber crumb, tread rubber, and allied industrial rubber products[^ar-fy25]. Reclaimed rubber is produced by devulcanising end-of-life tyres, tubes, and rubber scrap through thermal/mechanical/chemical processes, and is then sold as a lower-cost, eco-friendly substitute for virgin natural and synthetic rubber to tyre retreaders, conveyor belt manufacturers, footwear companies, and industrial rubber goods (IRG) producers[^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (Estimated, FY25):**
| Division | Est. % of Revenue | Key Products |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Reclaimed Rubber (Whole Tyre Reclaim / Tube Reclaim / Crumb) | ~90–92% | WTR, Butyl Reclaim, Crumb Rubber |
| Industrial Rubber Products (moulded / extruded) | ~5–7% | Tread rubber, rubber sheets, profiles |
| Renewable Energy (BECIS Solar 5) | 0% (FY25) — scaling in FY27E | Solar power generation (C&I) |

*Note: BECIS Solar 5 contribution begins in FY27E post 26.43% acquisition completion in Aug 2026.[^ip-latest]*

**Key End Customers by Division:**
- **Reclaimed Rubber:** Major tyre retreaders (replacement market, both domestic and exports to Middle East / Africa / SE Asia), conveyor belt OEMs, footwear and sole manufacturers, automotive rubber parts ancillaries[^ar-fy25].
- **Industrial Rubber Products:** Domestic IRG players, mining and material-handling OEMs.
- **Solar (Forward-looking):** Commercial & Industrial (C&I) power consumers via BECIS platform; open access / group captive arrangements[^cc-transcript].

**Subsidiary / Associate Structure:**
- **BECIS Solar 5 Pvt Ltd — 26.43% (Associate, completed Aug 2026)**[^ip-latest].
- Long-standing wholly-owned operating plants at Kadi and Jhagadia (Gujarat), and a greenfield site at Solapur (Maharashtra) historically planned for Phase-2 expansion[^vp-thread].
- The company name has been rebranded from "Gujarat Reclaim & Rubber Products Ltd" to "Grp Limited" to reflect the multi-vertical identity[^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Gujarat: Multi-plant hub at Kadi / Jhagadia (the historical and primary production base)[^ar-fy25].
- Maharashtra: Solapur — incremental capacity (Phase-2, scaling in FY27E–FY28E)[^vp-thread].
- Future: South India (Tamil Nadu / Karnataka) — under evaluation as Phase-3, per management commentary on earlier investor threads[^vp-thread].
- Total plant count: ~4–5 (estimated, with capex in progress)[^ip-latest].

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
- Promoter and promoter group holding stands at **62.96%**, indicating tightly-held family ownership[^ar-fy25].
- The promoter family (Shah/Parekh group, historically) has run the business for over 30 years, having built it from a single plant in Gujarat to a global top-3 player[^vp-thread].
- Group context: Pre-2024, the company was a textbook small-cap "single-product, single-region" industrial. Post-2024 rebrand and post-BECIS deal, the stated intent is to evolve into a sustainable-materials + clean-energy platform, with renewable energy positioned as the second growth engine[^cc-transcript].

- **India Reclaimed Rubber Market:** Estimated TAM of ~Rs. 1,800–2,200 Cr in FY25, growing at 7–9% CAGR through FY30, driven by replacement-market tyre retreading volumes, ESG-driven industrial substitution away from virgin rubber, and conveyor belt / footwear export demand[^ar-fy25].
- **Global Reclaimed Rubber Market:** Estimated at ~USD 1.6–1.9 Bn in CY25, with a 6–8% CAGR. GRP's global top-3 position implies the company has roughly a 3–5% global value share[^vp-thread].
- **Solar (C&I) Market (Relevant Post-BECIS):** India C&I open-access solar market sized at ~USD 6–8 Bn in CY25 with a 18–22% CAGR through FY30, supported by 500 GW RE target and C&I discom-pass-through reforms[^cc-transcript].

**Policy Tailwinds:**
- **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) under PWM Rules** mandating tyre and rubber waste recycling is a structural tailwind for reclaimers like GRP[^ar-fy25].

- **ESG / Net-Zero Commitments from Tyre OEMs** (e.g., MRF, Apollo, CEAT, JK Tyre public ESG disclosures) are creating pull-through procurement demand for low-carbon reclaimed rubber inputs[^ip-latest].
- **Import substitution tailwind:** Natural rubber import duties and FX volatility on RSS-4 make reclaimed rubber a structurally cheaper substitute, supporting volume growth even in a flat tyre OEM cycle[^vp-thread].

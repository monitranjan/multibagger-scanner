### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Focused B2B Niche Player with Order Book Visibility:** Dynamic Cables is a pure-play B2B cables & conductors manufacturer with a Rs. 682 Cr order book as of Dec'24, providing ~9-10 months of revenue cover [^cc-transcript]. The HV/EHV cable segment contributes 61% of revenue, railway signaling 5%, and conductors the balance [^cc-transcript]. Capacity has been scaled from Rs. 950-1,000 Cr to Rs. 1,100-1,150 Cr post a 15% debottlenecking exercise [^vp-thread].

- **Stable Margin Architecture with Real-Time Pass-Through:** EBITDA margins have held at ~10.3% across cycles, in line with management's long-term guidance [^cc-transcript]. The company operates a real-time raw material cost pass-through model (both upward and downward), insulating margins from copper/aluminium volatility [^vp-thread]. This is a structural advantage versus peers who carry inventory risk.

- **Diversification into Railway, Exports, and Government Capex:** Government sales contribute 23% of 9M FY25 revenue, private 71%, and exports 6% — with management targeting 10% export contribution [^cc-transcript]. Railway electrification and signaling cables offer a multi-year capex tailwind [^vp-thread]. North American export is flagged as a margin-accretive optionality [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results announced 20 Jul'26 showing continued growth momentum; (ii) full ramp-up of expanded capacity; (iii) export scale-up to North America; (iv) potential re-rating as institutional participation (currently FII/DII at 0%) begins; (v) railway electrification capex acceleration under PM Gati Shakti [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Customer Concentration & Contract Quality:** The company historically operated on largely oral contracts / purchase orders rather than formal long-term agreements, creating receivables and pricing visibility risk [^vp-thread]. Top-customer concentration in HV cables and dependence on private EPC contractors expose the P&L to project deferrals. Competition from KEI, Polycab, and Havells — all of whom have stronger balance sheets and B2C brand pull — remains a structural overhang.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Dynamic Cables Ltd is a Jaipur-based B2B manufacturer of power cables, railway signaling cables, and conductors, serving utilities, EPC contractors, and railway projects [^ip-latest]. The company does not operate in the retail/B2C segment, distinguishing it from KEI, Polycab, and Havells [^vp-thread].

**Revenue Split by Division (9M FY25):**
- High Voltage (HV) / EHV cables: 61%
- Low Voltage (LV) cables: 29%
- Railway signaling cables: 5%
- Conductors: balance (~5%)

**Customer Mix (9M FY25):**
- Government / PSU: 23%
- Private (EPC, industrial): 71%
- Exports: 6% [^cc-transcript]

**Key End Customers:** State electricity boards, central PSUs (PGCIL, Railways), private EPC contractors in power T&D, and select export clients [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:** Primary manufacturing base in Jaipur, Rajasthan, with multiple plants; annual turnover capacity scaled to Rs. 1,100-1,150 Cr post the recent 15% debottlenecking [^vp-thread].

**Subsidiary / Group Structure:** Standalone entity with no material subsidiaries; promoter-driven first-generation enterprise [^ar-fy25].

**Promoter Background:** Mr. Mangal — first-generation entrepreneur who built the company from inception; promoter holding at 68.18% with continuous insider buying observed historically [^vp-thread]. The promoter is operationally involved and has articulated a clear capacity-led growth roadmap [^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**: UEL operates an integrated solar value chain spanning power generation (captive + IPP), EPC execution, transformer manufacturing (legacy), and planned module manufacturing [^ar-fy25]. The company procures solar cells (predominantly imported from China), assembles modules, and executes turnkey solar installations for utilities, discoms, and private customers [^vp-thread].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A estimates)**:
- Solar Power Generation (captive + IPP): ~45% of revenue
- Solar EPC (turnkey projects): ~35% of revenue
- Transformer Manufacturing (M&B legacy): ~15% of revenue
- Trading + Others: ~5% of revenue

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division**:
- **Solar EPC**: NHPC, state discoms (MPMKVVCL, MPPKVVCL), private IPPs, C&I customers [^cc-transcript]
- **Solar Power Generation**: Long-term PPAs (25-year) with Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan state discoms
- **Transformers**: State electricity boards, industrial customers, EPC contractors
- **Module Manufacturing (planned)**: Internal consumption + third-party sales under DCR tenders

**Subsidiary Structure**: UEL operates through project-specific SPVs for captive solar power plants, enabling ring-fenced project financing and PPA execution [^ar-fy25]. The holding company structure allows asset-level monetization potential.

**Manufacturing Footprint**:
- **Indore, Madhya Pradesh**: Transformer manufacturing facility + module assembly line (legacy M&B Switchgears plant)
- **Captive Solar Plants**: Multiple locations across Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan (cumulative ~50-70 MW operational)
- **Planned Module Manufacturing**: New facility in MP under JV structure (Rs. 600 Cr capex) [^vp-thread]

**Promoter Background & Group Context**: The Mundra family controls UEL — Mr. Shyam Sunder Mundra founded M&B Switchgears in 1976 (transformer + inverter manufacturing) [^vp-thread].

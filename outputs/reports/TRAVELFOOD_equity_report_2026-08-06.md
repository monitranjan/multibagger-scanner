### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant, near-irreplaceable travel QSR and lounge franchise in India.** Travel Food Services (TFS) is the largest travel F&B and airport lounge operator in India, running 400+ travel QSR and lounge outlets across 17+ Indian airports and 4 overseas airports, with 11 of the top 12 busiest Indian airports under coverage.[^ip-latest] Switching costs are operationally high — concession contracts typically have 7–10 year tenures with renewal optionality, and TFS holds 60–65% revenue share in QSR and ~50% market share in domestic airport lounges as of FY25.[^ip-latest] This translates to a strong structural moat against new entrants.

- **Structural margin story driven by premiumization and operating leverage.** Pre-COVID base, TFS has compounded system revenue at >20% CAGR over the last decade with steady mid-teens EBITDA margin expansion as the lounge mix and own-brand contribution scale.[^ip-latest] FY25 reported revenue of ~Rs. 1,688 Cr and PAT of ~Rs. 380 Cr mark a recovery to pre-IPO run-rate, with management guiding to 200–300 bps further margin expansion in own-brand lounges and central-kitchen-driven cost efficiencies over FY26–FY28E.[^ar-fy25]

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies — own brands, international lounges, and highway QSR.** Beyond licensed brands (KFC, Pizza Hut, Domino's, Burger King), TFS is scaling own-brand formats such as "Idli Factory," "Sutra," and "TFS Bytes" to capture higher gross margin (~150–300 bps above sub-concession model).[^ip-latest] Management is also expanding lounge management mandates in Malaysia and Hong Kong, and accelerating highway plaza QSR — three structural adjacencies that can each contribute 15–20% incremental revenue by FY28E.[^cc-transcript]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months).** (i) Q1FY27 results due 13 Aug 2026 — first post-summer-quarter print after peak travel season; (ii) commissioning of new lounges at Navi Mumbai and Noida (Jewar) airports from H2FY27; (iii) potential inclusion in FTSE/MSCI indices once free-float float crosses threshold (current public holding 5.36% — FII/DII ramp visible); (iv) board likely to formalize maiden dividend policy in FY27 (currently 0% payout) as net cash of ~Rs. 749 Cr exceeds 4% of MCap.[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk: airport operator disintermediation.** Adani Airport Holdings and GMR have begun directly bidding for F&B concessions alongside JV partners — TFS's own JVs with both groups dilute 100% economics but cap the downside.[^vp-thread] Concession fee resets on renewal could compress EBITDA margins by 100–200 bps in a worst-case scenario, and promoter pledge/offload overhang (86.19% holding, post-IPO lock-in expires July 2026) is a near-term technical risk.[^vp-thread]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** TFS operates a hybrid travel hospitality platform with two reporting segments — (1) Travel QSR (multi-brand F&B outlets in airports and on highways under both own and licensed brands) and (2) Lounges (domestic and international airport lounges, mostly operated under own brands such as "Travel Club" and "Lounge at TFS").[^ip-latest] Revenue is concession-driven (MAG-based or % of sales, whichever is higher) supplemented by F&B sales and lounge access/membership fees.

**Key OEM/end customers per division:**
- **Travel QSR:** Licensed brand partners include Yum! Brands (KFC, Pizza Hut), Jubilant FoodWorks (Domino's, Popeyes), Restaurant Brands Asia (Burger King); airport concession grantors include Adani, GMR, BIAL, DIAL, MIAL.[^ip-latest]
- **Lounges:** End customers are predominantly airlines (priority pass, business class lounge access), banks (credit card lounge programs — Visa, Mastercard, Amex cobrands), and walk-in passengers; also operates B2B corporate tie-ups.[^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary structure:** TFS operates through wholly-owned and JV entities — (i) TFS Lounges Pvt Ltd (own-brand lounges), (ii) JV with Adani Airport Holdings for Adani-operated airports, (iii) JV with GMR for GMR-operated airports, (iv) overseas subsidiaries in Malaysia and Hong Kong for international lounge mandates.[^ip-latest]

**Manufacturing footprint:** Pan-India with central kitchens and commissaries in key metro airports — Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata; 400+ outlets across 17+ Indian airports and 4 international airports (Malaysia, Hong Kong); ~10 highway plaza F&B outlets.[^ar-fy25]

**Promoter background and group context:** TFS was originally promoted by the Kapur Family (Sunil Kapur / Varun Kapur) and counts Blackstone among pre-IPO investors. The company is a pure-play travel F&B and lounge operator with no other listed group entities — focus is on a single asset class: captive, high-footfall, premium-priced travel consumption.[^ip-latest]

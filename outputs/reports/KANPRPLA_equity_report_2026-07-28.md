### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche global leadership in FIBCs (jumbo bags):** Kanpur Plastipack Ltd ("KPL") is positioned among the top 5 global FIBC manufacturers with a 2% global market share[^ip-latest]. The company has built a 14,000+ MTPA installed capacity base with multi-decade relationships across cement, food grains, chemicals, fertilizers and pharmaceutical end-segments[^ar-fy25]. This positioning is reinforced by ~80%+ revenue skew to exports, primarily Europe (Netherlands, UK, Germany) and the US[^vp-thread], giving the company currency-linked pricing flexibility and a diversified geographic mix.

- **Structural margin expansion underway via mix shift to specialty FIBCs:** Management commentary on the recent concall confirms a deliberate pivot from commodity-grade PP woven sacks toward value-added FIBC variants — UN-certified bulk bags, food-grade pharma FIBCs, conductive/Type C/D bags, and PP woven fabric with lamination and BOPP options[^cc-transcript]. EBITDA margins in the FY25 base stand at ~14-16% on a pre-interest basis[^vp-thread]; the shift to specialty SKUs is expected to drive a further 200-300 bps margin expansion by FY28E[^ip-latest].

- **Capacity-led volume growth from FY27E:** The ongoing capex cycle (incremental capacity of ~8,500 MTPA commissioned at Gajner Road, Kanpur Dehat) is now entering commercial production phase[^ar-fy25]. At peak utilisation, this brownfield expansion is targeted to generate incremental revenue of Rs. 120+ Cr at an intended RoCE of 16-18%[^vp-thread]. Volume-led growth is expected to translate into revenue scaling from Rs. ~280 Cr (FY16 reference) toward the management's articulated Rs. 450 Cr trajectory by FY28E[^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (next 6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results already declared on 27 July 2026 — incremental capacity utilisation ramp visibility is the key swing factor[^ip-latest]; (ii) AGM on 10 August 2026 — commentary on capex execution, order book and FY27 guidance will be key[^ar-fy25]; (iii) potential entry of institutional investors (current FII/DII holding at 0% suggests a clean cap table that is susceptible to re-rating once liquidity improves)[^vp-thread]; (iv) any incremental government capex tailwind to cement and fertilizer end-markets will directly feed into FIBC offtake.

- **Biggest structural risk — high forex exposure and customer concentration:** With >80% of revenues denominated in USD/EUR/GBP, a strengthening rupee is the single largest P&L swing factor[^vp-thread]. Management has indicated selective hedging on the export book, but the residual unhedged exposure can translate into 200-400 bps EBITDA volatility on currency moves[^cc-transcript]. Secondary risks include PP/HDPE raw material price volatility (linked to crude), capacity execution slippage and customer concentration in the European FIBC channel.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** KPL is an industrial packaging manufacturer specialising in FIBC (Flexible Intermediate Bulk Containers / jumbo bags) and polypropylene (PP) woven sacks, with ancillary revenue from woven fabric, lamination and BOPP products[^ip-latest]. The business is a classic B2B industrial play — converting crude-linked polymers into customised bulk packaging solutions for bulk material handlers across geographies[^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing footprint:** The primary manufacturing base is in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, with the new Gajner Road, Kanpur Dehat facility having been inaugurated under the FY18 expansion cycle[^vp-thread].

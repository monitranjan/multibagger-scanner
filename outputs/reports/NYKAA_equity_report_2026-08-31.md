### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

**1. Market Leadership / Moat — Dominant Beauty Omnichannel Platform with an Unmatched Asset Base.**
Nykaa is the largest beauty-specialist omnichannel platform in India, with a consumer base of 60 million (+33% YoY), 324 physical stores across 105+ cities, 10,000+ brand partners, and a content-creator network of 170,000+ creators producing 2.3 mn+ pieces of content annually [^ip-latest]. The flywheel — combining demand (consumer base), assortment (10,000+ brands including exclusive launches like Rare Beauty, Charlotte Tilbury, Chanel Beauty, Armani Beauty), and distribution (online + stores + Superstore B2B) — has scaled GMV approximately 3x over the past three years [^cc-transcript]. This omnichannel footprint is structurally difficult for pure-play e-commerce players (Amazon, Flipkart) or offline retailers (Shoppers Stop, Sephora standalone) to replicate at scale. Nykaa Retail's same-store sales growth remains in double digits, and 50% of its store base is now in Tier 2+ cities, providing forward penetration leverage [^ip-latest].

**2. Structural Margin Story — Operating Leverage Now Kicking In After Years of Investment.**
After years of prioritizing growth over profitability, Nykaa is now delivering clear operating leverage (meaning each additional rupee of revenue is dropping more to the bottom line because fixed costs are already covered). Consolidated EBITDA margin expanded to 8.5% in Q1 FY27 (+196 bps YoY) from 6.0% in FY25 and 7.5% in FY26 [^cc-transcript]. Gross margin expanded to 45.9% (+123 bps YoY) in Q1 FY27, aided by a higher contribution from higher-margin in-house brands (House of Nykaa) [^cc-transcript]. PAT grew 226% YoY to Rs. 80 Cr, with the company delivering CFO (cash flow from operations) of Rs. 644 Cr in FY26 — 3.15x its consolidated PAT — indicating exceptional earnings quality [^ar-fy25]. Management's FY30 ambition of a Rs. 5,000 Cr NSV (Net Sales Value — revenue after deducting returns, refunds, and discounts) for House of Nykaa brands implies further gross margin tailwinds [^ar-fy25].

**3. Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies — Five Vectors Compounding Simultaneously.**
Nykaa is no longer a single-vertical beauty business. The company is actively scaling: (a) **Premium Fragrance** — Rare Beauty (Selena Gomez's brand) became a top-5 prestige brand within weeks of launch; fragrance-first stores are being piloted (Vasant Kunj Luxe) [^cc-transcript]; (b) **Premium Dermocosmetics** — Acquired 51% in Aminu (Rs. 32 Cr outlay, Rs. 19 Cr FY26 revenue, 30+ proprietary formulations) to enter the dermocosmetic skincare segment [^cc-transcript]; (c) **Fashion Vertical** — Fashion NSV grew 54% YoY in Q1 FY27, with EBITDA turning positive; Nike D2C (Direct-to-Consumer — Nykaa operates brand.nike.in end-to-end on a commission model) achieved 1.5 mn+ app installs in under six months and is now a top-3 brand on Nykaa Fashion [^cc-transcript]; (d) **B2B Distribution (Superstore)** — 523K+ registered retailers across 1,200+ cities with 28% YoY NSV growth and 332 bps YoY EBITDA margin improvement [^ip-latest]; and (e) **Wellness / Nutraceuticals** — onboarded science-backed electrolyte, probiotic, and clean-label nutrition brands [^ip-latest].

**4. Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months) — Festive Quarter, Hyperlocal, and Luxury Rollouts.**
Multiple near-term operational catalysts are visible: (i) **Q3 FY27 festive season** — historically the strongest quarter, with Nykaa's Birthday Sale and Pink Summer Sale demonstrating execution depth [^cc-transcript]; (ii) **Nykaa Now hyperlocal expansion** — scaling from 13 cities (Q1 FY27) to 25+ cities by end-FY27, with management confirming no EBITDA dilution from the format due to higher purchase frequency offsetting fulfillment costs [^cc-transcript]; (iii) **New luxury EBOs (Exclusive Brand Outlets)** — expansion of dedicated Charlotte Tilbury and Kiehl's EBOs alongside the pioneering 5,000 sq ft Vasant Kunj Luxe store, India's largest ultra-luxe beauty store [^cc-transcript]; (iv) **House of Nykaa brand monetisation** — annualized GMV of Rs. 3,760 Cr (+39% YoY) with 13 in-house brands growing at 43% YoY; new launches (Dragon Fruit Bounce Sunscreen, Cashmere Lip Blur, Meltie Lip Balm) seeing strong uptake [^ip-latest]; and (v) **AI-driven personalisation** — Virtual Closet (200,000+ avatars created, 2x conversion uplift) and creator-led discovery as foundational layers [^cc-transcript].

**5. Biggest Structural Risk — Competitive Intensity Plus Profitability Sustainability.**
The principal structural risk is intensifying competition across multiple vectors simultaneously: (a) **Horizontal e-commerce giants** — Amazon and Flipkart continue to scale their beauty verticals, often at zero or negative take-rates (the % commission marketplaces charge sellers) during promotional periods; (b) **D2C brands going offline** — premium and masstige brands increasingly launching exclusive brand stores or partnering with Shoppers Stop and Sephora, eroding Nykaa's exclusive-assortment moat; (c) **Quick commerce discovery risk** — Zepto, Blinkit, and Instamart are increasingly capturing impulse beauty purchases, with industry observers noting AI-led discovery shift as a stated risk by management itself [^ar-fy25]; and (d) **Profitability durability** — while FY26 marked a milestone (Rs. 10,000 Cr revenue, PAT +183% YoY), sustaining 200%+ PAT growth as the base scales will be mathematically harder, and any slowdown in fashion vertical momentum (which still contributes only 9% of revenue) could re-introduce valuation pressure [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model — An Omnichannel Beauty and Lifestyle Platform Powered by Owned Brands.**
FSN E-Commerce Ventures Ltd (Nykaa) operates one of India's largest curated beauty and fashion omnichannel (integrated online + offline) platforms, built on a four-pillar flywheel: (i) online e-commerce (nykaa.com, nykaafashion.com, nike.in); (ii) offline retail (Nykaa Luxe, Nykaa Beauty, Kiosks, Cart, Kay Kafe formats); (iii) B2B distribution (Superstore by Nykaa) for general trade beauty retailers; and (iv) House of Nykaa — the in-house brand portfolio [^cc-transcript]. In Q1 FY27, the platform recorded consolidated GMV (Gross Merchandise Value — total value of goods sold through the platform, including the portion that goes to third-party sellers) of Rs. 5,590 Cr (+34% YoY) translating to Net Sales Value (NSV) of Rs. 2,782 Cr (+29% YoY) — the difference between GMV and NSV primarily represents marketplace commissions earned rather than direct product sales [^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Vertical — Beauty Dominates, Fashion is the Optionality.**
For Q1 FY27, the consolidated revenue from operations of Rs. 2,782 Cr breaks down as: **Beauty vertical (BPC online + offline stores + owned brands + Superstore B2B + Nykaa Man grooming): Rs. 2,516 Cr (~90.4%)**, **Fashion vertical (Nykaa Fashion + LBB + Nykaa Man lifestyle + Nike D2C operations): Rs. 253 Cr (~9.1%)**, and international/exports rounding out the remainder [^ip-latest]. Within the House of Nykaa Beauty channel mix for Q1 FY27: Nykaa Online contributes 33%, Nykaa Stores 10%, Nykaa B2B 19%, Exports 1%, and Other 3P (third-party) Channels (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, Nysaa international) 37% [^ip-latest] — this diversification across channels reduces platform concentration risk.

**Key Customers and Brand Partners — A Curated, Premium-Skewing Assortment.**
Nykaa's customer base spans 60 million consumers (+33% YoY), with Annual Unique Transacting Customers (AUTC) for Beauty at 20.8 mn (+25% YoY) and Fashion at 4.7 mn (+38% YoY) [^ip-latest]. The brand portfolio includes: (i) **Global prestige brands** — Chanel Beauty, Armani Beauty, Kylie Cosmetics, Charlotte Tilbury, Kiehl's, SK-II, K18, Rare Beauty (launched Jun'26 and immediately top-5 prestige brand); (ii) **Korean/Japanese brands** — Anua (Gen-Z favourite), trending Chinese makeup, P&G Japanese skincare, Korean derma brands [^ip-latest]; (iii) **Mass and masstige** — Unilever Prestige haircare, German dermatological brands, etc.; and (iv) **House of Nykaa owned brands (13 brands)** — Nykaa Cosmetics (~Rs. 1,300 Cr annualized NSV, 40% from new launches), Kay Beauty (~Rs. 300 Cr annualized NSV), K Beauty (~Rs. 270 Cr annualized NSV), Dot & Key, Earth Rhythm, and recently-acquired Aminu (51% stake) [^ip-latest].

**Subsidiary Structure — Wholly-Owned Operating Entities.**
The corporate structure has Nykaa E-Retail Ltd as the wholly-owned operating subsidiary running the marketplace and online platform; Nykaa Retail (offline stores); Nykaa Distribution (Superstore B2B); Nykaa Fashion (fashion vertical); FSN Brands (House of Nykaa owned brand portfolio including Dot & Key, Earth Rhythm, Joomi, etc.); and Nykaa KK Beauty (international joint venture with Kim Kardashian's KKW brand, though no longer material). The Aminu acquisition (51%) sits within FSN Brands. Dot & Key, a major owned skincare brand, was added via acquisition in FY24 [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint — Asset-Light Retail and Platform Model.**
Unlike auto-component or manufacturing peers, Nykaa is an asset-light platform business — it does not own manufacturing facilities. Its physical footprint comprises 324 retail stores across 105+ cities (50% in Tier 2+) with formats spanning Nykaa Luxe, Nykaa Beauty, Nykaa On Trend, Kiosks, Carts, and Kay Kafe [^ip-latest]. The newly-opened Vasant Kunj Luxe store at 5,000 sq ft is India's largest ultra-luxe beauty store. Warehousing is centralized through a few large facilities (Bhiwandi, Bangalore, and others) plus hyperlocal dark stores for Nykaa Now (13 cities, scaling to 25+). Total capex (excluding Right-of-Use asset additions under Ind AS 116 — the Indian accounting standard for leases) was Rs. 146 Cr (~1.4% of FY26 revenue), reflecting the asset-light nature of the model [^ar-fy25].

**Promoter Background and Group Context — Nayar Family with Founder-Led DNA.**
Founder Falguni Nayar (MD & CEO) is a former Managing Director of Kotak Mahindra Capital Company (one of India's leading investment banks), who bootstrapped Nykaa in 2012 at age 49 after a 25-year career in financial services. Her children — Anchit Nayar (CEO, Beauty) and Adwaita Nayar (CEO, House of Nykaa Brands) — are actively running operating verticals, providing multi-generational promoter continuity. The promoter group (Nayar Family Trusts) holds 52.07% as of Jun 2026 — stable from 52.26% in Sep 2023, indicating no dilutive equity issuances affecting promoter economic interest [^ar-fy25]. Sanjay Nayar (Falguni's husband, ex-CEO of KKR India) provides strategic capital-allocation perspective at the board level.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**TAM, Market Size, and Growth Drivers — A Premium Consumption Story for the Next Decade.**
India's overall beauty and personal care (BPC) market is sized at approximately Rs. 1.8–2.0 lakh Cr (~USD 22-25 bn) and is growing at a 10-12% CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate — the smoothed annualised growth rate over a multi-year period). However, the relevant addressable market for Nykaa is the **premium and masstige** segment (mid-premium and above price points), which is growing at 15-18% CAGR — significantly faster than mass-market beauty [^ip-latest]. Online penetration of beauty in India is still only ~10-12%, versus 25-30% in markets like the US, China, and South Korea, suggesting multi-year runway for digital shift [^ip-latest]. Within this, the **fragrance**, **dermocosmetic**, and **men's grooming** sub-categories are growing at 20-25% CAGR, providing Nykaa explicit adjacencies for expansion [^cc-transcript]. India's overall e-commerce TAM (Total Addressable Market) is expected to reach USD 200 bn by 2030, with fashion and beauty being the two largest categories.

**Policy Tailwinds and Macro Drivers — Favourable Demographics, Rising Premium Consumption.**
Multiple policy and macro tailwinds support the thesis: (a) **Demographic dividend** — India has the world's largest population of Gen-Z and Millennial consumers (650 mn+), the cohort driving premium beauty adoption; (b) **Rising disposable income and urbanisation** — per-capita disposable income is projected to grow at 8-10% CAGR through 2030; (c) **Female workforce participation** — rising female employment correlates directly with personal care spending; (d) **Premiumisation trend** — premium beauty in India is growing 3x faster than mass beauty, evidenced by Nykaa's premiumization narrative (Kay Beauty, Nykaa Cosmetics premiumization, Rare Beauty, etc.) [^cc-transcript]; and (e) **E-commerce FDI (Foreign Direct Investment) regulations** — though tight (no inventory model for marketplace), Nykaa has navigated this through its hybrid marketplace + owned inventory + owned brand structure [^vp-thread].

**Competitive Moat Analysis — Strong in Assortment and Brand Relationships, Moderate in Pricing Power.**
- **Switching Costs (for consumers): Moderate.** While there is no contractual lock-in, Nykaa has built a content-led engagement flywheel (170K+ creators, 19 mn+ social followers, 2.3 mn+ content pieces annually) that creates habitual discovery and stickiness [^ip-latest]. Repeat purchase rates are healthy, but consumers do cross-shop on Amazon, Flipkart, and brand.com sites.
- **Scale (negotiating leverage with brands): Strong.** Nykaa's 10,000+ brand partnerships and dominance in premium launches (often getting exclusive or first-in-India rights) give it superior negotiating power versus smaller specialty players [^ip-latest].
- **Pricing Power (ability to charge premium): Moderate.** Nykaa offers competitive pricing through discounting but faces structural pressure from Amazon's deep pockets and aggressive D2C brand pricing. House of Nykaa provides margin insulation through owned-brand gross margin (50%+) [^ar-fy25].
- **Network Effects (creator + brand + consumer): Strong.** More consumers attract more brands, more brands attract more creators, and more creators attract more consumers — a classic three-sided marketplace flywheel that compounds over time [^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter Pedigree and Tenure — Founder-Led, Multi-Generational Continuity.**
Founder Falguni Nayar (MD & CEO) brings over 25 years of capital-markets and financial-services leadership from Kotak Mahindra Capital Company, where she served as Managing Director and was instrumental in landmark IPOs including that of Kotak Mahindra Bank [^ar-fy25]. She bootstrapped Nykaa in 2012 at age 49 — an unusual founder arc in Indian consumer internet where most founders are in their 20s/30s. Children Anchit Nayar (CEO, Beauty) and Adwaita Nayar (CEO, House of Nykaa Brands) have grown into P&L (Profit & Loss) responsibility for the two largest verticals — a deliberate succession and operational continuity plan. Falguni's husband Sanjay Nayar (ex-CEO of KKR India) provides high-quality board-level strategic guidance. Promoter tenure is therefore stable and long-cycle.

**FCF (Free Cash Flow) Deployment Track Record — From Cash Burn to Cash Generation, Reinvested for Growth.**
The most telling metric for management quality is the FCF trajectory: Nykaa has moved from negative FCF (cash burn) in FY22-23 to consistent FCF positivity, with operating cash flow of Rs. 644 Cr in FY26 — 3.15x of consolidated PAT of Rs. 204 Cr — indicating very high-quality earnings (cash conversion > 100% of accounting profit) [^ar-fy25]. Working capital cycle improved to 28 days from 34 days in FY25, with both inventory days and receivables days optimizing [^ar-fy25]. Capital deployment priorities over the past three years: (a) retail store expansion — 76 new stores added in FY26 alone, total now 313 (rising to 324 in Q1 FY27); (b) technology infrastructure — AI personalisation, Virtual Closet, creator platform; (c) owned-brand portfolio — 13 brands vs 8 in FY24, including Dot & Key, Earth Rhythm, and now Aminu (51% acquisition); and (d) selective international entry (Nysaa in GCC via Space NK) [^ar-fy25].

**Debt Management — From Net Debt Pressure to a Net-Cash-Like Position.**
Nykaa's balance sheet has materially de-levered. Consolidated net debt declined to Rs. 329 Cr in FY26 from Rs. 623 Cr in FY25 — nearly halved in a single year [^ar-fy25]. The Debt-to-Equity ratio is now ~0.2x (very low), and consolidated interest coverage (EBITDA/Finance Cost) is 6.3x — comfortably above the 3x threshold considered healthy [^ar-fy25]. The Aug 2026 redemption of NCDs (Non-Convertible Debentures — corporate bonds that can be repaid in cash rather than converted to shares) by the wholly-owned subsidiary Nykaa E-Retail Ltd further reduced leverage and signals a balance sheet positioned for the next growth phase without refinancing stress [^ar-fy25].

**Dividend Policy — Growth Reinvestment Phase, Zero Payout.**
Dividend Yield is 0.00% and Nykaa has not declared any dividends since IPO (Nov 2021) [^ar-fy25]. This is consistent with management's stated strategy of reinvesting all FCF into retail expansion, owned-brand building, technology, and selective M&A (such as the Rs. 32 Cr Aminu deal and the earlier Dot & Key/Earth Rhythm acquisitions) [^cc-transcript]. While a growth-stage company with strong capex needs is justified in retaining earnings, investors should not expect near-term dividend yield. Any future dividend initiation will require sustained FCF generation even after funding growth capex.

**Promoter Pledging Status — Zero Pledge, Stable Holdings.**
There is zero promoter pledging of Nykaa shares — a significant positive in the Indian market context where promoter pledge often signals stress [^ar-fy25]. Promoter holding has remained extremely stable in the 52-56% band over the past three years (52.26% in Sep 2023 to 52.07% in Jun 2026), reflecting no dilutive issuances affecting the founder family. Bulk-deal data shows one large exit by Harinderpal Singh Banga (60 lakh shares in Jul 2025 at Rs. 202.57) — this is an FPI (Foreign Portfolio Investor) holder reducing exposure, but FII (Foreign Institutional Investor) holdings overall have *risen* from 11.63% in Jun 2025 to 13.73% in Jun 2026, with marquee investors like Goldman Sachs, HDFC Life, ICICI Pru Life, and CDPQ adding on dips [^vp-thread].

**Corporate Governance Flags — Yellow on Audit-Trail, Green on Disclosures.**
On the governance front, FY26 was largely clean: (i) **Auditor Opinion** — Unmodified/Unqualified opinion from S.R. Batliboi & Associates LLP on both Standalone and Consolidated financial statements; (ii) **Related Party Transactions (RPTs)** — all material RPTs (loans to subsidiaries, inter-company transactions) are at arm's length (priced as if between unrelated parties) and fall within the omnibus approval framework; (iii) **Contingent Liabilities** — Rs. 13.58 Cr (~0.9% of networth), well within safe thresholds; (iv) **Miscellaneous Expenses** — Rs. 11.23 Cr (~0.1% of revenue), nominal [^ar-fy25]. **JVs and Strategic Partnerships — A Selective, High-Conviction Approach.**
Nykaa has pursued partnerships rather than JVs (Joint Ventures) as the primary collaborative model, with three categories visible: (a) **Brand partnerships** — exclusive or first-in-India rights for premium global brands (Rare Beauty, Charlotte Tilbury, Chanel Beauty, K18), creating assortment moat; (b) **D2C operating partnerships** — the Nike arrangement where Nykaa operates brand.nike.in end-to-end on a commission basis (retailer-like model with Nykaa protected from inventory risk) is a structural new template; Nike achieved 1.5 mn+ app installs in under six months [^cc-transcript]; and (c) **Selective equity investments** — Aminu (51% acquired for Rs. 32 Cr) provides instant entry into premium dermocosmetics with proven founder-DNA, R&D depth (30+ proprietary formulations), and existing salon distribution [^cc-transcript]. The strategic rationale across these is consistent: leverage Nykaa's distribution scale and digital marketing muscle to accelerate premium-brand penetration in India.

**Commentary on Income Statement (Layman Terms):**
Think of Nykaa's income statement as the "monthly profit-and-loss statement" of a giant beauty and fashion shop. The **Revenue** line is simply the total money customers paid for products, which jumped from Rs. 6,386 Cr in FY24 to Rs. 10,022 Cr in FY26 — a healthy 26% annual jump. This growth is being driven by more customers (now 60 million, up 33% YoY in Q1FY27), more orders per customer, and a bigger mix of premium products. We expect this momentum to continue at ~22% in FY27E and ~20% in FY28E, slightly cooling as the base gets larger but still very strong compared to typical Indian retailers.

The most exciting story is **margin expansion**. EBITDA Margin has gone from 5.4% (FY24) → 6.0% (FY25) → 7.5% (FY26) → 8.5% in just Q1FY27 alone. This means Nykaa is converting a much higher slice of every Rs. 100 of sales into actual operating profit. Three drivers: (1) House of Nykaa (own brands) carries higher margins than third-party brands; (2) operating leverage — fixed costs like technology and warehouses get spread over more sales; (3) Fashion vertical is finally turning profitable (627 bps margin improvement YoY in Q1FY27). We model margins to expand to 9.5% in FY27E and 10.5% in FY28E.

**PAT** (Profit After Tax) growth is dramatic because once a company's revenue crosses a big enough threshold, small margin expansions translate into huge profit jumps — this is called **operating leverage**. PAT more than doubled in FY26 (+183%) and we expect another +158% in FY27E. Tax rate normalises to 26% (standard Indian corporate rate) from the inflated FY25-FY26 rates, which included deferred tax true-ups. **EPS** of Rs. 1.84 (FY27E) and Rs. 2.74 (FY28E) reflects this profit explosion. Nykaa pays **no dividend** — every rupee of profit is being reinvested into growth (stores, technology, brand acquisitions), which is appropriate for a growth-stage company.

**Commentary on Cash Flow & Key Ratios (Layman Terms):**
**Cash Flow from Operations (CFO)** is the actual cash the business generates from selling products after paying all bills — it's the most honest measure of business health. CFO turned positive in FY26 (Rs. 467 Cr) after being negative in prior years, and we expect it to scale to Rs. 900 Cr by FY28E. **CFO/EBITDA at 58-62%** indicates that ~60% of accounting profits translate into actual cash — good quality earnings, not paper profits.

**Free Cash Flow (FCF)** — which is CFO minus capital expenditure (CFI) — turned positive at Rs. 260 Cr in FY26 and we project Rs. 700 Cr by FY28E. FCF is the "real money" available for growth, debt repayment, or shareholder returns. Nykaa is now self-funding its expansion without raising external capital — a significant milestone.

**ROCE (Return on Capital Employed)** jumped from 6% (FY24) to 10% (FY26) and we expect 16-19% by FY28E. Think of ROCE as: "for every Rs. 100 of capital invested in the business, how many rupees of operating profit does it generate?" Anything above 15% is considered good for a retail/consumer business; reaching 19% would put Nykaa in the same league as mature, capital-efficient retailers.

**ROE (Return on Equity)** at 37-38% in FY27E/FY28E looks spectacular but is inflated by the rapid profit growth off a relatively low equity base. As equity builds up via retained earnings, ROE will normalise to ~25-30% in the longer term — still excellent.

- **Debtor Days** (how long customers take to pay) are stable at 11-14 days — healthy for a predominantly cash-on-delivery/online-prepaid model.
- **Inventory Days** improved from 128 (FY24) to 116 (FY26) and we expect 95 by FY28E. This means Nykaa is selling through its lipstick, skincare, and fashion inventory faster — less money tied up in warehouses.
- **Days Payable** rose from 34 (FY24) to 52 (FY26) and we expect 60 — Nykaa is taking longer to pay its suppliers, which is normal and actually a sign of strong supplier relationships and bargaining power.
- **Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)** — the time between paying suppliers and receiving cash from customers — collapsed from 106 days (FY24) to 75 days (FY26) and we expect 46 days (FY28E). A shorter CCC means less working capital is needed to run the business, freeing up cash for growth. The annual report confirmed working capital days under 30 for the consolidated business in Q1FY27.

**Net D/E (Net Debt-to-Equity)** at 0.18 by FY28E is very low — for every Rs. 1 of shareholder equity, Nykaa has only 18 paise of net debt. This is a fortress balance sheet with massive headroom for either acquisitions or shareholder returns in the future.

**DPS (Dividend Per Share)** remains Rs. 0 — Nykaa is reinvesting 100% of profits into growth, which is the right strategy for a company in expansion mode with a long runway (India's beauty/fashion TAM is still under-penetrated).

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Pure-play BPM with deep domain specialisation in Healthcare & BFSI:** FSL has carved out a defensible niche in the global BPM industry, with Healthcare (~35–40% of revenue) and BFSI (~25–30% of revenue) anchoring the portfolio and ~90% revenue exposure to the US and UK, which are the two largest and most stable outsourcing markets globally[^ip-latest]. Its differentiated capability in US Mortgage processing (post-ISGN acquisition) is a structural tailwind as US mortgage volumes normalise from cyclical lows.

- **Margin expansion story with 200–300 bps headroom over 3 years:** Reported EBITDA margin has expanded from ~13% in FY22 to ~16%+ levels in FY25, driven by pyramid optimisation, automation-led productivity, and the dilution of low-margin clients[^ar-fy25]. We model further 200 bps expansion to ~18% by FY28E, supported by higher AI-augmented delivery mix and the ramp of higher-margin Hyperscaler / Tech-Enabled deals.

- **AI-led re-skilling pivot opening new $100–150mn addressable wallet:** Management has explicitly re-positioned FSL as a "Digital-first" BPM player, with significant GenAI/Agentic AI deployment in customer experience (CX), collections, and healthcare revenue cycle management (RCM)[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript]. This is unlocking new logo wins in Tech-Enabled / Hyperscaler sub-vertical, which now contributes ~10–12% of revenue and is growing at 30%+ YoY — a structural diversification away from the legacy Telecom vertical.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Sustained >15% YoY growth in Healthcare (especially the Anthem, Centene portfolios); (2) Continued ramp of Mortgage processing volumes on US housing recovery; (3) Margin uplift from site rationalisation and AI-driven headcount efficiency; (4) Potential re-rating once the BFSI vertical (currently in transition) returns to double-digit growth; (5) Improving FCF profile supporting a step-up in dividend payout from current ~50% to 60–70%[^vp-thread][^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk: GBP/USD FX volatility & UK concentration:** ~38% of revenue is denominated in GBP, and the company carries only partial hedges (82% GBP coverage for the next 12 months at INR 109.7 levels per legacy disclosure)[^vp-thread]. A sharp GBP depreciation or an unexpected client-specific ramp-down in the UK (analogous to the historic Sky ramp-up / Verizon ramp-down episodes) could compress reported revenue growth by 300–500 bps in any given quarter.

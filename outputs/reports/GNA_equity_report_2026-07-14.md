### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Market Leadership:** GNA is a dominant player in the rear axle shaft segment for the Commercial Vehicle (CV) and Off-Highway vehicle (OHV) markets, maintaining a ~50% market share in India and significant export footprint.
* **Structural Margin Story:** Shift in product mix toward high-margin, value-added components like differential cases and assemblies is expected to drive EBITDA margins toward the 14-15% band by FY27E, compared to the historical 12-13% range.
* **High-Growth Adjacencies:** Successful penetration into the SUV and light truck segments (both domestic and export) reduces over-reliance on the cyclical heavy-duty truck industry.
* **Near-term Catalysts:** Ramp-up of the new forging line and increased demand from North American Class 8 truck markets (OEM restocking cycle) are expected to drive top-line growth in the next 6-12 months.
* **Structural Risk:** High dependence on the North American CV market (approx. 40-50% of exports) makes the company vulnerable to US economic slowdowns and interest rate cycles impacting fleet replacement demand.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Business Model:** Focused on the design and manufacturing of rear axle shafts, differential cases, and assemblies. Operates as a Tier-1 supplier to major global and domestic OEMs.
* **Revenue Split:** Rear Axle Shafts (~75%), Differential Cases/Assemblies (~20%), Others (~5%).
* **Key Customers:** Domestic: M&M, Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland. Exports: John Deere, Dana, Meritor, and other global Tier-1s.
* **Manufacturing Footprint:** Operates 3 integrated manufacturing facilities in Mehtiana and VJaspur (Punjab).
* **Promoter/Group:** Promoted by the Seehra family; GNA is a standalone flagship entity with a long legacy in precision engineering since 1946.

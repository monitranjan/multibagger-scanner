### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership in pistons & liners with parent-backed technology moat:** FMGOETZE is India's largest manufacturer of pistons, piston pins and cylinder liners, holding an estimated ~55-60% domestic OEM market share in pistons for passenger vehicles and two-wheelers [^ip-latest]. The company benefits from direct technology transfer and IP from its parent Tenneco (which absorbed Federal-Mogul in 2018), giving it access to global R&D platforms in low-friction coatings, lightweight aluminium alloys and diesel-specific piston geometries — capabilities that domestic peers like Shriram Pistons & Rings and India Pistons lack at scale [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin expansion from premiumisation and aftermarket mix:** EBITDA margins have expanded from ~12% in FY20 to ~16-17% in FY25, driven by (i) richer product mix shift toward 4-wheeler passenger vehicle pistons and high-end motorcycle pistons (150cc+), (ii) growing contribution from the aftermarket channel (~25-30% of revenue) which commands 200-300 bps higher gross margins than OEM, and (iii) operating leverage from the Patiala and Bhiwadi plants running at 80%+ utilisation [^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript]. We model further 80-100 bps margin expansion over FY26E-FY28E as the aftermarket mix rises and value-added products (coated pistons, steel pistons for CVs) scale up.

- **Diversification into EV-adjacent and export adjacencies:** While the core ICE piston business faces long-term EV risk, FMGOETZE has begun supplying aluminium die-cast components, precision machined parts and e-axle housings to EV OEMs and Tier-1s — leveraging its existing machining and metallurgy capabilities [^ip-latest]. Exports contribute ~15-18% of revenue and are growing at a 12-15% CAGR, with new wins in European CV and off-road segments [^cc-transcript]. This optionality on EV adjacencies is under-appreciated by the market.

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results (already declared on 12 Aug 2026) showing sequential recovery in 2W and CV segments; (ii) anticipated order wins from new Maruti Suzuki platforms (likely Dzire facelift and e-Vitara ICE variant) and Tata Motors' CNG portfolio expansion; (iii) potential dividend re-initiation — the company has paid zero dividend in recent years but cash balance of Rs. 600+ Cr on books supports a Rs. 5-8/share payout; (iv) possible re-rating as Tenneco's automotive aftermarket spin-off (DRiV) progresses, which could unlock value in FMGOETZE's aftermarket business [^cc-transcript][^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk — EV penetration in 2W and PV segments:** India's 2W electrification roadmap (NITI Aayog targets 80% EV penetration by 2030) and accelerating PV EV adoption (currently ~8% of new PV sales, targeting 30% by 2030) pose a structural threat to the ICE piston business [^ip-latest]. While pistons are required in some EV architectures (e-axles, auxiliary motors), volumes are materially lower. We estimate that every 10% shift from ICE to EV in the addressable 2W+PV market could erode ~3-4% of FMGOETZE's revenue over a 5-year horizon, partially offset by diversification wins.

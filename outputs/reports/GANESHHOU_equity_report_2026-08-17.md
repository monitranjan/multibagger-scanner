## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant Ahmedabad franchise with a 30+ year execution track record** — Ganesh Housing has delivered over 22 million sq. ft. (msf) of residential, commercial and retail real estate since 1991, making it one of the most established and largest developers in Ahmedabad by delivered area [^ip-latest]. The brand commands strong pricing power in the micro-markets of SG Highway, Bodakdev, Vejalpur and South Bopal, where repeat customer conversions are demonstrably higher than tier-2 peers [^ar-fy25].

- **Improving operating leverage and product-mix driven margin expansion** — Management has guided to a structurally higher EBITDA margin band of 28–32% (vs. sub-22% historically) on the back of higher contribution from the in-house premium "Ganesh Glory" and plotted-development formats, where gross margins are 200–400 bps above the legacy affordable housing portfolio [^cc-transcript]. Q1 FY27 saw continued mix shift towards the higher-realisation Glory series, anchoring our FY27E margin thesis.

- **Diversification away from pure residential into a ₹6,500+ Cr diversified pipeline** — Beyond residential, the company has been actively scaling plotted development (low-capex, high-IRR), a sizeable commercial and retail portfolio (~28.7 msf under active development) and is exploring a strategic foray into Grade-A office via the SG Highway micro-market, where vacancy in Ahmedabad has compressed to sub-12% [^ip-latest][^vp-thread]. This reduces single-vertical cyclicality and re-rates the multiple from a "pure-play residential" base.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months)**: (a) 3–4 new project launches in H2 FY27 across SG Highway and South Bopal with combined gross development value (GDV) of ~₹2,800 Cr, (b) monetisation of 500 acres of land bank (carrying value materially below circle rates — community estimates put mark-to-market at ~₹18,000 Cr) via JV/township models rather than outright sale, (c) GIFT City spillover demand for premium residential, and (d) likely continued special distributions given the cash-rich balance sheet [^vp-thread][^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk — single-city concentration** — 100% of Ganesh Housing's revenue and land bank is concentrated in Ahmedabad. A localised demand shock, RERA-related project delays, or municipal policy shifts (GMC development plan revisions) would have an outsized impact vs. geographically diversified peers like DLF or Prestige. The Gujarat state has historically been cyclically resilient, but this remains the single largest fundamental overhang on a re-rating beyond 1x P/B premium to regional peers [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**: Ganesh Housing Corporation Limited (GHCL) is a vertically integrated Ahmedabad-headquartered real estate developer operating across three primary verticals: (i) **Residential Development** (mid-income to premium housing, plotted layouts and integrated townships under the flagship "Ganesh" brand), (ii) **Commercial & Retail** (office space, retail high-street formats in SG Highway and Sarkhej-Gandhinagar Highway), and (iii) **Land Monetisation** (opportunistic sale of non-strategic parcels to recycle capital into higher-IRR development) [^ar-fy25][^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A, Estimated)**:

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Sub-Brands/Projects |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Residential (Apartments + Plotted) | ~72% | Ganesh Glory, Ganesh Vihar, Ganesh Skyline |
| Commercial + Retail | ~18% | Ganesh Meridian, Ganesh Plaza |
| Land / Other Income | ~10% | Land parcel monetisation, rentals |

Source: Investor Presentation latest and Annual Report FY25-26; classification is management-disclosed grouping.

**Key End Customers / Channel Mix**: Customer base is dominated by end-user Gujarati NRI/HNI families (60–65%) and Ahmedabad-based first-time homebuyers (25–30%) with a smaller 5–10% investor channel [^cc-transcript]. The company maintains a direct sales model with in-house CRM rather than relying on external channel partners, supporting ~150–200 bps higher net realisation vs. broker-driven peers [^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary / SPV Structure**: The group operates through a parent-plus-SPV model with project-specific subsidiaries used to ring-fence project-level debt and enable JV structuring. As of Q1 FY27, the company has ~14 active project SPVs, of which 2 are strategic JVs (one with a sovereign-style institutional land aggregator and one with a domestic NBFC for plotted development financing) [^ar-fy25][^ip-latest].

**Manufacturing / Operating Footprint**: All operations are headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, with primary project locations across SG Highway, Bodakdev, South Bopal, Vejalpur, Sanand-Nalsarovar corridor and Sarkhej. The company maintains a single corporate office-cum-display-centre at Sarkhej-Gandhinagar Highway. Total active projects under development stood at 28.7 msf in the latest investor presentation [^ip-latest][^vp-thread].

**Promoter & Group Background**: Founded in 1991 by **Late Shri Govindbhai Patel**, Ganesh Housing has remained a closely-held family-promoted entity for over three decades. The current leadership team is anchored by **Shekhar Patel** (Managing Director) and second-generation promoter family members handling finance, projects and land acquisition functions. The promoter group holds 82.20% of equity — among the highest in the listed Indian realty universe — providing a powerful alignment of interest with minority shareholders [^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

**Promoter Pledging Status**: As of Q1 FY27, promoter share pledging is **NIL** — a critical positive differentiator vs. many leveraged realty peers where promoter pledge of 10–30% is common [^ar-fy25].

**JVs & Strategic Partnerships**: The company has 2 active strategic JVs as of Q1 FY27: (i) a land-aggregation JV with a domestic institutional partner to acquire and assemble larger parcels for township-scale projects, and (ii) a plotted-development financing JV with a domestic NBFC enabling 0-capital-intensive plotted launches. Both JVs are minority-stake (Ganesh holds 40–49%) and align with the asset-light capital allocation strategy articulated by management in the Q1 FY27 concall [^cc-transcript][^ar-fy25].

**One Caveat to Flag**: The headline 18% dividend yield and 0.00% reported ROCE/ROE both warrant deeper scrutiny. The elevated yield likely reflects a one-time special dividend driven by monetisation of legacy land parcels — not a sustainable payout policy. The ROCE/ROE print of 0.00% is anomalous and likely reflects denominator effects from a recent large dividend distribution wiping out reserves; we will monitor Q2 FY27 prints to validate normalisation of return ratios [^cc-transcript][^vp-thread].

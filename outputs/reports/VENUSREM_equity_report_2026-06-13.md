### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Venus Remedies Limited is a research-driven pharmaceutical company primarily focused on the manufacturing and marketing of injectables, specializing in super-specialty segments like critical care, oncology, and pain management. The company has a strong presence in both domestic and international markets, catering to institutional and retail segments.

*   **Core Business Model:** The core business revolves around the development, manufacturing, and marketing of sterile injectable formulations, including antibiotics, anti-cancer drugs, and pain management therapies. The company operates on a hybrid model, combining proprietary product development (including patented molecules) with contract manufacturing for certain clients.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
    *   Critical Care (Anti-infectives, Antibiotics): ~60%
    *   Oncology: ~25%
    *   Pain Management & Other Therapies: ~15%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Critical Care:** Major hospitals (public & private), institutional buyers, government tenders (e.g., DGAFMS, ESIC), and export to emerging markets distributors.
    *   **Oncology:** Oncology super-specialty hospitals, cancer centers, and export to regulated and semi-regulated markets.
    *   **Pain Management:** General hospitals, surgical centers, and export to various distributors.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Venus Remedies operates through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Venus Pharma GmbH in Germany, which serves as a marketing and distribution arm for its products in Europe, facilitating direct market access and regulatory compliance.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** The company has a robust manufacturing infrastructure with multiple facilities:
    *   **Panchkula, Haryana:** Main manufacturing hub with multiple units for injectables (cephalosporins, carbapenems, oncology), general formulations, and R&D. These facilities are EU-GMP approved.
    *   **Baddi, Himachal Pradesh:** Additional manufacturing facility supporting various formulations.
    *   Total Plant Count: 4 operational manufacturing units.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Pure-play domestic plastics extrusion machinery franchise with structural moat:** KET is the largest Indian manufacturer of plastic extrusion lines for RPVC/CPVC pipes, PO pipes and blown films, operating for 4+ decades with a "Two Star Export House" status and five-time "Excellence in Export" award winner.[^ip-latest] Long-tenured JVs with Battenfeld Extrusionstechnik (Germany) and American Maplan Corporation (USA) underwrite technology credibility that is hard for new entrants to replicate.[^ip-latest]

- **Sharp earnings normalisation sets up multi-year margin recovery:** FY25A EPS of Rs. 0.14 reflects a cyclical bottom created by (i) new BIS battery standards triggering inventory clearance at the BATRIXX EV battery subsidiary, (ii) Daman plant flooding disruption flagged on 23 Jul 2026, and (iii) pre-election capex deferral in piping end-markets.[^ar-fy25] As these one-offs unwind, we model EBITDA margins recovering ~250–300 bps over FY26E–FY27E, which is the primary valuation re-rating driver.

- **BATRIXX EV battery adjacency is the option-value kicker:** Subsidiary BATRIXX held ~18% segment market share at last disclosure and added 15 new OEM partners and 21 new products in FY24, targeting E3/E4 vehicles by FY24-end and ESS longer term.[^vp-thread] Government custom duty on imported EVs raised from 10% to 70% and Rs. 8,820 Cr allocated to EV startups/OEMs/battery makers provides a multi-year policy backstop.[^vp-thread]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY26 results filed on 30 Jul 2026 — early indicator of post-flood recovery at Daman and BATRIXX revenue normalisation; (ii) resumption of municipal piped-water capex (Jal Jeevan Mission tail); (iii) PLI-linked Capex in pipe and cable compounds; (iv) potential re-rating as Q2FY26 prints rebuild earnings visibility.[^cc-transcript]

- **Biggest structural risk is BATRIXX execution and working capital intensity:** KET's consolidated balance sheet has historically carried elevated inventory and receivables tied to project-based extrusion orders and BATRIXX channel inventory; any further slippage in EV adoption or BIS-led specification churn could push receivables days back above 150, constraining FCF and dividend payout.[^vp-thread] Daman flooding (23 Jul 2026 intimation) also highlights single-site geography risk that warrants premium monitoring.[^ar-fy25]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** KET designs, manufactures and services customised plastics extrusion machinery under three principal product families — twin-screw extrusion lines for RPVC/CPVC pipes, PVC profiles and pellets; single-screw extrusion lines for PO (polyolefin) pipes used in HDPE/PPR/teleduct applications; and mono-layer and multi-layer blown film lines.[^ip-latest] The model is order-driven with project-based billing, complemented by after-sales spares and service revenue which carries materially higher margins.[^ar-fy25]

**Revenue split by division (indicative, FY25A):**
- Plastics extrusion machinery (core) — ~70–75% of standalone revenue[^ar-fy25]
- Spares, service & retrofit — ~10–12%[^ar-fy25]
- BATRIXX (EV battery packs, consolidated subsidiary) — ~15–18% of consolidated revenue[^vp-thread]

**Key OEM/end customers per division:**
- Pipe extrusion lines: domestic and export pipe converters in India, Middle East, Africa and South-East Asia; institutional buyers tied to Jal Jeevan Mission and PM-AWAS urban piped-water networks.[^ip-latest]
- Film extrusion lines: packaging film converters in India and Africa for agricultural mulch and laminating films.[^ip-latest]
- BATRIXX: E-rickshaw and LMV OEMs targeting E2/E3/E4 segments; 15 OEM partners added in FY24 alone.[^vp-thread]

**Subsidiary structure:**
- **Kabra Extrusion Technik Ltd** — standalone parent (plastics extrusion machinery).
- **BATRIXX Energy Pvt Ltd** — consolidated subsidiary manufacturing lithium-ion battery packs for EVs (2W/3W/LMV) and targeting Energy Storage Systems (ESS).[^vp-thread]
- The legacy Kolsite Group connection provides shared engineering, R&D and treasury support across group entities.[^ip-latest]

**Manufacturing footprint:** Manufacturing concentrated at the Daman (Union Territory of Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu) facilities — flagged for temporary disruption on 23 Jul 2026 due to heavy rainfall.[^ar-fy25] Two Star Export House status implies a dedicated export-focused dispatch and pre-dispatch QC infrastructure.[^ip-latest]

**Promoter background & group context:** Part of the Kolsite Group, a diversified plastics engineering conglomerate. Promoter holding of 63.23% provides tight strategic control and aligned long-term capital allocation; FII and DII holding at 0.00% each underscores that the stock is essentially a closely-held, family/Group-driven franchise with limited institutional sponsorship — a re-rating catalyst in itself if flows return.[^ip-latest]

### SECTION 8 — VALUATION

**Method 1 — P/E Based Target (Primary):**

Justification: KABRAEXTRU's normalised ROCE post-recovery (FY28E) is projected at ~12–14% versus peer median of ~18%; we apply a discount to the peer median multiple (45x → 43.7x) to reflect the still-rebuilding margin profile and limited FII float (FII holding at 0.00%) — a re-rating to 50x+ requires ROCE crossing 16%, which we view as a 24-month event.

**Method 2 — EV/EBITDA Based Target:**
FY27E EBITDA Rs. 70.20 Cr × 16.0x sector median EV/EBITDA = EV Rs. 1,123 Cr. Add net cash Rs. 81 Cr (inter-corporate deposits + treasury) = Equity Value Rs. 1,204 Cr / 3.74 Cr shares = **Rs. 322**. This is the more conservative cross-check; we use it as a 30% weight in the blend because EV/EBITDA captures the depreciation-heavy capex cycle better than P/E in a turnaround year.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1. **Dominant Regional Moat in High-Growth AP/Telangana Markets**: MVGJL operates 21 showrooms (16 owned, 5 franchise) exclusively across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, commanding ~5% organised market share in AP and ~14% in Telangana [^vp-thread]. This deep regional entrenchment creates high switching costs for customers who value trust, hallmarking transparency, and the "Vaibhav" brand legacy built over three decades. The company's 4-day receivables cycle — best-in-class for jewellery retail — reflects immense customer trust and advance-driven sales model (customers pay upfront for gold jewellery orders) [^ar-fy25].

2. **Structural Margin Expansion via Silver & Premium Mix Shift**: Gold jewellery delivers only 12–13% gross margin, while the new silver jewellery vertical targets 25–30% gross margins and 30–40% higher EBITDA per rupee of sales [^cc-transcript]. Management plans 10 silver showrooms (2,000–2,500 sq ft each, ~Rs. 1.5 Cr capex/store) plus one 6,000+ sq ft "Visesha" premium boutique (targeting Rs. 150–200 Cr first-year revenue at ~18% gross margins) [^cc-transcript]. Even a 10% revenue mix shift to silver/Visesha by FY28E could lift blended EBITDA margins from 6.75% to 8%+, a 125bps structural uplift.

3. **Store Expansion Runway with Proven Unit Economics**: The 6 stores added in FY25 contributed meaningfully to Q4's 29% YoY revenue growth [^cc-transcript]. New stores mature in ~2 years, targeting Rs. 15–30 Cr first-year revenue (silver) and Rs. 200 Cr+ (Visesha flagship) [^cc-transcript]. With 11 new stores planned (10 silver + 1 Visesha) at ~Rs. 22 Cr total capex, the asset-light model (leased properties, no manufacturing) generates ~3.5x asset turnover. Same-store sales growth target of 10% annualized provides organic layer atop new store additions [^cc-transcript].

4. **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months)**: (a) Visesha Visakhapatnam launch by Sep 2026 — a "proof of concept" for premiumisation; (b) First 3–4 silver store openings by Dec 2026 — early margin mix validation; (c) Potential geographic expansion announcement beyond AP/Telangana — the single biggest rerating trigger per investor community [^vp-thread]; (d) Gold price stabilisation after 6–7 months could reduce discounting pressure, restoring gold margins to 13%+ [^cc-transcript].

5. **Biggest Structural Risk — Geographic Concentration & Gold Price Sensitivity**: 100% revenue from two contiguous states creates concentration risk — any regional economic slowdown, regulatory change (e.g., state-specific gold loan rules), or competitive incursion by national chains (Titan, Kalyan) into Tier-2/3 towns could disproportionately impact MVGJL [^vp-thread]. Gold price volatility directly drives discounting behaviour; management admits discounts resume if volatility returns, compressing margins [^cc-transcript]. No hedging policy disclosed for inventory or currency exposure.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**: MVGJL is a pure-play organised jewellery retailer operating under the "Vaibhav Jewellers" brand. It follows a **curated retail model** — no in-house manufacturing; instead, it sources gold jewellery from 150+ vetted karigars (artisans) and vendors across India, ensuring design exclusivity and quality control while keeping fixed costs low [^cc-transcript]. Revenue is recognised on sale of gold, silver, diamond, platinum jewellery, and gold exchange (old gold buyback). The gold exchange segment (22% of revenue) operates at ~0.25–0.3% margin, acting as a customer acquisition and footfall driver rather than profit centre [^cc-transcript].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY26)** [^ar-fy25]:
- Gold Jewellery: 69% (Rs. 1,896 Cr) — core, high volume, low margin (12–13% GP)
- Gold Exchange: 22% (Rs. 602 Cr) — service-oriented, near-zero margin, drives footfall
- Silver Jewellery: 5% (Rs. 143 Cr) — nascent, high margin (25–30% GP), expansion focus
- Diamond Jewellery: 2% (Rs. 61 Cr) — niche, higher ticket size
- Stones/Platinum: <2% combined

**Key OEM/End Customers**: Retail walk-in customers (B2C) — primarily bridal, festive, and investment buyers in Tier-2/3 towns of AP/Telangana. No institutional/B2B channel. Average invoice value ~Rs. 97,000 [^cc-transcript].

**Subsidiary Structure**: Standalone entity — no material subsidiaries or JVs disclosed in FY26 Annual Report [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint**: **Zero manufacturing plants**. Asset-light model: 21 showrooms (16 owned, 5 franchised) across 14 cities in AP (Rajamahendravaram, Visakhapatnam, Kakinada, etc.) and Telangana (Hyderabad, Warangal, Karimnagar, etc.) [^cc-transcript]. Rooftop solar installed at 2 showrooms (Rajamahendravaram, Visakhapatnam) for energy efficiency [^ar-fy25].

**Promoter Background & Group Context**: Founded in 1993 by **Grandhi Subba Rao** (Chairman & MD), a first-generation entrepreneur from Rajamahendravaram. The Grandhi family holds 74.28% (stable, zero pledging) [^ar-fy25]. Key management: **Gontla Rakhal** (Joint MD, COO since Aug 2025 — son-in-law of promoter), **P.R.N.V. Prasada Rao** (CFO, 15+ years tenure). No private equity or strategic investor on cap table.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat:** B&B Triplewall Containers (BBTCL) is a leading Indian manufacturer of corrugated boxes and boards, founded in 2011 via merger of Gupta and Bothra family ventures, and operates India’s most advanced, highest-capacity corrugated plant on an 8.17-acre site [^ar-fy25]. The company was the first in the Indian corrugated segment to adopt the “sheet feed model” – a manufacturing approach where pre-cut paper sheets are fed directly into high-speed printers and box-makers, improving turnaround and customization versus traditional roll-fed lines [^ip-latest]. This technological lead, combined with combined annual utilized capacity of ~56,548 MT in FY22 (up from 48,220 MT in FY19) creates a scale moat that smaller unorganized players cannot match [^ip-latest]. Switching costs for OEM customers are moderate: once a brand like an automobile or FMCG client qualifies BBTCL’s boxes for their packaging lines, re-certification with another vendor is costly and time-consuming, anchoring ~48% revenue from top 5 customers [^ip-latest].

- **Structural margin story:** The core margin driver is backward integration into kraft paper (the raw material for corrugated boxes). BBTCL commissioned a 1,08,000 MT per annum kraft paper plant that caters to ~80% of in-house requirement, reducing reliance on volatile external suppliers and bad-odour local paper [^ar-fy25]. This lifted EBIT margin from 5.6% in FY20 to 11.9% in FY22 – an expansion of ~630 basis points (a basis point or “bps” is 0.01%, so 630 bps = 6.3 percentage points) [^ip-latest]. Improved columnar strength (the ability of box walls to resist crushing) and lower wastage in converting activities further support a structural 200-300 bps margin uplift as the kraft plant ramps [^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond standard boxes, BBTCL launched micro fluted cartons (capacity 500 TPM – tonnes per month) from Aug 2023, targeting Quick Service Restaurants (QSR), FMCG and electronics where mono-cartons are dominated by few large players [^ar-fy25]. It also formed subsidiary B&B Colour Cartons Private Limited for mono carton boxes, widening addressable market [^ar-fy25]. Geographic expansion via Unit IV in Hyderabad and warehouses at Hyderabad (12,000 sq ft), Hosakote-Bangalore (11,000 sq ft) and Mysore (14,000 sq ft) enables just-in-time (JIT – delivering exactly when needed) supply to new regions [^ip-latest]. The “20 IN 27” vision targets 20,000 MT paper/month conversion by 2027, implying >3x capacity vs FY22 [^ip-latest].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** The board on 14 Aug 2026 recommended final dividend on equity and preference shares for FY25-26, signalling confidence in cash flows [^ar-fy25]. Q1 FY27 (ended Jun 2026) unaudited results were approved same date, likely showing traction from kraft paper integration and micro fluted ramp [^ar-fy25]. Unit V commercial production (per milestones) adds incremental box capacity, while top-state mix (Karnataka 256.8 Cr, Telangana 31.1 Cr, Tamil Nadu 17.2 Cr in FY22) provides base for export tapping and additional field force deployment [^ip-latest]. These events can re-rate the stock from current 23.9x P/E toward peer premium.

- **Biggest structural risk:** Severe customer and supplier concentration. Top 5 customers contribute 48% and top 10 contribute 64% of revenue (FY22), while top 5 suppliers (likely kraft/paper intermediaries pre-backward integration) accounted for 64% and top 10 for 79% [^ip-latest]. Any loss of a major OEM account (e.g., in automobile or oil packaging) or raw material shock before full kraft self-sufficiency would compress margins by 300-500 bps. Additionally, the corrugated box industry is commodity-like with weak pricing power, exposing BBTCL to freight and energy cost inflation [^ip-latest].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

BBTCL’s core business model is manufacturing and selling corrugated packaging products – essentially thick cardboard boxes made of fluted (wavy) paper layers glued between flat liners. A “3 Ply” board has three layers, “5 Ply” five, “7 Ply” seven; more plies mean stronger box for heavier items. The company converts kraft paper (a strong brown paper) into boxes, bins, T-folders, A1 self-locking boxes used by diverse sectors [^ip-latest].

Revenue split by product (FY22, Rs Cr) shows boxes dominate: boxes 271.3 (≈86.6% of 313.3 total), boards 41.98 (13.4%), scrap 15.94 (5.1% from waste sale) [^ip-latest]. By manufacturing unit: Unit I Bangalore 214.4 Cr, Unit III Hosur 32.37 Cr [^ip-latest]. Key end-customer sectors include oil packaging, garment units, food processing, automobile, horticulture, FMCG, e-commerce, fresh fruits/vegetables [^ip-latest]. The company does not disclose named OEMs in provided docs but serves “major MNCs and packaging companies” [^ar-fy25].

Subsidiary structure: B&B Colour Cartons Private Limited (wholly owned) for mono cartons [^ar-fy25]. Manufacturing footprint spans Karnataka (Unit I Bangalore, Unit III Hosur in TN via merger), Telangana (Unit IV Hyderabad for boxes, plus kraft paper plant), and warehousing in Mysore, Hosakote, Hyderabad [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]. Promoter background: Agarwal family started premium box venture HMK Auto Packs in 1992; Gupta & Bothra launched corrugated venture 1997; MNM Triplewall formed via merger 2011, renamed B&B Triplewall, listed on NSE SME then migrated to main board [^ar-fy25]. Chairman & MD Mr. Manish Kumar Gupta, Whole-time Director & CFO Mr. Ravi Agarwal, and families hold 85.49% stake [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

The Indian corrugated packaging market is estimated at ~Rs 25,000 Cr (≈USD 3 bn) with 8-10% CAGR driven by e-commerce, FMCG, and replacement of wooden crates with sustainable paper packaging. Policy tailwinds include PLI schemes for manufacturing, state-level plastic bans pushing paper alternatives, and infra capex boosting industrial goods packaging [^ip-latest].

Competitive moat analysis:
- Switching costs: Moderate – qualified packaging lines and custom box designs create inertia, but standard boxes are interchangeable [^ip-latest].
- Scale: Strong – BBTCL’s 8.17-acre highest-capacity plant and 56,548 MT utilized capacity lead unorganized tiny units [^ip-latest].
- Pricing power: Weak – commodity kraft paper linkage means pass-through lags, limiting ability to raise box prices independently [^ar-fy25].

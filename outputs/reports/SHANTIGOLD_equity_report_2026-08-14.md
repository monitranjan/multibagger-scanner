### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Pristine B2B plain-gold jewellery franchise with export-heavy mix:** Shanti Gold is one of the larger organised plain gold jewellery manufacturers in India, with an export-tilted revenue profile serving marquee retail chains across the Middle East, UK, and select Asian markets[^ip-latest]. The company's order book visibility from long-tenured B2B customers (3–10 year relationships with leading regional retail brands and bullion houses) provides stickiness that retail-led peers do not enjoy[^cc-transcript].

- **Operating leverage + backward integration driving structural margin expansion:** The company has steadily in-sourced activities earlier done via job-work (casting, finishing, chain-making) and is scaling in-house capacity, which management has guided will deliver 100–150 bps of EBITDA margin expansion over FY26E–FY28E[^cc-transcript]. Backward integration into gold bar melting and refining further compresses material cost, and the gold-on-lease model minimises working capital strain while capturing bullion price upside[^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification from plain-gold into studded, lab-grown, and silver jewellery:** Beyond its core plain-gold anchor (~85–90% of FY25 revenue), Shanti Gold is building adjacent verticals — diamond-studded and CZ-studded jewellery, lab-grown diamond (LGD) jewellery, and lightweight daily-wear silver — which together already contribute ~10–12% of revenue and are growing 30%+ YoY[^ip-latest]. The LGD pivot is structurally important: LGD jewellery carries 400–500 bps higher gross margins than mined-diamond equivalents and is a US/UK growth vector[^cc-transcript].

- **Near-term catalysts (next 6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results (already announced 13-Aug-2026) showing post-IPO capacity ramp; (ii) commissioning of the new state-of-the-art manufacturing facility (targeted H2 FY27) which will add ~40–50% to processing capacity[^ip-latest]; (iii) new customer additions in the US/UK markets and at least 2–3 marquee Indian retail chain wins expected by Q3 FY27[^cc-transcript]; (iv) potential initiation of dividends/buybacks given strong FCF and 74.89% promoter holding creates signalling value[^ar-fy25].

- **Biggest structural risk — gold price volatility and policy/discount dependence:** A ~5% adverse move in international gold prices can swing realisations and gross margins by 80–120 bps in a single quarter, and ~55–60% of revenue is export-oriented where USD/INR and import duty changes (currently 15% standard + Social Welfare Surcharge on gold) directly impact landed cost competitiveness[^ar-fy25]. Additionally, the company's customer concentration (top 10 customers ~45–50% of revenue) and exposure to discretionary demand in GCC/UK creates cyclicality that the bull case must discount[^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Shanti Gold International Ltd is a B2B plain gold jewellery manufacturer and exporter with a 25+ year operating track record, headquartered in Mumbai with manufacturing concentrated in Maharashtra and Gujarat[^ip-latest]. The company operates an asset-light, high-velocity model — converting bullion into finished chain, bangle, necklace, and tola-based jewellery primarily for export markets and large Indian organised retailers[^ar-fy25].

- **Domestic (~40–45% of revenue):** Pan-India organised retail chains, large-format jewellery showrooms, and select D2C/private-label programmes

**Manufacturing footprint:**[^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary structure:** Limited subsidiary footprint; primarily parent-level operations with a few step-down entities for export market servicing and LGD sourcing — to be confirmed in detailed subsidiary schedule[^ar-fy25]

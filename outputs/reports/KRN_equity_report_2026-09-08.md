### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Dominant Niche Leadership with High Switching Costs:** KRN has established a near-monopoly position in India for customized **Fin & Tube** (used in commercial ACs, chillers, data centre cooling) and **Bar & Plate** heat exchangers (used in heavy vehicles, railways, defence, compressors). Unlike commodity pipe-makers, KRN acts as a **thermal design partner** — its in-house engineering team co-develops proprietary coil geometries with OEMs (Original Equipment Manufacturers) like Blue Star, Voltas, Carrier, and Indian Railways. Once a design is qualified (tested for pressure drop, heat transfer efficiency, vibration resistance), the OEM rarely switches vendors because requalification takes 12–18 months and risks system performance. This **“design-in” moat** creates high switching costs (rated **Strong**) and allows KRN to command premium pricing over generic traders[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

*   **Structural Margin Expansion via Product Mix Shift & Operating Leverage:** The company is deliberately moving up the value chain from component supplier (bare coils) to **system integrator** (complete HVAC units, Bus AC kits, Adiabatic Coolers). System-level products carry 200–300 bps higher EBITDA margins than components because they bundle design, sheet metal fabrication, piping, and controls — all now done in-house. The new **Plant II (8.4 lakh sq ft, commissioned May 2025)** provides a 6x capacity jump (to ~6 mn units/yr) with high automation (robotic brazing, fin presses). As utilization climbs from current ~30% to 60%+ over FY27–FY28, fixed cost absorption (depreciation, rent, overheads) will drive **operating leverage**, potentially lifting consolidated EBITDA margin from ~19% (FY26) to 22–23%+[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (Bus AC, Data Centres, Exports):** Three structural growth vectors are opening simultaneously: (1) **Bus AC Systems** — acquired Sphere Refrigeration’s bus AC division in FY26, instantly gaining access to India’s ~₹1,000 Cr electric bus AC market (Olectra, Switch Mobility, PMI Electro) and a product portfolio (roof-mounted, split, electric compressor-driven units) that is now being cross-sold to KRN’s existing railway/industrial customers[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]. (2) **Data Centre Cooling (PAC/CRAC Units)** — leveraging Fin & Tube expertise to build Precision Air Conditioning units for server rooms; a market growing at 25%+ CAGR in India with high entry barriers (redundancy, tight temperature control)[^ip-latest]. (3) **Export Scale-up** — Q1 FY27 exports surged **177% YoY** to ~21% of revenue, led by USA (32% of exports), France, UAE, Italy. The company is qualifying with global Tier-1 HVAC OEMs (names confidential) for heat exchanger kits, moving from opportunistic exports to **multi-year supply agreements**[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript].

*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (a) **PLI (Production Linked Incentive) Approval** — application submitted under the White Goods/Components scheme; approval would unlock 4–6% incentive on incremental sales for 5 years, directly boosting PAT[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]. (b) **Plant II Ramp-Up** — commercial production started H1 FY27; each 10% utilization increment adds ~₹60 Cr revenue at near-incremental margins[^ar-fy25]. (c) **Bus AC Order Book Conversion** — first major OEM supply commenced Q1 FY27; management guided ₹100–150 Cr revenue from this segment in FY27[^cc-transcript]. (d) **QIP Proceeds Deployment** — ₹350 Cr raised (ADIA-backed) in FY26 fully funded Plant II capex (₹300 Cr) and working capital; zero debt-funded expansion risk[^ar-fy25].

*   **Biggest Structural Risk — Commodity Volatility & Capacity Utilization:** Copper and Aluminium constitute **60–65% of raw material cost**. While KRN has pass-through clauses with most large OEMs (price adjusted quarterly based on LME), there is a **1–2 quarter lag** and smaller customers may resist full pass-through, compressing margins during sharp commodity spikes (e.g., Cu >$10,000/t). Simultaneously, the **6x capacity jump** creates a binary risk: if order inflow (especially exports & Bus AC) delays by 2–3 quarters, fixed cost burden (depreciation ~₹40–50 Cr/yr on Plant II) will depress ROCE. Working capital intensity is also rising (inventory days up from 45 to 80+ days) as the company stocks 2.5 months of copper/aluminium to ensure delivery reliability — this locks up capital and exposes inventory to price declines[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** KRN is a **thermal management solutions provider** — it designs, engineers, and manufactures heat exchangers (devices that transfer heat between two fluids without mixing them) and complete HVAC (Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning) systems. Think of it as the “radiator specialist” for everything that generates heat and needs cooling: commercial buildings, factories, trains, buses, data centres, and refrigerators. The company operates on a **B2B (Business-to-Business) model** — selling to OEMs who integrate KRN’s coils/units into their final machines (e.g., a Blue Star chiller, an Olectra electric bus, a Carrier AHU).

**Revenue Split by Division (Estimated FY26 Consolidated):**
*   **Fin & Tube Heat Exchangers (~45%):** Condenser/evaporator coils for chillers, VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow), package ACs, data centre PAC units. High customization, recurring orders.
*   **Bar & Plate Heat Exchangers (~20%):** Charge air coolers, radiators, oil coolers for railways (locomotives, metro), defence vehicles, compressors, industrial hydraulics. Higher engineering intensity, better margins.
*   **Complete HVAC Systems (~15%):** Adiabatic coolers, dry coolers, remote radiators, air handling units (AHUs) — sold as plug-and-play modules to EPC contractors and end-users.
*   **Refrigeration & Refrigerator Components (~10%):** Wire-on-tube condensers (back of fridge), frost-free evaporators, ammonia evaporators for cold storage. Steady cash cow.
*   **Bus AC Systems (~5–7% - New):** Roof-mounted & split AC kits for electric/diesel buses. High growth, system-level margins.
*   **Sheet Metal, Tubings & Child Parts (~5%):** Backward integration — headers, inner-grooved tubes, finned tubes, structural frames. Captive consumption + external sales.

**Key OEM / End Customers (Representative):**

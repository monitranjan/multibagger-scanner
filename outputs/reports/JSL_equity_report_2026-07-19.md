## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Structural Moat:** JSL is India's largest stainless steel manufacturer with a consolidated capacity of ~3.4 MTPA post-JSAL merger (Jindal Stainless (Hisar) absorbed in FY24), giving it ~40% domestic market share. The asset base at Jajpur (Odisha) — a 2.9 MTPA single-location integrated complex — creates scale economics that no domestic peer (SAIL, Viraj Profiles, Salem Steel) can replicate. The Indian stainless steel market is structurally consolidated around JSL and a fragmented long tail, supporting pricing discipline.

- **Structural Margin Expansion Story:** EBITDA/ton has structurally migrated from Rs. 8,000–10,000 in FY21 to Rs. 18,000–22,000 in FY25, driven by (i) value-mix shift toward 200/300/400 series, (ii) captive nickel pig iron sourcing reducing raw material volatility, and (iii) operating leverage at Jajpur. We model 200–300 bps further margin expansion through FY27E as the 200-series cold-rolled capacity debottlenecks and ferro-chrome integration (subsidiary Jindal Steele) ramps up.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Three growth vectors are now inflecting — (a) **Defence-grade stainless** for artillery and naval applications (MoD order book visibility), (b) **Railways** (LHB coaches, metro coaches, Vande Bharat platform under-slung components) where JSL has ~55% wallet share with IR, and (c) **EV battery enclosures / battery casings** — early commercial wins with two-wheeler OEMs. These adjacencies together represent a Rs. 15,000–20,000 Cr TAM by FY30E.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Resolution of the Jajpur Phase-2 brownfield expansion (additional 0.4 MTPA) — capex sanction expected Q2FY27, (ii) Sharp recovery in nickel prices translating to inventory gains, (iii) Sustained China export curbs (anti-dumping duties in EU/US) redirecting flow into India, (iv) Q1FY27 results — likely strong on the back of INR 15,000+ Cr revenue run-rate, (v) Likely re-rating as MSCI EM weight upgrade post-merger clean-up.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Demand cyclicality tied to capex cycles in autos, real estate (architecture & building) and consumer durables — these four end-uses contribute ~70% of domestic stainless demand. A sharp Chinese export surge (post-property-sector deleveraging) remains the single largest risk to spreads. Steel is a globally traded commodity; JSL cannot insulate from a 15–20% Chinese price dump.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

The company sells to B2B industrial customers and is NOT exposed to retail consumers directly.

**Industry CAGR:** India stainless steel consumption has compounded at ~13% CAGR over FY20–FY25, materially ahead of global growth of ~3–4%. This is driven by substitution of carbon steel, growth in auto, railways and consumer durables. We expect a moderated 9–11% CAGR FY25–FY30E as the base effect kicks in.

**Relevant Policy Tailwinds:**

- **PLI Scheme for Specialty Steel:** JSL's Hisar and Jajpur facilities have qualified for the Rs. 6,322 Cr specialty steel PLI — capex outlay sanctioned at Rs. 1,555 Cr, with 25% capex subsidy inflow expected FY27–FY29.
- **Anti-Dumping Duties:** India imposed ADD on Chinese stainless flat products in 2022–23, retained through 2027. EU and US ADDs on Chinese 304/316 further support domestic pricing.
- **FAME-III / EV Push:** Government target of 30% EV penetration by 2030 — JSL is a key beneficiary via battery enclosures and precision tubes for EV drivetrains.
- **Defence Indigenisation (Positive Indigenisation List):** Imports banned across multiple weapon platforms — direct beneficiary for JSL's 310S, 321, 347 grades.
- **Capex on Railways:** 4,500+ Vande Bharat, 12,000+ LHB coaches, 1,000+ km of metro by FY30E — JSL is the dominant domestic supplier of 400-series rails and under-slung structural members.
- **Urban Infra / Smart Cities:** PEB buildings, metro stations, airport facades — all consume 316L/304 stainless in structural and aesthetic applications.

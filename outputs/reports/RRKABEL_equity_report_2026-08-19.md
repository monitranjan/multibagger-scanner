### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Expanding Moat**: RR Kabel has cemented its position as the #3 player in the Indian organized wires & cables industry, with an estimated market share of 12-15% by value. The company's competitive moat is anchored in its extensive distribution network spanning over 1.5 lakh retail touchpoints across India, with North and West regions contributing ~65% of domestic revenue [^cc-transcript]. The company's ability to grow W&C volumes at ~18% YoY—significantly ahead of the industry's 12-15% growth rate—demonstrates sustained market share gains, particularly in the higher-margin cables (B2B) segment where it has historically been underrepresented versus Polycab and KEI [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]. The ongoing Rs. 1,200 Cr Project RRise capex program, with ~80% allocated to cable capacity expansion, is expected to further solidify its competitive position and address the current ~90% cable capacity utilization bottleneck [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Expansion Story**: RR Kabel is in the midst of a multi-year margin expansion cycle that is fundamentally altering its earnings profile. Consolidated EBITDA margins expanded from 6.4% in FY25 to 8.1% in FY26, and further to 9.0% in Q1 FY27—a 205 bps YoY expansion [^ip-latest]. The Wires & Cables segment margin improved to 9.9% in Q1 FY27 (vs 7.6% in Q1 FY26), driven by a combination of scale benefits, improved product mix (shift towards higher-margin cables), and disciplined commodity management [^cc-transcript]. Management has maintained its FY28 EBITDA margin guidance of 10.5% for the W&C segment, implying further 56 bps expansion from current levels [^cc-transcript]. This margin trajectory is supported by operating leverage as cable capacity utilization improves and new capacities come online, with management explicitly attributing margin improvement to "scale benefits, better cost absorption, and improved product mix" [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies**: The FMEG (Fast Moving Electrical Goods) segment, contributing 9% of revenue, achieved a significant milestone by reaching operational breakeven in Q1 FY27—a dramatic turnaround from the Rs. 71 Mn loss in Q1 FY26 [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]. Management is targeting annual breakeven for FY27 and sustainable profitability within 2-3 years, with a revenue growth target of ~20% [^cc-transcript]. The segment is expanding into kitchen appliances (mixer grinders, cooktops) and premium products under the "RR Signature" portfolio, with premium products contributing 25% of FMEG revenue [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25]. Additionally, the export business (29% of revenue) is diversifying beyond the Middle East into Europe and other markets, providing a hedge against geopolitical disruptions and positioning the company to benefit from a prospective EU-India trade agreement that could boost export competitiveness [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 Months)**: Several catalysts are expected to drive re-rating in the next 6-12 months: (a) Capacity expansion at Silvassa (wires) and Waghodia (cables) coming online in Q2/Q3 FY27, with ~80% of the Rs. 600-650 Cr FY27 capex allocated to the cable side; (b) FMEG segment expected to achieve annual breakeven in FY27, validating the turnaround thesis; (c) Data center and renewable energy opportunities, where RR Kabel currently has "very less" share but significant runway as hyperscalers expand capacity in India; (d) Potential benefits from EU-India trade agreement boosting export competitiveness; (e) Continued market share gains from the unorganized-to-organized shift, which management estimates at 2-3% annually [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25]. The stock's proximity to its 52-week high of Rs. 2,920.90 suggests strong momentum, with technical support at the 50-DMA [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk**: The most significant structural risk is the company's contingent liability book of Rs. 13,643 Cr, which represents approximately 53% of net worth [^ar-fy25]. This comprises primarily disputed tax demands—Rs. 10,530 Cr under Income Tax and Rs. 7,306 Cr under GST [^ar-fy25]. While these are disputed and not admitted liabilities, the sheer magnitude warrants close monitoring and could create overhang in adverse scenarios. Additionally, commodity price volatility (copper and aluminum) remains a persistent risk, though management has demonstrated disciplined commodity management and timely pricing decisions to mitigate margin impact [^cc-transcript]. The leadership transition, while smooth, introduces execution risk as the second generation (Mahhesh and Rajesh Kabra as Joint MDs effective June 2026) takes charge [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

RR Kabel Limited is one of India's leading manufacturers of wires and cables, with a growing presence in the Fast Moving Electrical Goods (FMEG) segment. The company operates through two main business divisions that together generated consolidated revenue of Rs. 31,682 Mn in Q1 FY27—the highest ever quarterly revenue in the company's history [^ip-latest].

**Wires & Cables (W&C)**: The core business, contributing 91% of consolidated revenue in Q1 FY27 (Rs. 2,880 Cr), has demonstrated exceptional growth with revenue increasing 57% YoY, driven by 17% YoY volume growth [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]. Within W&C, cables grew at over 25% YoY by volume, while wires grew at approximately 12% YoY, reflecting the company's strategic pivot towards higher-margin B2B cables [^cc-transcript]. The segment serves both B2C (distribution channel) and B2B (industrial, power, infrastructure) customers. The company recently launched high-voltage cables up to 66kV, expanding its addressable market in the power transmission and distribution segment [^ar-fy25]. The W&C segment achieved a segment profit margin of 9.9% in Q1 FY27, expanding 232 bps YoY from 7.6% [^ip-latest].

**FMEG**: Contributing 9% of revenue (Rs. 288 Cr in Q1 FY27), this segment achieved operational breakeven in Q1 FY27—a significant turnaround from the Rs. 71 Mn loss in Q1 FY26 [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]. The segment manufactures and sells fans, lighting, switches, and kitchen appliances (mixer grinders, cooktops). The manufacturing model is asset-light, with approximately 1/3 in-house and 2/3 outsourced, allowing for capital efficiency [^cc-transcript]. Premium products under the "RR Signature" portfolio contribute 25% of FMEG revenue, indicating successful premiumization [^cc-transcript]. Management has guided for ~20% revenue growth and sustainable profitability within 2-3 years, with FY27 targeted as the year for annual breakeven achievement [^cc-transcript].

**Revenue Mix (Q1 FY27)**:
- By Business Segment: Wires & Cables 91%, FMEG 9% [^ip-latest]
- By Geography: Domestic 71%, Exports 29% [^ip-latest]

**Key Customers & Distribution**: The company serves over 1.5 lakh retail touchpoints through its extensive distribution network, with North and West India contributing ~65% of domestic revenue [^cc-transcript]. Management is focused on "expanding our distribution reach and deepening our engagement with dealers and distributors" with a focus on increasing depth and revenue per dealer rather than just adding touchpoints [^cc-transcript]. In the B2B segment, key customers include power utilities, infrastructure companies, industrial users, and data center operators. The company has a joint venture—RR-Imperial—for rubber cables, which serves as a related party transaction conducted at arm's length [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint**: RR Kabel operates manufacturing facilities across India, with key plants at Silvassa (wires) and Waghodia (cables) being the primary expansion sites under Project RRise [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25]. The company is undertaking a Rs. 1,200 Cr capex program over FY26-FY28, with Rs. 300 Cr deployed in FY26 and Rs. 600-650 Cr planned for FY27 [^cc-transcript]. Current capacity utilization stands at ~65-70% for wires and ~90% for cables, indicating that cable capacity expansion is the primary focus (~80% of capex) to address the bottleneck [^cc-transcript]. The modular expansion strategy aligns capacity additions with demand, reducing execution risk [^ar-fy25].

**Promoter Background & Group Context**: The company was founded by Tribhuvanprasad Kabra and Shreegopal Kabra, who have recently retired after establishing RR Kabel as a significant player in the Indian cables industry [^ar-fy25]. The second generation—Mahendrakumar Kabra (MD), Mahhesh Kabra, and Rajesh Kabra (Joint MDs effective June 2026)—has taken charge, ensuring continuity of strategy with refreshed execution focus [^ar-fy25]. The promoter group holds 61.38% of shares as of June 2026, with no pledging disclosed, indicating strong promoter confidence and alignment with minority shareholders [^ar-fy25]. The leadership transition has been smooth, with the new management articulating a clear "Project RRise" roadmap for margin expansion and B2B pivot under the ethos of "Powering the Next Phase" [^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary Structure**: The company has a joint venture—RR-Imperial—for rubber cables, which is consolidated in the financials and operates as a related party transaction at arm's length [^ar-fy25]. The company also has international subsidiaries for export operations, supporting the 29% export revenue contribution [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]. Guarantees provided by promoters for company borrowings have been noted in the annual report, which is standard practice but worth monitoring [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 8 — VALUATION

**Valuation Methodology Note (Sector-Specific):** R R Kabel operates in the **Cables - Power** sector, where valuation is anchored on **EV/EBITDA** (capital-intensive, project-tender driven, regulated returns) and **P/B** (asset-heavy balance sheet, ROCE-linked re-rating).

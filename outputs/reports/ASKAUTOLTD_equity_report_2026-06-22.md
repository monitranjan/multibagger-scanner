### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Two-Wheeler Braking Systems:** ASK Automotive is India's largest manufacturer of Advanced Braking Systems (ABS) for two-wheelers, holding a dominant market share of ~50% in the friction material segment (brake shoes, brake pads) and ~25% in the aluminium light-weighting segment (die-casting components). This leadership is underpinned by strong R&D capabilities, long-standing relationships with major OEMs like Hero MotoCorp, Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India (HMSI), TVS Motor, and Bajaj Auto, and a cost-efficient manufacturing base, creating significant entry barriers for new players.
*   **Structural Margin Improvement Driven by Premiumization & EV Shift:** The company is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-margin offerings such as advanced brake pads and lightweight aluminium components. The increasing penetration of disc brakes and ABS in two-wheelers, coupled with the rising demand for EV-specific components (e.g., motor housings, battery casings), is expected to drive a structural improvement in EBITDA margins by 150-200 bps over the next 3-4 years.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies and Aftermarket:** ASK Automotive is expanding its product portfolio beyond two-wheelers into electric vehicles (EVs) across two-wheelers and passenger vehicles, as well as the rapidly growing aftermarket segment. The aftermarket division, which offers higher margins, is a key focus area, with plans to increase its contribution to overall revenue from current ~12% to 18-20% by FY27E, leveraging its established brand and distribution network.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the successful ramp-up of new capacities for EV components, particularly for new OEM mandates in the e-2W and e-3W segments, and the potential for increased content per vehicle due to stricter safety regulations (e.g., mandatory ABS/CBS implementation). Furthermore, strong demand from key OEM partners, driven by a recovery in rural consumption and new model launches, will bolster order books and revenue growth.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is the high dependence on the Indian two-wheeler industry, which is susceptible to cyclical downturns, changes in consumer preferences, and regulatory shifts (e.g., abrupt changes in emission norms or EV subsidy policies). While diversification efforts are underway, a significant slowdown in the domestic two-wheeler market could materially impact ASK Automotive's revenue growth and profitability.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

ASK Automotive Ltd is a leading manufacturer of advanced braking systems and aluminium die-casting components for the automotive industry, primarily catering to the two-wheeler segment in India.

*   **Core Business Model:** The company operates on an OEM-centric model, designing, developing, and manufacturing critical safety and lightweighting components in close collaboration with major automotive original equipment manufacturers (OEMs). It also has a significant presence in the independent aftermarket (IAM) channel.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   **Advanced Braking Systems (ABS):** ~60% of revenue (includes brake shoes, brake pads, clutch plates, and other friction materials).
    *   **Aluminium Lightweighting Solutions:** ~28% of revenue (includes aluminium die-casting components like wheel hubs, engine casings, motor housings, and battery casings).
    *   **Aftermarket:** ~12% of revenue (servicing replacement demand for its products across both braking and aluminium components).
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Advanced Braking Systems:** Hero MotoCorp, Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India (HMSI), TVS Motor Company, Bajaj Auto, Suzuki Motorcycle India, Yamaha Motor India, Royal Enfield.
    *   **Aluminium Lightweighting Solutions:** Hero MotoCorp, HMSI, TVS Motor Company, Bajaj Auto, Suzuki Motorcycle India, and emerging EV players for specific e-motor and battery components.
    *   **Aftermarket:** Caters to a pan-India network of distributors and retailers.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** The company operates primarily as a standalone entity with no significant wholly-owned subsidiaries impacting core operations. All manufacturing and sales are consolidated under ASK Automotive Ltd.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** ASK Automotive has a robust manufacturing footprint across India, with 16 strategically located manufacturing facilities.
    *   **States:** Haryana (multiple plants in Manesar, Bahadurgarh), Rajasthan (Bhiwadi), Uttarakhand (Pantnagar), Gujarat (Sanand), Karnataka (Hosur).
    *   **Plant Count:** 16 manufacturing facilities as of FY24.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** The company was founded by Mr. Kuldip Singh Rathee and Mr. Vijay Rathee in 1989. The promoters have over three decades of experience in the automotive components industry and have successfully scaled the business from a small-scale friction material manufacturer to a market leader in critical two-wheeler components. The Rathee family continues to hold a significant majority stake and plays an active role in the strategic direction and day-to-day operations of the company.

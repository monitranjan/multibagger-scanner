### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership with structural moat:** Eternal operates India's #1 food delivery platform (Zomato) and #1 quick commerce platform (Blinkit), holding ~57% food delivery GOV share and a clear #1 position in the 10-minute grocery delivery category (Blinkit) [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest]. Network density of ~2.3 lakh active restaurant partners and 7.5 lakh+ delivery riders creates a two-sided flywheel that competitors (Swiggy, Zepto, Instamart) cannot easily replicate at this scale [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript].

- **Structural margin story taking shape:** Contribution margin in food delivery has expanded from 2.2% in FY23 to 4.1% in FY25, with management guiding to 5-6% by FY27E and double-digits longer-term, driven by last-mile delivery cost rationalization, advertising-led AOV expansion, and lower delivery fees per order [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25]. Blinkit's contribution margin turned positive (~1.4% in Q4FY26) — a critical inflection that we believe re-rates the quick commerce vertical from cash-burn to cash-generation by FY28E [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond core food and grocery, Eternal is building out three newer verticals — (i) going-out (District by Zomato), (ii) B2B supplies (Hyperpure), and (iii) the recently launched "Eternal" cross-category loyalty/membership layer — each of which can meaningfully expand the platform's GMV surface area and improve blended take-rates [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]. Going-out and B2B together contributed ~5% of consolidated GOV in FY26 but are growing at 60%+ YoY.

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (a) Q1FY27 results (already out, in line), (b) Blinkit store target ramp from 1,800+ to 2,500 stores by FY27 end driving GOV share gains vs. Zepto, (c) expected contribution margin breakeven at the consolidated level in FY27 (vs. -1.4% in FY25) — a key positive EPS inflection trigger [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread], (d) potential value unlock from any future vertical spin-off/listing of Blinkit or District at scale.

- **Biggest structural risk:** Regulatory tightening on platform commissions/restaurant pricing (a Karnataka-style commission cap is the canonical risk) and intensified quick commerce competition compressing Blinkit's contribution margin trajectory. Food delivery gross order value growth has also decelerated to ~17% YoY in Q1FY27 from ~22% in FY25, suggesting the category is entering a maturing growth phase [^vp-thread] [^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Eternal Ltd (formerly Zomato Ltd) is a pure-play, India-focused internet commerce platform company operating in food delivery, quick commerce, going-out, and B2B supplies. The business is asset-light (3PL logistics + dark store leasing model) and platform-based, with revenue generated via (a) commissions from restaurant partners, (b) customer delivery fees, (c) advertising income from restaurant listings and branded shelf placements, and (d) merchandise sales (Blinkit private label + inventory-led categories) [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest].

*Source: Annual Report FY25-26, Investor Presentation Q1FY27, Concall transcript Q1FY27* [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]

**Subsidiary Structure:** Eternal operates via key wholly-owned subsidiaries — Zomato Ltd (food delivery), Blink Commerce Pvt Ltd (Blinkit quick commerce), Zomato Hyperpure Pvt Ltd (B2B supplies), Zomato Entertainment Pvt Ltd (District going-out), and Zomato Payments Pvt Ltd (the recently operationalised UPI/on-platform payments entity). Cross-holding structure is wholly consolidated at the Eternal parent level [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest].

**Manufacturing / Operational Footprint:** As a digital platform, Eternal has no plants. Operational footprint comprises: (i) ~2,000+ active city/town presence for food delivery, (ii) 1,800+ Blinkit dark stores across India (concentrated in metros/Tier-1, expanding to Tier-2), (iii) 20+ Hyperpure warehouse hubs, and (iv) corporate offices in Gurugram (HQ), Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Pune [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest].

**Promoter / Group Background:** Eternal was founded in 2008 by Deepinder Goyal and Pankaj Chaddah, with Mr. Goyal serving as Chairman & CEO. The company is professionally run under a public-market governance framework post the 2021 IPO. Promoter (founder + related) holding stood at 23.40% as of 30-Jun-2026; there is no traditional industrial group/promoter entity. Significant institutional holders include Sequoia (now Peak XV), Ant Financial, and Baillie Gifford (historic), with the current cap table dominated by domestic mutual funds and FPIs [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest].

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**TAM and Industry Context:**
- India online food delivery TAM: ~Rs. 2,20,000 Cr (US$26 Bn) by FY28, growing at ~22% CAGR (FY25-FY28E) — driven by rising smartphone penetration, urbanisation, and restaurant digitisation [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest].
- India quick commerce TAM: ~Rs. 75,000 Cr (US$9 Bn) by FY28, growing at ~35% CAGR (FY25-FY28E) — the fastest-growing segment within Indian internet commerce [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript].
- Combined "food + grocery delivery" TAM addressable for Eternal: ~Rs. 3,00,000 Cr by FY28E.

**Policy Tailwinds:**
- (i) **Open Network for Digital Commerce (ONDC)** — net positive for the sector as it expands merchant addressable base; Eternal is a participating app [^vp-thread].
- (ii) **UPI/Zero-MDR architecture** — keeps payment costs near-zero for the platform, a structural margin tailwind.
- (iii) **GST clarity on restaurant services (post 2022)** — stabilised the tax treatment and reduced compliance friction.
- (iv) **No PLI/FAME** applicable for this sector (consumption/internet) [^vp-thread] [^cc-transcript].

**Competitive Moat Analysis:**

| Scale (delivery riders) | Strong | ~7.5 lakh rider network; rebalancing density advantage difficult to replicate [^ip-latest] |

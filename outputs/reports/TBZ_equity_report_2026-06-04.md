### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Tribhovandas Bhimji Zaveri Limited (TBZ) is one of India's oldest and most renowned jewellery retailers, with a legacy spanning over 160 years. The company designs, manufactures, and retails a wide range of gold, diamond, and precious stone-studded jewellery. Its core business model revolves around retail sales through its network of showrooms.

*   **Core Business Model**: TBZ operates on a retail-focused model, sourcing raw gold and diamonds, designing and manufacturing jewellery (partially in-house, partially outsourced), and selling directly to end-consumers through its showrooms. The company emphasizes craftsmanship, purity, and trust, which are critical in the Indian jewellery market.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A, indicative)**:
    *   Gold Jewellery: ~85-90% of revenue
    *   Diamond & Studded Jewellery: ~10-15% of revenue
    *   Precious Stones & Other: <1% of revenue
    (Note: Exact split not consistently disclosed; based on industry averages and company commentary)
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division**: As a B2C retailer, TBZ's end-customers are individual consumers. For sourcing, it procures gold from various domestic and international bullion banks and suppliers, and diamonds from established cutters and polishers.
*   **Subsidiary Structure**: TBZ operates primarily as a standalone entity with no significant listed subsidiaries.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint**: TBZ has in-house design and manufacturing capabilities, complemented by outsourced manufacturing to specialized karigars (artisans). Its primary manufacturing facilities are located in Maharashtra, supporting its retail operations across India.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context**: The company was founded in 1864 by Tribhovandas Bhimji Zaveri. The current management, led by Mr. Shrikant Zaveri (Chairman & Whole-Time Director), represents the fifth generation of the founding family. The Zaveri family has a long-standing history in the jewellery business, renowned for its commitment to quality and ethical practices. TBZ operates as a distinct listed entity with a clear focus on its retail jewellery business.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

* **Market Leadership in 2W/3W:** CEAT maintains a dominant position in the 2W/3W replacement market, leveraging a strong brand recall and a vast distribution network of ~5,000+ dealers, which provides a defensive moat against cyclical downturns.
* **Structural Margin Expansion:** Improved product mix toward high-margin SUV/PCR (Passenger Car Radial) segments and consistent cost-optimization initiatives (digitization of supply chain) are expected to sustain EBITDA margins in the 12-14% range, despite volatile rubber prices.
* **High-Growth Adjacencies:** Strategic pivot toward the OHT (Off-Highway Tire) segment, particularly for exports to North America and Europe, acts as a high-margin growth engine, de-risking the company from purely domestic 2W/3W demand volatility.
* **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** Improving replacement demand cycle, cooling raw material (crude oil/natural rubber) input costs, and increased capacity utilization at the Chennai plant are set to drive operating leverage in FY27E.
* **Structural Risk:** High dependence on the domestic 2W market and stiff competition from tier-1 players (MRF, Apollo) and low-cost imports/unorganized sector players pose risks to pricing power and market share.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

* **Business Model:** CEAT operates as a diversified tire manufacturer with a focus on the replacement market (approx. 65-70% of revenue), which offers better margins compared to the OEM segment.
* **Revenue Split:** 2W/3W (~45%), Passenger Car/SUV (~30%), Truck/Bus (~15%), OHT/Specialty (~10%).
* **Manufacturing Footprint:** Operates 6 major manufacturing facilities located in Bhandup, Nashik, Halol, Nagpur, Ambernath, and Chennai.
* **Promoter/Group:** Part of the RPG Group, led by Harsh Goenka. The group has a multi-decade legacy in Indian manufacturing, ensuring stable governance and long-term capital allocation strategies.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership in Optical Fiber Cable (OFC):** HFCL is one of India's largest indigenous OFC manufacturers with multi-million fkm annual capacity, positioning it as a primary beneficiary of BharatNet Phase-III, 5G backhaul build-out, and surging global OFC demand[^ip-latest]. Validation of global competitiveness came via the recent export order win of USD 54.81 million (~Rs. 522.73 Cr) from international customers[^ip-latest].

- **Structural Margin Expansion via Backward Integration:** HFCL's captive optical fiber manufacturing insulates margins from volatile global fiber prices (fiber constitutes 40-50% of raw material mix)[^cc-transcript]. EBITDA margins have expanded from 8.3% in Q1FY20 to ~14% levels, with the company buying fiber at $3.8/fkm vs peak of $8+/fkm, demonstrating pricing power through the cycle[^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Beyond core OFC, HFCL is scaling defense electronics (via HFCL Technologies subsidiary), railway signaling, private 5G networks, and data center connectivity solutions[^ip-latest]. This diversification reduces dependence on cyclical telecom capex and opens higher-margin revenue streams.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Rs. 400 Cr OFC capacity expansion approved by Board on 04 Aug 2026, funded via internal accruals + debt[^ip-latest]; (b) Strong order book visibility historically at Rs. 10,250 Cr levels with consistent inflow[^vp-thread]; (c) Acceleration of 5G rollout and BharatNet Phase-III execution; (d) Export order momentum with USD 54.81 Mn recent win signaling international traction[^ip-latest].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** High working capital intensity and concentration in government/PSU orders (BSNL, Railways, Defense) creates receivables risk and execution dependency[^vp-thread]. Legacy governance overhang from Ketan Parekh-era associations continues to weigh on sentiment, with periodic Moneylife articles flagging irregularities[^vp-thread]. Additionally, rich valuations at 59.90x P/E TTM leave limited margin of safety if order execution or margin trajectory disappoints.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

HFCL Limited (formerly Himachal Futuristic Communications Limited) is a leading Indian telecom equipment manufacturer with a diversified portfolio spanning optical fiber cables (OFC), optical fiber, telecom transmission/access equipment, and turnkey network services[^ip-latest].

**Revenue Mix (Estimated FY25):**
- Optical Fiber Cable (OFC): ~45-50% of revenue
- Telecom Equipment (Transmission/Access/Terminal): ~25-30%
- Turnkey Network Services: ~15-20%
- Defense Electronics & Others: ~5-10%[^ip-latest]

**Key OEM/End Customers by Division:**
- **OFC Division:** BSNL, Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel, Vodafone Idea, international telcos and export customers[^ip-latest]
- **Telecom Equipment:** Public sector telcos (BSNL/MTNL), private operators, enterprise customers[^cc-transcript]
- **Turnkey Services:** BSNL, MTNL, defense establishments, large multinationals[^vp-thread]
- **Defense:** Indian Army, DRDO labs, defense PSUs via HFCL Technologies subsidiary[^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary Structure:**

**Manufacturing Footprint:**

- Additional capacity expansion underway via Rs. 400 Cr capex announced Aug 2026[^ip-latest]
- Registered office recently shifted to Gurugram, Haryana (July 2026) following regulatory approval[^ip-latest]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**

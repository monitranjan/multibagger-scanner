### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche US-anchored engineering services moat:** Mold-tek Technologies is one of the few Indian-listed pure-play structural steel detailing and engineering services firms with entrenched Tier-1 US relationships, strengthened by the acquisitions of RMM Global and Crossroads Detailing — providing direct client access and offshore delivery leverage that is difficult for greenfield competitors to replicate at speed [^vp-thread].

- **Structural margin expansion story:** Revenue mix is shifting from low-margin detailing-only contracts toward higher-value design-consultancy and large-ticket project execution (e.g., Denver Airport extension, Amazon CVG Hub at Kentucky, 75-storey Oceanwide Center), which should drive EBITDA margin expansion of 200–300 bps over FY26E–FY28E as fixed-cost India delivery centres are better utilised [^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond the core Civil-Structural vertical, the Mechanical Engineering division is gaining traction with US industrial clients, while selective IT services work continues with existing customers — providing optionality without capital drain, as management has explicitly de-prioritised aggressive IT hiring [^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results (already announced 06 Aug 2026) showing sustained growth momentum; (ii) ramp-up of US infrastructure spending under the Infrastructure Investment & Jobs Act; (iii) potential new large-ticket project wins in US data-centre and airport segments; (iv) possible re-rating as liquidity improves and SEBI GSM overhang is monitored [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk:** SEBI Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) listing remains an overhang on institutional participation — reflected in 0.00% FII and 0.00% DII holdings — creating persistent liquidity discount and limiting float-driven re-rating potential until surveillance status is reviewed [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
Mold-tek Technologies operates as a pure-play engineering services outsourcing firm, delivering design, detailing, and consultancy solutions primarily to US-based structural steel fabricators, EPC contractors, and construction clients. The company leverages India-based delivery centres to provide cost-arbitraged, high-precision engineering output [^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (estimated, FY25):**
- Civil-Structural Engineering Services: ~70% of revenue (steel detailing, design-consultancy, large-ticket project subcontracts) [^vp-thread]
- Mechanical Engineering Services: ~25% of revenue (industrial machinery, product design) [^vp-thread]
- IT Services: ~5% of revenue (servicing existing captive/long-term clients only — no aggressive hiring planned) [^vp-thread]

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- Civil-Structural: US Tier-1 steel fabricators and EPC contractors; marquee projects include Denver Airport extension, Amazon CVG Hub (Kentucky), and the 75-storey Oceanwide Center [^vp-thread]
- Mechanical: US industrial OEMs and machinery manufacturers (specific names not disclosed in public domain) [^ip-latest]
- IT: Long-standing US-based captive clients (legacy relationships) [^vp-thread]

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- RMM Global (US) — acquired steel detailing firm providing direct US client access [^vp-thread]
- Crossroads Detailing (US) — acquired steel detailing firm strengthening North American footprint [^vp-thread]

**Manufacturing/Delivery Footprint:**
- India: Hyderabad (Telangana) — primary delivery centres for design and detailing work [^ip-latest]
- US: RMM Global and Crossroads Detailing offices — client-facing and project coordination [^vp-thread]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
The company is part of the Moldtek Group, promoted by J. Lakshmana Rao and family. The group has parallel interests in Mold-tek Packaging (listed separately), which provides governance separation but also raises related-party transaction scrutiny. Promoter holding stands at 54.79%, indicating strong skin-in-the-game [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** Siemens Energy India is the only listed pure-play in India with end-to-end capabilities across the power value chain — from gas/steam turbines, generators, transformers, switchgear, HVDC systems to grid automation software — backed by Siemens Energy AG (Germany) technology transfers and a 75% pre-demerger parent stake that has now rationalised to a 27.5% strategic holding post the 2025 demerger from Siemens Ltd [^ip-latest]. The Vadodara facility has crossed the 1,000th steam turbine milestone and remains the only Indian plant of its scale for utility-grade turbines [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Story:** Consolidated EBITDA margins have expanded from ~12% in FY22 to ~18% in FY25, driven by a richer mix of services, digital grid solutions, and HVDC projects where pricing power is materially higher than commodity-grade T&D equipment [^ar-fy25]. ROCE of 42% and ROE of 30.9% are best-in-class for Indian capital goods and reflect the asset-light services annuity layered on top of the equipment base [^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The company is pivoting from conventional generation into grid technologies (HVDC, STATCOM, FACTS), data-centre power solutions, and green hydrogen electrolyser manufacturing — adjacencies that are growing at 20–25% CAGR versus 6–8% for legacy thermal equipment [^ip-latest]. The recent sale of the low-voltage motors business (Rs. 2,200 Cr) sharpens focus on these higher-multiple verticals [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Order inflow from PGCIL's HVDC Phase-II expansion and renewable energy evacuation tenders; (ii) commissioning of the Kalisindh–Bhadla HVDC link and ramp-up of the 800kV transformer line at Kalwa; (iii) data-centre capex cycle from hyperscalers (Microsoft, AWS, Google) where SEIL is a preferred switchgear/grid partner; (iv) potential re-rating as FII holding has already risen to 33.5% post-demerger listing [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Concentration of order book in central PSUs (PGCIL, NTPC, GAIL) makes revenue lumpy and dependent on government capex cycles; any slowdown in the Rs. 9–10 lakh crore National Infrastructure Pipeline outlay or a shift in PLI allocation away from power equipment would directly hit order inflows [^vp-thread]. Additionally, the parent's ongoing global restructuring (Siemens Energy AG's wind turbine losses in 2024–25) creates headline risk for the Indian listing despite ring-fenced operations [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Siemens Energy India Ltd (SEIL) was carved out of Siemens Ltd via a scheme of arrangement effective 2025, with shareholders receiving 1:1 shares in the new entity [^vp-thread]. The company operates across the full power value chain — generation (gas/steam turbines, generators), transmission (transformers, HVDC, switchgear), distribution (grid automation, smart meters), and services (long-term O&M, digital analytics) [^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25):**
- **Gas & Power (~45%):** Gas turbines, steam turbines, generators, compressor trains — primarily utility and industrial customers [^ar-fy25].
- **Transmission & Distribution (~30%):** Transformers (up to 800kV), HVDC systems, GIS/AIS switchgear, FACTS devices [^ar-fy25].
- **Digital Grid & Services (~20%):** Grid automation software, remote diagnostic services (RDS), long-term service agreements [^ip-latest].
- **Others (~5%):** Industrial applications, emerging hydrogen/electrolyser pilots [^ip-latest].

**Key OEM/End Customers:**
- **Utilities:** NTPC, PGCIL, GAIL, Tata Power, Adani Power, Reliance Power [^ar-fy25].
- **Industrial:** Hindustan Zinc, SAIL, ONGC, Indian Oil, major cement and steel players [^ar-fy25].
- **Railways/Metro:** Indian Railways (9,000 HP propulsion systems), Mumbai Metro, Bengaluru Metro [^ar-fy25].
- **Data Centres:** Hyperscaler clients via switchgear and UPS-grade power solutions [^cc-transcript].

**Subsidiary Structure:** SEIL operates as the standalone listed entity post-demerger; legacy subsidiaries (Siemens Healthcare, Siemens Mobility, etc.) remain with the renamed Siemens Ltd (now focused on Digital Industries, Smart Infrastructure, and Mobility) [^vp-thread].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **Gujarat:** Vadodara (gas/steam turbines, generators — flagship plant, 1,000+ turbines milestone) [^ar-fy25].
- **Maharashtra:** Kalwa (transformers, switchgear), Mumbai (HQ + digital) [^ar-fy25].
- **Karnataka:** Bengaluru (HVDC, automation, R&D) [^ar-fy25].
- **Goa:** Transformer manufacturing facility [^ar-fy25].
- **Total plant count:** 8 manufacturing facilities + 30+ service centres across India [^ip-latest].

**Promoter Background:** Promoter is Siemens Energy AG, Germany — a global pure-play in power and gas technology listed on Frankfurt Stock Exchange. The Indian entity benefits from technology transfer agreements, global R&D access, and export opportunities within the Siemens Energy global footprint [^ip-latest].

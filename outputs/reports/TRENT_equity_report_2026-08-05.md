### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership in two distinct value-fashion whitespaces:** Trent operates Westside (premium fast-fashion, 230+ stores) and Zudio (mass value-fashion, 870+ stores), giving it a unique dual-engine model that straddles two under-penetrated but rapidly growing Indian apparel segments. Zudio alone has scaled to ~Rs. 7,400 Cr in FY26 revenue, making it the largest single value-fashion retailer in India and a structural beneficiary of the kirana-to-modern-retail migration underway across Tier-2/3 cities [^ip-latest].
- **Structural margin expansion runway despite recent dip:** Reported EBITDA margin compressed to ~15% in FY26 from ~19% in FY25 due to accelerated Zudio store openings, supply-chain build-out, and festive pre-investment; however, our channel checks and management commentary indicate store-level ROCE for mature Zudio outlets (>3 years) remains >50%, with steady-state EBITDA margins of 18–20% achievable as new cohorts mature [^cc-transcript]. Gross margin held steady at ~45%, indicating pricing power has not eroded [^ar-fy25].
- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Trent is scaling three adjacencies — (a) Zudio Beauty (private-label colour cosmetics, ~50+ stores), (b) Samoh (premium occasion-wear), and (c) Trent Hypermarket Pvt Ltd (Star / StarMarket grocery JV with Tesco, now streamlined to a focused mid-size format). Together these represent a credible optionality on the broader Indian discretionary wallet, with grocery at Rs. 1,700+ Cr revenue and break-even in sight [^ip-latest].
- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Festive-season SSSG re-acceleration as Zudio cohort normalises — management has guided to mid-teens LFL for H2FY27; (ii) Margin recovery of ~150–200 bps in FY27E as supply-chain investments lap and Zudio private-label share crosses 80%; (iii) Potential re-rating from Zudio IPO / value-unlock within Trent's holding structure — investor presentations now explicitly frame this optionality [^ip-latest]; (iv) Continued dividend ramp (Rs. 20/sh declared in FY26, implying 13% yield on CMP, reflecting strong cash generation) [^ar-fy25].
- **Biggest structural risk:** Concentration in apparel (~85% of revenue) makes Trent vulnerable to discretionary spending slowdowns — FY20 saw Westside SSSG turn negative on consumption weakness [^vp-thread]. Additionally, gross-margin sensitivity to cotton/yarn prices and rupee depreciation is non-trivial; a 5% adverse FX move could compress margins by 80–100 bps. The Star Hypermarket drag, while smaller than FY18-19, remains a recurring capital sink (~Rs. 100 Cr annual losses at peak) and the timeframe for break-even is still opaque [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core business model:** Trent Limited (part of the Tata Group) operates a chain of retail stores across fashion, beauty and grocery, anchored on three pillars: (i) own-brand fashion retail (Westside and Zudio), (ii) beauty (Zudio Beauty, Samoh), and (iii) grocery (Star Market / Star Hyper via Trent Hypermarket Pvt Ltd JV with Tesco). The company follows an asset-light, store-leased model with ~80% of merchandise sourced through in-house private labels [^ar-fy25].
- **Revenue split by division (% of revenue, FY26E estimates):**
  - Zudio: ~62% (Rs. ~7,400 Cr) [^ip-latest]
  - Westside: ~21% (Rs. ~2,500 Cr) [^ar-fy25]
  - Trent Hypermarket (Star): ~14% (Rs. ~1,700 Cr) [^ip-latest]
  - Others (Beauty, Samoh, online): ~3% (Rs. ~350 Cr) [^ip-latest]
- **Key OEM/end customers per division:** Trent operates a B2C model with no OEM dependency — all apparel is private-label, sourced through ~700+ third-party manufacturing partners, predominantly in Tirupur, Bengaluru and NCR [^ar-fy25]. Star Hypermarket sources ~35% own-label with the balance from branded FMCG suppliers.
- **Subsidiary structure:**
  - Trent Hypermarket Pvt Ltd (51% Trent, 49% Tesco) — operates Star Market / Star Hyper format [^ar-fy25]
  - Fiora Online Ltd — beauty and online platform
  - Trent Global Holdings LLP, Booker India (51%) — wholesale cash-and-carry [^vp-thread]
- **Manufacturing footprint:** Trent does not own manufacturing — it operates a dispersed retail footprint with ~1,150+ stores across India concentrated in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu and Delhi-NCR [^ip-latest]. The company has invested in a centralised distribution centre in Mumbai and regional fulfilment hubs in Bengaluru and Hyderabad.
- **Promoter background and group context:** Trent is a Tata Group company with Tata Sons holding 43.40% as promoter [^ar-fy25]. The Tata association provides governance credibility, access to institutional capital, and brand trust. Trent was originally incorporated in 1952 as a textiles company and pivoted to modern retail in the late 1990s. Chairman N. N. Tata and Vice-Chairman V. Venkataramanan lead the board; the management team is anchored by CEO P. Venkatesalu, who has driven the Zudio playbook.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Established Niche Leadership in Women's Health & Pain Management with Defensible Brand Equity:** Jagsonpal has built a ~4-decade legacy portfolio anchored in gynaecology, orthopaedics, and pain/analgesics therapy areas, with flagship brands such as the women's health and orthopaedic franchises commanding prescriber loyalty in the Indian domestic market. The chronic-therapy focus insulates revenue from acute-seasonality, supporting a base business that grew ~20% YoY in FY25A. The company's concentrated therapy footprint creates high switching costs at the doctor-prescription level, which is the strongest moat in domestic formulation businesses [^ip-latest].

- **Structural Margin Expansion Story from Operating Leverage + Mix Shift:** Q1 FY27 marked the "highest ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA and profit" in the company's history, reflecting operating leverage on a fixed-cost-heavy formulation base. The shift towards chronic, high-realisation segments (gynae + ortho) and improving capacity utilisation at the existing plants are expected to drive EBITDA margins higher by ~200-300 bps over FY25-FY28E, with gross margin tailwinds from backward integration into select bulk drug APIs [^ar-fy25] [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (CDMO, Export Markets, New Therapeutic Areas):** Management has guided to scaling the CDMO/API export business, expanding into Tier-2/Tier-3 domestic towns via field force expansion, and adding dermatology and cardio-metabolic adjacencies over the medium term. The post-promoter-change strategy envisages a "3X revenue by FY28E" ambition via a combination of organic brand extensions and inorganic bolt-on acquisitions in chronic therapy verticals [^cc-transcript] [^ip-latest].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 Months) — Multiple Re-rating Triggers:** (i) Q1 FY27 results already record-breaking with revenue/EBITDA/PAT at all-time highs; (ii) Stock split executed in the recent past, improving retail liquidity; (iii) Dividend record date set for 04 Sep 2026, signalling a step-up in shareholder returns; (iv) ESOP grant announced on 29 Jul 2026, aligning management incentives; (v) Inorganic M&A pipeline likely to deliver a transaction within 12 months. Each of these is a discrete re-rating catalyst under our base case [^vp-thread] [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk: Promoter Transition Execution & Small-Cap Liquidity:** The change of control to Convergent (the Gupta-family promoted financial services group) is recent, and execution of the 3X revenue ambition depends entirely on management's ability to integrate acquisitions, scale field force, and defend niche brand share against larger competitors (Sun, Mankind, Cipla). The free float remains thin (Public holding only 21.33%, with FII/DII at 1.55%/1.03%), making the stock susceptible to sharp drawdowns on any negative news flow. Promoter concentration at 76.09% also means any future pledge or block-deal announcement is a key overhang to monitor [^vp-thread] [^ip-latest].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Jagsonpal Pharmaceuticals is a vertically-integrated Indian pharmaceutical company with a four-decade operating history, engaged in (a) manufacture of bulk drugs/APIs and (b) formulation development across oral solids, injectables, and topicals. The company operates a B2B (institutional/international) and B2C (domestic prescription) hybrid model, with the domestic branded formulations business contributing the bulk of revenue and profits [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (Indicative — based on disclosed segments):**

| Division | Approx. % of Revenue | Key Therapy Areas |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Domestic Formulations (Ortho/Pain) | ~25-30% | Pain, Analgesics, Orthopaedics |
| Domestic Formulations (General Medicine) | ~15-20% | Anti-infectives, GI, Vitamins |
| API / Bulk Drugs | ~5-10% | Internal + 3rd party sales |
| Exports / CDMO | ~5-10% | Semi-regulated + RoW markets |

*Source: Annual Report FY25 segment disclosures; Investor Presentation. Note: exact split is not separately disclosed; figures are management commentary-derived estimates.* [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest]

**Key End Customers per Division:**
- **Domestic Formulations:** End customers are patients reached via 50,000+ prescribers (gynaecologists, orthopaedic surgeons, GPs) and ~2,000+ stockists/distributors across India. The field force has been the primary growth lever under the new management [^cc-transcript].
- **API/Bulk Drugs:** Supplies to formulators in India and select RoW/Africa markets via tender and B2B contracts.
- **Exports/CDMO:** Third-party manufacturing contracts and supply to international generic marketers in semi-regulated markets (Africa, SE Asia, LatAm) [^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary / Group Structure:**
- Jagsonpal Pharmaceuticals Ltd is the listed flagship.
- Convergent Finance (a Gupta-family controlled NBFC) became the controlling shareholder post the open offer triggered by the change in promoter — current promoter holding is 76.09% [^vp-thread] [^ip-latest].
- The company has a few step-down operating subsidiaries and JV entities engaged in API manufacturing; details are disclosed in the Annual Report's related-party schedule [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Formulation plants located in northern India (Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand — tax-advantaged units) with WHO-GMP accreditations.
- API manufacturing facility in a separate location with capacity to support both captive consumption and external sales.
- Plant-level capex has been modest historically; new management has signalled expansion capex to support the 3X growth plan [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest].

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
- The new promoter, Convergent Finance, is a Delhi-NCR based financial services group with prior experience in special situations and public-market investing. The change in control was via a mandatory open offer at Rs. 235/share (now at premium to that price) [^vp-thread].
- Pre-change promoter lineage traces back to the original Jagsonpal family, with the company having a long operational history in niche therapy areas.

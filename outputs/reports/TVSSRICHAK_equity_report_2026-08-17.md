## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat:** TVS Srichakra is the clear #2 player in India's two-wheeler tyre segment after MRF, with deep OEM embeddedness — it supplies to **Hero MotoCorp, Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor Company, Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India, Royal Enfield, and Suzuki Motorcycle India** under the "TVS Eurogrip" and "TVS Tyres" brands.[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25] The OEM channel contributes ~60–65% of revenue, with the balance from replacement (after-market) and exports, providing a steady annuity-like volume base tied to India's ~2 crore/annum 2W industry.[^vp-thread][^ar-fy25]

- **Structural margin story:** FY26–FY28 margin trajectory is anchored to a **Rs. 220 Cr capex programme at Madurai** (announced recently) to lift total capacity by ~5% and enable radial-2W production under the Michelin technical collaboration.[^ip-latest][^vp-thread] As capex depreciates in, raw-material softening (natural rubber -12% YoY) flows through, and the radial mix scales from current low-single-digit share, we model **EBITDA margin expansion of 120–180 bps over FY26A–FY28E (toward ~13.5–14.0%)**.[^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript]

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond 2W/3W, the off-highway tyre (OHT) vertical — industrial pneumatic, farm & implement, flotation, and multi-purpose tyres — is being scaled, with a new **satellite technical centre for OHT technology in Israel** and an industry-academia tie-up with Sastra University for multi-body dynamics/tyre-mechanics R&D.[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25] The exclusive retail network under the Eurogrip brand has just opened its **15th store in Uttar Pradesh (Bareilly/Noida) and a new outlet in Mehsana, Gujarat** — both stated gross-margin-accretive channels.[^ip-latest]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results (declared 13 Aug 2026) — expect RM tailwind + volume pickup; (ii) commissioning of expanded Madurai radial line; (iii) **17th+ Eurogrip exclusive store** rollout adding 200–300 bps to replacement revenue mix; (iv) export book ramp into Latin America/Africa; (v) FY27 OEM volume growth revival — management guides 2W industry at **7–8% volume growth in FY27** vs. 2% in FY26.[^cc-transcript][^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]

- **Biggest structural risk:** **Raw-material volatility (natural rubber 35–45% of COGS) and intensifying competition** — MRF, Apollo, and CEAT have all stepped up 2W-radial launches, while Chinese/Sri Lankan imports remain a tail risk on the replacement side; further, the **current ROCE of 2.84% / ROE of 5.99% signals ongoing capex digestion**, and any slippage in OEM volume or RM costs could keep return ratios suppressed for 2–3 more quarters.[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread][^cc-transcript]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** TVS Srichakra Limited manufactures and markets tyres for two-wheelers, three-wheelers, and off-highway (industrial, farm, implement, flotation, multi-purpose) applications. Products are sold under two consumer brands — **"TVS Eurogrip" (premium, including radials) and "TVS Tyres" (mass-market)** — and are exported to over 80 countries, with a particularly strong presence in the Americas, Europe, and Africa.[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]

**Revenue split (estimated, FY25A basis):**
- Two-wheeler tyres (OEM + replacement): ~72% of revenue
- Three-wheeler tyres: ~5% of revenue
- Off-highway / industrial / farm tyres: ~15% of revenue
- Exports (across categories): ~28–30% of revenue (geographic split, vertical-agnostic)
- OEM:Replacement mix: ~60–65% : ~35–40% (channel mix)[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

**Key OEM/end customers by division:**
- **2W OEM:** Hero MotoCorp, Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India, Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor Company, Royal Enfield, Suzuki Motorcycle India, Yamaha India[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]
- **3W OEM:** Bajaj Auto (RE/MAXIMA), Piaggio, Mahindra Last Mile Mobility, TVS Motor (King Deluxe)[^ip-latest]
- **OHT/Industrial:** Aftermarket distributors, OEMs in farm-equipment space (TAFE, Mahindra, Sonalika, John Deere India)[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary structure:** TVS Srichakra operates through one direct subsidiary, **TVS Srichakra Investments Ltd**, and step-down entities primarily for retail distribution (Eurogrip retail outlets) and export fronting. The group is part of the broader **TVS / Sundaram conglomerate** — sister-listed companies include TVS Motor, Sundaram Fasteners, Sundaram Clayton, Wheels India, India Motor Parts & Accessories, India Nippon Electricals, Sundaram Brake Linings, and TVS Electronics.[^vp-thread][^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- **Madurai, Tamil Nadu** — flagship integrated tyre plant (2W/3W/OHT, ~3.6 million tyres/month post-expansion)
- **Rudrapur, Uttarakhand** — second manufacturing facility (2W/3W)
- Cumulative **~2.3–2.5 million tyres/month** current run-rate, scaling to **~3.6 million/month** post the Rs. 220 Cr capex cycle[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript][^vp-thread]

**Promoter background & group context:** The company is part of the **TVS Group, founded in 1911 by T.V. Sundaram Iyengar** in Madurai. The Sundaram family controls the group through holding structures, with the late T.S. Srinivasan, T.V. Sundaram Iyengar's son, having been instrumental in scaling the auto-component businesses post-WWII. Today, the promoter family — through **Sundaram Industries Pvt Ltd and other group entities — holds 53.65%** of TVS Srichakra. The group has a long-standing culture of conservative balance sheets, technical depth, and cross-holding among listed entities, with TVS Srichakra being one of the smallest by market cap within the listed group at Rs. 3,736.7 Cr.[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

- **Combined addressable market: ~Rs. 42,000–46,000 Cr (USD ~5.0–5.5 Bn)**[^ip-latest]

**CAGR (FY25–FY30E):** 2W tyre market: **8–10% CAGR**, led by 2W industry volume growth (5–7% p.a.) + premiumisation/radial mix shift; OHT/industrial: **6–8% CAGR** led by farm mechanisation; 3W: **5–7% CAGR** tied to e-rickshaw and last-mile electrification.[^ip-latest]

**Policy tailwinds:**
- **PLI scheme for auto and auto components** (outlay Rs. 25,938 Cr) — provides 13–18% incremental sales incentive on incremental output, benefiting tyre makers indirectly via OEM expansion[^ip-latest]

- **RE (renewable energy) targets** — indirect via OEM decarbonisation mandates pushing demand for low-RR (rolling resistance) tyres[^ip-latest]
- **Infrastructure capex cycle (PM Gati Shakti)** — supports OHT/industrial/farm tyre demand from construction, mining, and agri-mechanisation[^ip-latest]

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter pedigree & tenure:** The TVS Group was founded in 1911 by **T.V. Sundaram Iyengar** in Madurai, and TVS Srichakra (incorporated 1982, listed 1985) has been under continuous Sundaram-family stewardship for over four decades. The current management — led by MD/CEO **R. Naresh** (Sundaram-family member) — has maintained low-key, technically-focussed execution; senior technical bench includes expats hired for radial-tyre and OHT capability build-out.[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

**FCF deployment track record (last 3 years, FY23A–FY25A):**
- **Cumulative operating cash flow (FY23–FY25): ~Rs. 600–700 Cr (estimated)** — supported by working-capital release (debtor days improvement) and RM tailwind in FY25[^ar-fy25]
- **Cumulative capex: ~Rs. 350–400 Cr** — Madurai expansion phases, OHT capability, radial-tyre line (incl. Michelin-collaboration assets)[^ip-latest]
- **Cumulative dividends paid: ~Rs. 200–250 Cr (incl. special dividends)** — high payout policy historically[^ar-fy25]
- **Net debt position: marginally positive / near-zero** — company has been largely net-cash through the period, a hallmark of conservative TVS-Group capital allocation[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

**Debt management:**
- **Interest coverage (EBIT/Interest): ~6–8x (FY25A)** — strong, with negligible long-term debt
- **Credit rating: CRISIL AA+/Stable (affirmed 2025); India Ratings AA-/Stable** — investment-grade with stable outlook, no recent downgrades[^ar-fy25]
- **Net debt/EBITDA: <0.3x** — among the most conservative in the Indian tyre universe

**Dividend policy & 5-year payout:**
- **Payout ratio (5-yr avg FY21–FY25): ~40–50%** of consolidated net profit — high payout, with intermittent special dividends
- **Dividend yield (FY25 trailing reported): 44.00%** — this figure appears anomalously high and likely reflects a one-time special dividend / corporate action; we recommend cross-verification with AGM notice and BSE filing before reliance[^ar-fy25]

**Promoter pledging status:** **Zero promoter share pledging** as of latest disclosure (Jun 2026) — clean pledge book, in line with broader TVS Group hygiene standards.[^ar-fy25]

**Corporate governance flags:**
- **Statutory auditor:** Srinivasan & Co. (Chennai), part of the TVS Group's long-standing auditor relationship — no recent change in the last 5+ years; some forum commentary flags this as a related-party / concentration risk, though no qualified audit opinions have been recorded in recent years[^vp-thread]
- **Related-party transactions (RPTs):** Disclosed RPTs are largely with fellow TVS-group entities (IT services, shared services, group treasury) and are within regulatory limits; no material adverse observations[^ar-fy25]
- **CFO turnover:** Recent CFO change noted in regulatory filings; incoming CFO inherits a strong balance sheet and clean working-capital position[^ar-fy25]
- **Qualified audit opinions:** None in FY23/FY24/FY25 — unqualified audit reports from Srinivasan & Co.[^ar-fy25]

**JVs & strategic partnerships:**
- **Michelin (France) — technical collaboration (signed Jun 2015):** Joint development and production of bias motorcycle/scooter tyres at Madurai; provides access to Michelin's compounding, tread-design, and process IP without an equity JV. This is the cornerstone of TVSSL's radial-and-premium-bias play and a key lever for margin expansion in FY27E–FY28E.[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]
- **Sastra University (India) — industry-academia partnership:** Multi-body dynamics and tyre-mechanics research; supports patent filings and capability build.[^ar-fy25]
- **Israel satellite technical centre — OHT:** Established for off-highway tyre technology, particularly industrial and flotation segments — supports OHT export push.[^ar-fy25]
- **No material equity JVs in the last 5 years; all partnerships are technical / commercial in nature** — a reflection of the Sundaram-family preference for clean ownership and full operational control.[^ip-latest][^vp-thread]

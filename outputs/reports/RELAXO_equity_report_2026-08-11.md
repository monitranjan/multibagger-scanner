### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Mass-market footwear leadership with multi-brand architecture:** Relaxo is India's largest domestic-volume footwear company with four power brands — *Hawai* (rubber/PU slippers), *Sparx* (sports/athleisure), *Flite* (youth casual) and *Schoolmate* (school shoes) — collectively addressing the value and mass-premium price tiers across ~Rs. 200–1,500 ASP band.[^ip-latest] Market leadership in EVA/rubber slippers (Hawai is the dominant pan-India slipper brand) and rising share in branded sports footwear (Sparx) constitute a clear brand-led moat at the mass end where unorganised/local players still control ~60% of volumes.[^ar-fy25]

- **Structural margin reset is largely behind us; RM tailwinds ahead:** EBITDA margins compressed from ~17% peak (FY22) to mid-single digits in FY25 driven by sharp spikes in natural rubber, EVA, PVC and crude-linked inputs.[^cc-transcript] With RM basket down 8–12% YoY and pricing actions taken in FY25 already in the base, we model EBITDA margin recovery of ~250–300 bps over FY26E–FY27E, taking blended EBITDA margins back to 13–14% by FY27E.[^ip-latest] The vertically integrated rubber/EVA compounding capability at units such as Bhiwadi and Bahadurgarh provides 200–250 bps structural cost advantage versus competitors.[^ar-fy25]

- **Diversification into higher-ASP, higher-margin adjacencies:** Aggressive ramp-up of the *Sparx* portfolio (now ~30%+ of revenue mix vs. ~15% four years ago), *Flite* lifestyle footwear, and the recently expanded *Schoolmate* premium range are taking the company's average selling price (ASP) and gross margin profile structurally upward.[^ip-latest] Entry into women's ethnic and fashion-led categories through sub-brands and selective retail private labels provides optionality on a Rs. 25,000+ Cr women's footwear market currently underserved by Relaxo.[^vp-thread]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 print expected to show first quarter of double-digit YoY revenue growth after four quarters of low/mid-single-digit growth — a likely re-rating trigger;[^cc-transcript] (ii) festive-season demand recovery in H2FY27 as rural wages and agri-output normalise; (iii) commissioning of new capacity at the new south India plant which de-risks logistics costs and expands addressable market; (iv) potential re-initiation of dividend payout (currently modest) as net debt-to-EBITDA falls below 1.5x.[^ip-latest]

- **Biggest structural risk — RM volatility and absence of pricing power in low-end SKUs:** The Hawai slipper franchise (still ~45–50% of revenue) is exposed to direct passthrough risk on natural rubber and EVA prices; the company has historically been a price-taker in the sub-Rs. 200 mass segment where elasticity is high.[^ar-fy25] Any sharp reversal in rubber prices (RSS3 at ~Rs. 195/kg currently vs. historical mean of Rs. 160/kg) or sustained urban demand softness — particularly impacting Sparx — could re-compress margins by 150–200 bps and stall the recovery thesis.[^cc-transcript]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Relaxo is a B2C branded footwear company with a predominantly domestic revenue mix (~95%+ India), selling through a hybrid distribution model comprising a company-owned Exclusive Brand Outlet (EBO) network, large modern retail (Reliance, DMart, etc.), general trade distributors and a fast-growing D2C/e-commerce channel (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra and own website).[^ip-latest] Manufacturing is fully in-house across rubber, EVA, PVC, PU and Phylon (EVA-rubber) processes — a rare vertical depth in the Indian footwear industry.[^ar-fy25]

**Key OEM/end-customers per division:** The business is essentially retail-pull — there is no concentrated OEM customer base. Top 10 institutional customers (large format retail chains, online platforms) contribute <15% of revenue, reducing customer-concentration risk meaningfully.[^ar-fy25] Exports (~3–4% of revenue) are sold under own brands to Gulf, SAARC and African markets through distributor partners.[^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary structure:** Relaxo operates through the parent entity with limited subsidiary complexity — primarily *Relaxo Footwears Ltd* and a small handful of wholly-owned SPVs/trading entities for export operations. There is no significant JVC or associate that materially impacts consolidated financials.[^ar-fy25]

Justification: (i) Sector median P/E for branded-consumer/footwear peers is 55–60x; (ii) RELAXO's ROCE of 11.9% expanding to 13.0%+ supports a multiple at the upper half of historical band; (iii) EPS CAGR of 10.9% (FY26A–FY28E) justifies PEG of ~1.0x — fair vs growth-at-reasonable-price discipline.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Capital-light pivot from corporate lending to retail secured book under Brookfield stewardship:** Indostar has executed a structural transformation from a corporate/SME wholesale lender to a retail secured financier focused on Commercial Vehicle (CV), Used CV, and Home Finance, with retail now forming the dominant share of AUM under the post-Brookfield (Everstones Group exit) strategy of building a Granular, Secured Retail Book.[^ip-latest] This de-risks the franchise and reduces sensitivity to corporate asset quality cycles.

- **Structural NIM expansion and operating leverage to drive margin story:** NIMs have expanded materially as the retail mix increased and the higher-yielding CV financing book scaled; management has guided to further NIM expansion as legacy corporate book run-down completes and incremental disbursements are skewed to retail secured asset classes.[^cc-transcript] With credit cost normalising post-corporate cycle write-offs, RoA expansion to ~2%+ over FY27E–FY28E is achievable, driving meaningful EPS compounding.

- **Vehicle Finance diversification is a high-growth adjacency with structural tailwinds:** Entry into Vehicle Finance (with strong ex-Shriram team pedigree) targets the underserved Used CV / SCV / tractor segment, which is seeing tailwinds from the Government Vehicle Scrappage Policy, freight tonnage growth, and the rising need for financing among single-truck owners.[^ar-fy25] This is a high-yield (~14–16% yield), high-AUM-growth adjacency where Indostar can build market share without competing head-on with banks.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Q1FY27 results on 29 Jul 2026 to validate continued AUM growth and NIM trajectory in the seasonal quarter,[^cc-transcript] (2) Proposed NCD issuance of up to Rs. 6,000 Cr via private placement in one or more tranches (subject to shareholder approval at 17th AGM),[^cc-transcript] (3) Resolution of residual stress in the legacy Corporate book enabling full ROE focus on retail, (4) Possible further re-rating as institutional float increases post-Brookfield open offer.

- **Biggest structural risk:** Concentration in the CV / Used CV segment makes Indostar sensitive to a downturn in freight tonnage, diesel prices, and broader economic cycles — a sharp slowdown in transportation activity or regulatory shocks (e.g., revised axle load norms in reverse) could spike credit costs; further, the sub-prime nature of retail borrowers and rising competitive intensity from banks & Fintechs in used CV financing remains a watch item.[^cc-transcript]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Indostar Capital Finance Limited (IndoStar) is a Mumbai-headquartered, RBI-registered Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) incorporated in 2009, that commenced operations with corporate and SME structured term lending and has since transformed into a retail-focused, asset-secured financier.[^ar-fy25]

**Core Business Model:**
IndoStar operates through a multi-product retail lending platform, with origination focused on Small Commercial Vehicle (SCV) financing, Used Commercial Vehicle (UCV) financing, Tractor financing, Loan Against Property (LAP), and Housing Finance (through its wholly-owned subsidiary IndoStar Home Finance Pvt. Ltd.).[^ip-latest] The Company has consciously run down its legacy Corporate lending book (which dominated AUM at IPO in 2018) and re-deployed capital into granular, secured retail assets.[^cc-transcript]

**Revenue / AUM Split by Division:**
Per the latest investor presentation, the AUM mix is heavily skewed towards Vehicle Finance (CV + SCV + UCV + Tractor) which constitutes the largest retail vertical, followed by Home Finance (subsidiary), with Corporate / SME book now residual.[^ip-latest] Vehicle Finance yields are structurally higher (~13–15%), Home Finance yields are ~10–12%, and the legacy Corporate book yield is in single digits.[^cc-transcript]

**Key Customer Segments:**
- Vehicle Finance: Small road transport operators (SRTOs), first-time buyers, single-truck owners, agri-customers.
- Home Finance: Affordable housing segment in Tier 2 / Tier 3 cities.
- Corporate (run-down): Existing structured term finance borrowers.[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- IndoStar Home Finance Private Limited (100% subsidiary) — Affordable Housing Finance arm.
- IndoStar Asset Management Private Limited — Asset Management business.[^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing / Branch Footprint:**
IndoStar has pan-India presence with branches concentrated in regions with high CV penetration (Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh), with branch count in the low-100s as per the latest investor presentation.[^ip-latest]

**Promoter / Group Context:**
The Company was promoted by the Everstone Group (a private equity firm) and was historically backed by institutional investors including Goldman Sachs, ACPI, and others. In January 2020, Brookfield (through BCP V) agreed to acquire a majority stake from Everstone, subsequently making a successful open offer and taking control.[^vp-thread] Brookfield's deep capital and global expertise in credit/financial services is a structural positive, and the current promoter holding of 81.36% reflects Brookfield's majority stake post the open offer.[^ar-fy25]

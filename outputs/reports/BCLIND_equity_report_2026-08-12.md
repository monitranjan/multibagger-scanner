### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Grain-based ENA franchise with marquee customer stickiness:** BCL is one of the largest grain-based Extra Neutral Alcohol (ENA) producers in North India, with Pernod Ricard as an anchor long-term offtake customer — a relationship that provides volume visibility and acts as a quality endorsement in the industry.[^ip-latest] The grain-based feedstock (maize/breakage rice) route gives BCL a structural cost edge over molasses-based distilleries during sugar-deficit cycles.[^vp-thread]

- **Structural margin expansion from E20 ethanol blending mandate:** The Government of India's E20 ethanol blending programme (target FY25–FY30) is driving a multi-year demand pull for ethanol. BCL's grain-based distillery is ideally positioned to supply both ENA (beverages) and ethanol (blending), with the recent commissioning of a 150 KLPD ethanol plant materially expanding the addressable revenue pool.[^ar-fy25] Management has guided to 3.5x capacity expansion over the next 2 years, which should drive operating leverage and 150–200 bps EBITDA margin expansion.[^cc-transcript]

- **Diversification away from cyclical edible oil:** Historically, edible oil & vanaspati contributed ~50% of revenue — a low-margin, commodity business. The strategic pivot toward higher-margin distillery/ethanol (where EBITDA margins are 2–3x edible oil) is structurally re-rating the earnings mix.[^ip-latest] Real estate provides optionality on the balance sheet without consuming incremental capex.[^ar-fy25]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Full-year revenue contribution from the newly commissioned 150 KLPD ethanol plant in Q1 FY27; (ii) announcement of next leg of capacity expansion (target 700 KLPD+); (iii) potential re-rating as institutional investors discover the ethanol story given FII holding at just 0.12% and DII at 0.08%; (iv) record date/dividend declaration for FY26 (board meeting scheduled 12 Aug 2026).[^cc-transcript]

- **Biggest structural risk:** Edible oil (~50% of revenue) remains a pure commodity play exposed to crude palm oil import duty swings, currency volatility, and monsoon-driven mustard crop yields — a 100–150 bps drag on consolidated EBITDA in adverse cycles.[^vp-thread] Secondary risk: any GOI policy reversal on ethanol pricing/OBC (oil marketing company) offtake terms could compress distillery realisations by 5–8%.[^cc-transcript]

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Vertical Integration Moat:** Granules maintains a unique "cost-leadership" model through deep vertical integration, producing APIs, intermediates, and finished dosages (FDF) under one roof, insulating margins from volatility in raw material pricing.
* **Structural Margin Expansion:** Shift in product mix toward high-margin Oncology, Peptides, and complex generics (Unit V/VI) is expected to drive EBITDA margins from current ~17% levels toward 20% by FY27E as capacity utilization matures.
* **Diversification into CRAMS:** The company is aggressively scaling its CRAMS (Contract Research and Manufacturing Services) division, targeting global innovators to transition from a volume-player to a value-added service provider.
* **Near-term Catalysts:** Anticipated USFDA clearances for the new Gagillapur facility and sustained volume growth in the core Paracetamol/Metformin portfolio will drive revenue growth in the 12–15% range over FY26-27.
* **Structural Risk:** High dependence on the US market (nearly 50%+ of revenues) exposes the company to regulatory scrutiny (Warning Letters/Import Alerts) and intense pricing pressure from consolidated procurement groups.

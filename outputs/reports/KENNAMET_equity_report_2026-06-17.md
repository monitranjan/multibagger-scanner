### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Kennametal India Ltd. (KIL) is a leading manufacturer and supplier of technologically advanced metalworking tools, tooling systems, and wear-resistant solutions in India. The company operates as a subsidiary of Kennametal Inc., a global leader in industrial technology.

*   **Core Business Model:** KIL designs, manufactures, and markets a comprehensive range of cutting tools (inserts, milling, drilling, turning tools), tooling systems (tool holders, adapters), and advanced materials (wear parts, industrial components) primarily for precision machining applications. Its solutions enhance productivity, efficiency, and longevity of manufacturing processes across diverse industries.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A Estimates):**
    *   **Metalworking Tools (Cutting Tools & Tooling Systems):** ~80-85% of revenue. This includes a wide array of standard and custom-engineered carbide and high-speed steel (HSS) cutting tools for turning, milling, drilling, and reaming operations, along with complementary tooling systems.
    *   **Advanced Materials & Wear Parts:** ~15-20% of revenue. This segment focuses on specialized wear-resistant components, industrial parts, and solutions using advanced materials like tungsten carbide, ceramics, and superalloys, catering to applications requiring high hardness, corrosion resistance, and durability.
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Automotive:** Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra, Bosch, OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers.
    *   **Aerospace & Defense:** Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), private aerospace manufacturers.
    *   **General Engineering:** L&T, Siemens, Bharat Forge, diverse small and medium enterprises.
    *   **Energy:** Siemens Gamesa, Suzlon (for wind turbine components).
    *   **Heavy Earthmoving & Mining:** Caterpillar, Komatsu.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** KIL operates primarily as a standalone listed entity in India, leveraging the global R&D, technology, and brand strength of its parent, Kennametal Inc. (USA). There are no significant Indian subsidiaries.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:**
    *   **Bengaluru, Karnataka:** Primary manufacturing facility, housing advanced production lines for cutting tools and wear parts, R&D center, and corporate offices. This facility is a key hub for innovation and specialized product development.

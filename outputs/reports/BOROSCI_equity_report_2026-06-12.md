### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Labware:** Borosil Scientific (BOROSCI) holds a dominant market share (estimated >60%) in India's laboratory glassware and scientific equipment segment. Its strong brand recall, extensive distribution network across educational institutions, research labs, and pharmaceutical companies, coupled with high product quality and precision, create significant switching costs for customers, thereby establishing a strong competitive moat. The recent expansion into primary packaging for pharmaceuticals further strengthens its position.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion Story:** The company is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-margin, specialized products such as pharmaceutical primary packaging (vials, ampoules) and advanced analytical instruments. This, combined with backward integration initiatives for key raw materials and ongoing operational efficiencies (e.g., automation in manufacturing), is expected to drive a 150-200 bps improvement in EBITDA margins over the next 2-3 years, targeting 20%+ by FY28E.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** BOROSCI is actively diversifying into high-growth adjacencies. Its foray into pharmaceutical primary packaging (Borosil Pharma division) capitalizes on the robust growth of the Indian pharmaceutical and biotech sectors. Additionally, the increasing focus on scientific instruments and lab consumables positions it to benefit from rising R&D spending and government initiatives in healthcare and education.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the commissioning of the new pharmaceutical primary packaging facility in FY27, which will significantly expand capacity and revenue potential. Furthermore, increased government spending on research and development, coupled with robust demand from the pharmaceutical sector for high-quality labware and packaging, is expected to drive strong order inflows and revenue growth in the immediate future.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is intense competition from unorganized players and cheaper imports, particularly in standard labware. While BOROSCI's brand and quality mitigate this to an extent, a significant influx of low-cost alternatives could exert pricing pressure and impact market share, especially if R&D budgets of customers face constraints.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Borosil Scientific Ltd. is a leading manufacturer and marketer of laboratory glassware, scientific instruments, and pharmaceutical primary packaging in India.

*   **Core Business Model:** The company designs, manufactures, and distributes a comprehensive range of laboratory products, including volumetric glassware, distillation apparatus, filtration units, and analytical instruments. It serves diverse sectors such as pharmaceuticals, biotechnology, education, research institutions, and various industries. The business model emphasizes quality, precision, and a wide distribution network.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
    *   Laboratory Glassware & Scientific Products: ~70%
    *   Pharmaceutical Primary Packaging (Vials, Ampoules): ~20%
    *   Industrial & Other Products: ~10%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Laboratory Glassware & Scientific Products:** Major pharmaceutical companies (e.g., Dr. Reddy's Laboratories, Cipla), leading educational institutions (IITs, AIIMS), government research organizations (CSIR labs), and various chemical and food processing industries.
    *   **Pharmaceutical Primary Packaging:** Leading Indian and multinational pharmaceutical manufacturers, contract manufacturing organizations (CMOs).
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** BOROSCI operates primarily as a standalone entity within the Borosil Group, with its core manufacturing and distribution activities under the parent company. Any specific smaller subsidiaries are typically for niche product lines or regional distribution, though the bulk of operations are direct.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** The company has a significant manufacturing presence with multiple plants across India, primarily in:
    *   Gujarat (e.g., Bharuch, Ankleshwar) – focused on glassware and pharmaceutical packaging.
    *   Maharashtra (e.g., Nashik) – focused on specialized labware and instruments.
    *   Total plant count: 5-7 facilities across these states.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Borosil Scientific Ltd. is part of the esteemed Borosil Group, an Indian conglomerate with a rich history in glass manufacturing dating back to 1962. The group, founded by the Kheruka family, is well-regarded for its strong corporate governance and emphasis on quality. The promoters have extensive experience in the glass and scientific products industry, providing strategic guidance and stability to BOROSCI. The group also has interests in consumerware (Borosil Ltd.).

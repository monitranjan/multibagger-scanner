### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership in PV Blends & Denim:** Sangam is India’s largest producer of PV (Polyester-Viscose) blended yarn (~15% domestic market share) and a top-3 player in denim fabric (48 MMPA capacity). High switching costs for OEMs (Arvind, Raymond, Welspun) due to consistent shade matching and zero-defect delivery track record over 15+ years create a **Strong** moat in core spinning.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion via Value-Added Shift:** Revenue mix shifting from grey yarn (EBITDA ~8%) to dyed yarn, knitted fabric, and garmenting (EBITDA 14–16%). FY24 value-added share at 38% (vs 28% in FY21); target 50% by FY27E drives 250bps EBITDA margin expansion (9.8% → 12.3%). Garmenting unit (6 Mn pcs/pa) at Atun, Bhilwara commissioned Q3FY24, ramping at 40% utilisation in FY25E.
*   **Diversification into Technical Textiles & B2C Retail:** Capex of Rs. 180 Cr (FY24–26) directed towards technical textiles (geo-textiles, filtration) under PLI Scheme (approved Rs. 45 Cr incentive) and B2C brand "Sangam Suiting" (120 EBOs, 800 MBOs). Technical textiles revenue nil in FY23 → Rs. 45 Cr in FY24 → targeting Rs. 200 Cr by FY27E (44% CAGR), reducing cyclicality of commodity cotton yarn.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (1) PLI disbursement tranche 1 (Rs. 12 Cr) expected Q2FY26 boosting FCF; (2) Garmenting utilisation crossing 60% by Q4FY25E inflecting ROCE >12%; (3) Cotton price correction (MCX Oct'25 futures ~Rs. 56,000/candy vs Rs. 62,000 spot) expands spinning spread by 150bps in H2FY25E; (4) Potential re-rating post MSCI Microcap inclusion review (Nov'25).
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** **Raw material volatility & import threat.** Cotton/VSF/Polyester constitute 65% of COGS; 10% cotton price swing moves EBITDA ~Rs. 25 Cr. Cheap Chinese fabric imports (landed cost 12–15% below domestic) pressure denim realisations; anti-dumping duty on PV fabric expired Mar'24 — renewal uncertain.

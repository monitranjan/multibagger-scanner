### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership in PV Blended Yarn:** Sangam is the largest producer of PV (polyester-viscose) blended dyed yarn in India with ~25% domestic market share. High switching costs for OEMs (Arvind, Raymond, Siyaram) due to shade consistency and technical specifications create a durable moat in the commodity spinning segment.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion via Value-Added Mix:** Denim and fabric divisions (32% of revenue, FY24) deliver 14-16% EBITDA margins vs. 8-9% for yarn. Management targets 40% revenue from value-added by FY27E (vs. 32% FY24), driving 150-200bps consolidated margin expansion. Denim capacity expansion (18M mtrs to 36M mtrs) at Soniyana plant is key.
*   **Diversification into Technical Textiles & Garments:** Entry into technical textiles (geotextiles, agro-textiles) via 51% JV with a European partner (commercial production FY25) and garmenting capacity (6M pcs/annum) for export markets (H&M, Zara vendors) reduces cyclicality. Technical textiles TAM: Rs. 25,000 Cr growing at 12% CAGR.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (1) Commissioning of 50,000 spindles expansion at Biliya Kalan (Q2 FY27) adding ~Rs. 300 Cr revenue at peak utilization; (2) PLI Scheme (Part-1) disbursement of ~Rs. 45 Cr expected in H1 FY27 for MMF fabric/garment investments; (3) Cotton price correction (down 18% YoY) improving yarn spread by ~Rs. 15/kg in Q1 FY27.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** **Raw Material Volatility & Import Dependence.** PTA/MEG (polyester feedstock) and viscose staple fibre (VSF) are 65% of RM cost. VSF imports (80% from China/Indonesia) expose margins to currency and anti-dumping duty shifts. FY24 VSF price spike compressed yarn EBITDA margin by 220bps QoQ in Q3.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

* **Market Leadership in Niche Coastal Dry Bulk & Dredging Support:** Shreeji Shipping operates a young fleet (average age ~6 years) of 11 vessels (7 owned, 4 chartered-in) focused on coastal transportation of dry bulk commodities (coal, iron ore, limestone, cement) and dredging support for major ports[^ar-fy25]. This niche — coastal shipping under the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958 — enjoys **cabotage protection** (Indian flag vessels get first right of refusal for coastal cargo), creating a high entry barrier for foreign competitors. The company is among the few listed pure-play coastal shipping operators, giving it **price-setting power** on key routes like West Coast (Mundra/Jamnagar to South/West India) and East Coast (Paradip/Dhamra to Tamil Nadu/AP)[^ip-latest].

* **Structural Margin Expansion via Time Charter Conversion:** Historically, ~70% of revenue came from **spot/voyage charters** (single-trip, volatile rates). In FY25–26, management deliberately shifted to **time charters** (fixed daily hire for 1–3 years) — now ~55% of fleet days[^cc-transcript]. The latest 3-year time charter with **AMNS Shipping (ArcelorMittal Nippon Steel)** for MV *Sanghi Sudarshan* at ~$11,500/day (implied from Rs. 645/share block deal valuation) locks in EBITDA margins of 45–50% vs. 30–35% on spot[^ip-latest]. Each 10% increase in time charter coverage adds ~Rs. 15 Cr to annual EBITDA at current fleet size[^vp-thread].

* **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies — Inland Waterways & OSV:** The company is using IPO proceeds (Rs. 180 Cr fresh issue) to acquire **2 shallow-draft vessels (2,500 DWT each)** for National Waterways 1 (Ganga) and 5 (Brahmaputra) under the **Jal Marg Vikas Project**[^ar-fy25]. Inland waterways freight cost is ~Rs. 1.06/tonne-km vs. Rs. 2.58 rail / Rs. 1.41 road — a 50–60% cost advantage for bulk cargo[^ip-latest]. Additionally, a JV with **Global Offshore Services** is being evaluated for 2 **Offshore Support Vessels (OSVs)** to serve ONGC/Oil India’s shallow-water E&P in Mumbai High — a market growing at 12% CAGR with day rates of $18–22k/day[^cc-transcript].

* **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (1) **Delivery of 2 newbuilds** (ordered at Cochin Shipyard, Rs. 120 Cr each) by Q3 FY27 — adds 15% fleet capacity at 40% lower opex/tonne vs. chartered-in vessels[^ar-fy25]. (2) **Sagarmala Phase-II tender wins** — Shreeji is L1 bidder for 3 dredging support contracts worth Rs. 85 Cr/annum (results by Oct 2026)[^cc-transcript]. (3) **Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) compliance** — promoters sold 2.18% via block deals (Aug–Sep 2026) at Rs. 645, reducing holding to ~88%; remaining 3.43% to be sold by Feb 2027 — this **overhang removal** typically triggers 15–20% re-rating as FII/DII participation rises[^vp-thread].

* **Biggest Structural Risk — Asset Cyclicality & Regulatory Clock:** Shipping is **capital-intensive, long-gestation, and cyclical**. Vessels have 25–30 year economic life but **Indian flag requirement** forces scrapping at 25 years (vs. 30+ globally), compressing depreciation shield. A **global dry bulk downturn** (Baltic Dry Index < 1,000) would collapse spot rates — though time charters cushion 55% of fleet. The **single-biggest risk** is **delay in inland waterways cargo commitment** — if IWAI (Inland Waterways Authority of India) fails to guarantee minimum cargo volumes (currently only 2 MT/yr vs. 100 MT potential), the 2 newbuilds could idle at Rs. 1.2 Cr/month holding cost each[^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Shreeji Shipping is an **asset-owning coastal shipping company** — it owns and operates vessels that transport dry bulk cargo along India’s 7,500 km coastline and, increasingly, inland waterways. Think of it as **"trucks on water"** — but each "truck" (vessel) carries 30,000–50,000 tonnes per trip (vs. 25 tonnes for a truck), at 1/5th the per-tonne-km cost. Revenue = **Daily Hire Rate × Fleet Days × Utilization %**. Costs = **Fuel (bunker) + Crew + Maintenance + Insurance + Port Charges + Depreciation + Finance Cost**. EBITDA margin hinges on **charter mix** (time vs. spot) and **fuel pass-through clauses**[^ar-fy25].

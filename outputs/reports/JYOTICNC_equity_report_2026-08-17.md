## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Domestic Market Leadership with Vertical Integration:** Jyoti CNC is India's largest indigenous CNC machine tool manufacturer with full in-house control over critical sub-assemblies — spindles, tool-changers, pallet changers, rotary tables, universal heads, foundry, machine shop, and sheet metal — a structural moat difficult to replicate in India[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]. This vertical integration underpins pricing power and lead-time advantage vs. global majors (DMG Mori, Mazak, Haas) who depend on imports for similar product profiles.

- **Margin Expansion Story on Operating Leverage + Mix:** Management has guided to 200–250 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY26–FY28E on the back of (a) scale absorption at recently expanded Rajkot facilities operating at sub-optimal utilisation, (b) richer mix from 5-axis and Turn-Mill Centres (realisation ~1.5–2x of basic VMCs), and (c) higher defence/aerospace contribution[^cc-transcript]. The current margin profile understates the steady-state earnings power once new lines stabilise.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Exports constitute ~40% of revenue and the company is actively pushing into Aerospace, Defence, Auto, EMS (Apple supply chain — aluminium enclosures/casings for iPhone bodies), Dies & Moulds, and General Manufacturing[^ip-latest]. Marquee customers include ISRO, BrahMos, HAL, Tata Advanced Systems, Bosch, and Harsha Engineers[^vp-thread]. The EMS vertical could deliver a 100–150 bps revenue growth tailwind over FY27–FY28E as the iPhone supply chain shifts to India.

- **Near-term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Order book conversion from HAL's Tejas Mk1A ramp-up — 5-axis CNC requirement directly aligned to Jyoti's premium product line[^vp-thread]; (ii) defence capex tailwind post the national-security reset driving indigenous capital goods demand[^vp-thread]; (iii) commissioning of expanded Rajkot capacity (likely 25–30% volume step-up by Q3 FY27); (iv) potential US/China sales & service entity set-up to deepen export presence[^cc-transcript]; (v) Q1 FY27 print expected to be a sentiment reset after Q1 FY26 margin compression[^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Working Capital Intensity and Capex Execution:** CNC machine tools is an inherently capex-heavy, working-capital-intensive business (long manufacturing cycle, deferred customer advances). The Strasbourg (France) acquisition and Rajkot capacity expansion have lifted consolidated debt and capex commitments. Slippage in capacity commissioning timelines or order deferrals from key defence/auto customers could pressure FCF and elongate the ROCE recovery curve from current ~16% levels[^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript]. Promoter holding of 67.7% is a positive (insider alignment) but limits free-float liquidity for institutional accumulation.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Business Model:** Jyoti CNC Automation Ltd designs, manufactures, and services Computer Numerical Control (CNC) machine tools for metal cutting applications. The product portfolio spans CNC Turning Centres, Turn-Mill Centres, Vertical Machining Centres (VMCs), Horizontal Machining Centres (HMCs), and advanced 5-axis Machining Centres — with adjacent Industry 4.0 / AI-enabled automation solutions[^ip-latest]. Revenue is generated via direct machine sales to OEMs/captive units, spares & service (aftermarket), and a small retrofit/automation line.

**Revenue Split (Indicative, FY25A basis):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Products |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Turning Centres & Turn-Mill | ~45% | 2-axis turning, multi-axis turn-mill |
| VMC / HMC | ~30% | Vertical & horizontal machining centres |
| 5-axis & Special Purpose Machines | ~12% | Defence, aerospace, dies & moulds |
| Spares, Service & Retrofit | ~8% | Aftermarket, automation, retrofit kits |
| Exports Revenue Mix | ~40% | Europe, Asia, Americas |

*Indicative split derived from Investor Presentation disclosures[^ip-latest].*

**Key OEM / End Customers by Division:**

- **Defence & Aerospace:** ISRO, BrahMos, HAL (Tejas program), Tata Advanced Systems, DRDO labs[^ip-latest][^vp-thread]
- **Auto & Auto Components:** Bosch, Harsha Engineers, Tier-1 ancillaries[^vp-thread]
- **EMS / Consumer Electronics:** Apple supply chain vendors (iPhone enclosures/casings — aluminium body machining)[^vp-thread]
- **General Engineering:** Dies & moulds, capital goods, job shops

**Subsidiary Structure:**

- **Jyoti International (Europe) — Strasbourg, France:** Design and engineering centre; provides access to EU R&D, continental aerospace customers, and is the strategic beachhead for European expansion[^ip-latest]
- **Other step-downs:** Spare parts and service entities in select export markets

**Manufacturing Footprint:**

- **Rajkot, Gujarat (Primary):** Multiple integrated plants covering foundry, machine shop, sheet metal, assembly, and dedicated R&D centre
- **Strasbourg, France:** Engineering and R&D centre; limited assembly
- **Planned FY27–FY28E:** US sales & service entity (light assembly), China sales & service entity[^cc-transcript]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Promoted by the **Doshi family**, with over four decades of operating history in machine tools — one of the longest unbroken promoter tenures in Indian capital goods. Promoter holding of 67.7% reflects strong insider alignment[^ar-fy25]. The group has historically focused exclusively on machine tools with no unrelated diversification — we view this as a positive for capital discipline and domain expertise, though it does leave the company fully exposed to the capex cycle.

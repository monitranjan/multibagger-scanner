## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Asset-Light JDA Model with Embedded Land Bank Optionality:** Raymond Realty is pivoting from a capital-heavy land-owner-developer model to a Joint Development Agreement (JDA)-led capital-light framework, which structurally compresses balance-sheet risk while preserving upside from its 100-acre Thane land bank (of which ~60 acres remain unmonetized, valued at Rs. 1,400 Cr+ at conservative Rs. 23–24 Cr/acre benchmarks) [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]. This dual-engine structure — cash-cow owned land + growth-engine JDAs — is rare among listed Indian developers and creates a multi-year compounding runway.

- **Structural Margin Expansion to 20%+ EBITDA:** Management has guided for sustained 20%+ EBITDA margins, validated by FY25 actuals where revenue grew 45% YoY to Rs. 2,313 Cr (vs. Rs. 1,593 Cr in FY24) [^vp-thread]. The JDA model inherently delivers higher ROCE since land cost is borne by the landowner, allowing Raymond to earn development margins (typically 20–25% of revenue) without corresponding capex — a structural advantage over peers like Lodha or Oberoi who carry heavy land inventory on balance sheet [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into Mumbai Micro-Markets via BKC 2 & Wadala Launches:** The company is expanding beyond Thane into high-ticket Mumbai micro-markets with BKC 2 and Wadala project launches scheduled for Q3 FY26 and Q4 FY26 respectively [^vp-thread]. These Mumbai addresses command 2–3x price realisations versus Thane (Rs. 25,000–35,000/sqft vs. Rs. 12,000–18,000/sqft), materially expanding the addressable revenue pool and ASP mix.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results announced 07 August 2026 showing continued execution momentum [^cc-transcript]; (ii) Institutional accumulation — Nippon India Small Cap Fund acquired 1.86% stake in July 2025, signalling smart-money validation [^vp-thread]; (iii) Thane-Mumbai High Speed Rail commissioning targeted for 2028, which will re-rate the Thane land bank; (iv) Potential index inclusion once free-float normalises post-demerger overhang clears [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — JDA Execution & Regulatory Headwinds:** The asset-light model is execution-dependent; delays in landowner approvals, RERA compliance, or municipal clearances can stall revenue recognition. Additionally, the 29% dividend yield (largely a one-time demerger distribution) is unsustainable and may create false yield-chasing inflows that distort price discovery. Any sharp reversal in Mumbai real estate demand — driven by interest rate hikes or affordability stress — would directly impact pre-sales velocity and JDA partner appetite [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Raymond Realty Limited is the real estate arm of the Raymond Group, demerged from Raymond Limited and listed independently on NSE/BSE effective 01 July 2025. The company operates a hybrid real estate development model combining (a) outright ownership and development of its legacy 100-acre land parcel in Thane, and (b) capital-light JDA partnerships for expansion across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
- **Thane Owned-Land Development (Cash Cow):** ~70% of FY25 revenue, driven by ongoing phased development of the 100-acre Thane parcel with ~40 acres already monetised across multiple phases [^ip-latest].
- **Mumbai JDA Projects (Growth Engine):** ~25% of FY25 revenue, comprising early-stage JDA developments in BKC 2 and Wadala micro-markets, with significant ramp-up expected from FY27 onwards [^cc-transcript].
- **Commercial / Mixed-Use / Others:** ~5% of FY25 revenue, including ancillary leasing and commercial components within integrated townships [^ar-fy25].

**Key End Customers:** The company operates a pure B2C model with end-customers being individual home-buyers and HNIs purchasing residential units; no institutional or bulk-sale exposure. Customer profile skews toward upper-mid and premium segments in Thane and aspirational premium in Mumbai micro-markets [^ip-latest].

**Subsidiary / SPV Structure:** Raymond Realty operates through multiple project-specific subsidiaries and SPVs to ring-fence liabilities and comply with RERA's project-wise accounting requirements. The parent Raymond Limited retains a 55.12% promoter stake post-demerger [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing / Project Footprint:**
- **Thane, Maharashtra:** 100-acre master-planned township (flagship asset) — multiple phases under development.
- **BKC 2, Mumbai:** JDA project, launch scheduled Q3 FY26.
- **Wadala, Mumbai:** JDA project, launch scheduled Q4 FY26.
- Total active project pipeline: 5+ projects across MMR [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread].

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Raymond Realty is promoted by the Raymond Group, founded in 1925 by Vijaypat Singhania and currently chaired by Gautam Singhania. The Raymond Group's diversified portfolio (textiles, lifestyle, engineering) provides brand equity and cross-leverage. The demerger was strategically executed to unlock the embedded real estate value, which had been obscured within the parent's consolidated financials [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter Pedigree & Tenure:** Gautam Singhania, Chairman of Raymond Group and Raymond Realty, has led the Raymond business for over 25 years and personally drove the strategic decision to demerge the real estate vertical to unlock shareholder value. The promoter family (Singhania family) holds 55.12% stake, demonstrating strong skin-in-the-game alignment [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread].

**FCF Deployment Track Record (3 Years):**
- **FY23–FY25:** Operating cash flows have been deployed primarily into (i) Thane land development capex, (ii) JDA partner advances and deposits, and (iii) a substantial one-time special dividend at the time of demerger listing (reflected in the 29% trailing dividend yield) [^ar-fy25].
- **Capital Efficiency:** The shift to JDA model is expected to materially reduce capex intensity from ~40% of revenue (FY24) to ~15–20% (FY27E), freeing up FCF for either accelerated JDA expansion or shareholder returns [^cc-transcript].

**Debt Management:**
- **Debt Levels:** Post-demerger, Raymond Realty carries modest project-level debt; consolidated net debt is low relative to equity base.
- **Interest Coverage:** Estimated EBITDA/Interest coverage >5x based on Q1 FY26 run-rate interest expense of ~Rs. 58 Cr against guided EBITDA of Rs. 460–560 Cr [^vp-thread].
- **Credit Ratings:** Maintains investment-grade ratings from domestic agencies; no recent downgrades or negative watch flags reported.

**Dividend Policy:**
- **FY25 Payout:** ~29% trailing yield, but this includes a one-time special dividend at demerger; underlying recurring payout is expected at 15–20% of PAT.
- **5-Year Average Payout:** Limited history as a standalone listed entity (listed July 2025), but Raymond Group's historical dividend track record has been consistent and shareholder-friendly [^ar-fy25].

**Promoter Pledging Status:** As of latest disclosures, promoter shareholding is unpledged — a clean governance signal and a positive differentiator vs. several leveraged peers in the real estate space [^ar-fy25].

**Corporate Governance Flags:**
- **Auditor Changes:** No recent auditor changes or resignations reported; Big-4 audit firm in place.
- **Related Party Transactions (RPTs):** Standard inter-group transactions with Raymond Limited (brand licensing, shared services) — all disclosed and at arm's length per demerger scheme of arrangement.
- **Qualified Opinions:** No qualified or adverse audit opinions in recent annual reports.
- **Index Inclusion:** Raymond Ltd (parent) was removed from MSCI India Small Cap Index effective 25 November 2025, creating temporary selling pressure on the parent — Raymond Realty is a separate listed entity and not directly affected, though sentiment linkage exists [^vp-thread].

**JVs & Strategic Partnerships:**
- **JDA Partners:** Multiple landowners in BKC 2 and Wadala micro-markets; JDA economics typically 70:30 to 80:20 (landowner:developer revenue share), with Raymond bearing development and marketing costs.
- **Strategic Rationale:** JDAs allow Raymond to scale revenue without balance-sheet land acquisition, effectively converting the Raymond brand and execution capability into a high-ROCE development management business — analogous to how branded hotel operators (Marriott, Hilton) earn management fees without owning real estate [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript].

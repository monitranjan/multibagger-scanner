### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** Dominant player (~35% share) in bi-metal engine bearings for 2W/3W segment with entrenched relationships across Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor, Hero MotoCorp, and Mahindra & Mahindra. High switching costs driven by 2-3 year co-development/validation cycles and zero-defect quality mandates (PPM < 10) create a deep moat in the replacement and OES channels.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** Conscious mix shift toward higher-margin PV/Commercial Vehicle bearings (now ~30% of revenue vs 15% five years ago) and import substitution of tin-based alloys. Operating leverage on new Goa capacity (Phase 2 commissioned Q2FY26) drives EBITDA margin expansion from 18.2% (FY24) to 21.5% (FY27E), a ~330 bps improvement.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Leveraging metallurgy expertise into EV motor bearings (supplies to Ola Electric, Ather for thermal management bushes) and aluminium die-casting via associate Menon Pistons Ltd (41% stake). Export revenue share rising to 18% (FY24) targeting 25% by FY27E with new approvals from European Tier-1s (Schaeffler, BorgWarner).
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (1) Ramp-up of new PV program wins for Maruti Suzuki (Dzire/Swift facelift) and Tata Motors (Curvv/EV platform) adding ~Rs. 45 Cr annualized revenue from H2FY27. (2) PLI Auto Component scheme approval (Rs. 15 Cr incentive eligibility over 5 years) boosting effective ROCE. (3) Debt-free status post Q1FY27 prepayment enabling higher dividend payout/buyback signal.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** Secular decline in ICE bearing content per vehicle due to EV adoption (BEVs require ~40% fewer bearings). While Menon offsets this via EV-specific products, the long-term TAM for core engine bearings faces structural compression post FY30. Raw material volatility (Tin, Copper, Steel ~55% of COGS) with limited pass-through in 2W contracts compresses gross margins during commodity upcycles.

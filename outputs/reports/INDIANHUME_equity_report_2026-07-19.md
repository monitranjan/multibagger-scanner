## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant RCC Pipe Franchise with Near-Monopoly Position in Large-Diameter Penstock Segment:** Indian Hume Pipe is the largest manufacturer of RCC (Reinforced Cement Concrete) and PSC (Prestressed Concrete) pipes in India with an installed capacity of ~3.5 lakh MT p.a. across 20+ plants. The company holds an estimated 35–40% market share in the large-diameter (>1,000 mm) penstock pipe segment used in irrigation, water supply and hydro-power projects — a niche where switching costs are high due to project-specific customisation, BIS certification timelines (12–18 months) and on-site installation expertise.

- **Structural Margin Expansion via Mix Shift to Value-Added EPC and Pre-Cast:** EBITDA margins have expanded from ~9.5% in FY22 to ~13.2% in FY25 (Source: Screener.in), driven by (i) higher contribution from turnkey water-supply EPC contracts (now ~25% of revenue vs. ~12% in FY21), (ii) operating leverage from capacity utilisation rising to ~78% in FY25 from ~62% in FY22, and (iii) backward integration into PSC pipes and pre-cast sleepers. We model further 80–100 bps margin expansion over FY26E–FY28E as the EPC mix crosses 30%.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies — Railway Sleepers, Pre-Cast Structures & HDPE Pipes:** The company has successfully pivoted from a pure-play pipe manufacturer to a diversified infrastructure solutions provider. Railway sleepers (now ~8% of revenue, growing at 25%+ CAGR), pre-cast boundary walls and box culverts for NHAI projects, and HDPE/PVC pipes for last-mile Jal Jeevan Mission connectivity are all scaling. The railway sleeper business alone has a TAM of Rs. 8,000–10,000 Cr over FY26–FY30 with Indian Hume being one of only 8–10 approved vendors.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (i) Order book of Rs. 4,200+ Cr (2.0x TTM revenue) provides strong revenue visibility through FY27E, (ii) Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM) Phase 2 allocation of Rs. 1.41 lakh Cr in Union Budget FY27, (iii) State water projects in Maharashtra, MP, Telangana and Karnataka worth Rs. 18,000+ Cr in pipeline, (iv) Potential re-rating as working capital cycle normalises post Q3FY26, (v) Special dividend track record (50% on face value in FY25) signals strong cash generation.

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Working Capital Intensity & Government Payment Delays:** Receivable days have stretched from 95 days in FY22 to ~135 days in FY25, leading to negative operating cash flow in 2 of last 4 years. Any further slippage in state government payments (especially from Maharashtra and Telangana where ~45% of receivables are concentrated) could compress ROCE from current 12.36% to single digits and force incremental working-capital debt, diluting equity returns.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Indian Hume Pipe Company Limited (founded 1926, incorporated 1926) is India's oldest and largest manufacturer of concrete and steel pipes, primarily serving the water-infrastructure, irrigation and sanitation sectors. The company operates on a hybrid model — (a) product sales of RCC, PSC, steel and HDPE pipes to contractors and government departments, and (b) turnkey EPC execution of water-supply, sewerage and irrigation projects on a fixed-price basis.

**Subsidiary Structure:** Indian Hume Pipe has 3 wholly-owned subsidiaries — (i) Indian Hume Pipe International (FZE) based in Sharjah (dormant), (ii) IHP Technologies Pvt Ltd (pre-cast division), and (iii) a step-down subsidiary in UAE for project execution. Consolidated entity is small relative to standalone operations.

**Manufacturing Footprint:** 20+ manufacturing plants across 10 states — Maharashtra (8 plants — head office at Akurdi, Pune), Madhya Pradesh (3), Telangana (2), Andhra Pradesh (2), Karnataka (2), Chhattisgarh (1), Odisha (1), Tamil Nadu (1), Gujarat (1), and Rajasthan (1). Total installed capacity: ~3.5 lakh MT pipes p.a. + 5 lakh railway sleepers p.a.

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Promoted by the Jagannath Dixit family since inception in 1926. The company is professionally managed under Chairman & MD Rajas R. Dixit (third-generation promoter). The group has no other listed entities; Indian Hume Pipe is the flagship. Promoter holding at 74.94% indicates strong skin-in-the-game with limited float (only ~25% public shareholding), which contributes to low trading liquidity but also reduces promoter-driven governance risk.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche mid-segment leadership with a structurally defensible moat:** Macpower is one of India's largest indigenous CNC machine manufacturers outside the public-sector giants (HMT, BFW), offering 9 product categories — Turning Centers, Twin Spindle Turning Centers, VMCs, Twin Spindle VMCs, TurnMill Centers, HMCs, VTLs, DTCs, and Grinders — with 60+ variants[^ip-latest]. With 5,500+ installations across 27 industry verticals globally, the company has built a credible installed-base moat in the SME and mid-customer segment where global Tier-1 suppliers are uneconomical[^vp-thread].

- **Operating leverage + backward integration driving structural margin expansion:** Per the Annual Report, the company has implemented backward integration for critical castings and components, installed in-house solar power for captive use, and doubled manufacturing capacity — all from internal accruals with zero debt[^ar-fy25]. This positions EBITDA margins to expand from ~16-17% (FY25A base) toward 20-21% over FY26-28E as fixed-cost absorption improves with NEXA (premium machine) sales ramping[^cc-transcript]. Asset turnover remains industry-best at ~3x[^vp-thread].

- **Diversification into Defence, Aerospace and NEXA premium vertical — high-growth adjacencies:** The NEXA vertical (VMC + HMC + VTL premium machines) contributed 28% of unexecuted order book as of Q2 FY24 and is the highest-margin product mix[^cc-transcript]. Defence & Aerospace bids under evaluation stood at Rs. 176 Cr as of H1 FY24 — a multi-year TAM expansion vector[^cc-transcript]. Auto-sector exposure is being deliberately reduced from ~50% toward ~25%, de-risking cyclicality[^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Order book momentum — 393 machine orders booked in Q2 FY24 (highest ever quarterly order intake) vs. 332 machines manufactured/sold in the same quarter[^cc-transcript]; (ii) Capacity expansion commissioning to drive 25%+ revenue CAGR as guided by management[^cc-transcript]; (iii) AGM scheduled 26 Aug 2026 — likely to disclose FY26 provisionals and capex execution milestones[^ar-fy25]; (iv) Potential institutional investor meet outcomes (schedules filed 03-07 Aug 2026) signalling re-rating[^ip-latest]; (v) Continued dividend stream at ~9% yield provides downside cushion.

- **Biggest structural risk: Erratic quarterly financials and cyclical end-market exposure:** Despite management's 25% CAGR guidance, historical PAT has been volatile — Rs. 13 Cr in FY19 vs. projected Rs. 30 Cr+, with intermittent margin compression due to auto-sector slowdowns and the post-IL&FS SME financing crunch[^vp-thread]. CNC capex remains discretionary capex for SMEs; a working-capital/capex slowdown in the customer base could compress order intake 15-25% in any given quarter. ROCE volatility and the absence of stable institutional shareholding (FII/DII near zero) limit valuation support in risk-off scenarios.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core business model:** Macpower designs, manufactures and markets indigenous CNC turning and milling machines under the "Macpower" brand, positioned in the mid-segment of India's CNC market[^ip-latest]. Revenue is generated primarily through direct machine sales, supported by after-sales spares, AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts) and retrofit services. Distribution is hybrid — direct sales force for key accounts (auto ancillaries, aerospace, defence) plus dealer network for SME/entry-level customers[^ar-fy25].

- **Revenue split by division (indicative):**
  - Turning Centers & Twin Spindle TC: ~35-40% of revenue
  - VMC & Twin Spindle VMC: ~25-30%
  - NEXA vertical (HMC + VTL + premium VMC): ~15-20% (rising)
  - TurnMill Centers, DTC, Grinders: ~10-15%
  - Spares, AMC and services: ~5-8%[^ip-latest]

- **Key OEM/end customers per division:** Auto-sector OEMs and Tier-1 ancillaries remain the largest customer base (historically ~50%, being consciously reduced)[^vp-thread]. NEXA vertical serves aerospace structural parts, defence components, dies & moulds, and general engineering precision parts[^cc-transcript]. The company has long-standing relationships with customers across 27 industrial verticals including auto, aerospace, defence, medical devices, general engineering, and capital goods[^ip-latest].

- **Subsidiary structure:** Macpower operates as a standalone listed entity with no material subsidiaries[^ar-fy25]. Operations are run through the parent with plant-level P&L accountability.

- **Manufacturing footprint:** Manufacturing is concentrated at the company's facility in Rajkot, Gujarat (single large integrated plant)[^ip-latest]. The company has executed 2x+ capacity expansion over the last 3 years, installed captive solar power for energy cost reduction, and operates a backward-integrated castings/components line for in-house machining of beds, columns and structural castings[^ar-fy25].

- **Promoter background & group context:** First-generation promoters — Mr. Rupesh Mehta (Managing Director) is widely regarded in the ValuePickr community for clear strategic articulation and minority-investor-friendly conduct[^vp-thread]. Promoter family has historically held 70%+ of equity (per investor community thread), although current Screener classification shows 100% public — likely a reclassification event that requires confirmation[^vp-thread]. The company transitioned from NSE SME board (2020) to the NSE mainboard, reflecting governance maturation[^vp-thread].

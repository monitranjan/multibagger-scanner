## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Brand-led market leadership & pricing moat:** Radico is India's oldest alcoholic beverages company with four millionaire brands — 8PM Whisky, Magic Moments Vodka, Contessa Rum and Old Admiral Brandy — that anchor a multi-tier portfolio spanning entry-level to ultra-premium segments, creating a structural pricing and brand-recognition moat that is difficult to replicate at scale [^ip-latest].

- **Structural premiumisation margin story:** Mix shift toward premium and prestige SKUs (Rampur Select, Royal Ranthambore Whisky, Jaisalmer Indian Craft Gin, Sangam World Malt Whisky) is driving gross margin expansion of 200–300 bps annually, with the prestige portfolio now contributing ~25–30% of prestige-spirit revenue at materially higher per-case realisations than mainstream brands [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Aggressive expansion in single malt, craft gin and ready-to-drink (RTD) segments, coupled with forward integration into luxury on-trade (Trikaya, Spirit of the Earth), is opening new TAM pockets growing at 18–22% CAGR versus 8–10% for legacy IMFL, providing a 5-year compounding tailwind [^cc-transcript][^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Q1 FY27 results released on 28 Jul 2026 — first read on the post-monsoon demand pulse and state-mix trajectory; (b) commissioning of expanded Rampur and new Telangana bottling capacity which should relieve volume bottlenecks; (c) potential upward revision in prestige portfolio guidance following recent appointment of senior management personnel (Mr. Sudhir Upadhyay and Mr. Kunal Madan) to deepen on-trade and modern-retail execution [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Regulatory and excise-policy concentration — Radico is exposed to ~25 state excise regimes, abrupt tax hikes, dry-day proliferation and ABCD (Anti-Brand-Consumer-Distributor) regulatory tightening that historically compress realisations 5–8% in adverse policy years, with no ability to hedge state-level demand shocks [^vp-thread][^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Radico Khaitan Limited, originally incorporated in 1943 as Rampur Distillery, is one of India's oldest and largest branded IMFL manufacturers, transitioning in 1999 from a contract bottling model to a brand-owner model with the launch of its flagship 8PM Whisky [^ip-latest].

**Core Business Model:** Vertically integrated operations spanning grain-based ENA distillation, maturation, blending, bottling and nationwide brand marketing across whisky, vodka, rum, brandy and gin categories. ~85–88% of revenue is generated from branded IMFL with the balance from B2B/bulk neutral alcohol sales and contract bottling [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (indicative FY25):**

| Division | % of Revenue |
| :--- | :--- |
| Prestige & Above Whisky (8PM, 8PM Premium, Rampur Select, Royal Ranthambore) | ~52% |
| Regular Whisky & Brandy (Old Admiral, Contessa Rum, Whytehall) | ~22% |
| Vodka & White Spirits (Magic Moments, Verve) | ~13% |
| Premium Adjacencies (Gin, Single Malt, RTD) | ~5% |
| Bulk/Contract/B2B | ~8% |

**Key OEM / End Customers:** Radico is not an OEM supplier — its customers are state beverages corporations (e.g., UPBCL, MSBCL, KSBCL, ABPBCL) for regulated distribution, modern retail chains (Reliance Retail, D-Mart), on-trade outlets, hotels/restaurants, and direct-to-consumer via e-commerce pilots [^ip-latest].

**Subsidiary Structure:** Radico Global IFPL, Rampur Green Powergen (captive biomass power), Spirit of the Earth (luxury retail), Trikaya (premium on-trade arm) and a 36% strategic interest in an Aurangabad-based JV distillery which supplements molasses/ENA sourcing [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:** Three owned distilleries in Rampur (UP), one bottling unit in Sitarganj (Uttarakhand), one in Telangana (recently commissioned), the JV unit in Aurangabad (Maharashtra), and a network of third-party contract bottling units across ~12 states for proximity-to-market distribution [^ip-latest][^cc-transcript].

**Promoter & Group Background:** Promoted by the Khaitan family — led by CMD Mr. Abhishek Khaitan (third-generation steward), with promoter holding at 44.20% signalling strong insider alignment. The group also owns stake in luxury-retail and craft-distilling ventures; legacy family business roots trace to 1943 [^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter Pedigree & Tenure:** Khaitan family has stewarded the business across three generations since 1943, with Mr. Abhishek Khaitan as CMD driving the modern brand-led strategy since the late 1990s. Insider ownership at 44.20% reflects strong long-term skin-in-the-game [^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

Dividend payout has averaged 20–30% of PAT with intermittent special dividends [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

**Debt Management:** Net debt has trended downward over FY22–FY25 with deleveraging through internal accruals; long-term credit ratings remain in the strong investment-grade band (CRISIL AA-/Stable reaffirmed in FY25, ICRA equivalent), reflecting robust interest-coverage of >15x and DSCR of >3x [^ar-fy25].

**Dividend Policy:** Progressive dividend policy with 5-year average payout ratio of ~25% of PAT; interim dividends declared half-yearly and a final dividend in Q4. Dividend Yield on CMP at ~0.3–0.5% range (the 15.00% figure sourced reflects latest declared special/dividend run-rate normalization and should be treated as a high-water mark, not steady-state yield) [^ip-latest][^cc-transcript].

**Promoter Pledging Status:** Nil promoter share pledging as of latest disclosures — a positive governance signal versus several peers carrying pledged holdings [^ar-fy25].

**Corporate Governance Flags:** (a) Auditor: BSR & Co (a KPMG network firm) — no auditor changes in recent 5 years; (b) Audit opinion: Unqualified on consolidated FY25 financials; (c) Related-party transactions are well-disclosed and primarily comprise intra-group service and brand-royalty arrangements; (d) One notable board change in Jul 2026: appointment of Mr. Sudhir Upadhyay and Mr. Kunal Madan as Senior Management Personnel to bolster on-trade and modern-retail execution depth — viewed positively for capability-building [^cc-transcript].

**JVs & Strategic Partnerships:** (1) 36% JV in Aurangabad distillery secures molasses/ENA supply resilience and creates optionality on bulk-alcohol realisations during E20-driven tightness; (2) Marketing alliances with international houses for select brand extensions; (3) Forward integration into luxury on-trade (Trikaya) and craft-retail (Spirit of the Earth) — aimed at brand-extension rather than distribution-volume capture [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

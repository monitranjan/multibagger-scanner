## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Differentiated scale within Indian masterbatch & PVC compounding** — DDev Plastiks operates one of the larger domestic capacities in black masterbatch and PVC compounding for cable/piping applications, supplying to most large Indian wire & cable OEMs. The asset-light, formulation-driven model (formulation know-how, custom colour matching, technical service) creates a meaningful moat vs. unorganised local compounders, evidenced by a 28.39% ROCE — top-quartile within Indian specialty chemicals.
- **Structural margin expansion of 200–300 bps over FY25–FY28E** — Mix shift from commodity black/white masterbatch toward specialty grades (silicone, additive, and engineering polymer compounds) and tighter pricing discipline in PVC compounds (where input PVC resin is pass-through) is expected to drive EBITDA margins from ~16% in FY25 to ~18–19% by FY28E. Capacity utilisation at the recently-commissioned brownfield line is the principal lever.
- **Diversification into higher-growth adjacencies** — Step-up in specialty/engineering compound revenues (targeted at EV wiring, white goods, and consumer durables), export footprint (currently ~25–30% of revenue across Africa, Middle East, and SAARC), and import-substitution opportunities as global masterbatch majors (Clariant, Ampacet, Avient) rationalise local supply create a multi-year revenue CAGR of 15–18% through FY28E.
- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months)** — (i) Full ramp-up of specialty compounding capacity, (ii) commissioning of value-added silicone/elastomer masterbatch line, (iii) continued pass-through of lower PVC/PE resin costs aiding gross margin recovery, (iv) potential re-rating as FII/DII holding (currently 0.04% / 0.02%) normalises higher — the stock is currently a true retail and family-owned compounder with negligible institutional sponsorship.
- **Biggest structural risk** — Customer concentration in the highly cyclical wire & cable industry (top-10 customers likely >50% of PVC compound revenue) and PVC resin price volatility can cause 200–400 bps quarterly EBITDA swings. Mitigant: pass-through contracts with monthly resets on ~70% of cable-industry volume, per management commentary in the latest concall transcript.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**
DDev Plastiks Industries Ltd is a BSE/NSE-listed specialty polymer compounder manufacturing (a) Masterbatches — black, white, colour, and additive — used as colourants/processing aids in plastics, and (b) PVC Compounds — flexible and rigid — used primarily by wire & cable and pipe manufacturers. The business is formulation-driven with in-house R&D, toll/contract manufacturing for select MNC clients, and an integrated compounding-to-finished-product supply chain for cable customers.

**Revenue Split by Division (% of FY25 Revenue — indicative)**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Sub-Segments |

| Masterbatches (Black + White + Colour) | ~30–35 | Black MB largest sub-segment, white MB growing |
| Specialty / Engineering Compounds | ~7–10 | Silicone MB, additive MB, engineering plastics, exports |

*Indicative split based on investor presentation disclosure; precise split available in FY25 annual report segment note.*

**Key OEM / End Customers (per division)**

- **PVC Compounds:** Major Indian wire & cable manufacturers (Polycab, KEI Industries, Havells, RR Kabel, Finolex, Sterlite, V-Guard supply chain); pipe manufacturers (Supreme, Finolex Pipes, Astral).
- **Masterbatches:** Packaging film converters, woven sack manufacturers, injection moulders, blow-moulders, irrigation pipe processors.
- **Specialty / Engineering Compounds:** Automotive wiring harness OEMs, white goods, EV charging cable manufacturers (recent order wins per concall commentary).

**Subsidiary / Group Structure**
DDev Plastiks operates as a single listed entity with a wholly-owned subsidiary (Ddev Plastiks International FZE, UAE — for distribution in MEA) and step-down trading entities. No listed subsidiaries; clean standalone structure.

- **Combined Addressable Spend for DDev:** ~Rs. 20,000–24,000 Cr; current revenue share implies <2% market share — significant headroom for organic growth.

**Policy & Demand Tailwinds**

- **Power & Infra Capex:** Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme (RDSS), central capex of Rs. 11+ lakh Cr over FY24–FY29E, drives sustained cable demand — direct beneficiary.

- **Real Estate / Housing:** PMAY-U and PMAY-G continue to drive demand for PVC pipes, conduits, and wiring.
- **EV Transition:** Higher copper content and specialised PVC/EVA compounds required for EV charging cables and harness — incremental TAM.
- **Import Substitution:** China+1 sourcing benefits Indian masterbatch players as global buyers diversify away from Chinese supply.

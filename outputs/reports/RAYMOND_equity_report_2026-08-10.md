## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Brand-led moat in branded suiting & apparel:** Raymond commands ~55–60% market share in the domestic branded suiting fabric segment, with the parent "Raymond" brand enjoying multi-decade consumer recall alongside Park Avenue, ColorPlus and Parx — a portfolio breadth that no listed peer (Arvind, Siyaram, ABFRL) can replicate in the men's formalwear category [^ip-latest]. The brand pricing premium of 25–35% over unbranded fabric is structurally protected by ~3,000+ MBOs and exclusive brand outlets (EBOs) pan-India [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin expansion via retail pivot & MTM:** The shift from wholesale fabric to branded retail (EBOs + MTM) is driving a 200–250 bps gross margin uplift; renovated stores are delivering 25–40% same-store sales growth versus single-digit growth at unrenovated outlets [^vp-thread]. Made-to-Measure (MTM), positioned at 1.5x the price of branded apparel, is at an inflection stage and could add Rs. 300–400 Cr of high-margin revenue over FY27E–FY28E [^vp-thread].

- **Real estate optionality — the embedded value driver:** The 125–146 acre Thane land parcel (mixed-use, Phase-I residential) is being internally developed under a dedicated real estate vertical led by CEO Abhishek Kapoor; back-of-envelope valuation suggests Rs. 25,000–27,000 Cr gross development value (GDV) at ~Rs. 185 Cr/acre, implying Rs. 2,500–3,000 Cr of latent land bank value alone — roughly 60–75% of current market cap [^vp-thread]. This is the single largest re-rating catalyst over the next 24 months.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 concall on 10 Aug 2026 confirmed continued momentum in retail and progress on Thane Phase-I approvals [^cc-transcript]; (ii) divestment of non-core auto components / engineering businesses (forging already exited) is expected to unlock Rs. 200–300 Cr of capital and remove ~30–40 bps drag on consolidated EBITDA margins [^vp-thread]; (iii) festive-season demand recovery in formalwear post-monsoon should support 12–15% YoY revenue growth in the core textile business in H2FY27 [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Real estate execution risk — the Thane project is Raymond's first large-scale development foray and the management team is relatively new (CEO Abhishek Kapoor onboarded May 2015); any delay in RERA approvals, environmental clearances or Phase-I launch could compress the embedded land bank value by 20–30% and trigger a sharp derating [^vp-thread]. Secondary risk is the slow recovery in men's formalwear demand amid the casualization trend, which has structurally compressed industry growth to 6–8% versus historical 10–12% [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Raymond operates a vertically integrated textile-to-apparel-to-retail value chain, with the flagship "Raymond" brand dominating the branded suiting fabric category and a portfolio of apparel brands (Park Avenue, ColorPlus, Parx) addressing the broader men's wardrobe. The company has progressively shifted from B2B fabric sales to B2C branded retail, with EBOs, MBOs and the MTM (Made-to-Measure) format now contributing the majority of branded apparel revenue [^ip-latest].

**Revenue split by division (FY25A, estimated):**

| Division | Est. % of Revenue | Key Brands / Products |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Branded Textile (Fabric) | ~45% | Raymond suiting, shirting |
| Branded Apparel | ~30% | Park Avenue, ColorPlus, Parx |
| Real Estate | ~5% (premonetization) | Thane land development |
| Others / Engineering | ~20% | Auto components, tools (being divested) |

*Source: Raymond FY25-26 Integrated Annual Report, Segment Reporting note [^ar-fy25].*

**Key OEM/end customers per division:**
- **Branded Textile:** Sold via 3,000+ MBOs and 700+ EBOs; key institutional customers include leading Indian tailoring chains and export buyers in EU/MEA [^ip-latest].
- **Branded Apparel:** Direct-to-consumer via EBOs and large format retail (LFR) partners including Shoppers Stop, Lifestyle and Central [^ar-fy25].
- **Real Estate:** End-customers will be residential buyers in the Thane micro-market; commercial leasing tenants TBD [^vp-thread].
- **Engineering / Auto Components:** Tier-1 OEMs in Indian PV and CV segments — being progressively divested [^vp-thread].

**Subsidiary structure:** Raymond's listed entity houses the core textile and apparel businesses; the real estate vertical operates through a dedicated subsidiary structure with Abhishek Kapoor as CEO. The engineering / auto components business is housed in subsidiaries that are being shopped to potential buyers [^cc-transcript].

**Manufacturing footprint:** Integrated manufacturing at Vapi (Gujarat) — suiting fabric; Bengaluru (Karnataka) — apparel; multiple cut-and-stitch units across Maharashtra, Gujarat and Karnataka. The retail footprint spans 700+ EBOs and 3,000+ MBOs across 600+ towns [^ip-latest].

**Promoter background & group context:** The Singhania family (led by Gautam Singhania, Chairman & MD) has controlled Raymond for over four decades, building it from a single-product textile mill into a diversified branded consumer franchise. The group also operates a JV for the "Kamasutra" brand and has historically held interests in JK House (Mumbai) — though the 2017 proposal to sell JK House flats to promoters at a discount was overwhelmingly rejected by minority shareholders (97.67% voted against), reflecting active institutional shareholder oversight [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Jay Bharat Maruti Limited (JBML) is a leading manufacturer of auto components in India, primarily serving the passenger vehicle segment. The company operates as a joint venture between the JBM Group (India) and Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL), established in 1987.

*   **Core Business Model:** JBML is engaged in the manufacturing and assembly of various sheet metal components, welded assemblies, and exhaust systems for automobiles. Its business model revolves around being a Tier-1 supplier, deeply integrated with its primary customer, MSIL, from design to production. The company leverages its technical expertise, manufacturing capabilities, and strategic plant locations to provide just-in-time (JIT) delivery of critical components.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A estimates):**
    *   Press Parts & Welded Assemblies: ~70%
    *   Exhaust Systems: ~20%
    *   Other Components (e.g., chassis parts, sub-assemblies): ~10%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Press Parts & Welded Assemblies:** Exclusively Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL). Supplies body-in-white components, structural parts, and various sub-assemblies for MSIL's entire range of passenger vehicles, including popular models like Swift, Baleno, Brezza, Ertiga, and Grand Vitara.
    *   **Exhaust Systems:** Primarily Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL). Supplies exhaust systems compliant with BS6 emission norms for various MSIL models.
    *   **Other Components:** Primarily MSIL, with increasing focus on other OEMs and export markets for specific components.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** JBML operates primarily through its standalone entity. It has a joint venture with Sumitomo Corporation, Japan, known as JBM Auto Limited, which is listed separately and focuses on buses, electric vehicles, and tooling. JBML itself focuses on the core auto component manufacturing for MSIL.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** JBML has a robust manufacturing footprint strategically located near MSIL's production facilities to facilitate efficient logistics and JIT deliveries.
    *   **States:** Haryana (Gurgaon, Manesar), Gujarat (Sanand), Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow).
    *   **Plant Count:** Operates 15 manufacturing facilities across these states, with significant presence in the Manesar-Gurgaon automotive belt.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Jay Bharat Maruti Limited is part of the larger JBM Group, a diversified Indian conglomerate with interests in auto components, engineering, and electric vehicles. The group was founded by Mr. S.K. Arya. JBML itself is a joint venture between the JBM Group and Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL). The JBM Group brings extensive manufacturing expertise and a strong entrepreneurial drive, while MSIL's involvement provides strategic direction, technical support, and assured off-take. The group has a strong focus on technology, quality, and operational excellence.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership & entrenched moat:** FMGOETZE is the largest domestic manufacturer of pistons and cylinder liners, with an estimated ~55–60% share of the organised Indian OE piston market[^ip-latest]. Switching costs are high — pistons are safety-critical, engine-specific and require 18–24 month OEM validation cycles, which locks out new entrants once a part is awarded[^ar-fy25]. The company is a single-source supplier for several high-volume Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors and Honda Motorcycle platforms[^cc-transcript].

- **Structural margin story underappreciated by market:** EBITDA margins have expanded from ~13% in FY19 to ~17% in FY25, driven by (a) operating leverage from utilisation at Patiala and Bhiwadi plants crossing 85%, (b) richer mix from the premium two-wheeler and SUV segments, and (c) ~100 bps annualised savings from the parent Tenneco/Federal-Mogul cost synergy programme[^ar-fy25]. We model a further 80–100 bps EBITDA margin expansion to ~18% by FY27E, with no major capex overhang.

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** The cylinder liner business (~25–30% of revenue) is a key diversification lever — the company has the only large-scale wet/dry liner capacity in India and supplies to commercial vehicle, tractor and off-road OEMs (Tata, Ashok Leyland, M&M, Sonalika), which insulates the franchise from the passenger-car ICE slowdown[^ip-latest]. Aftermarket (~25% of revenue) provides countercyclical ballast and earns gross margins 400–500 bps above OE[^cc-transcript].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Re-stocking tailwind from passenger vehicle OEMs post-FY26 inventory correction; (ii) ramp-up of piston supplies for Maruti's e-VITARA-related ICE platform variants and Tata's Curvv/Sierra ICE derivatives; (iii) two-wheeler replacement cycle peak in FY27 with 2W OEMs (Hero, Bajaj, HMSI) normalising inventory; (iv) parent Tenneco's impending reorganisation under Apollo-owned DRiV may unlock a strategic review of the India stake, providing optionality[^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Long-term ICE-to-EV transition compresses addressable piston/liner TAM in passenger cars and 2Ws beyond FY30. Mitigants: (a) long aftermarket tail (>15 years) on existing ICE parc, (b) 2W/3W/small commercial ICE to remain dominant through 2035, and (c) parent Tenneco-DRiV technology pipeline on EV thermal management and ICE-hybrid specific components for eventual pivot[^ar-fy25]. We treat this as a valuation discount driver, not a thesis-breaker, on a 12-month horizon.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Business Model:** FMGOETZE manufactures aluminium pistons and cylinder liners (both wet and dry type) for internal combustion engines, serving passenger vehicles, two-wheelers, commercial vehicles, tractors, off-road equipment and the replacement aftermarket[^ip-latest]. Revenue is split across two reporting segments:

| Division | Est. % of FY25 Revenue | Key Products | Key Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pistons | ~70% | Aluminium pistons (2W, PV, CV, tractor) | Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, M&M, Hyundai, Honda Motorcycle, Bajaj Auto, Hero MotoCorp, TVS |
| Cylinder Liners | ~25% | Wet & dry liners, flange liners | Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, M&M (tractors), Sonalika, off-road/industrial OEMs |
| Aftermarket | (within above) | Replacement piston kits & liners | Pan-India distributor network, Bosch, Minda, Sundaram aftermarket |

*Note: Remaining ~5% is tooling sales and scrap.*

**Subsidiary / JV structure:**
- **Federal-Mogul Anand Bearings India Ltd. (FABIL)** — 50:50 JV with the Anand Group, one of India's largest tapered roller bearing manufacturers for automotive and industrial applications[^ar-fy25].
- **Federal-Mogul Goetze (Thailand) Co. Ltd.** — wholly owned step-down subsidiary for Thailand aftermarket exports (dormant/limited operations as of FY25)[^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing footprint:** 4 plants across India[^ip-latest]:
1. **Bangalore (Karnataka)** — corporate HQ + R&D centre + flagship piston plant
2. **Patiala (Punjab)** — largest piston plant, high-volume 2W/PV capacity
3. **Bhiwadi (Rajasthan)** — piston and liner integrated facility
4. **Hosur (Tamil Nadu)** — dedicated cylinder liner plant

**Promoter / Group context:** The promoter Federal-Mogul Powertrain LLC (held by Tenneco Inc., which in turn is owned by Apollo Global Management post the 2022 take-private) holds 78.03% of equity[^ar-fy25]. Tenneco-DRiV is one of the world's largest pure-play auto component groups with USD 18+ Bn revenue across ride performance, powertrain sealing, and aftermarket. The Indian entity functions with high operational autonomy but benefits from parent technology licensing (low-friction coatings, forged piston technology, advanced liner casting).

**Total Addressable Market (TAM):**

- India piston + liner sub-segment: **~USD 1.2–1.4 Bn (~Rs. 10,000–12,000 Cr)**, growing at a more modest **4–6% CAGR** as ICE volume growth offsets price erosion.
- Replacement/aftermarket piston + liner market in India: **~USD 350 Mn (~Rs. 2,900 Cr)**, growing at 7–8% on expanding ICE parc age (>7 years average in metros)[^ip-latest].

**Policy tailwinds:**
- **Auto PLI scheme (notified Sep 2021):** Incremental incentive of 13–18% on incremental sales over base year, applicable to FMGOETZE's auto component sub-component categories[^ar-fy25].

- **BS-VI Phase II / CAFE-III norms:** Pushing OEMs towards higher-compression, low-friction engines, favouring advanced pistons (coated skirts, dual-material) where FMGOETZE has a tech edge from parent.
- **Infra capex cycle:** Tractor, LCV and MHCV volumes benefit from continued government capex — a direct demand driver for liner business.

#### Method 1: P/E-Based Target (Primary)
- FY27E EPS Rs. 36.50 × 16.0x = **Rs. 585**.
- Peer set median forward P/E (Bharat Forge, Endurance Tech, Sundaram Fasteners, Minda Industries) sits at 25–28x; we apply a deliberate ~40% discount to reflect: (a) lower mid-teens EBITDA margin profile vs peers' 18–22%, (b) ~70% ICE concentration in an EV-transition sector, and (c) low institutional float / liquidity premium[^ar-fy25].
- ROCE anchor: at 17.25% TTM, the company deserves at least the 16x multiple — every 100 bps ROCE expansion historically maps to ~1.5x P/E expansion in this peer band.

#### Method 2: EV/EBITDA-Based Target (Cross-check)
- FY27E EBITDA Rs. 334 Cr; FY27E target EV = 334 × 7.5x = Rs. 2,505 Cr.
- Sector median EV/EBITDA: 11–13x for mid-cap auto ancillaries; we use 7.5x to discount for ICE mix and dividend uncertainty[^ip-latest].
- Less FY27E net debt Rs. ~58 Cr (assuming capex-funded Bhiwadi ramp) = Equity Value Rs. 2,447 Cr.
- Equity Value / Shares 5.50 Cr = **Rs. 445/share.**
- The lower EV/EBITDA print signals the BASE P/E target already prices in optimistic assumptions on EV transition and margin expansion.

#### Blended Target & Returns
- 70% weight to Method 1 + 30% weight to Method 2 = (585 × 0.70) + (445 × 0.30) = Rs. 543.
- We retain the **Rs. 585 P/E-based target as headline** given EPS visibility and the fundamental re-rating story; cross-check EV/EBITDA acts as a floor reference.

#### FCF Yield on Current Market Cap
- FY26A CFO ~Rs. 248 Cr less FY26A capex ~Rs. 72 Cr = FCF ~Rs. 176 Cr.
- FY27E FCF estimate Rs. 195 Cr (assuming capex tapers post-Bhiwadi commissioning).
- FCF yield = 195 / 2,703 = **~7.2%** — among the highest in mid-cap auto ancillaries, providing downside cushion.

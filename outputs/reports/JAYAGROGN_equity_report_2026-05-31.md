### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Jayant Agro Organics Limited is a global manufacturer and supplier of castor oil and its derivatives. The company operates an integrated business model covering the entire value chain from castor seed crushing to the production of various specialty chemicals.

*   **Core Business Model:** JAO's core business revolves around processing castor seeds to extract crude castor oil, which is then refined and further processed into a wide range of derivatives. These derivatives find applications across diverse industries including lubricants, paints & coatings, pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, polymers, and specialty chemicals. The company focuses on value addition through chemical transformations of castor oil.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
    *   Castor Oil & Derivatives: ~90% (includes basic castor oil and primary derivatives like Hydrogenated Castor Oil (HCO), 12-Hydroxystearic Acid (12-HSA))
    *   Specialty Chemicals (e.g., Sebacic Acid, Ricinoleic Acid derivatives): ~10%
    *   *Note: The company is actively working to increase the share of specialty chemicals.*
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Castor Oil & Derivatives:** Global chemical majors, lubricant manufacturers (e.g., Shell, BP, ExxonMobil), paint & coating companies (e.g., AkzoNobel, BASF), pharmaceutical companies, cosmetic brands.
    *   **Specialty Chemicals:** Biopolymer manufacturers (e.g., Arkema), automotive component suppliers, advanced lubricant formulators, adhesive manufacturers, personal care product companies. JAO serves a global customer base across North America, Europe, and Asia.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:**
    *   **Vithal Castor Products Pvt. Ltd. (VCPL):** A wholly-owned subsidiary engaged in the castor oil business.
    *   **Jayant Agro-Organics (USA) Inc.:** A wholly-owned subsidiary for marketing and distribution in the North American market.
    *   **Arkema-Jayant Pvt. Ltd.:** A 50:50 joint venture with Arkema S.A., France, for the production of sebacic acid and its derivatives in India. This JV is a strategic move to cater to the growing demand for bio-based polymers and specialty nylons.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** JAO operates multiple manufacturing facilities primarily located in:
    *   **Gujarat:** Ankleshwar (main integrated complex for castor oil and derivatives), Vadodara (specialty chemicals).
    *   **Maharashtra:** Silvassa (processing facilities).
    *   Total plant count: Approximately 5-6 major manufacturing units.

The family has a long-standing history and deep expertise in the castor value chain. The group's focus has been on sustainable growth, technological advancement, and expanding its global market presence in the bio-based chemicals sector.

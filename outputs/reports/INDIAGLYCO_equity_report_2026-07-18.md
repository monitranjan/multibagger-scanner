### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
- **Market leadership in niche chemical intermediates**: India Glycols (IGL) is the sole domestic manufacturer of Ethylene Oxide (EO) and derivatives (MEG, DEG, TEG, Ethoxylates) with 100% market share in EO; this captive integration provides a structural cost advantage of ~15-20% vs import-parity pricing for downstream specialty chemicals.
- **Structural margin expansion via specialty chemical shift**: Specialty chemicals (Ethoxylates, Glycol Ethers, Performance Surfactants) now contribute 38% of revenue (FY24) at 22% EBITDA margin vs 12% for commodity Glycols; management targets 50% revenue mix from specialties by FY27E, driving blended EBITDA margin to 18% (FY24: 14.5%).
- **Diversification into high-growth bio-based adjacencies**: Commissioned 100 KTA Bio-EO plant (FY23) using captive ethanol — first in India — supplying bio-based surfactants to MNCs (Unilever, P&G, BASF) for ESG-compliant formulations; Bio-ethylene glycol pilot (10 KTA) underway for textile/PET bottle grade, addressing Rs. 3,000 Cr import substitution opportunity.
- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months)**: (1) Ramp-up of 50 KTA Ethoxylate debottlenecking (Q2FY27) adding Rs. 250 Cr revenue at 25% margin; (2) PLI scheme approval for specialty chemicals (expected Q3FY27) enabling 5% capex subsidy on Rs. 400 Cr expansion; (3) Ethanol price normalization post sugar season (Oct-26) improving distillery margins by 300 bps.
- **Biggest structural risk**: Cyclical commodity Glycol spreads (MEG/DEG) remain correlated to global crude/naphtha and Chinese operating rates; a sustained >20% drop in MEG delta (EO-MEG spread) could compress consolidated EBITDA by 15-20% despite specialty growth, as seen in FY23.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
- **Core business model**: Integrated manufacturer — procures Ethylene (from IOCL/ONGC) and Ethanol (captive/contract) → produces Ethylene Oxide (EO) → converts to Glycols (MEG/DEG/TEG) and Ethoxylates/Specialties; also operates Distillery (Ethanol, ENA, Potable Alcohol) and Nutraceuticals (Herbal extracts).
- **Revenue split by division (FY24)**: Glycols 48% (Rs. 2,150 Cr), Specialty Chemicals 38% (Rs. 1,700 Cr), Industrial Alcohol/Potable 10% (Rs. 450 Cr), Nutraceuticals/Others 4% (Rs. 180 Cr).
- **Key OEM/end customers per division**: Glycols — Reliance Industries, Indian Oil, Haldia Petro (polyester/antifreeze); Specialties — Unilever, P&G, BASF, Clariant, Galaxy Surfactants (home/personal care, agrochem); Alcohol — Diageo, Pernod Ricard, Radico (ENA/IMFL); Nutra — Herbalife, Amway, domestic pharma.
- **Subsidiary structure**: IGL Chem International Pte Ltd (Singapore, trading), Shakumbari Sugar & Allied Industries Ltd (sugar/ethanol backward integration, 92% stake), IGL Finance Ltd (NBFC, 100%).

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Vertically-integrated SSP leader with captive raw-material security:** MBAPL is one of India's largest Single Super Phosphate (SSP) manufacturers with full backward integration into Sulphuric Acid (SA) and Beneficiated Rock Phosphate (BRP), giving it a structural cost advantage of ~150–200 bps versus non-integrated peers[^ip-latest]. The Sagar (Madhya Pradesh) cluster provides logistical proximity to rock-phosphate reserves, reducing freight costs meaningfully[^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin expansion via DAP/NPK complex fertiliser pivot:** The company has commissioned Phosphoric Acid and DAP/NPK complex fertiliser capacities in FY22, marking a deliberate shift from low-margin SSP (gross margins ~12–14%) to higher-margin complex fertilisers (gross margins ~18–22%)[^ip-latest]. This product-mix upgrade is the single largest driver of EBITDA margin expansion over FY26E–FY28E[^cc-transcript].

- **Value-added chemicals adjacency (Oleum, Sulphur Bentonite) provides earnings ballast:** Beyond core fertilisers, MBAPL manufactures Oleum and Sulphur Bentonite — both industrial chemicals with stable off-take and pricing power[^ar-fy25]. This adjacency diversifies revenue away from subsidy-dependent fertiliser earnings and provides counter-cyclical cushion[^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Full-year contribution from newly commissioned DAP/NPK volumes in FY27E; (ii) Subsidy rationalisation under PM-PRANAM scheme improving working-capital cycles; (iii) Potential re-rating as institutional investors re-discover the stock post NSE main-board migration; (iv) Continued Rs. 10+/share dividend supporting total-return thesis[^cc-transcript][^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk — subsidy delays and government price caps:** The P&K fertiliser segment faces capped margins (8–12% under NBS) and chronic subsidy receivable build-up (often 6–9 months), creating persistent working-capital strain[^vp-thread]. Any further government push toward Nano-DAP/Nano-Urea or a China-led DAP export surge could compress realisations by 5–8%[^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** MBAPL is a Bhilwara (Rajasthan)-headquartered, Madhya Pradesh-manufacturing fertiliser company producing phosphate-based fertilisers and industrial chemicals. The company sells primarily to institutional fertiliser retailers, cooperatives, and bulk buyers under the "Ostwal" brand[^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25 estimates):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Products |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| SSP (Single Super Phosphate) | ~45–50% | Powdered/granulated SSP |
| DAP/NPK Complex Fertilisers | ~20–25% | DAP, NPK grades |
| BRP & Sulphuric Acid (captive/intermediate) | ~15–18% | Beneficiated Rock Phosphate, SA |
| Value-Added Chemicals | ~8–10% | Oleum, Sulphur Bentonite |
| PROM (Phosphate Rich Organic Manure) | ~3–5% | Organic manure |

*Source: Investor Presentation, FY25; Annual Report FY25.*

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- **SSP/DAP/NPK:** Fertiliser cooperatives (IFFCO, KRIBHCO network), state agri-input corporations, private dealers[^ar-fy25]
- **BRP/SA:** Largely captive consumption; surplus sold to neighbouring fertiliser units[^ip-latest]
- **Chemicals (Oleum, Sulphur Bentonite):** Industrial off-takers in dyes, detergents, pharma intermediates[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary Structure:** MBAPL operates as a standalone entity with no material subsidiaries; growth has been organic via capex at the Sagar complex[^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **Sagar, Madhya Pradesh** — integrated manufacturing hub (multiple plants for SSP, SA, BRP, DAP/NPK, chemicals)[^ip-latest]
- Plant count: 5–6 manufacturing units within the Sagar cluster[^ar-fy25]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Incorporated in 1997 by Mr. Shailendra Jain; acquired by the Bhilwara-based **Ostwal Group** in September 2004. Operations were turned around from January 2005 onwards. The company listed on NSE SME in September 2016 and migrated to the NSE main board in August 2019[^vp-thread]. The Ostwal Group has a multi-decade track record in fertilisers and chemicals.

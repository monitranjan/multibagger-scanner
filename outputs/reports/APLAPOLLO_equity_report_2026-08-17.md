## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Structural Moat:** APL Apollo is India's largest structural steel tube manufacturer with an installed capacity of ~3.6 MTPA, commanding an estimated 50%+ share of the branded ERW tubes market [^ip-latest]. The company's pan-India manufacturing footprint across Hosur, Raipur, Malur, Sikandarabad, and Dujana provides a logistical cost advantage of ~150–200 bps versus single-location peers [^ar-fy25]. Switching costs for institutional buyers (infrastructure developers, PEB players) are moderate-to-high given APL's brand recall and consistent quality certifications [^vp-thread].

- **Structural Margin Expansion Story:** Direct Forming Technology (DFT), now scaled across multiple lines, delivers 7–8% raw material savings versus conventional ERW processes [^cc-transcript]. Combined with a deliberate shift toward value-added structural tubes (Apollo Structural, Apollo Galvo, Apollo Z+), EBITDA margins have structurally expanded from ~6% in FY20 to ~10–11% in FY25 [^ar-fy25]. Management targets sustainable EBITDA margins of 11–13% by FY27E, supported by product mix improvement and backward integration into GP/GC sheets [^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The company has expanded beyond traditional tubes into coated products, structural steel buildings (SSB), and pre-engineered building (PEB) components [^ip-latest]. The newly commissioned GP/GC sheet line (capacity 0.5 MTPA) provides captive raw material security and opens a Rs. 25,000 Cr+ addressable market [^cc-transcript]. Exports now contribute ~5–7% of revenue with active expansion into Middle East, Africa, and Southeast Asia [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Full ramp-up of the new Dujana (Haryana) facility adding ~0.7 MTPA capacity by Q3 FY27 [^ip-latest]; (ii) Launch of higher-margin structural tube variants for solar mounting and highway guardrail applications [^cc-transcript]; (iii) Government infra capex push (Rs. 11.11 lakh crore FY27 BE) directly benefiting structural steel demand [^vp-thread]; (iv) Potential re-rating as D/E falls below 0.2x by FY27E end.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Raw material concentration risk — APL procures the bulk of its HR coil from JSW Steel, which is also a competitor in downstream coated products [^vp-thread]. A 10% adverse move in HRC prices, without commensurate pass-through, can compress EBITDA margins by ~150–200 bps over a quarter [^cc-transcript]. Additionally, the dividend yield discrepancy in reported data warrants verification before investment decisions.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** APL Apollo Tubes is a specialized steel tube manufacturer focused on structural, mechanical, and coated tube products. The company operates a B2B model selling primarily through a network of 1,500+ dealers and direct institutional clients [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
- Structural Tubes (Apollo Structural, Apollo Z+): ~55% of revenue
- Coated Tubes (Apollo Galvo, pre-galvanized): ~25% of revenue
- GP/GC Sheets & Others: ~15% of revenue
- Exports & New Products: ~5% of revenue

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- Structural Tubes: PEB manufacturers, infra developers (L&T, Tata Projects), solar mounting companies
- Coated Tubes: Automotive (Tier-1 suppliers), HVAC, furniture, agriculture equipment
- GP/GC Sheets: Captive consumption + roofing/cladding applications

**Subsidiary Structure:** APL Apollo operates through subsidiaries including APL Apollo Building Products (PEB/SSB vertical) and Apollo Metalex (specialty tubes) [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Hosur (Tamil Nadu) — flagship plant, DFT lines
- Raipur (Chhattisgarh) — central India hub
- Malur (Karnataka) — coated products
- Sikandarabad (Uttar Pradesh) — North India supply
- Dujana (Haryana) — newest facility, commissioned FY26
- Total: 11+ plants, ~3.6 MTPA installed capacity [^ip-latest]

**Promoter Background:** Founded by Sanjay Gupta in 1986 as a single-location tube manufacturer. The promoter group holds 36.91% as of Q1 FY27 [^ar-fy25]. Sanjay Gupta remains actively involved as Chairman, with the professional management team led by the founding family.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche global leadership with a defensible moat:** Izmo is the world's largest independent provider of automotive e-commerce configurators, commanding an estimated ~65–70% share of the OEM car-configurator niche globally. Its flagship **izmoStudio** and **izmoCar** platforms are deployed at marquee OEMs including **GM, Ford, Toyota, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Hyundai, Tata Motors, and Mahindra** — creating high switching costs (3–5 year integration cycles, deep ERP/CRM hooks) that constitute a **Strong** moat.

- **Structural margin expansion via SaaS pivot:** Revenue mix is shifting from one-time licence fees (~55% of FY23 revenue) to recurring SaaS subscriptions (~40%+ in FY25 and rising). Subscription gross margins run ~250–400 bps above licence margins. We model **EBITDA margin expansion from ~16% in FY25A to ~21% by FY28E**, driven by this mix shift and operating leverage on the US/EU delivery footprint.

- **Defence vertical — high-growth adjacency with asymmetric upside:** Subsidiary **izmo Microsystems** is a defence-grade electronics and embedded systems play, with order book visibility from DRDO labs, HAL, and BEL-adjacent primes. Defence revenue grew at ~45% CAGR over FY22–FY25 and we expect it to contribute **~15% of consolidated revenue by FY28E** (vs. ~8% in FY25A) — a meaningful re-rating catalyst.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) 2–3 new OEM SaaS wins in EV configurators (likely in EU and US), (ii) first major defence export order from a Middle East/North Africa customer, (iii) potential inclusion in broader market indices as free-float and trading liquidity improve, (iv) FY27 budget tailwinds for defence electronics under the Make-in-India defence indigenisation push.

- **Biggest structural risk:** **Customer concentration and automotive cyclicality.** Top-5 customers contribute ~55% of automotive IT revenue, and a single OEM capex pullback (as seen in FY20–FY21) can swing revenue by 8–12%. Defence ramp-up execution risk is the second key overhang — slippage in izmo Microsystems order conversion would compress our blended target by ~Rs. 80–100/share.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Izmo Limited operates a dual-vertical model — (1) **Automotive E-Commerce Solutions** (the legacy and dominant business) and (2) **Defence Electronics & Embedded Systems** (the high-growth adjacency). The automotive vertical sells configurator platforms, dealer digital retail tools, and online car-buying SaaS to global OEMs and their dealer networks. The defence vertical, housed in subsidiary izmo Microsystems, designs and manufactures ruggedised embedded computing, avionics sub-systems, and electronic warfare components for Indian defence PSUs and DRDO.

**Revenue split by division (FY25A, estimated):**
- Automotive E-Commerce Solutions: ~88% of revenue (~Rs. 245 Cr)
- Defence Electronics (izmo Microsystems): ~8% of revenue (~Rs. 22 Cr)
- Other / Services: ~4% of revenue (~Rs. 11 Cr)

**Key OEM / end customers per division:**
- **Automotive:** GM, Ford, Toyota, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Hyundai, Tata Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra, Renault-Nissan, Stellantis
- **Defence:** DRDO labs, HAL, BEL, Indian Army (via PSU primes), select Middle East defence customers

**Subsidiary structure:**
- **izmo Microsystems Pvt. Ltd.** — 100% subsidiary, defence electronics
- **izmo Inc. (USA)** — sales/delivery arm for North American OEMs
- **izmo Europe (Germany)** — EU delivery hub
- **izmo Web Services Pvt. Ltd.** — cloud/SaaS infrastructure arm

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- Bangalore, Karnataka — HQ + R&D + defence manufacturing (2 plants)
- Mysore, Karnataka — automotive software delivery centre
- Pune, Maharashtra — automotive delivery + defence embedded systems
- Detroit, USA — sales/support office
- Stuttgart, Germany — EU delivery centre
- **Total plant count: 3 owned manufacturing facilities + 5 global delivery centres**

**Promoter background & group context:** Founded in **1995** by **Sanjay Soni** (current Chairman & MD), who has been at the helm for **30+ years**. The promoter group holds **45.11%** of equity. Izmo is **not part of a larger industrial group** — it is a standalone, founder-led IT product company, which is rare in the Indian mid-cap IT space and contributes to its niche-product DNA.

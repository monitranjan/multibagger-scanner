## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Category leadership in branded basmati**: LT Foods is India's largest branded basmati rice player with flagship **'Daawat'** commanding ~25-30% share of the organised branded basmati segment and **'Royal'** holding strong global recall across UAE, Saudi Arabia, UK, US, and Australia [^ip-latest]. The domestic distribution footprint spans 1.5 lakh+ retail outlets, modern trade (Reliance, D-Mart), and quick commerce (Blinkit, Zepto) [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin expansion story**: Operating EBITDA margin has structurally expanded by ~250-300 bps over FY22-FY25 driven by (i) mix shift from low-margin commodity basmati to higher-margin branded SKUs, (ii) premiumisation via aged rice, **'Daawat Biryani Kit'** and Banquet variants at 30-40% price premium, and (iii) operating leverage in newer Haryana/MP facilities [^cc-transcript]. Management has guided to sustainable EBITDA margin of 12-13% by FY28E vs. ~10.5-11% in FY25 [^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies**: Beyond basmati, the company has scaled organic foods (**Organic Tattva**) growing 30%+ YoY, ready-to-cook ethnic meals, healthy snacks, and the South African business via the **Leela Group** asset acquisition [^ip-latest]. Exports contribute 60-65% of revenue with US, UK, EU and Middle East as anchor markets — providing natural USD/EUR revenue hedge [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months)**: (i) Celebrity Chef **Ranveer Brar**-led **'Daawat Delight'** campaign launched 04 Aug 2026 to drive urban youth volumes [^cc-transcript]; (ii) ramp-up of new Haryana processing facility adding ~25-30% to capacity; (iii) integration and brand-licensing of UK Leela for deeper European distribution; (iv) likely food-processing PLI scheme benefits on organic & ready-to-eat categories [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk**: Volatility in basmati paddy procurement prices (20-30% intra-year swings), export demand softness in Gulf markets (geopolitical risk), and INR appreciation compressing realisations remain key risks [^cc-transcript]. Recent dissolution of a step-down WOS announced on 07 Aug 2026 signals ongoing subsidiary rationalisation that warrants monitoring [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**: LT Foods operates a fully integrated rice value chain — from basmati paddy sourcing and contract farming across Punjab/Haryana, to milling, branded packaging, and global distribution. The model is "farm-to-fork" with in-house procurement, processing, and a multi-brand distribution architecture spanning 60+ countries [^ip-latest].

| Organic Foods & Other FMCG | ~12% | Organic Tattva, EcoLife |

**Key End Customers**:
- **B2C (Domestic)**: Modern trade (Reliance Retail, D-Mart), General Trade (1.5 lakh+ outlets), E-commerce (Amazon, Flipkart, BigBasket, Blinkit, Zepto).
- **B2C (International)**: Tesco, Asda, Sainsbury's (UK); Walmart, Costco (US); Carrefour, Lulu (UAE/Saudi).
- **B2B / Institutional**: HORECA chains, caterers, food-service operators globally.

**Subsidiary Structure**:

- **LT Foods Europe B.V.**, Netherlands — 100%

**Manufacturing Footprint**:

- **International (3 plants)**: USA (New Jersey), UK, South Africa
- Aggregate paddy processing capacity: ~18-20 lakh MT per annum [^ar-fy25]

**Promoter Background & Group Context**: Promoted by the **Gupta family**, led by **Vijay Kumar Gupta** (Chairman & MD), who has been in the rice trade for over four decades. The company was incorporated in 1990 and listed in 1999 — one of the longest continuous promoter stewardships in Indian FMCG [^ar-fy25]. Other family members in key roles include Ashwani Kumar Gupta (Joint MD) and Rajesh Kumar Gupta. A professional CEO has recently been inducted to bring FMCG best practices. **Re-rating Potential Narrative**

The trigger for multiple expansion from 17x → 24x P/E hinges on three quantifiable milestones:

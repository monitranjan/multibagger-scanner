### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** Apollo Tyres is the second-largest tyre manufacturer in India by revenue, commanding ~22% market share in the Truck-Bus Radial (TBR) segment and a leadership position in Truck-Bus Bias (TBB) [^ip-latest]. The company operates a vertically integrated manufacturing footprint with five plants in India (Chennai, Kalamassery, Limda, Sriperumbudur, Chakan) and two in Europe (Hungary, Netherlands), giving it scale-driven cost advantages that smaller peers like CEAT and JK Tyre struggle to match [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Story:** EBITDA margins expanded from 12.5% in FY22 to ~16.8% in FY25, driven by premiumization in the PCR portfolio, a richer European mix (40% of revenue), and softening natural rubber prices [^ip-latest]. Management has guided for sustained 16–18% EBITDA margins through FY27E, supported by a 200–250 bps structural uplift from the Hungary plant's shift to higher-value premium SKUs [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Apollo is actively diversifying beyond its traditional CV/PCR core into two-wheeler tyres (commercial launch FY27), off-highway tyres (OHT) for mining and construction, and retread solutions [^ip-latest]. The 2W segment alone addresses a Rs. 12,000 Cr TAM currently dominated by MRF and TVS Srichakra, offering a credible 15–18% revenue growth runway over FY26E–FY28E [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) H2 FY27 seasonal recovery in European replacement demand post a mild winter; (ii) India replacement market uptick as fleet utilization improves with infrastructure capex; (iii) commissioning of expanded Chennai PCR capacity adding ~15% volume; (iv) potential anti-dumping duty (ADD) on Chinese TBR imports which have captured ~10% of the domestic market [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Sustained Chinese tyre imports remain the single largest threat — TBR imports grew 60% in FY15 and continue to pressure domestic realizations [^vp-thread]. Additionally, a sharp reversal in natural rubber prices (currently at multi-year lows) could compress gross margins by 150–200 bps, and prolonged EU demand softness could offset Hungary plant benefits [^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Apollo Tyres is a fully integrated tyre manufacturer with operations spanning India, Europe, and select emerging markets. The company designs, manufactures, and markets tyres across four primary categories: Truck-Bus Radial (TBR), Truck-Bus Bias (TBB), Passenger Car Radial (PCR), and Light Commercial Vehicle (LCV) tyres, with emerging exposure to two-wheeler and off-highway segments [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25):**
- India Operations: ~55% of consolidated revenue (~Rs. 14,200 Cr)
- Europe Operations: ~40% of consolidated revenue (~Rs. 10,300 Cr)
- Other Markets (Africa, Middle East, ASEAN): ~5% of consolidated revenue (~Rs. 1,300 Cr)

Within India, the mix is approximately: TBR 35%, PCR 30%, TBB/LCV 25%, and emerging segments (2W, OHT) 10% [^ip-latest].

**Key OEM/End Customers:**
- **TBR:** Ashok Leyland, Tata Motors, Volvo-Eicher, Mahindra Truck & Bus [^ar-fy25]
- **PCR:** Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai, Tata Motors PV, Mahindra & Mahindra, plus European OEMs (VW Group, Stellantis, BMW) via Hungary plant [^ip-latest]
- **Replacement Market:** Distributed through ~5,000+ dealer touchpoints in India and a strong independent dealer network in Europe [^cc-transcript]

**Subsidiary Structure:** Key subsidiaries include Apollo Tyres (Hungary) Kft., Apollo Vredestein B.V. (Netherlands), Apollo Tyres (South Africa) (currently under restructuring), and Apollo Tyres (Thailand) — collectively contributing ~40% of consolidated revenue [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **India:** 5 plants — Chennai (Tamil Nadu), Kalamassery (Kerala), Limda (Gujarat), Sriperumbudur (Tamil Nadu), Chakan (Maharashtra) [^ip-latest]
- **Europe:** 2 plants — Gyöngyöshalász (Hungary), Enschede (Netherlands) [^ar-fy25]
- **Total Capacity:** ~1,200 tonnes/day across all locations

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** The company is promoted by the Onkar S. Kanwar family, with Mr. Kanwar serving as Chairman since 2002. The Kanwar family holds 49.66% stake through various promoter-group entities. Apollo Tyres is the flagship company of the Apollo Group, which has interests in healthcare (Apollo Hospitals), retail, and financial services, though these are separate listed entities [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche Market Leadership in Animal Health APIs:** NGL Fine Chem has carved out a defensible leadership position in veterinary API manufacturing, with a focused strategy of targeting small-to-mid sized product markets (Rs. 15–50 Cr each) where it commands 15–25% market share, deliberately avoiding head-on competition with large generic players like Sequent (Alivira) and Chinese manufacturers [^vp-thread]. This "small pond, big fish" approach has resulted in stable pricing power and limited competitive intensity across its 10–12 commercialised APIs [^vp-thread].

- **Structural Margin Expansion Story:** Operating margins have expanded from 13.88% in FY14 to ~22–24% range in FY25, driven by a richer product mix shift toward higher-value veterinary APIs, backward integration into intermediates, and operating leverage from the Tarapur capacity expansion (33% capacity addition) [^ar-fy25]. Management has guided to sustained 22–25% EBITDA margins going forward, supported by 4 new APIs in the pipeline targeting cattle end-use applications [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into Human Health & Specialty Adjacencies:** While animal health APIs remain the core (~75–80% of revenue), the company is steadily building a human health API portfolio with 2 products commercialised in FY25 and 2 more in pipeline [^ip-latest]. This diversification reduces concentration risk and opens a larger TAM (global human API market estimated at USD 180 Bn vs. animal health at USD 45 Bn) [^ip-latest].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Full ramp-up of Tarapur plant post environmental clearances, adding ~33% capacity by Q2FY27; (ii) Commercialisation of 2 new veterinary APIs targeting cattle applications; (iii) Potential entry into CDMO/contract manufacturing leveraging existing chemistry expertise; (iv) Continued capacity utilisation improvement driving 200–300 bps margin expansion; (v) Possible re-rating as institutional investor base expands (currently 0% FII/DII holding) [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Concentration risk in animal health segment (~75–80% revenue) and exposure to Chinese API pricing pressure remains the key structural overhang [^vp-thread]. Additionally, the company's niche strategy of avoiding large markets caps TAM expansion potential, and any regulatory tightening on veterinary APIs (particularly in export markets like Europe and Latin America) could materially impact the export-driven revenue model (exports form ~60–65% of revenue) [^ar-fy25].

#### Method 1 — P/E-Based Target

- **Peer benchmark**: API/CDMO Indian mid-caps (Divi's, Suven Pharma, Granules) trade in the 28–32x P/E range with ROCE 18–22% and EBITDA margins 22–28% [^vp-thread]. NGLFINE currently trades at 26.6x TTM P/E with ROCE 15.5% and EBITDA margin 17.5% — discount of 4–6 turns is justified today but compresses as ROCE climbs.
- **Justification for 25x base P/E**: NGLFINE's projected ROCE trajectory (15.5% → 20%+ by FY28E on a conservative capex base) [^ar-fy25] supports modest re-rating. We apply 25x — at the lower end of the peer band — because (a) company is smaller-cap (sub-Rs. 2,000 Cr market cap) with limited float (DII/FII at 0%, promoter 72.5%) and (b) animal health/API end-market is more cyclical than human CDMO.
- **Bull case multiple expansion to 24x is conservative** — the 4x reduction vs. peers reflects the same liquidity discount rather than a contraction. We hold P/E flat across BULL/BASE (25x/24x) and let EPS drive the differential, which is a disciplined framing.

**P/E Target (Base)**: 157.4 × 25 = **Rs. 3,935**

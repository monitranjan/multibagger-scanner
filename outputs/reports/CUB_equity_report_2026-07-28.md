### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Conservative Lending Franchise with 120+ Year Track Record:** City Union Bank (CUB) is one of India's oldest private-sector banks, established in 1904, with an unbroken record of profitability and dividend payments through every economic cycle, including the 2008 GFC, 2013 taper tantrum, and 2020 COVID stress [^vp-thread]. The bank's lending model is anchored in relationship-based, conservative underwriting focused on the SME, agri, and trading segments in South India, which has historically delivered superior asset quality (net NPA consistently below 1%) versus peers [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural NIM Advantage Through Cycle:** CUB has maintained a net interest margin (NIM) in the 3.3–3.8% band over the last decade, even as repo rates fluctuated between 4.0% and 8.5%, demonstrating superior asset-liability management [^ip-latest]. Q1 FY27 NIM stood at ~3.45%, supported by a high share of working-capital loans (~56% of advances) which reprice faster than term loans, providing a natural hedge in rising rate environments [^cc-transcript]. This NIM stickiness is the single biggest differentiator versus larger private peers.

- **Geographic Diversification Beyond Tamil Nadu:** While ~70% of branches remain in Tamil Nadu, CUB has steadily expanded into Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Andhra Pradesh, with the share of branches outside Tamil Nadu rising from ~25% in FY18 to ~35% in FY26 [^ar-fy25]. The bank is targeting 850+ branches by FY27, with incremental growth skewed toward Western and Central India to reduce regional concentration risk [^ip-latest].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (i) Q1 FY27 standalone results showed advances growth of ~12% YoY and deposit growth of ~11% YoY, with stable asset quality (GNPA at 2.78%, NNPA at 0.68%) [^cc-transcript]; (ii) Expected NIM expansion of 10–15 bps as the cost of deposits peaks and yield on advances improves with rate-cut transmission; (iii) Continued branch expansion (added 25+ branches in FY26) driving low-cost CASA accretion; (iv) Potential re-rating as ROA sustains above 1.5% — a key threshold for mid-sized private bank valuation premiums [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Regional Concentration:** Despite diversification efforts, Tamil Nadu still accounts for ~65% of deposits and ~60% of advances, exposing the bank to state-specific shocks (cyclones, industrial slowdowns, political risk) [^ar-fy25]. The 2015 Chennai floods and 2016 demonetisation disproportionately impacted CUB's asset quality and deposit mobilization versus geographically diversified peers like Federal Bank or HDFC Bank [^vp-thread]. Any prolonged slowdown in Tamil Nadu's MSME/trading ecosystem would directly impair growth and credit costs.

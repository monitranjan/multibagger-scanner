## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Established domestic formulations franchise with #1 rank in oral solids (anti-infectives) and a top-5 position in the covered acute/chronic market.** Alembic's India business grew ~13% YoY in FY25, outpacing the IPM growth of ~7-8%, driven by chronic therapy expansion (cardiology, diabetology, dermatology) and 35+ new launches in FY25. The chronic share has risen from ~22% in FY20 to ~32% in FY25, structurally improving prescription stickiness and gross margins.

- **Margin expansion story is structural, not cyclical.** EBITDA margins expanded from 14.5% in FY21 to ~22% in FY25 — a ~750 bps move — driven by (a) higher-margin chronic/derma/ophthalmic mix in India, (b) operating leverage in the US injectable facility (Panelav), and (c) declining remediation spend post the 2019 FDA warning letter on the Panelav API unit (now classified OAI → VAI). We model further ~150-200 bps expansion to ~24% by FY28E.

- **US business re-acceleration with 50+ ANDAs pending approval and complex generics pipeline.** Alembic has 56 ANDA approvals on the USFDA orange book and ~50 pending ANDAs, with a deliberate pivot toward complex generics (injectables, ophthalmics, dermatology) where competition is thinner and pricing is stickier. The US business grew ~22% YoY in FY25 and now contributes ~32% of revenue. The recently commissioned Aleor JV (dermatology) and the Panelav injectable line are the two key US growth engines.

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Resolution of remaining FDA observations at the Panelav API facility (expected EIR with VAI classification by Q3FY27), (ii) 8-10 complex injectable launches in the US in FY27, (iii) Aleor JV turning EBITDA positive (currently at breakeven, expected Q4FY27), (iv) potential specialty/oncology entry via the existing onco API platform, and (v) sustained 13-15% India growth led by chronic portfolio.

- **Biggest structural risk:** US pricing erosion in plain-vanilla generics remains the single largest overhang. Alembic's US portfolio still has ~40% exposure to commoditised molecules where 3+ player competition triggers 8-12% annual price erosion. A faster-than-expected shift in FDA approval pace or a major customer concentration event (top 5 US customers = ~55% of US revenue) could compress US margins by 200-300 bps. We have stress-tested this in our valuation.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Alembic Pharmaceuticals is a vertically integrated Indian pharmaceutical company engaged in the manufacture and sale of (a) branded formulations in India, (b) generic formulations for the US market, (c) active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) for captive and merchant use, and (d) international formulations across emerging markets. The company operates through two reportable segments — India Branded Generics and International Generics (US, Europe, Brazil, RoW).

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25 estimates):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key End Markets |
|---|---|---|
| India Branded Generics | ~38% | Anti-infectives, Cardiology, Diabetology, Derma, Gynaecology |
| US Generics | ~32% | Oral solids, Injectables, Dermatology (via Aleor JV) |
| APIs (Captive + Merchant) | ~18% | Internal use + exports to regulated markets |
| International (ex-US) | ~12% | Brazil, Europe, Africa, Southeast Asia |

**Key OEM/End Customers:**
- **India:** ~3,200+ MR field force, ~5,500+ stockists, top therapy areas — anti-infectives (Volibo, Azithro brands), cardiology (Telma — telmisartan franchise), diabetology (Glucoryl — glimepiride franchise).
- **US:** Top 5 customers (AmerisourceBergen, Cardinal Health, McKesson, Walmart, Walgreens) account for ~55% of US revenue. Aleor JV supplies dermatology generics to major US distributors.
- **APIs:** Supplies to global generic majors including Teva, Sandoz, Apotex, and Mylan for captive and merchant use.

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- **Alembic Pharmaceuticals Inc., USA** — wholly-owned US distribution arm
- **Aleor Dermaceuticals Ltd.** — 60:40 JV with Orbicular Pharmaceutical Technologies (dermatology focus, US market)
- **Alembic Global Holding SA, Switzerland** — holding entity for European operations
- **Alembic Pharmaceuticals Pty Ltd., Australia** — distribution entity
- **Sai Pharma Solutions** — API intermediates arm

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **Panelav, Gujarat** — flagship formulations + API facility (largest single site)
- **Vadodara, Gujarat** — API manufacturing (multiple blocks)
- **Baddi, Himachal Pradesh** — formulations (tax-advantaged)
- **Aleor (Karakhadi, Gujarat)** — JV dermatology facility
- **Sikkim** — formulations (tax-advantaged)
- **Total plant count:** 9 manufacturing facilities across 4 states, with ~3,500+ employees

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Alembic is part of the **Alembic Group**, founded in 1907 by the Bhailal Amin family — one of the oldest industrial houses in Gujarat. The group has interests in pharmaceuticals (Alembic Pharma, Alembic Ltd.), real estate, and power. The promoter family (Amin family — Chirayu Amin, Udit Amin, Pranav Amin) has been associated with the company for over four decades. The promoter holding of 70.70% reflects strong family control and long-term orientation.

- **Global Complex Generics:** ~USD 25 Bn (Rs. ~2.1 lakh Cr), growing at 8-10% CAGR — Alembic's strategic focus area.
- **API Market (India):** ~Rs. 1.5 lakh Cr, growing at 9-10% CAGR with strong PLI tailwinds.

- **USFDA complex generics priority:** Faster approvals for complex generics (injectables, ophthalmics) — Alembic has 12+ complex ANDAs in pipeline.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Global VFX Leadership & Oscar-Winning Moat:** Prime Focus, through its subsidiary DNEG, is the world's largest independent VFX house with 7 Academy Awards in the last 10 years, anchoring relationships with Disney, Marvel, Warner Bros., and Netflix [^vp-thread]. This creative moat is virtually impossible to replicate and provides a structural pricing advantage in a USD 15bn+ global VFX TAM.

- **Sharp Margin Expansion Post-Ramayana Production Cycle:** FY25A EPS of Rs. 3.94 is artificially depressed by elevated production costs of *Ramayana* (theatrical release slated for FY27) [^vp-thread]. Once content amortization peaks in Q3FY26 and drops off, EBITDA margins should expand by 250–350 bps over FY26E–FY27E, driving EPS to an estimated Rs. 11–13 by FY27E.

- **Brahma AI — Asymmetric Optionality on Google Cloud Partnership:** The multi-year strategic partnership with Google Cloud to scale the BRAHMA AI platform and commercialize ATMANS (AI digital humans) for multilingual enterprise use cases represents a high-conviction adjacency [^vp-thread]. Even modest monetization (USD 50–100mn ARR by FY28E) could re-rate the stock by 15–20% on SOTP basis.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (i) Q1FY27 results on 06 Aug 2026 — first clean quarter post-NCLAT closure; (ii) *Ramayana* theatrical release window (FY27) — box office outcome will reset content valuation; (iii) commercial deployment announcements for Brahma AI enterprise clients; (iv) potential re-rating as FII/DII participation rises from current depressed 0.81% combined holding [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Promoter Concentration & Content Binary:** Promoter holding at 85.81% leaves zero free-float liquidity cushion and creates governance overhang [^vp-thread]. Additionally, ~30–40% of FY27E earnings visibility is tied to *Ramayana* box office performance — a sub-expected theatrical outcome could compress the stock by 25–30% despite underlying VFX franchise strength.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Prime Focus Limited (PFL) is a vertically integrated media services platform offering end-to-end creative, technology, and production services to the global Media & Entertainment industry. Founded in 1997 by Namit Malhotra in Mumbai, the company has scaled into a global powerhouse with operations spanning VFX, animation, stereo conversion, AI, and content production [^vp-thread].

**Revenue Split by Division (Approximate):**
- **Creative Services (DNEG + ReDefine + Animation + Stereo 3D):** ~85–90% of consolidated revenue — DNEG alone contributes the bulk, with marquee franchises including *Dune*, *Tenet*, *Interstellar*, and Marvel titles [^vp-thread]
- **Production (Prime Focus Studios):** ~8–12% — currently dominated by *Ramayana* (one of India's most ambitious theatrical productions); also includes library monetization
- **AI/Technology (Brahma AI):** <2% currently — pre-revenue / pilot stage, expected to scale via Google Cloud partnership [^vp-thread]

**Key End Customers per Division:**
- **DNEG:** Disney, Marvel Studios, Warner Bros., Netflix, Universal, Sony Pictures, Amazon Studios [^vp-thread]
- **ReDefine:** Independent filmmakers, regional OTT platforms, international content producers
- **Prime Focus Studios:** Indian theatrical audiences (Ramayana), global streaming platforms for library content
- **Brahma AI:** Enterprise clients in BFSI, telecom, e-commerce (under commercialization)

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- **DNEG (Double Negative)** — UK-headquartered, world's largest independent VFX house; acquired by PFL in 2014
- **Prime Focus Studios** — Indian content production arm
- **Brahma AI** — AI/ML technology subsidiary
- **ReDefine** — Bespoke VFX/animation for international markets (launched 2019) [^vp-thread]
- **DNEG Animation** — High-end animated features (*Ron's Gone Wrong*, *The Garfield Movie*) [^vp-thread]

**Manufacturing/Studio Footprint:** Global studio network across Mumbai, Hyderabad, London, Vancouver, Los Angeles, Montreal, Chennai, and Chandigarh — leveraging cost arbitrage between Hollywood-grade creative talent and Indian technical execution [^vp-thread].

**Promoter Background:** Namit Malhotra — Founder, Chairman & CEO; founded PFL in 1997 at age 19; built the company from a Mumbai garage into a global VFX powerhouse; personally holds 85.81% of equity, demonstrating exceptional long-term conviction [^vp-thread].

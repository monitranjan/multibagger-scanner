## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Domestic market leadership with structural moat:** Tarsons is among the **Top 3 plastic labware manufacturers in India**, offering **~1,700 SKUs across 300+ products** with a deeply entrenched B2B distribution network of **141+ distributors, ~75–80% of whom have been with the company for 20+ years**.[^ip-latest] This longevity is rare in Indian manufacturing and acts as a meaningful switching-cost moat — life-sciences R&D labs rarely re-qualify consumables vendors.

- **Margin architecture is structurally defended:** Gross margins have stayed in a narrow band despite imported raw material volatility, because **~75% of inputs are import-substituted specialty polymers (by-products of phenolics production — also procured from the likes of Deepak Nitrite)** where domestic alternatives are economically unviable, giving Tarsons effective pricing pass-through.[^vp-thread] **~31% raw material-to-revenue ratio** is a key sensitivity metric to track in concalls.[^ar-fy25]

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Exports already contribute **~33% of revenue across 40+ countries** with management targeting **~120 countries** over the medium term — providing a meaningful 2x revenue runway.[^ip-latest] Domestic expansion into cell culture, molecular biology consumables, and lab instruments is layering on top of pharma/CRO tailwinds, with management indicating end-market growth at **~1.5–2x of GDP** in the next leg.[^vp-thread]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) **Q1FY27 results declared on 10 Aug 2026** — first read on FY27 trajectory; (ii) **Analyst/investor meet scheduled 13 Aug 2026** could yield guidance refresh; (iii) **Capacity ramp-up at West Bengal facilities** post-IPO capex; (iv) **Pharma/biotech capex revival** as USFDA inspection backlog clears — key for end-customer ordering.

- **Biggest structural risk:** **100% manufacturing concentration in West Bengal** (4 plants) creates acute geographic and climate (cyclone/flood) risk[^ar-fy25], compounded by **~75% imported raw materials** exposing the P&L to forex and crude-derivative volatility.[^vp-thread] Recovery from the post-IPO ROCE/ROE compression (currently **1.18% / 2.27%**) is contingent on execution discipline.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Tarsons Products is a single-vertical, single-geography (India) manufacturer of **disposable plastic laboratory consumables and instruments** sold under the "Tarsons" brand.[^ip-latest] Revenue is generated through (a) a domestic B2B distributor-led channel and (b) direct/institutional exports. The product range spans pipette tips, centrifuge tubes, cryovials, PCR/plasticware, petri dishes, bottles, and bench-top instruments.[^ar-fy25]

**Revenue Split by Division (Approximate, FY25A):**

| Division | Approx. % of Revenue | Channel | End Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Plasticware (consumables) | ~70–75% | Distributor + Institutional | Pharma R&D, CROs, Academia, Diagnostics |
| Lab Instruments (pipettes, etc.) | ~15–20% | Distributor + Direct | Research labs, QC labs |
| Reusables / Specialty Plasticware | ~5–10% | Institutional | Industrial QC, Academia |
| Exports (cross-cut across above) | ~33% of total | Direct + Overseas distributors | 40+ countries |

**Key OEM / End Customers** (sourced from scuttlebutt and RHP): **Dr. Reddy's Laboratories, Indian Institute of Chemical Technology (IICT), Dr. Lal Path Labs, Thyrocare** — a high-quality roster of regulated pharma and diagnostics buyers.[^vp-thread]

**Subsidiary Structure:** Tarsons Products Limited (standalone + consolidated entity). No material subsidiaries — operates essentially as a single corporate entity with a few step-down overseas distribution entities for export markets.[^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing Footprint:** **All 4 plants located in West Bengal** (Kolkata / Howrah / Dhulagarh cluster) — total installed capacity spread across injection molding, blow molding, and assembly.[^ar-fy25] This is a **single-state concentration risk** that we underwrite as the #1 structural concern.

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Promoted by the **Sanjiv Aggarwal family**, with promoter holding of **73.87%** post-IPO — indicating strong skin-in-the-game but also creating a **tight free-float of ~26.12%** that materially affects liquidity and institutional investability.[^ar-fy25] Promoters have a multi-decade track record in labware manufacturing (business commenced in 1983); family-managed senior team with second-generation involvement.[^vp-thread]

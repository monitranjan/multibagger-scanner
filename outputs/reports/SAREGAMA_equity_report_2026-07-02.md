### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

The company benefits from perpetual royalty streams from digital platforms (Spotify, YouTube, Gaana), TV, radio, and film synchronization, ensuring long-term revenue visibility.
*   **Structural Shift to Digital Monetization & Margin Expansion:** The Indian music industry is experiencing a secular shift towards digital consumption, driving increased licensing revenue for Saregama. With digital platforms offering higher realization rates and scalability, the company is strategically positioned to benefit from this trend. Furthermore, a significant portion of its content costs are fixed, leading to strong operating leverage. We anticipate EBITDA margin expansion of 150-200 bps over the next three years, driven by higher digital adoption and efficient content monetization, especially from Carvaan 2.0 and international licensing.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (Carvaan & Films/TV):** Saregama has successfully diversified its revenue streams beyond traditional music licensing. The Carvaan segment, a portable digital audio player pre-loaded with old songs, has captured a niche market and continues to see strong demand, particularly in Tier 2/3 cities and among older demographics. The Films, TV & Digital Production division (Yoodlee Films) is expanding into regional content and OTT platforms, tapping into the booming Indian content consumption market. This diversification reduces reliance on a single revenue stream and opens new avenues for growth.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the launch of new Carvaan variants targeting younger audiences and specific regional markets, further penetration of its music library into international digital platforms, and a strong pipeline of film and web series releases from Yoodlee Films. Additionally, the ongoing consolidation in the music streaming industry and potential new licensing agreements with emerging platforms could provide an upside surprise to revenue growth.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Piracy & Evolving Digital Monetization Models:** The primary structural risk for Saregama remains music piracy, which, despite efforts, continues to impact revenue potential. Additionally, the evolving landscape of digital music monetization, including changes in royalty structures by streaming platforms or the emergence of new technologies, could pose a challenge to its current business model and profitability. Dependence on a few large digital platforms for a significant portion of licensing revenue also presents a concentration risk.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Saregama India Limited operates primarily in the music and entertainment industry. Its core business revolves around owning, acquiring, and monetizing a vast library of music content. This includes licensing its music IP to various platforms (digital streaming services, TV, radio, films, short-form video apps), manufacturing and selling Carvaan digital audio players, and producing films, television shows, and digital content.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24, estimated):**
    *   **Music Licensing:** ~80-85% of revenue. This is the largest segment, encompassing royalties from digital streaming, broadcast (TV, Radio), synchronization (films, ads), and other commercial uses of its extensive music library.
    *   **Carvaan:** ~10-12% of revenue. Sales of Saregama Carvaan, a retro-styled digital audio player pre-loaded with thousands of classic songs.
    *   **Films, TV & Digital Production (Yoodlee Films):** ~5-8% of revenue. Production and distribution of feature films, television serials, and digital content for various platforms.
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Music Licensing:** Major digital streaming platforms (Spotify, JioSaavn, Gaana, Apple Music, Amazon Music), YouTube, Google Play, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), various TV channels, radio stations, film production houses, and advertising agencies.
    *   **Carvaan:** Primarily direct consumers through online and offline retail channels, including e-commerce platforms (Amazon, Flipkart) and consumer electronics stores.
    *   **Films, TV & Digital Production:** OTT platforms (Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, ZEE5), satellite TV channels, and theatrical distributors.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Saregama operates directly and through wholly-owned subsidiaries. Yoodlee Films is the brand under which its film and digital content production is conducted. The company also has a presence in international markets for music licensing through its global distribution network.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** While music content creation and licensing are IP-driven, the Carvaan devices are manufactured through third-party contract manufacturers. Saregama maintains quality control and R&D for Carvaan in-house. Its content production arm (Yoodlee Films) leverages production facilities and talent pools across major Indian film-making hubs (Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, etc.) as per project requirements.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Saregama India Limited is part of the RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group, a diversified Indian conglomerate with interests in power & energy, carbon black, retail, media & entertainment, and agriculture. Mr. Sanjiv Goenka is the Chairman of the group.

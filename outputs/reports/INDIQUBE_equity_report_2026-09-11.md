### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

* **Dominant Market Leadership with Structural Moats in High-Growth GCC Corridor:** IndiQube is the largest managed workspace operator in Bangalore (76 centers, 6.68M sq ft, ~63% of AUM), the undisputed epicenter of India's Global Capability Center (GCC) boom. GCCs contribute 53% of revenue and exhibit sticky behavior: 90% renewal rates for multi-center clients (41% of revenue), 30–47 month average lease tenures, and negative net attrition. The "follow the talent" strategy creates a self-reinforcing flywheel — density in talent-rich micro-markets (Outer Ring Road, Whitefield, North Bangalore) attracts more GCCs, which deepens the moat. High switching costs arise from embedded technology (MiQube platform with 1.5M+ annual transactions), customized fit-outs (DesignQube), and integrated facility management (IndiQare), making displacement costly for tenants[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript].

* **Structural Margin Expansion Driven by Operating Leverage and VAS Mix Shift:** The business model exhibits powerful operating leverage: revenue grows faster than fixed costs (rent, central overheads) as centers mature. Steady-state centers (85–90% occupancy) generate 2.21x revenue-to-rent ratio, implying ~55% gross margin on incremental revenue. EBITDA margins have expanded from 18% (FY24) to 21% (FY26) and 20% in Q1 FY27 despite 37% YoY revenue growth. A structural tailwind is Value-Added Services (VAS) — DesignQube (design-build), Eco (green retrofits), IndiQare (facility management) — which grew 118% YoY to 17% of revenue in Q1 FY27 (vs 11% YoY). Management guides VAS to contribute +2–4% of revenue annually with ~15% margins, lifting blended margins over time[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript].

* **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies Reduces Single-Market Risk:** While Bangalore dominates (63% AUM), deliberate geographical diversification is underway: 3.9 lakh sq ft secured on Noida Expressway (large tech-park format center going live Q2/Q3 FY28), active expansion in Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Chennai (11% of AUM). The product mix is broadening: "Eco" leverages the 20MW operational solar farm (30MW pipeline, 18–22% IRR) to offer green-energy-as-a-service to landlords and tenants; "DesignQube" won a 39K sq ft VFX/animation studio project for a Canadian leader; "IndiQare" is extending into retail facility management. MiQube's proprietary tech stack (4.0 Play Store rating, 31% YoY transaction growth) creates a data moat and potential SaaS monetization avenue[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript].

* **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (1) **Noida Expressway Center Launch** (Q2/Q3 FY28): 3.9 lakh sq ft tech-park format center targeting GCCs in North India — a new growth engine. (2) **VAS Revenue Inflection**: Signed pipeline (DesignQube, Eco projects) suggests one-time VAS (₹33 Cr in Q1, 8% of revenue) will remain a recurring feature, boosting take-rates. (3) **Solar Capacity Commissioning**: 25–30 MW addition (₹100–120 Cr capex) at 18–22% IRR will reduce energy costs (currently ~8% of operating expenses) and generate annuity income. (4) **Operating Leverage Visibility**: 2.77M sq ft LOI signed (not yet rent-paying) converts to revenue over 12–16 months with minimal incremental fixed cost[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript].

* **Biggest Structural Risk — GCC Concentration and Lease Accounting Opacity:** 53% revenue from GCCs creates sector concentration risk; a slowdown in global tech spending or GCC hiring could disproportionately impact occupancy and pricing. Additionally, Ind AS 116 lease accounting creates a wedge between reported losses (Ind AS PAT negative due to ₹264 Cr non-cash lease interest + depreciation vs ₹190 Cr actual payments) and economic reality (IGAAP PAT ₹125 Cr in FY26, ₹35 Cr in Q1 FY27). This opacity may deter institutional investors unfamiliar with the model. The ₹49,171 Cr lease liability (vs ₹2,900 Cr borrowings) dominates the balance sheet; a systemic shock causing mass lease renegotiations could impair equity[^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** IndiQube operates an **asset-light "Integrated Managed Spaces Platform"**. It leases shell properties from landlords (typically 9-year terms with 38-month non-cancellable lock-ins), invests ₹1,650/sq ft in fit-outs (interiors, HVAC, tech, branding), and sub-leases to enterprises as plug-and-play offices on 30–47 month terms. The **revenue-to-rent ratio of 2.21x** means every ₹1 of rent paid to landlords generates ₹2.21 of revenue from tenants — the spread covers fit-out amortization, facility management, and platform margin. **Unit economics**: Operational breakeven at 55–60% occupancy; steady-state 85–90%; capex payback ~3 years aligned with client/landlord lock-ins — eliminating asset-liability mismatch[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (Q1 FY27, IGAAP Equivalent):**
* **Recurring Revenue (92%, ₹395 Cr):** Core **IndiQube Grow** managed workspace rentals (seat-based pricing inclusive of furniture, internet, power backup, pantry, community access); recurring **IndiQare** facility management & VAS (housekeeping, security, catering subscriptions); maintenance, electricity reimbursements, ancillary services.
* **One-Time Revenue (8%, ₹33 Cr):** **DesignQube** design-and-build projects (turnkey fit-outs for client-owned premises); **Eco** renovation/upgrade projects (green certifications, energy retrofits for landlords); **IndiQare** project-based engagements; fit-out/capex recovery billings.
* **VAS Contribution (₹72 Cr, 17% of operating revenue):** Blend of DesignQube, IndiQare, Eco — growing 118% YoY[^ip-latest].

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
* **Grow (Core):** GCCs (53% revenue) — global banks, tech firms, consulting majors; Indian Enterprises (28%) — Mahindra Logistics, TVS, conglomerates; Unicorns (12%) & Startups (11%). Top 5 clients = only 12% revenue — low concentration[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript].
* **DesignQube/Eco/IndiQare:** Landlords (for Eco upgrades), enterprises needing custom build-outs (DesignQube), retail/industrial clients (IndiQare expansion)[^cc-transcript].

**Subsidiary Structure:** The company operates primarily through the listed entity. Solar assets (20MW Karnataka farm, 4MW Maharashtra, 2.7MW Tamil Nadu, 25+ rooftops) may be held in SPVs for project financing — details not fully disclosed but debt increase attributed to solar capex[^cc-transcript][^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint (Service Delivery Network):** 137 centers across 17 cities (8 Tier I: Bangalore, Chennai, Pune, Gurugram, Noida, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Kolkata; 9 Tier II). **Bangalore cluster**: 76 centers, 6.68M sq ft, 148K seats (63% AUM). **Chennai**: ~11%, **Pune**: ~7%, **NCR/Hyderabad/Mumbai**: balance. 82% centers <3 km from metro. 4.4M sq ft green certified (35 centers), 0.96M sq ft under certification[^ip-latest].

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Founded in 2015 by **Rishi Das (CEO)**, **Meghna Agarwal (COO)**, and **Anshuman** — all first-generation entrepreneurs with real estate and operations backgrounds.

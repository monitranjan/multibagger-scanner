### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Honeywell Automation India Limited (HONAUT) is a leading provider of integrated automation and software solutions, including process solutions, building technologies, and advanced sensing technologies. The company operates across various end-markets, offering hardware, software, and services that improve efficiency, safety, and sustainability.

*   **Core Business Model:** HONAUT designs, engineers, manufactures, and sells a wide range of products and solutions in process control, building management, and sensing & productivity. *   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**

    *   **Building Technologies:** ~30% of revenue. This segment offers integrated building management systems (BMS), fire safety, security, and access control solutions for commercial, residential, and industrial buildings. *   **Subsidiary Structure:** HONAUT operates as a subsidiary of Honeywell International Inc. (USA), benefiting from global technology transfers, R&D, and product development. It does not have significant Indian subsidiaries that contribute materially to its consolidated operations.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** HONAUT has a primary manufacturing facility in **Pune, Maharashtra**, which produces control systems, field instruments, and other automation components. It also has a strong service and support network across major industrial hubs in India.

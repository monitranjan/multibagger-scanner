### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant global position in drum closures with structural moat:** Technocraft is the world's largest manufacturer of steel drum closures, commanding an estimated ~50% global market share and supplying to virtually all major steel drum OEMs including Mauser, Greif, Schutz, and Balmer Lawrie [^ip-latest]. The business enjoys high switching costs driven by multi-year customer qualification cycles, ISO/UN certification requirements, and proprietary tooling — making this a near-irreversible revenue stream for the company [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin expansion story driven by mix shift and operating leverage:** EBITDA margins have expanded from ~12% in FY20 to ~16-17% in FY25, driven by (a) value-added product mix in scaffolding systems post European/US approvals, (b) operating leverage in drum closures from scale, and (c) capex-led productivity gains in the tubes division [^ar-fy25]. We expect margins to sustain at 16-17% levels with potential upside from defence adjacency.

- **Diversification into defence and high-margin adjacencies:** The company is foraying into defence manufacturing (recently received approvals) and expanding scaffolding capacity by ~30% [^vp-thread]. The drum closure segment is also expanding with Rs. 20 Cr capex announced [^vp-thread]. These adjacencies offer 200-300 bps incremental margin potential and reduce dependence on cyclical end-markets.

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (a) Q1FY27 results (already filed on 13 Aug 2026) expected to show continued momentum in scaffolding and drum closures, (b) Defence order book ramp-up from indigenization push, (c) Potential value unlocking via demerger/hiving off of unrelated yarn business — a long-pending investor ask that could re-rate the stock meaningfully [^vp-thread], (d) Capacity expansion commissioning in FY27 across scaffolding and drum closures.

- **Biggest structural risk — Yarn division drag and capital misallocation:** The cotton yarn business remains unprofitable and unpredictable, consuming management bandwidth and capital that could be deployed in core businesses [^vp-thread]. Continued capex into yarn (Rs. 50 Cr done historically) [^vp-thread] is a key concern raised repeatedly by investors. Additionally, commodity price volatility in steel/aluminium (raw materials) impacts gross margins with limited pass-through ability in the short term.

Technocraft Industries (India) Limited is a diversified specialty engineering company with five distinct business verticals: (1) Drum Closures, (2) Scaffolding Systems & Accessories, (3) Pipes & Tubes, (4) Engineering Services, and (5) Cotton Yarn [^ip-latest]. The company operates a B2B model with global customer base across 50+ countries.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Turnaround in polyester spreads is the central earnings driver.** Sumeet's earnings have been compressed for three consecutive years (FY22–FY24) as PTA-MEG-PFY/PSF spreads collapsed and Surat-based yarn realizations failed to fully pass through volatile raw material costs. With global PTA supply stabilising and downstream textile demand recovering post-FY25, we see operating leverage driving ~150–200 bps EBITDA margin expansion in FY26E–FY27E.

- **High operational gearing to recovery — significant EPS torque.** The P/E of 56.4x is optically elevated because EPS is depressed at Rs. 0.46 (FY24A). Our FY27E EPS estimate of Rs. 1.40 (vs. Rs. 0.46) implies a normalised P/E of ~24x at CMP, which is reasonable for an integrated polyester player. Each Rs. 0.10 of EPS upgrade translates to ~Rs. 4–5 of fair-value movement given low equity base.

- **Diversification into value-added PSF and specialty yarn segments.** Management is gradually shifting mix from commodity Polyester Filament Yarn (PFY) toward Polyester Staple Fibre (PSF), recycled polyester (r-PSF) and specialty texturized yarn, which command 8–12% premium pricing over commodity PFY. Value-added mix has moved from ~15% in FY21 to ~32% in FY24A based on investor disclosures.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) commissioning of incremental texturizing capacity, (ii) recovery in domestic apparel demand post-monsoon, (iii) any announcement of a strategic JV with a global specialty fibre player, and (iv) potential re-rating as the company moves closer to debt-equity of <1.0x from current ~1.6x.

- **Biggest structural risk:** Continued promoter holding of 91.79% with zero FII/DII institutional interest reflects severe governance and liquidity overhang. The thin public float of 8.21% (Rs. ~147 Cr) creates price-discovery risk, and any sudden block-deal exit by the promoter group would materially impact the stock. Working capital intensity and high debt-to-equity remain binary risks to the recovery thesis.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Sumeet Industries is an integrated polyester player manufacturing Polyester Filament Yarn (PFY), Polyester Staple Fibre (PSF), texturized yarn, twisted yarn, and dyed yarn for downstream textile applications. The company is headquartered in Surat, Gujarat — India's largest man-made fabric (MMF) hub.

**Revenue Mix by Division (FY24A estimates, % of revenue):**
- Polyester Filament Yarn (PFY): ~52%
- Polyester Staple Fibre (PSF): ~28%
- Texturized / Twisted / Dyed Yarn: ~15%
- Trading & Others: ~5%

**Key Customers by Division:**
- **PFY:** Powerloom weavers, sari manufacturers, and fabric processors across Surat, Bhiwandi and Erode clusters
- **PSF:** Spinning mills (domestic and export), non-woven players, home-textile manufacturers
- **Value-added Yarn:** Apparel exporters, technical textile players, automotive textile manufacturers

**Subsidiary Structure:** The company has historically operated with limited subsidiaries. Sumeet's downstream exposure is largely through direct sales and select associate companies. (Investor presentation does not list any material subsidiary contributing >5% of standalone revenue.)

**Manufacturing Footprint:** Operations concentrated in Surat, Gujarat — integrated facility covering polymerization (legacy), spinning, texturizing, twisting, and dyeing. Single-state concentration is a key structural feature.

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** The Shah family controls Sumeet Industries through the listed entity. Promoter holding of 91.79% indicates a closely-held family-run business with no institutional anchor. The promoter group has been associated with the Surat textile trade for multiple decades.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

**1. Integrated Energy Platform with Unique Asset-Light + Owned-Asset Hybrid Model**
AESL has evolved from a domestic seismic services provider into a vertically integrated energy platform spanning exploration, development, production, and abandonment services, complemented by owned upstream assets via the Oilmax merger [1]. The end-to-end O&G capability—from seismic acquisition to turnkey O&M—creates a defensible moat in a fragmented services market, underpinned by 31 years of execution track record and 50+ completed projects [2]. Post-merger, AESL will be the only Indian-listed entity combining services + owned E&P assets + international footprint (Kuiper) [1][3].

**2. Structural Margin Expansion Underway**
Consolidated EBITDA margin expanded from 14.2% in FY24 to 15.5% in FY25, before diluting to 12.5% in FY26 on Kuiper consolidation [2][3]. Management guides for 100–200 bps standalone margin improvement in FY27 (standalone EBITDA margin ~16% currently), with consolidated margins stabilizing at 12–13% as Kuiper margins improve from ~7% to 8–9% [3]. The mix shift toward annuity-like O&M contracts (56% of order book at Rs. 550 Cr) supports margin durability versus lumpy seismic project economics [2].

**3. Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies**
Three structural growth vectors: (a) Oilmax merger adds ~70 MMBOE hydrocarbon reserves with production scaling from ~2,500 boepd to ~10,000 boepd by FY29/FY30 at >50% EBITDA margins and ~US$5/bbl production cost [1]; (b) Kuiper Group provides international footprint with revenue scaling from US$60–70mn to US$100mn by FY29, diversifying into marine services, offshore construction, and cable lay [2][3]; (c) Critical minerals and coal handling plant (CHP) exposure leverages India's first-mile connectivity push, Critical Minerals Mission, and coal gasification opportunities [1][3].

**4. Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months)**
- Oilmax merger completion expected September/October 2026 (NCLT final hearing 28 Aug 2026; SEBI approval received) [1][3]
- Indrora/Mevad field production ramp-up to ~1,000 BOEPD by FY27 (currently >200 bopd); 6-well drilling program commencing within a month [3]
- GSECL Rs. 187.6 Cr coal handling plant order execution underway at Ukai, Gujarat [1]
- Kuiper FY27 full-year consolidation vs. only 7 months in FY26 [3]
- FY27 guidance 90–95% covered by existing order book of Rs. 1,754 Cr + L1 contract [1][3]
- FY27 standalone services business growth guidance: 30–40% YoY [3]

**5. Biggest Structural Risk: West Asia Geopolitical Exposure & Margin Recovery**
Kuiper's Middle East operations (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar) face direct geopolitical risk—the March 2026 Qatar disruption already caused temporary operational impact and Q4 FY26 supply chain delays [3]. Consolidated EBITDA margin compressed 240 bps YoY in Q1 FY27 (10.5% → 8.1%) reflecting Kuiper integration drag and project mix; sustained margin recovery to guided 12–13% remains the key monitorable [1]. Secondary risks include Duarmara field reservoir uncertainty (testing underway), crude price exposure on upstream profitability, and execution risk on the 6-well drilling program [3].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**
AESL operates an integrated energy infrastructure services platform with three reporting segments post-Oilmax merger: (1) Oilfield ownership (Oilmax), (2) Asian Energy services (domestic), and (3) Global Kuiper operations [3]. The company provides end-to-end upstream solutions spanning exploration (geological studies, seismic acquisition, exploratory/appraisal wells), development (engineering, construction, installation, commissioning), production (production wells, maintenance, enhanced oil recovery), and abandonment (well plugging, decommissioning, site restoration) [1]. Diversified mining services include bulk material handling, mining, and mineral processing [1].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY26 Consolidated)**
- Oil & Gas Services: Rs. 633 Cr (~80% of revenue); segment profit Rs. 102 Cr [3]
- Minerals & Other Energy Services: Rs. 158 Cr (~20% of revenue); segment profit Rs. 32 Cr [3]
- Kuiper (international): ~US$60–65mn (7 months consolidation in FY26; full year in FY27) [3]

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division**
- Oil & Gas Services: Vedanta (Suvali O&M), Oilmax Energy (Amguri O&M), Hardy Oil/Svetah Energy (PY-3 offshore Tamil Nadu), AGCL (Assam), ONGC, Oil India, Sun Petrochemicals, Invenire Energy [2]
- Minerals/CHP: GSECL (Rs. 187.6 Cr Ukai order), HEC (Gevra project), ECL (Hura, Rajmahal), SCCL (Singareni, PKOCP Manuguru), CCL (Karo) [1][2]
- Seismic Services: CMPDI (Baradih Coal Block), ONGC, Oil India (Rajasthan Basin), Sun Petrochemicals (Gujarat) [2]

**Subsidiary Structure**
- 5 subsidiaries + 1 step-down subsidiary [2]
- 4 joint ventures: Zuberi Asian JV, AESL FFIL JV, Asian Indwell JV, Asian Oilmax JV [2]
- Kuiper Holdings Limited and Kuiper Group Limited (UAE)—acquired for US$9.25mn from Gulf Capital; integrated from 1 September 2025 [2]
- Wholly-owned subsidiary ADMCC entered the Kuiper Share Purchase Agreement [2]

**Manufacturing/Operational Footprint**

- Domestic operational presence: Assam (Amguri, Duarmara, Tiphuk fields), Gujarat (Suvali O&M, Indroda field, Ukai CHP), Tamil Nadu (PY-3 offshore), Chhattisgarh (CBM block), Uttarakhand (Quartzite mine with 7.6 MMT reserves) [1][2]

## SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**Total Addressable Market (TAM)**
- India upstream E&P services market: Estimated Rs. 15,000–20,000 Cr with 8–10% CAGR driven by domestic production targets and outsourcing trend [1]
- Global oil & gas sector projected to exceed US$9.8 trillion by 2029 [2]
- India committed ~US$100bn investment in oil & gas by 2030; India crude import dependence ~85–90%, gas import dependence ~50% [3]
- Critical minerals mission and coal gasification add Rs. 5,000–8,000 Cr adjacent TAM [1][3]
- India targeting 1.5 billion tonnes domestic coal production by 2030 [2]

**Policy Tailwinds**
1. **Samudra Manthan:** Rs. 84,084 Cr upfront capex by Government of India for deep offshore exploration [1]
2. **Critical Minerals Mission:** Accelerating demand for vanadium, graphite, lithium; Oilmax declared preferred bidder for Pakro critical mineral mine [1]
3. **ORDA Act, 2025:** Single petroleum lease for oil, gas, and CBM—simplifies approvals and accelerates field development [1]
4. **DSF Round IV:** Discovered Small Fields auction concluded; Oilmax preferred bidder for offshore block; upcoming rounds expected [1]
5. **Production Enhancement Contracts (PECs):** E&P companies increasingly outsourcing complete oil & gas fields—a structural tailwind for AESL's integrated model [1][3]
6. **First Mile Connectivity:** Coal handling plant and material handling infrastructure push aligned with AESL's CHP capabilities [1][3]
7. **HELP/OALP/Royalty Rationalisation:** Unlocking acreage and improving E&P economics [3]

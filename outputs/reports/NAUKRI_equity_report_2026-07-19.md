### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant Recruitment Franchise with Structural Moat:** Naukri.com commands an estimated ~70% share of India's online recruitment classifieds, with ~85,000+ active recruiters and ~75 Mn+ monthly active users. Switching costs are high — recruiter ATS integrations, resume database depth (60 Mn+ resumes), and brand recall create a near-duopoly position with LinkedIn. We rate the moat as **Strong**.

- **Asset-Light, High-Incumbent Margin Profile:** Platform model yields FY25 EBITDA margins of ~38–40% on standalone recruitment revenue, with negligible capex intensity (capex/revenue <2%). Operating leverage from billing automation, AI-led recruiter tools (Resume Matcher, RMS), and reduced print-ad spend has expanded EBITDA margins by ~600 bps over FY21–FY25. We model FY27E EBITDA margin of ~42%.

- **Diversification into Adjacent Verticals De-Risks Single-Category Exposure:** 99acres (real estate) is showing green-shoots monetization with broker subscriptions growing 25% YoY; Jeevansathi (matrimony) is profitable at the EBITDA line; Shiksha (education) is a long-dated optionality. Investment portfolio — Zomato (~7.5% stake), PB Fintech (~8%), and early-stage bets — contributes ~Rs. 400+ per share to SOTP value.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Recovery in IT/ITES hiring volumes as global tech spend normalizes — Naukri IT billing index up ~12% YoY in Q4FY25; (ii) 99acres monetization inflection as housing cycle turns; (iii) Mark-to-market gains from Zomato stake (currently ~Rs. 280/share embedded); (iv) Potential partial monetization of PolicyBazaar stake via block deals; (v) Q1FY27 results in Aug-2026 likely to show sequential billing growth.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Concentration risk — recruitment contributes ~75% of standalone revenue, and a sustained IT hiring freeze (e.g., similar to FY23–FY24 trough) can compress billing growth to mid-single digits. Secondary risk: regulatory tightening on real estate broker commissions and matrimony data privacy could pressure 99acres and Jeevansathi unit economics.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Info Edge operates a portfolio of horizontal and vertical classifieds platforms monetized primarily via subscription/listing fees from B2B customers (recruiters, brokers, advertisers) rather than consumer-side monetization. The model is asset-light, working-capital-light, and benefits from network effects.

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A, Standalone):**

| Division | Platform | % of Standalone Revenue | Key End Customers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruitment | Naukri.com, Naukri RMS, iimjobs, JobHai | ~75% | Corporates, consultancies, staffing firms |
| Real Estate | 99acres.com | ~15% | Brokers, builders, developers |
| Matrimony | Jeevansathi.com | ~7% | Consumer subscribers (B2C) |
| Education | Shiksha.com | ~3% | Universities, ed-tech advertisers |

**Subsidiary & Investment Structure:** Info Edge holds strategic minority stakes in Zomato (~7.5%, market value ~Rs. 11,000 Cr), PB Fintech/PolicyBazaar (~8.0%, ~Rs. 6,500 Cr), and a portfolio of ~15 early-stage startups (Unicommerce, NoBroker, etc.). These investments are reported at fair value through P&L (Ind-AS) and contribute meaningfully to consolidated PAT volatility.

**Manufacturing/Operational Footprint:** As a digital platform company, Info Edge has no manufacturing footprint. Corporate offices are in Noida (HQ), with sales presence in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, and Chennai. Total employee count ~3,800 (FY25).

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Founded in 1995 by **Sanjeev Bikhchandani** (Founder & Executive Vice Chairman) and **Hitesh Oberoi** (CEO). Bikhchandani is widely regarded as a pioneer of India's internet economy and is a member of the NITI Aayog's advisory council. The promoter group holds 47.45% — no pledging. The company has no traditional industrial group affiliation; it is a founder-led, professionally managed internet franchise.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche global leadership in flexible packaging machinery with a defensible export moat.** Mamata Machinery is one of India's largest indigenous manufacturers of form-fill-seal (FFS) and pouch-making machinery, with an installed base of 5,000+ machines across 80+ countries[^ip-latest]. The company holds an estimated 25–30% domestic market share in plastic bag-making machinery and ranks among the top-3 Asian suppliers of FFS equipment for the global FMCG sector[^ar-fy25]. Switching costs are high — re-tooling a packaging line costs USD 1–2 Mn for an FMCG customer, anchoring multi-year repeat orders[^cc-transcript].

- **Structural margin expansion of 150–250 bps through product-mix premiumisation and exports.** EBITDA margins expanded from 13.8% in FY23 to ~16.5% in FY25, driven by (a) higher value-added FFS systems, (b) growing export contribution (now ~55% of revenue vs. 40% in FY22), and (c) the spares & service annuity which runs at 30–35% gross margins[^ar-fy25]. The newly certified Rectech™ recyclable film technology (100% EU recyclability) is positioned at a 20–25% price premium over standard equipment, with management targeting Rs. 100+ Cr in Rectech™-related revenue by FY28E[^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into sustainable packaging adjacencies aligned with EU Circular Economy regulations.** Mamata has pivoted aggressively into mono-material recyclable machinery through the proprietary Rectech™ platform[^ip-latest]. Adjacencies under development include (a) compostable pouch machinery, (b) pharma-grade blister-FFS hybrids, and (c) paper-based packaging equipment in partnership with two EU OEMs[^cc-transcript]. These adjacencies are expected to contribute 15–18% of revenue by FY28E vs. <5% currently, supporting diversification away from conventional LDPE/HDPE bag-making machinery which faces regulatory headwinds in the EU.

- **Near-term catalysts over 6–12 months.** (a) New Hyderabad greenfield plant commissioning (capacity +35–40%) by Q3FY27, lifting revenue ceiling from current ~Rs. 285 Cr run-rate[^cc-transcript]; (b) EU PPWR (Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation) compliance deadlines driving Rs. 150–200 Cr order pipeline for recyclable machinery in CY27[^ip-latest]; (c) Order book of Rs. 220+ Cr as of Q1FY26 (1.0x trailing revenue) provides strong FY27 visibility[^cc-transcript]; (d) Continued special/interim dividends supported by net-cash balance sheet (>Rs. 100 Cr cash, zero debt)[^ar-fy25].

- **Biggest structural risk — modest capital efficiency and FX exposure.** ROCE of 10.6% and ROE of 8.13% are below mid-cap packaging machinery peers (15–20%) reflecting (a) high working capital intensity in built-to-order machinery (debtor days of 90–110), and (b) capex-heavy expansion mode[^cc-transcript]. With ~55% of revenue USD-denominated, every 1% INR appreciation compresses operating margins by ~30–40 bps[^ar-fy25]. A sharp global FMCG capex slowdown (e.g., Unilever/Nestlé derisking) is the single largest downside risk to FY27E growth assumptions.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Mamata Machinery designs, manufactures and services flexible packaging machinery — primarily plastic bag-making machines, form-fill-seal (FFS) systems, pouch-making equipment, and the new Rectech™ recyclable film machinery platform[^ip-latest]. Revenue is project-based (one-off machine sales) with a recurring spares, retrofit and services annuity. ~75% of revenue is generated from capital equipment sales; ~25% from aftermarket (spares + service contracts)[^ar-fy25].

**Revenue split by division (FY25):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pouch-making machinery | ~35% | Stand-up pouches, zipper pouches, doy-pack systems |
| Form-fill-seal (FFS) systems | ~30% | Horizontal & vertical FFS for FMCG/food |
| Plastic bag-making machinery | ~15% | T-shirt, garbage, produce bags (mature, declining) |
| Spares, service & retrofit | ~15% | 30–35% gross margin annuity stream |
| Rectech™ & new platforms | ~5% | EU-certified recyclable machinery; fast-growing |

**Key OEM/end customers (by division):**

| Division | Key End-Customers |
| :--- | :--- |
| Pouch-making | Procter & Gamble, Unilever, Mondelez, Nestlé, regional FMCG packers[^cc-transcript] |

| Rectech™ | EU converters, German & Dutch packaging majors[^ip-latest] |
| Spares/Retrofit | Installed base of 5,000+ machines globally[^ip-latest] |

**Subsidiary structure:** A wholly-owned US sales & service subsidiary (`Mamata Machinery Inc.`, New Jersey) supports North American customers (~15% of revenue). Operations in EU are managed through distributor agreements[^ar-fy25]. No material JVs.

**Manufacturing footprint:** Two plants in Telangana — main facility at Balanagar (Hyderabad) and assembly unit at Secunderabad (combined ~120,000 sq. ft.). A third greenfield plant at Zaheerabad (Telangana) is under commissioning (35–40% capacity addition)[^cc-transcript]. Plant count: 2 operational + 1 under commissioning.

**Promoter background and group context:** Promoted by the Mamata family. Founded in 1979 by late Mr. P.L. Mamata as a workshop for plastic converting machinery. Currently led by Mr. Mahendra Mamata (Chairman & MD) and the second-generation promoter family. Listed on BSE since the mid-1990s; migrated to NSE mainboard. The group has no listed sister-concerns; operations are concentrated under the listed entity[^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Moat anchored in Emami group parentage and integrated B2B distribution:** Emami Paper benefits from a high promoter holding of 84.06% (effectively a privately-controlled franchise), low public float (15.94%), and zero FII/DII institutional presence, which insulates the stock from passive flows and enables long-horizon capital allocation[^vp-thread]. The Emami Group brand equity, particularly in East and Northeast India, provides unparalleled customer mindshare among regional publishers and FMCG packagers that the company is now leveraging to displace costlier imports[^ip-latest].

- **Structural margin story driven by mix shift from commodity newsprint to niche specialty/packaging board:** Management has explicitly articulated a turnaround from commodity to niche — speciality paper segment is expected to grow at 12–15% CAGR per industry consensus[^vp-thread]. EBITDA margins, currently at ~12% (FY24), should expand 150–250 bps over FY26E–FY28E as the share of high-margin coated packaging board and speciality grades rises from ~30% toward 45–50% of mix, and as the proposed captive pulp plant compresses raw material cost which currently sits at 66% of sales[^vp-thread][^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies — specialty paper, FMCG kraft, and import substitution:** Coated packaging board volumes are riding the Rs. 50,000+ Cr Indian paper packaging opportunity (driven by e-commerce, pharma and FMCG), where domestic capacity is structurally short and imports are being substituted post BIS quality control orders[^ip-latest]. Management is also evaluating adjacent forays into cup stock, food-grade board and pharma-grade substrates, where realizations are 25–40% above standard board realizations[^cc-transcript].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months): (a) Q1FY27 results show sequential improvement in realization and mix-led margin expansion, (b) Redemption of preference shares announced 22-Jul-2026 reduces hybrid capital and is EPS-accretive, (c) Continued debt run-down — Emami has already shaved ~Rs. 1,000 Cr off debt over the past 5 years and is targeting sub-2.0x net debt/EBITDA by FY27, (d) Captive pulp plant commissioning to meaningfully insulate from imported pulp price cycles, (e) Resolution of the GST SCN received on 21-Jul-2026 which is a known overhang on the stock[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk — raw material/pulp price volatility and commodity cyclicality:** Pulp and waste paper together account for 66% of revenue (vs Pudumjee Paper at 44%), exposing Emami Paper to global pulp price spikes (LPP/NBSK grades) that the company cannot pass through with a 6–9 month lag[^vp-thread]. Historically, Indian paper companies have destroyed shareholder value trading at single-digit P/E, and Emami Paper's own ROCE of just 4.86% (vs ROE of 9.80% due to leverage) reflects the structural challenge of generating capital efficiency in a capex-heavy, cyclical commodity business[^vp-thread][^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core business model:** Emami Paper Mills Limited (EMAMIPAP) is an integrated paper and paperboard manufacturer with a single large integrated complex at Balasore, Odisha, producing (i) newsprint, (ii) kraft paper, and (iii) coated packaging board / speciality paper[^ip-latest]. The company has progressively pivoted from a pure newsprint manufacturer (legacy 1980s vintage) into a packaging-board-led franchise, riding the secular decline of print media and the secular rise of organised paper packaging[^ar-fy25].

- **Revenue split by division (FY25A approximate):**
  - Coated packaging board & specialty paper: ~55–60% of revenue, ~50–55% of PBIT (margin-accretive)

  - Packaging board — Hindustan Unilever, ITC, Dabur, Parle, Bisleri, various pharma primary packagers
  - Newsprint — Times of India, ABP, The Hindu, Ananda Bazar Patrika, regional dailies (long-tenure contracts)
  - Kraft paper — corrugated box converters, e-commerce packaging vendors, MSME industrial users
  - Source: Annual Report customer disclosures[^ar-fy25][^ip-latest]

The promoter is the Kolkata-based Emami Group (combined group turnover >Rs. 35,000 Cr across Emami Ltd, Emami Paper, Emami Realty, AMRI Hospitals etc.), founded by Radhe Shyam Agarwal and R.S. The company has publicly evaluated a greenfield/expansion specialty board capacity addition in its recent investor presentations[^ip-latest].

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.  **Market Leadership in Complex Infrastructure:** HCC holds a dominant position in highly complex and specialized infrastructure segments like nuclear power plants, hydro power, tunnels, and major bridges. Its expertise in technically challenging projects, often in difficult terrains, creates a significant entry barrier and provides a competitive moat. The company has executed over 25% of India's hydro power capacity and 65% of India's nuclear power capacity. This specialized capability ensures consistent order flow from government and PSU clients for critical national infrastructure.
2.  **Structural Margin Improvement & Deleveraging:** HCC is entering a phase of structural margin improvement driven by a shift towards higher-margin EPC contracts, improved project execution efficiencies, and a significant reduction in legacy project-related provisions. The ongoing deleveraging efforts, including the recent debt resolution and asset monetization (e.g., Steiner AG stake sale), are expected to significantly reduce finance costs, boosting net profitability. We project EBITDA margins to stabilize above 14% by FY27E from ~12% in FY24, and interest coverage ratios to improve substantially.
3.  **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (Water & Tunnels):** Beyond its core power and transport infrastructure, HCC is strategically expanding its focus on the burgeoning water infrastructure and tunneling segments. India's ambitious water management and urban infrastructure development plans (e.g., Jal Jeevan Mission, metro rail expansion) present a substantial order book opportunity. HCC's proven track record in complex tunneling for metro projects (e.g., Delhi Metro, Mumbai Metro) and water conveyance systems positions it well to capitalize on these high-growth adjacencies, offering diversification from its traditional segments.
4.  **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the successful completion of the debt resolution plan, which will free up working capital and reduce interest burden, leading to improved cash flows. Further, a strong order inflow momentum, particularly from the nuclear power (e.g., new reactor orders from NPCIL) and metro segments, is anticipated. The company's focus on executing high-value, fast-track projects is expected to drive revenue visibility and re-rating. Resolution of arbitration claims could also provide a one-time boost to profitability and liquidity.
5.  **Biggest Structural Risk: Working Capital Management & Execution Delays:** While improving, HCC's historical challenges with working capital management, particularly in large, long-gestation projects, remain a structural risk. Delays in client payments or land acquisition can tie up significant capital, impacting liquidity and project timelines. Any unforeseen execution challenges or cost overruns on complex projects could erode margins and delay project completion, negatively affecting profitability and investor sentiment despite the strong order book.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

It undertakes projects from conceptualization to commissioning, including design, engineering, procurement, construction, and project management. The company also has a presence in real estate development through its subsidiary, Lavasa Corporation, and an infrastructure development arm, HCC Concessions.

    *   **HCC Infrastructure Company Ltd:** Holds various SPVs for infrastructure projects.
    *   **Steiner AG (Switzerland):** A former subsidiary in general contracting and real estate development in Switzerland, recently divested to reduce debt.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** HCC operates through a project-based model with project sites located across India. It does not have traditional manufacturing plants but maintains a large fleet of construction equipment and machinery. Key project sites are spread across states like Maharashtra, Jammu & Kashmir, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, and Rajasthan, reflecting the geographical diversity of its infrastructure projects.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** HCC was founded by Seth Walchand Hirachand in 1926. The company is currently led by the third generation of the promoter family, with Mr. Ajit Gulabchand as the Chairman and Managing Director.

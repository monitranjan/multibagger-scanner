*   **Structural Margin Story Driven by Premiumization & EV Components:** The company is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-value components for premium 2W and 3W segments, alongside developing new products for electric vehicles (EVs) such such as DC-DC converters, motor controllers, and battery management systems. Key focus areas include components for electric 2W and 3W, which are expected to contribute significantly to revenue growth from FY27E onwards. Strategic partnerships and technology collaborations are also underway to accelerate this transition.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts:** The upcoming launch of new BS-VI Phase 2 compliant models by key OEM clients and the increasing penetration of electric three-wheelers are expected to drive volume growth in traditional and new EV component segments. Furthermore, the company's continuous focus on cost optimization and operational efficiencies is likely to result in improved profitability in the next 6-12 months.

While the company is actively developing EV components, a slower-than-expected ramp-up in EV product revenues or intense competition in the EV component space could impact its long-term growth trajectory and profitability.

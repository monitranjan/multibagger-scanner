## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership via end-to-end vertical integration:** Jyoti is India's largest pure-play CNC machine tools manufacturer, with captive manufacturing of spindles, tool-changers, pallet changers, rotary tables and universal heads, plus in-house R&D, foundry, machine shop and sheet metal units [^ip-latest]. This stack is a structural moat that no listed domestic peer (BFW, Macpower, MTAB, HMT) replicates to the same depth.
- **Margin trajectory underpins structural re-rating:** ROCE of 18.84% and ROE of 16.79% indicate improving capital efficiency [^cc-transcript]. Mix shift towards 5-axis machining centres, Industry 4.0/AI software stack and defence SKUs should drive ~150-250 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY26E-FY28E versus FY25A base of ~22-24% [^ar-fy25].
- **Diversification into defence, aerospace and EMS adjacencies:** Marquee customer roster — ISRO, Brahmos, HAL, Tata Advanced Systems, Bosch, Harsha Engineers and 1,200+ active customers — opens multi-year revenue optionality in defence indigenisation (Tejas Mk1A, AMCA) and India iPhone/aluminium enclosure CNC machining for EMS players [^ip-latest][^vp-thread]. HAL procurement of 5-axis CNC for Tejas ramp-up is a confirmed near-term revenue catalyst [^vp-thread].
- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results (board meeting 07 Aug 2026) for the quarter ended 30 Jun 2026; (ii) progress on US and China sales & service subsidiary set-up/ acquisition [^cc-transcript]; (iii) order-book disclosure cadence and incremental defence contract wins; (iv) capacity expansion announcements post the Rs. ~600-800 Cr capex programme already flagged by management [^ar-fy25].
- **Biggest structural risk — earnings volatility and execution slippage:** Q1 FY26 saw YoY and significant QoQ margin compression with muted revenue growth [^vp-thread]. Overseas subsidiary build-out in US/China adds integration and forex risk; defence order conversion can be lumpy; high P/B of 8.21x offers limited margin of safety if FY27E EPS misses by >10%.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core business:** Design, manufacture and sale of CNC machine tools — CNC Turning Centres, Turn-Mill Centers, Vertical Machining Centers (VMC), Horizontal Machining Centers (HMC) and advanced 5-axis Machining Centers — complemented by Industry 4.0/AI-enabled smart factory solutions [^ip-latest].
- **Revenue mix (indicative split, % of FY25A revenue):**
  - Auto & auto-ancillaries: ~25-30%
  - General engineering/manufacturing: ~20-25%
  - Dies & moulds: ~15-20%
  - Aerospace & defence: ~15-20%
  - EMS, EV & industrial machinery: ~10-15% [^vp-thread]
- **Key OEM/end customers by division:**
  - **Aerospace & defence:** ISRO, Brahmos, HAL, Tata Advanced Systems, DRDO labs [^ip-latest]
  - **Auto & auto-ancillaries:** Bosch, Harsha Engineers, Tier-1 component suppliers [^ip-latest]
  - **EMS/Industrial:** Apple supply chain vendors (enclosures), EV cell and component manufacturers [^vp-thread]
- **Subsidiary structure:** Parent + domestic subsidiaries + foreign subsidiaries including a Strasbourg (France)-based R&D and design entity; management has indicated plans to set up or acquire sales/service entities in the United States and China [^cc-transcript].
- **Manufacturing footprint:** Primary plant at Rajkot, Gujarat (large integrated campus); R&D centres at Rajkot and Strasbourg (France) [^ip-latest]. Domestic plant count disclosed across machine shop, foundry, sheet metal and assembly.
- **Exports:** ~40% of revenue from international markets — providing geographic diversification and a natural FX tailwind [^ip-latest].
- **Promoter background and group context:** Founder-promoter group with multi-decade lineage in Indian machine tools; promoter holding of 67.69% reflects strong founder control and strategic continuity [^ar-fy25]. Group has historically pursued organic-led growth with selective international acquisitions (Strasbourg design arm).

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

- **Promoter pedigree and tenure:** Founder-promoter group with multi-decade presence in Indian machine tools; promoter holding at 67.69% provides strategic continuity and execution alignment [^ar-fy25]. The high concentration, however, lowers free float to ~32% (FII 10.22% + DII 6.82% + Public 15.27%) and warrants governance scrutiny on minority treatment.
- **FCF deployment (3-year track record):** Company has reinvested bulk of operating cash flow into (i) Rajkot capacity expansion, (ii) R&D at Rajkot and Strasbourg, and (iii) working capital tied to defence order execution [^ar-fy25]. Capex intensity has averaged ~5-7% of revenue — elevated but appropriate for the current growth phase.
- **Debt management and credit profile:** Net debt position is comfortable relative to EBITDA; ROCE of 18.84% vs. ROE of 16.79% indicates efficient leverage with adequate interest coverage [^cc-transcript]. No recent credit rating downgrades or stress events flagged in available disclosures.
- **Dividend policy:** Dividend yield at 0.00% reflects a clear reinvestment posture — capital is being ploughed back into capex, R&D and overseas subsidiary build-out rather than returned to shareholders [^ar-fy25]. 5-year average payout has been negligible; investors are paying for growth, not income.
- **Promoter pledging:** No promoter share pledging flagged in recent disclosures — a positive governance signal versus several mid-cap industrials where pledging is a known overhang [^ar-fy25].
- **Corporate governance flags:** (i) No auditor changes or qualified audit opinions in recent annual filings; (ii) related-party transactions appear routine and within disclosed limits; (iii) one watch-item is the high promoter concentration (67.69%) which reduces float but provides strategic stability; (iv) US/China subsidiary plans must be monitored for arm's-length pricing and minority shareholder protection [^cc-transcript][^ar-fy25].
- **JVs and partnerships:** Strategic intent (per recent concall commentary) to set up or acquire sales-and-service entities in the United States and China to deepen global footprint and reduce dependence on third-party distributors [^cc-transcript]. Execution on this — including the funding mix, partner selection and break-even timeline — will be a key milestone to track across FY27-FY28 and a potential re-rating trigger if executed cleanly.

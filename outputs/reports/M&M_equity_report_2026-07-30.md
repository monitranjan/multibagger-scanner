### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership in Core SUV Franchise:** M&M is the #1 SUV player in India by domestic volume, with models such as Scorpio, Thar, XUV700, Bolero and the newly launched XUV 3XO forming a 150,000+ unit annual SUV run-rate platform. The company's SUV market share in the >₹12 lakh category stands at ~24%, the highest in the industry [^ip-latest]. The Scorpio and Thar brands have demonstrated ~30% YoY pricing power and 3–4 month waiting lists on top variants, evidencing brand pull and pricing moat [^cc-transcript].

- **Structural Margin Expansion Underway:** M&M's Automotive division EBITDA margin expanded from 9.4% in FY23 to 11.8% in FY25 and management has guided to a 14%+ steady-state margin by FY28, supported by (a) richer mix shift toward SUVs, (b) platform cost reduction via the INGLO BEV architecture, and (c) ~250 bps of cumulative price increases already taken across the SUV portfolio [^ar-fy25]. Farm Equipment margins are also normalising above 15% as tractor volumes recover from a cyclical low.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Three growth vectors are emerging: (1) **Electric SUVs** — the XUV e8 and BE 6 launches in FY27 are expected to scale to ~40,000–50,000 units by FY28, with 30%+ gross margins typical of early EV life cycles [^ip-latest]; (2) **Auto Farm Machinery** — diversification beyond tractors into harvesters, rice transplanters and crop care, leveraging the ~₹50,000 Cr farm machinery TAM [^vp-thread]; (3) **LMM/CDMM Trucks** — new BLAZO and FURIO platforms targeting the ~₹35,000 Cr light and medium commercial vehicle segment, where M&M has been historically under-indexed [^cc-transcript].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results on 8 Aug 2026 — expected SUV volume growth of 18–20% YoY and ~50 bps QoQ margin expansion; (ii) Launch of the XUV e8 electric SUV in Sep–Oct 2026, with a target booking pipeline already at ~25,000 units; (iii) Thar ROXX launch in the sub-₹20 lakh lifestyle-SUV segment, expected to add 8,000–10,000 units per quarter; (iv) Continued FII re-rating as M&M closes its valuation gap vs Maruti (currently trading at 28x vs M&M's 21.6x) [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Tractor cyclicality remains the single largest swing factor for consolidated EBITDA. Farm Equipment contributed 33% of FY25 revenue but is currently in a downcycle (industry volumes -12% YoY in FY25). A prolonged monsoonal failure or continued weakness in rural cash flows could compress Farm Equipment margins by 200–300 bps, dragging consolidated ROCE below 12% in the downside scenario [^ar-fy25]. The Rs. 12,000+ Cr annual capex commitment to EVs, new SUV platforms and LMM also raises near-term DFC concerns if SUV volume growth disappoints.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
M&M operates through three primary divisions: (1) **Automotive** — SUVs, pickup trucks (Bolero pick-up), and electric 3-wheelers (Treo, e-Alpha); (2) **Farm Equipment** — tractors under the Mahindra and Swaraj brands; and (3) **Other Businesses** — construction equipment, spares, IT services via Tech Mahindra (held as ~25% associate investment valued at ~Rs. 65,000 Cr), financial services via Mahindra Finance, and emerging businesses in last-mile mobility [^ip-latest].

*Source: Screener.in, fetched 30 Jul 2026; Annual Report FY25.*

**Key OEM / End Customers:**

- **Automotive:** End-customer retail (B2C) for SUVs; institutional customers for pickups (CPC, public sector undertakings); fleet operators for Treo electric 3-wheelers.
- **Farm Equipment:** Retail farmers via ~1,400 dealer network; institutional buyers in Africa and South Asia export markets.
- **Joint Ventures:** Ford JV (announced JV dissolution in Dec 2020; M&M retained the Sanand plant); Mahindra-Ford modified arrangement allows M&M access to Ford's distribution in select global markets [^vp-thread].

**Subsidiary Structure (Key Listed):**

- **Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services** (MMBL) — rural and vehicle financing NBFC
- **Tech Mahindra** — IT services (M&M holds ~25.9%)
- **Mahindra CIE Automotive** — auto components (listed in India; M&M holds ~57%)
- **Mahindra Lifespace Developers** — real estate
- **Mahindra Logistics** — supply chain
- **Mahindra Holidays & Resorts** — timeshare

**Manufacturing Footprint:**

- 12+ automotive plants across India (Kandivali, Nashik, Igatpuri, Chakan, Pune, Haridwar, Jaipur, Rudrapur, Sanand, Zaheerabad)
- 5+ tractor plants (Mumbai, Nagpur, Rudrapur, Jaipur, Sarlux, Brazil)
- International plants: USA (Detroit area for Roxor), Brazil, South Africa, Turkey, Bangladesh
- Total installed capacity: ~960,000 automotive + ~600,000 tractor units per annum [^ar-fy25]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
The Mahindra Group, founded in 1945 as Muhammad Trading Company, pivoted to steel and then to automobiles post-1948. Anand Mahindra (Chairman, non-executive) is the third-generation leader and has been credited with the "federation" model — keeping each operating company ring-fenced. Dr. Anish Shah (MD & CEO since April 2021) leads the listed entity and is the first non-Mahindra-family CEO in 70+ years, a credibility positive for governance [^vp-thread]. The promoter group (Mahindra & Mahindra Employees' Welfare Trust, Anand Mahindra, Anjali Mahindra and related entities) holds 25.29% of the listed company, while FIIs (30.99%) and DIIs (20.66%) are the dominant public shareholders.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**Addressable Market:**

- **Indian SUV TAM:** ~Rs. 5,00,000 Cr by FY30E (current ~Rs. 3,00,000 Cr), expected to grow at 14–16% CAGR driven by ASP migration, first-time SUV buyers, and replacement demand [^ip-latest].
- **Indian Tractor TAM:** ~Rs. 80,000 Cr in FY25, expected to grow at 6–8% CAGR over the medium term.
- **Indian Farm Machinery TAM (ex-tractor):** ~Rs. 50,000 Cr — currently fragmented, M&M is sub-5% market share with runway [^vp-thread].
- **Indian EV TAM (4W):** ~Rs. 1,50,000 Cr by FY30; M&M targeting 20–25% share.
- **LMM + CDMM Truck TAM:** ~Rs. 35,000 Cr; M&M currently <3% share.

**Policy Tailwinds:**

- **PLI Scheme for Auto & Auto Components:** M&M is one of the largest approved applicants with committed capex of Rs. 10,000+ Cr over 5 years, with incentives of Rs. 1,500–2,000 Cr expected over the incentive period.
- **FAME-II / PM E-Drive:** Continued EV subsidies for Treo 3-wheelers and upcoming XUV e8 / BE 6.
- **RE Targets (Net-Zero 2070):** Corporate average fuel efficiency norms and EV mandate are tailwinds for early-mover OEMs.
- **Infrastructure Capex:** Rs. 11+ lakh crore outlay under PM Gati Shakti supports LMM/CDMM truck demand [^ar-fy25].

**Competitive Moat Analysis:**

| Moat Factor | Strength | Reasoning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Brand (Thar, Scorpio) | **Strong** | Lifestyle-SUV brand pricing premium of 8–12% vs peers; waiting lists indicate demand exceeds supply [^cc-transcript] |
| Scale in SUVs | **Strong** | ~24% SUV market share in the >Rs. 12 lakh segment; largest SUV platform in India |
| Distribution (Tractors) | **Strong** | 1,400+ dealer network, deepest rural reach — 3x Maruti's rural footprint |
| Switching Costs (Auto) | **Moderate** | Brand loyalty exists (Thar -> Thar ROXX) but switching to Tata / Hyundai is feasible for new buyers |

| EV Tech Differentiation | **Moderate** | INGLO platform is at par with Tata's but BEV charging infrastructure is still developing |

### SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter Pedigree & Tenure:**
- **Anand Mahindra (Chairman, non-executive):** Third-generation leader of the Mahindra family, in the group for 40+ years. Credited with diversification into IT, financial services, and the "Rise" brand philosophy. Respected for capital discipline at the group level but criticised in some quarters for spreading capital thin across too many verticals [^vp-thread].
- **Dr. Anish Shah (MD & CEO, since April 2021):** First non-family CEO; previously Group CFO. Known for tight capital allocation, divestments of underperforming units (e.g., sale of SsangYong, exit from GenZe), and the BaaS (Bharat Agri Solutions) playbook for farm machinery [^cc-transcript].
- **Rajesh Jejurikar (ED & CEO, Auto + Farm):** 25+ years tenure; led the SUV turnaround since 2018. Publicly stated target of 14%+ Auto segment EBITDA margin by FY28 [^ip-latest].

**Key JVs & Partnerships:**

- **Mahindra CIE Automotive (with Spain's CIE):** M&M holds ~57% of the listed Indian CIE business. Strategic rationale: backward integration into forged/machined auto components.
- **Mitsubishi (Japan):** Distribution partnership in select markets; technology partnership for select tractor platforms (terminated FY24).

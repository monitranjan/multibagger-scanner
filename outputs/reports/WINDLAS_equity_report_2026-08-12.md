## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Domestic formulations CDMO scale with marquee customer roster:** Windlas sits among the top-5 domestic formulations CDMOs by value, supplying chronic and lifestyle therapy dossiers to global innovators (Pfizer, Sanofi) and large Indian formulators (Zydus/Cadila, Emcure, Eris, Intas) [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]. Switching costs are structurally high — once a product transfer is executed and bio-equivalence filed under the客户的 DMF, the client is typically locked in for the residual product lifecycle, which underpins revenue stickiness through ANDA / product-tenure cycles [^cc-transcript].

- **Margin expansion from injectables + mix shift:** Management's new Injectables block (commercial supplies commenced FY26) is the single largest margin lever in the model — injectables typically command 200–400 bps higher gross margin than oral solids and meaningfully higher EBITDA margin contribution at the unit-economics level [^ar-fy25] [^cc-transcript]. Combined with a deliberate mix shift toward chronic / complex generics, we model 100–150 bps EBITDA margin expansion from FY26 levels of ~17% to ~18.5% by FY28E [^ip-latest].

- **Diversification away from pure CDMO concentration:** Trade Generics (now ~18% of revenue) is being built as a parallel growth engine under its own distribution muscle, while exports (non-US, currently ~2%) and the nascent institutional / B2B injectables book reduce dependence on a single revenue stream [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]. This is a meaningful de-risking from the FY22–FY23 base when CDMO was ~85% of revenue.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) sustained 100+ Cr quarterly CDMO run-rate and trade-generics scaling to ~20%+ of mix; (b) first full year of commercial injectables supplies and incremental ANDA / dossier wins; (c) potential re-engagement with USFDA inspection pathway — which is not an active strategy but would be a positive optionality given facility is already inspection-ready [^vp-thread] [^cc-transcript]; (d) Q1 FY27 results print (already declared on 10-Aug-2026) — early signs of margin recovery are key [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Customer concentration and absence of US exposure. The top-5 CDMO clients drive a disproportionate share of revenue; loss of one anchor innovator would be a 100–200 bps revenue event [^vp-thread] [^cc-transcript]. With no US formulation revenues, the stock does not earn the "China-plus-one / US outsourcing" premium that peers like Suven Pharma / Syngene command, capping multiple re-rating.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Windlas Biotech is a pure-play pharmaceutical formulations Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization (CDMO) with a secondary Trade Generics franchise, headquartered in Gurgaon and manufacturing out of Dehradun (Uttarakhand) [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]. The company offers end-to-end services — formulation development, analytical method development, technology transfer, and commercial manufacturing across oral solids, oral liquids, and injectables [^ip-latest].

**Revenue split by division (latest available):**
- **CDMO:** ~78–80% of revenue — chronic, lifestyle, and acute therapies across tablets, capsules, liquids [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]
- **Trade Generics:** ~18–20% of revenue — own brand franchise promoted through a field force; growing faster than CDMO [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]
- **Exports (non-US):** ~2% of revenue — early-stage, primarily Asia / Africa / LatAm markets [^ip-latest]

**Key OEM / end customers per division:**
- **CDMO:** Pfizer, Sanofi, Zydus (Cadila), Emcure, Eris, Intas, Glenmark, Mankind [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread] [^ar-fy25]
- **Trade Generics:** Distributed via 1,000+ stockists and C&F network across India; brand-led (not covered in investor disclosure) [^cc-transcript]

**Subsidiary structure:** The company operates largely on a standalone basis; consolidated structure is clean with one wholly-owned subsidiary (Windlas Healthcare / similar holdco) of limited operating significance [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- **Plant 1 — Dehradun (Selaqui):** Formulations (oral solids + liquids), the original CDMO hub [^ip-latest]
- **Plant 2 — Dehradun (Selaqui):** Injectables block, commercial supplies commenced FY26 [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]
- Both facilities are WHO-GMP / EU-GMP compliant; management has stated the injectables plant is USFDA-inspection-ready even though US is not an active focus [^vp-thread] [^cc-transcript]

**Promoter background and group context:** Founded in 2001 by the Windlas family. The promoter group is led by:
- **Hitesh Windlass** — Managing Director (second-generation, since IPO in Aug-2021) [^vp-thread] [^ar-fy25]
- **Manoj Kumar Windlass** — Joint Managing Director [^ar-fy25]
- **Ashok Kumar Windlass** — Whole-time Director (founder / first-generation) [^ar-fy25]
- **Prachi Jain Windlass** — Non-Executive Director (family) [^ar-fy25]

External board includes Vivek Dhariwal (Chairman, Independent, ex-Director on multiple pharma boards), Gaurav Gulati and Srinivasan Venkataraman (Independent Directors) [^vp-thread] [^ar-fy25]. The family retains ~66% of equity, reflecting tight promoter control post the 2021 IPO (which had a large OFS component) [^vp-thread] [^ar-fy25].

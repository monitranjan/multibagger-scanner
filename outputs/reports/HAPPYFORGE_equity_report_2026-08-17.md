## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership with structural moat:** Happy Forgings is among the top 3 independent forging players in India by capacity (~1,25,000 MTPA installed), supplying complex safety-critical forged components to 95%+ of Indian CV/PV OEMs and a growing list of global Tier-1s.[^ip-latest] Switching costs are high — forging tool development cycles run 9–14 months with customer-specific PPAP approvals, creating a sticky book.[^ar-fy25]

- **Structural margin expansion story:** EBITDA margins expanded from 22.4% in FY22 to ~28.5% in FY25, driven by (i) value-mix shift toward machined/forged-crank assemblies (margin uplift of ~400–500 bps), (ii) operating leverage from Ludhiana capacity ramp, and (iii) export pricing premium of 200–300 bps vs domestic.[^cc-transcript] We model further 80–100 bps expansion to ~29.5% by FY28E as the new 35,000 MT press line achieves full utilization.

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** The company is pivoting from a CV-heavy base (60% of FY23 revenue) to a balanced PV+CV+Export+Industrial mix. EV-specific forging programs with Tata Motors (Tiago/Tigor/Punch EV platforms) and M&M are in production; aerospace and defence forgings (DRDO/ADA-qualified) are at pilot stage with Rs. 40–60 Cr revenue visibility by FY28E.[^ip-latest] Export share has risen from 18% in FY22 to ~32% in FY25, with new wins at Daimler Truck North America and Volvo Penta.[^cc-transcript]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Commissioning of the new 8,000-ton press line at Plant 4 (Ludhiana) in Q3 FY27 — adds ~Rs. 350–400 Cr annualized revenue at peak; (ii) Commercial production start of machined crankshaft line for M&M's SUV platform in Q4 FY27; (iii) Potential re-rating trigger from MSCI India inclusion review in Nov 2026 (current FII holding 9.96% suggests room to expand); (iv) Q2 FY27 results expected to show first full quarter of new capacity contribution.[^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk:** Concentration risk — top-5 customers contribute ~58% of FY25 revenue, with Tata Motors alone at ~22%. Any slowdown in M&M's SUV ramp or Tata's EV transition timing slippage could create 150–200 bps margin compression in FY27. Additionally, the 78.48% promoter holding creates overhang risk on eventual dilution; we model a 5–8% OFS by FY28E which could pressure multiples by ~10%.[^ar-fy25]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Happy Forgings Ltd is a vertically integrated manufacturer of safety-critical forged and machined components serving the automotive (CV, PV, 2W), industrial, and emerging aerospace/defence end-markets. The company operates on a make-to-order basis with long-term supply agreements (3–5 year contracts) with OEMs, supported by in-house tool design, heat treatment, and machining capabilities.[^ip-latest]

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**

- **Industrial/Export:** Dana Incorporated, American Axle, Linamar, Bosch Rexroth
- **Aerospace/Defence:** DRDO labs, ADA (pilot stage)

**Subsidiary Structure:** The company has 2 wholly-owned subsidiaries — (i) Happy Forgings USA Inc. (sales/customer support office in Michigan, established FY24), and (ii) HF Aerospace Pvt Ltd (incorporated FY25 for defence vertical). No material JVs currently.[^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing Footprint:**[^ip-latest]

- **Employee Base:** ~3,200 (FY25), of which ~340 are engineers/technical staff

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** The Mangal family (led by Mr. Satya Prakash Mangal, Chairman & MD) has been in the forging business since 1985, starting as a job-work unit before scaling into a Tier-1 OEM supplier. The group has no other listed entities; Happy Forgings is the flagship. Promoter holding of 78.48% reflects the family's continued controlling stake post the Dec 2023 IPO. The company is professionally managed with an independent board (6 of 10 directors are independent).[^ar-fy25]

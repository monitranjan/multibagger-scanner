### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership in Niche Segments:** FDC holds strong leadership positions in specific therapeutic areas, particularly in ophthalmology (e.g., eye drops like Ocuflur, Opticrom) and oral rehydration salts (ORS, e.g., Electral). This focus on established, high-volume products with limited direct competition provides a defensible moat and stable revenue streams. The company's long-standing brand reputation and extensive distribution network in these segments are significant competitive advantages.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion Driven by Cost Rationalization & Mix Improvement:** FDC is expected to benefit from ongoing cost rationalization initiatives across manufacturing and supply chain, aiming for a 50-100 bps improvement in EBITDA margins over the next 12-18 months. Furthermore, an increasing contribution from higher-margin specialty products and a gradual shift towards branded generics in international markets are likely to support sustainable margin expansion.
*   **Strategic Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The company is actively diversifying its product portfolio beyond its traditional strongholds into new therapeutic areas like dermatology and pain management. Investments in R&D for new product development and strategic partnerships are aimed at tapping into faster-growing segments within the Indian pharmaceutical market and select international territories, reducing over-reliance on existing categories.
*   **Near-term Catalysts: New Product Launches & Export Growth:** Key near-term catalysts (6-12 months) include the launch of 3-5 new specialty products in the domestic market, expected to contribute meaningfully to revenue growth in FY27E. Additionally, increased traction in key export markets (e.g., Africa, Southeast Asia) for its branded generics and contract manufacturing services, driven by new regulatory approvals and expanded distribution, is anticipated.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Regulatory Scrutiny & Pricing Pressure:** The biggest structural risk for FDC is the evolving regulatory landscape and persistent pricing pressure, especially in the domestic market (DPCO) and key international generics markets. Any adverse changes in drug pricing policies or increased competition from larger generic players could impact profitability and market share, despite the company's established brand equity.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** FDC Limited is an integrated pharmaceutical company engaged in the manufacturing and marketing of a wide range of pharmaceutical formulations and active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs). The company primarily operates in the domestic Indian market and has a growing international presence, focusing on branded generics, ophthalmic products, and oral rehydration salts.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY26E Estimates):**
    *   Domestic Formulations: ~70% (Key products: Electral, Enerzal, Zifi, Ocuflur, Opticrom)
    *   International Business (Formulations & APIs): ~25% (Focus on branded generics in emerging markets, contract manufacturing in developed markets)
    *   Animal Health & Others: ~5%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Domestic Formulations:** Primarily targets general practitioners, specialists (ophthalmologists, pediatricians), hospitals, and pharmacies across India. No single OEM customer, direct to market approach.
    *   **International Business:** Includes direct sales to distributors and institutional buyers in various countries (e.g., Africa, Southeast Asia, CIS regions) and contract manufacturing for global pharmaceutical companies.
    *   **APIs:** Supplies to other pharmaceutical manufacturers globally.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** FDC operates through a lean subsidiary structure. Key overseas subsidiaries include FDC International Ltd. and FDC Inc. in the US, primarily for managing international sales and regulatory affairs.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** FDC has a robust manufacturing footprint across India with 10 state-of-the-art facilities. Key locations include:
    *   Maharashtra: Waluj (Aurangabad) - Formulations & APIs; Roha (Raigad) - APIs
    *   Goa: Verna - Formulations (Tablets, Capsules, Liquids, Ointments)
    *   Himachal Pradesh: Baddi - Formulations
    *   Gujarat: Vadodara - Formulations
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** FDC Limited was founded by Mr. Anand Chandavarkar in 1936. The company remains largely promoter-driven, with the Chandavarkar family holding a significant stake and actively involved in strategic management. The group has a long-standing reputation for ethical practices, quality products, and a conservative financial approach.

**Method 2: EV/EBITDA-based target**
Using a sector median EV/EBITDA multiple of 12.0x on our FY27E EBITDA of Rs. 480 Cr (assuming Net Debt of Rs. 100 Cr and 16.14 Cr shares outstanding):
*   FY27E Revenue: Rs. 2,000 Cr

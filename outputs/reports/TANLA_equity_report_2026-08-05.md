### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Defensive CPaaS market leader with scale moat:** Tanla Platforms is India's largest cloud communication platform, processing ~250 billion A2P (application-to-person) messaging transactions annually and powering 70%+ of India's enterprise CPaaS volumes through its Trilio, Karix, and Gamooga businesses. [^ip-latest] Switching costs are structurally High due to long-tenure enterprise contracts (banks, insurers, telcos) and deep DLT (Distributed Ledger Technology) template integration with every telecom operator in India — replicating this asset would require multi-year regulatory and integration effort.

- **Structural margin expansion story:** EBITDA margin expanded from ~13% in FY21 to 26%+ in FY25 — a ~1,300 bps structural uplift driven by (i) the shift from low-margin international A2P to higher-yield domestic enterprise messaging, (ii) operating leverage on a now-fixed direct-connect cost base with all Indian telcos, and (iii) platform-level SaaS attach via Trilio's omnichannel stack. We model another 200–250 bps of margin expansion to ~28–29% by FY28E as international mix moderates and enterprise volumes scale. [^ar-fy25] [^cc-transcript]

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** The FY24-acquired Gamooga (AI-led customer engagement platform) is now scaling with enterprise clients across BFSI and ecommerce, while Trilio's omnichannel CPaaS suite (WhatsApp Business API, RCS, voice bots) is opening wallet share with existing Tier-1 customers. AI-driven conversational analytics is the next leg of growth, with management commentary indicating 25–30% of new wins include an AI/automation component. [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) RBI-mandated DLT Phase III template scrubbing — expected to push grey-route traffic onto the legitimate DLT pipeline, expanding Tanla's monetisable volumes by an estimated 8–12%; (2) WhatsApp Business API price rationalisation should accelerate Tier-2/3 enterprise adoption, lifting Trilio volumes; (3) Traction in Gamooga's AI-led customer engagement wins should translate into visible revenue contribution from FY27E; (4) Potential inorganic move in conversational AI/voice bot space leveraging the ~Rs. 700 Cr+ net cash on balance sheet. [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk — regulatory concentration:** The DLT-based domestic A2P model, while defensible, depends entirely on TRAI/DLT framework continuity; any future template-pricing caps or direct enterprise-to-telco routing could compress monetisation per message. Mitigant: Tanla's diversification into WhatsApp/RCS/voice (multi-channel) reduces dependence on any single regulatory construct. [^vp-thread]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Tanla Platforms is a pure-play CPaaS (Communications Platform-as-a-Service) provider that enables enterprises to communicate with end-customers across SMS, voice, email, WhatsApp, RCS, and push channels via a unified API layer. Revenue is largely transactional — billed per message (CPaaS messaging), per minute (voice), or per seat/event (Trilio/Gamooga SaaS). [^ip-latest]

**Revenue split by division (FY25, indicative):** [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25]

**Key OEM/end customers per division:** [^ip-latest]
- **Trilio (India):** HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, Axis Bank, Reliance Jio, Flipkart, Amazon India, Zomato, Swiggy, Paytm, CRED, major life insurers and AMCs.
- **Karix (International):** Global banks, OTT apps, logistics majors, and enterprise clients across Middle East, Africa, SE Asia, and LATAM.
- **Gamooga:** Enterprise CX teams in BFSI, ecommerce, edtech, and D2C.

**Subsidiary structure:** Tanla Platforms Limited is the listed parent; operating subsidiaries include Karix Mobile Pvt. Ltd. (international CPaaS, acquired FY19 from Blackstone/GSO Capital), Gamooga (acquired FY24, AI-led customer engagement), and Trilio Software (domestic platform). [^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing/operating footprint:** Tanla is asset-light — its "operating footprint" is a combination of (i) direct-connect nodes with all major Indian telcos, (ii) international carrier interconnects across 200+ countries via Karix, and (iii) engineering centres in Hyderabad (largest), Bengaluru, Chennai, and Mumbai. [^ip-latest]

**Promoter background & group context:** Tanla was founded in 1999 by **Mr. D. Uday Kumar Reddy** (Chairman & MD) and is headquartered in Hyderabad. The company underwent a complete business model transition circa FY17–FY19 — moving away from telecom VAS (value-added services) and international SMS arbitrage into regulated enterprise CPaaS. The promoter group has consistently held 55–58% over the past five years, demonstrating long-term commitment. [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread]

India CPaaS — Tanla's core market — is ~USD 1.5–2 Bn currently and is growing at ~20% CAGR on the back of DLT compliance, WhatsApp Business API adoption, and AI-led CX automation. [^ip-latest]

- **RCS rollout:** Google-backed RCS is expected to scale over FY26–28E, expanding the channel mix.
- **Digital India / RBI digital lending norms:** Driving OTP, transaction, and notification volumes across BFSI.
- **No PLI-style direct subsidy** applies, but regulatory clarity and DLT are net positive for incumbents.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Fabless semiconductor design services moat with 25+ year operating history:** MosChip is one of the few listed Indian pure-play semiconductor design services companies with a 25+ year track record, holding 7+ active global semiconductor customers including marquee names like TSMC, Samsung, and CDAC, providing deep domain expertise across RTL design, physical design, verification, and ASIC turnkey services — a positioning that benefits materially from the global "China+1" supplier diversification theme.[^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]

- **Structural margin expansion narrative from IP-led productization pivot:** Beyond traditional services, MosChip is transitioning to a higher-margin IP and product model — including the in-house developed "Vidyut" power semiconductor IP platform and the "HPC" high-performance computing initiative — which management has guided will progressively lift EBITDA margins from current mid-teens levels toward 22–25% over the FY26–FY28 horizon as the product/IP mix scales.[^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]

- **Diversification into high-growth semiconductor adjacencies (OSAT, Silicon Photonics, Chiplets):** The company is executing a clear three-pronged adjacency expansion — (a) OSAT (Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly & Test) participation via approved government incentive applications, (b) Silicon Photonics co-design services for data center and HPC customers, and (c) Chiplet-based architecture design — directly addressing the global US$ 600 Bn+ semiconductor TAM, with Indian semi-conductor TAM itself estimated at US$ 100 Bn+ by 2030.[^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months) include Q1FY27 print, OSAT approval monetization, and order book disclosures:** The Q1FY27 results filed on 24-Jul-2026 will be the first read on the FY27 trajectory, with investor focus on (a) sequential revenue growth from the recently ramped ASIC turnkey programs, (b) progress on the Vidyut IP commercialization, (c) any order book / multi-year customer win disclosures — which historically the company has been reticent about, making Q1 a key inflection point.[^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk — execution, valuation, and promoter-of-concern signals:** Three structural concerns are: (1) ROCE/ROE of 0.00% and TTM P/E of 152x indicate the stock is priced for a steep execution curve with minimal margin for error, (2) Repeated insider selling reported in public domain discussions creates a corporate governance overhang[^vp-thread], and (3) the semiconductor design services industry remains intensely competitive against larger pure-play peers (e.g., Sasken, eInfochips/Coforge) and global offshore captives — risking margin compression if order ramps slip.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core business model:** MosChip Technologies Limited is a fabless semiconductor and embedded systems design services company that provides end-to-end silicon engineering services — from RTL/architecture design through physical implementation, analog/mixed-signal design, verification, FPGA prototyping, and ASIC turnkey services — to global semiconductor, IoT, automotive, and consumer electronics customers.[^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest]

- **Revenue split by division (estimated, FY25):**
  - ASIC Design & Turnkey Services: ~45–50% of revenue
  - Embedded Systems / IoT Services: ~25–30% of revenue
  - IP / Product (incl. Vidyut, upcoming HPC): ~5–10% of revenue
  - Verification & Physical Design: ~15–20% of revenue[^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]

- **Key OEM/end customers per division:**
  - ASIC/Foundry ecosystem: TSMC, Samsung Foundry (as mentioned in disclosed customer references)
  - Strategic / Government: CDAC (Centre for Development of Advanced Computing)
  - Embedded / IoT: Multiple global Tier-1 customers (disclosed as 7+ active semi customers overall)
  - The company has historically had limited customer concentration disclosure — a known investor grievance[^vp-thread]

- **Subsidiary structure:** MosChip operates through its wholly-owned US subsidiary (MosChip Technologies USA) and a Singapore subsidiary supporting APAC customer engagements, with the Indian parent housing the bulk of engineering headcount in Hyderabad and Bangalore.[^ar-fy25]

- **Manufacturing footprint:** MosChip is asset-light/fabless — no wafer fab ownership — but operates design centers in India (Hyderabad HQ, Bangalore, plus development centers), USA, and Singapore; any future OSAT participation would be the company's first move into physical manufacturing, contingent on government incentive approvals.[^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]

- **Promoter background and group context:** Founded in 1999 by a team of semiconductor industry veterans; the company is professionally managed under CEO/CMD leadership with deep semiconductor industry pedigree. The promoter group holds 42.74% as of the latest shareholding disclosures, but recent forums have flagged repeated insider (non-promoter) selling activity, which investors should monitor closely.[^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Sigma Advanced System Ltd (SASL) is a leading Indian company specializing in the design, development, and manufacturing of advanced sub-systems and components for the Aerospace & Defence (A&D) sector. The company's core business model revolves around providing high-precision, mission-critical solutions to major defence Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and governmental research organizations within India.

**Revenue Split by Division:**

*   **Aerospace Components & Systems:** ~60% of revenue. This segment includes complex machined parts, structural components, actuators, and hydraulic systems for fighter jets, transport aircraft, and helicopters.
*   **Missile Sub-systems:** ~25% of revenue. This division focuses on critical guidance, control, and propulsion sub-systems for various Indian missile programs.
*   **Space & Unmanned Systems:** ~15% of revenue. This includes components for satellites, launch vehicles, and systems for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).

**Key OEM/End Customers:**

*   **Aerospace:** Hindustan Aeronautics Limited (HAL), Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) - Aeronautical Development Agency (ADA), Indian Air Force, Indian Navy.
*   **Missile:** BrahMos Aerospace, DRDO (various missile labs), Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL).
*   **Space:** Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO), HAL (for launch vehicle components).

**Subsidiary Structure:**

SASL operates primarily through its own manufacturing facilities and R&D centers. It does not have significant operating subsidiaries but may engage in joint ventures for specific technological projects.

**Manufacturing Footprint:**

SASL has a robust manufacturing and R&D infrastructure spread across India, with its primary facilities located in:

*   **Karnataka:** Bengaluru (2 plants - Aerospace & R&D Hub)

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant B2B gold jewellery exporter with deep US retailer relationships.** Shanti Gold International Ltd is one of India's largest B2B gold jewellery manufacturers, with a 35+ year track record of supplying studded gold jewellery to marquee US retailers and wholesalers [^ip-latest]. The company has built long-tenure relationships with US mass-market and mid-tier retailers, providing a defensible order book and repeat-order visibility [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin expansion underway via lab-grown diamond (LGD) pivot and value-added studded jewellery.** Management is actively shifting mix toward lab-grown diamond-studded and design-intensive pieces, which command 200–400 bps higher gross margins than plain gold jewellery [^cc-transcript]. The LGD category is structurally cheaper for end-consumers (~50% lower than mined diamond equivalents) and is gaining rapid US shelf-space adoption [^vp-thread].

- **Diversification into branded retail and international markets reduces single-channel dependence.** Beyond core US B2B exports, the company is expanding its branded jewellery footprint through subsidiary entities and exploring newer geographies (Middle East, Europe) [^ip-latest]. This adjacency diversification is expected to lift the branded/retail revenue contribution from low single digits to mid-single digits over FY26E–FY28E [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months): Rights issue deployment, Q1FY27 results, US tariff clarity.** The Board approved a Rights Issue on 31 July 2026 with record date 06 Aug 2026, with proceeds earmarked for capacity expansion, LGD machinery, and working capital [^ar-fy25]. Q1FY27 results scheduled for 13 Aug 2026 board meeting will be the next key catalyst, alongside any incremental US tariff/trade clarity post the current trade-policy review cycle [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk: Gold price volatility and US tariff/trade-policy exposure.** With ~95% revenue from gold jewellery exports (predominantly US-bound), the company is exposed to (i) sharp gold price moves compressing realisations, (ii) potential US import tariffs on Indian-origin jewellery, and (iii) USD/INR currency swings [^vp-thread]. A 5% adverse gold price move can compress gross margins by ~80–120 bps in any given quarter [^cc-transcript].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Shanti Gold International Ltd operates a B2B gold jewellery manufacturing and export model, primarily catering to US-based retailers, wholesalers, and importers [^ip-latest]. The company designs, manufactures, and exports studded gold jewellery (rings, pendants, earrings, bracelets) under both customer-owned brands and selective in-house labels [^ar-fy25].

The company operates through wholly-owned subsidiaries handling specific functions — including international marketing arms, branded retail operations, and lab-grown diamond initiatives [^ar-fy25].

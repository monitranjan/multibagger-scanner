### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Senores Pharmaceuticals Ltd. is a leading Indian pharmaceutical company primarily engaged in the manufacturing and development of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) and providing Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization (CDMO) services. The company focuses on complex, high-value APIs across various therapeutic segments, with a strong emphasis on oncology, central nervous system (CNS), anti-infectives, and anti-diabetics.

*   **Core Business Model:**
    *   **API Manufacturing:** Development and production of a wide range of APIs for both regulated (US, Europe, Japan) and semi-regulated markets. The company specializes in multi-step synthesis and handling hazardous reactions.
    *   **CDMO Services:** Offers integrated services from process development and scale-up to commercial manufacturing of APIs and intermediates for innovator pharmaceutical companies globally. This segment involves custom synthesis and dedicated manufacturing for specific clients.

*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
    *   API Sales (Generics): ~70%
    *   CDMO Services: ~30%

*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **API Sales:** Global generic pharmaceutical companies (e.g., Teva, Sandoz, Mylan, Dr. Reddy's Laboratories, Cipla).
    *   **CDMO Services:** Innovator pharmaceutical companies (e.g., Pfizer, Novartis, AstraZeneca) and specialty generic players requiring custom synthesis.

*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Senores Pharma operates primarily through its wholly-owned subsidiary, Senores Lifesciences Pvt. Ltd., which manages specific R&D and manufacturing operations. The company also has a marketing and distribution arm in Europe, Senores Pharma GmbH.

*   **Manufacturing Footprint:**
    *   **Unit I (Vizag, Andhra Pradesh):** Focus on general APIs and intermediates.
    *   **Unit II (Jigani, Karnataka):** Dedicated to high-potency APIs (HPAPIs) and oncology products.
    *   **Unit III (Hyderabad, Telangana):** Multi-purpose facility for advanced intermediates and select regulated market APIs.
    *   **Unit IV (Ankleshwar, Gujarat):** Primarily dedicated to CDMO projects and new product scale-up.
    *   Total 4 manufacturing plants across 3 states.

*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Senores Pharmaceuticals was founded by Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma, a seasoned pharmaceutical professional with over 30 years of experience in API manufacturing and R&D. The group's vision has been to establish a technology-driven API and CDMO company with a focus on complex chemistry and global quality standards. The promoter group maintains a significant stake and active management involvement, guiding strategic direction and operational excellence.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

GPT Infraprojects Limited is an infrastructure development company primarily engaged in the execution of civil and infrastructure projects and the manufacture of concrete sleepers for railways. The company operates through two main segments:

*   **Infrastructure Division:**
    *   **Core Business Model:** Undertakes civil construction projects, including bridges, roads, railway infrastructure (tracks, earthwork, station buildings), industrial civil works, and building construction. Primarily works as an EPC contractor for government bodies and public sector undertakings.
    *   **Revenue Split:** Approximately 60-65% of total revenue.
    *   **Key OEM/End Customers:** Indian Railways (various zones), National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), State PWDs, various state government entities, and large industrial clients for building construction.
    *   **Manufacturing Footprint:** Operates a steel fabrication unit in West Bengal to support its civil construction projects, enhancing backward integration.
*   **Sleeper Division:**
    *   **Core Business Model:** Manufactures and supplies concrete sleepers (Pre-Stressed Concrete - PSC) and steel sleepers primarily for Indian Railways. Also manufactures railway track components.
    *   **Revenue Split:** Approximately 35-40% of total revenue.
    *   **Key OEM/End Customers:** Indian Railways (direct procurement), various railway project contractors.
    *   **Manufacturing Footprint:** Has multiple manufacturing units for concrete sleepers across India, strategically located to serve different railway zones. Key plants in West Bengal, Uttar Pradesh, and Madhya Pradesh.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** GPT Infraprojects has a wholly-owned subsidiary, GPT Steel Industries Limited, which manufactures steel sleepers and executes steel fabrication jobs, providing critical backward integration support.
*   **Promoter Background & Group Context:** The company was founded by Mr. Dwarika Prasad Tantia. The GPT Group has a history spanning several decades in infrastructure development.

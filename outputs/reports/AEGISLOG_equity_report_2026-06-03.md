### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** Aegis Logistics holds a dominant position in India's oil, gas, and chemical logistics sector, particularly in LPG and liquid terminals. With a network of strategically located terminals (e.g., Pipavav, Kandla, Kochi), Aegis benefits from high entry barriers due to significant capital expenditure, regulatory hurdles, and long gestation periods for new projects. This creates a strong competitive moat, ensuring sustained market share and pricing power in its core segments.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** The company is transitioning towards a higher-margin "Terminal & Port Logistics" business model, moving away from lower-margin sourcing and distribution of LPG. This shift, coupled with increasing throughput volumes and efficient asset utilization, is expected to drive a structural improvement in EBITDA margins by 150-200 bps over the next 3-5 years. The long-term contracts with major oil & gas players further de-risk revenue streams.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Aegis is strategically expanding its presence in the chemical logistics segment, leveraging its existing infrastructure and expertise. The planned expansion of chemical storage capacity at key ports and foray into specialized chemical handling services positions the company to capitalize on India's burgeoning chemical manufacturing growth, offering a new avenue for high-growth revenue and margin accretion.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include the commissioning of new LPG terminals (e.g., Haldia Phase 2, expansion at Pipavav) and increased throughput from existing terminals driven by rising industrial and residential demand for LPG. Furthermore, the successful integration and synergy realization from recent acquisitions/JVs in the gas distribution space could provide an upward revision to earnings estimates.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is the inherent cyclicality of the oil & gas sector and potential policy changes impacting LPG subsidies or import tariffs. While Aegis's long-term contracts mitigate some of this risk, a significant downturn in global energy prices or adverse government policies could impact throughput volumes and pricing power, affecting profitability. Additionally, environmental regulations and the shift towards renewable energy sources pose a long-term transition risk.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Aegis Logistics Ltd. is a leading integrated logistics company in India, primarily engaged in the storage and handling of petroleum products, chemicals, and gas. Its core business model revolves around providing port and terminal logistics services, which include receipt, storage, and dispatch of bulk liquids and gases. The company also has a significant presence in the sourcing, distribution, and marketing of LPG.

*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
    *   Liquid Logistics: ~45%
    *   Gas Logistics (LPG Terminals & Distribution): ~50%
    *   Others (Retail, AutoGas): ~5%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Liquid Logistics:** Major oil marketing companies (OMCs) like IOCL, BPCL, HPCL; large chemical manufacturers; various industrial clients.
    *   **Gas Logistics:** OMCs (for bulk LPG imports and storage), private LPG marketers, industrial consumers, commercial establishments, and residential customers (through its cylinder distribution network).
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Aegis operates through various subsidiaries and joint ventures. A key subsidiary is Aegis Vopak Terminals Ltd., a 51:49 joint venture with Royal Vopak, which manages a significant portion of its liquid and gas terminal operations. Other subsidiaries focus on specific regional distribution or retail initiatives.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** Aegis does not have a 'manufacturing' footprint in the traditional sense, but rather a robust 'logistics infrastructure' footprint. It operates a network of strategically located liquid and gas terminals across India, including:
    *   **Gujarat:** Pipavav, Kandla

    *   **West Bengal:** Haldia
    *   **Tamil Nadu:** Ennore
    *   **Andhra Pradesh:** Mangalore (planned expansion)
    The company has over 10 operational terminals and storage facilities across these states, with ongoing expansion projects.

Currently, the company is led by Mr. Anish Chandaria (Chairman & Managing Director) and Mr. Raj Chandaria (Executive Director).

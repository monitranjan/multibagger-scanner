## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Asset-Backed Floor Valuation Provides Asymmetric Risk-Reward:** Shalimar Paints owns ~67 acres of freehold land across three manufacturing locations — Howrah (1,45,000 sq.mtr.), Nashik (49,800 sq.mtr.) and Sikandrabad (41,242 sq.mtr.) — with a prime 1-acre spare parcel in Gurgaon Sector 32, one of the most sought-after commercial belts in NCR [^vp-thread]. Tangible fixed asset replacement value alone exceeds current market cap, providing a hard downside floor even in a liquidation scenario [^vp-thread].

- **Capital Raise Catalyst Cluster Unlocks Turnaround Optionality:** The Board approved a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) and a Preferential Issue on 12 Aug 2026, alongside an increase in authorised capital [^cc-transcript]. This is the first meaningful equity infusion in years and signals promoter/management commitment to deleveraging and growth capex. Combined with the appointment of Mr. Kundan Sangwar as CFO on 12 Aug 2026, governance and capital structure are being reset [^cc-transcript].

- **Massive Spare Land Capacity at Existing Plants — Capex-Light Growth Optionality:** Only 35% of total land area is currently utilised across the three plants — Howrah (72% spare), Nashik (54% spare), Sikandrabad (61% spare) [^vp-thread]. This translates to negligible brownfield capex for the next 5–7 years of volume growth, a structural advantage versus peers who must acquire greenfield land at premium urban prices.

- **Brand Heritage & Distribution Moat in Tier-2/3 Markets:** Shalimar is among India's oldest paint brands (established 1902, 124 years of continuous operations) with entrenched dealer relationships in East and North India where it enjoys higher mindshare than national peers [^ip-latest]. The decorative paints segment, which contributes the bulk of revenue, benefits from a low-recall, high-trust category where brand legacy matters [^ar-fy25].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Sustained Operating Losses & Working Capital Stress:** Shalimar has reported negative ROCE (-3.91%) and ROE (-22.94%) in FY25A with EPS of Rs. -8.29 [^ar-fy25]. Persistent losses have eroded book value (Rs. 29.91 vs. CMP Rs. 87.04 implying the market is paying ~2.9x book for a loss-making franchise). Until the company demonstrates a clear path to break-even — likely 2–3 quarters post QIP deployment — execution risk remains elevated [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Shalimar Paints operates a two-pillar business — Decorative Paints (interior/exterior emulsions, distempers, primers, wood finishes) and Industrial/Protective Coatings (auto OEM, marine, infrastructure, heavy-duty industrial finishes) [^ip-latest]. The decorative segment drives ~70% of revenue while industrial coatings contribute ~30%, with the latter carrying higher gross margins but lumpier order flow [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A est.):**
- Decorative Paints: ~70% of revenue (~Rs. 350 Cr)
- Industrial & Protective Coatings: ~30% of revenue (~Rs. 150 Cr)
- Exports (Nepal, Bangladesh): ~1.5% of revenue (~Rs. 7–8 Cr) [^vp-thread]

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- *Decorative:* Distributed via 8,000+ dealer network across India; key accounts include institutional housing projects, real estate developers, and government housing schemes [^ip-latest].
- *Industrial:* Auto OEMs (two-wheeler and four-wheeler ancillaries), infrastructure (bridges, metro rail), power sector (NTPC, BHEL ancillaries), and marine coatings [^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary Structure:** Shalimar Paints has a wholly-owned subsidiary in Nepal (Shalimar Paints Nepal Pvt. Ltd.) for cross-border distribution and a Bangladesh liaison for export sales [^ar-fy25]. No major JVs are currently operational.

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Howrah (West Bengal) — Eastern India hub, largest plant
- Nashik (Maharashtra) — Western India hub
- Sikandrabad (Uttar Pradesh) — Northern India hub
- Chinnapuliyur/Gummidipoondi (Tamil Nadu) — Southern India greenfield plant commissioned FY14 [^vp-thread]
- Total plant count: 4 (3 mature + 1 South India)

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** The promoter group holds 75.14% of equity, providing a stable controlling shareholder structure [^ar-fy25]. The original promoter family (Shalimar Group) has historical roots in paints manufacturing dating to 1902. Recent management overhaul has brought in professional leadership from Ingersoll Rand and Blue Dart backgrounds, indicating a shift toward institutional governance [^vp-thread].

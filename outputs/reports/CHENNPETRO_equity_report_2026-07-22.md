## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Capacity-led market leadership with structural moat:** CPCL operates a 11.5 MMTPA refinery at Manali (Chennai) running at ~113% capacity utilisation, one of the highest in the Indian PSU refining cohort, providing fixed-cost absorption leverage and a clear scale moat versus smaller standalone refiners [^ip-latest]. The upcoming Cauvery Basin Refinery (CBR, 9.0 MMTPA) doubles throughput and is the single largest capex catalyst on the horizon [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin story — GRM resilience + petchem diversification:** CPCL's GRM has averaged USD 7-9/bbl over FY22-FY25, materially above the Singapore complex benchmark, driven by yield optimisation, superior distillate slate (~85% light & middle distillates), and proximity to the Sri Lankan / Bangladesh export corridor [^ip-latest]. Forward earnings visibility improves as the Manali petchem complex (LPG, propylene, naphtha derivatives) ramps up, lifting non-fuel contribution from <5% to ~12-15% of EBITDA by FY28E [^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond refining, CPCL is investing in (i) green hydrogen pilot with IIT-Madras, (ii) CBG (compressed biogas) plants, (iii) ethanol blending, and (iv) specialty products (paraffin wax, MTO, hexane, LABFS, petroleum coke, sulphur) which are directly marketed by CPCL (~8% of revenue) and command premium realisations [^vp-thread]. The CBR project also includes a polypropylene unit, anchoring downstream petrochemical integration [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (a) Q1FY27 results on 23 Jul 2026 — expected to reflect normalisation of GRMs post the Q4FY26 inventory loss of Rs. 633 Cr [^vp-thread]; (b) Final dividend record date on 07 Aug 2026 at Rs. 54/share (yield ~4.1%) [^ip-latest]; (c) CBR project sanction and EPC award expected in H2FY27; (d) potential re-rating as PSU refiner valuations normalise post windfall-tax rollback [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Crack-spread volatility driven by global oil price shocks, geopolitical disruptions, and the structural decline in diesel demand from EV penetration (currently ~12% of new vehicle sales, projected 30%+ by 2030) [^vp-thread]. A sustained GRM compression below USD 5/bbl for 2-3 quarters would impair the FY27E earnings trajectory and delay the CBR payback.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** CPCL is a pure-play downstream petroleum refining company that converts crude oil into a slate of refined products. The company operates on a tolling + marketing model where ~92% of mainline fuels (LPG, MS, SKO, ATF, HSD, naphtha, fuel oil, lube base stocks, bitumen) are marketed by parent IOCL, while CPCL directly markets ~8% specialty products to industrial customers [^vp-thread].

**Revenue split by division (FY25A):**
- Motor Spirit (Petrol): ~28% of revenue
- High Speed Diesel: ~42% of revenue
- ATF / Kerosene: ~6% of revenue
- LPG / Naphtha: ~10% of revenue
- Petrochemical feedstocks & specialty products: ~8% of revenue
- Bitumen, lube base stocks, sulphur, pet coke: ~6% of revenue [^ar-fy25]

**Key OEM/end customers per division:**
- Mainline fuels: Marketed exclusively by IOCL through its 36,000+ retail outlet network [^ip-latest]
- Specialty products: Direct industrial customers in paints (paraffin wax), solvents (MTO), explosives (hexane), detergents (LABFS), and defence (NATO-grade diesel, JP-7 missile fuel, rocket propellant) [^vp-thread]
- Petrochemicals: Off-take partners include IOCL's polymer marketing arm and direct polymer converters [^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary structure:** CPCL is a 69.06%-owned subsidiary of Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. (IOCL), with the Government of India holding the residual stake through IOCL. No material operating subsidiaries; CPCL functions as a focused single-site refiner [^ip-latest].

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- Manali Refinery, Chennai (Tamil Nadu) — 11.5 MMTPA, integrated with petrochemical complex [^ip-latest]
- Cauvery Basin Refinery (CBR), Nagapattinam (Tamil Nadu) — 9.0 MMTPA, under development [^ar-fy25]
- Wind-solar hybrid captive power plant at Manali — 120 MW [^ip-latest]

**Promoter background:** Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. — India's largest PSU oil marketing company with ~58,000 PSU outlets, ~140 MMTPA refining capacity group-wide, and AAA credit profile. IOCL provides CPCL with crude sourcing leverage, offtake certainty, and treasury strength [^ip-latest].

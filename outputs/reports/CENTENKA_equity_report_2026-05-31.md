*   **Core Business Model:** CENKA's business model revolves around the production and sale of synthetic yarns and fabrics to various industrial and textile customers. This includes high-performance yarns for automotive applications, industrial uses, and general textile consumption. The company focuses on quality, technical specifications, and long-term customer relationships.

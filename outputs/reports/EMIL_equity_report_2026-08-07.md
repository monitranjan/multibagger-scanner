## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership & cluster-based moat:** EMIL is the 4th largest consumer durables and electronics retailer in India and the largest in the Southern region, with dominant market share in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh [^ip-latest]. The cluster-based expansion strategy — entering a geography, penetrating deeply, and becoming the market leader — mirrors DMart's proven playbook and creates high local brand recall, supplier negotiating leverage, and customer loyalty [^vp-thread].

- **Structural margin expansion story:** Mature stores (>4 years old; 84 stores) delivered 6.8% EBITDA margins in H1 FY26, while non-mature stores (<4 years; 131 stores) delivered only 3.0% — a 380 bps gap that will compress as the newer NCR and AP stores mature [^cc-transcript]. With 131 of 215 stores still in the gestation phase, operating leverage is a multi-year tailwind that has not yet played out.

- **Diversification reducing concentration risk:** Revenue mix is shifting away from the Hyderabad city cluster (which contributed ~58% of FY25 sales) toward Telangana ex-Hyderabad, Andhra Pradesh, and Delhi NCR — NCR revenues grew 38% YoY in Q2 FY26 with 11% SSSG [^cc-transcript]. Category mix is also diversifying: Mobiles 40%, Large Appliances 48%, Small Appliances 12% [^cc-transcript].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Strong summer season demand recovery after a weak Q1 FY26 (AC/cooler sales fell 40%); (ii) Insurance claim reversal of the Rs. 8 Cr Q1 FY26 godown fire loss provision; (iii) Operating leverage from 15 stores added in H1 FY26 turning EBITDA-positive; (iv) Festive season tailwind in Q3 FY26 [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Geographic concentration — Hyderabad city alone accounts for ~50%+ of consolidated revenue, exposing EMIL to Telangana-specific real estate slowdown, regional consumption shocks, and unseasonal weather (which materially impacted Q2 FY25 and Q1 FY26 sales) [^vp-thread]. Margin compression risk from aggressive store additions (40 stores added in FY25, 15 in H1 FY26) is the second key overhang.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** EMIL operates a multi-brand consumer electronics and durables retail chain under two brands — "Bajaj Electronics" (South India) and "Electronics Mart" (Delhi NCR) — selling over 8,000 SKUs across 100+ brands including LG, Sony, Oppo, Dell, Samsung, and Apple [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]. The company operates through two store formats: Multi-Brand Outlets (MBOs) and Exclusive Brand Outlets (EBOs), with a mix of owned and leased real estate.

**Revenue Split by Category (Q1 FY26):**
- Mobiles: ~40% of revenue
- Large Appliances (ACs, refrigerators, TVs): ~48%
- Small Appliances & IT: ~12% [^cc-transcript]

**Revenue Split by Geography (Q2 FY26):**
- Hyderabad city: Rs. 919 Cr (58% of Q2 revenue)
- Telangana (ex-Hyd): Rs. 226 Cr (14%)
- Andhra Pradesh: Rs. 242 Cr (15%)
- Delhi NCR: Rs. 114 Cr (7%)
- Kerala: nascent [^cc-transcript]

**Store Network (Q2 FY26):** 215 stores across 86 cities — 197 MBOs and 11 EBOs, of which 17 stores are owned and the balance on long-term leases [^cc-transcript]. Cluster-wise: Telangana + Hyderabad 116 stores, Andhra Pradesh 66, Delhi NCR 32, Kerala 1 [^cc-transcript]. Backed by 12 central warehouses.

**Key OEM/Brand Partners:** LG, Sony, Samsung, Oppo, Vivo, Dell, HP, Apple, Bosch, Whirlpool, IFB, Haier, Voltas, Daikin [^ip-latest].

**Manufacturing/Operational Footprint:** Retail stores and warehouses concentrated in Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Delhi NCR, and Kerala. No manufacturing operations — pure retailer.

**Promoter Background:** Founded in 1980 by **Pavan Kumar Bajaj** (no relation to the Bajaj Group). The promoter family has over four decades of retail experience in South Indian electronics. Promoter holding stands at 65.17% as of the latest filing [^ip-latest].

**Subsidiary Structure:** EMIL operates primarily through its standalone entity with no material subsidiaries disclosed in recent filings [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter Pedigree & Tenure:** Pavan Kumar Bajaj founded the business in 1980 and has over 44 years of retail experience in South Indian consumer electronics. The promoter family retains 65.17% holding, indicating strong skin-in-the-game and alignment with minority shareholders [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread].

**FCF Deployment Track Record (3 Years):** Operating cash flow post-Ind AS stood at Rs. 272 Cr in H1 FY26 vs Rs. 320 Cr YoY, reflecting working capital absorption from rapid store expansion [^cc-transcript]. Capex has been heavily skewed toward new store openings (40 stores added in FY25, 15 in H1 FY26) and owned property acquisition (17 owned stores). FCF has been negative in FY25 due to aggressive expansion; management has prioritised growth over cash returns.

**Debt Management:** Interest costs rose sharply to Rs. 39 Cr in Q2 FY26 vs Rs. 22 Cr YoY, and depreciation rose to Rs. 38 Cr vs Rs. 30 Cr, reflecting the debt-funded store expansion programme [^cc-transcript]. Interest coverage (EBITDA/Interest) has compressed to ~2.1x in H1 FY26 from ~3.7x in H1 FY25 — a yellow flag warranting monitoring. Specific credit ratings were not disclosed in the latest investor presentation; we recommend tracking CRISIL/ICRA updates.

**Dividend Policy:** Dividend yield is 0.00% — EMIL has not paid dividends since listing, with all cash flow reinvested into store expansion [^ip-latest]. This is appropriate for the current growth phase but limits appeal to income investors.

**Promoter Pledging:** No promoter pledge disclosed in recent filings — a positive governance signal [^ar-fy25].

**Corporate Governance Flags:**
- No auditor changes or qualified opinions flagged in FY25 [^ar-fy25]
- Related Party Transactions (RPTs) appear routine and within normal course of business based on annual report disclosures [^ar-fy25]
- One operational incident in Q1 FY26: fire at a Hyderabad godown causing Rs. 8 Cr inventory loss (fully insured; provision expected to reverse on claim settlement) [^cc-transcript]
- Divestment of 4 EBOs in Q2 FY26 generated other income of Rs. 17 Cr — indicates active portfolio rationalisation [^cc-transcript]

**JVs & Partnerships:** No material JVs or strategic partnerships disclosed. EMIL operates on a standalone retail model with brand-level supplier relationships rather than equity partnerships [^ip-latest].

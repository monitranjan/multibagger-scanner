### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & First-Mover Advantage in Electric Buses:** Olectra Greentech (OGL) benefits significantly from its early entry and established leadership in India's nascent electric bus (e-bus) market. Its strategic partnership with BYD (China) provides access to proven technology, enabling OGL to capture a substantial share of state transport undertaking (STU) tenders. The company's established manufacturing capabilities and servicing network across key states create a significant entry barrier for new players, providing a strong competitive moat in a rapidly growing segment.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion Driven by Localization & Scale:** OGL is strategically increasing localization of e-bus components, including battery packs and motors, moving beyond mere assembly. This initiative, coupled with anticipated economies of scale as order books expand, is expected to drive structural margin improvements. We project EBITDA margins to improve by 150-200 bps over the next 2-3 years, moving from current 14-15% levels towards 16-17% by FY28E, as component import reliance reduces and manufacturing efficiencies kick in.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Electric Truck & Construction Equipment:** OGL is strategically diversifying into the electric truck and electric construction equipment segments, leveraging its EV powertrain expertise. The recent foray into electric tippers (e-tippers) and the development of electric light commercial vehicles (e-LCVs) open up new, high-growth adjacencies. These segments are less tender-dependent than e-buses and offer potentially higher margins, reducing reliance on STU tenders and broadening the revenue base.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts: Robust Order Book Execution & PLI Scheme Benefits:** The company holds a strong order book of over 8,000 e-buses (as of Q4 FY26), providing significant revenue visibility for the next 2-3 years. Efficient execution of these orders, particularly the large KSRTC and MSRTC contracts, will be a key catalyst. Additionally, OGL is a beneficiary of the government's PLI scheme for Advanced Chemistry Cell (ACC) battery manufacturing and automobile components, which will further support localization efforts and profitability in the next 6-12 months.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Intense Competition & Technology Disruption:** The biggest structural risk for Olectra Greentech is the increasing competition in the e-bus and wider EV segments from established domestic players (e.g., Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland) and potential new entrants. Aggressive bidding in STU tenders could compress margins. Furthermore, rapid technological advancements in battery chemistry and charging infrastructure by competitors could disrupt OGL's current technological advantage if the company fails to innovate and adapt quickly.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Olectra Greentech Ltd (OGL) is primarily engaged in the manufacturing and supply of electric buses in India. The company operates on a build-own-operate (BOO) or outright sale model for state transport undertakings (STUs) and private operators. Beyond manufacturing, OGL also provides comprehensive after-sales service, charging infrastructure solutions, and maintenance contracts, establishing an integrated ecosystem for its electric vehicles.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (as of FY26 estimates):**
    *   Electric Buses: ~90-92%
    *   Electric Trucks (Tippers): ~5-7% (nascent, growing segment)
    *   Insulators: ~2-3% (legacy business, gradually de-emphasized)
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Electric Buses:** Major STUs across India, including Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation (MSRTC), Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC), Telangana State Road Transport Corporation (TSRTC), Pune Mahanagar Parivahan Mahamandal Ltd (PMPML), and various other municipal corporations.
    *   **Electric Trucks (Tippers):** Primarily focused on construction and infrastructure companies, mining operators, and logistics players for niche applications.
    *   **Insulators:** Power transmission and distribution companies (domestic and international).
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** OGL operates primarily through its parent entity. It has a joint venture with BYD Auto Industry Co. Ltd. (China) for technology transfer and manufacturing of electric buses.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:**
    *   **Telangana:** Primary manufacturing facility in Shamirpet, Hyderabad. This facility has an installed capacity of over 1,500 e-buses annually, with plans for significant expansion.
    *   **Other States:** Assembly units or service centers are being established in other regions based on tender requirements and market demand.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Olectra Greentech is part of the MEIL (Megha Engineering & Infrastructures Ltd) Group, a diversified infrastructure conglomerate with interests in irrigation, power, roads, and hydrocarbons. The MEIL Group's strong financial backing and project execution capabilities provide a robust foundation for OGL's capital-intensive EV ventures. Mr. K.V.R. Kishore, the Executive Director, has been instrumental in OGL's pivot to electric mobility. The promoter group's deep understanding of large-scale project execution and government contracts is a significant advantage.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Turnaround Driven by HyperSense AI Platform:** Subex is successfully transitioning from its legacy, high-maintenance on-premises revenue assurance models to its cloud-native HyperSense AI platform. HyperSense integrates Business Assurance, Fraud Management, and Network Analytics into a unified, AI-driven SaaS offering. This transition significantly lowers deployment times from months to weeks, enabling faster billing cycles and reducing customer acquisition costs.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion:** The shift towards a SaaS-dominated revenue model is expected to drive gross margins up by 400-500 bps over the next two years. Legacy delivery models required heavy on-site customization; the standardized HyperSense SaaS architecture reduces execution costs. We estimate EBITDA margins to scale from the current 11.5% to 16.5% by FY28E as operating leverage kicks in.
*   **Diversification into Enterprise and Fintech Fraud Risk:** Historically dependent on telecom operators, Subex is leveraging its core identity analytics and fraud management engines to address non-telecom verticals. The company has initiated pilot projects in the fintech and digital commerce sectors to provide real-time transaction fraud detection, opening up an incremental addressable market of over USD 500 Million.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** Key catalysts include: (a) Expected renewal of multi-year managed services contracts with Tier-1 operators in the EMEA and APAC regions at higher average contract values (ACV); (b) Rapid commercialization of the partnership with major hyperscalers (AWS and Azure) to co-sell HyperSense; and (c) Gradual recovery of cash flows as older, low-margin legacy contracts wind down by late FY27E.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The principal risk remains the company's highly fragmented shareholding structure (98.15% public holding) and the lack of a strong, well-capitalized promoter group. This limits Subex's capacity to raise aggressive equity capital for large-scale M&A and leaves the company vulnerable to corporate governance disruptions or hostile takeovers.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Subex Ltd is a global provider of telecom analytics software, specializing in Business Support Systems (BSS). The company helps global communication service providers (CSPs) maximize revenues, mitigate operational risks, and secure network ecosystems through real-time data analytics.
*   **Revenue Split by Division:**
    *   *Business Assurance & Fraud Management (Core Products):* ~72% of revenues. Consists of software licensing, SaaS subscriptions, and implementation of the HyperSense and legacy platforms.
    *   *Managed Services & Professional Services:* ~20% of revenues. Focuses on ongoing operational support, system integration, and custom solution delivery.
    *   *Network Analytics & IoT Security:* ~8% of revenues. Provides network asset management and cybersecurity solutions for connected devices.
*   **Key Customers:** Subex serves over 200 telecom operators globally. Key clients include Tier-1 players such as British Telecom (BT), Telefonica, Telstra, MTN Group, and Reliance Jio.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Subex operates through key wholly-owned subsidiaries to facilitate localized sales and compliance: Subex Americas Inc. (USA), Subex UK Ltd (United Kingdom), Subex Middle East FZE (UAE), and Subex Technologies Ltd (India).
*   **Manufacturing & Development Footprint:** As a pure-play software product firm, Subex does not have manufacturing plants. Its primary R&D and global delivery hub is located in Bengaluru, Karnataka, supported by regional sales and technical support offices in Denver (USA), London (UK), Dubai (UAE), and Singapore.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Subex is a professionally managed company with no identifiable promoter group holding significant operational control. The promoter shareholding stands at a nominal 1.36%. The company is governed by an independent Board of Directors and managed by an experienced executive leadership team with deep domain expertise in telecom software.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

*   **TAM & CAGR:** The global Telecom Revenue Assurance and Fraud Management market is estimated at USD 3.2 Billion (approx. Rs. 26,500 Cr) in 2026 and is projected to grow at a CAGR of 7.8% to reach USD 4.7 Billion by 2031. Growth is driven by the complexity of 5G billing architectures, IoT device proliferation, and rising digital identity fraud.
*   **Policy Tailwinds:** 
    *   *5G Rollouts:* Global telecom regulators mandate real-time monitoring of network slices, driving demand for advanced network analytics.
    *   *Data Protection Regulations:* Stricter compliance mandates (GDPR, India's DPDP Act) require operators to secure customer billing data and prevent leakages, directly benefiting Subex’s compliant product suite.
*   **Competitive Moat Analysis:**
    *   *Switching Costs (Strong):* Telecom operators rarely replace core revenue assurance and fraud management software due to the high risk of billing disruptions and integration complexities. Subex enjoys a customer retention rate exceeding 90%.
    *   *Scale (Moderate):* While Subex has a global footprint, it is smaller than diversified IT giants, limiting its marketing and R&D budgets.
    *   *Pricing Power (Moderate):* Subex maintains steady pricing for its core proprietary platforms but faces pricing pressure in generic managed services contracts.

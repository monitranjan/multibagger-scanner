## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Integrated Sugar-Ethanol-Cogeneration model with Bihar-UP asset base.** Magadh Sugar is part of the Kushwaha group (KMLM) with ~82.97% promoter holding and operates three sugar mills in Bihar (Gopalganj, Narkatiagunj, Hasanpur) plus a 60 KLPD distillery at Gopalganj, offering revenue diversification across sugar, ethanol, and power at the mill gate[^ip-latest]. Crushing capacity stands at ~13,500 TCD with power co-gen of ~38 MW, providing multiple earnings streams per tonne of cane crushed[^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin story anchored in ethanol blending economics.** With the Government of India targeting 20% ethanol blending (E20) by 2025-26 and the 2025 Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) program revising procurement pricing upward, distillery realisations are decoupled from the volatile sugar price cycle[^ar-fy25]. The 60 KLPD expanded distillery gives Magadh a captive, high-margin ENA/ethanol channel that materially smooths blended EBITDA margins vs pure-play sugar peers[^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies (cogeneration + distillery).** Beyond ethanol, the 38 MW bagasse-based cogeneration capacity provides PLF-linked power sales to the Bihar state grid under long-tenure PPAs, plus a grain-based ethanol window at Gopalganj[^ip-latest]. The grain/dual-feed distillery design allows the company to swing between cane juice, B-heavy molasses, and grain feedstock based on relative economics — a flexibility only a few North-Indian mills possess[^ar-fy25].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months).** (a) Q1FY27 results on 04 Aug 2026 (already filed) — watch for syrup/sugar mix shift and ethanol dispatch volumes; (b) Cane area map for SS2026-27 (UP + Bihar) — early reports suggest improved sowing; (c) Sugar season FRP notification for SS2026-27; (d) Any incremental ethanol procurement tender allocations from OMCs under EBP; (e) Continued re-rating as institutional investors (currently FII/DII at 0%) discover this under-researched name[^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk: Sugar cycle + cane pricing mismatch.** Indian sugar remains a 6-7 year cyclical with 2-3 year upswings; cane FRP is administered while sugar realisations are market-linked, creating structural margin compression in down-cycles[^vp-thread]. The lack of cane decontrol (delink from sugar price) and dependence on Bihar state policy (where cane price is politically sensitive) remain the single largest structural overhang on free cash flow generation[^cc-transcript].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Magadh Sugar & Energy Ltd is an integrated sugar manufacturer with adjacent distillery and cogeneration operations, primarily serving the Bihar and UP markets. The company follows a "sugar-plus" model where every tonne of sugarcane crushed yields three revenue streams: sugar, ethanol/ENA, and surplus power[^ip-latest].

**Revenue split by division (FY25):**
- **Sugar:** ~75-78% of revenue — refined and raw sugar sold to institutional buyers, traders, and via MCP (monthly quota) channels[^ar-fy25]
- **Distillery (Ethanol/ENA):** ~15-18% of revenue — ethanol supplied to OMCs under EBP program; ENA sold to liquor manufacturers[^ip-latest]
- **Cogeneration (Power):** ~5-7% of revenue — sale of surplus power to Bihar DISCOMs under PPA[^ar-fy25]
- **Others (by-products, bagasse sales):** ~2-3%[^ar-fy25]

**Key OEM/end customers per division:**
- **Sugar:** Institutional buyers, commodity traders, MCP allotments
- **Ethanol:** Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) — IOCL, BPCL, HPCL under EBP tenders
- **Power:** Bihar State Power (Holding) Co. Ltd / North Bihar Power Distribution[^ar-fy25]
- **ENA:** Adjacent liquor/IML manufacturers in Bihar, UP, Jharkhand

**Subsidiary structure:** The company is a part of the Kushwaha/Magadh group with KMLM and related entities as promoters. As of FY25, the company has limited subsidiary exposure — primarily a step-down holding structure for the distillery and cogeneration SPVs[^ar-fy25]. The consolidated entity largely mirrors the standalone, suggesting a focused operating perimeter.

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- **Bihar (primary):** 3 sugar mills — Gopalganj (HQ), Narkatiagunj, Hasanpur[^ip-latest]
- **Crushing capacity:** ~13,500 TCD (Tonnes of Cane per Day) across the three units
- **Distillery:** 60 KLPD grain/dual-feed plant at Gopalganj (commissioned/expanded in recent years)
- **Cogeneration:** ~38 MW bagasse-based capacity across mills
- **Plant count:** 3 sugar units + 1 distillery + 3 cogeneration units

**Promoter background and group context:** The Kushwaha family, led by Sanjay Nopany and the broader Nopany-Kushwaha group, controls the company with a high 82.97% promoter stake[^ip-latest]. The group has legacy interests in sugar (including associate exposure through other entities) and has demonstrated long-cycle commitment through multiple down-years without diluting equity or pledging promoter shares aggressively[^ar-fy25]. The high promoter concentration also explains the negligible institutional holding (FII 0% / DII 0%) — a feature typical of small-cap, family-controlled sugar names in Eastern India[^vp-thread].

- **USD equivalent:** Sugar TAM ~USD 13-14 Bn; Ethanol TAM ~USD 5-6 Bn (at 83 INR/USD)
- **CAGR:** Indian sugar consumption growing at ~3-4% CAGR; ethanol demand growing at ~10-12% CAGR through 2030 driven by E20 targets[^cc-transcript]

**Relevant policy tailwinds:**
- **EBP / E20 program:** 20% ethanol blending target by 2025-26 — direct demand pull for distillers
- **Sugar export policy:** Periodic government quota-based export windows during surplus years
- **Cane FRP:** Central government administered Fair & Remunerative Price (FRP) for sugarcane; SS2025-26 FRP at Rs. 340/quintal with ~74% recovery-linked premium
- **MOL (Minimum Indicative Export Quota):** Periodic buffer stock and release mechanism to manage domestic sugar prices
- **Bihar state policy:** State Advised Price (SAP) for cane — politically sensitive, periodic upward revisions
- **Infra capex:** Government push for ethanol pipelines, grain-based distillery capacity

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter pedigree and tenure:** The Kushwaha/Nopany group has been associated with Magadh Sugar since its incorporation and has navigated multiple sugar down-cycles (2008-2011, 2018-2020, 2022-2024) without diluting promoter equity or aggressively pledging shares[^ar-fy25]. The high 82.97% promoter holding is among the highest in the listed sugar space, providing a strong alignment-of-interest signal[^ip-latest]. Management has consistently demonstrated patient capital approach — the 60 KLPD distillery capex was funded through internal accruals and structured debt rather than aggressive equity dilution[^ar-fy25].

**FCF deployment track record (3 years):**
- **FY23-FY25:** Cumulative FCF generation has been modest, with capex on the distillery expansion (60 KLPD) absorbing significant operating cash flow[^ar-fy25]
- **Capex intensity:** Capex/Revenue ratio elevated during FY23-FY24 due to distillery expansion; expected to normalize from FY26-FY27
- **Working capital:** Seasonal working capital swings are funded through CC/OD facilities from SBI and other PSU banks
- **FCF trend:** Improving from FY25 onwards as distillery capex peaks out and ethanol dispatch volumes scale up[^cc-transcript]

**Debt management:**
- **Coverage ratio:** Interest coverage has been volatile, dependent on sugar cycle phase; in down-cycles, EBITDA/Interest drops to 1.5-2.0x; in up-cycles, rises to 4-5x[^ar-fy25]
- **Credit rating:** Reaffirmed in July 2026 (announcement dated 2026-07-08) — details on agency and exact rating to be verified from the credit rating PDF[^cc-transcript]
- **Lender mix:** Predominantly PSU banks (SBI, PNB, Bank of Baroda) — a positive given PSU bank discipline but also reflects limited access to cheaper private bank capital
- **Debt/Equity:** Moderate leverage, with long-term debt primarily funding the distillery capex

**Dividend policy (payout % and 5-year average):**
- **Current dividend yield:** 2.34% (as per header data)[^ip-latest]
- **5-year average payout:** Variable due to cyclical earnings; in up-cycles, payout has historically been 20-25% of PAT; in down-cycles, dividends have been nominal to preserve cash[^ar-fy25]
- **Recent trend:** Management has indicated a stable-to-rising dividend trajectory as ethanol cash flows provide earnings visibility

**Promoter pledging status:** Promoter shares are largely unpledged as of recent disclosures, a positive signal of financial discipline[^ar-fy25]. This is in contrast to several peers (Renuka Sugar historically, some UP-based mills) where promoter pledging has been a material overhang[^vp-thread].

**Corporate governance flags:**
- **Auditor changes:** No material auditor changes in recent years; statutory audit conducted by a reputable firm[^ar-fy25]
- **Related Party Transactions (RPTs):** RPTs exist within the Kushwaha group but are typically transaction-based and disclosed in the annual report; no major governance red flags
- **Qualified opinions:** No qualified audit opinions in recent annual reports[^ar-fy25]
- **AGM voting:** 12th AGM completed on 29 July 2026 with scrutinizer's report filed — resolutions passed with requisite majority (announcement 2026-07-29)[^cc-transcript]
- **Board composition:** Adequate independent director representation as per SEBI LODR norms
- **Disclosure quality:** Improving — quarterly results filed within stipulated timelines; investor presentation available on corporate website

**JVs or partnerships and strategic rationale:**
- **OMC contracts:** Long-term ethanol supply arrangements with OMCs (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL) under the EBP program — these are tender-based but provide multi-year demand visibility[^cc-transcript]
- **Bihar DISCOM PPA:** Power purchase agreement for cogeneration sales — provides stable, regulated cash flow
- **No major equity JVs:** Magadh has not entered into material equity JVs in recent years; growth has been organic/standalone
- **Strategic rationale:** The pure-play standalone model allows full control over cane area, distillery feedstock mix, and capex prioritization — appropriate for a mid-sized player navigating cyclicality[^ip-latest]

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Global Leadership in Critical Wear-Resistant Liners:** Tega Industries holds a dominant global position in specialized wear-resistant mill liners and other consumables for the mining, minerals processing, and bulk material handling industries. Its proprietary polymer-based solutions offer superior lifespan and efficiency compared to traditional steel liners, creating a strong competitive moat driven by product performance and customer stickiness due to high switching costs (re-engineering, downtime). The company is among the top 3 global players in its niche, with a strong presence in key mining geographies like South Africa, Australia, and Chile.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion driven by Product Mix and Operational Leverage:** We anticipate a structural improvement in EBITDA margins, moving from ~20% in FY24 to 22-23% by FY27E. This will be driven by a shift towards higher-margin polymer-based products, increased utilization of recently expanded capacities, and operational efficiencies from automation initiatives. The company's focus on premium, engineered solutions, coupled with its global manufacturing footprint, allows for optimized cost structures and better realization.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjuncts:** Tega is strategically diversifying beyond its core mining consumables into adjacent high-growth areas such as specialized rubber products for industrial applications (e.g., conveyor belts, hoses) and custom-engineered products for specific industrial processes. While currently a smaller revenue contributor, these segments are expected to grow at a faster pace, leveraging Tega's material science expertise and manufacturing capabilities, thereby reducing cyclicality inherent in the mining sector.
*   **Near-term Catalysts from Mining Capex Cycle and New Product Launches:** A sustained global mining capex cycle, driven by demand for critical minerals (copper, lithium, nickel) for the energy transition, is expected to provide a strong tailwind for Tega's core business. Additionally, the planned launch of new generation polymer compounds and digital monitoring solutions for mill liners in the next 6-12 months could further enhance product differentiation and capture market share. Strong order inflows and robust execution in key international markets are expected to drive revenue growth.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is the high dependence on global commodity cycles, particularly in the mining sector. A prolonged downturn in commodity prices or a significant slowdown in global mining capex could adversely impact demand for Tega's products, leading to revenue deceleration and pressure on profitability. While diversification efforts are underway, the core business remains susceptible to these macro-economic shifts.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Tega Industries is a global manufacturer and distributor of specialized wear-resistant lining products, primarily for the mineral beneficiation, mining, and bulk material handling industries. The company's products are critical consumables used to protect processing equipment (e.g., ball mills, SAG mills, chutes, hoppers) from abrasive wear and impact, thereby extending equipment life and improving operational efficiency. Tega provides customized solutions, often involving material science expertise in rubber, polyurethane, and composite materials.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   **Mineral Processing & Mining Consumables:** ~90% of revenue (wear-resistant liners for mills, screens, hydrocyclones, conveyor accessories).
    *   **Industrial & Other Products:** ~10% of revenue (specialized rubber products, custom-engineered solutions for other industrial applications).
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Mineral Processing & Mining Consumables:** Direct sales to large global mining companies (e.g., Anglo American, BHP, Rio Tinto, Vale) and various mid-tier miners. Also supplies to OEMs of mining equipment.
    *   **Industrial & Other Products:** Diverse industrial clients requiring specialized rubber and polymer components for applications in sectors like cement, power, and construction.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Tega operates through a robust global subsidiary structure, with key manufacturing and sales operations in major mining regions. Notable subsidiaries include Tega Industries (South Africa) (Pty) Ltd., Tega Industries (Australia) Pty Ltd., Tega Industries Chile SpA, and Tega Industries (USA) Inc. This localized presence facilitates direct customer engagement and service.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** Tega has a significant global manufacturing footprint to cater to its international clientele.

    *   **Chile:** 1 plant in Santiago.
    *   **Australia:** 1 plant in Perth.
    *   **USA:** 1 plant in Atlanta.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Tega Industries was founded in 1976 by Mr. Madan Mohan Mohanka. The company began as a joint venture with Skega AB, Sweden, and later became a wholly Indian-owned entity.

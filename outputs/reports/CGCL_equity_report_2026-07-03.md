While CGCL has robust underwriting and collection mechanisms, a prolonged economic slowdown or regional distress could lead to higher delinquencies and credit costs, impacting profitability and growth.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Capri Global Capital Limited (CGCL) is a diversified Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC) with a focus on serving the financial needs of underserved segments in India. Its core business model revolves around providing accessible and tailored financial products to individuals and micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs).

*   **Core Business Model:** CGCL operates primarily as a lending institution, offering a range of financial products including Affordable Housing Finance, MSME Loans, and Gold Loans. The company leverages a phygital (physical + digital) approach, combining an extensive branch network with technology-driven processes for lead generation, underwriting, and collection. Its strategy is to penetrate Tier 2/3 cities and rural/semi-urban markets, building strong local relationships.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   Housing Finance: ~55-60%
    *   MSME Loans: ~30-35%
    *   Gold Loans: ~5-10% (rapidly growing)
    *   Other (Construction Finance, etc.): <5%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Housing Finance:** Primarily caters to first-time homebuyers in the affordable housing segment, often self-employed individuals with informal incomes, seeking loans for purchase or construction of homes.
    *   **MSME Loans:** Provides working capital and term loans to small and micro-enterprises across various sectors, including manufacturing, trading, and services.
    *   **Gold Loans:** Serves individuals from all income segments requiring quick, short-term liquidity against their gold ornaments.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Capri Global Capital Limited is the parent entity. Its key subsidiary is Capri Global Housing Finance Limited, which focuses specifically on the affordable housing finance segment.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint (States, Plant Count):** As a financial services company, CGCL does not have a manufacturing footprint. Instead, it operates through a vast network of branches. As of FY24, CGCL has over 650 branches spread across various states in India, with a significant presence in states like Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Delhi-NCR, among others.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** CGCL is promoted by Mr. Rajesh Sharma, a seasoned professional with over two decades of experience in financial services.

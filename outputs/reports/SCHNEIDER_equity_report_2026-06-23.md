### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Schneider Electric Infrastructure Limited (SEIL) is a prominent player in India's electrical equipment sector, engaged in the manufacturing, design, and servicing of a wide range of products for the power distribution and transmission industry. Its core business model revolves around providing integrated solutions, from individual components to complete turnkey projects, primarily catering to utilities, industries, and infrastructure developers.

*   **Core Business Model:** SEIL manufactures and markets electrical distribution and power equipment, including transformers, switchgear, and related components. The company also offers associated services such as installation, commissioning, and maintenance. It leverages Schneider Electric's global technology and R&D capabilities to offer advanced, energy-efficient solutions.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (Estimated FY24A):**
    *   **Transformers (Power & Distribution):** ~55-60% of revenue. This includes a comprehensive range of transformers from distribution transformers to large power transformers.
    *   **Switchgear & Related Components (Medium & High Voltage):** ~30-35% of revenue. This segment comprises circuit breakers, switchboards, protection relays, and other grid automation products.
    *   **Services & Others:** ~5-10% of revenue. This includes installation, maintenance, upgrades, spare parts, and digital services.
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Utilities:** State Electricity Boards (SEBs), Power Grid Corporation of India (PGCIL), various private distribution companies (DISCOMs).
    *   **Industries:** Large manufacturing plants, process industries (e.g., cement, steel, chemicals), automotive.
    *   **Infrastructure:** Metro rail projects, airports, data centers, commercial and residential developers.
    *   **Exports:** Various utilities and industrial clients in Southeast Asia, Africa, and the Middle East, facilitated by the global Schneider Electric network.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** SEIL operates primarily as a standalone entity in India, with its operations fully integrated into the broader Schneider Electric global framework. It does not have significant direct subsidiaries in India.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** SEIL has a robust manufacturing presence in India, with key facilities located strategically to serve different markets:
    *   **Vadodara, Gujarat:** Primary manufacturing hub for power and distribution transformers, and some switchgear. (Total 3 plants in Vadodara)

Schneider Electric SE is a global leader in energy management and automation solutions, with a rich history spanning over 180 years.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche specialty chemicals franchise with 40-year vintage and entrenched customer stickiness.** Chembond has built a 5-decade-old specialty chemicals platform supplying to blue-chip customers across autos (OEMs), steel mills, fertilisers, refineries & petrochemicals, power plants and infra projects including metro systems, bridges and expressways[^ip-latest]. Long qualification cycles in auto/steel surface-treatment and ongoing technology tie-ups (legacy Henkel partnership, Ashland/Solenis in water) act as meaningful switching-cost moats[^vp-thread].

- **Structural margin trajectory via pivot from commodity to specialty adjacencies.** Management is consciously shifting the mix toward higher-margin businesses — Animal Health & Nutrition (enzymes, prebiotics, probiotics, acidifiers, toxin binders, dairy feed supplements), Industrial Coatings, Construction Chemicals, Polyamides and Adhesives & Sealants[^ar-fy25]. This re-mix should drive 150–200 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY26E–FY28E as legacy metal-treatment dilutes[^cc-transcript].

- **Optionality from H2O Innovation India JV and other subsidiaries is materially under-priced.** The H2O Innovation India JV (with H2O Innovation Canada) offers state-of-art tech for complete water solutions — from raw-water treatment to waste-water recycling — addressing India’s massive water infrastructure opportunity[^ip-latest]. Combined with the newly-launched Animal Health & Aquaculture vertical (enzymes, organic minerals, growth promoters) and other unlisted subsidiaries, the consolidated entity is materially undervalued on a SOTP basis[^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Subsidiary/JV value-unlock via potential demerger or strategic stake sale in H2O Innovation India or Animal Health; (ii) sustained ramp in Animal Health & Aquaculture revenues from a low base; (iii) likely re-rating as FII/DII participation picks up from current 0%/0% — historically low institutional ownership creates room for passive flows[^vp-thread]; (iv) Q1FY27 results declared 16 Jul 2026 and AGM completed 31 Jul 2026 set the stage for fresh disclosures[^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk: thin standalone profitability and weak ROCE.** Standalone NP margin is in low single digits (~4%) and ROCE is reported at 0.00% on Screener.in, indicating either depressed capital efficiency or an accounting artifact worth investigating[^vp-thread]. Consolidated PBT of ~Rs. 13 Cr on Rs. 300+ Cr turnover leaves little cushion against input-cost shocks, FX volatility, or any subsidiary impairment — a credible structural margin recovery is the key gating factor for re-rating[^ar-fy25].

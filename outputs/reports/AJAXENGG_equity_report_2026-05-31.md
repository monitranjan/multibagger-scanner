These adjacencies benefit from the same infrastructure growth drivers and allow Ajax to cross-sell to its existing customer base, enhancing wallet share. *   **Near-Term Catalysts from Government Infrastructure Push and Housing:** The sustained push by the Indian government on infrastructure development (e.g., National Infrastructure Pipeline, Gati Shakti) and affordable housing schemes (PM Awas Yojana) will drive consistent demand for construction equipment over the next 12-18 months. *   **Biggest Structural Risk: Cyclicality of Construction Sector & Raw Material Volatility:** While government impetus provides near-term stability, the construction equipment sector remains inherently cyclical and susceptible to economic downturns, interest rate fluctuations, and project funding delays. Additionally, volatility in raw material prices (steel, cement) can impact margins if not effectively passed on to customers, posing a risk to profitability despite localization efforts.

The business model emphasizes robust engineering, aftermarket support (spares and service), and a wide distribution network to ensure high equipment uptime and customer satisfaction.

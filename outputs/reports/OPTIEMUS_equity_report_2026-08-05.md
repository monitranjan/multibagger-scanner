## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Diversified multi-vertical platform with marquee global brand partnerships:** Optiemus has transformed from a single-line telecom distribution entity into a multi-vertical platform spanning licensed mobile handsets (BlackBerry), telecom distribution, enterprise mobility, drones/UAV systems, and electronics manufacturing services (EMS) — anchoring revenue visibility across at least four distinct cash flows.[^ip-latest] The BlackBerry licensing partnership (India, Sri Lanka, Nepal, Bangladesh) provides exclusive regional brand monetization, and management has been actively layering newer JVs/contracts (global smartphone ODM relationships, defence-adjacent drone programmes) on top of this base.[^ip-latest]

- **Structural margin re-rating underway as hardware mix shifts:** Historical consolidated EBITDA margins had been compressed (mid-single digits) due to low-margin telecom distribution; mix shift toward BlackBerry handsets (gross margin profile materially higher), drone systems, and EMS is expected to lift blended EBITDA margins by ~200–300 bps over FY26E–FY28E.[^ip-latest] FY25A EPS of Rs. 8.26 already reflects the start of this transition; we model a 30%+ EPS CAGR through FY27E as the higher-margin verticals scale.[^ar-fy25]

- **Defence & drones vertical — a high-growth adjacency that the market is under-pricing:** Management has positioned UAV/drone systems as a strategic growth pillar, leveraging its electronics hardware DNA, MeitY/DoT relationships, and existing manufacturing infrastructure.[^ip-latest] The resignation of Mr. Vrajesh Shelat (President-New Projects) on 31 Jul 2026 is an interim transition event, but the underlying thesis remains intact as the company continues to bid on defence orders and pursue MoD-aligned certifications.[^ip-latest] We estimate drones/defence could contribute 10–15% of consolidated revenue by FY28E vs. negligible contribution today.

- **Near-term catalysts (next 6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results (declared 04 Aug 2026) likely to show continued margin expansion; (ii) preferential allotment concluded on 04 Aug 2026 — proceeds expected to fund drone R&D and EMS capacity expansion, accelerating capex visibility; (iii) potential new BlackBerry product refreshes and channel expansion into tier-2/3 markets; (iv) defence order wins / trial conversions from drone pilots.[^ip-latest] Each of these is a discrete event that can trigger re-rating.

- **Biggest structural risk — capital inefficiency and re-investment intensity:** ROCE at 2.71% (TTM) signals that historical capital deployment has yielded sub-cost-of-capital returns, with the diversification push requiring sustained capex in drones/EMS that could keep ROCE depressed in the near term.[^ar-fy25] Additionally, the 73.27% concentrated promoter holding and minimal institutional sponsorship (FII 0.74%, DII 0.49%) result in thin float, which amplifies price volatility and creates governance overhang risk if capital allocation discipline slips.[^vp-thread] Any slippage in the margin trajectory or capex ROIC would warrant rating reconsideration.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Optiemus Infracom is a Delhi-headquartered diversified technology company operating across (a) Telecom & Mobility Distribution, (b) Branded Mobile Handsets (BlackBerry licensee), (c) Drones & Defence Electronics, and (d) Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS)/ODM. The company manufactures, distributes, and services technology products with a vertically integrated model spanning design, manufacturing, distribution, and after-sales support.[^ip-latest]

**Revenue split by division (FY25A, indicative):**
- Telecom Distribution & Handsets: ~55–60% of consolidated revenue (largest legacy vertical, but lowest margin)[^ar-fy25]
- BlackBerry Branded Handsets: ~20–25% of revenue (high gross margin, growing)[^ar-fy25]
- Drones / Defence Electronics: ~5–8% of revenue (nascent, fastest growth)[^ip-latest]
- EMS / ODM / Other: ~10–15% of revenue (mixed margin profile)[^ip-latest]

**Key OEM/end customers per division:**
- Telecom Distribution: Long-standing distribution partnerships with leading smartphone OEMs (Xiaomi, OPPO, Samsung, Vivo) and accessories brands across India.[^ar-fy25]
- BlackBerry Handsets: Exclusive licensee for BlackBerry-branded Android smartphones in India, Sri Lanka, Nepal, and Bangladesh — designs, manufactures, distributes, and provides after-sales support.[^ip-latest] Retail channel tie-ups include Amazon India for online distribution.[^vp-thread]
- Drones / Defence: Direct engagement with Indian defence/public sector buyers for UAV systems and surveillance-grade hardware; partnership / trial-stage relationships.[^ip-latest]
- EMS / ODM: Contract manufacturing relationships with global brands (referenced in valuepickr commentary regarding Wistron-Taiwan linkage for smartphone contract manufacturing).[^vp-thread]

**Subsidiary structure:** The group operates through Optiemus Infracom Ltd (listed parent) and a constellation of step-down subsidiaries covering manufacturing, distribution, and product-specific JVs. The 04 Aug 2026 preferential allotment was undertaken through a Preferential Allotment Committee of the Board, indicating active capital management at the parent level.[^ip-latest]

**Manufacturing footprint:** Manufacturing operations are based primarily in the National Capital Region (Noida/Greater Noida) with additional capacity in southern India tied to mobile/EMS partnerships.[^ar-fy25] The company runs dedicated lines for BlackBerry handsets, smartphone contract manufacturing, and is expanding drone assembly/production lines.[^ip-latest]

**Promoter background and group context:** Founded and led by the Gupta family, with Mr. Ashok Kumar Gupta as Chairman and key managerial personnel driving the diversification pivot from a pure-play telecom distributor into a multi-vertical technology platform.[^ar-fy25] The promoter group holds 73.27% of equity, giving it absolute control over strategic direction but also concentrating execution risk.[^ar-fy25]

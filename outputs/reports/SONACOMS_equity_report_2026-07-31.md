## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Precision forging moat with global EV tailwind:** Sona Comstar is the largest Indian precision forged bevel gear manufacturer globally, with ~55% revenue from driveline (differential gears/assemblies) and ~45% from motors (BSG, EV traction motors), supplying to 9 of the top 10 global passenger EV OEMs[^ip-latest]. The company's precision forming process yields ~30% higher material utilisation versus traditional gear cutting, a structural cost advantage of ~200–250 bps in gross margin that competitors find difficult to replicate[^ar-fy25].
- **Structural margin story driven by EV mix shift:** EV program revenue is structurally higher-margin (32–34% gross) than legacy ICE differential business (28–30% gross). With EV order book at 57% of total Rs. 24,800 Cr order book and a 2–4 year OEM SOP lag, we model 180 bps EBITDA margin expansion from ~26% in FY26 to ~27.8% by FY28E[^ip-latest]. This is margin expansion without commodity dependence.
- **Diversification into magnetless motors and 2W/3W powertrains:** The Israel JV for magnetless rare-earth-free motors is the single largest non-linear option — management has explicitly called it a "binary outcome" with potential to disrupt the 2W/3W EV space globally[^cc-transcript]. Even at 10% success probability, this adds Rs. 40–60 to our DCF target. Additionally, motor control unit (MCU) and PMSM/BLDC platforms position Sona in the higher-value electronics layer of the EV stack[^ar-fy25].
- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q2FY27 ramp of the Rs. 1,200 Cr North American EV differential program (SOP in Aug–Sep 2026); (ii) potential new BEV platform nomination from a European OEM in Q3FY27; (iii) magnetless motor prototype validation milestone in Q4FY27; (iv) commissioning of the Mexico plant (Phase 2) in H2FY27, unlocking USMCA-content wins worth ~Rs. 800 Cr of addressable order book[^ip-latest][^vp-thread].
- **Biggest structural risk:** Customer concentration — top 5 customers contribute ~62% of revenue with one North American EV pure-play at ~22% — combined with rare-earth magnet price volatility (NdPr oxide up ~40% YoY). A single program loss or a 6-month SOP delay at the top customer can compress FY27E EPS by 12–15%[^cc-transcript][^vp-thread]. Additionally, the magnetless motor R&D (~3.5% of sales) is binary and could disappoint, derailing the "Tesla disruptor" narrative.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Sona Comstar designs, manufactures and supplies highly engineered mission-critical automotive components — primarily precision-forged differential gears/assemblies, starter motors, BSG (belt-driven starter generator) systems, EV traction motors (BLDC and PMSM), and motor control units (MCU). The business operates on long-cycle OEM programs with 2–4 year validation cycles, 7–10 year production runs, and high switching costs due to IP/design ownership at the OEM level[^ar-fy25].

**Revenue split by division (FY25A):**
- Driveline (differential gears & assemblies): ~55% of revenue
- Motors (BSG, EV traction motors, starter motors): ~42% of revenue
- Other / MCU / prototype: ~3% of revenue

**Key OEM customers by division:**
- Driveline: Stellantis, Ford, Tata Motors, Mahindra, Renault-Nissan, John Deere[^ip-latest]
- Motors: Maruti Suzuki (BSG), Tata Motors (EV), a leading North American EV pure-play (EV traction), European premium OEM (PMSM)[^cc-transcript]
- End-market exposure: Passenger vehicles ~78%, Commercial vehicles & tractors ~14%, Off-road/industrial ~8%[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary structure:** The group operates through Sona BLW Precision Forgings Ltd (India, listed parent) and wholly-owned subsidiaries including Sona Comstar eDrive (motor and electronics arm), the Israel R&D JV (magnetless motor IP), and step-down subsidiaries in China, Mexico, USA for localised manufacturing and customer proximity[^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing footprint:** 9 facilities globally — 6 in India (Gurugram, Manesar, Pune, Chennai, Bangalore — combined ~60% of capacity), 1 in China (Shanghai), 1 in Mexico (Aguascalientes, Phase 1 operational, Phase 2 commissioning H2FY27), and 1 in the USA (North Carolina assembly line)[^ip-latest]. The Mexico and US facilities are critical for USMCA content and North American EV OEM wins.

**Promoter background and group context:** The Sona Group (originally Sona Steering Systems) is a third-generation Indian auto-ancillary group founded by the Kapur family. Sona BLW was carved out via a 2018 Singapore-based Comstar Technologies acquisition, giving it global product technology. The promoter holding stands at 27.98% (post IPO dilution and pre-IPO holdings rollover), with Singapore-based Comstar sellers and the Kapur family as the principal shareholder groups[^ar-fy25]. The Singapore component is a legacy of the Comstar deal and is not considered a governance overhang as they are passive financial holders.

Within this, the EV traction motor sub-TAM is the fastest-growing at ~22% CAGR.

**Policy tailwinds:**

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter pedigree and tenure:** The Kapur family (Sona Group) has been in precision automotive manufacturing for over three decades. Sona Steering (the listed predecessor entity) was founded in 1986, and the current Sona BLW structure was formed in 2018 via the strategic acquisition of Comstar Technologies (Singapore) — a deal that gave Sona global motor technology and a Tier-1 customer base. Key management: Sunjay Kapur (Chairman) with 25+ years in the business, and a professional CEO/CFO layer post-listing. Management continuity is strong; no senior exits in the past 36 months[^ar-fy25].

**FCF deployment track record (last 3 years FY23–FY25A):**
- Cumulative FCF generation: ~Rs. 1,950 Cr
- Cumulative capex deployed: ~Rs. 1,450 Cr (primarily Mexico plant + capacity expansion)
- Cumulative dividends paid: ~Rs. 165 Cr (~25% payout in FY25)
- Net cash position as of FY25 end: ~Rs. 720 Cr (positive net cash, no net debt)[^ar-fy25]
- Capital allocation discipline: 70%+ of FCF reinvested into growth capex, balance returned to shareholders and parked in liquid funds.

**Debt management:**
- Net debt/EBITDA: Negative (net cash) as of FY25
- Interest coverage ratio: 35x+ (essentially debt-free for working capital)
- Credit ratings: CRISIL A1+ (short-term) / AA+/Stable (long-term) reaffirmed in Mar 2026 — top-tier for an Indian auto-ancillary[^ar-fy25]

**Dividend policy:** FY25 payout at ~25% of PAT, translating to ~Rs. 0.40/share dividend yield of ~0.05% on CMP. 5-year average payout: ~22%. Dividend policy is not aggressive — management has clearly prioritised growth capex (Mexico, R&D) over buybacks or high payouts, which is appropriate at the current growth stage[^cc-transcript].

**Promoter pledging status:** Promoter shares pledged: 0% — clean shareholding, no pledging overhang[^ar-fy25].

**Corporate governance flags:**
- Auditor: BSR & Co. (a member firm of KPMG India) — auditor for 5+ years, no changes; no qualifications in FY25 audit report[^ar-fy25]

- One flag to note: the Singapore-domiciled Comstar seller group holds a non-trivial stake (post-IPO residual); while passive, any future block sale could create short-term supply pressure

**JVs and strategic partnerships:**
- Israel-based magnetless motor JV: ~Rs. 180 Cr committed over 3 years for R&D; strategic rationale is to bypass Chinese rare-earth magnet supply chain and address 2W/3W EV cost sensitivity[^cc-transcript][^vp-thread]
- North American customer co-development: Embedded engineering teams with two EV OEMs for next-gen platform wins
- No material M&A in pipeline; focus is organic capacity build-out (Mexico Phase 2 in H2FY27)[^ip-latest]

**Capital allocation scorecard:** Management has demonstrated disciplined allocation — net cash balance sheet despite 30%+ revenue CAGR, no value-destructive acquisitions, clean promoter pledging, and consistent reinvestment in growth capex. Governance and capital allocation quality is above peer median and supports a premium multiple, contingent on continued R&D and execution delivery[^vp-thread].

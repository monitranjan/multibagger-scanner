### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1. **CRAMS-led turnaround with margin expansion:** Bafna Pharma has pivoted from a low-margin domestic API/trading model to a Contract Research & Manufacturing Services (CRAMS)-focused franchise, driving EBITDA margins from sub-8% historically into the 15–17% range and reflecting a structural ~800 bps re-rating in profitability profile [^ip-latest].

2. **Niche oncology and complex generics moat:** Focused capability in high-burden therapeutic segments (anti-cancer, anti-tuberculosis, CNS) where regulatory entry barriers, USFDA/EMA plant compliance and DMF filings create genuine moat — evidenced by multiple CEPs, WC certifications, and 40+ international dossiers filed [^ar-fy25].

3. **Export-led growth re-acceleration:** Africa, ASEAN, Latin America and semi-regulated markets are driving the next leg of growth, with exports now contributing ~40–45% of revenue and a stated medium-term target of 60%; the CDMO export business has grown at a 25%+ CAGR over FY23–FY25 [^ip-latest].

4. **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Commissioning of the new oral solid dosage (OSD) facility at Baroda, (ii) USFDA audit outcome for the new block, (iii) onboarding of 2–3 new CRAMS clients in the high-potency API (HPAPI) space, (iv) potential re-rating on entry to mid-cap institutional indices, and (v) further reduction in working capital intensity releasing cash flow [^cc-transcript].

5. **Biggest structural risk — regulatory and concentration:** Heavy dependence on a limited number of CRAMS customers (top-5 ~55–60% of CRAMS revenue), African forex/currency risk, and a single USFDA-inspected facility mean any Form 483/OAI on the new plant, or customer attrition, could result in 200–400 bps EBITDA compression and 15–20% earnings de-rating [^ar-fy25].

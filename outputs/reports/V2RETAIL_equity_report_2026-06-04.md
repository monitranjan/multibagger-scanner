### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

V2 Retail Limited is an Indian value retail company primarily engaged in the business of retailing a wide range of apparel, general merchandise, and home products. The company operates through a chain of large-format retail stores predominantly located in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities across India. Its core business model revolves around offering trendy and affordable fashion to aspirational middle-income families.

*   **Core Business Model:** The company sources products directly from manufacturers, often in bulk, to maintain cost efficiencies. It focuses on a high-volume, low-margin strategy, emphasizing quick inventory turnover. A significant portion of its sales comes from private labels, which allows for better control over design, quality, and margins.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   Apparel (Men's, Women's, Kids'): ~70-75%
    *   General Merchandise (Home & Living, Footwear, Accessories): ~20-25%
    *   Other (e.g., Beauty & Personal Care, Groceries in select stores): ~5%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:** V2 Retail primarily serves individual end-consumers. For its private labels, it works with a network of contract manufacturers across India. For branded merchandise, it partners with various national and regional apparel and FMCG brands.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** V2 Retail Limited operates directly with no major listed subsidiaries. All retail operations are under the parent entity.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** V2 Retail does not have its own manufacturing facilities. It sources finished goods from third-party manufacturers located across various states in India, primarily focusing on clusters with cost-effective production capabilities for apparel and general merchandise. The company's retail store footprint extends across states like Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Jharkhand, Odisha, Assam, and West Bengal, with a growing presence in other northern and eastern states. As of FY24, the company operates over 100 stores.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** V2 Retail Limited was promoted by Mr. Ram Chandra Agarwal, a seasoned entrepreneur with extensive experience in the Indian retail sector. He founded the "Vishal Retail" chain before divesting it and subsequently establishing V2 Retail. The group's context is rooted in identifying and serving the value-conscious consumer segment in non-metro markets, leveraging deep understanding of local tastes and purchasing power.

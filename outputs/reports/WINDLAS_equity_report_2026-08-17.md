### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Domestic Formulations CDMO Market Leadership with Regulatory-Compliant Asset Base:** Windlas is positioned among the top 5 Indian formulations CDMO players, serving marquee customers including Pfizer, Sanofi, Cadila, Emcure, Eris, and Intas, with manufacturing facilities (Dehradun and Baddi) already aligned to USFDA standards, providing a clear on-shoring option for global innovators as India share in global formulations outsourcing continues to expand.[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]

- **Structural Margin Expansion Levers from Injectables and Mix Improvement:** The newly commissioned injectables facility (commercial supplies commenced FY26) is expected to lift blended EBITDA margins by ~150–200 bps over FY26–FY28E as high-value sterile dosage forms command 200–400 bps better gross margins than oral solids, alongside operating leverage on incremental CDMO scale.[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript]

- **Diversification Beyond Pure CDMO into Trade Generics and Regulated Exports:** The Trade Generics vertical (~18–20% of FY26 revenue) and the nascent Exports business (currently ~2% of revenue, ex-US markets) provide a parallel growth engine; the company is targeting ~Rs. 100 Cr exports revenue run-rate by FY28E, supported by dossier filings underway in semi-regulated geographies.[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript]

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (i) Sustained >100 Cr quarterly run-rate in CDMO demonstrated in Q1 FY27, (ii) ramp-up of the injectables line with 2–3 new molecule additions per quarter, (iii) potential USFDA re-inspection opportunity if management revisits US filings, and (iv) operating leverage as employee costs (one-off spike in FY26) normalise and new facility depreciation annualises.[^cc-transcript][^vp-thread]

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Customer Concentration and Ex-US Strategy Volatility:** Top 5 customers contribute a disproportionate share of CDMO revenue, leaving the business exposed to order-book lumpiness; further, the explicit non-pursuit of US markets (since the FY22 USFDA inspection) caps the addressable opportunity at semi-regulated and emerging markets, limiting TAM expansion versus peers like Akums Drugs and Suven Pharma who are actively pursuing US filings.[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Windlas Biotech operates as a contract development and manufacturing organisation (CDMO) for solid oral dosage forms (tablets, capsules) and injectables, with a complementary Trade Generics business that markets its own branded formulations in the domestic Indian market.[^ip-latest]

**Revenue Split by Division (FY26):**
- CDMO (Domestic Formulations): ~78–80% of revenue — anchor business serving innovator and generic customers.[^ip-latest]
- Trade Generics: ~18–20% of revenue — branded domestic formulations including chronic, acute, and gastro-intestinal therapies.[^ip-latest]
- Exports (Non-US): ~2% of revenue — early-stage regulated and semi-regulated markets.[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript]

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- CDMO: Pfizer, Sanofi, Cadila (Zydus), Emcure, Eris Lifesciences, Intas, Mankind, and other large domestic pharmaceutical companies.[^ar-fy25]
- Trade Generics: Distribution through 1,500+ distributors and a field force of ~250 representatives across India.[^ip-latest]
- Exports: Customers in Africa, Southeast Asia, and Latin America (semi-regulated markets).[^cc-transcript]

**Subsidiary Structure:** Windlas operates as a standalone entity with no material subsidiaries of operating significance; the Group structure is principally the promoter Windlass family holding.[^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Plant 1: Dehradun, Uttarakhand — formulations manufacturing (oral solids, injectables).
- Plant 2: Baddi, Himachal Pradesh — formulations manufacturing (oral solids).
- Total manufacturing capacity spans oral solid dosages and injectables, with facilities audited to USFDA, EU-GMP, and WHO-GMP standards (audit-readiness maintained even though US strategy is currently de-prioritised).[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

**Promoter Background and Group Context:** Promoted by the Windlass family — Mr. Hitesh Windlass (Managing Director) and Mr. Manoj Kumar Windlass (Joint Managing Director) — the company traces its origin to 1999 and listed via IPO in August 2021 at Rs. 460/share. The promoter family retains 66.22% of equity as of the latest shareholding data, providing significant skin in the game.[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

**Total Addressable Market (TAM):**
- The Indian formulations CDMO market is estimated at ~Rs. 25,000–30,000 Cr (~USD 3.0–3.6 Bn) as of CY25, with a projected CAGR of 12–14% through FY28E driven by outsourcing penetration, China+1 sourcing strategies, and patent cliff opportunities.[^ip-latest]
- The global formulations CDMO market stands at ~USD 130–150 Bn, with India share at ~2–2.5% and rising as Indian players clear regulatory hurdles.[^ip-latest]

**Policy Tailwinds:**
- **Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for Pharmaceuticals:** Provides incentives for incremental sales of high-value formulations, including complex generics and sterile dosage forms, of which Windlas's injectables line is a direct beneficiary.[^ip-latest]
- **China+1 Strategy:** Global innovator companies are diversifying supply chains away from China; India's cost-competitive, English-speaking, regulatory-aligned manufacturing base positions Windlas favourably for incremental CDMO orders.[^vp-thread]
- **Bulk Drug Parks and API Self-Sufficiency:** Reduces input cost volatility for CDMO players manufacturing finished formulations.[^ip-latest]

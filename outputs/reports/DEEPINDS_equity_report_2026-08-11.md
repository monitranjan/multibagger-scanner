## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant market position in natural gas compression creates a defensible moat:** Deep Industries Ltd (DIL) is the largest Natural Gas Compression services provider in India[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25], with community-discussed estimates pegging market share at ~80% in high-pressure compression[^vp-thread]. The asset-heavy charter-hire model and incumbent fleet position make the franchise structurally hard to dislodge on price, supporting recurring rental income at high incremental ROIC.

- **Structural margin story driven by long-duration charter contracts and fleet utilisation:** Operating margins have historically sustained above the 50% mark on the compression segment[^vp-thread] given multi-year, take-or-pay style contracts with upstream PSUs. Sustained day-rate discipline, captive O&M capabilities, and high asset utilisation (>85%) provide EBITDA visibility through commodity cycles[^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacent verticals reduces upstream cyclicality:** Beyond core Air & Gas Compression, DIL operates Workover & Drilling rigs and an in-house Oil, Gas & Coal Bed Methane (CBM) Exploration & Production (E&P) vertical[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]. Recent pivots into CNG compression for City Gas Distribution (CGD) operators and gas dehydration (claimed ~90% market share in select slides per management commentary) materially expand the addressable opportunity[^vp-thread][^cc-transcript].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months) include policy push, contract wins, and ESG disclosure uplift:** PNGRB's CGD network expansion to 300+ districts, the Government's target to raise natural gas's share in the energy mix to 15% by 2030, and PSU capex on compression infrastructure are key tailwinds[^ip-latest]. Catalysts on the radar: (i) follow-on contract wins from ONGC/Oil India in workover rigs, (ii) commencement of new CGD compression deployments, (iii) recent BRSR filing (FY26) potentially broadening institutional investor universe, and (iv) AGM on 01-Sep-2026 likely to articulate FY27 capex and dividend policy[^ar-fy25].

- **Biggest structural risk is upstream capex cyclicality and customer concentration:** ~70%+ revenue concentration with ONGC and Oil India creates single-customer dependency; any deferral in their capex (as seen in FY15–FY17) directly impacts rig utilisation and compression charter renewals[^ar-fy25]. Oil price volatility, PSU budgetary cycles, and delayed CGD bidding rounds remain key swing factors.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Deep Industries Ltd (DIL), incorporated in 1991, is a pure-play oil & gas services company operating across three verticals — (1) Air & Gas Compression on charter-hire, (2) Workover & Drilling rig services, and (3) Exploration & Production (Oil, Gas, CBM)[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]. The company was the first in India to deploy high-pressure Air & Gas compressors on a charter-hire basis[^vp-thread].

**Revenue split by division (estimated, FY25–FY26):**

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| E&P (Oil/Gas/CBM) | ~10–15% | Production-linked revenue |
| CNG / Gas Dehydration / Others | ~5–10% | Emerging |

*Estimates derived from investor presentation disclosures and segment commentary; precise split subject to AR notes[^ar-fy25][^ip-latest].*

**Key customers by division:**
- **Compression:** ONGC, Oil India, GAIL, GSPC, Reliance Industries, Shell (Rajmahal), focus on upstream + CGD players[^ip-latest].
- **Drilling/Workover:** ONGC (dominant), Oil India, small/private operators[^ar-fy25].
- **E&P:** JV/PSC partners; CBM blocks in central India.

**Subsidiary/JV structure:**
- **Deep Industries Inc.** — USA step-down subsidiary for international rig/compression assets[^ar-fy25].
- **Deep ONGC** and other minor step-downs for project-specific bidding.
- Limited partnership exposure via CBM/JV vehicles.

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- Main compression fleet and workshop: **Gujarat (Gandhinagar/Ankleshwar)** — primary hub[^ip-latest].
- Drilling/workover rig depots: **Assam (Sivasagar/Nazira)**, **Rajasthan (Barmer)** — co-located with ONGC/Oil India fields[^ar-fy25].
- Additional regional service centres near customer operating areas.

**Promoter & group background:**

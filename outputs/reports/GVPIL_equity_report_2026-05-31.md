### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** GVPIL holds a strong position in India's thermal power generation equipment and services market, particularly for steam turbines and generators, leveraging GE's global technology prowess. Its extensive installed base across India provides a significant recurring revenue stream through long-term service agreements (LTAs), spare parts, and modernization projects, creating high customer switching costs due to the critical nature of equipment and specialized expertise required.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** The company is undergoing a strategic shift towards higher-margin service contracts and modernization projects, moving away from large, lumpy, and often lower-margin EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) new build projects. This pivot, coupled with operational efficiencies and cost rationalization initiatives, is expected to drive a structural improvement in EBITDA margins by 200-300 bps over the next 2-3 years, as the legacy project backlog winds down and the services portfolio expands.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** GVPIL is actively exploring and positioning itself in adjacent high-growth segments within the broader energy transition landscape. This includes opportunities in retrofitting existing thermal plants for flexibility (e.g., co-firing, carbon capture readiness), hydro power modernization, and potentially leveraging its engineering capabilities for emerging areas like green hydrogen component manufacturing, aligning with India's long-term energy goals.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):**
    *   **Order Inflow Revival:** Expectation of increased order inflows from both public (NTPC, state gencos) and private sector players for modernization, R&M (Renovation & Modernization), and service contracts for existing thermal assets, driven by higher power demand and the need for operational efficiency.
    *   **Execution of High-Margin Backlog:** Successful execution and revenue recognition from the current backlog, which increasingly comprises higher-margin service and R&M contracts, will demonstrate the margin expansion thesis.
    *   **Government Focus on Grid Stability:** Policy emphasis on grid stability and flexible power generation to complement intermittent renewables will drive demand for GVPIL's solutions in thermal asset optimization.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The most significant structural risk is the accelerated transition away from thermal power generation in India. While thermal power remains critical for baseload and grid stability, aggressive renewable energy targets and policy shifts could lead to a decline in new thermal capacity additions and potentially impact the long-term service market if older plants are prematurely decommissioned. Additionally, intense competition from Chinese and domestic players for new orders remains a constant threat.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** GE Power India Ltd (GVPIL) is a leading player in the Indian power generation equipment and services market. Its core business involves the design, engineering, manufacturing, installation, and servicing of power generation equipment, primarily for thermal power plants (coal and gas-fired). The company provides comprehensive solutions from individual components to complete power islands and offers a wide range of services including maintenance, upgrades, and digital solutions to enhance plant performance and extend asset life.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (% of Revenue - FY25E):**

    *   **Hydro & Other Renewables:** ~5% - Focus on hydro power equipment and services, with potential for expansion into other renewable energy components.
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**

    *   **Hydro & Other Renewables:** NHPC, SJVN, state hydro power corporations.

The current structure sees GVPIL as part of GE Vernova, which houses GE's power, renewable energy, and digital businesses.

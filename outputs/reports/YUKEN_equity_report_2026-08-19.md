### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat:** Yuken India Ltd operates as the Indian manufacturing arm of the globally established Yuken Group (Japan), granting it a durable technical moat in the production of hydraulic valves, power packs, and pumps [^ar-fy25]. A hydraulic valve is essentially a traffic controller for pressurized oil inside a machine, directing force to where it is needed; because these components are critical to the safe operation of industrial machinery and mobile equipment, customers face very high switching costs once a Yuken product is certified and embedded into their OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) designs [^ar-fy25]. This creates a sticky revenue base despite the company’s small absolute size.

- **Structural margin story:** The company’s EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization — a measure of core operating profitability before non-cash and financing items) margin contracted by 101 basis points (bps — where 1 bps = 0.01%) to 11.8% in FY26, primarily due to employee cost inflation (+5.3%) and depreciation (+24.1%) from new capex outpacing revenue growth [^ar-fy25]. However, the structural margin thesis rests on the commissioning of the new YE-3 facility and Malur plant upgrades, where higher capacity utilization (the percentage of a plant’s total potential output that is actually being produced) is expected to spread fixed costs over a larger revenue base, aiding margin recovery over the medium term [^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Yuken has actively diversified from pure industrial hydraulics into mobile hydraulics (used in construction equipment like excavators) and expanded its export footprint to 15 international markets, leveraging the global Yuken Group’s distribution network [^ar-fy25]. The launch of the YE-3 manufacturing facility specifically targets this mobile hydraulics adjacency, which is tied to infrastructure spending and localization trends under the “Make in India for the World” initiative [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** The recently announced final dividend record date (August 2026) and the 50th AGM provide near-term corporate governance visibility [^ar-fy25]. More importantly, the pending export product approvals in progress and the ramp-up of the YE-3 facility commissioned in FY26 are tangible operational catalysts that could re-rate the stock as utilization improves [^ar-fy25]. The company also demonstrated strong cash conversion (CFO of Rs. 41.29 Cr vs PAT of Rs. 9.57 Cr), indicating underlying business quality that may attract institutional attention as earnings stabilize [^ar-fy25].

- **Biggest structural risk:** The most significant structural risk is the pronounced cyclicality of industrial capex demand coupled with raw material volatility [^ar-fy25]. The FY26 result showed a sharp PAT decline of -41.6% YoY and deteriorating ROCE (Return on Capital Employed — a metric showing how efficiently a company uses all its capital, both borrowed and owned, to generate profits; think of it as the interest rate your business pays itself) from 7% to 6% [^ar-fy25]. If the global trade disruptions and cost pressures persist, the company’s modest net debt/equity of 0.21 and interest coverage of 2.5x could come under strain, limiting further capex-led diversification [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Yuken India’s core business model is the design, manufacture, and sale of hydraulic components that transmit power through pressurized fluid — essentially acting as the “muscles and nerves” of heavy machines [^ar-fy25]. The company splits its revenue across three core divisions: Power Packs (36.81% of revenue — these are self-contained units that combine a motor, pump, and reservoir to generate hydraulic power), Valves (35.88% — the directional controllers), and Pumps (27.31% — the devices that push the fluid) [^ar-fy25]. These divisions serve OEMs and end-customers in industrial machinery, machine tools, and increasingly mobile/construction equipment segments [^ar-fy25].

The subsidiary structure includes Coretec, from which the company made related party purchases of Rs. 56.62 Cr (14% of standalone revenue), all conducted at arm’s length per auditors [^ar-fy25]. Manufacturing footprint is concentrated in the state of Karnataka, with the main Malur plant and the newly commissioned YE-3 facility representing the company’s capacity backbone [^ar-fy25]. The promoter group context is critical: Yuken Kogyo (Japan) increased its stake to 58.04% (from 56.16%) via preferential allotment, embedding deep technical pedigree and global alignment into the Indian entity [^ar-fy25]. The company celebrated 50 years of operation, pivoting its narrative toward becoming a global manufacturing hub [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

Policy tailwinds from infra capex and localization favor domestic hydraulic players [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Technology Moat:** TDPS is the dominant domestic manufacturer of AC generators in the sub-52MW segment, with technology partnerships with global prime-mover OEMs (steam, hydro, gas, wind turbine manufacturers) enabling it to supply generators across virtually all industrial applications [^ip-latest]. The company operates three dedicated manufacturing bases in Bangalore, including a specialized large-generator facility (52MW–200MW) that positions it uniquely in the Indian capital-goods landscape [^ip-latest].

- **Structural Margin Story & Export Pivot:** Manufacturing EBITDA margins have structurally expanded to 15%+ levels, with exports and deemed exports contributing ~68% of total manufacturing revenues — a significant de-risking from domestic cyclicality [^ar-fy25]. The export-led mix insulates the P&L from domestic capex slowdowns and provides natural USD hedging, while the company's technology-tie-up model eliminates R&D burden typically borne by standalone generator manufacturers [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The recently announced Build-To-Print Manufacturing Framework Agreement with Siemens Energy Inc. (14 Aug 2026) marks a transformational adjacency move — providing a multi-year revenue annuity from a Tier-1 global OEM [^cc-transcript]. Combined with the dedicated large-generator (52MW–200MW) facility, TDPS is now positioned to capture data-center backup power, industrial captive power, and the upcoming nuclear/thermal turbine-generator (TG) island opportunities [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Execution ramp-up of the Siemens Energy framework agreement with initial order inflows visible from Q2 FY27 onwards [^cc-transcript]; (ii) Stock split (record date 12 Aug 2026) improving retail liquidity and index-eligibility [^cc-transcript]; (iii) Fund raise announcement (14 Aug 2026) likely to fund large-generator capacity expansion and capex for Siemens volumes [^cc-transcript]; (iv) Order book trajectory — manufacturing order book stood at Rs. 1,049 Cr as of FY18 with sustained inflows of Rs. 100+ Cr per quarter historically [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** The stock trades at an elevated 88.40x TTM P/E and 22.57x P/B, pricing in flawless execution of the Siemens partnership and a sharp earnings recovery from the depressed FY25A EPS base of Rs. 17.60 [^ar-fy25]. Any delay in Siemens order conversion, domestic capex slowdown, or working-capital deterioration could trigger a sharp derating — the 52W low of Rs. 493 (vs. high of Rs. 1,569.80) demonstrates the historical volatility of this cyclical-capital-goods franchise [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** TD Power Systems designs and manufactures AC generators (alternators) in the 1MW to 200MW output range, catering to all major prime movers — steam turbines, hydro turbines, diesel engines, wind turbines, gas engines, and gas turbines [^ip-latest]. The company operates an asset-light, technology-tie-up model where it leverages global OEM relationships for prime-mover designs while manufacturing the generator (the electrical heart) in-house [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division:**

| Division | % of Revenue (FY25A est.) | Key Activity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Manufacturing (Generators) | ~85–90% | Core AC generator production across 1MW–200MW range |
| Projects (TG Island) | ~8–10% | Turnkey turbine-generator island execution (being wound down) |
| EPC | <2% | Legacy EPC business — fully exited by FY16 [^vp-thread] |

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**

| Division | Key Customers |
| :--- | :--- |
| Manufacturing – Domestic | Power generation EPCs, industrial captive power users, renewable energy developers |
| Manufacturing – Exports | Global OEMs (steam/gas/hydro turbine manufacturers), deemed-export customers |
| Projects | Domestic power plants, industrial TG-island customers |

**Subsidiary Structure:** DF Power Systems (Japan) — historical subsidiary for Japan-market access; EPC and project subsidiaries have been largely wound down [^vp-thread].

**Manufacturing Footprint:** Three manufacturing bases in Bangalore (Karnataka), including one dedicated large-generator facility for the 52MW–200MW segment [^ip-latest]. The Bangalore cluster provides engineering talent proximity and supply-chain density.

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** TDPS is a closely-held, promoter-driven company with the founding Khericha family holding 27.48% stake [^ar-fy25]. The company has historically operated with a conservative capital structure and a strong export orientation, distinguishing it from larger PSU-dominated peers in the Indian capital-goods space [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Integrated sugar-ethanol-cogeneration model with structural margin defensibility:** Uttam Sugar operates a fully integrated value chain spanning sugar manufacturing, distillery (molasses and grain/syrup-based ethanol), and bagasse-based cogeneration, which meaningfully derisks the inherently cyclical sugar P&L.[^ar-fy25] Cogeneration surplus is exported to the state grid under a long-tenure UP state utility PPA, providing a non-cyclical EBITDA layer that smooths sugar cycle volatility.[^ip-latest]

- **Ethanol-led structural margin re-rating (the primary re-rating lever):** Distillery capacity is being expanded from 150 KLPD to 250 KLPD at the Barkartpur plant with Rs. 96 Cr capex, alongside a ~2,000 TCD cane crushing uplift, signalling management's pivot toward the higher-margin ethanol value chain.[^vp-thread] With the Government's E20 ethanol blending programme (and pilot ED7 ethanol-diesel blending work by BPCL + Ashok Leyland) gaining traction, the addressable offtake for ethanol is structurally rising.[^vp-thread]

- **Capacity compounding track record creates operating leverage:** The company has compounded crushing capacity from 2,000 TCD in 2001 to 20,000 TCD in 2011, a ten-fold scale-up, and continues to layer incremental capacity into both sugar and distillery — supporting volume-led revenue CAGR going forward.[^vp-thread] Plant-level modernisation investments (steam optimisation technology capable of reducing steam consumption by up to 20%) further lift operating efficiency.[^vp-thread]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results on 10 August 2026 — first read on FY27 ethanol realisations and cane crush trajectory,[^cc-transcript] (ii) commissioning of the expanded 250 KLPD distillery and incremental crushing capacity (timelined for completion by Dec 2023 vintage, with FY27 ramp),[^vp-thread] (iii) annual ethanol price revision by Government of India (effective Nov cycle historically),[^vp-thread] (iv) potential sugar export quota window if domestic surplus persists.[^ip-latest]

- **Biggest structural risk — policy + cane cycle dual exposure:** Uttar Pradesh cane FRP (Fair and Remunerative Price) is reset by the central government, and any sharp upward revision directly compresses sugar mill margins, as sugar prices are admin / market linked while cane cost is politically administered.[^ip-latest] Compounding this, the FY24 ethanol pricing regime saw a clampdown on FCI rice supply for grain-based ethanol, creating feedstock visibility risk for grain/syrup-route distilleries.[^vp-thread] Cyclical inventory write-downs on closing sugar stock remain an additional P&L volatility vector.[^ar-fy25]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Uttam Sugar Mills Limited (USML) is an integrated sugar complex operating across three interlinked value streams — (1) white/refined sugar from sugarcane crushing, (2) ethanol via molasses-based and grain/syrup-based distilleries, and (3) bagasse-based cogeneration power exported to the Uttar Pradesh state grid.[^ip-latest] The integrated structure allows by-product valorisation (molasses → ethanol; bagasse → power; press mud → compost), which is the primary source of structural margin resilience versus standalone sugar millers.[^ar-fy25]

**Revenue split by division (approx., FY25 / management commentary):**
- **Sugar:** ~70–75% of revenue — core volume driver, cyclical realisation-linked.[^ip-latest]
- **Distillery / Ethanol:** ~15–20% of revenue — higher contribution margin, steadily rising as 250 KLPD capacity commissions.[^ip-latest]
- **Cogeneration Power:** ~5–8% of revenue — contracted PPA tariff with UP state utility, stable EBITDA contributor.[^ar-fy25]
- *(Minor: Compost / by-product sales rounding out the balance.)*[^ip-latest]

**Key end-customers per division:**
- **Sugar:** Institutional buyers, wholesalers, and packaged sugar bulk consumers; some branded white sugar exposure.[^ip-latest]
- **Ethanol:** Oil Marketing Companies (OMCs) — IOC, BPCL, HPCL — under the government's Ethanol Blending Programme (EBP) long-term offtake framework.[^vp-thread]
- **Cogeneration:** Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Ltd (UPPCL) / state DISCOM under long-tenure PPA.[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary / group structure:** Uttam Sugar operates primarily on a standalone basis with project-specific SPVs/divisions across its Barkartpur (UP) plant cluster.[^ar-fy25] The promoter family (Rajshree Sugars & Chemicals group lineage; Rajshree/Dewan family stewardship) holds 80.08% — one of the highest promoter holdings in the listed sugar mid-cap universe, which materially reduces float-related governance risk.[^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing footprint:** Operations are concentrated in Uttar Pradesh — the country's largest sugarcane producing state — with the flagship Barkartpur plant housing both the 250 KLPD distillery and cogeneration assets, alongside crushing units in UP's cane belt.[^ip-latest] The UP-centric, single-state cluster model delivers cane-procurement proximity advantages but also concentrates state-policy risk.

**Promoter background & group context:** The promoter group traces its lineage to the erstwhile Rajshree / Dewan sugar family in North India, with multi-decade operating history in UP sugar crushing. Promoter holding at 80.08% reflects a closely-held, operator-led ownership philosophy.[^ar-fy25] Recent capital actions (redemption of preference shares disclosed on 10 July 2026) signal active balance-sheet cleanup by the promoter group.[^cc-transcript]

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Post-Demerger Asset-Rich Valuation Disconnect:** Following the demerger of its high-margin retail brand 'Red Tape' into Redtape Limited, Mirza International (MIRZAINT) was left with the capital-intensive tannery, shoe manufacturing, and export business. The stock trades at a deep discount of 0.68x P/B (TTM) against historical averages of >2.0x. This valuation severely discounts the company’s massive replacement-cost asset base, which includes fully integrated tanneries and manufacturing facilities with an aggregate capacity of over 6.75 million pairs of shoes annually.
*   **Captive Contract Manufacturing Opportunity with Red Tape:** Although Red Tape has been demerged into a separate listed entity, MIRZAINT remains a primary manufacturing partner for Redtape Limited. This strategic relationship provides a stable domestic volume baseline, ensuring minimum capacity utilization levels. We expect domestic contract manufacturing volumes to grow at a 12% CAGR over FY25-FY28E, cushioning the business against export market volatility.
*   **Operating Leverage via Export Recovery:** The company's export business, which primarily caters to mid-to-premium brands in the UK and European Union, suffered from severe destocking and inflationary headwinds over the last 18 months. With inventory levels normalizing among European high-street retailers, we project export volumes to rebound. Every 10% increase in capacity utilization is estimated to expand EBITDA margins by approximately 120-150 bps due to high operating leverage inherent in its integrated tannery setup.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** Key near-term triggers include: (a) the stabilization of wet blue leather and raw hide prices, which will improve gross margins; (b) the signing of new white-label manufacturing contracts with emerging D2C footwear brands in India; and (c) potential monetization of non-core surplus land parcels in Uttar Pradesh, which could be utilized to clear residual working capital debt.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is customer and geographic concentration. More than 60% of export revenues are concentrated within the UK and European Union, leaving the company highly vulnerable to regulatory changes, import duties, and economic downturns in these regions. Additionally, stringent environmental norms governing tannery operations in the Ganga basin (Kanpur/Unnao cluster) pose ongoing compliance and capex risks.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Mirza International Limited operates an integrated leather processing and footwear manufacturing business. The company procures raw hides, processes them into finished leather at its in-house tanneries, and manufactures finished leather footwear for both export and domestic markets. The business model is primarily B2B, serving as an OEM/ODM partner for global brands and domestic retail players.
*   **Revenue Split by Division:** 
    *   *Footwear Division:* ~78% of total revenue (comprising export OEM sales and domestic contract manufacturing).
    *   *Tannery & Leather Division:* ~22% of total revenue (comprising sales of finished leather to third-party manufacturers and internal consumption transfers).
*   **Key OEM/End Customers:** 
    *   *Domestic:* Redtape Limited (primary buyer of finished leather footwear under long-term supply agreements).
    *   *International:* Major high-street retailers and fashion brands across the UK and Europe, including ASOS, Next, Dune, and various private labels.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** The company operates primarily through its key international marketing subsidiary, **Mirza (UK) Private Limited**, which handles warehousing, distribution, and customer relationship management for the UK and European markets, ensuring direct access to major retail buyers.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** MIRZAINT operates state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and tanneries located across Uttar Pradesh (primarily in the Kanpur and Unnao industrial clusters). The company has an aggregate installed capacity of over 6.75 million pairs of footwear per annum, supported by fully integrated leather processing units that ensure strict quality control from raw hide to finished shoe.
*   **Promoter Background & Group Context:** Founded in 1979 by the late Mr. Irshad Mirza, the Mirza Group is one of India's pioneer leather export houses. The promoters have over four decades of experience in leather tanning and footwear design. Following the restructuring, the promoter group maintains a strong 73.47% stake in MIRZAINT, aligning their interests closely with minority shareholders as they work to rebuild the profitability of the standalone manufacturing business.

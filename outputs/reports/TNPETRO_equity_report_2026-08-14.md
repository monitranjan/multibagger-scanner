### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant domestic LAB franchise with structural moat:** Tamilnadu Petroproducts (TPL) operates India's largest single-location Linear Alkyl Benzene (LAB) plant at Manali, Chennai, with installed capacity of ~1,20,000 MTPA — roughly 50%+ of India's total LAB capacity.[^ar-fy25] LAB is the principal raw material for LABSA, used in biodegradable detergents, a category with stable mid-single-digit volume growth driven by rural/semi-urban penetration and the gradual shift from soap-based to powder/liquid detergents.[^ip-latest]

- **Margin expansion via chlor-alkali integration:** TPL's chlor-alkali division (caustic soda, chlorine, hydrogen) was historically a drag during the FY22-FY24 downturn, but caustic realizations have rebounded sharply in FY25-26 with global tightness (China capacity rationalization, higher coal-based power costs in Europe).[^cc-transcript] With caustic spot prices running Rs. 4,500-5,000/MT above FY24 averages, this segment is now a margin tailwind rather than a stabilizer.[^vp-thread]

- **Diversification into specialty chemicals:** TPL is increasingly focusing on downstream specialty derivatives — epichlorohydrin, hydrogen peroxide (through associates and proposed capex), and refrigerants — to reduce LAB concentration from ~75% to <60% of revenue by FY28E.[^ip-latest] The recently announced cost-auditor appointment (Aug 2026) and the record-date announcement for FY26 dividend suggest management is in execution mode on FY27 capex.[^ann-div]

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (a) Caustic soda price tailwind sustaining into Q2/Q3 FY27; (b) Q1 FY27 results (typically July-end) likely to show YoY EBITDA expansion of 400-500 bps; (c) Potential special dividend beyond the regular Rs. 1.5/share payout, given cash on balance sheet exceeds Rs. 250 Cr; (d) Possible re-rating once TPL's ROCE crosses the cost of capital on a sustained basis — currently the single biggest overhang.[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk:** Single-site concentration — virtually all of TPL's operating assets are at the Manali complex, exposing it to cyclone/flooding risk (Cyclone Vardah in 2016 caused a multi-week shutdown), regulatory action, and logistics bottlenecks.[^ar-fy25] A second greenfield site or strategic acquisition outside Tamil Nadu would meaningfully de-risk this; until then, the stock carries a structural discount.

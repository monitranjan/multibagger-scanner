### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Specialty Carbon Black:** HSCL is India's largest producer of coal tar pitch (CTP) and a key player in specialty carbon black (SCB), with a significant market share in both. Its integrated value chain, from crude oil distillation to advanced carbon materials, provides a cost advantage and quality control. The company's focus on SCB, a higher-margin product used in niche applications like coatings, plastics, and high-performance tires, offers a strong competitive moat due to stringent customer qualification processes and technical expertise required.
*   **Structural Margin Story Driven by Product Mix Shift:** HSCL is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-value, higher-margin specialty products, particularly SCB and advanced carbon materials for Li-ion battery applications. This pivot from commodity-grade carbon black is expected to drive a structural improvement in EBITDA margins by 200-300 bps over the next 3-5 years, as SCB commands significantly better realizations and profitability compared to conventional carbon black.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjuncts – Li-ion Battery Materials:** The company is making significant strides in diversifying into the rapidly growing Li-ion battery materials segment, specifically anode materials (synthetic graphite). With planned capacity expansions and R&D investments in this area, HSCL is positioning itself to capitalize on India's EV and energy storage boom. This adjacency provides a substantial long-term growth runway and de-risks the business from cyclicality in traditional carbon black markets.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts:**
    *   **Capacity Expansion for Specialty Carbon Black:** Commissioning of additional SCB capacity in FY25-FY26 will directly contribute to revenue growth and margin expansion.
    *   **Ramp-up in Li-ion Anode Material Production:** Initial commercialization and increasing off-take agreements for Li-ion battery materials are expected to provide positive news flow and revenue contribution within the next 12-18 months.
    *   **Improved Demand from Tyre Sector:** A cyclical recovery in the domestic and export tyre industry, a key end-user for carbon black, could lead to better realizations and volumes for HSCL's conventional carbon black segment.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is the successful and timely scale-up of the Li-ion battery materials business. Delays in technology development, customer qualification, or obtaining necessary regulatory approvals, along with intense global competition from established players, could impede HSCL's ability to capture this high-growth opportunity and dilute its strategic pivot.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Himadri Speciality Chemical Limited (HSCL) is a global specialty chemical conglomerate primarily engaged in the manufacturing of advanced carbon materials. Its integrated business model involves the distillation of coal tar to produce a range of products, including coal tar pitch, specialty carbon black, advanced carbon materials, and other downstream derivatives.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A, indicative):**
    *   Specialty Carbon Black & Advanced Carbon Materials: ~35-40%

    *   Others (e.g., Construction Chemicals): ~5-10%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Specialty Carbon Black:** Used in various industries including coatings (paints, inks), plastics, engineering polymers, performance tires, and wire & cable. Customers include leading global and domestic players in these segments.
    *   **Coal Tar Pitch (CTP):** Primarily supplied to aluminium smelters for anode manufacturing (both pre-baked and Soderberg), graphite electrode manufacturers, and refractory industries. Key customers are large aluminium producers and electrode manufacturers.
    *   **Advanced Carbon Materials (Li-ion Battery):** Targeting EV battery manufacturers, energy storage solution providers, and consumer electronics companies globally.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** HSCL operates through various subsidiaries, including Himadri Advanced Materials Private Limited, which is focused on the development and manufacturing of advanced materials for Li-ion batteries.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** HSCL has a robust manufacturing footprint across India, with plants primarily located in:
    *   West Bengal (e.g., Haldia): Multiple units for coal tar distillation, carbon black, and other derivatives.
    *   Andhra Pradesh (e.g., Visakhapatnam): Facility for coal tar pitch.
    *   Odisha (e.g., Cuttack): Manufacturing unit for specialty chemicals.
    *   The company has a total of 10 manufacturing units across these states.

The Choudhary family has a long-standing history in the chemicals and manufacturing sector. HSCL started as a coal tar distiller and has progressively moved up the value chain, focusing on specialty chemicals and advanced carbon materials.

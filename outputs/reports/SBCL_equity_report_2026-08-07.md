### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche global leadership in EV shunt resistors:** SBCL is among the top 2–3 global manufacturers of ultra-low ohmic electron-beam-welded (EBM) shunt resistors used in battery management systems (BMS), competing directly with Vishay Intertechnology[^vp-thread]. The limited competitive set (2–3 players globally per Vishay's competitive landscape sheet) combined with 18–36 month automotive qualification cycles creates a defensible technology and switching-cost moat[^vp-thread]. SBCL's weld-precision capability and decade-long incumbency with Tier-1 BMS integrators reinforce its leadership position[^vp-thread].

- **Structural margin expansion story:** The shunt resistor segment is materially higher-margin than legacy bimetal products, and has driven consolidated EBITDA margin expansion from sub-15% in FY18 to a current ~26% trajectory[^vp-thread][^cc-transcript]. ROCE at 12.78% reflects the ongoing capacity build-out phase and we model meaningful expansion to 18–20% over FY26–FY28E as Solan Plant-3 capex completes and operating leverage materialises[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript]. Mix-led margin accretion of 150–200 bps is plausible over the forecast horizon on rising shunt-resistor share of revenue[^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond EV BMS, SBCL is gaining wallet share in smart energy meters (mandated under India's RDSS scheme targeting ~250 Mn installations), industrial automation current-sensing, and renewable energy storage applications[^vp-thread]. The legacy bimetal business (thermal protection for motors, refrigerators) provides stable cash flow but limited growth, with the company now seeding adjacencies via the wholly owned Shivalik Engineered Products Pvt Ltd subsidiary[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]. Geographic diversification into EU and US Tier-1 automotive customers provides further growth optionality[^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results (due Aug 2026) likely to show 35–40% YoY revenue growth on shunt resistor volume ramp[^cc-transcript]; (ii) commissioning of expanded Solan Plant-3 capacity in H2 FY27 to address the current demand-supply gap[^ip-latest]; (iii) new customer wins in EV two-wheeler and three-wheeler BMS segments where India penetration is accelerating[^cc-transcript]; (iv) potential classification of precision shunt resistors under the Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS), which offers 15–20% capex support[^vp-thread]; (v) resolution of CFO succession following the 6 Aug 2026 resignation announcement[^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk — customer concentration and product substitution:** Vishay Intertechnology remains the anchor customer for the shunt resistor segment, exposing SBCL to material client concentration risk and potential re-pricing pressure[^vp-thread]. Additionally, risk of substitution by Hall-effect ICs or other solid-state current-sensing technologies in next-generation BMS architectures remains a medium-term overhang that must be monitored through customer design-win disclosures[^vp-thread][^cc-transcript]. Promoter holding at 39.61% is relatively low for a family-run enterprise and the 6 Aug 2026 CFO resignation is a near-term governance flag requiring disclosure of succession plans[^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**
- SBCL operates two distinct manufacturing verticals: (i) Bimetals — thermal-expansion alloy strips used in thermal protection devices, motor starters, and circuit breakers; and (ii) Shunt Resistors — electron-beam-welded precision resistors used for current sensing in EV BMS, smart meters, industrial automation, and renewable energy systems[^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25 estimates)**
- Shunt Resistors: ~60% of revenue (primary growth driver; ~50%+ YoY growth)[^cc-transcript]
- Bimetal Products: ~35% of revenue (legacy, low-single-digit growth)[^ar-fy25]
- Tooling, Job-work & Others: ~5% of revenue[^ar-fy25]

**Key OEM / End Customers by Division**
- **Shunt Resistors:** Vishay Intertechnology (global distribution anchor); Tier-1 automotive BMS integrators in USA and EU; Indian smart meter OEMs under RDSS mandate; EV two-wheeler OEMs[^vp-thread][^cc-transcript].
- **Bimetal Products:** Domestic motor manufacturers (Havells, Crompton Greaves); refrigerator OEMs (LG, Whirlpool); industrial switchgear manufacturers[^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary Structure**
- **Shivalik Engineered Products Pvt Ltd** — wholly owned subsidiary, recent strategic vehicle for engineered components adjacency and forward integration[^ip-latest].
- **Shivalik Bimetal Engineers Pvt Ltd** — wholly owned subsidiary supporting specialised manufacturing operations[^ip-latest].
- Both subsidiaries underwent statutory auditor change on 7 Aug 2026, with M/s Walker Chandiok & Co LLP replacing the previous auditor, subject to shareholder ratification[^ip-latest].

**Manufacturing Footprint**
- **Solan, Himachal Pradesh** — primary and consolidated manufacturing hub with three plants (Plant 1, Plant 2 operational; Plant 3 under commissioning/expansion)[^ip-latest].
- Strategic HP location benefits from state industrial incentives and proximity to North Indian OEM clusters[^cc-transcript].
- Plant-3 expansion is expected to add ~40–50% incremental capacity once fully ramped, addressing the current order-book demand-supply gap[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript].

**Promoter Background and Group Context**
- Founding promoter groups are the **Ghumman and Sandhu families**, with multi-decade operating history in the bimetal and electrical contact industry[^vp-thread].
- **Mr. NS Ghumman, Managing Director**, is widely regarded as a pioneer of India's bimetal industry and provides deep technical continuity[^vp-thread].
- Shareholding mix: Promoter 39.61%; FII 9.16%; DII 6.11%; Public 45.12% — promoter holding has moderated from historical 50%+ levels, indicating partial stake dilution over time[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Differentiated manufacturing franchise in a niche HVAC sub-assembly.** KRN is among the very few Indian pure-play manufacturers of fin-and-tube heat exchangers and coils for HVAC&R, with 100,000+ sq ft of manufacturing space and over 1 billion units of annual capacity, supplying Tier-1 global OEMs including Daikin and Schneider Electric. [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread] Established in 2017, the company scaled from a single facility to a multi-product, multi-customer platform within seven years — a velocity rarely seen in Indian engineering mid-caps.

- **Structural margin story driven by value-added complex exchangers and export mix.** Q2 FY25 consolidated EBITDA margin stood at 21.7% (up ~150 bps YoY), and management indicates that exports carry ~2.0x the margin profile of domestic volumes. With exports currently ~15% of revenues and management guided to double this share over the medium term, blended EBITDA margin has a credible runway to expand a further 150–250 bps. [^vp-thread] The complex/exotic product mix (copper-to-copper, copper-to-aluminium, air condensers for bus ACs) anchors pricing power versus commoditised players.

- **Diversification into higher-growth adjacencies is materially de-risking the model.** KRN has set up a subsidiary for the Neemrana greenfield plant — representing a 6x capacity expansion — and is commercialising plate-and-bar exchangers, oil coolers and air condensers, broadening the wallet share with existing customers. [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread] End-market diversification across data centres, metros, railways, cold-chain logistics and bus AC applications insulates the company from any single HVAC segment downturn.

- **Near-term catalysts (next 6–12 months):** (i) Neemrana plant commissioning and ramp — IPO proceeds of Rs. 236 Cr allocated, Rs. 72 Cr utilised, balance Rs. 164 Cr parked in FDs earning 7–8.5% [^vp-thread]; (ii) export order wins from US/Europe under China+1 sourcing shifts; (iii) potential commercial milestones on plate heat exchangers; (iv) active investor engagement (Dubai, US roadshows) suggests further capital-market activity or strategic partnerships. [^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk — valuation premium and customer concentration.** At 119x TTM P/E and 15.94x P/B, KRN trades at one of the steepest multiples in Indian mid-cap engineering; any 200–300 bps slip in EBITDA margin or 500 bps shortfall in revenue growth could compress the multiple sharply. Customer concentration is meaningful — Daikin is a marquee relationship and any wallet-share loss would be material. Additionally, the company has a single manufacturing geography today (Neemrana commissioning is the mitigant) and is exposed to copper/aluminium commodity cycles, though largely passed through. [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model.** KRN Heat Exchanger and Refrigeration Limited, incorporated in 2017, designs and manufactures fin-and-tube heat exchangers and coils used as core sub-assemblies in HVAC&R systems — air conditioners, chillers, cold rooms, refrigerated trucks, bus ACs, metro coaches and data-centre cooling. [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread] The company operates a B2B contract-manufacturing model, with products specified in by the OEM's design and KRN delivering against approved vendor lists.

**Product portfolio and revenue mix.** KRN addresses five heat-exchanger architectures — shell & tube, spiral, plate, special and fin-and-tube — but is concentrated (~85–90% of revenue) in fin-and-tube exchangers, where it does both copper-to-copper and copper-to-aluminium variants. [^vp-thread] Management indicates ~97% of revenues are derived from commercial refrigeration/HVAC applications and only ~3% from RAC (room AC) — this is a deliberate focus, since commercial refrigeration is growing at a 20–25% CAGR in India, materially faster than the RAC segment. [^vp-thread]

**Customer base (key end-customers).**
- Daikin — global Tier-1 AC OEM; marquee relationship with reportedly sole/dual-source status on several SKUs. [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]
- Schneider Electric — data-centre and electrical infrastructure adjacency. [^vp-thread]
- Other domestic and export HVAC OEMs, cold-chain and bus-AC customers (full disclosure limited in current filings).

**Subsidiary structure.** The company has set up a subsidiary to anchor the Neomrana (Rajasthan) greenfield manufacturing facility, with IPO proceeds of Rs. 236 Cr earmarked for capacity build-out and Rs. 72 Cr deployed to date. [^vp-thread] A second subsidiary/entity has been incorporated for newer product lines including plate-and-bar exchangers and oil coolers.

**Manufacturing footprint.**
- Existing integrated facility: 100,000+ sq ft, annual capacity of over 1 billion units. [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]
- Neemrana plant (Rajasthan): under construction, targeted for a ~6x capacity multiplier versus the current base; commissioning milestone is a critical FY26–27 catalyst. [^vp-thread]
- Plant count: 2 (1 operational, 1 under construction) as of report date.

**Promoter background and group context.** Founder-promoter Santosh Kumar Yadav leads the company and has been the public face of the equity story through multiple investor interactions. [^vp-thread] The promoter group holds 68.49% of equity, indicating tightly-held controlling stakes with skin in the game. [^ip-latest] No listed parent or group entity; KRN is a standalone, founder-led enterprise.

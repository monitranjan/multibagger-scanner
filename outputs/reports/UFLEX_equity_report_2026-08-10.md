### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Deep cyclical-value setup with asset-backed downside support:** Uflex trades at 7.59x P/E (TTM) and 0.30x P/B (TTM) — a steep discount to flexible packaging peers Polyplex (P/B ~1.2x), Cosmo Films (P/B ~1.5x) and Jindal Poly Films (P/B ~0.9x) [^vp-thread]. Book value per share of Rs. 1,124.81 vs. CMP of Rs. 492.45 implies the market is pricing the equity at less than half of net asset value, providing hard asset-backed downside support [^ip-latest].

- **Margin recovery story from trough EBITDA margins:** Consolidated EBITDA margins compressed to ~9–10% in FY24–FY25 on raw material (BOPET/PP resin) volatility and elevated finance costs (Rs. 43.3 Cr in a single quarter per concall commentary) [^cc-transcript]. With crude derivatives stabilising and capex cycle peaking, we model 150–200 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY26E–FY27E, driving EPS from Rs. 43.91 (FY25A) to ~Rs. 59 by FY27E [^ar-fy25].

- **Vertically integrated BOPET-to-converting play with six operating divisions:** Uflex is the only Indian player with end-to-end integration across Film, Engineering (machinery for in-house use), Holography, Converting (laminates/pouches), Cylinders (gravure) and Chemicals (inks/adhesives) [^ip-latest]. This insulates gross margins from third-party supply shocks and creates cross-selling optionality — a structural moat that pure-play BOPET peers like Polyplex lack [^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results due 14 Aug 2026 — first read on margin trajectory post-stabilisation; (ii) Credit rating re-affirmation announced 07 Aug 2026 — likely to support debt cost reduction; (iii) ESG rating disclosure (07 Aug 2026) opens door to ESG-mandate institutional flows; (iv) Capacity utilisation ramp-up at new Mexico and Egypt subsidiaries post capex peak [^cc-transcript] [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk — promoter governance overhang:** CMD Ashok Chaturvedi carries a 2010 CBI court conviction in the Noida land scam case (4-year sentence) and the company faced 2014 IT raids uncovering Rs. 100 Cr of alleged bogus sale documents [^vp-thread]. With promoter holding at 82.06% and FII/DII ownership at a combined 1.37%, institutional appetite remains structurally capped until governance remediation is visible [^ar-fy25].

The company operates a fully integrated value chain from polymer chips to finished printed packs.

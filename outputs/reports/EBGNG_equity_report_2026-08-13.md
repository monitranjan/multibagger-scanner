### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership with structural moat** — GNG is the largest refurbisher of laptops and desktops in India and among the largest globally, with a market-leading position validated by 1Lattice [^ip-latest]. The company is India's largest Microsoft Authorised Refurbisher in terms of refurbishing capability as of FY25, providing direct access to genuine Microsoft-licensed software, which is a hard-to-replicate regulatory and brand asset [^ip-latest]. Full value-chain integration (sourcing → refurbishment → sales → after-sales/warranty) under the "Electronics Bazaar" brand creates meaningful switching costs for institutional and B2B customers.

- **Structural margin story driven by mix and scale** — Refurbishment economics deliver materially higher gross margins (typically 200–400 bps above new-device distribution) and the company has demonstrated a structurally expanding margin profile post-IPO, with FY26 reported as a beat versus guidance [^vp-thread]. Operating leverage from underutilised capacity, growing contribution of higher-margin geographies (US, Europe), and warranty-backed premium pricing for the "as-good-as-new" product position support a 150–200 bps EBITDA margin expansion trajectory over FY25–FY28E.

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies** — Geographic expansion into the US, Europe, Africa and UAE provides multi-currency revenue and reduces single-market concentration risk [^ip-latest]. The Europe circular-economy / Right-to-Repair regulatory wave is a multi-year tailwind, currently in early stages in France and Ireland and likely to expand across the EU [^vp-thread]. Adjacency into enterprise asset disposition (corporate IT refresh cycles) and educational/government procurement represents a Rs. 15,000+ Cr addressable layer on top of the core refurbishment TAM.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months)** — (i) Q1 FY27 print (already filed, 30-Jul-2026) and the H1 FY27 trajectory will be the first read on post-IPO run-rate sustainability [^cc-transcript]; (ii) ramp-up of the Texas subsidiary (Kay Kay Overseas Corporation USA — 100% owned via Electronics Bazaar FZC) is expected to drive meaningful US revenue contribution from Q2 FY27 onwards [^vp-thread]; (iii) potential new customer wins in the EU ESG-driven refurbishment procurement pipeline; (iv) favourable semiconductor shortage dynamics tightening supply of new laptops and pushing institutional buyers toward refurbished alternatives [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk** — Supply-side concentration and pricing volatility: GNG's refurbishment pipeline depends on enterprise-grade lease-return / IT-refresh inventory from corporates, lessors and OEMs; any disruption to this upstream supply (e.g., OEMs extending device life-cycles, lower corporate refresh volumes on macro slowdown, or competing buyers in the US/EU) would directly compress gross margins and volumes [^ar-fy25]. Secondary risks include (a) regulatory tightening on cross-border e-waste imports, (b) FX exposure (USD/EUR revenue ~50%+ of mix), and (c) execution risk on the US subsidiary build-out.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** GNG Electronics Ltd operates a fully-integrated, asset-light refurbishment model under the proprietary brand "Electronics Bazaar." The company procures end-of-lease, end-of-life and trade-in laptops/desktops/ICT devices from corporates, lessors, OEMs and brokers; refurbishes them through certified facilities (hardware repair, component replacement, data sanitisation, OS re-imaging); and sells them via a multi-channel B2B and B2C distribution network with a standard warranty [^ip-latest]. Unlike competitors who operate as single-link players, GNG is present across the entire value chain — sourcing, refurbishment, sales, and after-sales service [^ip-latest].

**Revenue split by division / vertical (FY25 indicative):**
- **Refurbished Laptops** — Core segment, ~70–75% of revenue
- **Refurbished Desktops & Workstations** — ~10–12% of revenue
- **Spare Parts & Components** — ~5–7% of revenue
- **After-Sales / Warranty Services** — ~3–5% of revenue
- **Other ICT Devices (monitors, peripherals)** — ~3–5% of revenue
*Indicative splits based on Investor Presentation disclosures; precise segmental split to be detailed in FY25 Annual Report [^ip-latest].*

**Key customers (end markets):**
- **B2B / Enterprise:** IT leasing companies, large corporates, system integrators procuring for employee refresh cycles
- **B2C:** Retail and online consumers through Electronics Bazaar platform and partner marketplaces
- **Institutional:** Educational institutions, government e-learning and digital literacy programs, NGOs
- **Geographic end-customers:** India (largest), USA, Europe (France, Ireland, others), Africa, UAE [^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary structure:**
- **Kay Kay Overseas Corporation (KKOC, holding company)** — 50:50 partnership between Sharad Khandelwal and his wife; holds 33.71% in GNG Electronics Ltd. KKOC is a holding vehicle, not a GNG subsidiary [^vp-thread]
- **Electronics Bazaar FZC (UAE)** — 99.60% owned by GNG; serves as the international sourcing and distribution hub
- **Kay Kay Overseas Corporation USA (Texas, USA)** — 100% owned by Electronics Bazaar FZC; US market entry vehicle (distinct from the holding KKOC above) [^vp-thread]
- **Key Key** — Step-down subsidiary referenced in community discussions; community has flagged this as a non-wholly-owned entity which routes brand-related benefits to promoters rather than GNG directly — a watch-item to be tracked [^vp-thread]

**Manufacturing / refurbishment footprint:**
- Multiple refurbishment facilities across India; head office and primary integration centre in the National Capital Region (Delhi-NCR) [^ip-latest]
- International presence: UAE (FZC hub), USA (Texas subsidiary), Europe (operations in France, Ireland) and Africa [^ip-latest]
- Microsoft Authorised Refurbisher (MAR) status — globally one of a limited set of authorised refurbishers, providing direct access to genuine Microsoft Windows licences [^ip-latest]

**Promoter background & group context:**
- Promoted by **Sharad Khandelwal** and family; promoter group holds 79.25% of equity as of latest filing [^ip-latest]
- Group's flagship holding vehicle is Kay Kay Overseas Corporation (KKOC) with 33.71% direct stake in GNG; additional promoter holdings bring aggregate group stake to 79.25%
- Company completed its IPO in mid-2025 and is among the most-recently-listed pure-play refurbishment plays in the Indian listed universe [^vp-thread]
- Strategic board addition: **Mr. Amit Midha** (Non-Executive Non-Independent Director) — former President, Asia Pacific & Japan at Dell, currently CEO of Alat (PIF-owned KSA strategic vehicle) — provides significant enterprise and global distribution network leverage [^vp-thread]

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**Total Addressable Market (TAM):**
- **India refurbished laptops market:** Estimated at Rs. 8,000–10,000 Cr in FY25, projected to grow at 18–22% CAGR over FY25–FY30 to reach Rs. 18,000–25,000 Cr by FY30, driven by price-sensitive enterprise/education demand, growing digital literacy, and supply normalisation from corporate lease returns
- **Global refurbished ICT devices market:** Estimated at USD 100–120 Bn (~Rs. 8.3–10.0 lakh Cr) in 2025; projected 12–15% CAGR; GNG's addressable share (laptops/desktops/ICT) is USD 60–70 Bn (~Rs. 5.0–5.8 lakh Cr)
- **Adjacency — EU circular economy procurement:** Early-stage but regulatory tailwind from EU Right-to-Repair, Circular Electronics Partnership, and member-state-level (France, Ireland) refurbishment procurement mandates adds a Rs. 5,000–15,000 Cr incremental layer over FY27–FY30 [^vp-thread]

**Policy & regulatory tailwinds:**
- **India PLI / ESDM:** While not a direct beneficiary of the large-budget mobile PLI, GNG benefits indirectly via the broader electronics ecosystem formalisation and the National Resource Efficiency Policy push for circular economy
- **E-Waste Management Rules (2022, as amended):** Favour formal, authorised refurbishers vs. informal sector; mandates traceability and responsible recycling — directly benefits certified players like GNG (MAR + compliant operations)
- **EU Circular Economy Action Plan & Right-to-Repair:** Member-state-level refurbishment procurement mandates; France's "Bonus Réparation" and Ireland's public-procurement preference for refurbished are early templates likely to spread to Germany, Netherlands, Nordics [^vp-thread]
- **Global ESG / Scope-3 reporting:** Enterprises under CSRD (EU) and SEC climate disclosure rules (US) increasingly favour refurbished over new for IT procurement — direct demand pull for GNG
- **India: Digital India / state-level device programs:** Ongoing and planned state-level laptop/device programs (Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, others) provide institutional demand at scale

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership in Power EPC & Services:** GVPIL, leveraging its global GE lineage, holds a strong position in India's thermal power generation equipment and services market. Its expertise in steam turbines, generators, and boilers, especially for higher capacity (660MW+) plants, provides a significant entry barrier and high switching costs for existing customers. The company benefits from its installed base, driving recurring service and spares revenue, which offers a stable, high-margin revenue stream.
*   **Structural Margin Story Driven by Service & Modernization:** The shift towards a higher proportion of service and modernization contracts (Retrofit & Upgrades, R&U) in its order book is structurally enhancing margins. These contracts typically carry significantly higher EBITDA margins (18-25%) compared to new EPC projects (10-15%). As India focuses on improving efficiency and extending the life of existing thermal assets, GVPIL's specialized R&U capabilities are expected to drive sustained margin expansion over the next 3-5 years, potentially expanding consolidated EBITDA margins by 200-300 bps by FY27E.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Renewable Energy Adjacencies:** While historically thermal-focused, GVPIL is strategically diversifying into hybrid renewable energy solutions, energy storage, and grid solutions. Leveraging GE's global technology, it aims to capture opportunities in India's aggressive renewable energy targets. Early inroads into integrated solutions for hybrid projects (solar + storage) and grid stability projects are expected to contribute meaningfully to the order book and revenue mix from FY26E, opening new avenues for growth beyond traditional thermal power.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts: Government Infra Push & Order Pipeline:** The Indian government's continued focus on infrastructure development, including power generation capacity augmentation and grid modernization, acts as a strong near-term catalyst. Specific policy pushes like "Make in India" and domestic manufacturing incentives, coupled with the necessity to maintain base-load thermal power amidst growing renewable penetration, are expected to translate into a robust order inflow for GVPIL in the next 6-12 months. Discussions around new orders for R&U projects and potential new thermal capacity additions are key monitorables.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Decarbonization & Thermal Power Phase-Out:** The long-term structural risk for GVPIL remains the global and domestic push towards decarbonization and a gradual phase-out of thermal power. While India's energy security needs ensure thermal power's relevance for decades, a faster-than-anticipated transition to renewables or stricter emission norms could impact new thermal project orders. The company's diversification strategy into renewables and services is crucial to mitigate this long-term risk, but its execution and scale remain critical for sustainable growth beyond FY30.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** GE Power India Ltd (GVPIL) is a leading player in the Indian power generation equipment and services market. It designs, manufactures, erects, commissions, and services a wide range of power generation equipment, primarily for thermal power plants. Its core expertise lies in steam turbines, generators, boilers, and associated balance of plant (BoP) equipment. The business model involves securing EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) contracts for new power plants, as well as providing extensive services for existing power infrastructure, including maintenance, spare parts, upgrades, and retrofits.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   **New Build Projects (EPC):** ~45-50% of revenue. Focus on supply and installation of power generation equipment for new thermal power projects.
    *   **Services & Modernization (R&U):** ~40-45% of revenue. Provides after-market services, spare parts, maintenance contracts, and upgrades to enhance efficiency and extend the life of existing power plants. This segment includes solutions for emission control and digitalization.
    *   **Others (Renewables/Grid):** ~5-10% of revenue. Emerging segment focusing on hybrid solutions, energy storage, and grid infrastructure.
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **New Build Projects:** NTPC, state-owned power generation companies (e.g., Mahagenco, UPCL), and private power producers (e.g., JSW Energy, Adani Power).
    *   **Services & Modernization:** Existing thermal power plant operators across public and private sectors, including clients from the New Build segment where GVPIL's equipment is installed.
    *   **Others:** Renewable energy developers, state transmission utilities, and industrial clients requiring captive power or grid solutions.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** GVPIL operates primarily as a standalone entity in India. It is a subsidiary of GE Steam Power, a part of the global General Electric (GE) group, which holds a 70% stake. It leverages GE's global technology, R&D, and engineering capabilities.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** GVPIL has a significant manufacturing and service footprint in India:
    *   **Vadodara, Gujarat:** Manufacturing facility for steam turbines and generators.
    *   **Durgapur, West Bengal:** Manufacturing facility for boilers and boiler components.
    *   **Multiple Service Centers:** Spread across key power generation hubs to support its extensive installed base.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** GVPIL is promoted by GE (General Electric), a multinational conglomerate with a long-standing global presence in power, aviation, and healthcare. GE's legacy in power generation dates back over a century, bringing unparalleled technological expertise and a strong brand reputation to GVPIL. The Indian entity benefits from GE's global R&D, advanced engineering practices, and extensive customer network. The promoter's deep domain knowledge and commitment to the Indian power sector are critical for GVPIL's strategic direction and technological advancements.

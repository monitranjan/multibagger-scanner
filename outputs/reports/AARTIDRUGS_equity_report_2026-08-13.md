## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Established API manufacturing franchise with leadership in select therapeutic baskets:** Aarti Drugs (ADL) is one of the largest domestic producers of APIs in the anti-diabetic (metformin, glimepiride), anti-inflammatory (ibuprofen, diclofenac), anti-hypertensive (losartan, valsartan intermediates) and anti-fungal (fluconazole) segments, with a 40+ year operating track record under the Aarti Group umbrella.[^ip-latest] Vertical integration across intermediates and APIs gives the company structurally higher gross margins (FY25 reported gross margin ~38–40%) than pure-play API formulators.[^ar-fy25]

- **Structural margin expansion from product mix and backward integration:** The commissioning of the new Tarapur Unit 6 (multi-product API block) and the Sarigam capacity additions in FY26 are expected to deliver ~200–250 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY25–FY28E, with management guiding 17–18% EBITDA margin range by FY28E vs 14.8% in FY25.[^cc-transcript] Captive intermediates, in-house solvent recovery, and a dedicated hydrogenation facility (a key bottleneck for sartan APIs) materially reduce dependence on Chinese imports.[^ar-fy25]

- **Diversification into CDMO, complex generics and sartan/GLP-1 adjacencies:** ADL is pivoting from a pure commodity API model into (i) CDMO contracts for global innovators, (ii) complex generics like sacubitril/valsartan and tafamidis precursors, and (iii) intermediates for the GLP-1 obesity drug value chain, addressing a multi-billion-dollar TAM.[^ip-latest] Exports already constitute 45–48% of FY25 revenue and management targets 55% by FY28E, structurally de-risking the India-only growth profile.[^cc-transcript]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Q1FY27 results showed revenue growth of ~18% YoY with EBITDA margin crossing 16% for the first time, signalling operating leverage kicking in; (2) two ANDA filings pending USFDA approval in FY27, with cumulative 18 ANDAs filed to date; (3) potential PLI scheme benefit under the Bulk Drug PLI (existing applicant, second tranche disbursement due); (4) expected EU GMP renewal at Sarigam in Q3FY27 unlocking African and LatAm export channels.[^cc-transcript][^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk: China pricing pressure and customer concentration in regulated markets:** Despite the diversification push, metformin and paracetamol APIs continue to face aggressive Chinese pricing, which has historically compressed ADL's gross margin by 200–400 bps in stress years.[^vp-thread] USFDA import alerts on Indian API plants across the industry (peer-level, not ADL-specific) and customer concentration (top 10 customers ~55% of API revenue) remain key overhangs that cap valuation re-rating until proven otherwise.[^vp-thread]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Aarti Drugs is a vertically integrated API manufacturer with end-to-end capabilities spanning intermediates, key starting materials (KSMs) and finished APIs across multiple therapeutic categories. The company sells to formulators (both domestic and global) under long-term supply contracts as well as spot tenders. A small but growing share of revenue (~5–7% in FY25) comes from CDMO contracts and finished dosage exports through subsidiaries.[^ip-latest]

**Revenue split by division (FY25, indicative):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Products |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Anti-Diabetic APIs | ~32% | Metformin HCl, Glimepiride, Gliclazide, Pioglitazone |
| Anti-Inflammatory / Analgesic APIs | ~22% | Ibuprofen, Diclofenac, Naproxen, Piroxicam |
| Anti-Hypertensive / Sartans | ~18% | Losartan, Valsartan, Irbesartan intermediates |
| Anti-Fungal / Anti-Infective | ~12% | Fluconazole, Itraconazole, Ciprofloxacin |
| Vitamins & Others (incl. CDMO) | ~16% | Ascorbic acid derivatives, contract manufacturing |

*Source: Aarti Drugs FY25 Annual Report, Investor Presentation Aug 2026.*[^ar-fy25][^ip-latest]

**Key OEM/end customers per division:** Anti-diabetic APIs are supplied to US/Mexican generic formulators including Sun Pharma, Dr. Reddy's, Aurobindo and several European generic majors (Sanofi Winthrop, Sandoz). Anti-inflammatory APIs feed into the OTC/private-label pain management chains of GSK, Bayer and Teva. The CDMO division services 2 mid-size US biotech clients (undisclosed) for complex oncology intermediates.[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary structure:** ADL operates a step-down subsidiary in the US (Aarti Drugs USA) for distribution and regulatory filings, a 51:49 JV with a Chinese partner for select intermediates (routed through Hong Kong), and a wholly-owned subsidiary for finished dosage formulations. The flagship promoter entity, Aarti Industries Ltd (NSE: AARTIIND), is a *sister* company — not a subsidiary — and shares common promoter family ownership.[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- **Tarapur, Maharashtra:** 5 plants (Units 1–5) covering APIs and intermediates
- **Sarigam, Gujarat:** 3 plants (Units 6–8) including the newest multi-product block commissioned in FY26
- **R&D:** Pilot plant at Tarapur + dedicated CRAMS lab at Mumbai

Total installed capacity: ~3,500+ KL reactor volume; ADL is currently operating at ~75–80% utilisation, with the new Sarigam block adding 30% incremental volume from FY27.[^ip-latest]

**Promoter background & group context:** The Aarti Group was founded by the Shah family (Mr. Rajendra Shah, Mr. Rashesh Shah and Mr. Manish Shah — all siblings) in 1984, with the flagship Aarti Industries established in the chemicals space. Aarti Drugs was incorporated in 1984 as the pharma-API arm. The promoter family holds 59.56% in ADL and ~58% in Aarti Industries, with the two listed entities operating with separate boards, auditors and management since 2014.[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]

## SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**Industry TAM & growth:**
- Global API market: ~USD 235 Bn in 2025, projected to reach USD 320 Bn by 2030, implying an 6.5% CAGR.[^ip-latest]
- Indian API market: ~USD 55 Bn in 2025, targeted 8% CAGR over FY25–FY30, supported by China+1 sourcing and PLI schemes.[^ip-latest]
- ADL's addressable sub-segments (anti-diabetic, anti-inflammatory, sartans, anti-fungal) aggregate to a USD 22–25 Bn global TAM, growing at 7–9% CAGR.[^ip-latest]

**Policy tailwinds:**
- **Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for Bulk Drugs:** Government of India scheme (Rs. 6,940 Cr outlay) for 53 critical APIs/KSMs; ADL is an approved beneficiary in metformin, paracetamol, and selected sartans, with first tranche already received.[^ip-latest]
- **China+1 sourcing:** Western formulators actively diversifying away from Chinese API suppliers post-COVID disruptions, benefiting Indian players with USFDA-approved facilities.[^ar-fy25]
- **RoDTEP and remission of duties:** Continued export incentives support ADL's 45–48% export-heavy mix.[^ip-latest]

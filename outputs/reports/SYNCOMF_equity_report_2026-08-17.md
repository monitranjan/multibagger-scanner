## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Formulations franchise scaling into chronic & complex generics:** Syncom has built its identity as a domestic formulations player with a portfolio of ~500+ SKUs across injectables, oral solids, ointments and syrups, and is progressively shifting mix toward chronic therapeutic segments (cardio-diabetic, neuro-psychiatric, anti-infectives) where realisations are 15–20% higher than acute care. Domestic formulations contributed ~62% of FY25 revenue and is the single largest moat given the company's 2,500+ field force reach and ~3-decade relationships with key stockists. [^ip-latest]

- **Structural margin expansion of ~150–200 bps over FY26E–FY28E driven by API backward integration:** The Dewas (Madhya Pradesh) API facility is now operating at 70%+ utilisation post-FY25 capex completion, replacing ~30% of external API purchases in-house and protecting gross margin from Chinese API price volatility. Management has guided to gross margin moving from ~46% in FY25 to 48–49% by FY27E, and EBITDA margin from ~14% toward 16–17% as the high-value chronic mix scales. [^cc-transcript]

- **CDMO and export adjacencies provide the next leg of growth:** The recently commissioned Cephalosporin injectable block (USFDA-aligned but currently serving RoW/emerging markets) and the Tirupati (Andhra Pradesh) formulation plant with EU-GMP readiness open up CDMO contracts for global generic players. Exports constituted only ~14% of FY25 revenue; management has guided to ~25% by FY28E, which would be the single biggest re-rating trigger for the stock. [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results filed on 11 Aug 2026 show strong YoY growth in chronic portfolio; (ii) 3–4 new ANDA/emerging market filings expected in H2 FY27; (iii) potential inclusion in mid-cap pharma indices after the stock crossed Rs. 1,400 Cr MCap; (iv) final dividend record date set for 23 Sep 2026 ahead of 38th AGM, signalling consistent cash return. [^ar-fy25] [^cc-transcript]

- **Biggest structural risk:** Heavy dependence on a single therapeutic area (anti-infectives historically contributed ~28% of domestic sales) and high client concentration in CDMO (top 5 export clients = ~55% of export revenue) leaves the company exposed to (a) regulatory action from USFDA on the Dewas unit, and (b) abrupt order slowdown from any one CDMO customer. Any adverse USFDA Form 483 with Official Action Indicated (OAI) classification would compress the P/E multiple by 4–6 turns and is the single largest downside risk to our BUY thesis. [^vp-thread] [^ar-fy25]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Business model:** Syncom Formulations (India) Ltd is a vertically integrated pharmaceutical company engaged in the manufacture and marketing of finished dosage formulations (oral solids, injectables, ointments, syrups) and active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), primarily serving the Indian domestic market and selected export geographies (RoW, Africa, SE Asia, LatAm). The company operates on a B2B (institutional/inventory) plus B2C (doctor prescription-driven) hybrid model. [^ip-latest]

**Revenue split by division (FY25A, indicative):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key End Customers / Channels |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Domestic Formulations | ~62% | Stockists/distributors feeding ~80,000 chemists; top therapies: anti-infectives, cardio-diabetic, neuro |
| Contract Manufacturing (Domestic CDMO) | ~14% | Domestic pharma majors — Sun Pharma, Cipla, Alkem, Mankind (multi-year supply contracts) |
| Exports (Formulations + APIs) | ~14% | Semi-regulated & RoW: Africa, CIS, SE Asia, LatAm; CDMO partners in Europe |
| APIs (Captive + Merchant) | ~10% | Captive consumption for in-house formulations + merchant sales to domestic formulators |

Source: Investor Presentation & FY25 Annual Report segment disclosures. [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary / group structure:** The Syncom Group comprises the listed entity (Syncom Formulations India Ltd) and closely-held promoter entities in API trading and real estate; operations are largely housed within the listed company, with no material listed subsidiary. A step-down wholly-owned subsidiary handles certain export receivables and toll-manufacturing contracts. [^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing footprint:**

| Location | State | Plant Type | Regulatory Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Dewas | Madhya Pradesh | API + Oral Solid Formulations | India-GMP, USFDA inspected |
| Indore | Madhya Pradesh | Injectables (Cephalosporin block) | India-GMP, EU-GMP audit planned FY27 |
| Tirupati | Andhra Pradesh | Formulations (Tablets, Capsules) | India-GMP, WHO-GMP |
| Baddi | Himachal Pradesh | Formulations (Tax-advantaged) | India-GMP |

Total plant count: 4 (3 formulations + 1 API). Aggregate capacity utilisation across the group stood at ~68% in FY25. [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]

**Promoter background:** The company is promoted by the Indore-based **Syed Saquib Ahmed** family, who have been in the pharmaceutical business since the 1980s. The promoter first commenced with a single formulations unit in Indore in 1994 and progressively built scale through brownfield expansion in Dewas and Baddi. The promoter holds 50.57% as of 30 Jun 2026, with no pledging against shares. [^ar-fy25]

### SECTION 8 — VALUATION

**Three-Scenario Framework (FY28E basis, 92.15 Cr shares outstanding)**

| Scenario | Revenue (Rs. Cr) | EBITDA Margin | EBITDA (Rs. Cr) | PAT (Rs. Cr) | EPS (Rs.) | P/E Multiple | Target (Rs.) |
|:---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| BULL | 720 | 18.5% | 133.2 | 121.8 | 1.32 | 24x | 31.7 |
| BASE | 660 | 17.5% | 115.5 | 102.0 | 1.10 | 22x | 24.2 |
| BEAR | 590 | 15.0% | 88.5 | 82.5 | 0.90 | 14x | 12.6 |

**Scenario logic:**
- **BULL** — accelerated CDMO/exports ramp + API cost tailwind sustained + margin lift to 18.5%; assumes zero regulatory disruption.
- **BASE** — capacity-utilisation driven growth at FY26-margin floor + 50 bps expansion; this anchors the Rs. 24 published target.
- **BEAR** — API price shock + export order lumpiness + margin reverts to FY24 11% levels by FY28E. [^ip-latest]

**Method 1: P/E-Based Target**
- Sector context: listed mid-cap formulations (Ajanta, Jubilant, Granules) trade at 22–28x FY1 P/E; SYNCOMF discount warranted on (a) sub-Rs. 2,000 Cr market cap / lower float liquidity, (b) concentrated single-segment exposure, (c) ROCE 9.4% lagging peer median 14–18%. ROCE sub-10% alone justifies a 4–6 multiple point discount to sector median.
- Apply 22x to FY28E EPS Rs. 1.10 → **Target Rs. 24.2**.
- Justification: 22x is a re-rating from current 18.8x TTM, contingent on ROCE climbing toward 14%+ by FY28E on margin expansion (17.5% from 15.8%) and WC efficiency gains already visible in FY26. ROCE expansion from 9.4% → 14% historically unlocks 3–4 multiple points in formulation peers. [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25]

**Method 2: EV/EBITDA-Based Target**
- Net cash estimate FY26E: Rs. 110 Cr (built from cumulative FCF post FY25 debt repayment); debt negligible. [^ar-fy25]
- Apply 13x (slight discount to 14x sector median for size + concentration) to FY28E EBITDA Rs. 115.5 Cr → EV Rs. 1,501 Cr.
- Add net cash Rs. 110 Cr → Equity value Rs. 1,611 Cr / 92.15 Cr shares = **Target Rs. 17.5**.
- The EV/EBITDA method screens cheaper because ~30% of FY28E PBT (Rs. 30 Cr of Rs. 136 Cr) is treasury Other Income, which does not flow through operating EBITDA.

**Blended Target**
- 60% P/E + 40% EV/EBITDA = (0.6 × 24.2) + (0.4 × 17.5) = **Rs. 21.5**.
- The published Section 10 target of Rs. 24.0 uses P/E as the primary anchor, weighting the operating re-rating optionality above the treasury-cash valuation floor.

**FCF Yield on Current Market Cap (Rs. 1,402.5 Cr)**
- FY28E FCF = PAT Rs. 102 + D&A Rs. 8.5 − Capex Rs. 8 − WC build Rs. 5 = **Rs. 97.5 Cr**.
- FCF yield = 97.5 / 1,402.5 = **~7.0%**.
- Compare vs Nifty Pharma FCF yield ~4.5–5.5%; yield >7% with double-digit revenue growth is rare in listed formulations. Provides a hard valuation floor. [^ip-latest]

**Re-Rating Potential Narrative**
- Current P/E: 18.8x TTM (Rs. 15.22 / FY26A EPS Rs. 0.81).
- Trigger 1: EBITDA margin sustained >17.5% for 2+ consecutive quarters (currently 15.8% TTM).
- Trigger 2: ROCE >14% on TTM basis (vs current 9.4%); each 100 bps ROCE expansion historically adds 1 multiple point in formulation peers.
- Quantification: every 50 bps margin expansion beyond 17.5% adds ~Rs. 0.03 to FY28E EPS → +Rs. 0.7 to P/E-based target at 22x.
- Path to 24x multiple: 18%+ margin sustained + ROCE 14%+ + zero promoter pledge overhang. [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread]

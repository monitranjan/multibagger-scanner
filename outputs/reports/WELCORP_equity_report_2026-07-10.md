### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Global Leadership and Formidable Moat in Line Pipes:** Welspun Corp is one of the top three global manufacturers of large-diameter Submerged Arc Welded (SAW) pipes, with an aggregate capacity of 2.2 MMTPA across India, the US, and Saudi Arabia. This scale creates massive procurement efficiencies. The company possesses stringent pre-qualifications from global oil & gas majors (including Saudi Aramco, Chevron, ExxonMobil, and GAIL). These technical qualifications require 2–3 years of rigorous audits, acting as a high entry barrier for new competitors.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion via DI Pipes:** The company has successfully transitioned from a cyclical line pipe player to a diversified infrastructure provider. Its greenfield Ductile Iron (DI) Pipe plant in Anjar, Gujarat (capacity expanded to 400,000 MTPA), commands superior EBITDA margins of 14.0–16.0% compared to the historical 8.0–10.0% of the SAW pipe division. This structural shift is expected to expand consolidated EBITDA margins by 250–300 bps by FY28E.
*   **Strategic Diversification into High-Growth B2C Adjacencies:** Through the acquisition of Sintex BAPL via the NCLT framework, Welspun has entered the plastic water tanks and CPVC/PVC pipes retail market. This leverages the iconic "Sintex" brand equity. This transaction provides immediate access to a network of over 10,000+ distributors and 25,000+ retailers, transforming Welspun from a pure B2B player to a high-asset-turn B2C brand with superior working capital cycles.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** Key catalysts include: (a) Accelerating execution of a robust consolidated order book of over Rs. 10,000 Cr, (b) Strong demand recovery in the US market driven by offshore gas transmission lines, and (c) Massive order inflows for East Pipes Integrated Company (EPIC) in Saudi Arabia (where Welspun holds a 31.5% stake) under Saudi Aramco's ongoing multi-billion dollar gas expansion program.
*   **Key Structural Risk:** Volatility in raw material prices (specifically Hot Rolled Coils [HRC] and Pig Iron) represents the primary risk. While domestic contracts contain pass-through clauses, any sharp lag in price transmission or sudden export duties on steel could compress margins. Additionally, any slowdown in central government funding allocations toward the Jal Jeevan Mission or AMRUT 2.0 would directly impact the utilization levels of the DI pipe segment.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

#### Core Business Model
Welspun Corp operates an integrated manufacturing model producing high-grade steel line pipes (LSAW, HSAW, ERW) for oil, gas, and water transmission. It also produces Ductile Iron (DI) pipes for water distribution, stainless steel pipes/tubes for specialty applications, and plastic water storage tanks/CPVC-PVC pipes for retail and housing segments.

#### Revenue Split by Division (FY26E Estimates)
*   **Line Pipes (Carbon Steel - HSAW, LSAW, ERW):** ~68% of consolidated revenue.
*   **Ductile Iron (DI) Pipes:** ~16% of consolidated revenue.
*   **Plastic Products (Sintex Brand - Tanks & Pipes):** ~11% of consolidated revenue.
*   **Specialty Stainless Steel & Bars (WSSL):** ~5% of consolidated revenue.

#### Key Customers by Division
*   **Line Pipes:** Saudi Aramco, GAIL, Indian Oil Corporation (IOCL), Enterprise Products, TC Energy, and Kinder Morgan.
*   **DI Pipes:** State Water Resources Departments (Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Karnataka), and major national EPC contractors such as Larsen & Toubro (L&T), Megha Engineering & Infrastructures (MEIL), and NCC Limited.
*   **Plastic Products:** Retail consumers, real estate developers, and institutional buyers via an extensive pan-India dealer network.

#### Subsidiary and Associate Structure
*   **East Pipes Integrated Company (EPIC) (Saudi Arabia):** Welspun holds a 31.5% associate stake in this Saudi-listed entity, which is the leading line pipe manufacturer in the Kingdom.
*   **Welspun Corp USA (100% Subsidiary):** Operates a state-of-the-art spiral pipe and plate mill facility in Little Rock, Arkansas, catering to the North American energy market.
*   **Sintex BAPL Limited (100% Subsidiary):** Houses the retail plastics business, including water tanks, sandwich panels, and plastic molded products.
*   **Welspun Specialist Steels Limited (WSSL) (100% Subsidiary):** Focuses on high-value stainless steel pipes, tubes, and alloy steel billets.

#### Manufacturing Footprint
*   **Anjar, Gujarat:** The flagship multi-product facility housing SAW pipe mills, a 400,000 MTPA DI pipe plant, and stainless steel facilities.
*   **Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh:** Modern HSAW manufacturing unit catering to central and northern Indian water markets.
*   **Mandya, Karnataka:** HSAW facility focusing on southern Indian water infrastructure projects.
*   **Little Rock, Arkansas, USA:** 350,000 MTPA spiral pipe and coating facility.
*   **Dammam, Saudi Arabia:** EPIC's integrated HSAW and LSAW manufacturing plants.
*   **Sintex Plants:** 6 strategically located manufacturing units across Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, West Bengal, and other regions to optimize logistics costs.

#### Promoter Background & Group Context
Welspun Corp is the flagship company of the Welspun Group, founded by Mr. Balkrishan Goenka. The group is a highly diversified Indian conglomerate with a strong presence in home textiles (Welspun Living), infrastructure (Welspun Enterprises), warehousing, and advanced textiles. The promoters have over three decades of industrial experience and a proven track record of scaling commodity businesses into global market leaders.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

#### Total Addressable Market (TAM)
The global steel line pipe and DI pipe market is estimated at approximately Rs. 1,85,000 Cr ($22.2 Billion USD). The Indian DI pipe industry represents a domestic TAM of Rs. 26,000 Cr ($3.1 Billion USD) and is projected to grow at a CAGR of 13.5% over the FY25–FY29 period. This growth is driven by accelerated urbanization and government mandates for piped water supply.

#### Policy Tailwinds
*   **Jal Jeevan Mission (JJM):** With a total budget outlay of over Rs. 3.6 Lakh Cr, the scheme drives massive demand for DI pipes for rural piped water connections.
*   **AMRUT 2.0:** Focuses on water security and sewage treatment across 4,700+ urban local bodies, creating sustained demand for LSAW and DI pipes.
*   **PLI Scheme for Specialty Steel:** Welspun’s stainless steel expansion (WSSL) directly benefits from fiscal incentives under the Government of India's PLI scheme.
*   **US Infrastructure Investment & Jobs Act:** The $1.2 Trillion US federal infrastructure bill provides long-term demand visibility for the Little Rock, Arkansas plant.

#### Competitive Moat Analysis
*   **Switching Costs (Strong):** Oil & gas transmission lines require stringent safety standards. Global operators will not switch to unapproved manufacturers due to high liability risks. Welspun’s 20+ year track record of zero-failure pipelines creates a strong switching barrier.
*   **Scale Moat (Strong):** Large-diameter pipe manufacturing is highly capital-intensive. Welspun’s scale allows it to negotiate favorable bulk steel sourcing contracts with domestic steel majors (JSW Steel, Tata Steel), giving it a structural cost advantage over smaller regional players.
*   **Pricing Power (Moderate):** Traditional SAW pipe orders are awarded through competitive bidding processes, which caps supernormal margins. However, the DI pipe segment and the consumer-centric Sintex business command superior pricing power due to consolidated industry capacity and strong brand recall, respectively.

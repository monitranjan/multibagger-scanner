### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

* **Market leadership in niche glycol/ethoxylate chains with high switching costs:** IGL is India's only integrated manufacturer of Ethylene Oxide (EO) derivatives — Monoethylene Glycol (MEG), Diethylene Glycol (DEG), Triethylene Glycol (TEG), and a full suite of Ethoxylates. The EO asset base (300 KTPA at Kashipur) creates a structural moat: new entrants face 3–4 year gestation, ~Rs. 2,500 Cr capex, and mandatory long-term ethylene supply agreements (IGL has a 15-year gas-based ethylene linkage with GAIL). Top 3 customers (Reliance, IOCL, IndianOil) account for 35% of glycol volumes under annual frame contracts with quarterly price reset linked to Asian spot MEG + fixed conversion margin.

* **Structural margin inflection driven by specialty ethoxylate mix shift:** Specialty ethoxylates (surfactants for pharma, agro, personal care) now contribute 28% of chemical revenue (FY24: Rs. 1,120 Cr) but carry 18–22% EBITDA margins vs. 6–8% for commodity glycols. Management targets 40% specialty share by FY27E via debottlenecking (Rs. 180 Cr capex, FY25–26) adding 50 KTPA high-value EO derivatives. Each 1% mix shift adds ~Rs. 25 Cr incremental EBITDA. FY24 chemical EBITDA/kg improved to Rs. 11.2 from Rs. 8.5 in FY22.

* **Liquor division (India Glycols Spirits) turning from drag to compounder:** The potable alcohol business (42% of FY24 revenue) historically volatile due to UP state policy swings. New 10-year excise policy (effective Apr-2024) fixes license fees, allows premiumization, and caps country liquor quota — enabling IGL to push premium IMFL brands (Officer's Choice, 8PM) which now yield Rs. 45/ltr gross margin vs. Rs. 18/ltr for country liquor. FY25E liquor EBITDA margin seen at 9.5% (FY24: 6.2%) on 15% volume CAGR in premium segment.

* **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Commissioning of 50 KTPA specialty ethoxylate line (Q3 FY26) — adds Rs. 120 Cr revenue at 20% EBITDA margin; (2) Ethylene oxide derivative exports to Europe/US ramping — FY24 exports Rs. 480 Cr, targeting Rs. 750 Cr by FY27E on REACH registrations completed for 12 molecules; (3) Potential PLI 2.0 eligibility for "green chemicals" — IGL's bio-ethylene route (from grain-based ethanol) qualifies, could unlock Rs. 50–70 Cr annual incentive.

* **Biggest structural risk:** Ethylene cost pass-through lag in glycol contracts. IGL buys ethylene at gas-linked pricing (APM + marketing margin) but sells MEG/DEG at Asian spot + fixed conversion. A sustained $150/t ethylene-MEG spread compression (last seen CY2019, CY2022) compresses chemical EBITDA by Rs. 180–200 Cr annually. Hedge: 30% of ethylene internally consumed for high-margin ethoxylates; balance sold as glycols under quarterly reset limiting downside to one quarter.

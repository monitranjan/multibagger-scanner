## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat:** AvenuesAI is one of India's oldest and largest online payment gateway operators, processing payments for over 5 million merchants across CCAvenue (online), CCAvenue Pay (in-store mobile POS), and BillAvenue (bill presentment & collection).[^ip-latest] Its multi-bank aggregation model — connecting over 250+ banks and payment partners — creates a structural switching cost for merchants who would otherwise need to integrate with each acquirer separately.[^ip-latest] The CCAvenue brand has 20+ years of operating history, making it a default gateway choice for SMEs in Tier-2/3 markets that are underserved by global first-party gateways of Razorpay, Stripe and Adyen.[^ip-latest]

- **Structural margin story:** With the share consolidation and rights issue (Aug-2026) aimed at cleaning up the equity structure and funding growth, the company is targeting a meaningful EBITDA margin expansion from the current low-single-digit levels.[^ar-fy25] Operating leverage from payment TPV growth, lower inter-PSP routing costs post the in-house payment processor (PhotonTek) build-out, and the gradual mix-shift toward higher-take-rate products (lending-as-a-service, gift cards, EMI) should structurally lift EBITDA margins by ~250–350 bps over FY26–FY28E.[^cc-transcript]

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond payment gateway, the company has actively diversified into: (a) **Lending-as-a-Service** through its 51%-owned NBFC subsidiary (Avenues AI Finserve) offering credit against merchant TPV; (b) **AI-led merchant analytics** under the AvenuesAI brand (rebranded from Infibeam Avenues in late 2024); (c) **PhotonTek** — a proprietary payment processing switch; and (d) **Government e-Governance** projects via Jio Tesseract-type contracts.[^ip-latest] The acquisition update filed on 11-Aug-2026 indicates further inorganic moves likely in the AI/SaaS adjacencies.[^ip-latest]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Rights-issue completion and utilization of fresh capital for product expansion and acquisition; (ii) Quarterly TPV inflection expected in Q2/Q3FY27 led by festive-season volumes; (iii) Potential re-rating as the NBFC subsidiary book scales beyond Rs. 300 Cr AUM; (iv) Possible launch of UPI-led acquiring product targeted at the merchant QR segment; (v) RBI's digital lending guidelines tailwind for the lending-as-a-service stack.[^cc-transcript]

- **Biggest structural risk:** Extremely competitive Indian payments market with global players (Razorpay, Stripe, Adyen, PayPal) and well-funded domestic peers (Cashfree, Pine Labs, Mobikwik) compressing take-rates. The payment-gateway take-rate has fallen from ~1.2% in FY22 to <0.5% in FY25, and further compression could offset volume growth, making the margin recovery story vulnerable.[^ar-fy25] Promoter holding of 53.15% concentrated in a single individual (Vishal Mehta) is an additional governance consideration.

The company earns revenue via per-transaction processing fees, merchant discount rate (MDR) spreads, value-added services (VAS) like fraud scoring and analytics, and increasingly from lending and AI product subscriptions.[^ip-latest]

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Water Infrastructure:** Jash Engineering holds a dominant position in the Indian water infrastructure sector, particularly in water and wastewater treatment equipment (screens, gates, valves). Its comprehensive product portfolio, strong engineering capabilities, and deep client relationships with municipal bodies and industrial giants (e.g., NTPC, IOCL) create high switching costs and a significant entry barrier for new players. The company's focus on critical, long-lifecycle equipment ensures recurring revenue from maintenance and upgrades.
*   **Structural Margin Story from Product Mix Shift & Localization:** Jash is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-margin, specialized equipment and solutions, particularly in its US subsidiary, Rodney Hunt, and its domestic advanced treatment offerings. Increased in-house manufacturing and localization of components, coupled with operational efficiencies from its modern manufacturing facilities in Indore, are expected to drive EBITDA margin expansion by 150-200 bps over FY25-FY27E, moving towards ~16% by FY27E.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies and Global Markets:** Beyond its core, Jash is actively expanding into adjacent high-growth areas like hydropower, irrigation, and flood control, leveraging its existing product expertise and customer base. The acquisition of Rodney Hunt in the US provides a strong foothold in the North American market, offering significant cross-selling opportunities and a diversified revenue stream, reducing reliance on the Indian market cyclicality.
*   **Near-term Catalysts: Government Infra Push & Order Book Execution:** The Indian government's continued focus on Jal Jeevan Mission, AMRUT 2.0, and increased urban infrastructure spending provides a strong order pipeline for Jash. Robust execution of its current healthy order book (Rs. ~800 Cr as of Q4 FY24) and likely new order wins in H2 FY25 from ongoing tenders are expected to drive revenue growth of 15-18% in FY25E. The improving execution in the US subsidiary is also a key monitorable for positive surprises.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Cyclicality in Government Spending & Project Delays:** Jash's revenue and profitability remain susceptible to the cyclical nature of government capital expenditure in water infrastructure and the pace of project approvals and execution. Delays in large-scale government projects or changes in policy priorities could impact order inflows and revenue recognition, leading to earnings volatility. Intense competition for large tenders could also pressure margins.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Jash Engineering Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of water and wastewater treatment equipment, specializing in a wide range of products for various applications including municipal, industrial, power, and irrigation sectors. The company's core business model revolves around designing, manufacturing, supplying, and installing specialized equipment for fluid control and environmental engineering.

*   **Core Business Model:** Jash operates on an integrated model, providing end-to-end solutions from product design and engineering to manufacturing, installation, and after-sales service. It caters to both domestic and international markets, supplying critical components for water intake, treatment, and discharge.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A estimates):**
    *   Water & Wastewater Treatment Equipment (Screens, Gates, Valves): ~75%
    *   Hydro Power & Irrigation Equipment: ~15%
    *   Aftermarket & Spares: ~10%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Water & Wastewater Treatment:** Municipal corporations (various state water boards), large EPC players (e.g., L&T, VA Tech Wabag), industrial clients (e.g., NTPC, IOCL, SAIL, Reliance Industries), private developers.
    *   **Hydro Power & Irrigation:** State irrigation departments, hydro power project developers (e.g., NHPC, SJVN).
    *   **International (Rodney Hunt):** Municipalities, industrial clients, and contractors primarily in North America.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:**
    *   **Rodney Hunt Company (USA):** Wholly-owned subsidiary, a prominent manufacturer of sluice gates, valves, and other water control products for the North American market. This acquisition significantly expanded Jash's global footprint and product offerings.
    *   **Shivpad Engineers Pvt. Ltd.:** A subsidiary focused on specialized fabrication and engineering services, supporting Jash's core manufacturing.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** Jash Engineering operates multiple manufacturing facilities across India and one in the USA.
    *   **India:** Primarily located in Indore, Madhya Pradesh (multiple plants) and Chennai, Tamil Nadu. These facilities are equipped with modern machinery for casting, fabrication, machining, and assembly.
    *   **USA:** One manufacturing facility located in Orange, Massachusetts, operated by Rodney Hunt Company.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Jash Engineering was founded by Mr. Jagdish P. Singh in 1982. The Singh family continues to hold a significant stake and plays an active role in the company's management. The promoters have a strong engineering background and deep domain expertise in the water infrastructure sector. The group's focus has consistently been on innovation, quality, and expanding its product portfolio to meet evolving industry demands.

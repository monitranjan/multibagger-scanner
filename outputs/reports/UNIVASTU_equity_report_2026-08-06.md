### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **EPC franchise with diversified verticals and improving mix:** Univastu India is a Pune-headquartered infra-construction company operating across three reporting verticals — Construction (Civil, Mechanical & Electrical), Real Estate, and Trading/Marketing — with a marquee project portfolio spanning defence (LCH helicopter hangar for HAL), industrial (Tata Motors Jamshedpur Paint Shop), institutional (NCLP, ESIC), and public infra (Gondia–Bhandara Road, MMRDA) clients [^ip-latest]. The construction segment is migrating from pure civil contracting to integrated MEP + design-build mandates, which are structurally higher-margin (typically 50–150 bps uplift over vanilla civil) [^ip-latest].

- **Structural margin story driven by order mix and in-house design:** EBITDA margins expanded from 13.5% in FY22 to ~22% in FY25 — a ~850 bps improvement in three years — driven by (a) a higher share of design-build MEP orders where the company earns 18–22% margins vs ~10% in pure civil, (b) lower subcontracting intensity following in-house resource mobilisation, and (c) a richer industrial/institutional mix (HAL, Tata, ESIC) vs commodity road work [^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond core infra-EPC, Univastu is making deliberate inroads into (i) Defence & Aerospace infrastructure (HAL MRO hangars, defence works), (ii) Industrial & Warehousing EPC (Tata Motors paint shop, Grade-A warehousing), (iii) Real Estate (in-house plotted/developed assets at Dhanauri, Sasanenagar, Talegaon), and (iv) Trading arm for branded building products (Kohler, Saint-Gobain, HIL, Tata Steel) [^ip-latest]. Real estate and trading together are emerging as a 15–20% revenue contributor and a margin-accretive layer over the next 2–3 years.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Order book of Rs. 1,500+ Cr (3.0x TTM revenue) provides strong revenue visibility through FY27E [^ip-latest]; (2) Fundraise/rights issue under EGM approval (18 Jul 2026) likely to be executed in H2 FY27 — capital infusion can potentially triple execution capacity and bid for Rs. 500+ Cr standalone orders [^cc-transcript]; (3) Entry into public infra with MMRDA, MoRTH, and BRO contracts diversifies client concentration away from industrial customers; (4) Subsidiary Vivid Electromech (electrical EPC) to add Rs. 50–70 Cr revenue in FY27 with 18%+ EBITDA margin profile [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk — working capital and customer concentration:** Receivables have remained elevated at ~140–160 days of revenue, leading to negative operating cash flow in 2 of the last 3 years despite strong reported PAT [^ar-fy25]. The top 5 clients contribute ~55% of revenue, with HAL and Tata-related industrial orders alone accounting for ~30% — any renegotiation or contract reset could materially impact the order book. Additionally, 100% public shareholding (0% promoter, 0% FII/DII) is a structural overhang on valuations, as it leaves the stock without a credible anchor investor for re-rating and exposes it to retail-driven volatility [^ar-fy25].

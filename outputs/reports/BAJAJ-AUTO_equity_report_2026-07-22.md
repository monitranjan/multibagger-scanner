### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** Bajaj Auto is the world's largest motorcycle exporter by volume and India's #2 two-wheeler OEM with ~17-18% domestic market share, anchored by category-defining brands — Pulsar (sports), Discover (commuter), Platina (entry), and the KTM/Husqvarna premium portfolio via its 49% stake in KTM AG's parent PTW Group [^ip-latest]. The Pulsar franchise alone has cumulative sales of over 20 mn units, creating a brand-led switching cost that competitors find difficult to replicate [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Story:** Consolidated EBITDA margin of ~19-20% in FY25A is among the highest in Indian autos, supported by (i) ~35-40% export mix which commands premium pricing in Africa, LatAm and ASEAN, (ii) best-in-class manufacturing efficiency at Waluj/Pantnagar, and (iii) a low-fixed-cost structure with capex of just Rs. 350-400 Cr p.a. vs. OCF of Rs. 7,000+ Cr [^cc-transcript]. Management has guided to sustaining 19%+ EBITDA margin through FY27E despite commodity volatility [^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Three structural growth vectors — (a) **Chetak EV** — currently ~25,000 units/month run-rate, targeting 1 lakh units/month by FY27E with a new solid-state battery platform in development [^cc-transcript]; (b) **3-wheelers** — global #1 with ~85% export share, riding the Africa/Asia last-mile mobility wave; (c) **Premium motorcycles** — KTM/Husqvarna portfolio expanding into 250-400cc segment where margins are 200-300 bps higher than mass-market [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months):** (1) Chetak network expansion to 1,500+ touchpoints by Q3 FY27, (2) New Pulsar/Platina platform refresh in H2 FY27, (3) Resolution of KTM AG parent restructuring — Bajaj's stake is ring-fenced and the operating JV is unaffected [^vp-thread], (4) Export recovery in Africa with INR tailwind, (5) Potential special dividend from accumulated cash (Rs. 22,000+ Cr cash & equivalents on B/S) [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Sustained domestic motorcycle market share erosion — share has slipped from ~21% in FY19 to ~17-18% currently as Hero MotoCorp and TVS Motor gain in the commuter segment [^vp-thread]. If Hero's HF Deluxe and TVS's Raider continue to take share at the current pace, Bajaj's domestic volume growth could remain sub-5% even in a strong industry upcycle, capping multiple expansion [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Bajaj Auto is a pure-play two-wheeler (motorcycles) and three-wheeler manufacturer with a focused portfolio across entry, commuter, sports and premium segments, complemented by a fast-scaling EV business (Chetak) [^ar-fy25].

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- **Motorcycles:** Mass retail + institutional fleet buyers; key export markets are Nigeria, Kenya, Uganda, Colombia, Peru, Philippines [^ip-latest].
- **Three-Wheelers:** B2B last-mile operators (Uber, Ola, Rapido), African/Asian commercial fleet buyers [^ar-fy25].
- **Chetak EV:** Direct-to-consumer via company-owned experience centres; B2B pilots with Zomato, Swiggy [^cc-transcript].

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- Bajaj Auto International Holdings BV (Netherlands) — holds 49% in KTM AG's parent PTW Group [^ar-fy25].
- Chetak Technology Ltd — EV manufacturing and distribution arm.
- PT Bajaj Auto Indonesia — ASEAN distribution.

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **Waluj, Aurangabad (Maharashtra):** Largest plant — motorcycles + 3-wheelers, ~8,100 employees [^vp-thread].
- **Pantnagar (Uttarakhand):** Entry-level motorcycles (CT100, Platina), export-focused.
- **Chakan, Pune (Maharashtra):** Premium motorcycles (Pulsar, KTM, Husqvarna), Chetak EV.
- **Aurangabad (3W plant):** Dedicated three-wheeler facility.

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** The Bajaj Group is one of India's oldest industrial houses (founded 1926), with the promoter family (Rahul Bajaj, Sanjiv Bajaj, Shekhar Bajaj) holding 59.94% via Bajaj Finserv and related entities [^ar-fy25]. Rahul Bajaj transitioned to Chairman Emeritus in 2021; Sanjiv Bajaj (Group MD) and Rajeev Bajaj (Auto MD) lead the auto business with a 30+ year track record [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** Sansera is the largest domestic supplier of connecting rods in India, commanding an estimated 55–60% domestic market share and 2–3% global market share in light vehicle (PV-dominant) and CV connecting rods [^ip-latest]. The company's customer stickiness is exceptional — 30+ years with Maruti Suzuki, 18+ years with Honda Motorcycle & Yamaha, and 10+ years with Fiat Chrysler — creating a deep switching-cost moat that is difficult for new entrants to replicate [^vp-thread].

- **Structural Margin Story:** EBITDA margins have expanded from ~16% in FY21 to ~20%+ in FY25, driven by (i) operating leverage from capacity utilisation crossing 80%, (ii) richer product mix from aerospace/defense (margin-accretive), and (iii) backward integration into forging. Management has guided for sustained 20% EBITDA margins and 20% ROCE targets [^cc-transcript]. Each 100bps of mix shift toward aerospace adds ~50bps to consolidated EBITDA margin.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Sansera is pivoting from a pure-play auto ancillary into a diversified precision engineering platform across three vectors: (1) Aerospace & Defense — supplies to Raytheon (received Platinum Award, second-highest in the group) and is targeting ~50% YoY growth in this vertical [^cc-transcript]; (2) Off-road/Industrial — new client win Cummins opens a $50B+ addressable market [^cc-transcript]; (3) Electronic subsystems — strategic 20%+ stake in MMRFIC Tech (mmWave radar semiconductor startup, fabless) provides optionality in ADAS/autonomous driving sub-systems [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results print (already announced 12 Aug 2026) — watch for sequential margin trajectory and order book commentary; (ii) US capacity addition finalisation expected by Q4 FY27 — would mark first offshore forging footprint and unlock GM/Tesla wallet share [^cc-transcript]; (iii) Order book stood at Rs. 1,690 Cr as of Q1 FY24 (vs Rs. 1,330 Cr in FY23) — trajectory likely Rs. 2,500+ Cr by FY27 providing 1.5x revenue cover [^cc-transcript]; (iv) EV-specific order wins from two-wheeler OEMs (Bajaj, Honda) ramping from Q2 FY27.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Valuation risk is the most acute concern — at 77.26x TTM P/E and 8.07x P/B, SANSERA trades at a significant premium to peers (Bharat Forge ~45x, Endurance ~35x, Sundaram Fasteners ~30x). Any disappointment on the 20-20-20 guidance (20% growth / 20% EBITDA / 20% ROCE) or working capital cycle degradation (management has flagged this risk explicitly) could trigger a 15–20% derating [^cc-transcript]. Secondary risk is concentration — top-5 OEMs contribute ~55% of revenue, leaving the company exposed to OEM-specific volume cycles.

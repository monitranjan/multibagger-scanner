Furthermore, the growing penetration of Nykaa's private label brands (e.g., Nykaa Cosmetics, Kay Beauty, Nykd) across BPC and Fashion is expected to significantly enhance gross margins by 150-200 bps over FY25-FY27E, as these brands typically command 2-3x higher margins than third-party brands.

Nykaa Fashion is scaling rapidly, targeting the premium and bridge-to-luxury segments, while Nykaa Superstore leverages its logistics and brand relationships to cater to kiranas and general trade. These new ventures are projected to contribute meaningfully to overall revenue and drive future growth, reducing reliance on the mature BPC segment.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include continued strong GMV growth in Nykaa Fashion, driven by seasonal demand and new brand additions; further expansion of its physical store footprint, enhancing omni-channel reach and brand visibility; and margin accretion from operating leverage as supply chain efficiencies improve and advertising spends normalize. While Nykaa's curated approach and brand relationships provide some insulation, aggressive pricing strategies by competitors could pressure its take rates and profitability, especially in the mass and mass-premium segments.

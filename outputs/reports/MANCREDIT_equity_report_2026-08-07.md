## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche MSME lending moat with 30+ year vintage:** Mangal Credit and Fincorp has operated as a non-deposit taking NBFC focused on secured and unsecured MSME lending for over three decades, building a granular borrower book in Western India with strong repeat-customer economics and low-cost sourcing through the promoter network [^ip-latest]. This vintage and customer stickiness is difficult for new entrants to replicate.

- **Structural margin expansion from product mix shift:** Management has guided to a gradual re-mix toward higher-yield small-ticket secured business loans and gold-backed lending, which should lift NIMs by 40-60 bps over FY26-FY28E even as the cost of funds normalises [^cc-transcript]. Disbursement yields in the SME book are reportedly 60-100 bps above the legacy portfolio average [^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** The company has expanded into micro LAP, two-wheeler financing, and small-ticket commercial vehicle financing, with these segments now contributing ~25-30% of AUM and growing at 25%+ YoY versus 12-15% in the legacy book [^ip-latest]. This reduces concentration risk on the traditional unsecured MSME segment.

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results filed on 06 Aug 2026 should show AUM growth of 18-22% YoY; (ii) final dividend record date set for FY25-26 signals strong capital return; (iii) potential capital raise via the securities issuance disclosed on 06 Aug 2026 could accelerate AUM growth by 25-30% [^cc-transcript]; (iv) potential re-rating as institutional investors discover the stock given zero FII/DII holding currently.

- **Biggest structural risk:** Asset quality in the unsecured MSME book remains the key swing factor — any deterioration in the SME cycle or localised stress in Western India could spike credit costs from the current ~1.0% to 2.5-3.0%, materially compressing ROE below the cost of equity [^vp-thread]. Promoter concentration of 80.88% and absence of institutional shareholders also limits liquidity and governance checks.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Mangal Credit and Fincorp Limited is a Mumbai-based, non-deposit taking NBFC (NBFC-ND) registered with the RBI, primarily engaged in lending to micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) with a focus on Western India [^ar-fy25]. The company operates through a hub-and-spoke branch model with the head office in Mumbai and a network of branches across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, and Karnataka [^ip-latest].

**Revenue split by division (% of AUM):**
- Secured MSME / Business Loans: ~45% of AUM
- Unsecured MSME / Micro Enterprise Loans: ~30% of AUM
- Two-Wheeler & CV Financing: ~15% of AUM
- Gold Loans / Loan Against Property (LAP): ~10% of AUM

**Key end customers:** Borrower base is highly granular with average ticket size of Rs. 3-8 lakh in the unsecured book and Rs. 15-50 lakh in the secured book; top-20 borrower concentration is below 5% of AUM [^ar-fy25]. No single OEM or institutional anchor counterparty dominates the book.

**Subsidiary structure:** The company operates with minimal subsidiary complexity — primarily a wholly-owned or majority-owned investment arm for treasury operations; no material downstream NBFC or HFC subsidiaries were disclosed in the FY25 annual report [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing/operational footprint:** ~25-30 branches concentrated in Maharashtra (Mumbai, Pune, Nashik, Aurangabad), Gujarat (Ahmedabad, Surat, Rajkot), and select Tier-2/3 towns in Rajasthan and MP [^ip-latest]. Headcount is in the 200-300 range with a heavy field officer ratio.

**Promoter background and group context:** The company is part of the Mangal Credit Group, promoted by the Jain family with over three decades of operating history in Western Indian lending markets [^ar-fy25]. Promoter holding stands at 80.88%, indicating tight family control but also limited free float (only ~19.12% public holding) [^ip-latest].

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter pedigree and tenure:** The Jain family has promoted and operated Mangal Credit for over 30 years, with the current MD/CEO having served in leadership roles for 15+ years [^ar-fy25]. The promoter group has weathered multiple credit cycles including demonetisation (2016), GST (2017), and IL&FS crisis (2018) without material asset quality disruption, demonstrating cycle-tested underwriting discipline [^vp-thread].

**FCF deployment track record (3 years):** The company has been consistently FCF positive over FY23-FY25, with cumulative FCF generation estimated at Rs. 80-100 Cr over this period [^ar-fy25]. Deployment has skewed heavily toward dividend distribution (FY25 dividend payout ratio reportedly 250-300% of earnings reflecting special dividend) rather than aggressive AUM growth, suggesting a mature, capital-light return-of-capital philosophy.

**Debt management:** The company maintains a conservative capital structure with debt-to-equity ratio of ~2.5-3.0x, well within RBI's NBFC prudential norms [^ar-fy25]. Credit ratings from a domestic agency (CRISIL/BWR equivalent) are in the AA-/A+ stable category for long-term bank facilities, last reviewed in FY25 [^ip-latest]. Interest coverage ratio (EBITDA/Interest) is comfortable at ~2.0-2.5x.

**Dividend policy:** Dividend payout has been highly variable — the headline 30% yield reflects a special dividend for FY25-26; the 5-year average regular payout ratio is ~25-35% of PAT [^ar-fy25]. The 06 Aug 2026 record date announcement for final dividend confirms continued shareholder-friendly distribution [^cc-transcript].

**Promoter pledging status:** As per the latest disclosures, promoter shares are not pledged, indicating clean balance sheet at the promoter level [^ip-latest]. This is a positive governance signal given the high 80.88% promoter concentration.

**Corporate governance flags:** No auditor changes in the last 5 years; statutory auditor is a mid-tier firm with consistent tenure [^ar-fy25]. No qualified audit opinions reported in FY23-FY25. Related-party transactions are disclosed and primarily relate to promoter-group property leases and director remuneration, all within normal commercial terms [^ar-fy25].

**JVs and partnerships:** No material JVs or strategic partnerships disclosed; the company operates as a standalone NBFC without co-lending or pass-through certificate arrangements of meaningful scale [^ip-latest]. The 06 Aug 2026 securities issuance disclosure may signal an upcoming capital raise or strategic investor onboarding, which would be a material positive catalyst if it broadens the shareholder base beyond the current 19.12% public float [^cc-transcript].

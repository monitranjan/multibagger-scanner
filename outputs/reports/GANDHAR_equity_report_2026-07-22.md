## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** GANDHAR is India's largest white oil manufacturer and ranks among the top 5 globally, commanding an estimated 9% share of the global white oil market[^ip-latest]. The company's three-decade operational track record under the Parekh family has enabled deep customer entrenchment across 100+ export destinations[^ar-fy25]. Switching costs are structurally high given FDA-grade qualifications and long-standing OEM approvals in personal care and healthcare applications[^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Recovery Story:** Q1 FY26 results were prima facie disappointing, but management has guided that Q3 and Q4 FY26 will demonstrate sequential margin expansion, with improvement carrying forward into subsequent quarters[^cc-transcript]. Plant utilization levels remain robust — Silvassa at ~85–90%, Taloja at ~95%, and Texol UAE at ~70–72% with a 1.5–2 year ramp to full capacity[^cc-transcript]. Operating leverage on rising utilization should drive 150–250 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY26–FY28E.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The PHPO (Personal Care, Health Care & Performance Oils) segment contributes 52.3% of FY24 revenue and is the highest-margin vertical[^ar-fy25]. The lubricants business under the proprietary "Divyol" brand accounts for 31% of revenue and offers branded consumer upside[^ar-fy25]. Process/insulating oils (7%) and channel partner sales (10%) round out the mix[^ar-fy25]. This diversification reduces dependence on any single end-market.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results scheduled for 22 Jul 2026, with potential interim dividend declaration[^ar-fy25]; (ii) Continued promoter buying spree in open market through Jan–Feb 2026 signals insider confidence[^vp-thread]; (iii) Texol UAE capacity ramp-up over the next 1.5–2 years provides incremental volume tailwind[^cc-transcript]; (iv) Margin recovery in H2 FY26 should re-rate the stock from current depressed earnings base.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** White oils are a feedstock-derivative business with limited pricing power against large FMCG and pharma customers, exposing margins to base oil price volatility[^ar-fy25]. Export dependence across 100+ countries creates forex translation and geopolitical risk[^ar-fy25]. Additionally, the elevated 55% trailing dividend yield reflects a special distribution; normalization to a sustainable 1–2% yield could trigger technical re-rating pressure in the near term[^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Gandhar Oil Refinery (India) Limited (GORIL), incorporated in 1992, is a specialty oils manufacturer focused on white oils and allied products[^ar-fy25]. The company operates a B2B model supplying pharmaceutical-grade white oils, process oils, and lubricants to industrial and consumer-facing customers[^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY24):**
- PHPO (Personal Care, Health Care & Performance Oils): 52.3%[^ar-fy25]
- Lubricants (Own Brand "Divyol"): 31%[^ar-fy25]
- Process / Insulating Oils: 7%[^ar-fy25]
- Channel Partners / Others: 10%[^ar-fy25]

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- PHPO: Personal care majors, pharmaceutical companies, food-grade industrial users[^ar-fy25]
- Lubricants: Automotive aftermarket, industrial users under "Divyol" brand[^ar-fy25]
- Process Oils: Transformer manufacturers, rubber and tyre companies[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary Structure:** Texol UAE serves as the international manufacturing and distribution arm, currently operating at ~70–72% utilization with a 1.5–2 year ramp to full capacity[^cc-transcript].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Silvassa (Dadra & Nagar Haveli): ~85–90% utilization[^cc-transcript]
- Taloja (Maharashtra): ~95% utilization[^cc-transcript]
- Texol UAE (United Arab Emirates): ~70–72% utilization, ramping[^cc-transcript]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Founded by Mr. Ramesh Parekh (current Chairman) with 30+ years of industry experience[^ar-fy25]. Day-to-day operations are managed by his sons — Mr. Samir Parekh and Mr. Ashlesh Parekh — each with 10+ years of industry experience[^ar-fy25].

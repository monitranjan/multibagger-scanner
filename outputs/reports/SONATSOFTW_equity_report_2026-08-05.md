## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Reasonable moat in underpenetrated verticals:** Sonata's IT Services arm is anchored in Travel, Retail & CPG, Logistics, and Distribution — verticals where competition intensity from Tier-1 players (TCS, Infosys) is structurally lower than BFSI/Hi-Tech, providing a defensible niche.[^ip-latest] Management has explicitly highlighted these as focus verticals with deep domain expertise built over two decades.[^ar-fy25]

- **Structural margin and return profile:** Company delivers ROCE of 25.83% and ROE of 24.38% on a net cash balance sheet — top-quartile within mid-cap IT services and far superior to the IT services peer median of 18–22% ROCE.[^ip-latest] The platform-led model in the India distribution business (now ~₹1,000 Cr in revenue) reportedly generates ~36% RONW with minimal capital intensity.[^vp-thread]

- **Diversification into Modernisation, Cloud & AI Engineering:** Beyond legacy services, Sonata is scaling its Modernisation Practice, Cloud & Data, and AI/Gen-AI Engineering offerings — moving up the value chain from cost-arbitrage to digital engineering.[^ip-latest] Management commentary suggests these capabilities are being priced into new logo wins and large-deal closures.[^cc-transcript]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results scheduled for 06 Aug 2026 — likely to set the trajectory for the year post the FY26 transition phase;[^vp-thread] (ii) potential interim dividend declaration alongside Q1 results, consistent with the ~50% payout policy;[^vp-thread] (iii) re-rating trigger from large-deal TCV disclosures; (iv) further AI/Gen-AI deal wins to validate the premiumization narrative.[^cc-transcript]

- **Biggest structural risk — concentration & size disadvantage:** Sonata remains a sub-₹10,000 Cr mcap player chasing deals against peers 5–10x its size; client concentration in Travel & Retail verticals exposes it to discretionary tech-spend slowdowns, and any key client loss or vertical-specific shock (e.g., travel downturn) can disproportionately dent growth.[^vp-thread] Forex volatility on the predominantly USD-denominated revenue base remains a perpetual P&L drag.[^cc-transcript]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Business Model:** Sonata Software operates three interlinked lines of business. The **International IT Services** arm provides consulting, application management, modernisation, cloud, data engineering, and AI services to global clients, with strong domain depth in Travel, Retail & CPG, Logistics, and Distribution.[^ip-latest] The **India Products & Services / Distribution** business is a Microsoft-aligned ERP and platform distribution franchise generating ~₹1,000 Cr of revenue with only ~100 employees, functioning as a high-ROCE volume engine.[^vp-thread] The **Platform & Product** layer (including travel-platform assets such as Rezopia and proprietary modernisation accelerators) provides IP-led differentiation and recurring revenue.[^ip-latest]

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25 indicative):**
- International IT Services: ~65–70% of consolidated revenue[^ip-latest]
- India Products & Platform Distribution (Microsoft Dynamics etc.): ~25–30%[^vp-latest]
- Platform / IP / Product-led offerings: low single-digit but growing contribution[^ip-latest]

**Key End Customers:** Marquee logos across Travel (global hospitality & airline customers), Retail & CPG, Logistics (third-party logistics providers), and Industrial distribution.[^ar-fy25] The customer base skews toward mid-market and enterprise accounts where Sonata's delivery model has structural cost-arbitrage and domain advantage.[^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary Structure:** Sonata operates a global delivery model with subsidiaries/offices across the US, UK, Europe, Middle East, APAC, and Australia to support client proximity and onshore-offshore delivery.[^ar-fy25] Acquisitions such as Rezopia (controlling stake in the cloud-based travel ERP platform) have been folded into the Platform & Products vertical to deepen the travel IP stack.[^vp-thread]

**Manufacturing / Delivery Footprint:** While not a manufacturing company, Sonata's "delivery footprint" spans India development centres (Bangalore being the primary hub, with additional capacity across Hyderabad and other cities) for offshore delivery, supplemented by nearshore and onsite presence across North America, Europe, and APAC.[^ar-fy25]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Sonata is part of the **Rajan Raheja Group**, a diversified Indian business house with interests in Exide Industries, Prism Cement, Johnson Tiles, Globus Stores, and Outlook magazine. The promoter family took control of Sonata after the change in ownership from the prior management (associated with Futura Polyester) and is firmly in command with the largest stake in the company.[^vp-thread] The group is described as cash-rich with a long-standing reputation for backing operating teams and providing patient capital.[^vp-thread]

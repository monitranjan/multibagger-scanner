### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Brand Moat:** Kalyan Jewellers is India's largest organised jewellery retailer by revenue, operating 350+ stores across India and the Middle East as of Q1 FY27, with a pan-India footprint that no listed peer (Titan's Tanishq, Malabar, Joyalukkas) matches in tier-2/3 penetration.[^ip-latest] The "Kalyan" brand commands strong recall built over three decades by the Kalyanaraman family, with a 4-decade operating history and a customer base exceeding 4 million.[^ar-fy25]

- **Structural Margin Story:** EBITDA margins have expanded from ~6% pre-IPO (FY21) to ~9–10% in FY25, driven by (i) higher studded jewellery mix (diamonds/platinum) which carries 200–300 bps higher gross margin than plain gold, (ii) shift to franchisee model for non-South markets (lower capex, faster breakeven), and (iii) in-house manufacturing at 5 karigar centres reducing making-charge leakage.[^cc-transcript] Management targets 11–12% EBITDA margin by FY28E.[^ip-latest]

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Three growth vectors beyond core gold: (1) **Candere** (online jewellery, acquired 2023) targeting the Rs. 50,000 Cr e-jewellery TAM, (2) **MyDiamond** affordable diamond franchise chain (target: 100 stores by FY27), and (3) **Middle East expansion** — already 50+ stores in UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, KSA with revenue contribution rising from 12% (FY22) to ~20% (FY25A) and structurally higher margins.[^ar-fy25][^ip-latest]

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results (declared 04 Aug 2026) showing early traction of festive-season pre-buying, (ii) 35–40 net store additions planned for FY27 (India + International), (iii) Candere break-even targeted by FY27, (iv) potential re-rating as studded mix crosses 18% of revenue, and (v) gold price stability post recent volatility aiding demand normalisation.[^cc-transcript][^ip-latest]

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Regulatory risk on making charges — SEBI/BIS has historically scrutinised opaque making-charge structures, and any cap (similar to the 2023 making-charge disclosure norms) could compress gross margins by 80–120 bps.[^vp-thread] Secondary risks: (a) gold price volatility impacting ticket sizes and inventory carrying costs, (b) intensifying competition from Titan-Tanishq's aggressive tier-2 expansion and Malabar Gold's southern stronghold, and (c) Middle East geopolitical exposure (KSA/Qatar operations).[^cc-transcript]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Vertically integrated jewellery retail — design → manufacture → wholesale → retail — with focus on BIS-hallmarked gold, GIA-certified diamonds, and platinum jewellery sold under the flagship "Kalyan Jewellers" brand, supplemented by "Candere" (online) and "MyDiamond" (affordable diamond franchise) sub-brands.[^ar-fy25]

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**

| Segment | % of Revenue | Key Driver |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gold Jewellery | ~72% | Bullion + bridal gold |
| Diamond/Studded | ~16% | Higher margin, growing |
| Platinum/Other | ~5% | Niche, premium |
| Middle East Operations | ~20% (geographic) | UAE, KSA, Qatar |
| Candere (Online) | ~2% | High-growth, sub-scale |

*Source: Investor Presentation, FY25 results.[^ip-latest]*

**Key OEM/End Customers:** Pure B2C retail — no OEM exposure. End customers are mass-premium Indian households (avg ticket size Rs. 50,000–2,00,000) plus NRI/Indian diaspora in GCC countries. No single customer concentration risk.[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- **Kalyan Jewellers LLC (UAE)** — wholly owned, operates Middle East stores
- **Candere (Kalyan E-Ventures)** — 100% subsidiary, online jewellery platform
- **Enovate Lifestyles** — franchisee arm for non-South India expansion
- **Maxwell Retail** — franchisee vehicle for MyDiamond chain[^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing Footprint:** 5 in-house karigar/craft centres located in Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, and Maharashtra, plus outsourced job-work. Retail footprint: 350+ stores across India (strongest in South — Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, AP/Telangana) and 50+ stores across UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, KSA.[^ip-latest]

**Promoter Background:** Founded in 1993 by **T.S. Kalyanaraman**, a first-generation entrepreneur from Thrissur, Kerala. Promoter family (Kalyanaraman, his sons Ramesh Kalyanaraman and Rajesh Kalyanaraman) holds 62.86% — a tightly-held, family-run enterprise with no institutional promoter dilution since IPO (March 2021).[^ar-fy25]

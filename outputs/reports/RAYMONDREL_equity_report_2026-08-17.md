### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Irreplaceable land bank in Thane creates high barrier to entry:** The company owns ~100 acres on Pokhran Road, Thane, with ~60 acres remaining for future development. At conservative market rates of Rs. 23-24 Cr/acre, the residual land bank alone is worth ~Rs. 1,400 Cr, covering 36% of current market cap[^vp-thread]. Proximity to Viviana Mall, Metro Line 4, and the upcoming Thane-Mumbai High Speed Rail (2028) enhances realizations[^ip-latest].
*   **Structural margin expansion via JDA-led asset-light model:** Management is pivoting from capital-intensive owned-land development to Joint Development Agreements (JDAs) in MMR. This reduces upfront land cost, improves ROCE (target >25% vs historical ~15%), and sustains EBITDA margins at 20%+ despite revenue scaling[^cc-transcript]. Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin of 22.5% validates the mix shift[^ip-latest].
*   **Diversification into high-growth MMR micro-markets via JDAs:** The "Ten X" platform is targeting 4-5 new JDA launches in FY27 across BKC 2, Wadala, and Thane West, adding ~4 msf of saleable area without balance sheet loading[^vp-thread]. The recently incorporated wholly-owned subsidiary Ten X Mahalaxmi Ltd (Aug 2026) will house the BKC 2 JDA, ring-fencing project risk[^ar-fy25].
*   **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (1) Launch of BKC 2 (Q3 FY27) and Wadala (Q4 FY27) projects — high-ticket MMR assets with >Rs. 30,000/psf pricing power[^cc-transcript]; (2) Potential MSCI/Nifty Smallcap index inclusion post 1-year listing anniversary (Jul 2026) could trigger passive inflows[^vp-thread]; (3) Monetization of remaining Thane inventory (Ten X Era/Prime) at rising price tiers[^ip-latest].
*   **Biggest structural risk: Execution dependency on JDA partners and regulatory approvals in MMR.** The asset-light model transfers construction risk but introduces partner counter-party risk and approval delays (environment, MHADA, airport height restrictions). Any slippage in the 4-5 targeted JDA launches would defer the revenue inflection to FY28[^ar-fy25]. Promoter pledging at 0% currently but warrants monitoring given group leverage[^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):** Thane Owned Projects ~82% (Rs. 1,897 Cr), JDA/Other ~18% (Rs. 416 Cr). FY27E guidance implies JDA share rising to 35-40% as BKC 2/Wadala revenue kicks in[^ip-latest].

MMR JDAs: BKC 2 (0.6 msf, launch Q3 FY27), Wadala (0.9 msf, launch Q4 FY27). End customers are largely self-employed professionals and corporate employees from Thane/Mumbai[^cc-transcript].

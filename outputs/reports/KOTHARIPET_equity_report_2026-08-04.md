### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat — Domestic PIB Oligopoly:** Kothari Petrochemicals (KPL) is the largest producer of Polyisobutylene (PIB) compounds in India, with a dominant domestic market share of ~60% pre-COVID, scaling to 80–90% post-COVID as global peers (BASF, TPC Group, DAELIM, Chinese players) faced raw material disruption [^vp-thread]. Globally, KPL is among the top PIB producers addressing a niche ~USD 2 Bn global market, with limited competitive intensity and minimal dumping risk [^vp-thread].

- **Structural Margin Story — Vertical Integration & Pricing Power:** EBITDA margins expanded materially to ~24% in FY25 (vs. ~16–18% pre-FY23), driven by (i) value-added HR-PIB mix, (ii) formula-linked pricing arrangements now covering the customer book, and (iii) utility cost-pass-through clauses embedded in contracts [^vp-thread]. Gross margin per tonne has compounded at high single digits over FY21–FY25, demonstrating pricing power in a B2B specialty chemicals context.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies — HR-PIB & Non-Auto End Markets:** KPL commissioned a Rs. 6 Cr pilot plant at Manali for HR-PIB (High Reactivity PIB) in FY24, enabling entry into lubricants, fuel additives, adhesives, cosmetics, plastic masterbatch and tire sealants [^vp-thread]. This reduces dependence on the cyclical 2T engine oil segment; non-auto applications (rubber, adhesives, masterbatch) are de-risking the revenue base and offer higher per-kg realisation premium [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Ramp-up of HR-PIB commercial volumes post pilot validation — incremental Rs. 30–50 Cr revenue potential at superior margins; (ii) Raw material supply normalisation confirmed by the company's exchange filing on 02 Jul 2026, removing a key overhang [^ar-fy25]; (iii) Re-rating trigger from credit rating reaffirmation (filed 13 Jul 2026); (iv) Potential AGM 14 Aug 2026 commentary on capex roadmap and dividend policy [^ar-fy25].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — Raw Material Volatility & Import Dependence:** PIB is derived from C4 hydrocarbon streams available only at refineries, naphtha crackers and MTBE crackers — all crude derivatives [^vp-thread]. Raw material availability remains the single biggest risk; peers have historically shut plants due to feedstock unavailability. While KPL has formula-linked pricing and long-term offtake arrangements, a sharp spike in C4 spreads or a domestic cracker shutdown could compress margins by 200–300 bps in any given quarter.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Kothari Petrochemicals Limited (KPL) manufactures Polyisobutylene (PIB) compounds and derivatives — a specialty synthetic rubber/polymer — used primarily as a base stock in two-stroke (2T) engine oils, lubricants, and increasingly in adhesives, rubber modifications, plastic masterbatch, cosmetics, and sealants [^ar-fy25]. KPL operates a single-site integrated manufacturing model centred at Manali, Chennai, with downstream compounding capability.

**Revenue Split by Division (Estimated FY25):**
- Conventional PIB (Medium/Low Molecular Weight) — Lubricants & 2T Oils: ~65–70% of revenue [^vp-thread]
- High Reactivity PIB (HR-PIB) & Specialty Derivatives: ~15–20% (ramping from a low base post Manali pilot) [^vp-thread]
- Other Industrial Applications (Adhesives, Rubber, Masterbatch): ~10–15% [^vp-thread]

**Key OEM/End Customers:** KPL primarily serves B2B industrial customers — lube blenders, automotive lubricant marketers (2T segment), adhesive formulators, rubber compounders, and masterbatch producers. Customer concentration is moderate with top-10 customers contributing an estimated ~55–60% of revenue; the company does not disclose specific customer names given confidentiality clauses [^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary Structure:** KPL operates with no material subsidiaries as of FY25; the company is a focused single-entity specialty chemicals manufacturer. There are no listed JVs, though management has alluded to evaluating strategic partnerships for HR-PIB commercialisation [^cc-transcript].

**Manufacturing Footprint:** Single integrated plant at Manali (Chennai), Tamil Nadu, with commissioned pilot capacity for HR-PIB. Total installed PIB capacity is estimated at 8,000–10,000 MTPA (medium-high molecular weight) [^vp-thread]. The recent restoration of raw material supply to normal levels (02 Jul 2026 filing) confirms Manali operations are running at normalised utilisation.

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** KPL has no identifiable promoter group as per shareholding disclosures (Promoter holding 0%) [^ar-fy25]. The company is professionally managed, with the founder Kothari family historically associated with the South Indian chemicals trading and manufacturing ecosystem. The board comprises independent directors and professional management with sectoral expertise in petrochemicals and lubricants.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1. **Differentiated platform-led model with structural moat:** Persistent has successfully pivoted from a pure-play IT services vendor to a software-driven, platform-led organization. The ~18% IP-led revenue mix (vs. <10% historically) creates stickier revenue, deeper client integration, and materially higher switching costs — a key competitive moat versus pure-play peers[^ip-latest].

2. **Margin expansion story intact despite wage headwinds:** Q1 FY26 EBITDA margin sustained at ~16-17% even as the company invested in AI/GenAI talent and ramped up senior hires[^cc-transcript]. Subcontractor rationalization, pyramid optimization, and GenAI-led productivity (targeted 15-20% efficiency gains on existing projects) underwrite a 100-150 bps structural margin uplift over FY26-28E[^ip-latest].

3. **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond core BFSI, the company has built meaningful exposure to Healthcare (~$200M+ revenue), and emerging verticals like Retail/Consumer and Hi-Tech[^ar-fy25]. Strategic partnerships with hyperscalers (Microsoft, AWS, Google, Oracle) for GenAI-led modernization initiatives position the company as a beneficiary of the SMAC + AI capex cycle[^ip-latest].

4. **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (a) Sustained large-deal TCV momentum — Q4 FY25 TCV of ~$640M (multi-year high) provides revenue visibility for FY27E[^cc-transcript]; (b) Improvement in sub-contractor mix (targeted to drop below 10% from ~14%) driving utilization gains[^cc-transcript]; (c) Expansion of the AI/GenAI portfolio with hyperscaler co-sell motions expected to drive incremental wallet share; (d) Anticipated improvement in top-tier BFSI tech spending cycle from H2 FY27 onwards[^ip-latest].

5. **Biggest structural risk:** Disproportionate exposure to BFSI (~33% of revenue) and the broader deal cycle remains the most significant risk — any prolonged BFSI tech spending freeze (as witnessed in FY23-24) would disproportionately impact Persistent versus more diversified peers[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Persistent Systems Limited is a Pune-headquartered global IT services company specializing in digital transformation, enterprise modernization, and product engineering services. The company operates a hybrid delivery model — combining traditional IT services with proprietary IP-led platforms and accelerators — across BFSI, Healthcare, Consumer/Retail, and Hi-Tech verticals[^ip-latest].

**Revenue mix by segment:**

- **BFSI:** ~33% of revenue — largest vertical, anchored by long-tenured relationships with US and European financial institutions[^ar-fy25]
- **Healthcare:** ~20-22% of revenue — among the highest mix among Indian IT peers, anchored by life-sciences and provider relationships[^ip-latest]
- **Consumer & Retail / Hi-Tech:** ~25-27% of revenue — including emerging software platform players[^ar-fy25]
- **Other verticals (Tech/SIP):** ~18-20% — including emerging verticals and Tech OEM engagements[^ip-latest]

**Revenue mix by service line:** ~82% Services / ~18% IP-led platforms — the IP segment is targeted to scale to ~25% over the medium term[^ip-latest].

**Key OEM/end customers per vertical:**
- BFSI: Multiple large US banks, capital markets firms; long-tenured relationships (5+ years)[^ar-fy25]
- Healthcare: Life sciences and provider clients in the US/EU; partnerships with hyperscalers[^ip-latest]
- Tech/SIP: Multiple silicon, software platform, and product engineering clients[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary structure:** Persistent operates through multiple subsidiaries including Persistent Systems Inc. (US), Persistent Systems Pte Ltd. (Singapore), Persistent Systems (UK) Ltd., and acquired entities including Data Glove Inc., Sureline Inc., and Youperience Inc. (France) — a wholly-owned subsidiary[^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing/delivery footprint:** Persistent operates a global delivery model with India centers in Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Nagpur, Goa, and Indore, plus near-shore/off-shore delivery centres in the US, UK, Netherlands, and Japan[^ar-fy25].

**Promoter background:** Founded in 1990 by **Dr. Anand Deshpande** (Founder, Chairman, and Managing Director) — Persistent was conceived as a product engineering services company with deep academic roots (Dr. Deshpande holds a PhD and is a published researcher). The promoter group holds ~32.92% as a stable, founder-led entity[^ar-fy25].

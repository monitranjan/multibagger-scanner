### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.  **Market Leadership & First-Mover Advantage in E-Bus Segment:** Olectra Greentech holds a dominant position in India's nascent electric bus market, driven by its strategic partnership with BYD. The company has successfully secured and executed large orders from various state transport undertakings (STUs) across India, including Telangana, Maharashtra, and Karnataka. This early mover advantage, coupled with proven technology and operational experience, creates a significant entry barrier for new players and establishes Olectra as a preferred vendor. The company's robust order book of over 8,000 e-buses (as of Q4FY24) provides strong revenue visibility for the next 3-4 years.
2.  **Structural Margin Expansion driven by Localization & Scale:** The company is strategically focused on increasing localization of components, moving from a CKD (Completely Knocked Down) model to a higher domestic value addition. This, coupled with rising production volumes and economies of scale from its new manufacturing facility at Seetharampur, is expected to drive significant EBITDA margin expansion. We project EBITDA margins to improve by 200-250 bps over FY25-FY27, as the company reduces reliance on imported parts and optimizes its supply chain.
3.  **Diversification into High-Growth Electric Truck & Construction Equipment:** Olectra is strategically expanding beyond electric buses into other high-growth EV adjacencies. The company has launched India's first heavy-duty electric tipper truck and is actively exploring opportunities in electric light commercial vehicles (LCVs) and construction equipment. This diversification leverages its core EV powertrain expertise and existing manufacturing infrastructure, opening up new revenue streams and reducing dependence on the potentially cyclical e-bus market. The partnership with BYD provides access to proven EV technology across these segments.
4.  **Near-Term Catalysts: Order Book Execution & New Facility Ramp-up:** Key near-term catalysts (6-12 months) include accelerated execution of its substantial e-bus order book, particularly from the CESL (Convergence Energy Services Limited) tenders and various STU orders. The successful ramp-up of production at its state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Seetharampur (with an initial capacity of 5,000 units/annum, scalable to 10,000) will be crucial for meeting delivery timelines and improving operational efficiency. Furthermore, any new large order wins, especially in the e-truck segment, would provide significant positive impetus.
5.  **Biggest Structural Risk: Intense Competition & Policy Dependency:** The biggest structural risk for Olectra is the intensifying competition in the Indian EV market, particularly from established players like Tata Motors and Ashok Leyland, and new entrants. Aggressive bidding by competitors could pressure margins and market share. Additionally, the company's growth is highly dependent on government policies and subsidies (e.g., FAME-II, state EV policies). Any withdrawal or reduction in these incentives could significantly impact the demand for electric buses and the profitability of the company.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Olectra Greentech Ltd (formerly Goldstone Infratech Ltd) is a pioneer in India's electric mobility space, primarily engaged in the manufacturing and sale of electric buses and, more recently, electric tipper trucks. The company operates on an OEM model, leveraging technology from its strategic partner, BYD Auto Industry Co. Ltd. (China). It also provides after-sales service, charging infrastructure solutions, and comprehensive annual maintenance contracts (AMCs) for its electric buses, ensuring long-term revenue streams.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   Electric Buses & Related Services: ~95%
    *   Electric Trucks & Others: ~5% (emerging segment, expected to grow)
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Electric Buses:** Primarily various State Transport Undertakings (STUs) across India, including Telangana State Road Transport Corporation (TSRTC), Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation (MSRTC), Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC), Kerala State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC), Himachal Road Transport Corporation (HRTC), and Convergence Energy Services Limited (CESL) for inter-state and city operations.
    *   **Electric Trucks:** Currently targeting specific industrial and mining clients for its heavy-duty electric tipper trucks.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Olectra Greentech has a relatively lean subsidiary structure. Its key operational entity is the parent company itself, with no significant listed or unlisted subsidiaries impacting core operations.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** The company operates its primary manufacturing facility in India.
    *   **Telangana:** Seetharampur, Hyderabad. This is a greenfield facility with a current installed capacity of 5,000 electric buses per annum, scalable to 10,000 units. This modern plant is crucial for meeting its expanding order book and future growth ambitions.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Olectra Greentech is part of the MEIL (Megha Engineering & Infrastructures Ltd) Group, a diversified infrastructure conglomerate with interests in irrigation, power, hydrocarbons, transportation, and manufacturing. The promoters, led by Mr. K.V.R. Kishore, have a strong engineering and project execution background. The MEIL group's financial strength and project management expertise provide a robust backing for Olectra's ambitious growth plans in the EV sector. The group's long-term vision for sustainable mobility underpins Olectra's strategic direction.

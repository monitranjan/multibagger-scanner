### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Niche Market Leadership:** RBZ Jewellers occupies a specialized position in the gold jewelry market, focusing on "Antique Gold Jewellery" with a strong B2B presence, serving as a preferred supplier for national and regional retail chains.
* **Margin Expansion Potential:** The company’s shift toward increasing its B2C retail footprint (Harit Zaveri brand) is expected to drive margin expansion, as retail sales command higher premiums compared to the wholesale-heavy legacy business.
* **Asset-Light Scalability:** The business model relies on a well-established network of artisans and Karigars, providing flexibility in production without the heavy overheads associated with massive captive manufacturing units.
* **Near-Term Catalyst:** The ongoing expansion of the Harit Zaveri showroom network in Gujarat and entry into new geographies are expected to drive top-line growth in FY27E and FY28E.
* **Structural Risk:** High concentration of revenue from the B2B segment and the inherent volatility in gold prices pose risks to inventory valuation and working capital cycles.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Core Model:** RBZ operates as a dual-model entity: B2B wholesale (supplying to major retail brands) and B2C retail (under the Harit Zaveri brand).
* **Revenue Split:** Approximately 85-90% of revenue is derived from the B2B segment, with the remaining 10-15% coming from the B2C retail segment.
* **Manufacturing/Footprint:** The company operates a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, which serves as the hub for its design and production activities.
* **Promoter Context:** The company is led by the Zaveri family, who bring decades of experience in the jewelry industry, focusing on design-led innovation and antique craftsmanship.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
* **Industry Overview:** The Indian jewelry industry is highly fragmented, with a shift toward organized players driven by GST implementation and hallmarking mandates.
* **Competitive Moat:**

    * **Scale:** Moderate; significant in the regional wholesale space but nascent in national retail.
    * **Pricing Power:** Moderate; tied to gold price fluctuations and labor premiums.

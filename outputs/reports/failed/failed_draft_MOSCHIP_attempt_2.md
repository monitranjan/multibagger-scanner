### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Strategic Semiconductor Focus:** Moschip is a pure-play semiconductor design services company, capitalizing on the massive shift toward domestic fabless design. It holds a unique position as a key partner for government-backed initiatives like the DLI (Design Linked Incentive) scheme.
* **Structural Margin Expansion:** As the company transitions from low-margin legacy staffing/services to high-value IP-led ASIC design and Turnkey solutions, we anticipate an improvement in EBITDA margins from current levels toward a 15–18% band by FY27E.
* **Diversification into High-Growth Verticals:** The company is aggressively scaling its presence in Automotive (ADAS, Infotainment) and Industrial IoT, reducing reliance on legacy communication projects and diversifying its client base toward Tier-1 global OEMs.
* **Near-term Catalysts:** Execution of recent multi-year orders in the networking and connectivity space, coupled with potential breakthroughs in silicon design for domestic industrial hardware, will drive revenue growth in the 6–12 month horizon.
* **Structural Risk:** Execution risk remains the primary concern, specifically regarding talent retention in the high-cost engineering domain and the potential for project delays in complex ASIC development cycles which could impact cash flow visibility.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Business Model:** Moschip operates as a semiconductor design services firm, providing Turnkey ASIC/SoC development, RTL design, Verification, and Physical Design services. It also offers embedded systems and software development.
* **Revenue Split:** ASIC/Semiconductor Design Services (~70%), Embedded Systems/Software (~30%).
* **Customer Base:** Serves global semiconductor companies, automotive OEMs, and defense/industrial conglomerates. Major focus on "Fabless" chip companies needing end-to-end design support.
* **Manufacturing/Footprint:** Headquarters in Hyderabad, with design centers across India (Bangalore, Pune). The company operates as a design-house (fabless), leveraging global foundries for production.
* **Promoter Context:** The company has seen increased involvement from the promoter group post-strategic restructuring. It is increasingly viewed as a key strategic asset in India's sovereign semiconductor roadmap.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Market Leadership:** CEAT maintains a dominant position in the domestic 2W/3W segment and is rapidly expanding its footprint in the high-margin SUV/Passenger Vehicle (PV) replacement market, leveraging its strong brand recall and distribution network.
* **Structural Margin Story:** Shift in product mix towards premiumization (high-rim size tires) and operating leverage from recent capacity expansions (Chennai/Nagpur) are expected to drive EBITDA margins toward the 14–15% band by FY27E.
* **Diversification:** Strategic focus on the OHT (Off-Highway Tires) export market provides a hedge against domestic cyclicality, with CEAT targeting high-growth specialty segments in Europe and North America.
* **Near-term Catalysts:** Softening trend in key raw material indices (natural rubber and crude-linked derivatives) combined with price hikes taken in FY26 are expected to expand gross margins by 150–200 bps in the next 12 months.
* **Structural Risk:** The primary risk remains high volatility in raw material prices and potential demand destruction in the CV segment if industrial activity decelerates, impacting the high-margin TBR (Truck & Bus Radial) volume.

---

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Business Model:** CEAT is a leading Indian tire manufacturer with a diversified portfolio spanning 2W, 3W, PV, CV, and OHT segments. Revenue is balanced between OEM supply and the higher-margin Replacement market (approx. 65% of revenue).
* **Revenue Split:** 2W/3W (40%), Passenger Vehicles (30%), Truck & Bus/CV (25%), and Specialty/OHT (5%).
* **Manufacturing:** Operates 6 major manufacturing facilities across India (Mumbai, Nashik, Nagpur, Halol, Chennai, Ambernath) with a total capacity exceeding 1,200 metric tonnes per day.
* **Group Context:** Part of the RPG Group, led by Harsh Goenka. The group provides strong corporate governance oversight and financial backing, ensuring long-term capital stability.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
* **TAM:** The Indian tire industry is valued at approx. Rs. 85,000 Cr, growing at a 7–9% CAGR, supported by rising vehicle penetration and infrastructure-led CV demand.
* **Competitive Moat:**
    * **Switching Costs (Strong):** High loyalty in the replacement market due to safety-critical nature of tires.
    * **Scale (Strong):** Massive distribution network of 4,000+ dealers and 500+ exclusive showrooms.
    * **Pricing Power (Moderate):** Limited by competitive intensity from MRF, Apollo, and JK Tyre, though brand strength allows for premium positioning.

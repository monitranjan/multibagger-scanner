### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** Gokaldas Exports (GEX) is one of India's largest apparel manufacturers and exporters, with a strong focus on woven garments. Its integrated manufacturing capabilities, from fabric to finished product, coupled with long-standing relationships with global marquee clients such as Gap, H&M, Levi's, and Columbia, provide a significant competitive moat. The ability to cater to diverse product categories (e.g., trousers, shirts, dresses, activewear) and an established global supply chain network enhance its stickiness with customers, making switching costs high for large volume orders.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** GEX is executing a strategy to improve operating margins (EBITDA%) through several levers. This includes increasing the share of value-added products, enhancing operational efficiencies through automation and lean manufacturing principles, and optimizing its raw material sourcing strategy. The recent acquisition of Atraco Group (effective Q3FY24) is expected to contribute to margin expansion by leveraging backward integration and optimizing the supply chain for knitwear, potentially adding 50-75 bps to overall EBITDA margins over the next 2-3 years as synergies materialize.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The acquisition of Atraco Group marks a strategic diversification into knitwear manufacturing, a segment with high growth potential and complementary to GEX's existing woven strength. This expands GEX's product portfolio and allows it to cater to a broader range of customer requirements, including activewear and athleisure, which are high-growth categories globally. Furthermore, the company is actively exploring opportunities in technical textiles and higher-margin specialized apparel, which could unlock new revenue streams and improve profitability.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the full integration and realization of synergies from the Atraco acquisition, which is expected to boost revenue and profitability from FY25 onwards. A potential rebound in global apparel demand, particularly from key markets like the US and Europe, driven by easing inflationary pressures and improved consumer sentiment, would directly benefit GEX. Additionally, ongoing government support through schemes like the PLI scheme for Textiles and Apparel, which GEX is a beneficiary of, will aid in capacity expansion and cost competitiveness.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk for GEX is its high dependence on export markets, particularly North America and Europe, making it vulnerable to global macroeconomic downturns, geopolitical tensions, and currency fluctuations. Intense competition from other low-cost manufacturing hubs like Bangladesh and Vietnam, coupled with evolving trade policies and tariffs, could impact its pricing power and order book. Furthermore, any significant disruption in the global supply chain or adverse changes in fashion trends could negatively affect demand.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Gokaldas Exports Limited (GEX) is a leading Indian apparel manufacturer and exporter, primarily focused on woven garments. The company operates as a complete solution provider, offering design, development, manufacturing, and logistics services to its global clientele.

*   **Core Business Model:** GEX operates on a "cut-make-trim" (CMT) and "free-on-board" (FOB) model. It manufactures a wide range of apparel products for leading international brands and retailers. The business model emphasizes efficient production, quality control, and timely delivery to meet the demanding requirements of global fashion cycles.

*   **Subsidiary Structure:** GEX primarily operates through its wholly-owned subsidiaries, including Gokaldas Images Private Limited. The recent acquisition of Atraco Group involved acquiring its subsidiaries, further expanding the global manufacturing footprint.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** GEX has a robust manufacturing presence across South India.
    *   **States:** Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh.
    *   **Plant Count:** Over 20 manufacturing facilities, including the recently acquired units from Atraco Group. These facilities are equipped with modern machinery and adhere to international compliance standards.

The current promoter, Clear Wealth Consultancy Services LLP, acquired a majority stake in 2020. The management team has extensive experience in the apparel manufacturing and export industry, with a focus on operational excellence and global client relationship management. The group aims to leverage India's position as a preferred sourcing destination for apparel globally.

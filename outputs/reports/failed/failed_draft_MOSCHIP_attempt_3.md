### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Strategic Position in Semiconductor Design:** Moschip is a pure-play semiconductor and system design services company. Its moat lies in its deep expertise in ASIC/SoC design, which is highly sought after as India pivots toward becoming a global semiconductor hub under the ISM (India Semiconductor Mission).
* **Margin Expansion via High-Value IP:** The company is shifting from low-margin hardware staffing to high-margin intellectual property (IP) and turnkey ASIC design services. We expect EBITDA margins to expand by 250–300 bps over FY25-FY27 as the product-mix shifts toward complex 5nm/7nm node designs.
* **Diversification into High-Growth Verticals:** Moschip is aggressively expanding into industrial, automotive, and networking segments. Its recent work in SerDes (Serializer/Deserializer) and high-speed interface IP provides a recurring revenue stream via licensing, reducing dependency on cyclical service contracts.
* **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Anticipated conversion of the large order book from Tier-1 global semiconductor OEMs and potential government-backed incentives under the Design Linked Incentive (DLI) scheme are expected to drive revenue acceleration in H2 FY27.
* **Structural Risk:** High client concentration remains a primary risk, with a significant portion of revenue derived from top-tier accounts. Any slowdown in global semiconductor capital expenditure or delays in tape-out milestones could lead to volatility in quarterly earnings.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Core Business Model:** The company operates via two primary verticals: Semiconductor Services (ASIC design, RTL design, physical design, and verification) and System Engineering (IoT/embedded solutions).
* **Revenue Split:** Semiconductor Services (~75%) and System Engineering (~25%).
* **Key Customers:** Includes major global semiconductor companies, automotive OEMs, and aerospace/defense contractors (names remain confidential per NDA protocols).
* **Infrastructure:** Operates out of primary R&D centers in Hyderabad and Bengaluru, with a global footprint supporting clients in the US and Europe.
* **Promoter Context:** Led by a management team with deep experience in the silicon design ecosystem; the group has successfully pivoted from legacy board-level design to complex chip-level design services.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
* **Industry Overview:** The global semiconductor design services market is growing at a CAGR of ~15-18%. India’s domestic design market is expected to reach $10B+ by 2030, supported by the DLI and PLI schemes.
* **Competitive Moat:**
    * **Switching Costs (Strong):** Deep integration into customer design cycles creates high stickiness.
    * **Scale (Moderate):** Currently a mid-sized player; competing with global giants like Synopsys or Cadence, but gains price competitiveness through its India-based talent pool.
    * **Pricing Power (Moderate):** Pricing is linked to design complexity; highly specialized IP designs command premium rates.

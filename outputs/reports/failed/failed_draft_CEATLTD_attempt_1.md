### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Market Leadership:** CEAT maintains a dominant position in the 2W and 3W tire segments in India, supported by a strong brand recall and a vast distribution network of over 4,500 dealers.
* **Structural Margin Expansion:** Sustained focus on product mix premiumization (moving toward larger rim sizes in SUVs) and operational efficiencies in the manufacturing process are expected to drive EBITDA margins toward the 14-15% band by FY27E.
* **High-Growth Adjacencies:** Increased penetration into the TBR (Truck and Bus Radial) segment and export markets (Europe/USA) provides a hedge against domestic cyclicality.
* **Near-Term Catalysts:** Strong replacement demand cycle, easing of raw material prices (crude/natural rubber), and increased OEM off-take in the SUV segment are likely to act as key valuation triggers over the next 6–12 months.
* **Structural Risk:** High volatility in raw material costs (rubber and petroleum derivatives) remains the primary risk, as the company faces challenges in passing on sudden cost spikes to price-sensitive 2W/3W consumers.

---

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Business Model:** CEAT is a leading tire manufacturer under the RPG Group. It operates across multiple categories: 2W/3W (approx. 45% of revenue), Passenger Vehicle (30%), and Truck/Bus/Off-Highway Tires (25%).
* **Manufacturing Footprint:** Operates 6 major manufacturing plants located in Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Tamil Nadu, with a strong focus on automation and Industry 4.0 standards.
* **Promoter Context:** Part of the RPG Group, led by Harsh Goenka. The group has a multi-decade legacy in diversified sectors, providing stable capital support and professional governance.
* **Subsidiaries:** CEAT holds significant interests in international distribution arms to facilitate its global export strategy, particularly in emerging markets and high-end replacement segments.

    * Switching Costs: Moderate (Brand loyalty in 2W segment).
    * Scale: Strong (Top-tier manufacturing capacity).
    * Pricing Power: Moderate (High competition from MRF, Apollo, and JK Tyre).

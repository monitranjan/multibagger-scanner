### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Niche Antique Gold Jewellery Focus:** RBZ commands a specialized moat in the "Antique Gold Jewellery" segment, which carries higher margins compared to plain/mass-market gold jewellery, insulating the firm from pure bullion-price competition.
* **B2B-to-B2C Transition:** The company is aggressively scaling its retail presence (Harit Zaveri brand) to capture the higher-margin B2C segment, diversifying away from its historical reliance on pure B2B wholesale distribution.
* **Operating Leverage:** With a stable manufacturing footprint in Ahmedabad, the company is poised to see margin expansion (target 100-150 bps) as the revenue mix shifts toward higher-margin retail sales through FY27E.
* **Near-Term Catalyst:** Scaling of the "Harit Zaveri" retail footprint and the upcoming wedding season demand in Q3/Q4 FY27E are expected to drive volume growth and inventory turnover improvements.
* **Structural Risk:** High concentration of business in the Gujarat region and susceptibility to gold price volatility/regulatory changes in the gems & jewellery sector remain the primary structural headwinds.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Business Model:** RBZ operates as a leading player in the Antique Gold Jewellery manufacturing space, providing a comprehensive range of products including Jadau, Meena, and Kundan work.
* **Revenue Split:** ~85% B2B (Wholesale to large retailers) and ~15% B2C (Direct retail sales via Harit Zaveri store).
* **Manufacturing:** Single, state-of-the-art facility in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, which integrates design, manufacturing, and distribution.
* **Promoter Background:** Led by Rajyesh Gupta, the promoter group has over two decades of experience in the jewellery industry, maintaining strong relationships with pan-India retail chains.
* **Corporate Structure:** RBZ Jewellers Ltd is a standalone entity focusing on high-end craftsmanship, positioning itself as a "jeweller to the jewellers" while expanding its own retail brand.

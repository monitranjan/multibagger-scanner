### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Strategic Semiconductor Focus:** Moschip is one of the few listed Indian pure-play semiconductor and system design companies, providing a unique play on the domestic design-led manufacturing (DLI) theme.
* **Margin Expansion Potential:** As the company shifts from low-margin turnkey services to high-value IP licensing and ASIC design, we expect EBITDA margins to expand by 300-400 bps over FY25-FY27E as operating leverage kicks in.
* **Diversification into High-Growth Verticals:** Strong traction in AI-driven data centers, automotive electronics, and industrial IoT provides a massive TAM expansion, moving away from legacy consumer electronic dependencies.
* **Near-term Catalysts:** Execution of recent wins in the government-backed DLI schemes and expected ramp-up in the semiconductor turnkey business are likely to act as key stock price triggers over the next 6-12 months.
* **Structural Risk:** High client concentration with top 5 customers accounting for a significant revenue share; any delay in tape-outs or project cancellations could lead to volatile quarterly earnings.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Core Business:** Semiconductor design services (RTL design, physical design, verification) and turnkey ASIC solutions.
* **Revenue Split:** Semiconductor Design Services (~70%), Turnkey ASIC/System Design (~30%).
* **Key Customers:** Global Tier-1 semiconductor companies, automotive OEMs, and networking equipment providers.
* **Manufacturing/Operations:** Headquartered in Hyderabad with design centers in Bangalore, Pune, and Ahmedabad.
* **Group Context:** Led by CEO Srinivasa Kakumanu; the company has repositioned itself from a legacy IoT player to a full-stack semiconductor services firm, aligning with the "Make in India" semiconductor mission.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
* **TAM:** The global semiconductor design services market is expected to grow at a CAGR of ~15-18% through 2030, with India’s share increasing due to cost-effective engineering talent.
* **Competitive Moat:**
    * **Switching Costs (Strong):** Deep integration into customer product development cycles makes switching vendors costly.
    * **Scale (Moderate):** Currently mid-sized; scaling is required to compete with global giants like Synopsys or Cadence-led service arms.
    * **Pricing Power (Moderate):** High-end specialized IP provides pricing power, but commoditized design services face margin pressure.

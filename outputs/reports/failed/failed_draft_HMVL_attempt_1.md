### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** HMVL benefits from a strong regional leadership position in the Hindi print media segment, particularly in key states like Bihar, Jharkhand, and Uttarakhand, through its flagship "Hindustan" newspaper. This regional dominance, coupled with deep local content creation and distribution networks, creates a significant entry barrier and high brand recall, fostering subscriber loyalty and advertiser stickiness. The established trust and reach provide a sustainable competitive moat.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** Despite the structural headwinds facing print media, HMVL has demonstrated resilience in maintaining and improving operating margins through stringent cost control measures, particularly in newsprint consumption and printing efficiencies. Further margin expansion is expected from increasing digital advertising revenue, which typically carries higher margins, and a calibrated approach to subscription price hikes in core markets. We anticipate EBITDA margin expansion of ~50-75 bps over FY22-FY24 levels by FY27E.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** HMVL is strategically expanding its digital footprint through initiatives like Live Hindustan and various regional news portals, aiming to capture the growing digital advertising market. While still a smaller portion of revenue, this diversification provides a crucial growth avenue and hedges against the long-term decline in traditional print readership. Investments in local content and hyper-localization are key to scaling these digital offerings.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include a potential rebound in advertising expenditure post-elections, especially from government and corporate sectors, which typically benefit regional print media. Additionally, the ongoing focus on cost optimization and the gradual increase in digital subscriber base and ad revenue contribution are expected to drive earnings growth. Any positive regulatory changes or government spending on public awareness campaigns could also provide a boost.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk remains the long-term decline in print readership and advertising revenue due to the shift towards digital media. While HMVL is diversifying, the pace of digital revenue growth may not fully offset the decline in print, impacting overall revenue growth and profitability in the long run. Intense competition from digital news platforms and social media for audience attention and advertising spend also poses a significant threat.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Hindustan Media Ventures Limited (HMVL) is primarily engaged in the publication of newspapers and periodicals, and the provision of related services. Its core business revolves around the print media segment, with a strong focus on Hindi-language publications.

*   **Core Business Model:** HMVL generates revenue primarily through advertising sales in its print publications and, to a lesser extent, through circulation revenue from the sale of newspapers. The company also derives revenue from its digital platforms and other allied services.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (% of revenue) (FY24A):**
    *   Print Media (Newspaper & Periodicals): ~95-97%
    *   Digital & Others: ~3-5%

*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Print Media:** Advertisers include a diverse mix of government agencies, corporate clients (FMCG, auto, real estate, education), and local businesses. Readers constitute the end customers for circulation revenue.
    *   **Digital:** Advertisers on digital platforms, primarily programmatic and direct advertisers.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** HMVL operates primarily through its standalone entity. It is a part of the HT Media Group.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** HMVL has a robust printing infrastructure across several states in Northern and Eastern India, primarily in Bihar, Jharkhand, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and Delhi-NCR. The company operates multiple printing plants to ensure timely distribution and regional coverage. Specific plant count details are generally not publicly disclosed but are strategically located to serve its key markets.

HT Media Group has interests spanning print, radio (Fever FM), and digital media, providing HMVL with strategic synergies and a strong brand lineage within the Indian media landscape.

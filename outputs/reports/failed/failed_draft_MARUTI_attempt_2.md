### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** Maruti Suzuki India (MSIL) maintains an unassailable market leadership in the Indian passenger vehicle (PV) segment with a ~42% market share (FY26E), driven by its extensive sales and service network (over 4,000 touchpoints), strong brand recall, and deep understanding of Indian consumer preferences for fuel efficiency and low maintenance. Its cost-efficient manufacturing capabilities and robust vendor ecosystem create significant entry barriers for new players, reinforcing its competitive moat.
*   **Structural Margin Story through Premiumization & Mix Shift:** MSIL is actively pursuing a premiumization strategy, increasing its focus on higher-margin SUV segments (e.g., Grand Vitara, Fronx, Brezza) and premium NEXA channel vehicles. This shift in product mix, coupled with a higher share of CNG vehicles (which command better margins than entry-level petrol variants) and improved realization from feature-rich models, is expected to drive a structural expansion in EBITDA margins by 150-200 bps over FY27E-FY29E, moving towards a sustainable ~12.5-13% range.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (EVs & Exports):** MSIL is strategically investing in the Electric Vehicle (EV) segment with its first EV model, eVX, slated for launch in FY25E, followed by a pipeline of 5-6 models by FY30E. This foray into EVs, supported by parent Suzuki's global EV strategy and localization efforts, positions MSIL to capture a share of India's rapidly growing EV market. Furthermore, MSIL is leveraging its manufacturing prowess to significantly scale up exports, particularly to Africa, Latin America, and ASEAN markets, aiming to achieve 750,000 units by FY30E, diversifying revenue streams and de-risking domestic market reliance.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** The upcoming launch of the eVX in H2 FY25E is a significant near-term catalyst, expected to generate strong consumer interest and establish MSIL's presence in the EV space. Additionally, a robust monsoon forecast for 2026 is likely to boost rural demand, a key segment for MSIL, driving volume growth. Continued strong demand for its SUV portfolio (Grand Vitara, Fronx) and new model refreshes will also underpin sales performance.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The most significant structural risk is the intense and increasing competition in the Indian PV market, particularly from aggressive new entrants and established players in the SUV and EV segments. Sustaining market share leadership and pricing power amidst this fierce competition, especially as new technologies emerge, poses a long-term challenge. Failure to innovate rapidly and adapt to changing consumer preferences for advanced features and powertrains could erode its competitive edge.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL) is India's largest passenger vehicle manufacturer, engaged in the complete value chain from designing, manufacturing, and selling passenger cars, utility vehicles, and vans, to providing related services like financing, driving schools, and spare parts.

*   **Core Business Model:** MSIL's core business revolves around mass-market and premium passenger vehicles, with a strong focus on fuel efficiency, low cost of ownership, and extensive after-sales service. The company operates through two primary sales channels: ARENA for its mass-market models and NEXA for its premium offerings.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY26E Projections):**
    *   Passenger Vehicles (Hatchbacks, Sedans, Vans): ~60%
    *   Utility Vehicles (SUVs, MUVs): ~35%
    *   Spare Parts, Accessories, and Services: ~5%
    *(Note: Exact historical split by vehicle segment is not publicly disclosed, but this reflects current strategic focus and volume trends.)*
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Passenger Vehicles:** Direct sales to individual consumers, fleet operators, and government agencies. Key models include Alto, Swift, Baleno, WagonR, Dzire, Celerio.
    *   **Utility Vehicles:** Direct sales to individual consumers. Key models include Brezza, Grand Vitara, Fronx, Ertiga, XL6.
    *   **Spare Parts, Accessories, and Services:** Network of authorized service stations and spare part dealers catering to MSIL vehicle owners.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** MSIL has a lean subsidiary structure. Key subsidiaries include:
    *   **Maruti Suzuki Insurance Broking Private Limited:** Provides motor insurance services.
    *   **Maruti Suzuki India Service Limited:** Focuses on service and parts operations.
    *   **SMG (Suzuki Motor Gujarat Private Limited):** While not a direct subsidiary, SMG is a 100% subsidiary of Suzuki Motor Corporation (Japan) and acts as a contract manufacturer for MSIL, producing vehicles like Baleno, Swift, Dzire, and some SUV models. This arrangement ensures dedicated capacity and operational efficiency for MSIL.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** MSIL operates large-scale manufacturing facilities across India:
    *   **Gurgaon, Haryana:** Two plants (Plant 1 & Plant 2)
    *   **Manesar, Haryana:** Three plants (Plant 3, Plant 4 & Plant 5)
    *   **Hansalpur, Gujarat:** Suzuki Motor Gujarat (SMG) operates three plants (A, B & C) dedicated to producing vehicles for MSIL.
    *   Total plant count: 8 operational plants (5 directly by MSIL, 3 by SMG).
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Maruti Suzuki India Limited is a subsidiary of Suzuki Motor Corporation, Japan. Suzuki Motor Corporation holds a 60.28% stake in MSIL. The company was established in 1981 as a joint venture between the Government of India and Suzuki Motor Corporation. Suzuki took majority control in 2002. The promoter, Suzuki Motor Corporation, is a globally renowned Japanese multinational corporation specializing in automobiles, motorcycles, and outboard motors, bringing significant R&D, technological expertise, and global best practices to MSIL.

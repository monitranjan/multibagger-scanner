### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Niche B2B Leadership:** RBZ Jewellers commands a dominant position in the antique gold jewellery segment, serving as a preferred supplier to large national retail chains. This B2B-heavy model ensures stable offtake and reduces the high customer acquisition costs typical of B2C jewellery retail.
* **Structural Margin Expansion:** As the company transitions toward a higher mix of B2C retail (Harit Zaveri brand) and branded jewellery, operating margins are expected to expand by 150-200 bps by FY27E due to superior pricing power and lower inventory carrying costs compared to traditional wholesalers.
* **Adjacency Growth:** Strategic expansion into the gold loan sector and diversification of retail footprints in Western India provides a high-growth runway, leveraging the company’s existing gold inventory management expertise.
* **Near-term Catalysts:** Planned expansion of the 'Harit Zaveri' retail footprint (adding 2-3 new showrooms by FY27E) and expected improvement in inventory turnover ratio following the integration of advanced ERP systems are key triggers for the next 6-12 months.
* **Structural Risk:** High concentration in the antique jewellery segment makes the company sensitive to shifts in consumer fashion trends. Additionally, any volatility in gold prices impacting the B2B order book remains a primary structural threat.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Business Model:** Primarily a B2B manufacturer and wholesaler of antique gold jewellery, with a growing B2C retail vertical under the brand "Harit Zaveri".
* **Revenue Split:** B2B Wholesale (~85%), B2C Retail (~15%).
* **Key Customers:** Supplies to major pan-India jewellery retail chains; specific client names include leading national organized retailers.
* **Manufacturing:** Operates a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, known for intricate antique design craftsmanship.
* **Promoter Context:** Founded by Rajendrakumar Zaveri; the promoter group maintains a strong 78.74% stake, ensuring high alignment with minority shareholders and long-term capital preservation.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
* **TAM:** The Indian jewellery market is estimated at ~$70-75 billion, with the organized segment (where RBZ operates) growing at a CAGR of 12-14%.
* **Policy Tailwinds:** Mandatory Hallmarking (HUID) and PMLA compliance acts as a massive tailwind, forcing unorganized players to consolidate or exit, benefiting established manufacturers like RBZ.
* **Competitive Moat:**
    * **Switching Costs (Strong):** High-end antique jewellery design requires specialized artisans; OEM clients face high switching costs due to the complexity of design replication.
    * **Scale (Moderate):** Significant manufacturing capacity in Ahmedabad provides cost-efficiency, though still smaller than national giants like Titan.
    * **Pricing Power (Moderate):** Limited by the nature of the gold-plus-making-charge model, though brand equity in 'antique' designs allows for premium making charges.

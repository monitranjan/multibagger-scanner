### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Ganesh Benzoplast Ltd. (GBL) is a diversified company primarily engaged in the business of liquid chemical storage and manufacturing of specialty chemicals and food preservatives.

*   **Core Business Model:**
    *   **Liquid Storage Terminals (LST):** GBL operates third-party storage tanks for various bulk liquid chemicals (hazardous and non-hazardous), petrochemicals, and edible oils at major Indian ports. It provides comprehensive storage and handling solutions, including drumming, heating, and blending services. This segment generates revenue through storage charges based on capacity utilization and duration.
    *   **Chemical Manufacturing:** GBL manufactures a range of specialty chemicals and food preservatives, including Benzoic Acid, Sodium Benzoate, Benzyl Alcohol, and other derivatives. These products cater to industries such as food & beverages, pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, and industrial applications.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   Liquid Storage Terminals: ~60-65% of revenue
    *   Chemicals Manufacturing: ~35-40% of revenue
    (Note: Exact split varies slightly year-on-year based on demand and pricing dynamics.)
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Liquid Storage Terminals:** Major chemical manufacturers, traders, petroleum companies, and food processing companies requiring bulk liquid storage and logistics. Specific customer names are typically under confidentiality agreements, but include large domestic and international players in the chemical and edible oil space.
    *   **Chemicals Manufacturing:** Pharmaceutical companies, food & beverage manufacturers, personal care product companies, and agrochemical firms.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** GBL primarily operates as a standalone entity with no major listed subsidiaries. Any smaller, unlisted subsidiaries are typically for specific project vehicles or operational purposes.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:**
    *   **Liquid Storage Terminals:** Operates facilities at key ports including Mumbai (Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust - JNPT), Kandla (Gujarat), and Goa (Mormugao Port Trust). These locations provide strategic access for import/export and domestic distribution.
    *   **Chemical Manufacturing:** Production facilities are located in close proximity to raw material sources and/or consumption centers, primarily in Maharashtra (e.g., Khopoli).

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Dominant Positioning in 2W/3W:** CEAT maintains a strong moat in the 2W/3W replacement market, holding a significant market share in India, which provides stable cash flows and high-margin recurring revenue.
* **Structural Margin Expansion:** Shift toward premiumization (high-margin SUV and radial tyres) and continuous cost optimization in rubber procurement are driving EBITDA margin expansion toward the 13-14% range.
* **Diversification into OTR & Exports:** Aggressive expansion into Off-the-Road (OTR) tyres and exports to Europe/North America is reducing dependence on the cyclical domestic PV/CV segments.
* **Near-term Catalysts:** Sustained replacement demand in the CV segment and the ramp-up of the Chennai plant (focused on passenger vehicle radials) are expected to drive volume growth in FY27E.
* **Structural Risk:** High sensitivity to crude oil derivatives (carbon black, synthetic rubber) and volatility in natural rubber prices remain the primary margin headwinds.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Business Model:** Primarily engaged in manufacturing automotive tyres, tubes, and flaps. Operates a balanced portfolio across 2W/3W, Passenger Vehicles (PV), and Commercial Vehicles (CV).
* **Revenue Split:** 2W/3W (~40%), PV/SUV (~30%), CV (~25%), Others/OTR (~5%).
* **Manufacturing:** Operates 6 major manufacturing facilities across India (Mumbai, Nashik, Halol, Nagpur, Chennai, and Ambernath).
* **Customer Base:** Strong OEM presence with Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra, and Bajaj Auto.
* **Group Context:** Flagship company of the RPG Group, led by Chairman Harsh Goenka, providing strong institutional pedigree and governance oversight.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Robust Moat:** Maruti Suzuki India (MSIL) maintains an undisputed market leadership in the Indian passenger vehicle (PV) segment with ~42% market share (FY26E). Its extensive sales and service network (over 4,000 touchpoints), strong brand recall, and deep understanding of Indian consumer preferences create a significant competitive moat. The company benefits from a diversified product portfolio across entry-level, mid-segment, and increasingly SUV categories, catering to a wide customer base.
*   **Structural Margin Improvement Story:** MSIL is poised for structural margin expansion driven by a shift in its product mix towards higher-margin SUVs (expected to comprise 35%+ of volumes by FY28E from ~25% in FY24), increasing localization levels, and an improving cost structure. Furthermore, the company's aggressive push into hybrid and CNG vehicles, which command better realizations and offer fuel efficiency benefits, will contribute to a 100-150 bps improvement in EBITDA margins over the next 2-3 years.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (EV & Exports):** MSIL is strategically diversifying into the electric vehicle (EV) segment with its first EV model expected in FY27E, leveraging its partnership with Suzuki Motor Corporation for technology and manufacturing. This positions it to capture growth in a rapidly expanding market. Additionally, the company is aggressively expanding its export footprint, targeting new markets in Africa, Latin America, and Southeast Asia, aiming for exports to contribute 15%+ of total volumes by FY28E, providing a hedge against domestic market cyclicality.
*   **Near-term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include the successful launch of its new generation Swift and Dzire models in H2 FY27E, which are expected to rejuvenate sales in the high-volume compact segment. Further, the sustained momentum in SUV sales, particularly with new models like the Fronx and Grand Vitara gaining traction, will drive volume and revenue growth. Any positive news flow regarding government incentives for hybrid vehicles could also provide an additional boost.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Intensifying Competition & EV Transition Pace:** The biggest structural risk for MSIL is the intensifying competition in the SUV segment from both domestic (e.g., M&M, Tata Motors) and international players (e.g., Hyundai, Kia), which could pressure market share and pricing. Furthermore, a faster-than-anticipated transition to EVs in India, coupled with MSIL's relatively late entry into the segment, poses a long-term risk if it struggles to scale up its EV portfolio competitively against established and new players.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL) is India's largest passenger vehicle manufacturer, engaged in the complete value chain of designing, manufacturing, and selling a wide range of passenger cars, multi-utility vehicles, and vans. The company also sells spare parts, accessories, and offers services such as financing, insurance, and Maruti Driving Schools.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY26E estimates):**
    *   Passenger Vehicles (Cars, Hatchbacks, Sedans, SUVs, Vans): ~95%
    *   Spares, Accessories & Services: ~5%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:** MSIL primarily caters to the retail consumer market in India and increasingly, international markets through exports. Its entire fleet of vehicles is sold under the "Maruti Suzuki" brand.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** MSIL operates directly and through a few key subsidiaries. Notably, Suzuki Motor Gujarat Private Limited (SMG), a 100% subsidiary of Suzuki Motor Corporation, manufactures vehicles exclusively for MSIL, acting as a contract manufacturer and significantly contributing to MSIL's production capacity.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** MSIL has a robust manufacturing footprint primarily in India:
    *   **Gurugram, Haryana:** Oldest and largest plant, manufacturing various models.
    *   **Manesar, Haryana:** Modern facility, producing a range of popular models.
    *   **Hansalpur, Gujarat (Suzuki Motor Gujarat):** State-of-the-art facility dedicated to manufacturing vehicles for MSIL, expanding capacity, particularly for newer models and exports.
    *   Total plant count: 3 major manufacturing complexes with multiple production lines.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** MSIL is a subsidiary of Suzuki Motor Corporation (SMC), Japan, which holds a 58.19% stake. SMC is a globally renowned manufacturer of automobiles, motorcycles, and outboard motors. The promoter group's deep automotive expertise, technological prowess, and commitment to the Indian market have been instrumental in MSIL's success and market dominance.

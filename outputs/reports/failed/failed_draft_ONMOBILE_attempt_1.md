### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Niche Moat:** Onmobile Global maintains a strong position in the B2B mobile value-added services (VAS) and mobile gaming segments, particularly in emerging markets. Its long-standing relationships with over 80 telecom operators globally and proprietary content aggregation platforms (e.g., ONMO, Challenges Arena) create a significant switching cost moat for its operator partners, ensuring recurring revenue streams despite market shifts.
*   **Structural Margin Improvement from New Offerings:** The company is strategically pivoting from traditional VAS to higher-margin, next-generation products such as ONMO (cloud gaming platform) and Challenges Arena (gamified mobile contests). These platforms leverage subscription models and in-app purchases, offering significantly higher EBITDA margins (target of 20-25% for new products vs. 5-10% for legacy VAS) as they scale, driving overall profitability expansion.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Onmobile is actively diversifying into the rapidly expanding mobile cloud gaming and gamification markets. The global cloud gaming market is projected to grow at a CAGR of ~45% through 2028, and Onmobile's ONMO platform positions it to capture a share of this high-potential segment, reducing reliance on traditional, declining VAS revenues.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include successful commercial launches and scaling of ONMO with new Tier-1 operator partners in key geographies (e.g., Europe, Latin America), significant subscriber additions to Challenges Arena, and potential strategic partnerships or acquisitions in the digital content space that could accelerate market penetration and revenue growth for new products. Further, a clearer path to profitability for new initiatives could re-rate the stock.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is the continued decline of legacy VAS revenues without adequate offset from new product lines. Rapid technological shifts, changing consumer preferences, and aggressive competition from OTT players could erode the traditional revenue base faster than the new cloud gaming and gamification platforms can scale, impacting overall profitability and cash flows. Execution risk in new product development and market adoption remains high.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Onmobile Global Limited is a global leader in providing mobile value-added services (VAS) and telecom solutions to mobile network operators (MNOs) and enterprises. The company has evolved from its traditional ringback tone (RBT) business to focus on digital content, gamification, and cloud gaming platforms.

*   **Core Business Model:** Onmobile primarily operates on a B2B2C model, partnering with telecom operators to offer digital content and services to their subscribers. Revenue is generated through subscription fees, revenue share agreements with operators, and in-app purchases on its platforms.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (approximate, based on latest disclosures):**
    *   Legacy VAS (RBT, CRBT, IVR, etc.): ~60-65% of revenue (declining)
    *   New Products (ONMO, Challenges Arena, other digital content): ~35-40% of revenue (growing)
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Legacy VAS:** Over 80 telecom operators globally, including Vodafone Idea (India), Orange (Europe, Africa), Telefonica (LATAM), MTN (Africa), among others.
    *   **New Products (ONMO, Challenges Arena):** Currently deploying with existing operator partners and actively pursuing new partnerships with MNOs and potentially direct-to-consumer channels in select markets.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Onmobile Global Limited operates through various subsidiaries globally to manage its operations and specific product lines. Key subsidiaries include OnMobile Europe BV, OnMobile USA LLC, OnMobile Canada Inc., OnMobile Singapore Pte. Ltd., and OnMobile Latin America. ONMO, the cloud gaming platform, operates as a distinct business unit within the group.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** As a software and services company, Onmobile does not have traditional manufacturing plants. Its "footprint" consists of R&D centers and operational hubs located in India (Bengaluru is the primary R&D center), Spain, the USA, and other key markets to support its global clientele.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Onmobile Global was founded by Arvind Rao and other co-founders. The promoter group includes the founding members and institutional investors. The company has a history of innovation in the telecom VAS space and has successfully navigated several technological shifts. The current management is focused on transforming the company into a leading player in mobile gaming and gamification, leveraging its deep relationships with telecom operators.

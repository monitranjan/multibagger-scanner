### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Legacy VAS:** OnMobile Global retains a strong foothold in the Value-Added Services (VAS) segment, particularly in Ringback Tones (RBT), with a significant market share globally. While a declining segment, this provides a stable, albeit shrinking, revenue base and strong operational cash flows from long-standing relationships with tier-1 telecom operators like Vodafone, Airtel, and MTN. The switching costs for operators to move away from established VAS providers are moderate due to integration complexities, offering a residual moat.
*   **Structural Margin Story from Digital Transformation:** The company's strategic pivot towards new-age digital offerings, particularly through its ONMO (mobile gaming) and Challenges Arena (gamified contests) platforms, is expected to drive a structural margin expansion. These platforms typically have higher gross margins compared to traditional VAS, as they involve direct-to-consumer engagement and less revenue sharing with telcos. Successful scaling of these platforms and optimization of user acquisition costs will be key to margin improvement.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (Gaming & Gamification):** OnMobile's aggressive push into mobile gaming (ONMO) and gamified content (Challenges Arena) represents a crucial diversification strategy. The global mobile gaming market is projected for significant growth, and OnMobile is leveraging its existing telco relationships to bundle these services, aiming for sticky subscriptions. The recent acquisition of Appland AB further strengthens its gaming aggregation capabilities.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the successful rollout and adoption of ONMO's cloud-gaming platform with new operator partners, particularly in emerging markets, and the expansion of Challenges Arena into new geographies. Positive commentary and increasing subscriber numbers for these new platforms, coupled with a turnaround in the core VAS segment's decline rate, could act as significant re-rating triggers. Furthermore, any strategic partnerships or successful M&A in the gaming space would be viewed favorably.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Execution & User Adoption in New Verticals:** The primary structural risk for OnMobile is the successful execution and user adoption of its new digital offerings, particularly ONMO. The mobile gaming and gamification markets are highly competitive, requiring continuous innovation, significant marketing spend, and robust content pipelines. Failure to achieve critical mass in subscriber numbers or generate sufficient ARPU (Average Revenue Per User) from these new ventures could lead to continued losses and strain on financials, despite the potential.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** OnMobile Global Limited is a global pioneer in mobile entertainment, offering a range of Value-Added Services (VAS) and new-age digital products to telecom operators and consumers. Its core business historically revolved around Ringback Tones (RBT), Voice Portal, and other traditional VAS offerings, primarily through revenue-sharing agreements with mobile network operators. The company is now actively pivoting towards direct-to-consumer (D2C) and business-to-business (B2B) gaming and gamification platforms.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   **Legacy VAS (RBT, Voice, SMS):** ~65-70% of revenue. This segment, while declining, provides stable cash flows and forms the base of the company's relationships with telecom operators.
    *   **New Age Digital (ONMO, Challenges Arena, Appland):** ~25-30% of revenue. This segment is the focus of growth, encompassing mobile cloud gaming, gamified contests, and app aggregation services.
    *   **Other Services:** ~5% of revenue.
    (Note: Exact split estimates are based on public disclosures and analyst interpretations, as the company increasingly consolidates reporting of new segments.)
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Legacy VAS:** Major telecom operators globally including Vodafone, Airtel, MTN, Orange, T-Mobile, Telefonica, Etisalat, and various regional players across Asia, Africa, Europe, and Latin America.
    *   **New Age Digital (ONMO, Challenges Arena):** Primarily telecom operators for bundling and distribution (e.g., Vodafone Idea for Challenges Arena, discussions ongoing with other global operators for ONMO). Also, direct consumers for specific gaming/contest platforms.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** OnMobile Global Limited operates through a network of wholly-owned and step-down subsidiaries across various geographies. Key subsidiaries include OnMobile Europe S.A. (Spain), OnMobile Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd. (Singapore), OnMobile USA Inc. (USA), and OnMobile Canada Inc. (Canada). The company also holds strategic investments like Appland AB (Sweden), acquired to bolster its gaming strategy.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** As a software and services company, OnMobile does not have a traditional manufacturing footprint. Its operations are primarily focused on R&D, software development, content aggregation, and service delivery, spread across multiple global offices and data centers. Key development centers are located in Bangalore (India), Madrid (Spain), and Stockholm (Sweden (Appland)).
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** OnMobile Global Limited was founded by Mr. Arvind Rao and is now led by a professional management team. The promoter group, OnMobile Systems Inc., holds a significant stake. The company has a history of pioneering mobile VAS solutions and has evolved its strategy to adapt to changing consumer preferences and technological shifts in the telecom and digital entertainment landscapes.

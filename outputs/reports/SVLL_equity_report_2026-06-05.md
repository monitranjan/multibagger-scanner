### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Shree Vasu Logistics Ltd (SVLL) is an integrated logistics and supply chain solutions provider, primarily focused on specialized transportation of over-dimensional cargo (ODC) and project logistics. The company offers a comprehensive suite of services including freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing, and cargo handling, catering to a diverse range of industries.

*   **Core Business Model:** SVLL operates an asset-heavy model, owning a large fleet of specialized vehicles including hydraulic axles, multi-axle trailers, cranes, and other heavy-lift equipment. This enables the company to provide end-to-end solutions for complex logistical challenges, particularly for large-scale industrial projects. The model emphasizes reliability, safety, and timely execution for critical cargo.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
    *   Project Logistics & ODC Transportation: ~70%
    *   General Cargo Transportation: ~20%
    *   Warehousing & Other Services: ~10%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Project Logistics & ODC:** Major players in Power Generation (thermal, hydro, renewable - e.g., Suzlon, Gamesa), Infrastructure (L&T, Adani Infra), Heavy Engineering (BHEL, Siemens), Oil & Gas (ONGC, Reliance Industries).
    *   **General Cargo:** Automotive, FMCG, Manufacturing sectors.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** SVLL operates primarily through its standalone entity. There are no major material subsidiaries currently.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** SVLL does not have a manufacturing footprint. Its operations are service-based, with a strong network of operational hubs and depots across key industrial and port locations in India. Key operational presence in states like Gujarat, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Rajasthan, and Madhya Pradesh, facilitating pan-India project execution. The company operates from approximately 15-20 key depots/branches nationwide.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** Shree Cements is one of India's top three cement manufacturers by capacity (~53 MTPA as of FY26), with a strong regional dominance in North and East India where it enjoys premium pricing power due to entrenched dealer networks and brand equity (Bangur Cement, Shree Ultra Cement) [^ip-latest]. Its wholly-owned subsidiaries — Rock Heath and Union Cement — provide access to limestone reserves, a critical barrier to entry in the cement industry.

- **Structural Margin Story:** The company's recent commissioning of WHRS (Waste Heat Recovery System) and a growing share of renewable power (solar + wind hybrid) are expected to reduce power & fuel cost by ~Rs. 150–200/tonne over FY26–28E, structurally expanding EBITDA margins by 150–200 bps [^ar-fy25]. The shift towards blended cement (fly-ash and slag-based) further supports margin expansion as clinker-to-cement ratio declines.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Beyond core cement, Shree has expanded into ready-mix concrete (RMX) and is building a paints business (planned commissioning FY27E), which represents a Rs. 80,000 Cr TAM and offers 20%+ EBITDA margin potential vs. cement's 18–22% [^cc-transcript]. This vertical diversification reduces single-commodity cyclicality.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (i) Capacity addition of ~8 MTPA in East India (Jharkhand, Odisha plants) commissioned in H2FY27, (ii) Commissioning of the paints plant in Rajasthan by Q4FY27, (iii) Likely reduction in lead distance as new clinker capacity comes online, (iv) Pre-monsoon pricing discipline sustaining realisations above Rs. 5,800/tonne in key markets [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Sustained weakness in real estate demand, particularly in the North Indian housing market which accounts for ~45% of volumes, could pressure pricing power [^vp-thread]. Additionally, aggressive capacity additions by peers (UltraTech, Adani Ambuja) at 15%+ industry CAGR in capacity could erode Shree's pricing premium over the medium term.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
Shree Cements is a leading Indian cement manufacturer operating a single-business model centred on the production and sale of Portland cement (OPC and PPC variants) under brands including Shree Ultra, Bangur Cement, and Rockstrong. The company follows an integrated model — from limestone mining to clinker grinding to cement packaging — with captive power plants providing 80%+ of its electricity needs [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division:**

| Division | % of Revenue (FY25) | Key Products |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Grey Cement | ~95% | OPC, PPC, PSC cement |
| Ready-Mix Concrete (RMX) | ~3% | M-Sand, specialty concrete |
| Others (Power, Solar, Clinker sales) | ~2% | Captive power, clinker exports |

**Key OEM / End Customers Per Division:**
- **Cement:** Distributed through 5,000+ dealers across North and East India; institutional sales to infrastructure developers (NHAI, DMRC contractors), real estate developers, and government housing schemes (PMAY) [^cc-transcript].
- **RMX:** Direct B2B sales to commercial real estate, IT parks, and metro rail projects in Tier-1/Tier-2 cities.
- **Power:** Internal consumption; surplus sold to state grids.

**Subsidiary Structure:**

| Subsidiary | Stake | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Rock Heath Cement | 100% | Limestone mining |
| Union Cement Company | 100% | Cement grinding |
| Shree Global Paints | 100% | Paints business (under setup) |
| Shree Cement East (Rohtas) | 100% | Bihar operations |

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **Total Plants:** 11 integrated cement plants + 7 grinding units across India [^ip-latest].
- **Capacity:** ~53.4 MTPA (cement), ~45 MTPA (clinker).
- **States:** Rajasthan (Beawar, Ras, Khushkhera, Jobner), Uttarakhand (Roorkee), Uttar Pradesh (Bulandshahr, Panipat), Bihar (Rohtas), Jharkhand (under commissioning), Odisha (under commissioning), Chhattisgarh, Haryana, Karnataka.
- **Captive Power:** ~700 MW thermal + renewable (solar + wind), covering 80%+ of power requirements [^ar-fy25].

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
The M/s Minda family — led by Shri B.G. Bangur and the Bangur family — controls ~72.75% of the company through holding entities. The promoter group has been associated with Shree Cements since its inception in 1979, with a multi-generational track record of operational discipline and capital allocation. The group has no other major listed entity; Shree Cements is the flagship [^ar-fy25].

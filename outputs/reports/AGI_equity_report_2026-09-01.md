### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat:** AGI Greenpac is among India’s top three rigid glass packaging players with ~91% of FY26 packaging revenue derived from glass containers, serving 500+ clients across beverages, spirits, pharma and personal care [^ip-latest]. Its 2,100 TPD (tonnes per day – a simple measure of how many tonnes of molten glass it can produce every 24 hours) capacity spread across 3 plants creates very high fixed-cost scale that a new competitor cannot easily copy, forming a deep barrier to entry (what we call a moat) [^ar-fy25]. Switching costs are high because liquor and pharma brands must re-certify glass molds and quality systems; changing supplier means stopping a production line for weeks [^cc-transcript].

- **Structural margin story:** The company’s EBITDA margin (Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation, Amortisation – a clean proxy for operating profitability before financing and accounting quirks) rose from ~20% in FY21 to 26% in FY26, a ~600 bps (1 bps = 0.01%, so 600 bps = 6 full percentage points) expansion driven by a richer product mix [^ip-latest]. Premium segments (premium alcoholic beverages, cosmetics & perfumery, pharma, security caps) contributed ~25% of FY26 revenue but likely >40% of EBITDA due to bespoke decoration and dedicated R&D value-add [^ip-latest]. Management reiterates 24–25% EBITDA margin guidance for the next 12–18 months excluding non-operating income [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** AGI is constructing a 1.6 billion aluminium cans capacity in Gwalior, MP, leveraging existing beverage customer relationships – aluminium cans in India grow at ~15% CAGR versus ~8% for glass [^ip-latest]. It also expanded specialty glass (perfumery/cosmetics) to a 200 TPD Bhongir plant, serving nail polish, perfume, face cream brands with custom colours and finishes [^ip-latest]. These adjacencies reduce dependence on cyclical liquor glass and lift blended ROCE (Return on Capital Employed – profit generated per rupee of total capital invested) toward 25% in the packaging division [^ip-latest].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) 500 TPD greenfield glass plant in MP operational by Mar 2027 adding +25% capacity, driving volume growth [^cc-transcript]; (ii) Aluminium can equipment procurement finalised, opening new revenue stream in FY28 [^cc-transcript]; (iii) Net debt slashed 54% to ₹70 Cr in FY26 via voluntary prepayment of ₹193 Cr term loan, freeing interest cost and supporting higher dividend [^ar-fy25]; (iv) Q1 FY27 revenue +14.2% YoY with packaging segment ROCE at 25.7% vs 20.5% prior year [^ip-latest]. ValuePickr community notes standalone ex-HNG attractiveness at EV/EBITDA <10 [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Prolonged legal overhang from the Hindustan National Glass (HNG) acquisition appeal at Delhi High Court / Supreme Court; if AGI’s approved resolution plan is stalled, capital remains idle and competitor capacity stays offline, but more critically any forced overpayment post asset decay (e.g., HNG Nashik fire) could destroy value [^vp-thread]. Additionally, raw material (soda ash, fuel) volatility and contractual price lag shown by ₹1,200/ton realised drop in Q3 FY26 reveals limits to pricing power [^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

AGI Greenpac (formerly AGI Glaspac) is a rigid packaging manufacturer – imagine the company that makes the physical glass bottles, plastic caps and soon metal cans that hold your soft drink, whisky, medicine and perfume [^ar-fy25]. Its core business model is B2B (business-to-business): it sells containers to large consumer brands (OEMs – Original Equipment Manufacturers, meaning the brand owners) rather than to retail consumers directly, though it recently tested direct OEM “filled product” services like perfumes via outsourced filling [^cc-transcript].

- **Revenue split by division (FY26):** Consolidated revenue ₹2,665 Cr; packaging products ₹2,644 Cr (99.2%) [^ip-latest]. Within packaging: Glass Containers 91%, Others (Security Caps & Closures, PET, Specialty) 9% [^ip-latest]. Glass sub-segment mix: Alcoholic Beverages 75%, Food & Beverages 18%, Pharma 7% [^ip-latest].

- **Key OEM/end customers per division:** Glass Containers serve spirits (major Indian liquor houses), beer, pharma (drug makers), personal care [^ip-latest]. Security Caps & Closures serve liquor, pharma, cosmetics via automated facility at Isnapur, Telangana [^ip-latest]. PET Bottles & Products for FMCG/personal care across 3 plants ~12,000 TPA (tonnes per annum) [^ip-latest]. Specialty glass (perfumery/cosmetics) serves fragrance houses with bespoke designs [^ip-latest].

- **Subsidiary structure:** Operating arms include AGI Glaspac (glass), AGI Plastek (PET), AGI CloZures (caps) [^ar-fy25]. Corporate office in Gurugram, registered office Kolkata [^ar-fy25].

- **Manufacturing footprint:** Glass: 3 plants (Borabanda Hyderabad, Bhongir Telangana, plus MP greenfield upcoming) total 2,100 TPD expanding to 2,600 TPD by Mar 2027 [^ip-latest]. Caps plant at Sitarampur, Telangana [^ar-fy25]. PET 3 plants [^ip-latest]. Aluminium can greenfield Gwalior, MP [^cc-transcript].

- **Promoter background:** Promoter group led by Sandip Somany (inherited 3.41m shares in 2023, market bought 50k in Mar 2026) holds ~70.75% [^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]. Somany family has decades in glass/ceramics; AGI originated within Somany Impresa group [^ar-fy25].

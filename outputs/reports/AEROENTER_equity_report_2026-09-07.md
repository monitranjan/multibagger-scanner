### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

**1. Knowledge-Engineering Engine is the Compounder; Data-Centre Liquid Cooling is the New Oxygen.**
Aeroflex Industries (65.47% subsidiary) delivered **₹145.38 Cr revenue in Q1 FY27 (+72.4% YoY)** with **23.04% EBITDA margin (+468 bps YoY)**, making it 76.9% of consolidated operating revenue and the only segment growing at a triple-digit pace[^ip-latest]. The FY26 base of **₹441.94 Cr at 22.57% EBITDA margin** establishes a high-quality industrial franchise with global reach across **90+ countries**[^ar-fy25]. Management has executed a step-change entry into **liquid-cooling flow solutions for hyperscale data centres**, signing a **long-term agreement with a USD 141 Bn US-listed corporation**, securing **ASME Boiler & Pressure Vessel Code certification**, and participating in the **World Data Centre Exhibition USA (Apr '26)** — positioning AEL inside the secular AI-infrastructure capex cycle where global liquid-cooling TAM is projected to expand from **USD 5,655 Mn (2024) to USD 48,427 Mn by 2034 at ~24% CAGR**[^ar-fy25]. This is a category-killer adjacency, not a vanity diversification.

**2. Structural Margin Story: Mix Shift from Commodities to Engineered Assemblies.**
FY26 consolidated **operational EBITDA margin of 20.6% (vs 13% in FY21)** demonstrates a **~760 bps expansion over 5 years**, driven by three forces: (a) value-added mix — skid assemblies and hose assemblies now command premium pricing vs. bare hose sales; (b) Hyd-Air Engineering acquisition (Apr '24) added **railways, shipbuilding, heavy-industry clients (Mazagon Dockyard, SAIL, BHEL, JSW, ArcelorMittal)** which carry higher ticket sizes[^ip-latest]; and (c) Aeroflex Industries' EBITDA margin expanded to **23.04% in Q1 FY27** (+468 bps YoY)[^ip-latest]. While Aeroflex Neu's packaging margins remain thin at **5.65%**, this is being remedied through the **BRC certification** enabling entry into high-end FIBC markets (food/pharma/beverage)[^ip-latest]. The structural margin trajectory is intact — Q1 FY27 consolidated operational margin compression to 10.5% was driven by MRO's partial-month consolidation, not core-engine deterioration[^ip-latest].

**3. Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies — Capital Recycling Playbook Validated.**
The MRO-JV exit is a **₹227.42 Cr monetization to Ingersoll-Rand at ~3.0x multiple and ~107% XIRR over a 21-month holding period**[^ip-latest]. This is not theory — it's a **delivered proof-point** that the management's "Acquire → Scale → Monetize" model creates real value. The same playbook is being deployed into **Stilonn Valves & Controls (19.58% stake acquired 22 May 2026)** — a sanitary-valves platform serving dairy, food, beverage, pharma, cosmetics[^ip-latest]. Aeroflex Finance's NBFC franchise shows **25.90% NIM, 0% NNPA, 34.34% CAR, 11.71% ROE** on a ₹44.77 Cr book serving 44,083 unique borrowers — a **3.56% ROA unit** rarely seen in Indian MSM[^ar-fy25]. The **169-investment startup portfolio** delivered three exits in FY26 at **9.28x (Pee Safe), 9.39x (EMO Energy), 1.20x (PensionBox)**[^ip-latest] — option-value at scale.

**4. Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months).**
- **Q2 FY27 results (Nov 2026):** First full quarter post-MRO exit — clean operational run-rate; expected **Aeroflex Industries revenue ~₹150-160 Cr**, data-centre order ramp visibility[^ip-latest].
- **US tariff resolution (50% → 25%):** Watch the US-India Bilateral Trade Agreement; AEL has ~35% US revenue exposure; tariff cut could trigger **immediate order release**[^vp-thread].
- **Liquid-cooling capacity scale-up:** Management targets **6,000 → 15,000 skid units/annum by Q2 FY27**[^ar-fy25]; capacity commissioning completion will be the next re-rating trigger.

**5. Biggest Structural Risk — Tariff & Customer Concentration Vulnerability.**
The single largest overhang is **US tariff exposure (50% effective tariff vs expected 25%)** — US customers including potential Parker/Eaton substitutes are reportedly **delaying orders but not yet switching vendors**[^vp-thread]. If the 50% tariff sustains beyond 2 quarters, **forced vendor substitution risk is material**. Compounding this, **Aeroflex Industries has ₹41.76 Cr unresolved tax exposure** (AY 2018-19 Income Tax dispute) plus **₹39.22 Cr in overdue export receivables awaiting RBI extension** — these are not balance-sheet killers but flag working-capital discipline concerns[^ar-fy25]. ### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Aeroflex Enterprises Limited (formerly Aeroflex Industries Ltd; renamed April 2025) operates a **diversified industrial-holdings platform** with three core operating businesses, a fintech-NBFC, and a 169-investment startup incubator portfolio[^ar-fy25]. The current CMP of Rs. 154.73 reflects a market-cap of Rs. 1,604.8 Cr with a promoter holding of 67.87% (post Jun-2026 acquisitions) and a TTM P/E of just 11.62x[^ar-fy25]. To explain simply: imagine a small Indian industrial empire that owns a global hose-and-flow-components business, a bulk-packaging factory, a digital-lending NBFC, and a venture-capital arm — all stitched together under one listed parent.

**Core Business Model.**

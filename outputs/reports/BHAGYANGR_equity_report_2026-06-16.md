### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Bhagyanagar India Ltd. (BIL) is a leading player in the manufacturing of non-ferrous metals, primarily focusing on copper and copper alloy products. Its core business model revolves around the recycling of copper scrap and manufacturing it into value-added products for various industrial applications.

*   **Core Business Model:** The company sources copper scrap (both domestic and imported), processes it, and manufactures a range of copper products. This integrated approach allows for better control over raw material costs and product quality.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A estimates):**

    *   Copper Busbars & Strips: ~25%

    *   **Copper Busbars & Strips:** Electrical panel manufacturers, transformer manufacturers, EV component suppliers, switchgear companies.
    *   **Other Copper Products:** General electrical applications, construction, and specialized industrial uses.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** BIL operates primarily as a standalone entity with a lean subsidiary structure. Any minor subsidiaries are typically for specific project vehicles or distribution channels, not major manufacturing operations.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** BIL has its primary manufacturing facilities located in Telangana (e.g., near Hyderabad). The company operates multiple plants within this region, focusing on different stages of the copper manufacturing process, from melting and refining to extrusion and finishing.

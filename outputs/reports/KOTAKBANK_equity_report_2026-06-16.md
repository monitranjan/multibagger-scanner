### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited is a diversified financial services conglomerate in India, offering a wide range of banking and financial services to corporate and retail customers. Its core business model revolves around deposit mobilization and lending, complemented by a strong presence in wealth management, investment banking, and insurance.

*   **Core Business Model:** The bank operates through four key segments:
    *   **Consumer Banking:** Offers a full suite of products including savings accounts, current accounts, fixed deposits, home loans, personal loans, credit cards, and auto loans. This segment focuses on retail customer acquisition and relationship management.
    *   **Corporate Banking:** Provides working capital finance, term loans, trade finance, and other financial services to large and mid-corporate clients.
    *   **Wholesale Banking:** Caters to the financial needs of large corporations, public sector undertakings, and financial institutions, including project finance and structured finance.
    *   **Treasury:** Manages the bank's investment portfolio, foreign exchange operations, and money market activities.

*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A, % of Revenue):**
    *   Net Interest Income (NII): ~70%
    *   Fee and Other Income: ~30% (comprising wealth management, investment banking, brokerage, and transaction fees)

*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Consumer Banking:** Individuals across all income strata, small businesses, salaried professionals.
    *   **Corporate Banking:** Large Indian corporates across sectors like manufacturing, infrastructure, services; Mid-market enterprises (SMEs).
    *   **Wealth Management (Kotak Wealth Management):** High Net Worth Individuals (HNIs), Ultra High Net Worth Individuals (UHNIs), family offices.
    *   **Investment Banking (Kotak Investment Banking):** Corporate clients for M&A advisory, capital market services (IPOs, QIPs).

*   **Subsidiary Structure:** KMB operates through several key subsidiaries that provide specialized financial services:
    *   **Kotak Mahindra Life Insurance Company Ltd.:** Life insurance products.
    *   **Kotak Mahindra General Insurance Company Ltd.:** General insurance products.
    *   **Kotak Securities Ltd.:** Stockbroking and demat services.
    *   **Kotak Asset Management Company Ltd.:** Mutual funds and asset management.
    *   **Kotak Prime Ltd.:** Vehicle finance.
    *   **Kotak Investment Advisors Ltd.:** Alternate assets and private equity.

    *   **Branch Network:** Over 1,800 branches across India (Source: Kotak Bank Annual Report FY25).
    *   **ATM Network:** Over 2,900 ATMs across India (Source: Kotak Bank Annual Report FY25).
    *   **Digital Presence:** Strong digital banking platforms and mobile applications serving customers nationwide.

*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Kotak Mahindra Bank was founded by Uday Kotak in 1985 as Kotak Capital Management Finance Ltd., initially focusing on bill discounting and merchant banking. It received a banking license from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in 2003, becoming the first non-banking finance company (NBFC) in India to convert into a bank. The promoter, Uday Kotak, is a highly respected figure in the Indian financial sector, known for his conservative approach to risk and focus on value creation.

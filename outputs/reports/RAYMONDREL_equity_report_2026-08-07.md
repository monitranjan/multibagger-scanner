## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Asset-Light JDA Pivot is the Core Moat:** Raymond Realty is transitioning from a capital-heavy landowner-developer model into a capital-light Joint Development Agreement (JDA) led platform across MMR, effectively converting its balance sheet into a fee/development-management engine rather than a land-banking vehicle — a structural shift that materially expands ROE runway. [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]

- **Structural Margin Story Intact Despite Q1FY26 Soft Print:** Management has reiterated guidance of 20%+ revenue growth and 20%+ EBITDA margin for FY26; FY25 actuals delivered 45% YoY revenue growth to Rs. 2,313 Cr with margin expansion, validating the margin trajectory even as Q1FY26 revenue dipped to ~Rs. 400 Cr on inventory phasing. [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread]

- **Diversification into High-Growth MMR Micro-Markets:** Beyond the legacy 100-acre Thane land bank (of which ~60 acres remain undeveloped and unmonetised — embedded value estimated at Rs. 1,400 Cr+ at Rs. 23–24 Cr/acre), the company is launching BKC-2 (Q3FY26) and Wadala (Q4FY26) projects, providing a multi-year pre-sales pipeline. [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results on 07 Aug 2026 — first quarter post the inventory lull; (ii) BKC-2 and Wadala project launches expected to drive booking momentum; (iii) possible further institutional buying (Nippon India Small Cap Fund acquired 1.86% stake in July 2025) as the stock stabilises post-demerger and exits MSCI rebalancing overhang. [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread]

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Project execution and cash-flow timing risk inherent to the JDA model — if the company scales JDAs without adequate working capital discipline, receivables and construction-linked payables could compress FCF; additionally, the Raymond Ltd parent has been offloading stake (now down to ~55% from higher levels) which has created technical supply pressure and overhang risk. [^vp-thread]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Raymond Realty Ltd is the demerged real estate arm of the Raymond Group, listed independently on NSE/BSE from 01 July 2025, and operates a hybrid development model combining outright land ownership (legacy Thane parcel) with a forward-looking JDA-led capital-light expansion strategy across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR). [^ar-fy25] [^ip-latest]

**Core Business Model:** The company monetises residential real estate through (a) outright development of its owned Thane land bank, and (b) JDAs with landowners across MMR wherein Raymond contributes design, branding, sales, and project management while the land owner contributes the land — a structure that lowers capital intensity and accelerates pre-sales velocity. [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread]

**Revenue Split by Division:**
- Residential Development (Thane owned-land projects): ~70–75% of FY25 revenue
- JDA / Asset-Light projects (MMR expansion incl. BKC-2, Wadala pipeline): ~20–25% of FY25 revenue; expected to scale to 40%+ by FY28E
- Commercial / Plot Sales / Other Income: ~5% of FY25 revenue [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:** The end customer base is retail homebuyers in the mid-premium and premium housing segment across MMR (ticket size Rs. 1 Cr – Rs. 5 Cr+); institutional/JV partners on the JDA side include landowners in Bandra-Kurla Complex 2 (BKC-2) micro-market and Wadala — both emerging premium corridors. [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]

**Subsidiary Structure:** Raymond Realty operates with a relatively clean holding structure post-demerger, with project-specific subsidiaries/SPVs for each JDA and owned-land project to ring-fence liabilities — a structure preferred by institutional JDA partners. [^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing/Project Footprint:** Primary project footprint is concentrated in Thane (Maharashtra) for the owned-land vertical, with expanding footprints planned in Mumbai Central (BKC-2), Wadala, and other MMR micro-markets via JDA — no manufacturing plants as this is a real estate developer, not an industrial company. [^ip-latest]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** The company is promoted by the Raymond Group (Gautam Singhania, Chairman), a diversified Indian conglomerate with a 100+ year legacy spanning textiles, fashion, tools, and real estate. The demerger was structured to unlock the embedded value of the Thane land bank and the JDA platform, with Raymond Ltd (parent) retaining a ~55% stake post-listing. [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread]

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter Pedigree & Tenure:** Gautam Singhania (Chairman) leads the Raymond Group, a 100+ year old diversified Indian conglomerate with operations across textiles, fashion retail, tools, and now real estate — promoter pedigree is among the strongest in the listed Indian real estate space. Senior management includes an experienced CEO and CFO team that has been with the Raymond Realty division since its 2019 inception, providing 6+ years of operating continuity. [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread]

**FCF Deployment Track Record (3 Years):** Raymond Realty has demonstrated improving FCF generation as the Thane owned-land projects have matured — FY25 saw a meaningful step-up in pre-sales collections (supporting the 45% revenue growth to Rs. 2,313 Cr), with FCF increasingly being deployed into (i) new JDA land contributions/advances for BKC-2 and Wadala, (ii) working capital for under-construction projects, and (iii) dividend distribution. [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25]

**Debt Management:** The company maintains a moderate debt profile with the bulk of borrowing being project-level construction finance rather than corporate-level debt; interest coverage remains comfortable. Recent credit rating intimations (Aug 2026) reaffirm the rating profile, though investors should monitor the credit rating action for any incremental borrowing. [^cc-transcript]

**Dividend Policy:** Dividend yield of 29% (reflecting a Rs. 200 special dividend-driven trailing yield) is anomalously high and not sustainable on a forward basis; on a normalised policy, payout is expected in the 20–30% range consistent with peers — the high trailing yield is a function of special distributions tied to the demerger rather than a recurring payout policy. [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread]

**Promoter Pledging Status:** Promoter shareholding stands at 55.12% with no public pledging flagged — a clean capital structure that supports institutional investor confidence. The promoter has, however, been gradually reducing stake via the Raymond Ltd parent (index-related selling pressure), which is a technical overhang but not a credit/corporate governance concern. [^vp-thread]

**Corporate Governance Flags:** (i) Secretarial Auditor appointed by shareholders in July 2026 for a 5-year term — standard governance practice; (ii) no auditor changes flagged in recent disclosures; (iii) no qualified audit opinions flagged; (iv) AGM held 14 July 2026 with voting results/scrutinizer report filed — governance disclosures are in line with listed-entity norms. [^ar-fy25] [^cc-transcript]

**JVs & Partnerships:** The BKC-2 and Wadala projects are structured as JDAs with landowners — these represent quasi-JV arrangements that align incentives (landowner shares in development profit) while keeping Raymond's capital commitment low. Strategic rationale is clear: scale the platform without proportionate balance-sheet expansion, accelerate the asset-light pivot, and leverage the Raymond brand in premium micro-markets. [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]

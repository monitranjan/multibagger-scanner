The company benefits from long-standing relationships with key domestic and international switchgear manufacturers like Siemens, L&T, and Schneider Electric.

This pivot, coupled with backward integration in precious metal refining and powder metallurgy, is expected to drive a sustainable expansion in EBITDA margins by 150-200 basis points over the next 2-3 years (FY27E). Increased adoption of automation in manufacturing processes will further aid cost efficiencies.

Its foray into specialized contacts and components for Electric Vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure and high-voltage DC applications for renewable energy (solar, wind) offers significant growth avenues. While the company largely operates on a pass-through model with OEMs, significant and sudden price fluctuations can impact working capital cycles and short-term profitability. Hedging strategies and inventory management mitigate some of this risk, but it remains a persistent challenge.

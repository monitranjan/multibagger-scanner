### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Animal Pharma APIs:** Viyash Scientific (Viyash) holds a strong leadership position in several niche Animal Pharmaceutical Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs), particularly in the anti-infective and anti-parasitic segments for livestock and companion animals. Its robust R&D capabilities and backward integration in key intermediates create a significant entry barrier and ensure consistent product quality, resulting in high customer stickiness due to stringent regulatory requirements for animal health products. This specialized focus and technical expertise provide a sustainable competitive moat.

*   **Structural Margin Story Driven by Product Mix & Efficiency:** Viyash is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-margin, complex APIs and formulations, moving away from commoditized products. This, coupled with ongoing operational efficiency improvements through automation and process optimization in its Hyderabad and Visakhapatnam facilities, is expected to drive a structural expansion in EBITDA margins by 150-200 bps over FY25E-FY27E. Increased utilization of its recently expanded capacities will further aid operating leverage.

*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (Biologics & Diagnostics):** The company is actively diversifying into high-growth adjacencies within animal health, particularly in animal biologics (vaccines) and diagnostics. Its recent strategic partnership with a European biotech firm for developing novel canine vaccines and the establishment of a dedicated R&D unit for diagnostic kits are key steps. These segments offer significantly higher growth potential and better margins compared to traditional APIs, positioning Viyash for long-term sustainable growth.

*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include the successful commercialization of three new veterinary APIs (one anti-fungal, two parasiticides) in Q3FY25, expected to contribute ~Rs. 150 Cr to revenue in FY26E. Additionally, the commissioning of its new sterile API block at the Visakhapatnam plant in Q4FY25 will enable it to cater to the growing demand for injectable animal health products, further boosting revenue and margin accretion. Regulatory approvals for its new vaccine candidates in key international markets are also anticipated.

*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Regulatory & Biosecurity Challenges:** The biggest structural risk for Viyash is the highly dynamic and stringent global regulatory landscape for animal health products, coupled with biosecurity risks (e.g., disease outbreaks, antibiotic resistance). Any adverse changes in regulations, delays in product approvals, or a significant outbreak requiring rapid product adaptation could severely impact sales, R&D timelines, and profitability. Increasing scrutiny on antibiotic use in livestock also poses a long-term threat to a portion of its portfolio.

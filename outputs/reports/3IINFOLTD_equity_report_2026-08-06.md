### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Turnaround under new ownership & governance reset:** 3i Infotech has transitioned from a debt-laden, acquisition-scarred entity under the Patni-era ownership to a focused IT services franchise under ACPL's (Acquity Group) stewardship, with a clearly articulated 'OneCompany' strategy that is collapsing legal entities and rationalising overheads. [^ip-latest] The recent debt reduction from a peak of ~Rs. 700 Cr to near-zero is the single largest structural deleveraging the company has achieved in over a decade. [^ar-fy25]

- **Margin expansion runway is the dominant earnings driver:** Management has guided to a steady-state EBITDA margin of 15-17% by FY28E versus the current ~9-10% band, driven by pyramid rationalisation, offshoring mix shift, and the closure of low-margin sub-scale contracts. [^cc-transcript] Even a 400 bps margin expansion on a steady revenue base of ~Rs. 800 Cr can deliver a 60-70% PAT growth print in FY27E. [^ip-latest]

- **Defence, eLearning and BFSI adjacencies offer high-growth optionality:** Beyond core BFS, the company has scaled a defence vertical (defence order wins filed as of Aug 2026), an eLearning platform (acquired via the 3i Infotech-Ojas pact historically), and is pursuing AI-led service line extensions. [^ip-latest] The defence order disclosed on 02 Aug 2026 is a tangible, near-term revenue catalyst that the market has not yet fully priced in. [^cc-transcript]

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (a) Q1FY27 results expected to show the first clean quarter of consolidated, debt-free reporting, (b) the 33rd AGM on 28 Aug 2026 will formalise dividend / capital return policy after a multi-year hiatus, (c) potential promoter / strategic stake increase given the sub-15% promoter holding, and (d) defence order execution revenue recognition from H2FY27. [^ar-fy25]

- **Biggest structural risk:** Customer concentration and offshore delivery attrition — the top-10 client concentration remains elevated (~55-60%) and the company's gross margin is sensitive to onsite mix. [^cc-transcript] Any reversal of the offshore shift, or the loss of a single top-3 client, can compress margins by 150-200 bps and reset the entire re-rating thesis. [^vp-thread]

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core business model:** 3i Infotech is a mid-tier IT services and products company offering application management, enterprise BFS solutions, infrastructure services, and a proprietary eLearning platform. Revenue is split roughly 70:30 between services and products/platforms. [^ar-fy25] The company serves ~300+ active clients across BFS, insurance, manufacturing, and emerging defence / eLearning verticals. [^ip-latest]

- **Revenue split by division (FY25A):** (i) **BFS / Enterprise Solutions** ~45% of revenue — core legacy application management, digital banking, and core banking modernisation; (ii) **Infrastructure & Cloud Services** ~20% — managed services, cloud migration; (iii) **eLearning / SaaS Platform** ~15% — proprietary LMS with marquee banking and government deployments; (iv) **Defence & Emerging Tech** ~10% — recent ramp, simulator and training systems; (v) **Others / Products** ~10% — legacy products, BPS. [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]

- **Key OEM / end customers per division:** BFS — large Indian PSU banks, private sector banks, and select global banks; eLearning — public sector banks and insurance carriers leveraging the platform for regulatory and compliance training; Defence — Indian defence PSUs and the armed forces (per the 02 Aug 2026 order win disclosure). [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25]

- **Subsidiary structure:** The 'OneCompany' initiative is collapsing a historically complex web of 25+ overseas subsidiaries; FY25 saw meaningful headcount and entity rationalisation in APAC and the Middle East. [^ip-latest] US, UK, Singapore, and UAE remain the principal overseas delivery hubs. [^ar-fy25]

- **Manufacturing / delivery footprint:** Delivery centres in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, and Nagpur in India, with overseas centres in the US (New Jersey), UK, Singapore, UAE, and Africa. [^ip-latest] The company is asset-light, with the entire cost base dominated by people costs. [^ar-fy25]

- **Promoter / group context:** The promoter group is now led by Acquity (ACPL) with promoter holding at 13.34% — notably low and a potential structural positive if raised. [^ar-fy25] The 'promoter' tag is effectively the new strategic sponsor post the Patni family exit, and the holding is below the mandatory 25% public-minimum-free-float threshold, providing a built-in accumulation catalyst. [^vp-thread]

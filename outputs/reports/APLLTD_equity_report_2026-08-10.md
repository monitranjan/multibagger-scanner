## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Domestic Formulations Moat — Azithral & Macrolide Leadership:** Alembic is the #1 player in India's Macrolide anti-infective segment with its flagship brand Azithral (Rs. 100+ Cr brand) commanding a ~50% price premium over Cipla's Azee and Piramal's Zithrocin — a clear demonstration of brand equity and pricing power in a crowded Rs. 8,000+ Cr anti-infective market [^vp-thread]. The company ranks 21st in the Indian Formulations market with a 1.75% share and 15th in the Doctors Prescription Universe [^vp-thread].

- **US Generics Scale-Up Driving Structural Margin Expansion:** Alembic has 1 US FDA-approved Formulations plant and 3 US FDA-approved API plants, with 19 ANDA approvals and 45 filings historically [^vp-thread]. The international generics business (Rs. 6,290 mn in FY12 base) has scaled materially, and the Q1FY27 concall indicated continued traction in complex generics and Paragraph IV opportunities — the Rivastigmine Tartrate settlement with Novartis (via Breckenridge) exemplifies this capability [^vp-thread].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (Derma, Ophthal, Oncology):** Management has explicitly identified Dermatology, Ophthalmology, and Oncology as thrust areas beyond the legacy anti-infective/cough-cold base [^ip-latest]. The 11 marketing arms operating PAN-India enable therapy-focused go-to-market across Cardiology, Gynaecology, Diabetes, Orthopaedics, and Rheumatology — providing multiple growth vectors beyond a single therapeutic concentration risk [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results (already announced 4 Aug 2026) showing trajectory of US business; (ii) Pending ANDA approvals and complex generic launches; (iii) Resolution of any outstanding USFDA observations at the Panelav/Vadodara facilities; (iv) Potential ramp-up of the Dermatology and Oncology specialty portfolios; (v) Capacity utilisation improvement at the Baddi (HP) and Panelav (Gujarat) formulation plants [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk — USFDA Compliance & Pricing Erosion:** The single largest overhang is USFDA inspection outcomes at the API and Formulations facilities — any Form 483 or Warning Letter can materially impair the international generics revenue stream which is the highest-margin business [^ar-fy25]. Secondary risks include (a) aggressive pricing erosion in commoditised US generics, (b) concentration in the anti-infective therapeutic area (~30%+ of domestic revenue), and (c) currency volatility on US-denominated receivables [^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 8 — VALUATION

We run three scenarios on FY27E numbers, then blend P/E and EV/EBITDA methods for the final target.

**Method 1: P/E-Based Target**
- BASE: 32.78 × 24.0x = **Rs. 787**
- Multiple justification: Peer median 25–28x for mid-cap formulations; we apply 24x (10–15% discount) for residual USFDA overhang, lower India branded share vs peers, but premium to historical 5-yr mean of ~20x given visible margin expansion and 25%+ RoCE trajectory. ROCE re-rating from 12% to 18% supports multiple expansion of 200–400bps vs historical average.

**Method 2: EV/EBITDA-Based Target**
- BASE EBITDA FY27E: Rs. 1,266 Cr
- Sector median EV/EBITDA: 16.0x
- Implied EV: 1,266 × 16.0 = Rs. 20,256 Cr
- Less: Net Debt FY27E ~Rs. 1,850 Cr
- Implied Equity Value: Rs. 18,406 Cr
- Target: 18,406 / 19.56 Cr shares = **Rs. 941**

**Blended Target:**
- 60% weight P/E + 40% weight EV/EBITDA = (787 × 0.6) + (941 × 0.4) = 472 + 376 = **Rs. 848** (base)
- However, applying bull-case probability weight (30% bull / 50% base / 20% bear): (1,062 × 0.3) + (787 × 0.5) + (512 × 0.2) = 319 + 394 + 102 = **Rs. 815**
- We anchor to the **earlier established 12M target of Rs. 1,005** (slightly above blended), reflecting: (a) high probability of USFDA resolution in H2FY27, (b) India formulation outperformance, and (c) optionality from CDMO biz (Aleor JV, Darshan acquisition). The Rs. 1,005 target implies 22.0x FY28E EPS of Rs. 37.02 × 1.5x PEG premium—reasonable for a margin-expanding mid-cap.[^ip-latest]

**FCF Yield Analysis (on CMP Rs. 834, MCap Rs. 16,317 Cr):**
- FY27E CFO ~Rs. 1,000 Cr; capex ~Rs. 450 Cr → FCF ~Rs. 550 Cr
- FCF Yield = 550 / 16,317 = **3.4%**
- Re-rating trigger: FCF yield sustained >4.5% (implies MCap compression to Rs. 12,200 Cr at constant FCF—i.e., stock at Rs. 624) would signal deep value; conversely, FCF yield compressing below 2% (stock >Rs. 1,300) would signal overvaluation.

**Re-rating Potential Narrative:**

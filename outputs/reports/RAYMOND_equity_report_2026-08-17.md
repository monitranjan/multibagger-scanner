## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Brand Moat & Market Leadership:** Raymond commands an estimated ~60% share of the organised Indian suiting fabric market, with the "Raymond" trademark enjoying near-iconic recall built over 100 years[^ip-latest]. The portfolio spans four power brands — Raymond, Park Avenue, ColorPlus and Parx — covering the full price pyramid from premium suiting (Rs. 5,000–15,000/mtr) to mass premium casualwear[^ar-fy25]. This brand stack is difficult to replicate and forms the core pricing-power moat[^vp-thread].

- **Structural Margin Story — Renovated Store Productivity:** Per management commentary, renovated Raymond stores are delivering 25–40% YoY growth versus single-digit growth at unrenovated outlets[^cc-transcript]. The Made-to-Measure (MTM) vertical, positioned at ~1.5x premium to branded apparel, is at an inflection stage and is expected to drive 200–300 bps gross margin expansion as the mix shifts toward bespoke, higher-yield SKUs[^vp-thread]. EBIT margin trajectory is therefore expected to inflect from sub-5% currently toward 8–10% by FY28E[^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into Real Estate (Thane Land Bank):** The 146-acre mixed-use Thane land parcel — Phase I being residential — represents a ~Rs. 27,000 Cr gross development value opportunity (~Rs. 185 Cr/acre benchmarked to Mumbai micro-market)[^vp-thread]. Management has appointed a dedicated CEO (Mr. Abhishek Kapoor) and a full real estate team since May 2015, signalling intent to monetise in-house rather than via outright sale[^cc-transcript]. This is the single largest embedded value driver and is not adequately reflected in apparel-business multiples.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Launch of Phase I Thane residential project with pre-sales expected to commence by H2 FY27; (ii) Festive-season tailwind for the suiting business (Q3 FY27 historically contributes ~35% of annual textile revenue); (iii) Continued exit from non-core auto components / engineering businesses, with proceeds earmarked for debt reduction and brand capex[^cc-transcript]; (iv) Likely re-rating once quarterly ROCE normalises above 8% as one-offs roll off.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Real estate execution risk is the dominant overhang — Thane project timelines, RERA approvals, and absorption velocity in a softening Mumbai MMR micro-market could materially delay value unlocking[^vp-thread]. Secondary risks include (a) sustained weakness in the auto components / engineering segment dragging consolidated ROCE, and (b) any adverse RPT or promoter-related governance event that could compress the holding-company discount currently embedded in the stock[^vp-thread].

**Method 1 — P/E-based target:**

- **Base-case P/E target: Rs. 820.** Apply 34x target P/E to FY28E EPS of Rs. 24.07 = Rs. 818 ≈ Rs. 820.
- **Multiple justification:** 34x sits 10% below the BSE Consumer Discretionary index forward P/E of ~38x and is a discount to growth peers (Trent ~70x, Page Industries ~55x) reflecting Raymond's hybrid business mix (textile + real estate + auto components) and unfinished turnaround in the engineering segment.
- **Cross-check vs ROCE:** Implied ROCE at Rs. 820 market cap (~Rs. 5,350 Cr) and FY28E EBIT of Rs. 185 Cr is ~12-13% — in line with cost of equity for diversified manufacturing/realty plays, supporting the multiple as fair rather than expensive [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** Tinna Rubber & Infrastructure (Tinna Rubber) is a dominant player in the crumb rubber manufacturing segment, a critical input for various industries including road construction (crumb rubber modified bitumen - CRMB), automotive (recycled rubber products), and sports surfacing. The company benefits from a strong first-mover advantage, proprietary technology in devulcanization, and a robust collection network for end-of-life tires (ELT). This creates high entry barriers for new players, particularly in achieving consistent quality and scale, establishing Tinna Rubber's market leadership.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** The company is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-margin, value-added products like devulcanized rubber and specialized rubber compounds. This move away from basic crumb rubber, coupled with increasing operational efficiencies from capacity expansions and backward integration into processing ELTs, is expected to drive a structural improvement in EBITDA margins by 200-300 bps over the next 3-5 years. The focus on customized solutions for specific industrial applications further enhances pricing power.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Tinna Rubber is actively diversifying its revenue streams beyond traditional road construction into high-growth adjacencies. This includes expanding its presence in specialized rubber compounds for automotive components (e.g., anti-vibration mounts, sealing solutions), sports surfacing (athletic tracks, playgrounds), and other industrial applications. The company is also exploring opportunities in pyrolysis oil and carbon black recovery, leveraging its existing feedstock and processing capabilities, which could open new revenue avenues.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include the commissioning of its expanded devulcanized rubber capacity at the new facility, expected to ramp up production and sales in H2 FY25. Furthermore, increasing government impetus on sustainable infrastructure development and circular economy principles, potentially leading to higher adoption of CRMB in road projects and recycled content mandates, will directly benefit Tinna Rubber. New customer wins in the industrial and automotive segments for its specialized rubber compounds are also anticipated.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk for Tinna Rubber is its significant dependence on the availability and consistent quality of end-of-life tires (ELTs) as its primary raw material. Fluctuations in ELT collection costs, potential regulatory changes impacting ELT processing, or increased competition for feedstock could impact input costs and operational stability. While the company has a strong collection network, any disruption could affect its cost structure and profitability.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Tinna Rubber & Infrastructure Ltd. is a leading integrated waste tire recycling company in India, primarily engaged in the manufacturing of crumb rubber and other recycled rubber products. The company's core business model revolves around the collection and processing of end-of-life tires (ELTs) into various value-added rubber products.

*   **Core Business Model:** The company procures ELTs, processes them through mechanical and chemical means to produce crumb rubber (fine granulated rubber powder), devulcanized rubber, and specialized rubber compounds. These products are then supplied to various industries.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A estimates):**
    *   Crumb Rubber & Modified Bitumen: ~60-65% (primarily used in road construction as CRMB)
    *   Devulcanized Rubber & Specialized Compounds: ~25-30% (used in automotive, industrial, and sports applications)
    *   Other Recycled Rubber Products (e.g., rubber tiles, molded products): ~5-10%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Crumb Rubber & Modified Bitumen:** State Public Works Departments (PWDs), National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) contractors, local municipal corporations, and bitumen manufacturers.
    *   **Devulcanized Rubber & Specialized Compounds:** Tier-1 automotive component manufacturers (for anti-vibration parts, gaskets, seals), industrial manufacturers (for conveyor belts, rubber linings), sports surfacing companies.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** The company operates primarily through its standalone entity. No significant listed subsidiaries that materially alter the consolidated business profile.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** Tinna Rubber has a robust manufacturing footprint across India.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** Petronet LNG (PLL) holds a dominant position in India's LNG regasification infrastructure, operating two of the largest terminals: Dahej (Gujarat) with 17.5 MMTPA capacity and Kochi (Kerala) with 5 MMTPA. This creates a significant entry barrier due to high capital intensity, long project gestation periods, and strategic location advantages. The long-term take-or-pay contracts with anchor customers (IOCL, BPCL, GAIL, ONGC) provide revenue visibility and a stable earnings profile, acting as a strong economic moat.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** The company operates on a tolling model, charging a fixed regasification fee per MMTPA, which insulates it from commodity price volatility of LNG. The Dahej terminal, operating at over 90% utilization, benefits from economies of scale. Furthermore, the ongoing debottlenecking and expansion projects at Dahej (to 20 MMTPA by FY27E) and the improving utilization at Kochi (driven by pipeline connectivity expansion) are expected to drive incremental operating leverage and margin expansion in the medium term.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** PLL is strategically diversifying beyond core regasification. Key initiatives include the foray into LNG bunkering services (targeting marine fuel demand), small-scale LNG distribution for industrial and commercial customers in remote areas, and a potential floating storage and regasification unit (FSRU) project. These adjacencies tap into evolving energy demand patterns and offer new avenues for growth, leveraging PLL's existing infrastructure and expertise.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the commissioning of the Dahej expansion to 20 MMTPA (expected by Q4 FY27), which will immediately add incremental capacity and revenue. Further, the progress on the Kochi-Mangaluru natural gas pipeline and the impending commissioning of the Kochi-Bengaluru pipeline will significantly boost Kochi terminal's utilization, currently below optimal levels, driving a re-rating for the stock. Clarity on the long-term strategic plan for the Gopalpur FSRU project could also provide a positive trigger.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk for PLL is the underutilization of its Kochi terminal due to insufficient pipeline connectivity in South India. While progress has been made, any delays in further pipeline infrastructure development by GAIL or other entities could prolong the drag on profitability from the Kochi asset. Additionally, a significant shift in India's energy policy favoring domestic gas production or other alternative fuels over imported LNG could impact long-term demand growth.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Petronet LNG Limited (PLL) is India's premier natural gas infrastructure company, primarily engaged in the import and regasification of Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG). The company operates on a "toll-road" business model, charging a fixed fee for regasifying imported LNG for its customers.

*   **Core Business Model:** PLL imports LNG in its liquid form, stores it in cryogenic tanks at its terminals, and then regasifies it back into natural gas, which is then transported via pipelines to various customers. The company primarily enters into long-term (typically 20-25 years) take-or-pay contracts with its anchor customers, ensuring revenue predictability and high asset utilization.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A Estimates):**
    *   LNG Regasification & Terminal Services: ~98%
    *   Other Services (e.g., storage, bunkering): ~2%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Regasification:** Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. (IOCL), Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (BPCL), GAIL (India) Ltd., Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Ltd. (ONGC). These four public sector undertakings (PSUs) are also the promoters of PLL and off-take a significant portion of the regasified LNG. Other customers include various industrial users, city gas distribution companies, and power plants.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** As of FY25, PLL operates with minimal subsidiaries directly impacting its core regasification business. Its primary operations are consolidated under the parent entity. However, it has strategic investments and joint ventures for specific projects, such as an equity stake in a joint venture for the Dahej-Vijaipur pipeline.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:**
    *   **Dahej, Gujarat:** Operates India's largest LNG regasification terminal with a current capacity of 17.5 MMTPA. This terminal is strategically located near major industrial hubs and pipeline networks in Western and Northern India.
    *   **Kochi, Kerala:** Operates a 5 MMTPA LNG regasification terminal, catering primarily to demand in South India.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Petronet LNG Limited was formed as a joint venture by the Government of India to create an import facility for LNG, thereby enhancing India's energy security. Its promoters are four leading public sector energy companies: GAIL (India) Ltd., Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. (IOCL), Bharat Petroleum Corporation Ltd. (BPCL), and Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Ltd. (ONGC). Each holds a 12.5% stake, totaling 50% promoter holding. This unique structure provides PLL with strong backing, assured off-take, and strategic alignment with India's energy policy. The promoters are also key customers, creating a symbiotic relationship.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Multi-pronged technology moat — "only-player" status in 4 critical products:** HBL is the only Indian manufacturer of PLT (Pure Lead Tin) batteries (only 2 global suppliers, addressing the data-centre / AI capex cycle), 1 of only 3 Indian Railways-approved vendors for Kavach (TCAS), the only Indian developer of TMS (Train Management System), and the world's 2nd largest manufacturer of Ni-Cd batteries. The 2–3+ year vendor-approval runway for Kavach creates a structural entry barrier for new competitors.

- **Structural margin re-rating story ahead:** Management has explicitly flagged that R&D for new products (Kavach, TMS, electro-optics, fuzes/grenades) has been largely expensed, which is the primary reason profitability has been subdued. FY24 capex peak is behind us; the next 2–3 years will see lower R&D and capex intensity, and operating leverage on a high-fixed-cost base should expand EBITDA margins by 200–300 bps over FY26–28E. The legacy industrial-battery business provides cash-flow ballast to fund the higher-margin electronics ramp.

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies — Railway + Defence + Data Centre:** (a) Railway Electronics: Kavach + TMS + station electronics — Rs. 15,000–20,000 cr TAM over 4–5 years (35,000 Rkm rollout); (b) Defence: Tonbo Imaging partnership + electro-optics, fuzes, grenades — riding the Make-in-India indigenisation wave; (c) PLT batteries: addressable in India + global data-centre UPS market; (d) Electric Mobility: niche battery packs / charging components.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Next round of Kavach tenders from Indian Railways — HBL has already won c. Rs. 600 cr of the c. Rs. 2,000 cr awarded in the current round; (2) First revenue recognition from TMS pilots; (3) Defence order-book expansion through the Tonbo Imaging platform (referenced in the management's first investor presentation dated 13 Feb 2026); (4) PLT order wins from data-centre clients; (5) FY27 guidance refresh / capacity expansion announcements.

- **Biggest structural risk:** Customer concentration with Indian Railways — Railways is the dominant buyer of the largest growth engine, and any slowdown in Kavach rollout, budget reallocation, or vendor diversification (e.g., 4th vendor approval) would materially impact the thesis. Secondary risks: working capital elongation (Railway receivables historically stretch 6–9 months), simultaneous product-ramp execution risk (Kavach + TMS + defence), and global competition in PLT from majors (EnerSys, East Penn).

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** HBL Power Systems is a Hyderabad-based, technology-led manufacturing company with four operating verticals — (1) Industrial Batteries (Ni-Cd, lead-acid, PLT), (2) Railway Electronics (Kavach/TCAS, TMS, station signalling), (3) Defence Electronics (electro-optics via Tonbo, fuzes, grenades), and (4) Electric Mobility & New Energy. The fundamental identity is a "technology-solutions" business — identifying capability gaps in Indian railways and defence, and developing in-house, internationally benchmarked products.

**Revenue split by division (indicative, % of FY25A revenue):**
- Industrial Batteries: ~55–60% (Ni-Cd, lead-acid, PLT)
- Railway Electronics: ~20–25% (Kavach, TMS, signalling — fastest growing)
- Defence Electronics: ~10–12% (Tonbo, electro-optics, fuzes/grenades)
- Electric Mobility & Others: ~5–8%

**Key OEM / end customers by division:**
- Industrial Batteries: Indian Railways, telecom tower companies, data centres (domestic + global), industrial OEMs
- Railway Electronics: Indian Railways (direct + via project partners such as RVNL/IRCON), Metro Rail corporations
- Defence: DRDO labs, Indian Army, defence PSUs (HAL, BEL), international defence customers via Tonbo
- Electric Mobility: EV OEMs, fleet operators, PSU tenders

**Subsidiary / JV structure:**
- HBL-Tonbo Imaging (defence electro-optics platform — strategic partnership/JV)
- SCIL Infracon (railway project execution arm)
- Multiple step-down subsidiaries for product-specific manufacturing and exports

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- Telangana (Hyderabad — corporate HQ, central R&D, battery + electronics manufacturing)
- Andhra Pradesh (Visakhapatnam — defence electronics)
- Karnataka (battery manufacturing)
- Exports to 50+ countries across industrial-battery and defence-electronics divisions

**Promoter / group background:** Founded by Dr. A. J. Prasad, a first-generation technocrat entrepreneur, in 1977 — giving the company c. 49 years of operating history. Family-promoted; promoter and promoter group historically hold the majority stake, with continuity of vision and involvement of next-generation family members in operations. Listed on both NSE (HBLPOWER) and BSE.

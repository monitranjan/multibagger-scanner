## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** Man Industries is one of India's largest manufacturers and exporters of large-diameter Carbon Steel Line Pipes, with a total installed capacity of ~1 million tonnes p.a. split equally between LSAW (500,000 MT) and HSAW (500,000 MT) pipes [^ip-latest]. The company holds API certifications mandatory for high-pressure hydrocarbon line pipe applications, creating a regulatory moat that restricts new entrants [^ar-fy25]. Strategic port-proximate locations at Anjar (Gujarat) and Pithampur (Madhya Pradesh) provide logistical cost advantages of ~150–200 bps versus inland competitors [^ip-latest].

- **Structural Margin Story:** EBITDA margins have expanded from sub-8% historically to mid-teens in recent quarters, driven by (i) a richer export order mix (Middle East, Africa, Americas), (ii) value-added anti-corrosion coating revenue (FBE, Coal Tar Enamel, 3LPE) layered on base pipe sales, and (iii) capacity utilization moving from ~60% to ~85%+ post-COVID [^cc-transcript]. Management has guided to sustained 14–16% EBITDA margins on the back of a Rs. 4,500+ Cr order book providing 18–24 months of revenue visibility [^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Beyond core oil & gas line pipes, the company is pivoting into (a) potable water transmission pipelines ( Jal Jeevan Mission tailwind), (b) city gas distribution (CGD) infrastructure for players like GAIL/IGL, and (c) structural piling for infra capex [^cc-transcript]. The coating systems business — historically a captive ancillary — is now being marketed to third-party pipe manufacturers, opening a higher-margin services revenue stream [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Preferential allotment of 12,19,512 convertible warrants to promoters and 77,74,383 equity shares to non-promoters completed in Q1 FY27 — a strong insider confidence signal [^ip-latest]; (2) Monitoring Agency Report filed 12 Aug 2026 confirming end-use of preferential issue proceeds for capex/working capital [^ip-latest]; (3) Likely resolution of the long-pending Man Infraprojects demerger/distribution overhang, which has historically capped re-rating [^vp-thread]; (4) Order inflows from Middle East hydrocarbon projects (Aramco, ADNOC) expected to be announced in H2 FY27 [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** The Man Group promoter family dispute remains the single largest overhang on the stock, periodically creating governance uncertainty and capping institutional investor participation [^vp-thread]. Additionally, the business is exposed to global steel HRC price volatility (input cost ~70% of revenue) and lumpy export order timing, which can cause quarter-to-quarter EBITDA variance of 200–400 bps [^cc-transcript].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Man Industries (India) Limited manufactures large-diameter carbon steel line pipes used in cross-country transmission of crude oil, natural gas, petrochemicals, and potable water. The company operates an integrated model spanning pipe manufacturing (LSAW + HSAW) and anti-corrosion coating application, enabling it to capture value across the supply chain [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (Estimated FY25):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key End-Use |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| LSAW Line Pipes (16"–56") | ~45% | Cross-country oil & gas, water mains |
| HSAW Line Pipes (18"–130") | ~40% | Hydrocarbon transmission, structural |
| Anti-Corrosion Coating (FBE, 3LPE, CTE) | ~12% | Captive + third-party coating services |
| Others (wind power, scrap) | ~3% | 7 MW windmills in Gujarat; ancillary |

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- **Oil & Gas (Export):** Middle East national oil companies, African hydrocarbon projects, Latin American pipeline operators [^cc-transcript]
- **Oil & Gas (Domestic):** GAIL, IOCL, HPCL, Reliance, Adani Gas for CGD networks [^ar-fy25]
- **Water:** Government utilities under Jal Jeevan Mission / AMRUT schemes [^cc-transcript]

**Subsidiary Structure:** The Man Group historically comprised Man Industries (MANINDS) and Man Infraprojects (MIPL). A demerger scheme was announced to distribute MIPL shares to MANINDS shareholders in a 1:1 ratio, intended to unlock the real estate value of MIPL's Mumbai landbank separately from the steel pipe business [^vp-thread]. The demerger has been delayed by promoter family disputes but remains a latent value-unlock trigger.

**Manufacturing Footprint:**

| Location | State | Products | Land Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Anjar, Kutch | Gujarat | LSAW + Coating | ~100 acres |
| Pithampur | Madhya Pradesh | HSAW + Coating | ~50 acres |
| Windmills | Gujarat | Power (captive) | 7 MW capacity |

Both plants are strategically located within 50–80 km of major ports (Kandla, Mundra), enabling efficient export logistics [^ar-fy25].

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** The Man Group was incorporated in 1988 and is promoted by the Singh family. The group has historically been associated with large-diameter pipe manufacturing and real estate development through MIPL. Promoter holding stands at 57.29%, with the balance held by public (41.97%), FIIs (0.44%), and DIIs (0.29%) — indicating very limited institutional coverage and significant re-rating potential if governance normalizes [^ip-latest].

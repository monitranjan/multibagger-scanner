### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant domestic moat in Polyisobutylene (PIB):** Kothari Petrochemicals commands an estimated 60–80% domestic market share in PIB compounds — a niche, technically demanding specialty polymer — and ranks among the largest PIB producers globally in a ~USD 2 Bn addressable market [^vp-thread]. The company is the single largest producer of PIB compounds in India, with limited domestic competition and high entry barriers due to raw material (C4 hydrocarbon stream) access constraints [^vp-thread].

- **Structural margin expansion story:** The shift to formula-linked pricing arrangements (pass-through of raw material and utility costs) protects gross margins from C4 feedstock volatility, while the commissioning of the INR 6 Cr HR-PIB pilot plant at Manali is expected to lift blended realisations and EBITDA margins by 200–300 bps over FY26–FY28E [^vp-thread]. Q1 FY27 results demonstrated extraordinary resilience — revenues and profits grew sharply YoY even as peer specialty chemical companies dependent on crude derivatives reported weak numbers [^vp-thread].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies reducing auto cyclicality:** While traditional PIB demand is tied to 2T engine oils and lubricants (auto-linked), the company is actively expanding into adhesives, cosmetics, plastic masterbatch, rubber compounds, tyre and sealants — applications that are structurally growing and largely insulated from EV penetration risk [^vp-thread]. High-molecular-weight (HM) PIB, which constitutes ~65% of the global PIB market, is a recent diversification thrust with significant revenue headroom [^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Full ramp-up of HR-PIB pilot capacity at Manali; (ii) restoration of normal raw material supply levels (per 2 Jul 2026 exchange filing) supporting volume recovery in H2 FY27 [^ar-fy25]; (iii) potential credit rating upgrade following the 13 Jul 2026 rating review announcement; (iv) Q1 FY27 results (filed 14 Aug 2026) likely to confirm margin trajectory and HR-PIB commercialisation progress [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Raw material concentration risk — PIB production depends on C4 hydrocarbon streams from refineries, naphtha crackers and MTBE crackers, and historical supply disruptions have forced industry-wide plant shutdowns [^vp-thread]. Additionally, the threat of resumption of imports from large global players (BASF, TPC Group, DAELIM, Chinese producers) once domestic supply normalises could compress pricing power, though limited historical dumping suggests this risk is moderate rather than acute [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
Kothari Petrochemicals manufactures Polyisobutylene (PIB) compounds — a specialty polymer derived from the C4 hydrocarbon stream — used primarily as viscosity modifiers in lubricants, additives in two-stroke (2T) engine oils, and as base polymers in adhesives, sealants, rubber compounds, cosmetics and plastic masterbatch [^vp-thread]. The business operates on a B2B specialty chemicals model with long-standing customer relationships in the lubricant and automotive aftermarket segments.

**Revenue Split by Division:**
- **Conventional PIB (Low/Medium Molecular Weight):** ~80–85% of revenue — primarily serving lubricant formulators and 2T engine oil blenders [^vp-thread]
- **High Molecular Weight (HM) PIB / HR-PIB:** ~10–15% of revenue (rising) — newer applications in adhesives, tyre, sealants, and rubber [^vp-thread]
- **Pilot / R&D Pipeline:** HR-PIB capacity at Manali (INR 6 Cr investment) expected to commercialise in FY27 [^vp-thread]

**Key OEM / End Customers:**
- Domestic lubricant majors and 2T oil blenders (customer names not publicly disclosed due to confidentiality)
- Adhesive and sealant manufacturers
- Rubber and tyre compounders
- Cosmetic and personal care formulators [^vp-thread]

**Subsidiary Structure:**
Kothari Petrochemicals operates as a standalone entity with no material subsidiaries disclosed in recent filings; the Manali HR-PIB pilot plant is an in-house capex project [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Primary manufacturing facility at Manali, Tamil Nadu (including the new HR-PIB pilot plant) [^vp-thread]
- Plant count: 1–2 integrated manufacturing sites (specific count not separately disclosed)
- Geographic concentration in South India, with distribution pan-India [^ar-fy25]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
Kothari Petrochemicals is a professionally managed company with 100% public shareholding and no identifiable single promoter group [^ar-fy25]. The "Kothari" branding reflects legacy association with the broader Kothari business family, though current management operates independently. The company has historically maintained a low-profile, debt-averse operating philosophy with strong working capital discipline (~30 days) [^vp-thread].

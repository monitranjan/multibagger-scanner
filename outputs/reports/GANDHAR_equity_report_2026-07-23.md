### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

* **Market leadership in niche white oils with global scale.** Gandhar Oil Refinery (India) Ltd (GORIL) is India's largest white oil manufacturer and ranks among the top-5 global players, with a stated ~9% share of the global white oil market [^vp-thread]. The company exports to 100+ countries, giving it a structural export franchise that is rare among Indian specialty chemical mid-caps and de-risks dependence on domestic cyclicality [^ip-latest].
* **Structural margin story anchored in Personal Care, Healthcare & Performance Oils (PHPO).** PHPO contributes ~52.3% of FY24 revenue and is the highest-margin vertical within the portfolio, with the Personal Care sub-segment commanding premium realisations due to pharmaceutical/cosmetic grade certifications [^vp-thread]. Management has guided to margin expansion in Q3-Q4 FY26 on the back of Silvassa (~85-90% utilisation), Taloja (~95% utilisation) and Texol UAE (~70-72% utilisation) operating leverage [^cc-transcript].
* **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies — Lubricants (Divyol) and Process Insulating Oils.** The Lubricants division (~31% of FY24 revenue) is scaling under the owned brand "Divyol", which gives GORIL a direct B2C channel and reduces dependence on institutional tendering — a key moat extension [^vp-thread]. The Process Insulating Oil segment (~7% of FY24 revenue) is benefiting from the transformer/distribution capex cycle driven by India's grid modernisation programme [^ip-latest].
* **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months).** (i) Q1 FY27 results showed a sharp sequential recovery in PAT, with the board declaring an interim dividend of Rs. 2/share (100% of FV) — a clear signal of management confidence in cash generation [^ip-latest]; (ii) Texol UAE ramp-up to full utilisation over the next 1.5–2 years provides incremental USD-denominated revenue at likely 200-300 bps higher margins; (iii) Capacity utilisation at Taloja at ~95% is approaching saturation, foreshadowing brownfield capex/capacity announcement as a re-rating trigger [^cc-transcript]; (iv) Continued promoter open-market buying observed in Jan-Feb signals insider conviction at current levels [^vp-thread].
* **Biggest structural risk — margin volatility from base oil spread compression.** GORIL's EBITDA margins are highly sensitive to the crude-by-product base oil price differential, which has compressed materially in FY26 (ROCE at just 6.11% vs. historical 18-22% range) [^ip-latest]. A continued squeeze — or a sharp reversal of the recent spread normalisation — could keep FY27E PAT below normalised levels. Additional overhang: low institutional shareholding (FII 1.98%, DII 1.32%) means liquidity-driven drawdowns are likely if Q2/Q3 FY27 fails to demonstrate the trajectory implied by Q1 [^vp-thread].

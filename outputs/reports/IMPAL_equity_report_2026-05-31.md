### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** IMPAL holds a strong position in the organized automotive aftermarket distribution segment, particularly for commercial vehicle (CV) and tractor parts. Its extensive pan-India network of over 100 branches and 17,000+ retailers, coupled with exclusive distribution rights for premium brands like TVS Girling, Rane, and Brakes India, creates a significant entry barrier and strong brand recall. The company's deep relationships with OEMs and its proprietary supply chain management system further solidify its competitive moat.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** The shift towards higher-margin product categories, including braking systems, engine components, and electrical parts, is expected to drive margin expansion. Furthermore, the company's focus on operational efficiencies through inventory optimization and leveraging its digital ordering platform (B2B portal) should lead to a sustained improvement in EBITDA margins by 50-75 bps over the next 2-3 years, moving towards 8.5-9.0%.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** IMPAL is strategically expanding its product portfolio into high-growth segments such as electric vehicle (EV) components for the aftermarket, lubricants, and digital solutions for garage services. While nascent, these initiatives are expected to contribute meaningfully to revenue growth and diversify risk from traditional IC engine parts over the medium to long term, capitalizing on the evolving automotive landscape.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the sustained recovery in CV sales, leading to higher aftermarket demand, and the implementation of stricter vehicle inspection norms which will drive replacement cycles. Additionally, the successful rollout and adoption of its new dealer incentive programs and digital ordering platform are expected to boost sales volumes and improve operational efficiency in FY27E.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is the increasing threat from unorganized players and counterfeit products in the aftermarket, which can exert pricing pressure and dilute brand value. While IMPAL's focus on genuine parts and strong brand tie-ups mitigates this to an extent, a significant surge in counterfeit products or aggressive pricing by unorganized channels could impact market share and profitability.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** India Motor Parts And Accessories Limited (IMPAL) operates as a leading distributor of automotive spare parts for various vehicle segments across India. The company procures genuine spare parts directly from Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) and Tier-1 suppliers and distributes them through its extensive network of branches and retailers to independent garages, workshops, and end-users.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (% of Revenue, FY25A estimates):**
    *   Commercial Vehicle (CV) Parts: ~55% (Brakes, Engine Parts, Suspension)
    *   Tractor Parts: ~20% (Engine Components, Transmission Parts)
    *   Passenger Vehicle (PV) Parts: ~15% (Filters, Electricals, Belts)
    *   Two-Wheeler & Others: ~10% (Lubricants, Garage Tools, Accessories)
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **CV Parts:** Ashok Leyland, Tata Motors, Daimler India (through authorized suppliers like Brakes India, Rane, Wheels India). End customers are fleet operators, transport companies, and independent CV repair workshops.
    *   **Tractor Parts:** Mahindra & Mahindra, TAFE, Escorts Kubota (through suppliers like Rane, TVS Srichakra). End customers are farmers and agricultural equipment repair centers.
    *   **PV Parts:** Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai, Tata Motors (through suppliers like Bosch, Gabriel India). End customers are multi-brand car service centers and independent garages.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** IMPAL primarily operates as a standalone entity with no significant wholly-owned subsidiaries impacting core operations.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** IMPAL is purely a distribution company and does not have its own manufacturing facilities. Its operational footprint consists of a network of strategically located warehouses and over 100 sales branches spread across various states in India, ensuring efficient logistics and last-mile delivery. Key states with a strong presence include Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, and West Bengal.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** IMPAL is part of the TVS Group, a highly respected Indian conglomerate with a rich heritage in automotive manufacturing, distribution, and financial services. The promoters bring deep industry knowledge, strong ethical values, and a long-term vision. The TVS Group's reputation for quality and reliability provides a significant advantage in forging strong supplier relationships and maintaining customer trust.

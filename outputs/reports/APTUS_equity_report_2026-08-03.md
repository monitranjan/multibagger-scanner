## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Differentiated low/middle-income HFC with a defensible South India moat:** Aptus has built a 218-branch network concentrated in Tier-2/3 South Indian markets (Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka) catering predominantly to the Low-Income Group (LIG) and self-employed segments, with average ticket size ~Rs. 8-10 lakh — a customer cohort structurally under-served by banks and large HFCs (HDFC, LIC Housing) [^ip-latest]. This is a Strong moat given 13+ years of operational vintage, granular 40%+ new-to-credit customer base, and field-level underwriting expertise that is difficult to replicate [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural spread / NIM story intact at 7%+ even as industry compresses:** Disbursement yields of ~16-17% on the asset book (vs. 8-9% benchmark home loan rates) reflect a genuine credit-risk premium rather than teaser pricing, given that 73% customers are LIG and a large share is self-employed with thin documentation [^ip-latest]. Cost of funds at ~8-9% (a mix of NHB refinance, bank term loans, NCDs and sub-debt) keeps reported NIMs in the 7.0-7.5% band — among the highest in the listed HFC peer set [^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into adjacent asset classes (SBL 22%, Quasi-Housing 16%, Top-Up 4%) reduces single-product concentration risk** and lifts blended yield. Management has guided expansion into Maharashtra, Odisha and Chhattisgarh to derisk South India geographic concentration from current ~95%+ to ~80% over 3 years [^cc-transcript]. The Small Business Loan (SBL) book, while microfinance-like, has shown stress-cycle resilience with GNPA < 1% even through demonetisation, IL&FS, and COVID-19 [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 PAT print confirming post-IPO AUM trajectory beyond Rs. 15,000 Cr mark with steady spreads; (ii) incremental branch additions in MH/OD/CG driving first disbursements by Q3FY27; (iii) potential NHB refinance line sanctioning and incremental bank sanctions expected to lower cost of funds by 30-50 bps; (iv) re-rating trigger as AUM crosses the Rs. 20,000 Cr threshold where institutional liquidity typically improves [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk — geographic + customer concentration in a thin-file segment:** ~95% AUM from 4 South Indian states and 73% LIG / self-employed customers with limited formal income documentation creates a tail risk during regional shocks (cyclones, droughts, political disruption in AP/TN) that could simultaneously impact both collection efficiency and fresh disbursements [^vp-thread]. Granular portfolio (avg ticket ~Rs. 9 lakh, 100,000+ customers) provides diversification at the loan level, but does not insulate against state-level macro stress — this is the single most-watched metric on concalls [^cc-transcript].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core business model:** Aptus Value Housing Finance is a Chennai-headquartered, RBI-registered HFC (NHB classified) primarily originating low-ticket housing loans, quasi-housing loans, small business loans (SBL), top-up loans and insurance-backed loans to the self-employed and LIG segment in Tier-2/3 India [^ip-latest]. Founded in 2009-10 by Mr. P. Anandan (ex-Murugappa Group CFO, 30+ years), the company listed on the bourses in Aug 2021 and currently operates a 218-branch retail network [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread].

- **Revenue / AUM split by division (per FY25 disclosures):**
  - Housing Loans: ~54% of AUM
  - Quasi-Housing Loans: ~16% of AUM
  - Small Business Loans (SBL): ~22% of AUM
  - Top-Up Loans: ~4% of AUM
  - Insurance / Other Loans: ~4% of AUM [^ip-latest]

- **Geographic AUM split (per latest investor presentation):**
  - Tamil Nadu: 79 branches, ~50% of AUM
  - Andhra Pradesh: 69 branches, ~29% of AUM
  - Telangana: 29 branches, ~11% of AUM
  - Karnataka: 21 branches, ~10% of AUM [^ip-latest]

- **End customer profile:** LIG ~73%, self-employed majority, ~40% new-to-credit (no prior bureau history) [^ar-fy25]. Average ticket size ~Rs. 8-10 lakh housing, ~Rs. 4-5 lakh SBL [^vp-thread]. Average loan-to-value (LTV) ~55-60% in housing, providing structural downside protection [^cc-transcript].

- **Key OEM/end customers:** Aptus does not serve OEM-style customers in the auto-component sense — its counterparties are individual retail borrowers. Institutional lender relationships (liabilities side) include HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, SBI, Bank of Baroda, Union Bank, NHB, IFC (World Bank arm), and several mutual fund houses for NCD subscriptions [^ar-fy25] [^cc-transcript].

- **Subsidiary / JV structure:** Aptus has a wholly-owned housing finance subsidiary structure with a co-lending arrangement framework with select PSU banks; no material subsidiary listed entity. The company has entered a strategic co-lending model to scale the SBL book without balance sheet expansion at the same pace [^ar-fy25].

- **Manufacturing / branch footprint:** "Plant equivalent" for an HFC is the branch network. Current count: 218 branches across 4 southern states (TN, AP, Telangana, Karnataka); head office at Chennai [^ip-latest]. Expansion plan into Maharashtra, Odisha, Chhattisgarh flagged — first branches expected operational in FY27 [^cc-transcript].

- **Promoter & group background:** Promoter Mr. P. Anandan (founder-Chairman), a Chartered Accountant with 3 decades at Murugappa Group (incl. CFO role), post-retirement founded Aptus in 2009-10. Promoter holding stands at 26.60% as of latest filings (down from ~31% pre-IPO, normal post-listing dilution pattern) [^vp-thread]. Other major shareholders include FIIs (29.44%) and DIIs (19.62%) — a healthy institutional mix supportive of float and price discovery [^ar-fy25].

In USD terms, total housing finance credit outstanding is ~USD 320-340 Bn with the affordable sub-segment at ~USD 35-40 Bn [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25].

- **Policy tailwinds:**
  - **PMAY-U 2.0** (relaunched FY25-26) targeting 1 Cr houses with credit-linked subsidy; direct demand pull for HFCs serving EWS/LIG [^ip-latest].
  - **RBI priority sector lending (PSL)** status on housing loans up to Rs. 35 lakh (metro) / Rs. 25 lakh (non-metro) gives banks incentive to co-lend with HFCs like Aptus [^ar-fy25].

  - **RBI risk-weight rationalisation (Apr 2025)** on housing loans above Rs. 75 lakh — neutral for Aptus as 95%+ of its book is sub-Rs. 30 lakh ticket [^ip-latest].

  - **Underwriting IP (thin-file customers) — Strong:** 13+ year vintage, GNPA < 1% across cycles, proprietary field-underwriting model [^vp-thread] [^cc-transcript].

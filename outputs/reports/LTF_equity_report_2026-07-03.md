### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Rural & Semi-Urban Financing:** L&T Finance (LTF) has successfully pivoted its business model towards high-growth, high-yield retail segments, particularly in rural and semi-urban India. Its strong parentage (L&T Group), established branch network of over 2,000 customer touchpoints (as of FY24), and deep understanding of local markets provide a significant competitive moat in two-wheeler finance, farm equipment finance, and microfinance. This specialized focus, coupled with robust underwriting and collection capabilities, positions LTF as a leader in these underserved yet burgeoning segments.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion through Retailisation:** The "Right to Win" strategy, focused on transforming into a retail finance company, is driving structural margin expansion. The retail portfolio now constitutes ~90% of the total loan book, up from ~50% in FY21. This shift towards higher-yielding retail assets (e.g., microfinance ~20% yield, two-wheeler ~15% yield) and a reduction in lower-margin wholesale and infrastructure finance is expected to expand Net Interest Margins (NIMs) by 50-75 bps over the next 2-3 years. Coupled with improved asset quality and lower credit costs, this transformation is poised to deliver superior profitability.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjuncts via Digitalisation:** LTF is strategically leveraging its digital platforms to penetrate new, high-growth adjacencies and enhance customer experience. Its "Planet App" and other digital initiatives are enabling faster loan disbursements, improved customer onboarding, and efficient cross-selling of products like consumer loans and personal loans. This digital-first approach in its core segments, along with prudent expansion into new, synergistic product offerings, will drive incremental revenue streams and market share gains without significant physical branch expansion.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include continued strong disbursement growth in retail segments (e.g., 20%+ YoY in two-wheeler and farm equipment finance), further reduction in GNPA/NNPA ratios as legacy wholesale assets run down, and successful integration of digital lending solutions leading to operational efficiencies. The ongoing economic recovery in rural India, driven by good monsoons and government spending on infrastructure, will further support asset quality and growth for LTF's core segments. Additionally, any potential re-rating post the complete exit from wholesale finance would be a significant trigger.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Interest Rate Volatility & Competition:** The primary structural risk for LTF stems from sustained high interest rates, which could compress NIMs and impact asset quality, particularly in rate-sensitive retail segments. Intense competition from large banks and other well-capitalized NBFCs, especially in the rapidly digitizing lending space, poses a challenge to market share and pricing power. While LTF's established niche offers some protection, aggressive pricing by competitors could force a trade-off between growth and profitability.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** L&T Finance Ltd (LTF) operates as a diversified non-banking financial company (NBFC) with a strategic focus on retail finance. Its primary business revolves around providing financial products and services to individuals and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in rural and semi-urban India. The company has significantly de-risked its balance sheet by exiting most of its wholesale and infrastructure finance businesses, transitioning to a retail-focused entity.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   Rural Business (Farm Equipment, Two-Wheeler, Microfinance): ~60-65%
    *   Housing Finance (Home Loans, Loan Against Property): ~20-25%
    *   Infrastructure Finance (Legacy & Select Project Finance): ~10-15% (rapidly declining)
    *   Other Retail (Personal Loans, SME Finance): <5%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Farm Equipment Finance:** Farmers, dealers of leading tractor manufacturers (e.g., Mahindra & Mahindra, TAFE, John Deere).
    *   **Two-Wheeler Finance:** Individual buyers, dealerships of major two-wheeler brands (e.g., Hero MotoCorp, Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor Company).
    *   **Microfinance:** Women borrowers in Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) across rural and semi-urban areas.
    *   **Housing Finance:** Salaried and self-employed individuals for home purchase/construction, small businesses for Loan Against Property.
    *   **Infrastructure Finance:** Select infrastructure developers (largely a run-down book).
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** LTF operates primarily through its wholly-owned subsidiaries, L&T Finance Limited (the listed entity), and L&T Housing Finance Limited, among others. The company has streamlined its structure following the merger of its lending subsidiaries in December 2022 to optimize capital and operational efficiencies.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint (states, plant count):** As an NBFC, LTF does not have a "manufacturing footprint" in the traditional sense. Its operational footprint consists of a vast network of physical branches and customer touchpoints across India. As of FY24, LTF boasts over 2,000 customer touchpoints, with a significant presence in rural and semi-urban areas across various states, facilitating direct customer interaction and loan disbursement/collection.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** L&T Finance Ltd is promoted by the Larsen & Toubro Group, one of India's largest and most respected conglomerates with a legacy spanning over eight decades. The L&T Group has diversified interests across engineering, construction, manufacturing, IT services, and financial services. The strong backing of the L&T Group provides LTF with significant brand recall, financial stability, and access to capital markets, enhancing its credibility and operational leverage in the financial services sector.

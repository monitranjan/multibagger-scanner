### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Specialist moat in antique bridal gold jewellery:** RBZ occupies a defensible niche as a design-led B2B manufacturer of antique bridal gold jewellery, with a 24,000 sq. ft. manufacturing facility capable of producing 1,700–2,000 kg of gold per year [^ip-latest]. The company is a strategic vendor partner to Titan — the highest-quality customer endorsement possible in Indian jewellery — alongside Malabar, Joyalukkas, Senco, Bhima, Kalamandir and P N Gadgil [^vp-thread]. This customer roster is the single most important moat: switching costs for national retailers are high given RBZ's design IP and proven execution on large bridal orders.

- **Asset-light B2B model with structural margin resilience:** The job-work and wholesale model insulates RBZ from retail inventory risk and gold price volatility on the buy-side, while design and craftsmanship capture value-add. Management has explicitly stated they are not pursuing the Aditya Birla (Indriya) account as existing client wallet share is already strong [^vp-thread] — a disciplined capital allocation signal. We model EBITDA margins holding in the 8–10% range with ~50–80 bps expansion potential as scale builds on the recently-IPO'd capital base.

- **Retail optionality via the Harit Zaveri brand:** The 11,667 sq. ft. flagship "Harit Zaveri" showroom in Ahmedabad provides a captive retail demand sink and brand-building vehicle [^ip-latest]. While retail is currently a small revenue contributor, it offers a structural margin uplift path (retail jewellery EBITDA margins run 200–400 bps above wholesale) and a hedge against B2B customer concentration. Any incremental retail expansion would be a re-rating catalyst.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results just announced on 11 Aug 2026 — first full quarter post-IPO, expect revenue growth disclosure and margin trajectory [^cc-transcript]; (ii) Festive season (Oct–Nov 2026) typically drives 30–35% of annual jewellery demand — direct tailwind for both wholesale order book and Harit Zaveri walk-ins; (iii) Continued formalization of the unorganized sector (currently 60% of retail jewellery) under hallmarking mandates and GST tightening [^ar-fy25]; (iv) Potential institutional investor discovery as the stock completes 12 months of trading history post-listing.

- **Biggest structural risk — gold price volatility and customer concentration:** RBZ carries working capital intensity tied to gold inventory cycles, and the top 3 customers (Titan, Malabar, Joyalukkas) likely account for >50% of revenue [^vp-thread]. A sharp gold price correction could compress realisations, while loss of any single anchor customer would be material. The 78.74% promoter holding also means limited free float (Rs. ~120 Cr) — liquidity risk for institutional sizing [^ip-latest].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** RBZ Jewellers is a vertically-integrated gold jewellery player operating across two distinct revenue engines — (a) B2B design-led manufacturing and job-work for national retailers, and (b) B2C retail through the legacy "Harit Zaveri" brand in Ahmedabad [^ip-latest].

**Revenue split by division (estimated):**
- **Job Work & Wholesale (Antique Bridal Gold):** ~85–90% of revenue — designs, manufactures and distributes antique bridal gold jewellery on wholesale and job-work basis to national retailers [^vp-thread].
- **Retail ("Harit Zaveri" flagship):** ~10–15% of revenue — bridal, occasional and daily-wear jewellery across price ranges, sold through the 11,667 sq. ft. Ahmedabad showroom [^ip-latest].

**Key OEM/end customers per division:**
- *Wholesale/Job Work:* Titan (strategic vendor partner), Malabar Gold, Joyalukkas, Senco Gold, Bhima Jewellers, Kalamandir (West), P N Gadgil (West) [^vp-thread].
- *Retail:* Walk-in consumers in Ahmedabad and surrounding Gujarat markets via Harit Zaveri.

**Subsidiary structure:** RBZ operates as a single-entity standalone company with no material subsidiaries disclosed in the latest investor presentation [^ip-latest]. The Harit Zaveri retail brand is operated as a division/brand of the listed entity rather than a separate subsidiary.

**Manufacturing footprint:**
- *Manufacturing facility:* ~24,000 sq. ft. with annual capacity of 1,700–2,000 kg of gold jewellery [^vp-thread].
- *Retail footprint:* 1 flagship showroom — Harit Zaveri, 11,667 sq. ft., Ahmedabad, Gujarat [^ip-latest].
- *Geographic concentration:* Operations are heavily Gujarat-centric (manufacturing + retail), with wholesale distribution pan-India via customer retailer networks.

**Promoter background and group context:** The promoter group traces its lineage to the Harit Zaveri jewellery house — a multi-generational Gujarat family with deep roots in Ahmedabad's jewellery trade. The 78.74% promoter holding reflects the family's continued controlling stake post-IPO [^ip-latest]. The "Harit Zaveri" brand carries legacy brand equity in the Gujarat market, providing RBZ a defensible retail position in its home turf.

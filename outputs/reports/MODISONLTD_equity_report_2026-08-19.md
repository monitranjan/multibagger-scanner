### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

**1. Dominant Market Leadership & Structural Moat in Electrical Contacts:**
Modison is the **sole Indian manufacturer of electrical contacts across LV, MV, HV, and EHV switchgear segments under a single roof** [^ip-latest]. The company commands an estimated **78–82% market share in High Voltage (HV) contacts** and **30–35% in Low Voltage (LV)** in India [^cc-transcript]. This near-monopolistic position in HV is reinforced by decades of technical collaboration with Doduco (Germany) since 1983, proprietary copper-chrome alloy development, and ISRO recognition as an import substitute for brazing alloy foils [^ar-fy25]. The "amongst largest single-site HV electrical contact facilities globally" positioning creates significant entry barriers for new competitors [^ip-latest].

**2. Structural Margin Expansion Story:**
EBITDA margins expanded sharply from **9.43% in FY24 to 9.60% in FY25 to 16.10% in FY26** — a 667 bps expansion over two years [^ar-fy25]. This margin trajectory is driven by operating leverage on a 44.89% revenue surge to ₹71,033 Lakhs in FY26, premium pricing in HV (where competition is negligible), and successful pass-through of silver/tungsten price escalations via customer agreements [^cc-transcript]. Management has guided for sustained **10–12% PAT margin** going forward, with FY27 revenue target of ~₹880 Cr implying ~19–20% growth [^cc-transcript]. The structural shift from a commodity contact supplier to a technology-led specialty player is now visible in the P&L.

**3. Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:**
Beyond core switchgear, Modison is penetrating **defence (tungsten heavy alloy parts), railways, pharmaceuticals, glass, and renewable energy (silver powder for PV cells)** [^ip-latest]. The **ISRO achievement** — developing a new alloy previously imported from Russia in 8–10 months versus ISRO's 5–6 year struggle with global suppliers — opens a strategic adjacency in space and defence [^cc-transcript]. The **Precious Metal Compounds** division (silver nitrate, silver sulphate, silver oxide) has "major customers on board" with CR, LR, and AR grade capabilities [^ip-latest]. Exports to 22+ countries including USA, Germany, Japan, and South Korea provide geographic diversification, though exports remain ~12% of revenue [^ar-fy25].

**4. Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):**
(i) **Q1 FY27 results** (announced 13 Aug 2026) will validate the FY27 trajectory toward ₹880 Cr revenue guidance [^cc-transcript]; (ii) **ISRO order ramp-up** following technology transfer completion — a marquee reference customer for defence/space applications [^cc-transcript]; (iii) **Data center and AI infrastructure capex** driving incremental switchgear demand globally, with management explicitly citing this as a positive [^cc-transcript]; (iv) **Export customer additions** — targeting 20–25 new customers beyond existing GE/ABB/Siemens relationships [^cc-transcript]; (v) **Capacity expansion** — borrowing limit enhanced from ₹30,000 to ₹50,000 Lakhs signaling aggressive capex plans [^ar-fy25]; (vi) **Long-term target pull-forward** — ₹1,000 Cr target moved to FY27–28 (from FY30), with ₹1,365 Cr targeted by FY30 [^cc-transcript].

**5. Biggest Structural Risk — Working Capital & Cash Flow Quality:**
The most significant concern is **two consecutive years of negative operating cash flow** despite record profits [^ar-fy25]. FY26 CFO was **-₹6,418 Lakhs versus PAT of ₹7,255 Lakhs** — a conversion ratio of -88%. Working capital absorption of ₹16,694 Lakhs in FY26 alone reflects **inventory ballooning 72.4% (₹21,980 Lakhs) and receivables up 85.7% (₹15,997 Lakhs)** [^ar-fy25]. WIP specifically surged 76% to ₹19,037 Lakhs, raising NRV impairment risk. Additionally, **RPT concentration with promoter-controlled Modison Copper Pvt Ltd (MCPL)** at ₹5,042 Lakhs (7.1% of turnover) with a proposed FY27 limit of ₹8,000 Lakhs (11.27% of turnover) is a governance flag requiring ongoing monitoring [^ar-fy25]. The February 2026 refinery fire resulted in ₹1,063 Lakhs charged to exceptional items with only ₹170 Lakhs of insurance confirmed [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
Modison Limited is a **vertically integrated manufacturer of electrical contacts and contact materials** serving the LV, MV, HV, and EHV switchgear industry [^ip-latest]. Founded in 1975 as a silver refining company, it commenced electrical contact manufacturing in 1978 at Vapi [^ip-latest]. The company is **fully backward integrated** from precious metal refining to finished contact products, with 1,000 MT refining capacity and 500 employees [^ip-latest]. FY26 standalone revenue stood at ₹71,033 Lakhs (+44.89% YoY), with domestic contributing 88% (₹63,084 Lakhs) and exports 12% (₹8,516 Lakhs) [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division:**
The company does not disclose explicit numeric revenue split by division, but qualitative segment structure is as follows [^ip-latest]:

| Segment | Key Products | Estimated Revenue Contribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Low Voltage (LV)** | AgNi, AgCdO, AgSnO₂ wires, Silver Nickel, Silver Graphite, button contacts, bimetal rivets, brazing alloys | ~50–55% (largest segment) |
| **High Voltage (HV)** | CuW plugs/tulips for 77 kVA–800 kVA SF6 and GIS circuit breakers | ~25–30% (highest margin) |
| **Medium Voltage (MV)** | CuCr discs, arcing contacts (tulips), non-arcing contacts, finger contacts | ~10–12% |
| **Precious Metal Compounds** | Silver Nitrate, Silver Sulphate, Silver Oxide (CR/LR/AR grades) | ~3–5% |
| **B2C / Others** | Bullion, cutlery (Pearl Empire, Italia, Victoria), silver powder for PV, tungsten defence parts | ~5–8% |

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- **LV:** Havells, C&S Electric, Orient Electric, HPL, and other domestic switchgear OEMs [^ip-latest]
- **HV:** L&T (supplied ZH contacts), GE, ABB, Siemens — global Tier-1 switchgear manufacturers [^cc-transcript]
- **MV:** Vacuum interrupter manufacturers, medium-voltage switchgear OEMs [^cc-transcript]

- **Exports:** Customers in USA, Mexico, Canada, Brazil, Turkey, France, Austria, Germany, Netherlands, Estonia, Croatia, Switzerland, Italy, Sweden, Hungary, Poland, Russia, Kuwait, Iran, Japan, China, South Korea, Vietnam [^ip-latest]

As of FY26, Modison has divested **Modison Solartech Private Limited** (formerly Modison Hitech), which ceased to be a subsidiary on June 9, 2025 [^ar-fy25]. The only remaining subsidiary is **Modison HV Private Limited** (100% owned), with negligible operations (turnover ₹1.50 Lakhs, PAT -₹2.57 Lakhs in FY26) [^ar-fy25]. **Modison Copper Pvt Ltd (MCPL)** is a separate promoter-controlled entity (not a subsidiary) that supplies semi-finished materials to Modison and accounts for ₹4,229 Lakhs of purchases and ₹757 Lakhs of sales in FY26 [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
- **Market Leadership / Moat:** Vidhi holds the position of the 2nd largest food colour manufacturer in Asia, operating with a combined installed capacity of 675 MTPM (Metric Tonnes Per Month – essentially how many thousands of kilograms of dye they can produce each month) [^ip-latest]. The company’s economic moat is anchored in high regulatory barriers; they possess US FDA (Food and Drug Administration) certifications which function like an exclusive quality passport required to sell colours into the American food and cosmetic supply chains, making it exceptionally difficult for unestablished players to displace them [^ar-fy25].
- **Structural Margin Story:** The primary structural margin driver is the deliberate business mix shift away from low-margin "trading" (buying and reselling chemicals made by third parties, which yielded a thin 3.1% EBITDA – Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization, a clean proxy for operational cash profit before non-cash costs) toward in-house "manufacturing" (which delivered a robust 24.7% EBITDA in Q1FY27) [^ip-latest]. Management is aggressively pushing value-added products (cosmetic dyes, CoatIcon coatings) from just 5% of the mix to a targeted 10–12% in FY27, structurally expanding overall EBITDA margins by an estimated 400 bps (basis points; 100 bps equals 1%, so this is a 4% absolute margin lift) [^cc-transcript].
- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The company is constructing two greenfield (brand new) facilities: Dahej GIDC (300 MT/month capacity) serving cosmetics, plastics, and paints, and Arjun Foods at Roha (200 MT/month) for pharma/healthcare end-markets [^ip-latest]. They have also launched "CoatIcon," a strategic entry into tablet coating systems for the pharmaceutical sector, currently in aggressive sampling and qualification with multinational pharma clients [^cc-transcript].
- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** Full utilization of both Dahej and Roha facilities is guided for FY27, with Dahej’s first phase alone expected to contribute INR 125–150 Cr in incremental sales [^cc-transcript]. The CoatIcon product line is undergoing ~6-month stability tests with pharma companies; successful qualifications will act as a sharp re-rating trigger for the stock [^cc-transcript]. Q1FY27 already demonstrated +66.4% revenue growth YoY, signaling demand normalization post-tariff fears [^ip-latest].
- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Extreme revenue concentration remains the Achilles' heel: the top 10 customers constitute ~69.88% of total revenue, and the top 10 countries ~80.81% [^ip-latest]. Coupled with severe working capital leakage (Operating Cash Flow was negative INR 456L despite a positive PAT of INR 4,357L in FY25 due to receivables build-up), any demand shock from a single key client or a US tariff hike to 50% could disproportionately impair financial stability [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
- **Core Business Model:** Vidhi operates as a single-reportable-segment entity focused on synthetic food colours and specialty chemicals [^ar-fy25]. Think of them as the "paint mixers" for the global food, drug, and cosmetic industry—they create the vibrant reds, yellows, and blues seen in candies, pills, and lipsticks. Their model involves both manufacturing these colours in-house and trading third-party chemicals to maintain client wallet share and distribution leverage [^ip-latest].
- **Revenue Split by Division:** In Q1FY27 (total INR 146.3 Cr), Manufacturing Sales dominated at INR 99.6 Cr (68.1% of revenue) while Trading Sales contributed INR 46.7 Cr (31.9%) [^ip-latest]. Geographically, the Americas are the largest demand sink at 53% of sales (up from 44% in FY26), followed by Europe (19%) and Australasia (17%) [^ip-latest]. Notably, management’s stated strategy is to gradually exit low-margin trading, yet trading sales rose 59.6% in FY25, creating a narrative vs execution gap that investors must monitor [^ar-fy25].
- **Key OEM/End Customers:** While specific client names are redacted for confidentiality, the top 10 customers drive ~69.88% of revenues, indicating deep, sticky relationships with global food and cosmetic majors [^ip-latest]. The product portfolio includes US FDA Certified Colours, D&C (Drug & Cosmetic) dyes, and Aluminum Lakes (a specialized dye bonded to aluminum substrate for use in capsules and surface coatings) [^ip-latest].
- **Subsidiary Structure:** The company owns Arjun Foods (Roha MIDC), a wholly-owned subsidiary planned for 200 MT/month pharma capacity, though it remained dormant/loss-making (INR 5.6L loss) in FY25 [^ar-fy25]. No external Joint Ventures are disclosed; growth is entirely organic [^ar-fy25].
- **Manufacturing Footprint:** Two key plants: Dahej GIDC (Gujarat) commissioned Dec-2023, lifting capacity from 325 to 675 MTPM, and Roha MIDC (Maharashtra) [^ar-fy25][^ip-latest]. R&D is concentrated in two facilities near Mumbai with >60 chemists ranging from BSc to PhD levels [^cc-transcript].
- **Promoter Background:** The Manek family (Bipin Manek as Chairman/MD, Mitesh as CFO, Mihir as JMD) controls 67.51% stake via family trusts with zero pledging (meaning they haven't borrowed against their shares, a strong governance positive) [^ar-fy25]. The group context is a focused, promoter-driven specialty chemical play with three decades of domain expertise [^cc-transcript].

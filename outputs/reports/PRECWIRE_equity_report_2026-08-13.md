## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership in niche copper winding wires with high switching costs:** Precision Wires India is one of the largest organised players in enamelled copper winding wires (ECW) and paper-covered/glass-fibre insulated conductors in India, with an estimated 18–20% domestic market share in the super-fine gauge (<0.5mm) segment.[^ip-latest] Customer qualification cycles in motor and transformer OEMs typically run 18–24 months, creating strong incumbency advantages and limiting Chinese import substitution.[^cc-transcript]

- **Structural margin expansion of 150–200 bps over FY26E–FY28E:** EBITDA margins expanded from 6.8% in FY22 to 11.4% in FY25 driven by (i) value-mix shift towards fine-gauge and inverter-duty wires, (ii) backward integration into continuous-cast copper rod, and (iii) operating leverage from the new Sikkim plant.[^ar-fy25] Management has guided for 12.5–13.0% EBITDA margin sustainability on concall, supported by a 220 bps reduction in copper scrap loss over the last three years.[^cc-transcript]

- **Diversification into EV-traction and renewable adjacencies:** The company is commissioning a dedicated high-voltage winding wire line for EV traction motors (target commissioning Q3FY27) and has begun supplying to solar inverter transformer manufacturers, addressing a Rs. 4,200 Cr addressable market growing at 28% CAGR.[^ip-latest] This is incremental to the core Rs. 8,500 Cr ECW market and provides a second growth engine beyond cyclical industrial demand.[^vp-thread]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results (already declared 10 Aug 2026) showing 14% YoY revenue growth and 180 bps margin expansion,[^cc-transcript] (ii) preferential allotment of Rs. 350 Cr announced 10 Aug 2026 to fund Sikkim Phase-2 capacity expansion,[^cc-transcript] (iii) EGM on 5 Sep 2026 for MOA/AOA amendments enabling further capital raise flexibility,[^cc-transcript] and (iv) potential re-rating as institutional investor base widens from current 0.63% FII+DII holding.[^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk — copper price volatility and working capital intensity:** Copper constitutes ~78% of raw material cost, and a Rs. 50/kg adverse move in LME copper can compress gross margins by 220–280 bps in any single quarter.[^ar-fy25] Additionally, the business carries 95–110 days of net working capital (FY25), making it vulnerable to interest rate cycles and creating FCF conversion risk despite reported profitability.[^cc-transcript]

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Dominant ODM Moat in Premium LED Lighting:** IKIO is a leading Original Design Manufacturer (ODM) in the premium LED lighting segment (strip lights, linear lights, and downlights). Its design-led model creates high switching costs for marquee clients like Signify (formerly Philips Lighting India), which relies on IKIO for end-to-end product development, tool design, and component manufacturing. This deep integration makes client relationships highly sticky.
*   **Structural Margin Protection via Backward Integration:** Unlike pure-play electronic manufacturing services (EMS) peers that operate on thin assembly margins (4-6%), IKIO maintains a superior EBITDA margin profile of 15-18%. This is driven by 100% backward integration of critical components, including in-house SMT lines, plastic injection molding, tool rooms, and LED driver design, which insulates the company from global supply chain shocks and raw material price volatility.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Export Adjacencies:** Through its subsidiary Royalux, IKIO is successfully diversifying into high-margin Recreational Vehicle (RV) lighting and components for the North American market. Additionally, the company is leveraging its plastic molding and electronics expertise to enter the automotive ABS components and domestic commercial lighting segments, reducing its historical dependence on residential LED lighting.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** The primary near-term catalysts include the commercialization and phase-wise ramp-up of the state-of-the-art Noida Sector 80 and Sector 85 facilities. This expansion is expected to double the existing manufacturing capacity. Furthermore, a cyclical recovery in the US RV market and new customer acquisitions in the domestic commercial lighting space will drive volume growth in FY27E.
*   **Key Structural Risk — High Customer Concentration:** IKIO’s largest customer, Signify, historically contributes over 50% of its LED lighting revenues. Any slowdown in Signify’s domestic market share, inventory destocking, or insourcing of manufacturing presents a material risk to IKIO’s top-line growth and capacity utilization levels.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** Somany Ceramics (SOMANYCERA) holds a strong position as one of India's top three organized tile manufacturers, with a comprehensive product portfolio spanning ceramic, vitrified, polished vitrified, and digital tiles. Its extensive distribution network of over 15,000 dealers and sub-dealers, coupled with a strong brand recall built over five decades, creates a significant competitive moat, making it difficult for new entrants to replicate its reach and trust. The company's focus on diverse price points, from mass-market to premium segments, ensures broad market penetration and resilience across economic cycles.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** The company is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-margin vitrified and large-format tiles, which command better realizations and profitability. Furthermore, ongoing backward integration into key raw materials and continued focus on operational efficiencies, including optimizing logistics and energy costs, are expected to drive a sustained improvement in EBITDA margins by 150-200 bps over the next 2-3 years, reaching ~14.5% by FY28E.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** SOMANYCERA is actively diversifying beyond its core tile business into high-growth adjacencies like sanitaryware and bath fittings. This segment, currently contributing ~10% to revenues, is witnessing robust growth, driven by increasing disposable incomes, urbanization, and government initiatives like Swachh Bharat Abhiyan. The company's established brand and distribution network provide a strong platform to scale these newer categories, capturing a larger share of the overall home improvement market.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include a robust recovery in real estate demand, particularly in the housing sector, which directly translates to higher tile consumption. The upcoming festive season (Q3FY27) is expected to provide a significant boost to sales. Additionally, the commissioning of new capacities, especially in vitrified tiles, coupled with the ongoing benefits of gas price moderation, should lead to improved operating leverage and stronger Q2FY27 and Q3FY27 earnings performance.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk for SOMANYCERA is its susceptibility to fluctuations in real estate cycles and construction activity. A prolonged slowdown in the housing and commercial real estate sectors, coupled with intense competition from unorganized players, could exert pressure on volumes and pricing, impacting profitability. Additionally, volatility in input costs, especially natural gas prices, remains a key operational risk, though the company has demonstrated effective cost management in the past.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Somany Ceramics Limited is a leading Indian manufacturer and retailer of ceramic tiles and allied products. The company offers a wide range of products including ceramic wall and floor tiles, vitrified tiles (glazed and polished), digital tiles, sanitaryware, and bath fittings.

*   **Core Business Model:** SOMANYCERA operates on a hybrid model, combining in-house manufacturing with outsourcing arrangements. It manufactures a significant portion of its tile requirements and sources the rest from joint venture partners and third-party manufacturers, allowing for flexibility and scalability. The company sells its products through an extensive pan-India distribution network comprising exclusive showrooms, multi-brand outlets, and modern retail formats.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
    *   Tiles: ~88%
    *   Sanitaryware & Bath Fittings: ~10%
    *   Other Building Materials: ~2%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Tiles:** Primarily caters to the retail segment (individual homeowners, small builders) and institutional clients (large real estate developers, government projects, hotels).
    *   **Sanitaryware & Bath Fittings:** Caters to both retail customers and institutional projects, leveraging cross-selling opportunities with its tile business.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** The company has key subsidiaries including Somany Global Limited (for international operations) and Somany Fine Ceramics Limited (manufacturing focus). It also has significant investments in joint ventures for manufacturing capacities.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** SOMANYCERA operates multiple manufacturing facilities across India, strategically located to serve key markets and optimize logistics.
    *   **States:** Gujarat, Haryana, Andhra Pradesh
    *   **Plant Count:** Owns and operates 9 manufacturing plants (including JVs and associate companies) across these states, with a significant concentration in Gujarat, a hub for tile manufacturing.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Somany Ceramics is part of the H.L. Somany Group, a diversified conglomerate with interests in various sectors. The company was founded by Late Mr. H.L. Somany in 1969. The current management, led by Mr. Abhishek Somany (MD & CEO), represents the third generation of the promoter family.

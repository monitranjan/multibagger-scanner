## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Global leadership in a structurally attractive niche:** BKT is the world's largest player by volume in the off-highway tyre (OHT) segment, commanding an estimated ~3% global market share in specialty tyres versus global majors like Michelin, Bridgestone and Goodyear who are ceding share due to high-cost legacy plants [^ip-latest]. The niche is protected by entrenched entry barriers — long OEM approval cycles (3–5 years), application-specific R&D, and a fragmented aftermarket distribution moat that incumbents like BKT have built over two decades [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin and ROCE superiority:** BKT has delivered EBITDA margins of 22–25% and ROCE of 22%+ consistently over the last decade, materially ahead of Indian tyre peers (MRF, Apollo, CEAT) which operate in the 12–18% ROCE band [^ar-fy25]. Pricing power is structurally higher — BKT commands 30–35% pricing flexibility versus international majors in replacement/aftermarket channels, allowing it to pass through raw material inflation with a 1–2 quarter lag [^vp-thread].

- **Diversification into adjacent high-growth segments:** Beyond the core agri/OTR tyre franchise, BKT is expanding into 2W, 4W, LCV and passenger vehicle radials through its Bhuj (Gujarat) greenfield plant, which is being scaled to ~260,000 MTPA capacity [^ip-latest]. This opens a Rs. 25,000+ Cr domestic addressable market where BKT currently has negligible share, providing a multi-year volume runway.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Bhuj plant ramp-up and commissioning of incremental OHT capacity expected to drive 18–22% volume growth in FY27E [^cc-transcript]; (ii) softening natural rubber prices (down ~12% YoY) likely to expand gross margins by 150–200 bps in H2FY27 [^vp-thread]; (iii) continued share gains in the US replacement market where BKT brand acceptance is accelerating; (iv) potential re-rating as the Street begins to model the passenger vehicle radial business.

- **Biggest structural risk:** Raw material (natural rubber) price volatility remains the single largest P&L risk — rubber constitutes ~35–40% of raw material cost and BKT's ability to fully pass through price hikes has a 1–2 quarter lag, as evidenced in FY24 when margins compressed ~250 bps before recovery [^ar-fy25]. Secondary risks include EUR/USD receivables exposure (~50% of revenue invoiced in EUR/USD) and potential US/EU anti-dumping actions on Indian tyre imports [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Balkrishna Industries Limited (BKT) is a specialty tyre manufacturer focused exclusively on the off-highway tyre (OHT) segment, serving agricultural, construction/earthmoving (OTR), industrial, material handling, forestry, lawn & garden, and ATV applications [^ip-latest]. The company operates a B2B + B2C hybrid model — direct OEM supplies to global tractor and construction equipment majors, complemented by a deep aftermarket distribution network across 160+ countries [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
- Agricultural Tyres: ~55% of revenue (largest segment, includes tractor, harvester, implement tyres)
- OTR / Construction Tyres: ~25% of revenue (earthmoving, mining, loader, skid-steer)
- Industrial / Material Handling / ATV / Others: ~20% of revenue (forklift, lawn-garden, ATV, specialty)

**Key OEM / End Customers:**
- **Agricultural OEMs:** John Deere, AGCO (Massey Ferguson, Fendt), CNH Industrial (Case IH, New Holland), CLAAS, Mahindra, TAFE [^ip-latest]
- **OTR / Construction OEMs:** Caterpillar, JCB, Volvo CE, Komatsu, Liebherr (aftermarket-led)
- **Industrial / Material Handling:** Toyota Material Handling, Kion Group, Hyster-Yale
- **Aftermarket:** Distributed through 1,500+ dealers globally; BKT brand commands premium positioning in Europe and North America [^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- BKT Europe GmbH (Germany) — European sales/marketing hub
- BKT Tires USA Inc. — North American distribution
- BKT Tires Canada Inc. — Canadian operations
- BKT Australia Pty Ltd. — ANZ distribution
- Step-down subsidiaries in Mexico, Turkey and other markets for localized stocking [^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- **Maharashtra:** Aurangabad (flagship plant), Waluj (auxiliary)
- **Gujarat:** Bhuj (greenfield, large-scale expansion underway)
- **Rajasthan:** Chopanki (auxiliary)
- Aggregate installed capacity being scaled from ~150,000 MTPA toward ~260,000 MTPA by FY28E [^ip-latest]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** BKT is promoted by the Poddar family (Siyaram Poddar Group), with the business currently led by **Arvind Poddar (Chairman & Managing Director)** and **Rajiv Poddar (Joint Managing Director)** — second-generation professional promoters who have scaled the company from a Rs. 166 Cr revenue base in FY01 to over Rs. 9,000+ Cr today [^vp-thread]. The promoter holding stands at 58.56% with no pledging, reflecting strong skin-in-the-game.

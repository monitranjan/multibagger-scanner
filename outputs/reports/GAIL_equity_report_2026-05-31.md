### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

GAIL (India) Limited is India's largest state-owned natural gas processing and distribution company, primarily involved in the transmission, marketing, and processing of natural gas. It plays a pivotal role in the country's energy infrastructure.

*   **Core Business Model:** GAIL's core business revolves around the transmission of natural gas through its extensive pipeline network, marketing of natural gas (including imported LNG), and processing of natural gas to produce petrochemicals and LPG.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A, indicative):**
    *   Natural Gas Marketing: ~80-82%
    *   Gas Transmission: ~10-12%
    *   LPG & Liquid Hydrocarbons: ~4-5%

    *   City Gas Distribution & Others: ~1-2%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Gas Transmission:** Power generation companies (e.g., NTPC), fertilizer manufacturers (e.g., IFFCO, KRIBHCO), steel plants, refineries, CGD entities (e.g., IGL, MGL), and industrial consumers.
    *   **Natural Gas Marketing:** Same as transmission, plus direct sales to large industrial users and CGD companies.
    *   **Petrochemicals:** Plastic processors, packaging industry, automotive component manufacturers, and other downstream chemical industries.
    *   **LPG & Liquid Hydrocarbons:** LPG bottling plants, industrial customers.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** GAIL holds significant stakes in several joint ventures and subsidiaries, including:
    *   Indraprastha Gas Limited (IGL) – City Gas Distribution (CGD)
    *   Mahanagar Gas Limited (MGL) – City Gas Distribution (CGD)
    *   Petronet LNG Limited (PLL) – LNG regasification
    *   Brahmaputra Cracker and Polymer Limited (BCPL) – Petrochemicals
    *   Gujarat State Petronet Limited (GSPL) – Gas transmission
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** GAIL operates a vast network across India.
    *   **Pipelines:** Spanning across numerous states including Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Punjab, Haryana, etc.
    *   **Processing Plants:** Major petrochemical complex at Pata (Uttar Pradesh), LPG recovery plants across various locations.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** GAIL is a Maharatna Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) under the administrative control of the Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas, Government of India. The Government of India is the primary promoter, holding the majority stake. The company plays a strategic role in achieving India's energy security and transitioning to a gas-based economy.

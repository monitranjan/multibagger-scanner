### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

* **Core Holding Moat:** TVS Holdings (TVSHLTD) acts as the primary holding company for the TVS Group, with its flagship investment being its ~50.26% stake in TVS Motor Company (TVSM). This provides exposure to one of India’s most efficient 2W/3W manufacturers with dominant market share in premium motorcycles and EV scooters.
* **Structural Margin Expansion:** TVS Motor is undergoing a product-mix shift toward premiumization (Apache, Ronin, Norton) and high-margin EV segments (iQube, X), which drives dividend cash flow and value accretion for TVSHLTD shareholders.
* **Diversification into Financial Services:** Through its subsidiary TVS Credit Services, the company has built a robust NBFC franchise focusing on vehicle financing, MSME loans, and consumer durables, currently managing an AUM exceeding Rs. 25,000 Cr with a focus on asset quality.
* **Near-term Catalysts:** (1) Potential IPO or strategic stake sale in TVS Credit Services to unlock value, (2) Sustained growth in TVS Motor’s export markets and EV penetration, (3) Consolidation of group synergies in the automotive supply chain.
* **Structural Risk:** Concentration risk remains high; the company’s valuation is inherently sensitive to the market performance of TVS Motor Company. Any cyclical downturn in the 2W segment directly impacts the dividend inflow and NAV of TVSHLTD.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

* **Business Model:** TVSHLTD operates as a core investment company (CIC). Its value is derived from the performance of its subsidiaries, primarily TVS Motor Company (Automotive) and TVS Credit Services (Financial Services).
* **Revenue/NAV Split:** TVS Motor accounts for >80% of the underlying NAV. The financial services arm provides steady interest income and fee-based revenue, serving as a hedge against manufacturing cyclicality.
* **Subsidiary Structure:** TVS Motor Company (Flagship), TVS Credit Services (NBFC), and various other strategic investments in the auto-component and logistics space.
* **Manufacturing Footprint:** Through TVS Motor, the group operates 5+ major plants in India (Hosur, Mysuru, Nalagarh, etc.) and international facilities in Indonesia.
* **Promoter Background:** The Nirmal Minda/TVS family pedigree is synonymous with manufacturing excellence, lean management, and conservative capital allocation.

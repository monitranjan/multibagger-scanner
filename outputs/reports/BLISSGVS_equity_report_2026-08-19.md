## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

**1. Global Leadership in Suppositories & Pessaries — A Defensible Niche Moat:** Bliss GVS is the world's largest manufacturer of suppositories and pessaries, a niche dosage form with high entry barriers due to specialized manufacturing infrastructure, limited technical know-how, and stringent regulatory requirements for rectal/vaginal drug delivery [^ar-fy25]. The company commands leadership across 50+ therapeutic segments and 16+ dosage forms with 150+ brands, giving it pricing power in a fragmented global generics market [^ar-fy25]. This niche dominance translates into structural margin advantages versus broad-based generic players who compete on volume.

**2. Structural Margin Recovery Story with Operating Leverage:** Consolidated EBITDA margins recovered to 17.7% in FY26 (₹164 Cr EBITDA on ₹927 Cr revenue) from the FY25 dip, with standalone EBITDA margins at 18.4% [^ar-fy25]. The 49.3% YoY PAT growth in FY26 (consolidated PAT of ₹134.7 Cr vs ₹90.3 Cr in FY25) demonstrates significant operating leverage as fixed costs get absorbed across higher volumes [^ar-fy25]. With borrowings reduced to zero on a standalone basis (from ₹51 Cr in FY25) and net cash position of ₹205 Cr, interest costs have compressed materially, providing a 130 bps tailwind to net margins [^ar-fy25].

**3. Geographic Diversification into Regulated Markets — A Multi-Year Growth Vector:** The company has aggressively expanded beyond its Africa-heavy base (72% of consolidated revenue) into higher-margin regulated markets — Kenya subsidiary (Theralife Pharma Ltd) operational from Nov 2025, DRC subsidiary from Jan 2026, UK subsidiary established, Philippines branch office opened, and sales commenced in Iraq and Canada [^ar-fy25]. Europe contributes 13.5% of revenue and is growing, while North America at 3.3% represents a long runway. The WHO GMP certification received in June 2026 acts as a critical regulatory validation for export market expansion [^ar-fy25].

**4. Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (a) Q1 FY27 PAT growth of 83% YoY (₹5.14 Cr vs prior year) signals strong momentum continuing into FY27 [^ar-fy25]; (b) Semi-solid manufacturing facility at Vevoor commissioned, suppository expansion at Unit 2 (~₹69 Cr invested) coming onstream — capacity unlock for FY27 revenue ramp; (c) Kenya and DRC subsidiaries expected to contribute meaningfully from FY27 onward as integration completes; (d) Potential EU-India FTA could open incremental European market access; (e) Digital transformation via SAP S/4HANA (Project JEET 2.0) expected to improve working capital cycles and operational efficiency [^ar-fy25].

**5. Biggest Structural Risk — Africa Revenue Concentration & Governance Overhang:** Africa accounts for 72.2% of consolidated revenue (₹670 Cr of ₹927 Cr), creating significant geographic concentration risk [^ar-fy25]. Any currency depreciation, regulatory tightening, or political instability in key African markets (Nigeria, Kenya, etc.) could materially impact revenues. Compounding this, three directors resigned in FY26 (former MD Gagan Harsh Sharma and two WTDs), creating management transition risk even as Mr. Narsimha Kamath (promoter, 30.66% stake) assumes the MD & CEO role [^ar-fy25]. Additionally, BGIPL subsidiary exposure of ₹43.7 Cr (with negative net worth) and unresolved insider trading contra-trade violation remain governance overhangs [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Bliss GVS Pharma operates as a single-segment pharmaceutical formulations company specializing in niche dosage forms. The company's core competency lies in suppositories and pessaries — where it holds global market leadership — supplemented by oral solid dosages (OSD), semi-solids, capsules, and dry syrups [^ar-fy25]. The business model is B2B-focused with a strong distribution network across emerging markets (primarily Africa) and growing presence in regulated markets (Europe, North America). The company manufactures 150+ brands across 50+ therapeutic segments, leveraging contract manufacturing arrangements and its own brand portfolio [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**Total Addressable Market (TAM):** The global pharmaceutical market is estimated at USD 1,772 billion in 2025, growing at a CAGR of 6.15% [^ar-fy25]. India is positioned as the "Pharmacy of the World," supplying approximately 20% of global generic medicines by volume [^ar-fy25]. The Africa pharmaceutical market — Bliss GVS's primary market — is growing at a CAGR of 6.52%, driven by rising healthcare access, population growth, and increasing disease burden [^ar-fy25]. The global suppositories market, while niche, is estimated at USD 1.5-2 billion with limited competition, allowing Bliss GVS to command premium pricing.

**Policy Tailwinds:** (1) **PLI Scheme for Pharmaceuticals:** The Indian government's Production Linked Incentive scheme for bulk drugs and medical devices supports domestic API manufacturing capacity, benefiting formulators with backward integration potential; (2) **China Plus One Opportunity:** As global pharma companies diversify away from China-dependent supply chains, Indian formulators like Bliss GVS are positioned to capture incremental contract manufacturing business [^ar-fy25]; (3) **EU-India FTA:** A potential Free Trade Agreement could reduce tariff barriers for Indian pharmaceutical exports to Europe, directly benefiting Bliss GVS's 13.5% Europe revenue stream [^ar-fy25]; (4) **WHO GMP Certification:** The June 2026 WHO GMP approval enhances credibility for export markets and domestic tender business [^ar-fy25]; (5) **Africa Healthcare Expansion:** Increasing donor funding and government healthcare spending in Sub-Saharan Africa supports volume growth.

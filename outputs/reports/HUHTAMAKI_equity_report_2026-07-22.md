### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** Huhtamaki India is India's leading manufacturer of primary consumer packaging and labelling materials, with a 69.60% promoter holding from Finnish multinational Huhtamaki Oyj providing global technology access and customer validation [^ip-latest]. The company serves marquee FMCG clients including HUL, ITC, Nestle, Britannia, Cadbury, Coca-Cola, Pepsi, GSK, and Marico, creating high switching costs given qualification cycles in flexible packaging [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Story:** EBITDA margins have expanded from ~8% in FY20 to ~13-14% in FY25, driven by a richer product mix (premium flexibles, pharma labels), operating leverage from the Guwahati and Sikkim plants, and benign polymer raw material costs [^cc-transcript]. We model further 80-100 bps margin expansion over FY26E-FY28E as the Guwahati facility ramps up utilisation from current ~60% to ~80% [^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** The Webtech subsidiary (merged in FY18) gives Huhtamaki India a leadership position in pressure-sensitive pharma labels, a segment growing at 12-15% CAGR vs. 8-10% for FMCG flexibles [^ar-fy25]. The Sikkim plant caters to pharma majors who have set up manufacturing there for tax benefits, providing a captive, sticky customer base [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months):** (1) Q2FY27 results expected to show sequential margin recovery post-monsoon demand uptick in FMCG; (2) Guwahati plant Phase-2 commissioning adding gravure cylinder capacity; (3) Potential value unlocking from the global parent's restructuring — Huhtamaki Oyj has been actively reviewing its India operations as a strategic hub for South Asia [^cc-transcript]; (4) Possible re-rating as institutional interest returns given promoter stability and clean balance sheet.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Raw material volatility remains the primary risk — polymer resins (polyethylene, polypropylene, PET) constitute ~55-60% of input costs and are linked to crude oil prices [^ar-fy25]. A 10% spike in polymer prices without commensurate pass-through can compress EBITDA margins by 150-200 bps. Secondary risk is FMCG demand cyclicality — a rural slowdown or down-trading in categories like biscuits, soaps, and snacks directly impacts packaging volumes [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Huhtamaki India manufactures primary consumer packaging (flexible packaging films, laminates, pouches) and labelling materials (pressure-sensitive labels, shrink sleeves, wrap-around labels) for the FMCG, pharma, and food & beverage industries [^ip-latest]. The company operates on a B2B model with long-term supply agreements (typically 2-3 years) with branded consumer companies, earning margins through value-added printing, lamination, and converting rather than commodity film manufacturing [^ar-fy25].

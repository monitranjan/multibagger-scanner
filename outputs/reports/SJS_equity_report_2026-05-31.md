### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS (5 bullet points)

*   **Market Leadership in Aesthetic Components:** SJS is a dominant player in the Indian decorative aesthetics market for automobiles, holding significant market share in 2D/3D decals, domes, and optical plastics. Its strong R&D capabilities and integrated manufacturing allow it to cater to evolving design trends and complex specifications from leading OEMs across 2-wheelers, passenger vehicles, and commercial vehicles, creating a strong design-in moat.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion driven by Premiumization:** The company is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-value, higher-margin offerings like chrome-plated parts, optical plastics, and precision molded components. This premiumization, coupled with operational efficiencies from automation and backward integration in key processes, is expected to drive a sustainable 150-200 bps improvement in EBITDA margins over the next 3-4 years, moving towards the 23-24% range.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** SJS is successfully expanding its product portfolio and end-market exposure beyond traditional automotive decals. Its foray into chrome-plated parts for automotive and consumer durables, and optical plastics for medical devices and white goods, taps into larger, faster-growing segments. The recent acquisition of a stake in a precision component manufacturer further bolsters its capabilities in these high-potential adjacencies.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts from New OEM Orders & Capacity:** The ongoing strong demand cycle in the automotive sector, particularly in premium 2-wheelers and passenger vehicles, is a key driver. SJS is benefiting from new model launches by major OEMs (e.g., Bajaj Auto, Maruti Suzuki, TVS Motors) where its value-added components are integral. Furthermore, recent capacity expansions at its Bengaluru and Pune facilities are expected to come online, supporting higher volumes and market share gains in FY27.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: OEM Concentration & Auto Cycle Volatility:** A significant portion of SJS's revenue remains concentrated with a few large automotive OEMs, making it susceptible to their production schedules and market share shifts. While diversification efforts are underway, a prolonged slowdown in the overall automotive sector or loss of key OEM contracts could materially impact growth and profitability, despite the company's strong niche position.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

SJS Enterprises Ltd is a leading player in the Indian decorative aesthetics industry, specializing in the design, development, and manufacturing of a diverse range of aesthetic and functional components. The company's core business model revolves around providing customized solutions to its OEM clients, primarily in the automotive sector, but also increasingly in consumer durables, medical devices, and other industrial applications. Its expertise spans from initial design concept to mass production, leveraging advanced manufacturing technologies.

**Revenue Split by Division (FY26A Estimates):**

*   **Chrome Plated Parts:** ~20% (Includes interior and exterior chrome parts for automotive, consumer durables)
*   **Other Products:** ~5% (Includes components for medical devices, white goods, and other industrial applications)

*   **Bengaluru, Karnataka:** The primary and largest manufacturing hub, focusing on decals, 3D logos, and optical plastics.

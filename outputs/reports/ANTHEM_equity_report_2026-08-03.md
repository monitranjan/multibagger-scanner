### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Pioneer status in India's fermentation CDMO space creates a defensible moat:** Anthem is one of the few Indian CRDMOs operating at scale across both small-molecule API manufacturing and microbial biologics via its proprietary fermentation platform, giving it a structural first-mover advantage in a niche dominated by Chinese suppliers facing increasing geopolitical and ESG scrutiny[^ip-latest]. Replacement of Chinese fermentation capacity by global innovators is now an active strategic priority for Big Pharma, directly benefiting Anthem's positioning.
- **Structural margin expansion story driven by shift up the value chain:** FY25 EBITDA margin expansion of ~370 bps YoY to ~26.5% reflects the increasing mix of higher-margin development, peptide, and biologics services vs. legacy commercial API volumes[^ar-fy25]. Management has guided to sustaining 26–28% EBITDA band over FY26–FY28E as the share of development-linked revenues rises from ~38% in FY25 to a targeted 50% by FY28E[^cc-transcript].
- **Diversification into peptides and biologics mitigates API-only risk:** Anthem has built peptide synthesis capacity (~2,000+ amino acid residues) and microbial-based biologics capabilities, addressing the GLP-1-driven peptide opportunity and the biosimilars pipeline[^ip-latest]. These adjacencies are growing at 25%+ CAGR vs. mid-teens for the base API business, structurally lifting blended growth.
- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) commissioning of the new peptide manufacturing block in Q3FY27, adding ~30% peptide capacity; (ii) potential addition of 2–3 Big Pharma CDMO contracts in biologics; (iii) PLI-linked production-linked incentive benefits materialising from H2FY27; and (iv) potential MSCI India inclusion review in Sep 2026, which could trigger passive flows[^vp-thread].
- **Biggest structural risk — customer concentration and China+1 overcapacity:** Top-10 customers contribute ~65% of revenues, creating concentration risk; the loss of any single large innovator client would dent growth materially[^ar-fy25]. Additionally, incremental fermentation capacity additions globally — including from new Indian players — could compress pricing in commoditised small-molecule APIs by 100–200 bps over FY27–FY28E if supply additions outpace demand growth[^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
Anthem Biosciences is a Bengaluru-headquartered, science-led CRDMO offering end-to-end services from discovery, process development, clinical supply, and commercial manufacturing across small-molecule APIs, peptides, and biologics[^ip-latest]. The company operates an asset-light R&D model with deep integrated fermentation capabilities — a niche capability among Indian CRDMOs.

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
- **API Services (incl. intermediates and fermentation):** ~62% of revenue
- **Custom Synthesis & Contract Development:** ~24%
- **Peptides & Biologics (emerging):** ~14%

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- **API:** Several top-20 global generic players and innovator pharma; FY25 disclosed customer count of 400+[^ar-fy25]
- **Custom Synthesis:** Mid-to-large innovator pharma and Japanese generic players[^cc-transcript]
- **Peptides/Biologics:** Leading US biotechs and large pharma in early-stage development

**Subsidiary Structure:**
Anthem operates through its wholly-owned subsidiary — **Anthem Bio Sciences (USA) Inc.** — for US business development, and **Anthem Biopharma Pvt. Ltd.** handles biologics-focused manufacturing[^ar-fy25]. No material JV exposure; growth has been largely organic and self-funded.

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- Karnataka (Bengaluru): 3 facilities — API manufacturing, R&D centre, biologics
- Tamil Nadu (Hosur): 1 fermentation-cum-API plant
- Aggregate reactor volume ~1,500+ KL[^ip-latest]

**Promoter Background and Group Context:**
Founded in 2006 by **Ajaya Bhamidipaty** and **Raghavendra Bhamidipaty**, both PhDs with prior stints at Bristol-Myers Squibb and other global pharma majors[^ar-fy25]. Promoters remain operationally active — Chairman & MD Ajaya Bhamidipaty oversees science, while CEO Raghavendra Bhamidipaty heads commercial and strategy. The Bhamidipaty family cumulatively holds 84.18% — a high promoter alignment but also a key overhang for free float and index inclusion[^ar-fy25].

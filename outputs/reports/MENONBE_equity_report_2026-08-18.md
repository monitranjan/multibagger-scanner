### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat — Strong franchise in Indian engine bearings with structural lock-in.** Menon is the largest listed Indian pure-play in the engine bearing segment with ~20–25% domestic market share, operating in a 4-player domestic oligopoly alongside global majors (Daido, Kingsteel, Federal-Mogul-type entities) [^ar-fy25][^ip-latest]. The company supplies bi-metal bearings, bushes and thrust washers (30–225 mm diameter) to John Deere (single-source for 20+ years), Cummins, Tata Motors, Mahindra, Eicher, Escorts and Allison Transmission; no single customer exceeds 10–12% of revenue [^ip-latest]. Switching costs run 6–12 months of testing/validation and zero-PPM rejection track records create a defensible moat.

- **Structural margin story — Operating leverage and mix shift driving 300 bps YoY EBITDA expansion.** Consolidated EBITDA margin reached 23.8% in Q1 FY27 versus 20.8% in Q1 FY27 — a 298 bps YoY jump; full-year FY26 EBITDA margin printed 21.5% versus 18.6% in FY25 (289 bps expansion) [^ip-latest]. PAT margin of 15.0% in Q1 FY27 (+270 bps YoY) reflects a structural mix shift toward (a) higher-value thrust washers and machined Alkop components (~Rs. 750/kg realisation), (b) in-house machine building that lowers tooling overhead, and (c) absence of low-margin Dubai/Africa brake exports in Q1 FY27 which historically compressed segment EBITDA to 12–14% [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies — EV, exports and railway.** The Alkop subsidiary has secured Porsche E-Mobility (via Eaton) and Tesla (via Concentric) — EV share at 4–5% of Alkop today, targeted to reach 8–10% by FY27 [^ip-latest][^cc-transcript]. Railway brake lining opportunity unlocks post-dynamometer commissioning (Sep 2026): Rs. 4–5 Cr Year 1, scaling to Rs. 25–30 Cr by FY28 [^cc-transcript]. Exports are guided to rise from 34% of revenue in FY26 to 37% by FY27, with new geographies (Canada, Africa, Europe) opening after the recent US/Canada customer visits [^ip-latest][^cc-transcript].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months).** (i) Railway dynamometer commissioning by Sep 2026 followed by RDSO approval — Rs. 25–30 Cr revenue stream within 2 years from a near-zero base [^cc-transcript]. (ii) US/Canada trip monetisation — RFQs already received from Magna, Linamar, Allison, Eaton and Honeywell representing Rs. 65–75 Cr of incremental annual revenue potential [^cc-transcript][^ip-latest]. (iii) Rs. 25 Cr of additional capex over FY27-28 already approved and largely funded internally, providing immediate revenue uplift from 25–30% Bi-Metal capacity expansion [^ip-latest]. (iv) Africa direct merchant export entry — Rs. 9 Cr (bearings) + Rs. 6–7 Cr (brake linings) potential identified [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk — ICE concentration and aluminium price volatility.** Bi-Metal still accounts for ~72% of consolidated revenue and is structurally linked to M&HCV, tractor and industrial engine platforms. While EV penetration in these segments is low, sustained global EV adoption and stricter emission norms (BS-VII/Euro 7) could compress long-cycle replacement demand [^ar-fy25][^substack-1]. Aluminium price volatility is an immediate watch-item — management disclosed rates have moved from Rs. 200–250/kg historical levels to Rs. 340–370/kg, a >50% increase that directly moves Alkop realisation and gross margins [^cc-transcript]. Substack/independent research also flagged rising debtor days (113 vs. 83 four years ago) and trade receivables up Rs. 30 Cr YoY as a working-capital quality concern [^substack-1][^substack-2].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model.** Menon Bearings Limited (CIN: L29130PN1991PLC062352) manufactures precision engine components — bi-metal bearings, bushes, thrust washers and aluminium die-cast parts — for automotive, industrial and off-highway applications. The company has organised operations into three product divisions housed under one holding company and three wholly-owned subsidiaries (WOS). The business model targets niche, high-precision, value-added components in M&HCV, tractor, industrial engine, compressor and genset segments — not commoditised mass-market parts [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

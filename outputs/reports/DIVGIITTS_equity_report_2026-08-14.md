### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Niche market leadership in torque transfer systems (Strong moat):** DIVGIITTS is India's dominant manufacturer of Torsen-type limited-slip differentials under a long-standing technology licence from JTEKT (Japan), with an estimated ~70% share in Indian passenger-vehicle Torsen differentials and ~40% share in hypoid gear sets supplied to top OEMs (Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra) [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]. High customer-switching costs (5–7 year validation cycles, sub-tier platform integration) and limited Tier-1 alternatives (GKN, JTEKT, Dana) underwrite a defensible niche position.

- **Structural margin expansion story:** EBITDA margin has expanded ~600 bps from ~14% in FY21 to ~21% in FY25, driven by (i) operating leverage on volume growth (revenue CAGR 22% over FY22–FY25), (ii) richer mix from Torsen differentials (45% of FY25 revenue vs. 35% in FY21), and (iii) backward integration into precision gear cutting [^cc-transcript] [^ar-fy25]. Management has guided to sustained 20–22% EBITDA band through FY27E despite raw-material headwinds.

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies reducing ICE concentration risk:** Acquired Sundaram Fasteners' gear-cutting division in FY24 (now renamed Divgi MX-Linear Motion Technologies), broadening the addressable market into gear box components and providing entry into aerospace and EV transmission adjacencies [^ip-latest]. New product pipeline includes reducer gearboxes for electric CV joints, EV transfer cases, and Tier-2 aerospace components (Boeing/HAL supply chain qualification underway).

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Jamshedpur greenfield plant ramp-up (commissioned Q3FY25, expected to reach 70%+ utilisation by Q2FY27), (b) Mahindra XUV.e8 and Tata Curvv platform SOPs driving incremental Torsen content per vehicle, (c) FY27 order book disclosed at Rs. 1,200 Cr (~1.2x TTM revenue), (d) potential re-rating on Q2FY27 margin print from operating leverage on Jamshedpur volumes [^cc-transcript] [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Customer concentration — top-5 OEMs contribute ~68% of FY25 revenue, with Maruti Suzuki alone ~28% [^ar-fy25]. Second-order risk is the long-term ICE-to-EV transition reducing per-vehicle demand for traditional differentials (each ICE differential has limited EV analogue); management is hedging via EV-adjacency pipeline but FY28+ visibility remains nascent [^vp-thread] [^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** DIVGIITTS designs, manufactures and supplies precision-engineered torque transfer products — primarily Torsen limited-slip differentials, hypoid gear sets, transfer cases, front-axle and rear-axle assemblies, and gear box sub-components — to automotive OEMs in India and select export markets. The company operates a single-business, multi-product platform under one roof of torque transfer technologies [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25].

**Revenue split by division (FY25A):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Products | Key OEM Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Differentials | ~45% | Torsen LSD, hypoid gear sets | Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Mahindra, SML Isuzu |
| Gear Box Components | ~28% | Precision gears, shafts, synchros | Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors, Hyundai (exports) |
| Axle Assemblies | ~22% | Front/rear axle assemblies | Ashok Leyland, VE Commercials, Daimler India |
| Others (EV / Exports / Tooling) | ~5% | EV reducers, tooling, aerospace | Boeing (T2), HAL, export OEM Tier-1s |

**Manufacturing footprint (4 plants, ~3,200 employees):**
- **Jamshedpur (Jharkhand)** — greenfield, commissioned FY25, capacity ~8 lakh differential units p.a.
- **Bangalore — Hoskote (Karnataka)** — flagship, Torsen differentials and hypoid gears
- **Supa (Maharashtra)** — gear box components (acquired from Sundaram Fasteners)
- **Haridwar (Uttarakhand)** — axle assemblies

**Subsidiary structure:**
- Divgi MX-Linear Motion Technologies Pvt Ltd (wholly-owned; acquired Sundaram Fasteners gear division, FY24)
- Divgi Tooling Solutions Pvt Ltd (wholly-owned; captive tooling)

**Promoter & group context:** Promoted by the Divgi family — founded in 1977, with Niraj Divgi as Chairman & Managing Director, the promoter has held ~63.25% stake post-IPO [^ip-latest] [^ar-fy25]. The business was bootstrapped with technology licensing from JTEKT (Japan) in the 1990s and scaled via Maruti Suzuki Tier-1 status. The group does not have unrelated listed entities; operations are entirely focused on torque transfer systems.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Defensible moat in Driver Information Systems (DIS) underpins core franchise**: Pricol commands ~53% market share in 2W DIS in India and ranks #2 globally by volume in DIS[^ip-latest][^vp-thread]. The company also holds ~75% market share in the commercial vehicle (CV) DIS segment in India[^vp-thread]. Long 3–5 year product cycles, deep OEM platform integration, and a sticky customer base (HMSI, Bajaj, TVS, Royal Enfield, Maruti Suzuki) create high switching costs for OEMs — a Strong moat by our framework[^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin expansion via mix, content, and scale**: EBITDA margin trajectory is improving, supported by (a) product mix shift from mechanical to digital instrument clusters, (b) higher electronics content per vehicle in DIS and sensors, and (c) operating leverage from the Pantnagar and Pune plant ramp-up[^ar-fy25]. Management has guided to mid-teens EBITDA margins vs. low double-digits historically[^cc-transcript]. The pumps exports business operates at mid-to-low 20% margin, materially accretive to consolidated margin as export contribution scales toward 25%+ by FY28E[^vp-thread].

- **Diversification into EV, sensors, telematics, and exports reduces 2W concentration risk**: Strategic push into (a) automotive sensors (temperature, MAP, position, fuel level, speed), (b) coolant pumps for EV thermal management, (c) telematics/connected vehicle solutions (TCUs, BCMs, displays), and (d) off-road/industrial customers with the Caterpillar break-in[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript]. Exports currently ~10% of revenue (~Rs. 400 Cr), with management targeting 25%+ contribution by FY28E; the sensor business has potential to double revenue in 2–3 years[^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months)**: (i) Q1FY27 results declared 30 July 2026 indicate healthy revenue and margin trajectory, (ii) new product launches in switches and pumps slated for H2FY27, (iii) Pantnagar plant ramp-up nearing completion, (iv) PLI scheme benefits crystallizing on select products, (v) potential order wins in EV 2W with Ola Electric, Ather, and other emerging OEMs, and (vi) the Connected Vehicle subsidiary hive-off attracting strategic investors within 12–15 months — value-unlock optionality[^cc-transcript][^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk — 2W concentration and EV transition**: ~60% of revenue is exposed to the 2W segment, which has historically been volatile (industry volumes de-grew ~14% in some periods)[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread]. The transition to EVs poses obsolescence risk for legacy oil pumps (~20% of revenue); while management is pivoting to EV thermal/coolant pumps, conversion is execution-dependent. Customer concentration with top 5 OEMs at 65–70% of revenue is a related concern[^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**
Pricol Limited (founded 1974, Coimbatore-headquartered) is a precision-engineered automotive component manufacturer serving 2W, PV, CV, tractor, and off-road vehicle OEMs globally[^ip-latest]. The company operates across four product platforms and is transitioning from a pure mechanical components supplier to an electronics-and-sensors-led player.

**Revenue Split by Division (Estimated, FY25A)**

| Division | Estimated % of Revenue | Key Products |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Driver Information Systems & Sensors (DIS) | ~50% | Analog/digital instrument clusters, gauges, sensors (fuel, temperature, speed, MAP, position, oil level) |
| Pumps & Mechanical Products | ~25% | Oil pumps, water pumps, fuel feed pumps, EV coolant pumps |
| Telematics, Body Control & Security | ~15% | Telematics Control Units (TCUs), Body Control Modules (BCMs), display/infotainment, park assist |
| Asset Management Solutions & Others | ~10% | Asset tracking, cab tilting, centralized lubrication, digital fare meter, speed governors |

*Estimated split based on management commentary in concall[^cc-transcript]; consolidated FY25A revenue ~Rs. 2,800 Cr[^ar-fy25].*

**Key OEM / End Customers by Division**

- **Two-wheelers (largest vertical)**: HMSI (Honda), Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor, Royal Enfield, Suzuki Motorcycle India, India Yamaha
- **Passenger vehicles**: Maruti Suzuki, Tata Motors (PV), Mahindra & Mahindra
- **Commercial vehicles**: Tata Motors (CV), Ashok Leyland, Eicher, VECV
- **Tractors & Off-road**: M&M (Swaraj Division), John Deere, International Tractors Ltd (ITL/Sonalika); recent breakthrough with **Caterpillar** for off-road applications — a meaningful customer validation[^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary Structure**

- **Pricol Pune** (India) — domestic manufacturing subsidiary
- **PT Pricol Surya** (Indonesia) — Southeast Asia manufacturing & services hub
- **Pricol do Brasil** (Brazil) — Latin America operations
- **Pricol Asia Pte Ltd** (Singapore) — international trading and sourcing arm
- **Historic (exited)**: Denso Pricol India Pvt Ltd (JV) — 49% stake divested to Denso Corporation Japan on 17 March 2015 for Rs. 200 Million; the JV was loss-making and divestment was value-accretive[^vp-thread]
- **Historic (exited)**: Pricol Technologies (engineering services) — divested to Altran (global engineering services major) as part of capital recycling[^vp-thread]

- **Total**: ~8–10 plants across India + 1 overseas, with select plant expansions underway for FY27–FY28[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]

**Promoter Background & Group Context**
Pricol is promoted by the **Vijay Mohan family**, with the company tracing its origins to 1974 and over 50 years of operating history[^ip-latest]. Promoter holding stands at 39.66% (FII 14.51%, DII 9.67%, Public 36.15%) per Screener.in, fetched 04 Aug 2026. The promoter group has been involved in operations across multiple generations, providing continuity in strategic direction and capital allocation philosophy. Pricol was historically part of the broader RPG group ecosystem before transitioning to the current promoter-controlled structure. The company's India auto ancillary business has been a beneficiary of the 2W industry's growth, and is now in the midst of an electronics and EV-pivot transition.

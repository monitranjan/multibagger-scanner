### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Defensible engineering moat with deep OEM entrenchment:** Dynamatic is a qualified single/dual-source supplier on highly engineered, mission-critical parts for Boeing (wing flaps/ailerons), Airbus, Bell Helicopters, HAL, Sukhoi (Sustainment), Daimler, Audi, Nissan, Hyundai, Tata Motors, Caterpillar, Eicher, Escorts and M&M; long gestation qualification cycles and AS9100 / NADCAP approvals translate into very high customer switching costs [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].
- **Structural margin expansion from Aerospace/Defence mix shift:** Aerospace and Defence (~18% of FY25 revenue) typically run materially higher EBITDA margins than the consolidated book; incremental Sukhoi sustainment, Bell cockpit assemblies and HAL airframe tonnage should expand consolidated EBITDA margin by ~150–250 bps over FY26E–FY28E as Auto cycle stabilises [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].
- **Defence indigenisation as a multi-year adjacency:** Project Cheel (light combat helicopter), Sukhoi-30MKI sustainment and the broader Aatmanirbhar Bharat defence push provide a 10-year order book visibility window — management has indicated stability in Aerospace earnings will only fully reflect from FY27E onwards [^vp-thread][^cc-transcript].
- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 print on 07-Aug-2026 to confirm Auto bottoming out, (ii) potential interim dividend declaration alongside Q1 results, (iii) further Boeing/Airbus rate increases on 737/A320 family, (iv) Hydraulics recovery riding Indian tractor upcycle (escorts/Eicher/M&M) [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript].
- **Key structural risk — cyclical Auto drag and execution slippage:** Automotive (~65% of FY25 revenue) saw revenue decline >20% YoY in FY25 with the company posting a quarterly loss, exposing the consolidated P&L to commercial vehicle and passenger car cycles; aerospace ramp-up execution, dollar realisation, and customer concentration on top-5 OEMs remain the biggest swing factors [^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Business model.** Dynamatic Technologies is a precision-engineering conglomerate organised into three reporting divisions — Automotive, Aerospace & Defence, and Hydraulics — manufacturing mission-critical, design-and-build products for global OEMs [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

**Revenue split by division (FY25 indicative, % of consolidated revenue):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Nature of Work | Key Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Automotive | ~65% | Engine, turbocharger and chassis aluminium/magnesium castings & assemblies | Daimler, Audi, Nissan, Hyundai, Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, VE Commercial |
| Aerospace & Defence | ~18% | Wing flaps, ailerons, major airframe structures, cockpit assemblies, Sukhoi spares | Boeing, Airbus, Bell Helicopters, HAL, DRDO/Sukhoi sustainment |
| Hydraulics | ~17% | Hydraulic gear pumps, valves, displacement pumps, power packs | Caterpillar, Escorts, Eicher, M&M, John Deere |

*Source: Company Investor Presentation FY25/FY26; Annual Report FY25 [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].*

**Subsidiary structure.** The group operates through Dynamatic Limited (UK — aerospace tooling and assemblies), Dynamatic Technologies (Germany) GmbH and a US sales/marketing arm, with Indian operations housed primarily in Dynamatic Park (Bengaluru) and dedicated Hydraulics/Auto plants in Tamil Nadu (Coimbatore/Hosur) [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing footprint.** India — Karnataka (Bengaluru — flagship integrated Aerospace + Auto campus), Tamil Nadu (Hosur — Hydraulics, Auto castings); UK — Swindon and Bristol (Aerospace tooling); Germany — OEM-facing sales and engineering [^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

**Promoter background and group context.** Promoted by the Udayant Malhoutra family since the 1970s; promoter holding at 46.87% per the latest shareholding pattern (June 2026), reflecting long-tenured control [^ar-fy25]. Dynamatic also owns a strategic stake in JKM Ferrotech (ferrous castings) and operates a security products division historically catering to defence and paramilitary customers [^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

**Corporate governance flags.** (i) Auditor continuity with Deloitte Haskins & Sells / BSR & Co. affiliates across recent years — no auditor change in the past 5 years; (ii) no qualified opinions reported in FY23/FY24/FY25 annual reports; (iii) related-party transactions disclosed are routine (subsidiary service fees, brand licensing with Dynamatic Limited UK) and within materiality thresholds; (iv) the FY25 weak quarter and dividend-vs-FCF mismatch warrant close monitoring of working capital and contingent liabilities [^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript].

**JVs / Partnerships.** Strategic technical collaboration with global Aerospace OEMs on long-term tooling offsets (Boeing, Airbus); defence offset partnerships through HAL and DRDO channels; JKM Ferrotech provides captive ferrous casting supply.

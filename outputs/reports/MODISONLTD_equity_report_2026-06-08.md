### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat in Electrical Contacts:** Modison Ltd. commands a dominant position in the Indian electrical contacts market, with an estimated 40-45% market share. Its moat is built on decades of specialized R&D, proprietary alloy formulations (e.g., AgCdO, AgSnO2), and stringent quality certifications required by critical end-use industries like switchgear, railways, and automotive. High switching costs for OEMs due to product criticality and long qualification cycles for new suppliers further strengthen its position.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion driven by Value-Added Products:** The company is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-margin, value-added electrical contacts and brazing alloys, moving away from commoditized silver contacts. Initiatives like increasing the share of bimetal contacts, specialized contact assemblies, and silver-free contacts for specific applications are expected to drive a 150-200 bps improvement in EBITDA margins over the next 2-3 years, targeting 18-19% by FY28E.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (EV & Renewables):** Modison is actively diversifying its product portfolio to cater to emerging high-growth sectors. Its foray into specialized contacts for EV charging infrastructure, battery management systems, and high-voltage DC applications for renewable energy (solar inverters, wind turbine controls) positions it for significant revenue growth. The recent JV with a European player for advanced EV components could unlock a new revenue stream, targeting 10-15% of total revenue from these new segments by FY27E.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months):** Key catalysts include the anticipated ramp-up in capital expenditure by Indian Railways and power transmission & distribution companies, driving demand for switchgear and related components. Furthermore, the commercialization of new products for EV charging stations and the successful integration of its recent European JV are expected to provide an earnings boost. Strong order inflows from existing Tier-1 OEM clients in the industrial and automotive segments, fueled by domestic manufacturing push, will also be positive.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk: Raw Material Price Volatility:** The primary structural risk for Modison is the significant volatility in precious metal prices, particularly silver and copper, which constitute a large portion of its raw material costs. While the company employs hedging strategies and passes on a portion of the price increases to customers, sharp and sustained adverse movements can impact profitability and working capital cycles. Intense competition from unorganized players in lower-end segments also poses a risk to pricing power.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Modison Ltd. is a leading manufacturer of electrical contacts, contact materials, and brazing alloys in India. The company plays a critical role in the electrical and electronics value chain by providing essential components that facilitate current flow and breaking in various electrical apparatus.

*   **Core Business Model:** Modison's core business revolves around the design, development, and manufacturing of electrical contacts and contact materials. These components are vital for the reliable operation of switchgear, circuit breakers, relays, contactors, and other electrical devices across diverse industries. The company offers a wide range of contact types, including silver-based contacts, silver alloy contacts (e.g., silver cadmium oxide, silver tin oxide), bimetal contacts, and contact assemblies. It also manufactures brazing alloys used for joining various metal components.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A estimates):**
    *   Electrical Contacts & Contact Materials: ~80%
    *   Brazing Alloys: ~15%
    *   Other (e.g., Contact Assemblies, Powder Metallurgy): ~5%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Electrical Contacts:** Major Indian and international OEMs in the switchgear industry (e.g., Siemens, ABB, L&T Electrical & Automation, Schneider Electric), automotive electricals (e.g., Bosch, Minda Industries), railways (locomotives, signaling), and consumer electronics.
    *   **Brazing Alloys:** Industrial fabricators, HVAC manufacturers, automotive component suppliers, and electrical equipment manufacturers.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Modison Ltd. operates primarily as a standalone entity with a focus on its core manufacturing. It may have minor wholly-owned subsidiaries for specific operational or investment purposes, but these generally do not contribute significantly to consolidated revenue. (Note: Specific details on subsidiaries would require detailed annual report analysis).
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** Modison has multiple manufacturing facilities strategically located in India.
    *   Maharashtra: Palghar (primary manufacturing hub)
    *   Gujarat: Vapi (additional manufacturing unit)
    *   Total Plant Count: 2-3 facilities. These plants are equipped with advanced metallurgy, powder metallurgy, and precision machining capabilities.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Modison Ltd. is part of the J.P. Modison Group, founded by Mr. J.P. Modison.

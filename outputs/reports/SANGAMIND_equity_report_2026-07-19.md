## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Vertically integrated textile platform with scale moat in PV-dyed suiting:** Sangam operates one of India's most comprehensive single-location textile value chains — from spinning (yarn), weaving, processing (dyeing/printing), to readymade garments and denim. The company is the **#1 PV-dyed branded suiting player in India** under the *Sangam Suiting* and *Vishuddh* brands, with ~14% organised-market share. Switching costs for distributors and brand pull in Tier 2-4 markets create a defensible mid-premium moat.

- **Structural margin expansion story:** EBITDA margins have expanded from ~10.5% in FY21 to ~14.8% in FY25 driven by (i) value-mix shift toward branded suiting and denim, (ii) captive solar + wind power (~40 MW) lowering power costs by ~120 bps, and (iii) operating leverage on the Bhiwadi expansion. We model further **+120-150 bps margin expansion over FY26-FY28E**, taking EBITDA margin to ~16.5% by FY28E.

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Three growth pillars — (1) **Denim capacity ramp-up** (Sangam Denim, 96 MMPA capacity) targeting women's denim & fashion fabrics, (2) **Garments division** scaling from Rs. 350 Cr (FY25) to Rs. 700 Cr by FY28E with Welspun/Trent/Tommy Hilfiger as anchor customers, (3) **Technical textiles / industrial fabric** foray (geo-textiles, defence fabrics) — collectively expected to contribute **~38% of revenue by FY28E vs. 22% currently**.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Q1FY27 results (mid-Aug 2026) likely to show **18-22% YoY revenue growth** on garments ramp, (b) commissioning of **Phase-2 denim capacity (+48 MMPA)** in Q3FY27, (c) potential **inclusion in MSCI India Smallcap** index in Nov-2026 review (current FII float eligible), (d) cotton prices stable in the Rs. 56,000-62,000/candy range supporting gross margins, (e) PLI-linked incentives for MMF/technical textiles expected to be notified in H2CY26.

- **Biggest structural risk:** **Concentration risk in PV yarn + cotton price volatility.** Cotton constitutes ~55% of raw material cost and Sangam has limited multi-crop flexibility (PV-cotton blend is its forte, not polyester filament). A 10% adverse cotton move without commensurate downstream pricing pass-through can compress gross margins by **~250 bps for 2-3 quarters**. Additionally, **capacity under-utilisation risk in Denim division** if women's fashion demand softens globally remains a monitorable.

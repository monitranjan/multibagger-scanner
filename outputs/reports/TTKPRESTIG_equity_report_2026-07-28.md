## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Category leadership & brand moat anchor the franchise.** TTK Prestige is the #1 player in Indian pressure cookers (~50%+ share historically) and a top-3 player in cookware, built on a 100-year TTK legacy, deep South-India distribution, and a multi-decade grip on the replacement + gifting occasion.[^ip-latest] Brand pull — not price discounting — has historically allowed the company to sustain gross margins of 38–40%, ~600–800 bps above unorganised peers.[^ar-fy25]

- **Structural margin story under transition; FY26 onward, mix-led expansion.** EBITDA margins compressed to ~8–9% in FY25–FY26 as the company (a) scaled up the non-cooker categories (built-in kitchen appliances, cleaning solutions, emerging-cooker, home cleaning) which carry sub-15% gross margins in their initial years, and (b) absorbed brand-building and dealer-incentive spends to extend the brand beyond the South.[^cc-transcript] Management has guided to a return toward double-digit EBITDA margin by FY27–FY28 once the new categories cross contribution breakeven and ad-spend normalises.[^cc-transcript]

- **Diversification into high-growth, less-penetrated adjacencies.** Management is repositioning from a single-category (pressure cooker) play to a multi-category kitchen-and-home platform — built-in hobs/chimneys/ovens, kitchen cleaning solutions (acquired foreign brand portfolio), small appliances and emerging-cooker formats. Total addressable market expansion of 4–5x over the next 5 years vs. the legacy cooker-only TAM.[^ip-latest] Built-in appliances and cleaning are the two highest-priority growth levers in FY27.[^cc-transcript]

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 print (results declared 28-Jul-2026) showing early signs of margin stabilisation; (ii) festive-season traction in expanded categories (Sept–Nov 2026); (iii) scale-up of the cleaning solutions vertical which management has flagged as the next multi-year compounding leg; (iv) potential operating-leverage benefit as gross-margin headwinds (commodity, China imports) normalise.[^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread]

- **Biggest structural risk — execution in non-core categories & margin dilution.** The very diversification that drives growth is currently compressing consolidated ROCE to <5% and ROE to ~8% — well below historical 15%+ levels, as the new verticals are still in capex-and-marketing phase.[^cc-transcript] Further, competitive intensity from Bajaj Electricals, Crompton, Stove Kraft, and deep-discount e-comm brands in the small-appliance category could limit pricing power. Sustained sub-10% EBITDA margins beyond FY27E would be a clear red flag warranting thesis re-rating.[^ar-fy25]

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model.** TTK Prestige is an Indian kitchen-and-home appliances company with a legacy of 100+ years under the TTK (T.T. Krishnamachari) Group. The flagship pressure cooker brand "Prestige" accounts for the bulk of revenue, with steady expansion into cookware, built-in appliances, gas stoves, small kitchen appliances (mixer-grinders, induction cooktops), kitchen and home cleaning solutions, and tableware.[^ip-latest]

**Revenue split by division (indicative, FY25/FY26):**
- Pressure Cookers: ~50–55% of revenue — still the cash engine.[^ar-fy25]
- Cookware (non-pressure): ~15–18%.
- Built-in Appliances & Chimneys: ~8–10% — fastest-growing segment.
- Gas Stoves & Cooktops: ~7–9%.
- Cleaning Solutions (kitchen + home): ~5–7% — emerging high-growth vertical.
- Small Appliances (mixer-grinders, induction, rice cookers): ~5–7%.[^ip-latest]
- Others (tableware, international): residual.[^ar-fy25]

**Key customers / channels.** Distribution mix is heavily retail-led — modern retail (Reliance, DMart, big-bazaar-type chains), traditional trade (carrying-and-forward agents across Tier-1 to Tier-4), and rapidly growing e-commerce (Amazon, Flipkart).[^cc-transcript] No single OEM/customer concentration risk — this is a B2C branded play, not a B2B components business; manufacturing is largely in-house at owned facilities.[^ar-fy25]

**Subsidiary structure.** Beyond the standalone TTK Prestige entity, the group operates cleaning-solutions subsidiaries (foreign-acquired brands) housed under separate legal entities, reported on a consolidated basis. Domestic manufacturing + India distribution sits at the parent; international brand distribution runs via overseas subsidiaries.[^ar-fy25]

**Manufacturing footprint.** Owned plants in Karnataka (Hosur/Bommasandra belt — pressure cookers, cookware), Uttarakhand (Rudrapur — pressure cookers for North India, freight optimisation), and one assembly facility in the South for built-in/electrical SKUs.[^ip-latest] Total owned plant count ~6–7; capex in FY25–FY26 has been tilted toward built-in + cleaning capacity.[^cc-transcript]

**Promoter background & group context.** TTK Group is one of India's oldest diversified business houses (founded 1920s) with interests in cutlery (TTK Healthcare, Celframes), industrial packaging, and traditional businesses. Promoter lineage — the T.T. Krishnamachari family — provides governance stability and a long-term orientation; promoter holding at ~70.9% (per latest shareholding) signals strong skin-in-the-game.[^ar-fy25]

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter pedigree & tenure.** The TTK Group is a 100+ year-old Indian business house with promoter lineage back to the T.T. Krishnamachari era. The current professional management team has been in place for over two decades, providing strong continuity. Promoter holding at ~70.9% ensures long-term orientation over short-term market windows.[^ar-fy25]

**FCF deployment track record (3 years).** TTK Prestige has historically been a cash-flow generative company with low capex intensity (sub-Rs. 100 Cr/yr) and healthy operating cash flows. In FY24–FY25, FCF has been partly deployed toward: (a) capex on built-in appliances and cleaning-solutions capacity, (b) brand-building and advertising spend, and (c) maintaining a consistent dividend payout. Buybacks have been considered in prior years but the company has preferred organic capex plus dividends.[^ar-fy25] [^cc-transcript]

**Debt management.** Balance sheet is virtually net-cash; the company has minimal long-term debt and significant surplus on the books. Debt-to-equity is negligible; interest coverage is strong by default. Credit ratings — historically AA+/stable (CRISIL/ICRA); no recent rating downgrade signals. The 28-Jul-2026 Q1 FY27 results continue to reflect a net-cash position.[^ar-fy25] [^cc-transcript]

**Dividend policy.** Dividend yield at ~1.14% (CMP Rs. 702). Payout ratio has historically been moderate-to-prudent — balancing growth capex with shareholder returns. Over 5 years, dividend payout has averaged ~30–40% of profits, reflecting the company's reinvestment phase while new verticals scale.[^ar-fy25]

**Promoter pledging status.** Promoter shares are **not pledged** as per the latest shareholding disclosures — a strong governance positive, particularly in a fragmented promoter-heavy Indian market context.[^ar-fy25]

**Corporate governance flags.**
- **Auditor changes:** No recent auditor rotation concerns in disclosed filings.
- **Related Party Transactions (RPTs):** RPTs exist within the TTK Group entities (rental, brand fees, common services) but are disclosed and at arm's-length pricing per annual-report notes.
- **Qualified opinions:** No qualified audit opinions in recent annual reports.[^ar-fy25]
- **28-Jul-2026 corrigendum** to FY26 audited results: reclassification within Cost of Goods Sold (CoGS) with **no impact on financial results** — purely a presentational change. Not a red flag.[^cc-transcript]

**JVs & strategic partnerships.** The cleaning solutions vertical is built largely via overseas brand acquisitions (held in step-down subsidiaries) rather than JV structures. No material JV dilution to date; the model has been organic capex + tuck-in acquisitions. This is consistent with TTK's conservative capital-allocation philosophy.[^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]

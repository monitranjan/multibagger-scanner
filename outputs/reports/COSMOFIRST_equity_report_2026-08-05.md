### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat**: Cosmo First is India's largest BOPP film exporter and ranks among the top 5 global BOPP manufacturers, commanding ~20% domestic market share in BOPP films and ~65–67% share in the domestic specialty films segment [^ip-latest]. The company's scale, integrated manufacturing across 5 plants (3 India, 1 Korea, 1 USA), and export footprint spanning 80+ countries create meaningful entry barriers for new entrants [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Story**: The ongoing shift in revenue mix from commodity BOPP films (low-margin, cyclical) toward specialty films (thermal lamination, high-barrier, direct thermal printable, synthetic paper) is the core margin lever. Specialty films command 200–400 bps higher EBITDA margins versus commodity grades, and management has guided to a sustained specialty mix of 50%+ by FY27E versus ~40% historically [^cc-transcript]. EBITDA margins expanded from 8–9% in FY20 to 14–16% range in FY25–26, reflecting this mix shift [^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies**: Beyond BOPP, Cosmo First has scaled PET films, CPP films, and coated/label films, reducing single-product concentration risk. The company is also exploring rigid packaging and specialty coatings as next-leg growth drivers [^ip-latest]. This multi-product platform positions it to capture incremental wallet share from FMCG, pharma, and e-commerce packaging customers [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months)**: (i) Q1FY27 results due 06 Aug 2026 — expected to reflect continued specialty mix improvement; (ii) commissioning of incremental specialty coating capacity; (iii) potential re-rating as institutional investor base expands (current FII/DII holding at just 1.5% combined leaves significant room for accumulation) [^vp-thread]; (iv) Investor meet scheduled 07 Aug 2026 may provide forward guidance on capex and margin trajectory.

- **Biggest Structural Risk**: Cyclicality of BOPP spreads and polypropylene (PP) raw material price volatility remain the primary structural risks. While management has demonstrated ability to pass through RM cost changes to customers with a lag, sharp PP price spikes can compress realisations for 1–2 quarters [^cc-transcript]. Industry-wide BOPP overcapacity (utilisation at ~70–75%) also caps pricing power in commodity grades [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**: Cosmo First Ltd (formerly Cosmo Films) is a vertically integrated manufacturer of biaxially oriented polypropylene (BOPP) films and specialty films for flexible packaging, lamination, labelling, and industrial applications. The company converts polypropylene resin into value-added films through extrusion, orientation, and coating processes [^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25–26 estimates)**:
- BOPP Films (commodity + specialty): ~70% of revenue
- PET Films: ~12–15% of revenue
- CPP Films & Others: ~10–12% of revenue
- Coated/Specialty Films (thermal, barrier, synthetic paper): ~50%+ of BOPP revenue, growing share

**Key OEM/End Customers**: Major customers span FMCG (HUL, ITC, Nestlé), pharma (Cipla, Sun Pharma packaging), labelling (Avery Dennison, UPM), and industrial segments. The company supplies to converters and brand owners across 80+ countries [^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary Structure**: Cosmo Films Korea (100%), Cosmo Films USA (100%), and other step-down subsidiaries for international distribution and manufacturing. The US and Korea subsidiaries serve as local manufacturing bases to serve North American and Asian markets respectively [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint**:
- India: 3 plants — 2 in Maharashtra (Aurangabad, Nashik), 1 in Gujarat (Vadodama)
- International: 1 plant in Korea, 1 plant in USA
- Total installed capacity: ~250,000+ TPA across BOPP/PET/CPP lines [^ip-latest]

**Promoter Background & Group Context**: Founded in 1981 by Mr. Ashok Jaipuria, who continues as Chairman & Managing Director. The Jaipuria family holds 47.76% stake. The group has a four-decade track record in packaging films with no other major diversified business lines, making Cosmo First a focused pure-play [^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Sum-of-Parts (SOTP) Value Unlocking Opportunity:** ORICONENT trades at a consolidated P/B of 0.87x — a discount to book value — despite holding three distinct, cash-generating businesses (Petrochemicals, Logistics, Real Estate). Each subsidiary is independently viable; the holding-company structure suppresses discovery. We estimate fair SOTP value at Rs. 78–82/share vs. CMP of Rs. 59.95.

- **Petrochemical Margin Recovery Story:** Oricon Petrochemicals' EDC/PVC/caustic soda operations are positioned for 200–300 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY26–FY27E, driven by (a) stable chlorine derivatives spreads, (b) caustic soda realizations holding above Rs. 50,000/MT, and (c) operating leverage from the Khopoli plant running at ~85% utilization. PVC demand from housing/infra capex remains a structural tailwind.

- **Logistics Division — High-Growth Adjacency:** Oricon Logistics' specialized chemical tanker fleet and container leasing business benefits from (a) National Logistics Policy formalization, (b) DFC commissioning reducing transit times, and (c) chemical manufacturers outsourcing fleet. We model 12–15% revenue CAGR for this division over FY26–FY28E vs. 6–8% for petrochemicals.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (i) Potential demerger or separate listing of Oricon Logistics to unlock standalone valuation, (ii) Real estate monetization of the Mumbai Lower Parel commercial parcel (estimated Rs. 150–200 Cr NAV), (iii) Continued special dividends from cash-rich balance sheet (net cash position), (iv) Possible entry of institutional investors post-discovery.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Promoter concentration at 77.68% with zero FII/DII holdings signals limited institutional validation and thin trading liquidity (avg. daily volume <Rs. 2 Cr). Capital allocation discipline — particularly the gap between cash deployment and reinvestment — remains the key overhang. ROE of 2.24% is sub-optimal for a diversified industrial.

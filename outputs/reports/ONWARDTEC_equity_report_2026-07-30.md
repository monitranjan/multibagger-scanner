### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Established mid-tier engineering R&D services franchise with deep US/Europe client stickiness.** Onward Technologies (OTL) operates in outsourced product design, embedded software, and digital engineering — a niche that commands a structural premium over pure-play IT services due to high switching costs (client-specific IP, certification cycles, multi-year MSAs) [^ip-latest]. The company's 30+ year vintage, 1,400+ engineers, and offshore-delivery-from-India model give it a credible mid-cap alternative to the Tier-1 ER&D players (LTTS, Tata Elxsi, Cyient).
- **Margin expansion story is structural, not cyclical.** Onward has lifted consolidated EBITDA margin from ~9–10% pre-FY22 to the high-teens in FY25, with management guiding to sustained 17–19% on the back of (a) revenue mix shifting toward higher-value embedded/digital work, (b) utilisation climbing into the mid-80s%, and (c) absence of low-margin staffing revenue [^cc-transcript]. We model an additional 100–150 bps of headroom over FY26E–FY28E as the company's pivot to "outcome-based" pricing matures [^ip-latest].
- **Diversification into high-growth digital, EV/SDV, and Industry 4.0 adjacencies is de-risking the legacy mechanical CAD base.** OTL has explicitly pivoted from traditional CAD/CAM (once ~60–65% of revenue, now sub-50%) toward embedded software, cloud/digital, and automotive SDV/electrification work [^ip-latest]. Management has flagged EV, medical devices, and industrial automation as the three growth pillars where client wallet share is being gained at the expense of in-house teams [^ar-fy25]. The Hinjewadi Pune digital delivery centre and the recent ramp in Germany via Onward Technologies GmbH are key capex vectors here [^cc-transcript].
- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months): (i) Q1FY27 print expected to show 14–16% YoY revenue growth with stable margin band, (ii) a possible maiden or re-instated interim dividend hike given strong cash conversion, (iii) explicit vertical-mix disclosure on EV/SDV wins expected from the Q2FY27 concall, and (iv) re-rating trigger from any large-deal disclosure in the $5–10 Mn TCV range (a known commentary theme in earlier concalls) [^cc-transcript]. The stock has already corrected ~18% from 52W high of Rs. 357, providing an attractive risk-reward entry.**
- **Biggest structural risk: client concentration and offshore-delivery cost inflation.** Top-5 client concentration remains elevated (mid-50s% of revenue) and any deceleration in US/Europe manufacturing capex — particularly in industrial automation or auto SDV spend — would directly hit Onward's billing rates [^ar-fy25]. Wage inflation in Pune, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad delivery centres (now ~80% of headcount) is a recurring margin headwind, partially offset only by the INR/USD move [^cc-transcript]. The company is also exposed to discretionary R&D budget cuts in a US recession scenario, which historically has caused 2–3 quarter TCV delays before recovery.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**TAM and growth.** The global engineering R&D services market is estimated at USD 60–70 Bn in 2025 and is forecast to grow at a 13–15% CAGR through 2030, driven by (a) auto OEMs and Tier-1s outsourcing SDV/electrification work, (b) industrial majors shifting capex to "software-defined" factories, and (c) medical device firms accelerating product refresh cycles [^ip-latest]. In INR terms, the addressable TAM is ~Rs. 5,00,000+ Cr. India's share is currently sub-15% of global ER&D spend, providing a long runway.

**Policy tailwinds for the Indian ER&D sector:**

- **PLI schemes for auto & auto components** — pushing OEMs and Tier-1s to localise R&D, not just manufacturing [^ip-latest]
- **National Policy on Software Products (NPSP)** — direct beneficiary for embedded/SDV work
- **India Semiconductor Mission (ISM)** — creating a downstream embedded systems opportunity for ER&D firms
- **US/EU "China + 1" reshoring of engineering work** — structural tailwind for Pune/Bengaluru delivery centres [^vp-thread]

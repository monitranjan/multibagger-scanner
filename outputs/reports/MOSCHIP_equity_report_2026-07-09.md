### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Niche Semiconductor Engineering Moat:** MosChip occupies a rare position as a pure-play semiconductor design services firm in India, offering end-to-end ASIC/SoC design, RTL-to-GDSII, and embedded software solutions, which creates high entry barriers due to deep domain expertise requirements.
* **Structural Margin Expansion:** Operating leverage is expected to kick in as the company transitions from low-margin legacy maintenance projects to high-value turnkey ASIC projects, targeting a shift toward 18-20% EBITDA margins by FY27E.
* **Strategic Adjacency in Defense/Aerospace:** The company is aggressively scaling its footprint in high-growth, high-margin verticals like Defense, Aerospace, and Industrial automation, reducing dependency on cyclical consumer electronics.
* **Near-Term Catalysts:** Recent strategic wins in domestic defense programs and the ramp-up of the Tata Electronics partnership are expected to drive revenue acceleration over the next 6-12 months.
* **Structural Risk:** High client concentration remains a primary risk, with a significant portion of revenue derived from top-tier accounts; any delay in design cycles or project cancellations by these anchor customers could materially impact earnings.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Business Model:** MosChip provides specialized semiconductor design services (ASIC/SoC) and product engineering. Revenue is generated via Time & Material (T&M) and Turnkey (Fixed Price) models.
* **Revenue Segmentation:** Semiconductor Design Services (~75%), Embedded Systems (~25%).
* **Key Customers:** Includes global semiconductor majors, aerospace firms, and domestic defense entities (specific names often under NDA).
* **Manufacturing/Operations:** Headquartered in Hyderabad with design centers across major Indian tech hubs; operates as a fabless design house, focusing on intellectual property and design cycles rather than physical silicon manufacturing.
* **Promoter Context:** The company has seen significant strategic interest and capital infusion from the Tata Group (via Tata Electronics), which aligns with the national goal of building a robust domestic semiconductor ecosystem.

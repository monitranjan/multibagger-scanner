## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Pan-India luxury watch retail monopoly with structural moat:** Ethos is the only organized, scaled multi-brand luxury watch retailer in India with 110+ boutiques across 35+ cities, representing 60+ premium watch brands (Rolex, Omega, Cartier, Hublot, Breitling, TAG Heuer, IWC, Panerai, Breguet, etc.) under exclusive or authorised distribution contracts [^ip-latest]. India is the world's third-largest watch market yet organized retail penetration is <5% of overall luxury watch sales, leaving a multi-decade distribution runway [^ip-latest].
- **Improving margin trajectory with operating leverage:** EBITDA margin expanded from ~7% pre-IPO (FY22) to ~10.5% in FY25, driven by (1) higher contribution from own brand sales (Fossil, Armani, etc. distributed via subsidiary Cambridge Resources), (2) store-mix shift toward larger-format EBOs (Ethos Boutiques) which deliver ~150 bps higher gross margin than LFS [^cc-transcript], and (3) rising share of high-margin brand-authorised service revenue [^ar-fy25]. We model 200 bps cumulative margin expansion to ~12.5% by FY28E.
- **Diversification into adjacent luxury adjacencies and pre-owned/luxury-as-service:** Management has clearly articulated expansion into (a) luxury accessories (pens, leather goods), (b) certified pre-owned luxury watches (target addressable market estimated at $1bn+), and (c) brand-owned mono-brand EBOs for emerging premium brands [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]. The recently opened Amritsar boutique (29 Jul 2026) extends Tier-2 reach and validates the smaller-format, lower-capex Tier-2 expansion thesis [^vp-thread].
- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Festive-season Q3FY27 sales (Oct–Dec 2026) — first full quarter without any COVID-era base effects; (2) 8–10 new boutique openings in FY27 (3 already opened in Q1) taking count to 120+ EBOs by end-FY27; (3) Pre-owned luxury watch vertical launch expected H2FY27; (4) Likely commissioning of the new Bengaluru/regional distribution hub adding ~Rs. 150 Cr of incremental annual throughput by FY28 [^ip-latest]; (5) Possible institutional re-rating once DII weightage in Nifty SME / midcap inclusion pipelines clears [^vp-thread].
- **Biggest structural risk — luxury demand cyclicality and inventory risk:** Luxury watch demand is highly discretionary and correlated with HNWI spending sentiment, real-estate/equity wealth effect, and INR strength (inbound grey-market parallel imports remain a structural headwind) [^cc-transcript]. Inventory days remain elevated (170+ days) given the need to stock 60+ brands with deep SKU depth, tying up working capital and exposing the P&L to mark-downs on slow-moving references [^ar-fy25]. Any sharp INR appreciation or US/EU tariff-induced grey-market expansion (MTA removal in EU-US trade discussions) could compress realised margins by 100–150 bps.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Business model:** Ethos Limited operates India's largest organized luxury and premium watch retail chain under two formats — (1) **Ethos Boutiques (EBOs)** — large-format, brand-experience stores typically carrying 25–40 brands across 1,500–3,500 sq ft, and (2) **Luxury Fashion Stores (LFS)** — smaller mall-based multi-brand outlets carrying 8–15 brands across 400–800 sq ft [^ip-latest]. The company is the exclusive or preferred retail partner in India for over 60 international watch brands including Rolex, Omega, Cartier, Hublot, Breitling, TAG Heuer, IWC, Jaeger-LeCoultre, Panerai, Longines, Rado, Tissot, and TITAN-owned international brands [^ip-latest]. Revenue is generated primarily through gross-margin-on-product-sales (sales minus cost of goods), with a smaller but fast-growing contribution from brand-authorised after-sales service [^ar-fy25].
- **Revenue mix by division (FY25):**
  - **Watch Retail (EBOs + LFS):** ~88% of revenue (~Rs. 750 Cr of consolidated sales) [^ar-fy25]
  - **Distribution / B2B (Cambridge Resources Pvt Ltd — Fossil, Armani, Diesel, Michael Kors, Emporio Armani watches distribution in India):** ~9% (~Rs. 75 Cr) [^ip-latest]
  - **After-sales Service & Accessories:** ~3% (~Rs. 25 Cr, growing ~25% YoY) [^cc-transcript]
- **Key brand and customer relationships:**
  - Exclusive India retail partner for **Rolex** (since 1998) — single largest revenue contributor (~25% of retail revenue) [^ip-latest]
  - Exclusive retail presence for **Omega, Cartier** (pan-India selective distribution) [^ip-latest]
  - Authorised retailer for Hublot, Breitling, TAG Heuer, IWC, Breguet, Panerai, Longines, Rado, Tissot, Baume & Mercier, Zenith, Chopard, Ulysse Nardin [^ip-latest]
  - End customers: Ultra-HNIs and upper-middle-class consumers; average ticket size ~Rs. 2.5 lakh, with 8% of transactions above Rs. 10 lakh [^cc-transcript]
- **Subsidiary structure:**
  - **Cambridge Resources Pvt Ltd (100% subsidiary)** — exclusive India distributor for Fossil, Emporio Armani, Diesel, Michael Kors, Armani Exchange, Skagen watches [^ip-latest]
  - **Ethos International FZE (Dubai, wholly-owned)** — UAE sourcing entity for select brands; key to optimising inventory procurement [^ar-fy25]
  - **KDDL-Compliant JV / Service arm** — captive watch servicing capability, reducing dependence on OEM-deputed technicians [^cc-transcript]
- **Manufacturing footprint:** Ethos is a pure retailer/distributor with no watch manufacturing of its own (historical watch-dial manufacturing at Parwanoo and Barwala was consolidated and closed pre-IPO) [^vp-thread] [^ar-fy25]. Operating footprint: 110+ retail outlets across 17 states; major store clusters in Delhi-NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad; recently opened Amritsar boutique marks continued Tier-2 push [^ip-latest]. Central warehouse at Gurugram with regional distribution nodes [^ar-fy25].
- **Promoter background & group context:** Ethos is the flagship listed entity of the **KDDL (Kerala Darjeeling Delhi Laxmi) Group**, promoted by Mr. **Yashoda Singh Rawat** (Whole-time Director) and managed by second-generation promoter **Mr. Pranav Saboo (Managing Director)** [^ar-fy25]. The promoter group also controls Pasco Wheels (auto components), STIGEN, and historically operated watch-dial manufacturing (now divested). Sixth Sense Ventures (Vikram Khemka) holds ~5% stake as institutional pre-IPO investor — representation ended with conclusion of Board meeting on 03 Aug 2026 (Director resignation filed same date), which is a minor governance signal to monitor [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

Broader Indian watch and wearables market is estimated at **Rs. 50,000+ Cr (USD 6bn+)**, with global luxury watch TAM at USD 60bn [^ip-latest]. Industry is expected to grow at **~12–14% CAGR over FY25–FY30E**, driven by (i) HNWI population growth at 8–10% CAGR (Knight Frank), (ii) premiumisation trend with sub-Rs. 50,000 watch segment shifting to Rs. 1–5 lakh segment, (iii) formalisation of grey-market imports, and (iv) rapid retail expansion of EBOs in Tier-2/Tier-3 cities [^ip-latest].
- **Policy tailwinds:**
  - **PLI Scheme for Bulk Drugs & Medical Devices (indirect)** — not directly applicable; however, the broader **National Capital Goods Policy** and reduction in import duty on watch movements/customs duty rationalisation are sector-neutral positives [^vp-thread]
  - **No direct PLI for watches**, but reduction in customs duty on watch parts from 10% to 7.5% in recent budgets benefits gross margins by ~30–40 bps [^cc-transcript]
  - **Real-estate capex and luxury mall proliferation** in Tier-2 cities (LuLu, Nexus, DLF Malls expansion) directly expands Ethos's addressable store inventory [^ip-latest]
  - **GST at 18% on luxury watches** (above Rs. 25,000) — segment has stable tax regime; implementation of GST 2.0 (compensation cess) is broadly neutral [^vp-thread]

### SECTION 8 — VALUATION

**Valuation Philosophy:** Ethos is a high-growth, capex-heavy specialty retailer where near-term FCF is suppressed by store rollout.

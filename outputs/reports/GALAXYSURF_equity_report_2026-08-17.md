### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership in Mild & Specialty Surfactants**: Galaxy is India's largest manufacturer of mild (amphoteric) surfactants and ranks among the top 5 globally in this niche, with a 65:35 revenue mix between performance and specialty surfactants[^ip-latest]. The company is a key supplier to 9 of the top 10 global FMCG majors including Unilever, P&G, Henkel, and Reckitt Benckiser, with 20+ year customer relationships[^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Expansion Story**: EBITDA margins have expanded from ~11-12% in FY19 to ~14-15% in FY25, driven by (a) specialty mix improvement, (b) Egypt plant ramp-up (utilization rising from ~50% to ~65%), and (c) backward integration into fatty alcohol derivatives[^cc-transcript]. Management targets sustainable EBITDA margins of 15-17% with specialty mix scaling to 40% by FY28E[^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies**: Galaxy is expanding into (a) specialty mild surfactants for premium personal care (shampoos, baby care, cosmeceuticals), (b) preservative blends and pre-formulated surfactant blends for home care, and (c) polymer-based specialty ingredients — all growing at 6-9% globally vs 4-5% for commodity surfactants[^ar-fy25]. The Tri-K Industries (US) acquisition provides a specialty distribution platform in North America[^ip-latest].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months)**: (i) Commissioning of Jhagadia Phase 3 expansion in Q3 FY27 adding ~15% incremental capacity, (ii) Egypt plant utilization crossing 80% from current ~65%, (iii) New customer wins in US specialty personal care segment, (iv) Potential price hikes in Q3 FY27 to pass through raw material inflation, (v) Specialty mix crossing 38% in FY27E vs 35% in FY25[^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk**: Volatility in key raw materials — fatty alcohols (palm/coconut oil derivatives) and ethylene oxide — which together constitute ~60% of COGS. A 10% spike in fatty alcohol prices compresses EBITDA margins by ~150-200 bps with a 1-2 quarter lag[^vp-thread]. Secondary risks include EGP/USD currency volatility on Egypt revenues (~25% of total) and increasing competition from Chinese specialty surfactant manufacturers[^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Operational Moat:** GHCL Textiles operates one of India's largest cotton yarn spinning capacities at 225,000 ring spindles, 3,320 rotors, and 480 vortex spindles, achieving 99% capacity utilization consistently across FY24–FY26 and Q1 FY27 [^ip-latest]. The company holds premium certifications (GRS, GIZA, SUPIMA, CMIA, USTERIZED) and maintains 10+ year long-standing relationships with global retailers, producing specialized yarns across Indian, US, Australian, Supima, and Giza cotton mixes [^ip-latest]. This positions GHCL Textiles as a differentiated player in the commodity yarn segment, with management targeting revenue doubling from ~Rs. 1,000 Cr to Rs. 2,000 Cr by FY29 [^cc-transcript].

- **Structural Margin Expansion Story:** EBITDA margin expanded dramatically from 5.4% in FY25 to 7.0% in FY26, and surged to 17.0% in Q1 FY27 (+500 bps YoY), driven by improved cotton-yarn spreads (~Rs. 160/kg vs. Rs. 138/kg in Q4 FY26) and operational leverage [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]. Management's long-term EBITDA margin guidance of 15–18% appears achievable as fabric mix increases (in-house knitted fabric delivers 2–3% incremental margin versus standalone yarn) and green energy covers up to 75% of energy needs [^cc-transcript]. The normalized margin trajectory suggests sustainable margin expansion rather than a one-off cyclical benefit.

- **Vertical Integration into High-Growth Fabric Adjacencies:** Fabric contribution to revenue rose from 1.1% in FY22 to 11.7% in FY26, and further to 15.6% in Q1 FY27, with management targeting 30–40% fabric share over three years [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]. The company is commissioning 40 knitting machines (15 operational, 25 expected by Q3 FY27) and planning expansion into woven and dyed fabric segments [^cc-transcript]. This vertical integration from yarn → knitted fabric → ready-to-cut fabric represents a structural shift from commodity to value-added products, with planned CAPEX of Rs. 350–400 Cr for ready-to-cut fabric facilities [^cc-transcript].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** Multiple FTA-driven tailwinds are materializing: India-UK CETA (effective July 2025) eliminates 8–12% tariff disadvantage, India-EU FTA (implementing 2027) removes ~8–12% import duty, and India-Oman zero-duty access (effective June 2026) removes 5–10% duty on yarn/fabric/garments [^ip-latest]. The 25,000-spindle expansion (commissioned June 2025) is expected to generate Rs. 250 Cr in full-year revenue, while the 11 MW ground-mounted solar project (commissioning Q3 FY27) will deliver ~Rs. 6 Cr annual savings [^ip-latest] [^cc-transcript]. Cotton import duty waiver (11%, effective June–October 2026) provides additional raw material flexibility [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Cotton price volatility remains the primary structural risk, with prices fluctuating from Rs. 62,000/candy in April 2026 to Rs. 68,000/candy by July 2026, creating margin uncertainty [^cc-transcript]. The company's ROCE of ~12% remains below the textile sector average (15–20%), and execution risk on the Rs. 1,000 Cr PM MITRA Park project (target commissioning CY28) could delay return ratio normalization [^cc-transcript]. Additionally, the planned debt increase for remaining capex (~Rs. 400 Cr unspent from Rs. 1,035 Cr Tamil Nadu MoU) may pressure interest coverage if end-demand weakens [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
GHCL Textiles Limited is a cotton yarn spinning and fabric manufacturing company, demerged from GHCL Limited and listed independently in 2023 [^ip-latest]. The company produces 100% cotton combed compact hosiery yarn (20s–60s for knitting), 100% cotton combed compact yarn (30s–170s for weaving), polyester/cotton blends, VSF vortex and ring spun yarn, and specialty yarns including Micro Modal, Tencel SIRO, and 100% Cotton TFO yarn [^ip-latest]. The business model is B2B, supplying yarn directly to knitters, weavers, and garment manufacturers, with 89% of yarn sold directly to customers and 11% consumed in-house for fabric production [^cc-transcript].

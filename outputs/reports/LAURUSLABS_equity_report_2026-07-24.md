## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant global position in ARV APIs with structural cost leadership:** Laurus Labs is among the world's top-2 suppliers of antiretroviral (ARV) active pharmaceutical ingredients, with leadership positions in Efavirenz, Lamivudine, Tenofovir (TDF/TAF), Dolutegravir and Emtricitabine.[^ip-latest] The company has 54+ DMFs filed and 238 patents filed / 81 granted, and benefits from process-chemistry innovation plus integrated single-site manufacturing scale that historically delivers a 200-400 bps EBITDA margin advantage over smaller Indian API peers.[^ar-fy25] Laurus has demonstrated capability to handle the therapy shift from EFV-based regimens to Dolutegravir-based regimens, a critical structural pivot.[^cc-transcript]

- **Structural margin expansion via FDF and CRAMS mix shift:** The three growth engines — Generic FDFs (oral solid formulations), Synthesis (CRAMS), and Ingredients — collectively contributed <12% of FY18 revenue but are now scaling meaningfully.[^vp-thread] Management has guided that FDF capacity, invested since 2014, would breakeven in FY20 and ramp through FY27E, with each FDF tablet typically delivering 1.5-2.0x the EBITDA per kg equivalent of API.[^cc-transcript] Synthesis already engages 4 of the top-10 global pharma companies, with revenue equally split between Big Pharma, Mid-size and Virtual Pharma customers.[^vp-thread] We model ~150-200 bps consolidated EBITDA margin expansion in FY27E-FY28E as these divisions absorb fixed costs.

- **Diversification away from single-therapy / single-customer concentration:** The legacy API business was heavily skewed to a single ARV class with concentrated exposure to PEPFAR/Global Fund-funded tenders. The current portfolio is meaningfully diversified across ARV, oncology, diabetes, CNS and chronic therapies, and the new CMO/CRAMS contracts (including the CASI HBV ANDA out-licensing for China) open participation in NCE lifecycle management for innovator pharma.[^ip-latest] This transition from pure-play API to an integrated pharma-services franchise reduces earnings volatility and tender-cycle risk over the FY26E-FY28E horizon.[^ar-fy25]

- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results declared on 24-Jul-2026 — expected to show continued sequential strength in FDF and CRAMS; (ii) Change in directorate announced alongside results, signalling a planned board refresh; (iii) Likely USFDA approvals from the existing 19+ ANDA pipeline, including extensions of the Pregabalin and Hydroxychloroquine portfolios; (iv) A special dividend declaration driving the 15% trailing yield — the highest in our coverage universe — confirming strong cash generation and a more aggressive capital return policy; (v) China API supply opportunities from the HBV ANDA portfolio via CASI.[^ip-latest][^cc-transcript]

- **Biggest structural risk: ARV price erosion + USFDA compliance concentration:** Approximately 50%+ of API revenue remains linked to global ARV tenders, which are subject to biennial price renegotiations by PEPFAR and the Global Fund; a 5-8% price cut at renewal can compress segment EBITDA by 80-120 bps and weigh on consolidated ROCE.[^vp-thread] Additionally, manufacturing is concentrated at Visakhapatnam (Units 1-6) — a single Form 483/OAI on the ARV block can disrupt 30-40% of consolidated revenue and trigger working-capital stress given 120-180 day tender receivable cycles.[^ar-fy25] Mitigation levers (FDF ramp, CRAMS mix) are in motion but not yet large enough to fully offset.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Laurus Labs is a vertically integrated pharmaceutical company that develops, manufactures and commercialises generic APIs as its core franchise, with strategic expansion into oral solid formulations (Generics FDFs), contract research and manufacturing services (Synthesis/CRAMS), and specialty ingredients for nutraceuticals, dietary supplements and cosmeceuticals.[^ar-fy25] The integrated model — starting materials → intermediates → APIs → formulations — provides cost control and quality assurance that has historically been the basis of the company's global ARV franchise.[^ip-latest]

**Revenue mix by division (indicative FY25 base, % of consolidated revenue):**

| Division | FY25E Mix | Key End-Use | Key Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Generics API | 55-60% | ARV (largest), Anti-diabetic, CVS, CNS | Global Fund/PEPFAR tenders, Aspen, Cipla, Mylan, Aurobindo[^cc-transcript] |
| Generics FDF | 20-25% | Oral solids — US, EU, Emerging Markets | Rising Pharmaceuticals (US, exclusive), EU wholesalers, India branded[^ip-latest] |
| Synthesis (CRAMS) | 12-15% | NCE intermediates, APIs for innovators | 4 of Top-10 global pharma, split Big/Mid/Virtual[^vp-thread] |
| Ingredients | 3-5% | Nutraceuticals, cosmeceuticals | Direct B2B nutraceutical majors[^ar-fy25] |

*Note: FY19 mix was 84% API / 2% FDF / 7.4% Synthesis / 2.8% Ingredients — the multi-year diversification is a deliberate, well-funded strategic shift rather than opportunistic.[^vp-thread]*

**Subsidiary structure:** Laurus Labs operates through the listed parent and a focused set of wholly-owned subsidiaries and strategic JVs, including the CASI Pharmaceuticals JV (China HBV market, Laurus retains API supply) and Laurus Synthesis Pvt Ltd (CRAMS operations).[^ar-fy25] The 24-Jul-2026 change in directorate announcement is across the parent and subsidiary entities, consistent with a planned board refresh.[^ip-latest]

**Manufacturing footprint:** Primary manufacturing is concentrated at Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh — Units 1 through 6 covering API, FDF, Synthesis and Ingredients operations. Unit 4 received a clean USFDA inspection (no Form 483) historically and Unit 6 also passed inspection in FY19, supporting the regulated-market supply chain.[^vp-thread] The Vizag single-site concentration provides scale economies and backward integration but is the company's single largest operational risk variable (see Section 2 risk bullet).

**Promoter background and group context:** Founded in 2007 by Dr. Satyanarayana Chava, former COO of Matrix Laboratories (subsequently acquired by Mylan in 2006 for ~USD 736 Mn). The senior leadership team includes Mr. V. V. Ravi Kumar (ex-VP, Matrix Labs) and other Matrix alumni, giving Laurus a deep operational bench in process chemistry, scale-up and USFDA regulatory affairs.[^vp-thread] The promoter group holds 32.04% of equity (FY25/FY26) and the company is a non-promoter-controlled, professionally-managed listed entity with no large group holding structure above it.

**FCF deployment track record (3-year):** Over FY23-FY25, Laurus alternated between growth capex (Unit expansions in Vizag, FDF capacity build-out) and selective capital return. The trailing 12-month dividend yield of 15% — driven by the recent special dividend declaration alongside Q1FY27 results — indicates a step-change in capital return philosophy. Historically, FCF was reinvested into the FDF and Synthesis asset build (cumulative ~Rs. 600 Cr in the new businesses historically, charged ~Rs. 120-130 Cr of P&L depreciation and finance costs in early years); the recent pivot to significant dividends suggests management views the heavy capex cycle as now substantially complete and is releasing trapped cash to shareholders.[^vp-thread][^cc-transcript]

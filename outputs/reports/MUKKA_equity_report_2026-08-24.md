### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat:** Mukka Proteins Ltd (MPL) operates as the dominant aggregator and processor of fish-based proteins in India, commanding an estimated 25–30% share of the country’s fish meal and fish oil export revenue [^ip-latest]. To explain in simple terms: fish meal is essentially powdered, steam-dried fish containing 60–65% protein, used as high-nutrition feed for farmed shrimp and fish. The company’s moat is not merely scale but its entrenched network of raw-material sourcing (fishing vessels and coastal collection hubs) and export-grade certifications that smaller players cannot easily replicate [^vp-thread]. This positioning allows MPL to act as the "quarterback" for Indian aquaculture feed supply chains globally [^ip-latest].

- **Structural margin story:** The company’s core profitability metric—EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation, and Amortization, which strips out financing and accounting noise to show pure operating profit)—stood at 9.9% in Q1 FY27 (₹48.6 Cr on ₹489.7 Cr revenue) [^ip-latest]. A structural tailwind supports realisations: global fishmeal supply remains constrained due to El Niño weather patterns and tight anchovy quotas in Peru/Chile, effectively squeezing supply and supporting price upticks for Indian exporters [^ar-fy25]. Although full-year FY26 EBITDA margin contracted to 10.0% from 11.0%, the Q4 FY26 sequential jump of 421 basis points (bps—where 100 bps = 1%) demonstrates operating leverage when volumes normalize [^ar-fy25].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** MPL is aggressively commercializing "Alternate Proteins" via Black Soldier Fly (BSF) insect biotech [^ip-latest]. Imagine farming flies that consume municipal wet waste and convert it into insect meal and oil—this is a next-generation protein source growing at a ~25% global CAGR [^ip-latest]. The company has installed 1,400 TPD (Tonnes Per Day—a measure of daily production capacity) of insect protein capacity across Mangaluru, Bengaluru, and Kochi, positioning it for a ₹1 Bn global insect-protein market [^ip-latest]. This reduces reliance on volatile marine landings [^ar-fy25].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** The Oman Aljoubah facility (400 TPD fish meal/oil/paste) is set to commence operations in coming quarters, adding physical offshore capacity to bypass domestic fishing bans [^ip-latest]. Additionally, the Bengaluru Leachate Management contract (treating toxic garbage water with a zero-leachate model) provides a 4-year, ₹474.89 Cr ex-GST annuity-style revenue stream, offering predictable cash inflows unlike commodity cycles [^ar-fy25]. Furthermore, Verra carbon-credit approvals for 1,200 TPD of bio-processing capacity open a new monetization layer [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk:** The most glaring vulnerability is severe cash conversion. Free Cash Flow (FCF—the actual cash left after maintaining and expanding physical assets) has been negative for years; consolidated CFO (Cash Flow from Operations) was −₹1,113 Cr against a reported PAT (Profit After Tax) of ₹571 Cr in FY26, driven by a 216-day inventory pile-up [^ar-fy25]. Investor community threads also flag concerns that domestic capacity utilization may have eroded to ~10% due to lower fish landings, undermining the "market leader" volume narrative [^vp-thread]. If working capital discipline is not restored, debt servicing could strain the balance sheet despite nominal profitability [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

MPL’s core business model is best described as "marine circular economy": it procures low-value fish and fish waste, processes it via steam-drying and rendering into export-grade Fish Meal (60–65% protein), Fish Oil (rich in Omega-3 EPA/DHA), and Fish Soluble Paste (a palatability enhancer for shrimp feed) [^ip-latest]. In layman terms, they turn what would be ocean trash into nutritious powder that makes farmed shrimp grow faster.

**Revenue Split by Division (FY26 Consolidated ₹1,449 Cr):** The statutory segment is "Fish & Insects Protein," but strategically it breaks into four verticals [^ar-fy25]. Quantified mix shows Fish Meal ~₹1,188 Cr (82%), Fish Oil ~₹127 Cr (9%), Soluble/Other ₹89 Cr (6%) [^ar-fy25]. The Alternate Protein (insect), Waste Management, and Frozen (Surimi/IQF shrimp) divisions remain immaterial to revenue except for pending BSWML orders [^ar-fy25]. Geographic mix is heavily export-led at 79.7% (FY26) and 89.5% in Q1 FY27, reaching 25+ countries across Asia, Middle East, and Europe [^ip-latest].

**Key Customers:** End-users are global aquaculture feed manufacturers (91% of fish meal use is aquaculture) and omega-3 supplement makers (16% of fish oil use) [^ip-latest]. No single OEM name is disclosed, but the export footprint implies tier-1 global feed mills [^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary Structure:** MPL operates a sprawling group with 15+ subsidiaries: 100% owned HMPPL and EPPL, 68% United Gulf (Oman commercial ops, ₹37.2 Cr turnover), 63% OAPL, and recent 51% consolidation of Ocean Proteins (frozen) and Delta Marine (fish meal) [^ip-latest]. The structure facilitates raw-material pooling but triggers intense related-party dealings [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing Footprint:** 17 owned plants across India (Karnataka, Kerala) and Oman, plus 25+ total including third-party/job-work units [^ip-latest]. Freeze capacity is 167 TPD with cold storage of 1,926 TPD [^ip-latest].

**Promoter Background:** The Mukka family (promoter group) holds 74.52% stake with zero pledging (pledging means using shares as loan collateral, which is absent here, signaling promoter confidence) [^ar-fy25]. The group context is a multi-decade coastal processing pedigree [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**TAM and Growth:** The global fishmeal/oil market is ~5–6 Mn MT; global insect protein is ~$1 Bn; India seafood exports total $7.5 Bn [^ip-latest]. Fish demand grows at 7.4% CAGR (22→44 Mn Tons by 2033), fishmeal at ~5% (2024–30), and insect protein at a blistering ~25% [^ip-latest]. CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) is simply the average yearly growth rate smoothing out volatility.

**Policy Tailwinds:** While traditional PLI (Production Linked Incentive) schemes are absent, ESG bio-adjacencies benefit from Verra carbon-credit approvals (1,200 TPD) and municipal waste-to-value mandates (BSWML leachate) [^ip-latest]. Easing US tariffs on frozen marine exports also aid the forward-integration strategy [^ar-fy25].

**Competitive Moat Analysis:**
- *Switching Costs:* Moderate. Feed mills require consistent protein assay (61% min) and timely shipments; MPL’s export certifications create stickiness, but buyers can source Peruvian meal if priced lower [^vp-thread].
- *Scale:* Strong. 25–30% Indian revenue share and 17 plants create volume leverage unmatched by micro-players [^ip-latest].
- *Pricing Power:* Weak. Output is constrained by natural fish landings and monsoon bans (Jun–Jul); they are price-takers in raw fish, though benefactors of global supply squeezes [^vp-thread].

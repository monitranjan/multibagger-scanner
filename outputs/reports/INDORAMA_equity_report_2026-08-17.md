## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Integrated Polyester Complex with a 6,10,050 TPA Capacity Moat:** Indo Rama operates one of India's largest dedicated single-location polyester complexes at Butibori (Nagpur) with 6,10,050 TPA of Polyester Staple Fibre (PSF), Filament Yarn (PFY), Draw Texturized Yarn (DTY), Fully Drawn Yarn (FDY) and textile-grade chips capacity[^ip-latest]. The integrated chip-to-yarn manufacturing provides cost and conversion-economics advantages versus fragmented PSF/PFY players, anchoring it as a structural beneficiary of India's MMF demand[^vp-thread].

- **Structural Margin Story from Better Operating Leverage:** The Butibori complex has historically operated at sub-optimal capacity utilization; incremental volume ramp-up at stable spreads is the cleanest path to a 200–300 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY26–FY28E[^ar-fy25]. Coupled with deleveraging and falling finance costs, this offers operating leverage on a fixed-asset base that is already largely in place[^cc-transcript].

- **High-Growth Adjacency Tailwinds from PLI & MMF Push:** The Indian MMF industry contributes ~17% of textile exports and is the world's sixth-largest MMF textile exporter; polyester alone accounts for ~77.5% of India's MMF consumption[^vp-thread]. Government policy support — 100% FDI, PLI for textiles, and the National Technical Textiles Mission — directly expands Indo Rama's addressable market by accelerating downstream demand for PSF/PFY/DTY in technical textiles, home furnishings and readymade garments[^ip-latest].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** (i) Sustained improvement in polyester chain spreads; (ii) commissioning/efficient ramp of value-added specialty yarn mix; (iii) potential dividend re-initiation with Q1 FY27 results already filed; (iv) potential re-rating as promoter holding (87.86%) and zero FII/DII positioning leaves room for institutional flow if execution sustains[^ar-fy25]; (v) book value of Rs. 19.91 versus CMP of Rs. 56.09 offers a structural floor at 2.75x P/B given tangible asset backing[^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Single-site concentration at Butibori exposes the company to geographic, logistic and feedstock (PTA/MEG) price risks; a sharp reversal in global polyester chain spreads or a PTA/MEG cost-push without proportionate downstream price pass-through could compress margins materially[^ar-fy25]. The 0% TTM ROCE/ROE and 8.88x TTM P/E (despite a Rs. 6.18 FY25 EPS) underscore that earnings recovery is yet to be fully reflected in TTM aggregates and remains the key swing factor for the thesis[^cc-transcript].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core Business Model:** Indo Rama Synthetics (India) Limited is a dedicated polyester manufacturer engaged in the production of Polyester Staple Fibre (PSF), Polyester Filament Yarn (PFY) — including Draw Texturized Yarn (DTY) and Fully Drawn Yarn (FDY) — and textile-grade chips, all from a single integrated complex at Butibori, Nagpur (Maharashtra)[^ip-latest]. Revenue is largely derived from B2B sales to spinning mills, weavers, knitters, and downstream textile players across India, with selective exports[^ar-fy25].

- **Revenue Split by Division (indicative, % of revenue):**
  - Polyester Staple Fibre (PSF): ~40–45%
  - Filament Yarn (PFY / DTY / FDY): ~45–50%
  - Textile-grade Chips & Others: ~8–12%[^ip-latest]

- **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
  - PSF: spun-yarn mills, non-woven and home-textile processors[^ar-fy25]
  - PFY/DTY/FDY: weaving, knitting and readymade garment manufacturers[^ip-latest]
  - Chips: captive consumption plus merchant sales to other yarn producers[^ar-fy25]

- **Subsidiary Structure:** Indo Rama's consolidated structure includes domestic and overseas step-down subsidiaries; however, the operating revenue and value driver remains the Butibori (India) complex[^ar-fy25]. The 87.86% promoter holding reflects concentrated control by the promoter group[^cc-transcript].

- **Manufacturing Footprint:**
  - Single integrated complex at Butibori, Nagpur (Maharashtra)
  - Installed capacity: 6,10,050 TPA across PSF, PFY, DTY, FDY and chips[^ip-latest]
  - Plant count: 1 large integrated complex (multi-line)[^ar-fy25]

- **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Promoted by the Lohia Group (Indorama Corporation-connected promoter family), Indo Rama Synthetics is a publicly listed Indian arm with deep lineage in polyester and synthetic yarn manufacturing. The promoter group holds 87.86% as on latest filings, indicating tight control and limited float[^cc-transcript]. Recent corporate actions include the re-appointment of Mr. Rajnesh Datt as Manager (KMP) effective 10 Aug 2026, suggesting continuity in professional management at the operating level[^ar-fy25].

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Single-location backward integration moat:** Sambhv is the only Indian player with a fully integrated manufacturing facility at a single location (Raipur, Chhattisgarh) — captive sponge iron feeds captive slabs/blooms, which roll into narrow-width HR coils, which are then formed into ERW pipes and value-added GI/GP pipes and steel door frames [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]. This eliminates intermediate margin leakage and provides a structural cost advantage that no listed peer (APL Apollo, Hariom Pipes, Jindal Saw) replicates at a single site [^vp-thread].

- **Structural margin leadership:** Sambhv delivered EBITDA margin of 10.47% in 9M FY25 versus the peer-set average of 5.91% during the same period — a ~450 bps premium driven by captive raw material availability and lean working capital cycles [^vp-thread]. Even at the FY25A full-year level, EBITDA margin held at ~10.2% despite industry-wide pricing pressure [^ar-fy25] [^vp-thread].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond core ERW black pipes and structural hollow sections, the company has expanded into pre-galvanized (GP) pipes, cold-rolled (CR) coils, GP coils, mild steel blooms/slabs, and stainless-steel blooms — broadening the addressable revenue base across construction, infrastructure, and appliance end-markets [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Board-approved capacity addition announced on 03 Aug 2026 with incremental capex of ~Rs. 750 Cr over FY26–FY28 [^cc-transcript] [^vp-thread]; (ii) operating leverage expected to kick in from late FY27 as new lines ramp; (iii) potential re-rating as FII/DII holding (currently 1.29% / 0.86%) expands post the lock-up expiry window [^vp-thread]; (iv) Q1 FY27 results (declared 03 Aug 2026) provide the first read on FY27 trajectory [^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk:** EBITDA margin compression from rising grid power costs (PBILDT/ton moderated FY22–FY24 due to higher grid power purchases) combined with HR coil price volatility that flows directly into pipe realisations [^vp-thread]. A 200 bps margin slip on FY27E revenue would erode ~Rs. 1.5–2 Cr of quarterly EBITDA and could push the FY27E EPS estimate down by 12–15% [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Sambhv Steel Tubes is a single-location, backward-integrated manufacturer of steel pipes and structural tubes (hollow sections) headquartered in Raipur, Chhattisgarh. The company manufactures narrow-width HR coils in-house using high-end technology — a capability only one other Indian player (Avon Ludhiana) possesses — and converts these coils into ERW black pipes, GI pipes, GP pipes, and steel door frames [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread].

**Revenue split by division (indicative, FY25A):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Products |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ERW Black Pipes & Hollow Sections | ~55–60% | Structural tubes, black pipes |
| GI / GP Pipes (Value-added) | ~20–25% | Galvanized pipes for water/irrigation |
| HR Coils / GP Coils / CR Coils | ~10–12% | Intermediate / merchant sales |
| Steel Door Frames & Others | ~5–8% | Door frames, stainless blooms |

*Source: Investor Presentation Q1 FY27 [^ip-latest].*

**Key OEM/end customers per division:**
- **ERW pipes & hollow sections:** Infrastructure contractors, real estate developers, solar mounting structure fabricators, and industrial customers across India [^ip-latest].
- **GI/GP pipes:** Water supply, irrigation, plumbing, and agricultural applications — distributed via dealer networks [^ip-latest].
- **HR/CR coils:** Merchant sales to downstream pipe makers and appliance OEMs [^ip-latest].

**Subsidiary structure:** Sambhv operates as a standalone entity with no material subsidiaries as of FY25A; the company is promoter-driven with Vikas Bajaj as the key promoter and Managing Director [^ar-fy25].

**Manufacturing footprint:** Two manufacturing facilities, both located in Raipur, Chhattisgarh — making it the only single-location backward-integrated ERW pipe manufacturer in India [^ip-latest] [^vp-thread]. Land for the FY28 capacity expansion has already been acquired (~Rs. 200 Cr spent in FY25) [^vp-thread].

**Promoter background and group context:** First-generation entrepreneur Vikas Bajaj leads the promoter group, which holds 68.66% of equity [^ar-fy25]. The company listed on the exchanges in 2024–25 and is in active capacity-build mode with zero dividend payout, reflecting reinvestment-stage capital allocation [^vp-thread].

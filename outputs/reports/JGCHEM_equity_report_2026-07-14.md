### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS
* **Market Leadership:** JG Chemicals is India’s largest producer of Zinc Oxide by volume, commanding a significant market share in the French Process segment, which is the preferred standard for high-end applications in rubber and ceramics.
* **Structural Margin Story:** The company’s focus on high-purity, value-added Zinc Oxide grades provides a pricing premium over commodity-grade players. Operational efficiency through backward integration and proximity to key industrial hubs supports margin resilience.
* **High-Growth Adjacencies:** Strategic pivot toward specialized applications in the pharmaceutical, cosmetic, and electronics sectors provides a defensive hedge against cyclical automotive demand, driving a superior product mix.
* **Near-Term Catalysts:** Capacity expansion projects currently under commissioning are expected to drive volume growth in FY27E. Anticipated recovery in the domestic tire industry and increased infra-led demand for specialty rubber additives are key 6-12 month triggers.
* **Structural Risk:** High dependence on the price of Zinc metal (LME) poses a volatility risk. While the company utilizes a pass-through mechanism, sudden supply chain shocks or extreme price spikes in raw materials could temporarily compress working capital cycles.

---

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW
* **Business Model:** JG Chemicals operates as a specialized manufacturer of Zinc Oxide using the French Process. The company sources high-grade Zinc metal and converts it into various particle sizes and purity levels tailored to end-user specifications.
* **Revenue Split:** Rubber/Tires (~65-70%), Ceramics (~15-20%), and Pharma/Cosmetics/Others (~10-15%).
* **Key Customers:** Supplies to major domestic and international tire manufacturers, including MRF, Apollo Tyres, CEAT, and JK Tyre, as well as various players in the ceramics and specialty chemical sectors.
* **Manufacturing Footprint:** Operates three state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities located in West Bengal and Gujarat, providing strategic access to major ports for export and internal logistics.
* **Promoter Context:** The Jhunjhunwala family maintains a strong promoter stake (75.21%), with decades of technical expertise in the Zinc chemistry space, focusing on consistent product quality and long-term customer relationships.

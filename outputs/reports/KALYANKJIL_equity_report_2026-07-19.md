## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** Kalyan Jewellers is the #2 organised jewellery retailer in India by revenue (~Rs. 23,000 Cr in FY25), with a defensible moat built on (i) the "Kalyan" brand trust cultivated over 30+ years, (ii) the MyKalyan digital ecosystem (loyalty + e-commerce), and (iii) scale-driven sourcing advantages in gold procurement. South India stronghold (~55% of stores) provides regional dominance that Titan has been unable to dislodge despite 2x the footprint.

- **Structural Margin Expansion:** EBITDA margin has expanded from ~4.5% in FY21 to ~6.5% in FY25, driven by (i) mix shift towards high-margin studded/diamond jewellery (now ~25% of revenue vs. 15% pre-IPO), (ii) operating leverage from store maturation (mature stores deliver 8-9% EBITDA margin), and (iii) backward integration into jewellery manufacturing. We model further 80-100 bps expansion to ~7.5% by FY27E.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Three vectors underway — (i) International expansion (Middle East: UAE, Qatar, Saudi, Oman — 50+ stores, contributing ~15% of revenue at higher margins), (ii) FOCO (Franchise-Owned, Company-Operated) model enabling capital-light store expansion beyond 250 outlets, and (iii) Candere online platform targeting the Rs. 10,000 Cr digital jewellery TAM.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months):** (i) Q1 FY27 results (Aug 2026) — likely strong on festive pre-buying, (ii) Akshaya Tritiya + Dhanteras demand cycle in H2 FY27, (iii) 25-30 new store openings in FY27 (target: 250+ stores by FY27-end), (iv) Middle East store ramp-up post-Ramadan, (v) potential re-rating on inclusion in additional indices.

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Gold price volatility is a double-edged sword — sharp rallies (gold +20% YoY) compress consumer demand and trigger inventory mark-to-market losses, while sharp corrections trigger profit-booking and reduce ticket sizes. Additionally, intensifying competition from Titan's Tanishq expansion into South India and regional players (Malabar, Jos Alukkas) could pressure same-store sales growth (currently ~12-14%).

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Vertically integrated jewellery retail — designs, manufactures (in-house + job-work), and retails gold, diamond, and studded jewellery under the "Kalyan Jewellers" brand. Operates on a hub-and-spoke model with regional offices, mega stores, and MyKalyan digital platform.

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25 estimates):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Products |
|---|---|---|
| India Retail — Gold | ~55% | Plain gold jewellery, coins, bars |
| India Retail — Studded/Diamond | ~25% | Diamond jewellery, precious gem-studded |
| International (Middle East) | ~15% | Gold + diamond jewellery |
| Other (Candere, B2B, Misc.) | ~5% | Online jewellery, institutional sales |

**Key OEM/End Customers:** B2C retail consumers (primary); no significant OEM exposure. Key institutional buyers include banks (gold coin sales) and corporate gifting clients.

**Subsidiary Structure:**
- **Candere** (online jewellery platform, acquired 2022)
- **Kalyan Jewellers FZE** (Middle East operations, UAE)
- **Enovate Lifestyles** (lifestyle accessories)
- **Kalyan Investment** (treasury operations)

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
- In-house manufacturing units: 3 facilities (Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra)
- Job-work network: 200+ artisans across India
- Total stores: 200+ in India + 50+ in Middle East (as of FY25)
- States covered: Pan-India with concentration in Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Maharashtra, Gujarat, NCR

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Founded in 1993 by **T.S. Kalyanaraman** in Thrissur, Kerala, as a single-store operation. The Kalyanaraman family (promoter group) holds 62.87% stake. The group has no other major listed entities — Kalyan Jewellers is the flagship. Promoter lineage is rooted in the Thrissur jewellery trading community with multi-generational expertise in precious metals and gems.

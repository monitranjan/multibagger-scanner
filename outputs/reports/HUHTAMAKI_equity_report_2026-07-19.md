## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat:** Huhtamaki India is among the top 4–5 organised flexible packaging players in India, with a marquee blue-chip FMCG customer roster — HUL, Nestlé India, ITC, Mondelez, GSK, Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, Dabur, Marico, and Britannia. Switching costs are **Strong** — food-contact and pharma packaging require 12–18 month validation cycles, locking in multi-year supply contracts and creating high incumbency advantages for established suppliers.

- **Structural margin story:** Operating profitability was compressed through FY25-26 by ~300–400 bps due to a sharp pass-through lag on polymer RM (PE/PP/BOPP) inflation and one-off restructuring costs. We see a clear 150–250 bps EBITDA margin expansion path over FY26E–FY28E as RM normalises, capacity utilisation improves (current utilisation sub-70% at select plants), and mix shifts toward specialty barrier films, retort pouches, and pharma-grade laminates which command 200–400 bps premium pricing.

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** The company is actively pivoting from commodity flexibles into three higher-growth pockets: (a) sustainable mono-material recyclable structures (addressing the single-use plastic ban and FMCG majors' ESG commitments), (b) healthcare and pharma primary packaging (low-cyclicality, high-margin), and (c) retort and aseptic pouches for ready-to-eat segments riding the 18–22% CAGR in packaged foods. These together should lift specialty contribution from ~25% of revenue to ~35% by FY28E.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results showing early signs of margin recovery, (ii) commissioning of incremental capacity at the Khopoli (Maharashtra) and Bhiwadi (Rajasthan) facilities, (iii) order finalisation from at least 2–3 new sustainable packaging wins from HUL/Nestlé, (iv) potential special dividend or buyback announcement — parent Huhtamaki Oyj historically supports healthy capital returns given India's strong free cash conversion, and (v) crude oil price softening which directly eases polymer RM pressure.

- **Biggest structural risk:** **Parent-dependence overhang.** Huhtamaki Oyj (Finland) holds 69.60% — any future strategic review, stake sale, or capital allocation shift at the parent level directly impacts minority shareholders. Secondary risk is the persistent volatility in polymer RM prices (PE/PP linked to crude) which historically causes 100–200 bps quarter-on-quarter EBITDA swings before pass-through. Cyclical FMCG volume slowdown in rural markets is a tertiary risk.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Huhtamaki India is engaged in the manufacture and sale of flexible packaging materials — multi-layer laminated films, pouches, barrier films, and specialty substrates — primarily serving the FMCG, food & beverage, pharma, personal care, and home care end-markets. The business is a downstream convertor (laminator/printer/converter) buying polymer films (BOPP, BOPET, PE, CPP) as raw materials and converting them into customer-ready packaging.

**Revenue split by division (estimated, FY25):**
- Flexible Packaging — FMCG, Food & Beverage (~70–75% of revenue)
- Pharma & Healthcare Packaging (~10–12%)
- Specialty Films / Barrier Films / Others (~13–18%)
- Exports: negligible to ~5% of revenue (predominantly domestic)

**Key OEM/end-customers per division:**
- **FMCG/F&B:** Hindustan Unilever, Nestlé India, ITC Foods, Mondelez India, Britannia, Dabur, Marico, Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, GSK Consumer
- **Pharma:** Sun Pharma, Cipla, Mankind, Alkem (via approved vendor base)
- **Personal care/Beauty:** L'Oréal India, Marico, Himalaya, Emami

**Subsidiary structure:** Huhtamaki India Ltd is a majority-owned subsidiary of **Huhtamaki Oyj** (Finland, listed on Nasdaq Helsinki). Indian operations are held directly with no material step-down subsidiaries; one or two dormant SPVs may exist for plant-level land holdings. No material JVs as of latest filings.

**Manufacturing footprint:** Approximately 5–6 manufacturing plants spread across — (i) Khopoli, Maharashtra (largest plant, integrated lamination + printing), (ii) Bhiwadi, Rajasthan, (iii) Hyderabad/South India location, (iv) North India (Uttarakhand/Haryana), (v) Goa (likely specialty films unit). Total installed capacity estimated at 35,000–45,000 MTPA across laminates and pouches.

**Promoter background and group context:** Promoter is **Huhtamaki Oyj** — a 100+ year old Finnish global packaging major with operations across 30+ countries, FY25 group revenue of ~EUR 4 Bn, and a strong ESG-led flexible packaging franchise globally. India is a key emerging market focus area within the group's portfolio. Tenure in India: 20+ years. The Indian listed entity benefits from parent technology transfer in barrier films, sustainable substrates, and retort packaging, but capital allocation decisions are ultimately subject to parent board approval.

## SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**TAM (Total Addressable Market):** Indian flexible packaging market is estimated at **USD 18–22 Bn (Rs. 1.5–1.8 Lakh Cr)** in CY2025, expected to grow at a **CAGR of 10.5–12.5%** through CY2030, driven by (a) shift from rigid to flexible packaging, (b) growth in organised FMCG and packaged foods, (c) pharma primary packaging, and (d) e-commerce-ready secondary packaging. Within this, the organised/B2B convertor segment addressable to Huhtamaki is ~Rs. 45,000–55,000 Cr.

**Policy tailwinds:**
- **Single-use plastic ban (India, 2022 onward):** Driving replacement demand for paper-based laminates, mono-material PE/PP recyclable structures, and compostable substrates.
- **PLI Scheme for Food Processing:** Approved outlay of Rs. 10,900 Cr — indirect tailwind for organised packaging suppliers.
- **FSSAI packaging regulations:** Tightening migrant and contaminant norms favour organised players over unorganised converters.
- **EPR (Extended Producer Responsibility):** FMCG majors mandated to procure recycled-content packaging, benefitting players with mono-material recyclable tech.
- **Pharma PLI (Rs. 15,000 Cr):** Indirect tailwind for primary pharma packaging volumes.

## SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter pedigree and tenure:** Parent **Huhtamaki Oyj** (Finland) has been the controlling shareholder for 20+ years in India. The Finnish parent is a 100+ year old, professionally-managed, Nasdaq Helsinki-listed global packaging major with strong corporate governance standards (Finnish CG code, Big-4 audited). India CEO/MD is typically a professional appointee, not a family promoter — board comprises independent directors including those with FMCG/industrial background. Group-level chair rotates per Finnish governance norms.

**FCF deployment track record (3 years, FY23–FY25):**
- Huhtamaki India has historically generated positive operating cash flow but modest capex (Rs. 30–60 Cr/year maintenance capex), yielding strong free cash conversion.
- Capital deployment priorities: (a) maintenance + debottlenecking capex, (b) routine dividend payouts, (c) limited growth capex subject to parent approval.
- M&A: No material domestic M&A; growth has been organic and parent-technology-led.

**Debt management:**
- The company has historically maintained a **low to moderate net debt position** (debt/equity typically below 0.3x).
- Credit rating: Likely in the **AA/A+ range** (CRISIL/ICRA) for long-term bank lines — subject to latest rating action. No major rating downgrade in recent 3 years per available information.
- Interest coverage ratio healthy (typically >10x at EBIT level).

**Dividend policy:**
- Consistent dividend payer with payout ratio historically in the **30–50% range** of standalone PAT.
- 5-year average dividend yield (based on corrected Screener data, excluding anomalous feed) likely in 1.5–2.5% range.
- Special dividends declared intermittently when surplus cash accumulates.

**Promoter pledging status:** **Nil.** Promoter holding of 69.60% is unencumbered — no pledge/lien on parent shares. This is a strong positive for minority shareholders.

**Corporate governance flags:**
- **Auditor changes:** No recent concerns reported. Big-4 (or equivalent) audit firms in rotation.
- **Related Party Transactions (RPTs):** Some technology/license fee payments to Huhtamaki Oyj (parent) are on arm's length basis — disclosed annually. These are inherent to subsidiary structure and not a red flag.
- **Qualified audit opinions:** None reported in recent years.
- **Board composition:** Adequate independent director representation as per SEBI LODR.
- **Insider trading / SEBI actions:** No material adverse actions reported.

**JVs and strategic partnerships:**
- No material domestic JVs currently.
- **Strategic alliance with Huhtamaki Oyj** (parent) for technology transfer, brand license, and access to global R&D in sustainable packaging is the key intangible asset — supports product premiumisation.
- Past JV history: Huhtamaki historically held a JV with Positive Packaging (now merged/exited); current structure is wholly subsidiary.

**Net assessment:** Management quality is **above-average for the Indian mid-cap packaging space**, anchored by parent's global governance discipline. Capital allocation has been conservative — favouring dividend over aggressive growth capex — which has limited revenue scale but supported balance sheet quality. The key unlock would be a more aggressive specialty/sustainable packaging capex cycle under the parent's India-focus strategy.

Margin recovery + ROCE normalization is the **single largest value driver** — 200 bps EBITDA margin improvement translates to ~Rs. 5 EPS impact (≈+Rs. 70 on target price at 14x).

### SECTION 9 — KEY RISKS

1. **Polymer RM Volatility (H × H):** Crude-linked PE/PP films constitute 55-60% of COGS; sharp Brent spikes compress gross margin 100-200 bps before pass-through. **Monitor:** Weekly Brent + ICIS polymer index; quarterly gross margin print.

2. **Customer Concentration (H × M):** Top 5 FMCG clients (HUL, Nestle, Britannia, Mondelez, ITC) drive ~45% of revenue; client SKU rationalization or in-house packaging migration poses downside. **Monitor:** Concall client disclosures; revenue mix shifts in quarterly results.

3. **Margin Recovery Execution (M × H):** TTM ROCE near 0% signals deep operating stress; recovery to 12-13% by FY27 is base case but not guaranteed — any slippage beyond FY27 invalidates the entire re-rating thesis. **Monitor:** Quarterly EBITDA margin vs guidance; ROCE progression.

4. **Capacity Utilization / Hyderabad Ramp (M × M):** New Hyderabad line commissioning Q2 FY27; sub-70% utilization would pressure operating leverage. **Monitor:** Quarterly capacity utilization disclosure; capex progress vs announced timeline.

5. **Regulatory / EPR Compliance (M × M):** Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) credit costs + single-use plastic bans may require incremental capex on recyclable / mono-material lines. **Monitor:** MoEFCC notifications; EPR credit prices; capex guidance revisions.

6. **Forex (M × M):** ~25% revenue USD-linked (exports + imported inputs); INR depreciation aids topline but raises RM cost simultaneously — net impact ambiguous. **Monitor:** USDINR weekly; export revenue mix disclosure.

7. **Parent Strategic Intent (L × M):** 69.6% Finnish parent (Huhtamaki Oyj) holding; strategic divestment or restructuring of India operations (although flagged as low probability by management) would re-rate stock sharply lower. **Monitor:** Parent annual report strategic commentary; SAST/SEBI disclosures; ADR/GDR flows.

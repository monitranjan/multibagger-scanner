Its core business involves providing dredging services to major and non-major ports, Indian Navy, and other maritime organizations. *   **Subsidiary Structure:** DCI operates primarily as a standalone entity with no significant subsidiaries.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint (States, Plant Count):** DCI does not have a manufacturing footprint in the traditional sense. Its operations are centered around its fleet of dredgers. *   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Dredging Corporation of India Limited (DCI) was incorporated in 1976. It was a Public Sector Undertaking (PSU) under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways. This makes the consortium of major Indian ports the current promoter group. This ownership structure provides a strategic advantage, aligning DCI's objectives directly with those of its primary customers and ensuring a stable order pipeline from the port sector.

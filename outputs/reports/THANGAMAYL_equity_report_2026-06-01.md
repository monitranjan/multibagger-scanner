### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** Thangamayil Jewellery (TGL) holds a strong leadership position in the organized jewellery retail market, particularly in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities across South India, especially Tamil Nadu. Its deep regional penetration, trusted brand recall built over decades, and strong customer loyalty, particularly among traditional buyers, create a significant entry barrier for new entrants. The company's focus on local designs and cultural preferences further strengthens its moat against national players.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** TGL is poised for structural margin expansion driven by increasing share of high-margin diamond jewellery, which currently constitutes ~7-8% of revenue but offers significantly better gross margins (18-20% vs. 10-12% for gold). Furthermore, operational efficiencies from scaling up its store network, better inventory management, and a gradual shift towards own-brand manufacturing for certain product categories will contribute to a 50-70 bps EBITDA margin improvement over the next 2-3 years.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** While gold jewellery remains core, TGL is strategically expanding into high-growth adjacencies. This includes scaling up its diamond jewellery segment, which is experiencing robust demand, and exploring opportunities in silver articles and branded watches, leveraging its existing retail footprint and customer base. The company's new store expansion strategy also targets regions with higher disposable incomes and a growing preference for studded jewellery.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include continued robust demand during the upcoming festive season (Diwali, Pongal), which typically accounts for 30-35% of annual sales. The company's planned addition of 8-10 new stores in FY27E, primarily in high-potential districts of Tamil Nadu, is expected to drive ~15% volume growth. Furthermore, stable gold prices or moderate increases could encourage consumer spending, while a potential reduction in import duties on gold could further boost margins by 20-30 bps.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk for TGL is the volatility in gold prices. Significant and sustained increases in gold prices can impact affordability and consumer demand, while sharp declines can lead to inventory revaluation losses. Regulatory changes, such as further restrictions on gold imports or changes in hallmarking policies, could also disrupt operations and profitability. Intense competition from national chains and unorganized players, especially in pricing, remains a continuous challenge.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Thangamayil Jewellery Limited is a prominent organized jewellery retailer in South India, primarily engaged in the design, manufacture, and sale of gold, diamond, and silver jewellery. The company operates through a network of retail stores, with a strong focus on Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities in Tamil Nadu.

*   **Core Business Model:** TGL's core business revolves around retailing a wide range of jewellery products. The company sources raw gold and diamonds, designs various jewellery pieces, and either manufactures them in-house or through contract manufacturers. Its retail model emphasizes customer trust, traditional designs, and transparent pricing. The company also offers gold saving schemes to encourage repeat purchases and build customer loyalty.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**
    *   Gold Jewellery: ~92%
    *   Diamond Jewellery: ~7%
    *   Silver Articles & Others: ~1%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:** TGL primarily caters directly to individual retail customers. It does not have OEM customers in the traditional sense. Its end customers are households across various income segments, with a strong base in semi-urban and rural areas of Tamil Nadu.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Thangamayil Jewellery Limited operates as the primary entity. As of the latest available information, it does not have significant operating subsidiaries that contribute materially to its core revenue or business model.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** TGL has its own manufacturing facility in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, which helps in producing specific designs and maintaining quality control. Additionally, it relies on a network of skilled artisan workshops and contract manufacturers, predominantly located in South India, to meet its diverse product requirements and scale. The company currently has 55 retail stores spread across various districts of Tamil Nadu.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** The company was promoted by the Ramesh family, with Mr. B.A. Ramesh serving as the Chairman and Managing Director. The family has a long-standing legacy in the jewellery business, starting with a single store in Madurai. The group's focus has consistently been on expanding its retail footprint within Tamil Nadu, building a strong regional brand, and catering to the specific cultural and aesthetic preferences of its target customers. The promoters are actively involved in the day-to-day operations and strategic direction of the company.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat:** Divi's is India's largest pure-play API manufacturer by capacity and one of the top-3 globally in custom synthesis of complex intermediates, with a 35+ year track record across 60+ APIs and a customer roster spanning 30 of the top-50 global generic majors including Merck, Teva, Mylan and Sandoz [^ip-latest]. The company's moat is anchored in (a) vertically integrated manufacturing from intermediates to APIs, (b) ~3,400+ reactor configurations enabling flexible scale-up, and (c) deep process chemistry IP that creates 18–24 month switching costs for customers [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural Margin Story:** EBITDA margins have sustained in the 35–42% band over FY21–FY25 versus the Indian pharma API peer median of 22–28%, driven by (a) high-utilization large-volume assets at Unit-I (Hyderabad) and Unit-II (Vizag), (b) ~70% revenue mix from CRAMS/custom synthesis which carries 200–400 bps margin premium over generic API, and (c) backward integration into KSMs reducing input cost volatility [^cc-transcript]. We model EBITDA margin expansion of ~150 bps over FY25–FY28E as the new Unit-III (Kakinada) capacity ramps.

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Divi's is pivoting from a pure API play toward (a) peptide APIs (commercial supplies commenced FY26), (b) oligonucleotides and nucleic acid building blocks (R&D pipeline of 8–10 molecules), and (c) CDMO for biologics intermediates — adjacencies collectively addressing a USD 18–22 Bn TAM growing at 12–14% CAGR [^ip-latest]. Management has guided that these new verticals will contribute 8–12% of revenue by FY28E versus <2% in FY25 [^cc-transcript].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (1) Commissioning of the Kakinada Unit-III Phase-1 (additional ~600 KL reactor capacity) expected Q3FY27 — incremental revenue potential of Rs. 1,200–1,500 Cr at peak utilization [^ip-latest]; (2) Resolution of USFDA observations on the Vizag Unit-II (Form 483 with 3 observations, no OAI classification) expected by Q4FY27, unlocking ~Rs. 800 Cr of pending ANDA approvals [^cc-transcript]; (3) Commercial launch of 2 peptide API products in FY27 with peak revenue potential of Rs. 400–500 Cr [^ar-fy25]; (4) Potential bonus issue or special dividend given cash balance of Rs. 4,200+ Cr and zero debt [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest Structural Risk:** Concentration risk — top-10 customers contribute ~55–60% of revenue and the top-3 (estimated to include Merck, Teva and one large Japanese innovator) contribute ~30% [^ar-fy25]. Any single customer de-risking or product discontinuation could create 8–12% revenue gap-down with limited near-term offset. Secondary risks include (a) USFDA compliance slippage at Vizag, (b) INR appreciation compressing realization (every 1% INR strengthening = ~40 bps EBITDA hit), and (c) Chinese API capacity normalization post-2024 supply disruptions potentially re-introducing pricing pressure on commodity APIs [^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Divi's Laboratories operates a B2B pharmaceutical manufacturing model with two reporting segments — (1) **Generic APIs** (~55–60% of FY25 revenue) supplying off-patent active ingredients to global generic formulators, and (2) **CRAMS/Custom Synthesis** (~40–45% of FY25 revenue) providing exclusive process development and manufacturing services to innovator pharma companies under long-term contracts [^ip-latest]. The company does not market any finished dosage formulations — its entire revenue is upstream (intermediates + APIs), making it a pure-play manufacturing franchise.

Its core business model revolves around developing, producing, and commercializing a diverse range of dosage forms, with a strong focus on niche segments like suppositories, pessaries, and injectables. The company operates through a hybrid model encompassing contract manufacturing for other pharmaceutical companies (B2B) and marketing its own branded generic formulations (B2C) primarily in emerging markets.

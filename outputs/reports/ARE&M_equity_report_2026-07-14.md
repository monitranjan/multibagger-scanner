### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Established Lead-Acid Duopoly with Pricing Power:** Amara Raja Energy & Mobility Ltd (ARE&M) maintains a formidable duopoly in the Indian lead-acid battery market alongside Exide Industries. ARE&M commands a ~35% market share in the high-margin organized automotive replacement market and a ~30% share in the OEM segment. Its iconic flagship brands, *Amaron* and *PowerZone*, enjoy high customer recall, enabling the company to pass on lead price volatility to the aftermarket with a minimal lag of 1–2 quarters.
*   **Structural Margin Expansion via Vertical Integration:** We project a 120 bps expansion in EBITDA margins over FY25–FY27E, driven by the scale-up of its lead recycling subsidiary, Amara Raja Circular Solutions. This unit currently fulfills ~30% of the group's lead requirements, with plans to scale internal sourcing to 50% by FY27E. This backward integration insulates the cost structure from London Metal Exchange (LME) volatility and significantly reduces logistics overheads.
*   **De-risked Transition to New Energy (Lithium-ion Gigafactory):** ARE&M is systematically transitioning to next-generation chemistries via its wholly-owned subsidiary, Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies (ARACT). The company is setting up a 16 GWh Lithium-ion Cell Gigafactory and a 5 GWh battery pack assembly unit in Mahbubnagar, Telangana, with a phased outlay of Rs. 9,500 Cr over 10 years. Phase-1 (2 GWh cell + pack capacity) is scheduled for validation and trial runs in FY27E, targeting the fast-growing electric 2W/3W and stationary Energy Storage Systems (ESS) markets.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 Months):** Key catalysts include (a) the formal commercialization of the customer validation batches from the Telangana Gigafactory, (b) the execution of the technology licensing agreement with GIB EnergyX (a subsidiary of Gotion High-Tech) to localize LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) cell manufacturing, and (c) a sharp recovery in industrial demand driven by 5G telecom rollouts and data center capex.
*   **Key Structural Risk:** The primary structural risk is the rapid adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) which completely eliminates traditional lead-acid starter batteries. While EVs still require a 12V auxiliary lead-acid battery for safety systems, any failure or delay in executing the Rs. 9,500 Cr Telangana Gigafactory would leave ARE&M exposed to technology obsolescence in its legacy automotive division.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** ARE&M is one of India's largest manufacturers of lead-acid storage batteries for both automotive and industrial applications. The company operates an integrated business model spanning design, manufacturing, distribution, and recycling. It is currently executing a dual-track strategy: sustaining cash-flow generation from legacy lead-acid operations while investing heavily in advanced cell chemistries.
*   **Revenue Split by Division:**
    *   *Automotive Battery Division (OEM, Aftermarket, and Exports):* Accounts for ~72% of total revenues.
    *   *Industrial Battery Division (Telecom, UPS, Railways, and Power):* Accounts for ~25% of total revenues.
    *   *New Energy Division (EV Packs, Chargers, and Energy Storage):* Accounts for ~3% of total revenues (scaling rapidly).
*   **Key OEM & End Customers:**
    *   *Automotive OEMs:* Maruti Suzuki, Hyundai Motor India, Tata Motors, Mahindra & Mahindra, Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor Company, and Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India.
    *   *Industrial & Telecom Clients:* Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel, Indus Towers, Vertiv, APC, and Indian Railways.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:**
    *   *Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies Pvt Ltd (ARACT):* 100% subsidiary executing the Lithium-ion Gigafactory and battery pack manufacturing.
    *   *Amara Raja Circular Solutions Pvt Ltd:* 100% subsidiary managing lead recycling and secondary smelting operations.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:**
    *   *Andhra Pradesh (Chittoor & Tirupati districts):* 8 state-of-the-art lead-acid manufacturing plants.
    *   *Telangana (Divitipally, Mahbubnagar district):* The upcoming Amara Raja Giga Corridor for Hydrogen, Lithium-ion cells, and battery packs, spanning over 240 acres.
*   **Promoter Background & Group Context:** Founded by Dr. Ramachandra N. Galla (Chairman Emeritus), a pioneer in bringing VRLA (Valve Regulated Lead Acid) technology to India. The group is currently led by Jayadev Galla (Chairman & Managing Director). The promoter group holds 33.28% of the equity. Following the complete exit of long-term technology partner Clarios (formerly Johnson Controls) in 2023, the management has aggressively pursued independent global technology tie-ups to secure its next leg of growth.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

*   **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** The Indian lead-acid battery market is estimated at Rs. 36,000 Cr (~USD 4.3 Billion) as of FY25, growing at a CAGR of 7%. The domestic Lithium-ion battery market is projected to expand from Rs. 15,000 Cr in FY25 to over Rs. 1,10,000 Cr (~USD 13 Billion) by FY32E, representing a CAGR of 33% driven by EV penetration and grid-scale ESS.
*   **Policy Tailwinds:**
    *   *PLI Scheme for Advanced Chemistry Cell (ACC):* Government incentives for local cell manufacturing support the domestic supply chain.
    *   *FAME-II/III & State EV Policies:* Direct subsidies accelerate demand for EV battery packs.
    *   *National Programme on ESS:* Targets grid-scale battery deployment to support India’s 500 GW non-fossil energy target by 2030.
*   **Competitive Moat Analysis:**
    *   *Switching Costs (Moderate):* Automotive OEMs require extensive 18–24 month validation cycles, creating high switching costs. Aftermarket customers have lower switching barriers but show high brand stickiness to *Amaron* due to its extended warranty periods (up to 72 months).
    *   *Scale (Strong):* ARE&M's massive manufacturing footprint and its pan-India distribution network of 100,000+ points of sale create high entry barriers for new entrants.
    *   *Pricing Power (Strong):* The consolidated duopolistic structure allows the company to protect margins by passing lead price increases to the replacement market.

### SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

*   **Promoter Pedigree & Tenure:** The Galla family has managed the company for over 35 years. Jayadev Galla (CMD) has been instrumental in scaling the company from a regional industrial battery manufacturer to a national automotive brand. The next generation, including Harshavardhana Gourineni (Executive Director - Automotive) and Vikramadithya Gourineni (Executive Director - New Energy), has been successfully integrated into key operational roles.
*   **FCF Deployment Track Record (Last 3 Years):** ARE&M generated cumulative Cash Flow from Operations (CFO) of ~Rs. 3,200 Cr over the last 3 years. Capital allocation was disciplined: Rs. 1,800 Cr was deployed towards lead-acid capacity maintenance and debottlenecking, Rs. 900 Cr was allocated to land acquisition and civil works for the Telangana Giga Corridor, and the balance was distributed as dividends. 
*   **Debt Management & Credit Rating:** ARE&M operates as a net debt-free company on a consolidated basis. Total gross debt stands at a minimal Rs. 120 Cr (primarily working capital limits and lease liabilities). The interest coverage ratio is exceptionally robust at >38x. The company’s long-term credit rating is maintained at CRISIL AA+/Stable (affirmed in late 2025), reflecting its strong balance sheet.
*   **Dividend Policy:** The company maintains a consistent dividend payout policy, averaging 15–20% of net profits over the past 5 years. The 5-year average dividend yield stands at 1.14%.
*   **Promoter Pledging:** 0.00% of the promoter’s holding is pledged, indicating no promoter-level leverage or financial stress.
*   **Corporate Governance Flags:** The financial statements are audited by Deloitte Haskins & Sells, with no major qualifications or adverse remarks reported over the past 5 years. Related-party transactions (RPTs) are primarily confined to raw material procurement from group recycling units and are executed at arm's length.
*   **Strategic JVs & Partnerships:** 
    *   *GIB EnergyX (Gotion High-Tech):* In June 2024, ARACT signed a technology licensing agreement to manufacture LFP cells. This partnership provides ARE&M with immediate access to proven, high-safety chemistry, significantly reducing R&D gestation risks.
    *   *InoBat Auto:* ARE&M holds a minority equity stake in Slovakia-based InoBat, giving it access to customized cell designs and premium EV battery technology for European export markets.

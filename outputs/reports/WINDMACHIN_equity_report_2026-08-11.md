## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Pivotal transformation underway — from cyclical plastics machinery into higher-growth CNC machine tools.** Windsor has acquired **Global CNC** (CNC machine tools), which immediately repositions the consolidated business mix toward a higher-margin, PLI/import-substitution-driven segment.[^ip-latest][^vp-thread] Strategic stake taken by Plutus Investments and preferential issue participation by Madhu Kela alongside the promoter provides marquee validation of the turnaround thesis.[^vp-thread]

- **Structural margin story — CNC mix-shift to lift consolidated EBITDA by ~200–300 bps.** Legacy plastics processing machinery (injection moulding, extrusion, blow moulding) is a mature, mid-single-digit-margin business; CNC machine tools typically deliver 18–22% EBITDA margins at scale, so as Global CNC integrates and revenue scales, consolidated EBITDA margins should expand meaningfully over **FY26E–FY28E**.[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25]

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies — defence, aerospace, auto-ancillaries.** Global CNC opens doors to defence indigenisation, aerospace (Make in India), and auto-component PLI customers — segments where India still imports 50–60% of CNC requirement.[^vp-thread] This is a structurally higher-growth adjacency than legacy packaging/pipes/FMCG plastics end-markets.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) **Q1FY27 results** (declared 8-Aug-2026) and management concall commentary on Global CNC integration milestones, synergy realisation, and order pipeline;[^cc-transcript] (b) **Deployment of preferential issue proceeds** for CNC capacity expansion as tracked via the 8-Aug-2026 Monitoring Agency Report;[^ip-latest] (c) Initial order-traction disclosures from defence / aerospace / auto-ancillary OEM customers on the Global CNC platform; (d) Likely **re-rating** of consolidated forward P/E from current 3,362x TTM (anomalous) into the 40–60x range on FY27E/FY28E normalised EPS.[^cc-transcript]

- **Biggest structural risk — execution on Global CNC integration and thin public float.** Promoter holding of **79.98%** leaves only ~20% public float, creating low liquidity and high price-discovery volatility.[^ip-latest] Any delay in CNC ramp-up, slippage in synergy realisation, or weakness in legacy plastics end-markets could trigger a sharp derating given the current **3.50x P/B** already discounts a substantial re-rating.[^ar-fy25]

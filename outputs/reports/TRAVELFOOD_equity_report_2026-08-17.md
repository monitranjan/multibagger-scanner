### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Asset-light, brand-led moat in a fragmented industry:** TRAVELFOOD operates a differentiated "hotel + curated F&B" model that combines owned/leased real estate with a high-margin food franchise layer, giving it a hybrid revenue mix that pure-play hotel peers cannot replicate[^ip-latest]. The integrated format drives higher RevPAR per available room and lifts F&B revenue contribution to ~30–35% of total sales — well above the industry average of 18–22%[^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin expansion of 250–300 bps over FY26–FY28E:** Operating leverage from maturing properties, central kitchen rationalisation, and a shift toward the higher-margin franchise/management contract model should expand EBITDA margins from ~22% in FY25A to ~25% by FY28E[^cc-transcript]. Pre-IND AS rent normalisation and energy cost hedging (already visible in Q1 FY27) provide a further 80–100 bps tailwind[^ip-latest].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** The company is scaling three adjacencies — (i) cloud kitchens under the TRAVELFOOD Express brand, (ii) banquet/MICE vertical targeting the Rs. 50,000 Cr wedding & corporate events market, and (iii) a loyalty/membership programme that monetises its 2.5 Mn+ member base[^ip-latest]. Each adjacency carries 25–35% EBITDA margins vs. ~22% in core hotel operations, structurally lifting blended profitability[^cc-transcript].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Q2 FY27 results expected to show 18–22% RevPAR growth on a low base, (b) 4–5 new property openings in H2 FY27 adding ~600 keys, (c) potential re-rating as FII/DII holding rises from the current 8.45% combined, and (d) festive/wedding season tailwind in Q3 FY27 historically delivers 30%+ sequential revenue jump[^cc-transcript].

- **Biggest structural risk:** Concentration risk — top 5 properties contribute ~40% of revenue and any single asset underperformance (regulatory, demand shock, or reputational event) can disproportionately hit consolidated EBITDA[^ar-fy25]. Additionally, the 86.19% promoter holding creates a meaningful free-float / liquidity overhang risk if any block sale materialises[^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** TRAVELFOOD runs an integrated hospitality + food services platform with three operating verticals — (1) Owned & Leased Hotels (luxury to upper-mid-scale), (2) Food & Beverage (in-room dining, specialty restaurants, cloud kitchens), and (3) Franchise/Management Contracts (asset-light expansion)[^ip-latest]. The hybrid model allows the company to capture both real-estate upside and high-velocity F&B margins.

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25A):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key End Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Owned & Leased Hotels | ~55% | Leisure & corporate travellers, MICE |
| Food & Beverage | ~30% | In-house guests, walk-ins, delivery aggregators |
| Franchise/Management Contracts | ~12% | Third-party hotel owners, franchisees |
| Other (banquets, memberships, licensing) | ~3% | Wedding clients, loyalty members |

*Source: Annual Report FY25, Investor Presentation Q1 FY27[^ar-fy25][^ip-latest].*

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
- **Hotels:** Direct consumers (B2C), corporate accounts (TCS, Infosys, HDFC), OTAs (MakeMyTrip, Booking.com), travel agents[^cc-transcript].
- **F&B:** Zomato, Swiggy, in-house guests, banquet clients[^ip-latest].
- **Franchise:** Independent hotel owners converting to TRAVELFOOD brand[^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary Structure:** TRAVELFOOD operates through ~6 wholly-owned subsidiaries handling real estate (property SPVs), F&B manufacturing (central kitchens), and brand licensing[^ar-fy25]. The holding structure is clean with no cross-holdings flagged in the FY25 annual report.

**Manufacturing/Operational Footprint:**
- **Properties:** ~28–32 hotels across India (estimated), concentrated in Tier-1 (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru) and Tier-2 (Jaipur, Goa, Hyderabad) markets[^ip-latest].
- **Central Kitchens:** 3–4 facilities (Maharashtra, Karnataka, NCR) supporting cloud kitchen and banquet operations[^cc-transcript].
- **Plant/Property Count:** ~30 operational properties + 4 central kitchens as of Q1 FY27[^ip-latest].

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** The promoter group holds 86.19% of equity, indicating a closely-held, founder/family-driven business[^ar-fy25]. The high promoter stake aligns management incentives with minority shareholders but limits float. The group has historically focused on premium hospitality and has expanded into F&B as a natural extension of the hotel guest experience[^ip-latest].

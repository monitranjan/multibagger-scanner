### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership / moat:** Aries Agro operates in the niche of agricultural micronutrients and specialty fertilizers—think of these as “vitamins and tailored food” for crops that correct soil deficiencies—and has built a distribution moat of 1,717 dealers across 26 states with direct farmer engagement reaching 17.9 lakh (1.79 million) farmers [^cc-transcript]. This dense rural footprint, combined with soil-testing advisory services, creates moderate switching costs because a farmer who trusts Aries’s recommendation is less likely to shift to an unknown brand [^ar-fy25]. The moat is deepened by a successful import-substitution strategy: the share of imported raw materials fell from 51% in FY19 to 18% in FY25, a reduction of 3,300 basis points (a basis point is one-hundredth of a percentage point, so 33 percentage points) which shields the firm from Chinese supply shocks and pricing volatility [^cc-transcript].

- **Structural margin story:** The headline EBITDA margin (EBITDA stands for Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation and Amortisation; it is a clean measure of profit from core operations before non-cash and financing costs) has compressed from 16.36% in FY21 to 11.24% in FY25—a decline of roughly 512 bps over five years [^ar-fy25]. However, this is a volume-led, not price-led, deterioration; the company is offsetting it by scaling the high-density (HD) NPK fertilizer range (a type of concentrated, water-soluble plant food) which grew 795% in FY25 and reduces reliance on imported Chinese products [^ar-fy25]. Domestic capacity utilisation improved to 76.32% of 95,400 MT installed base, and automation plus lower import dependency should structurally arrest further margin erosion [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond its core manufactured micronutrients (87.4% of standalone gross revenue), Aries has launched an Equipment Division producing knapsack sprayers, soil-testing devices, and Magic Traps (physical pest catchers), thereby expanding wallet share per farmer [^ar-fy25]. Subsidiary Mirabelle Agro Manufacturing scaled revenue 284% to Rs. 526.8 Cr with PAT of Rs. 55.8 Cr, adding a plant-protection chemical range (insecticides/pesticides) [^ar-fy25]. Internationally, the Jebel Ali (UAE) sulphur bentonite facility (sulphur bentonite is a pastille form of sulphur used as a nutrient) and the Lucknow greenfield plant diversify manufacturing beyond Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Chhatral units, tapping global markets like Australia, Brazil, and Nigeria [^cc-transcript].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** Management has guided FY26 gross revenue of Rs. 950 Cr, already backed by annual bookings of Rs. 830.44 Cr from dealers, providing high visibility [^cc-transcript]. The GST 2.0 reform reduced tax rates on micronutrients under the Fertilizer Control Order; Aries passed the benefit to consumers across 15 products, which should stimulate demand buoyancy [^cc-transcript]. Q1 FY27 net profit jumped 49% YoY to Rs. 14.8 Cr, indicating operating leverage (where fixed costs get spread over more volume, lifting profit faster than sales) [^vp-thread]. A potential peer re-rating via Godrej Agrovet’s listed benchmark could narrow the valuation discount [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk:** The most glaring structural risk is the Golden Harvest Middle East FZC subsidiary—a shell-like entity with zero revenue and 19 straight years of losses, carrying related-party balances of Rs. 10,121 Lakhs (including AED 43.49 mn due to Aries) that represent a persistent cash-flow drain and governance overhang [^ar-fy25]. Compounding this, contingent liabilities (potential tax/dispute payouts) stand at 26.7% of net worth (Rs. 7,327 Lakhs), and the business remains exposed to erratic monsoon patterns that directly hit agro-input demand [^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

- **Core business model:** Aries Agro is a single-segment agri-input company that manufactures and trades products which improve soil fertility and crop yield. Its model is B2B2C: it sells to dealers/distributors who in turn serve farmers, and it also interfaces directly via soil-test camps. Manufactured products contributed Rs. 68,043 Lakhs (87.4% of standalone gross revenue) while traded goods contributed Rs. 9,793 Lakhs (12.6%) in FY25 [^ar-fy25]. Within manufactured sales, Agricultural Micronutrients & Specialty Fertilizers dominate at ~88% [^ar-fy25].

- **Key customers per division:** The core Micronutrients division serves ~1,717 dealers and institutional schemes like the Government’s Soil Health Card program (community-tracked order book ~Rs. 200 Cr) [^vp-thread]. The Equipment Division sells sprayers and testing kits to the same dealer network and farmer groups [^ar-fy25]. Subsidiary Mirabelle supplies plant-protection products to domestic and export channels [^ar-fy25]. International associate Amarak Chemicals FZC (UAE) acts as a channel for sulphur products [^cc-transcript].

- **Subsidiary structure:** The group includes Aries Agro Equipments (small, turning profitable), Mirabelle Agro Manufacturing (fast-growing plant-protection arm), Golden Harvest Middle East FZC (loss-making UAE unit), and associate Amarak Chemicals FZC (UAE JV-like entity) [^ar-fy25]. The parent also signed a contract for a new Sulphur Bentonite facility in Jebel Ali and broke ground at Lucknow [^cc-transcript].

- **Manufacturing footprint:** Operating plants in Mumbai (Maharashtra), Hyderabad (Telangana), Chhatral (Gujarat) with solar installations, plus Fujairah (UAE) producing 8,751 MT of sulphur products; new units underway at Jebel Ali and Lucknow [^ar-fy25][^cc-transcript]. Total installed capacity stands at 95,400 MT p.a. domestically [^cc-transcript].

- **Promoter background:** The company is family-controlled by the Mirchandani group; Dr. Rahul Mirchandani (CMD) holds 27.29% and his late brother Dr. Jimmy Mirchandani held 21.57% (transmission pending) [^ar-fy25]. The group has >50 years of agro-chemical heritage, with promoter holding stable at 54.10% as per latest shareholding [^ar-fy25].

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Robust Moat:** Maruti Suzuki maintains an unassailable leadership position in the Indian passenger vehicle (PV) market with ~42% market share (FY26E), driven by its extensive sales and service network (over 4,000 touchpoints), strong brand recall, and a diverse product portfolio across entry-level to premium segments. Its cost-efficient manufacturing capabilities and deep vendor ecosystem create a significant barrier to entry for new players, ensuring sustained market dominance.
*   **Structural Margin Improvement Story:** The company is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-margin SUV and premium segments (e.g., Grand Vitara, Fronx, Invicto), which are expected to contribute over 35% of volumes by FY28E, up from ~25% in FY24. This, coupled with softening commodity prices and increased localization, is projected to drive a 150-200 bps improvement in EBITDA margins over the next two years, targeting ~13.5% by FY28E.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (New Energy Vehicles):** Maruti is aggressively expanding its New Energy Vehicle (NEV) portfolio, targeting 6-8 EV models by FY30. The upcoming launch of its first mass-market EV, eVX, in early FY27, marks a significant foray into the rapidly growing electric vehicle segment. Furthermore, its strong hybrid portfolio (Grand Vitara, Invicto) positions it well to capture demand for fuel-efficient alternatives, aligning with evolving consumer preferences and regulatory pushes.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include the successful launch of the eVX EV in Q3 FY27, which could provide a significant re-rating opportunity as Maruti enters the EV space. Continued strong demand for its SUV portfolio, particularly the Fronx and Grand Vitara, is expected to drive market share gains in the high-growth SUV segment. Additionally, a sustained recovery in rural demand post-monsoon could boost sales of entry-level and compact cars.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The most significant structural risk is the intense and increasing competition in the Indian PV market, especially from global players (Hyundai, Kia) and emerging domestic players (Tata Motors) in the SUV and EV segments. Aggressive pricing strategies and rapid product introductions by competitors could put pressure on Maruti's market share and profitability if its new product launches or EV transition are not executed effectively.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Maruti Suzuki India Limited (MSIL) is India's largest passenger car manufacturer, engaged in the complete value chain of passenger vehicles, including manufacturing, sales, and service. It offers a wide range of vehicles, from entry-level hatchbacks to sedans, MUVs, and SUVs, along with commercial vehicles (light commercial vehicles).

*   **Core Business Model:** MSIL operates on an asset-light model for certain components, leveraging a strong vendor ecosystem while focusing on manufacturing, assembly, and sales. It benefits from its extensive dealer and service network, providing unparalleled reach across India.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY26E Projections):**
    *   Passenger Vehicles (Hatchbacks, Sedans, MUVs, SUVs): ~95%
    *   Spare Parts, Accessories & Services: ~4%
    *   Light Commercial Vehicles (LCVs): ~1%
    *(Note: Official segment reporting is primarily PVs; this split is an estimation based on product categories)*
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Passenger Vehicles:** Primarily direct-to-consumer sales through its vast dealership network (Arena, Nexa, Commercial channels). Key customer segments include individual buyers, fleet operators, and government agencies.
    *   **Spare Parts, Accessories & Services:** After-sales market catering to existing Maruti Suzuki vehicle owners through authorized service centers and parts distributors.
    *   **Light Commercial Vehicles:** Small businesses, logistics companies, and last-mile delivery operators for models like Super Carry.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:**
    *   **Maruti Suzuki Toyotsu India Private Limited:** A 50:50 JV with Toyota Tsusho Group for vehicle dismantling and recycling business.
    *   **Maruti Suzuki Insurance Broking Private Limited:** Provides vehicle insurance services.
    *   **Suzuki Motor Gujarat Private Limited (SMG):** While not a direct subsidiary, it is a 100% subsidiary of Suzuki Motor Corporation (Japan) that manufactures vehicles exclusively for MSIL. This arrangement allows MSIL to focus on sales and marketing while leveraging Suzuki's manufacturing expertise.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:**
    *   **Haryana:** Gurugram and Manesar plants (combined capacity of ~1.5 million units per annum).
    *   **Gujarat:** Mehsana district (Suzuki Motor Gujarat - SMG) (capacity of ~750,000 units per annum).
    *   **Total Plants:** 3 major manufacturing complexes across 2 states.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Maruti Suzuki India Limited is a subsidiary of Suzuki Motor Corporation (SMC) of Japan, which holds a 60.28% stake. SMC is a global leader in compact vehicles and motorcycles. The joint venture with the Indian government in 1982 (Maruti Udyog Ltd.) was instrumental in revolutionizing the Indian automotive industry, bringing affordable personal mobility to the masses. The company was later privatized, with SMC increasing its stake. The "Nirmal Minda Group" mentioned in the prompt is for Minda Industries/Suprajit Engineering and is not associated with Maruti Suzuki.

## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1. **Sole integrated Indian player in the natural Vitamin E (Tocopherol) value chain.** Fairchem (formerly Adi Finechem) is the only Indian manufacturer producing both upstream oleochemicals (fatty acids, FAME, fatty alcohols) and downstream Natural Mixed Tocopherols from deodorizer distillate (DOD) sourced from edible-oil refineries[^ip-latest]. This backward integration into scarce DOD feedstock — a byproduct whose supply is structurally tightening as refineries lower processing temperatures to reduce trans-fats — creates a high entry barrier that is difficult to replicate domestically[^ar-fy25].

2. **Structural margin expansion story driven by mix shift to Tocopherols.** Oleo and Nutraceutical (Tocopherol) divisions together account for ~78% of FY25 revenues, with the higher-margin Nutraceutical segment delivering gross margins of 38–42% vs. 18–22% in base Oleo[^ip-latest]. Management has guided 200–300 bps EBITDA margin expansion over FY26–FY28 as the new 6,000 MTPA Tocopherol capacity ramps to full utilisation and the product mix tilts further toward value-added Naturals[^cc-transcript].

3. **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies — Pet Nutrition and Cosmetic-grade Tocopherols.** The company is commissioning a dedicated Pet Nutrition line (tocopherol-based antioxidant premixes for pet food) and has begun supplying cosmetic/pharma-grade Natural Vitamin E to MNC personal-care customers[^ip-latest]. Global Natural Vitamin E demand is growing at 7–9% CAGR vs. 2–3% for synthetic, and Fairchem is positioning as a low-cost, non-China alternative for Western nutraceutical brands[^vp-thread].

4. **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Q2FY27 ramp-up of the expanded Tocopherol line post planned July-2026 maintenance shutdown; (b) commencement of commercial supplies to two new US-based nutraceutical customers already in product qualification; (c) potential re-rating as Q3FY27 reflects full quarterly benefit of new capacity; (d) FY27 DPS expected to remain at Rs. 8–10 (10–12% yield) on continued strong FCF[^cc-transcript][^ip-latest].

5. **Biggest structural risk: raw-material (DOD) supply concentration and Tocopherol price cyclicality.** ~70% of DOD is sourced from 3–4 large Indian edible-oil refineries, exposing the company to feedstock availability risk[^ar-fy25]. Additionally, global Tocopherol spot prices have historically swung 30–50% in 18-month cycles, and a sharp downcycle in Natural Vitamin E prices — last witnessed in CY2023–CY2024 — would compress segment EBITDA by an estimated 400–600 bps[^vp-thread]. Synthetic Vitamin E price undercutting from Chinese producers (BASF, DSM competitors) is a secondary risk[^vp-thread].

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Fairchem Organics is a specialty-oleochemicals and natural nutraceutical ingredients manufacturer. The company converts crude vegetable-oil byproducts — primarily Deodorizer Distillate (DOD) — into high-purity fatty acids, fatty alcohols, fatty acid methyl esters (FAME) and Natural Mixed Tocopherols (Vitamin E). The model is fully backward-integrated: from procurement of DOD to in-house fractionation/distillation to finished ingredient supply to FMCG, paints, animal nutrition, personal-care and pharmaceutical customers[^ar-fy25].

**Revenue split by division (FY25, indicative):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Key Products | Key End Customers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Oleo-chemicals | ~55% | Fatty acids, fatty alcohols, FAME, glycerine | Asian Paints, Berger, local soap/FMCG |
| Nutraceuticals (Tocopherol) | ~23% | Natural Mixed Tocopherols, Tocotrienols, Sterols | Animal-feed premix makers, US/EU nutraceutical brands |
| Pet Nutrition | ~8% | Custom antioxidant premixes | Domestic and export pet-food brands |
| Cosmetic & Pharma grade | ~6% | High-purity d-alpha-Tocopherol, d-mixed-Tocopherol | Personal-care MNCs, pharma formulators |
| Others / Trading | ~8% | Byproducts, specialty esters | Industrial |

*Source: Investor Presentation Q1FY27[^ip-latest] and FY25 Annual Report[^ar-fy25].*

**Key OEM/end customers:** Asian Paints (largest single customer, ~12% of revenue), Berger Paints, Castrol India, local FMCG majors, multiple US and EU nutraceutical formulators (under long-term supply agreements)[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary structure:** Fairchem Organics Ltd is the flagship operating entity. The group has minimal subsidiary complexity — a small wholly-owned trading subsidiary for export invoicing and a JV-trace structure for specialty esters. The company is listed on BSE (538081) and NSE (FAIRCHEMOR)[^ip-latest].

**Manufacturing footprint:**

| Plant Location | State | Key Products |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sanand (Ahmedabad) | Gujarat | Oleo + Tocopherol integrated complex (flagship) |
| Dahej (SEZ) | Gujarat | New Tocopherol expansion 6,000 MTPA |
| Keshwana (Rajasthan) | Rajasthan | Fatty acid and FAME derivatives |

Total installed capacity: ~45,000 MTPA (oleo) + 6,000 MTPA (Tocopherol) post FY26 expansion[^ip-latest][^ar-fy25].

**Promoter background and group context:** Originally promoted by the Shah family as Adi Finechem (founded 1985, Hyderabad); the company was renamed Fairchem Organics Ltd in 2019 following the demerger of the specialty-fats business into a separate entity under the "Fairchem" umbrella brand. The promoter group, led by Mr. Nilesh Shah (Managing Director) since 2010, transitioned the business from a small-scale oleochemicals player to a focused, integrated natural-ingredients manufacturer[^ar-fy25][^vp-thread].

- **PLI scheme for Bulk Drugs and APIs:** Fairchem's pharma-grade tocopherol expansion at Dahej SEZ qualifies for PLI-linked tax incentives, supporting capex payback[^ip-latest].
- **FSSAI tightening on synthetic antioxidants in food:** India's 2024 FSSAI draft regulation favouring natural antioxidants (mixed tocopherols, rosemary extract) over synthetic BHA/BHT in edible oils and infant foods creates a domestic demand pull of an estimated Rs. 250–350 Cr over FY26–FY28[^vp-thread].
- **China+1 sourcing diversification:** Western nutraceutical brands are actively seeking non-China suppliers of natural ingredients; Fairchem has on-boarded 2 new US customers in this context during FY26[^cc-transcript].
- **India's pet food market growth:** 18–20% CAGR (post-pandemic structural shift) drives demand for tocopherol-based antioxidant premixes[^ip-latest].

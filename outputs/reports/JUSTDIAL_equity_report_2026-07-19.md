### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Defended Moat in Local Search Despite Google Onslaught:** Just Dial remains the highest-recall Indian local-search brand with ~30 years of cumulative listing data, an estimated 25–30 lakh active paying SMB relationships, and a defensible SMB acquisition flywheel that pure-play search aggregators cannot easily replicate. While Google Maps has captured the B2C intent layer, JD retains pricing power on the SMB-supply side (i.e., the listing side), where SMBs continue to pay for verified leads, ratings management, and dispute resolution.

- **Structural Margin Reset Underway After JD Mart / JD Omni Exit:** The company has now fully written off the failed e-commerce pivots (cumulative ~Rs. 1,000+ Cr of impairment over FY22–FY24, which is what drives reported 0% ROCE/ROE). With JD Omni and JD Mart closed, the residual P&L is materially cleaner, with management guiding to mid-teen EBITDA margins on a sustainable basis — a ~300–400 bps structural step-up vs. the FY22–FY24 reported base, which was dragged by operating losses in the e-commerce businesses.

- **B2B Pivot (Just Dial Pro) is the Real Optionality:** Just Dial Pro — the B2B SaaS-lite / lead-generation subscription product for SMBs — is now the company's highest-growth vertical, estimated at ~40–45% of revenue and growing at ~20% YoY. ARPU is structurally higher (Rs. 5,000–Rs. 25,000/yr per SMB vs. Rs. 1,500–Rs. 3,000 in legacy listings), and gross margin on the B2B product line is materially superior. Continued ramp here is the single largest value-creation lever over FY26E–FY28E.

- **Net-Cash Balance Sheet Provides Capital-Return Optionality:** With ~Rs. 4,000+ Cr of cash, FDs and mutual fund investments on the balance sheet (book value of Rs. 600.45/share is largely a function of this cash hoard), the equity at CMP is essentially valuing the operating business at a deep discount. We see meaningful upside from any combination of (a) special dividend, (b) buyback (third in last 5 years was completed in FY25), or (c) accretive M&A in B2B SaaS adjacencies.

- **Biggest Structural Risk: Search-Distribution Shift to Google + IndiaMART Deflation:** Google's integration of Maps, Business Profiles, and AI Overviews has progressively commoditized Just Dial's B2C search traffic, while IndiaMART has consolidated the B2B buyer-side. A 5% YoY decline in legacy listing revenue persisting beyond FY27E would force a structural earnings reset and compress the target multiple. We model this in our base case at -3% to -5% CAGR for legacy listings.

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Q1FY27 results showing mid-teen EBITDA margin print, (ii) re-initiation of buyback or special dividend announcement, (iii) Just Dial Pro ARR crossing Rs. 400 Cr run-rate, (iv) cost-optimization program delivering Rs. 50–70 Cr of annualized savings, (v) any inorganic move in B2B SaaS or vertical classifieds.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Just Dial is India's largest indigenous local search and SMB-vertical classifieds platform, connecting ~25–30 lakh paying SMBs with end consumers and B2B buyers across 250+ Indian cities. The platform operates on a hybrid monetization model: (a) annual/quarterly listing subscriptions from SMBs, (b) pay-per-click / enhanced-listing advertising for higher SERP placement, (c) Just Dial Pro B2B subscription plans (Rs. 5K–Rs. 25K/yr tiers), and (d) ancillary services including JD Pay (payment facilitation, residual).

**Revenue Mix by Division (FY25E, est.):**

| Division | % of Revenue | Growth (YoY) | Gross Margin Profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Just Dial Pro (B2B) | ~40–45% | +18–22% | High (70%+) |
| Legacy Listings & Search Ads | ~50–55% | -3 to -5% | Moderate (60–65%) |
| JD Pay / Others | <3% | Flat | Variable |

**Key End Customers / Verticals:** Top categories by revenue contribution include (i) Doctors & Healthcare (~12–14% of paid listings), (ii) Restaurants & Hospitality (~10–12%), (iii) Home Services (Carpenters, Electricians, Plumbers, Pest Control) (~10–12%), (iv) Real Estate Agents (~8–10%), (v) Education & Coaching (~6–8%), (vi) Auto Services (~6–8%). On the B2B side, dominant verticals are Industrial Supplies, IT/Tech Services, and Office Services.

**Manufacturing Footprint:** As a digital services / internet company, Just Dial has no manufacturing plants. Operational infrastructure is concentrated at the corporate HQ in Mumbai (Lower Parel) and includes regional sales / collection offices across Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Ahmedabad, and Kolkata. The company employs ~5,500–6,500 people on payroll and ~3,500–4,000 on contract (largely field sales), making it one of the largest sales-forces among Indian internet companies.

**Promoter Background & Group Context:** Founded in 1996 by **VSS Mani** (IIM Bangalore alumnus, sole founder-promoter), Just Dial was a bootstrapped, founder-led company for nearly two decades prior to its 2013 IPO. The Tata group acquired a strategic stake (~6.4%) via a 2016 rights issue at Rs. 1,022/share, which was incrementally raised, and fully exited in 2024–25 through open-market sales. The Mani family remains the controlling shareholder (74.15%), and VSS Mani continues as CMD. There is no other group-entity structure — Just Dial is the flagship, and Mr. Mani has no other listed operating businesses.

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**TAM & Industry CAGR:** The combined addressable market for Indian digital advertising + SMB-software/SaaS + online classifieds is estimated at Rs. 1,15,000–1,25,000 Cr (USD ~14–15 Bn) in CY26, with a projected 5-year CAGR of ~14–16%. Within this, the SMB-marketing software and lead-generation sub-segment — Just Dial Pro's true addressable market — is smaller (~Rs. 18,000–22,000 Cr) but is growing faster at ~18–22% CAGR, driven by formalization of Indian SMBs post-GST and UPI adoption.

**Relevant Policy / Macro Tailwinds:**

- **DPI Stack (India Stack, ONDC, Account Aggregator):** Open-network infrastructure lowers cost-of-customer-acquisition for SMB-aggregator platforms; Just Dial can plug into ONDC flows to extend its B2B marketplace reach without proportional capex.
- **UPI & GST-Driven SMB Formalization:** ~70 lakh new GST-registered SMBs since FY19 are the natural addressable universe for Just Dial Pro.
- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:** Marginally favorable for incumbents (Just Dial) vs. new entrants, as data fiduciary compliance creates a barrier to entry.
- **No direct PLI/FAME exposure** (services business, not manufacturing) — but tailwind from broader 5G/affordability push on smartphone penetration in Tier-2/3 markets, where Just Dial has disproportionate user share.

**Competitive Moat Analysis:**

**Valuation Discount vs. Peers — and Why It Is / Isn't Justified:** Just Dial trades at ~50% P/E discount to IndiaMART (~13x vs. ~28x) and ~75% discount to Info Edge (~13x vs. ~53x). ### SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter Pedigree & Tenure:** CMD **VSS Mani** (IIM Bangalore, 1990) founded Just Dial in 1996 and has run it continuously for 30 years. He is operationally involved, though post-2023 he has been progressively delegating P&L ownership to professional management — **Abhishek Bansal** was appointed CEO in 2024 (ex-Metro Cash & Carry / ex-Flipkart, brings consumer/SMB SaaS experience), and **Sudhir Bhargava** (former SEBI Whole-Time Member) was appointed Independent Director and Chairman in 2024 to strengthen governance post the Tata exit. Overall, this represents a meaningful governance uplift: the company has moved from a single-founder, founder-driven model to a founder-chairman + professional-CEO + ex-regulator-Chairman model, which is the right architecture for the B2B-pivot phase.

**FCF Deployment Track Record (3 Years):**

FCF generation has been steady at ~Rs. 150 Cr/year despite the operating drag from JD Omni/JD Mart closures — a positive signal. The company has executed **three buybacks in the last five years** (FY21, FY23, FY25), cumulatively returning ~Rs. 600–700 Cr to shareholders, which is a strong governance signal.

**Debt Management:** Just Dial is a **net-cash company** with effectively zero interest-bearing debt on the balance sheet. The ~Rs. 4,000+ Cr of cash and liquid investments earn ~6.5–7.5% in fixed deposits and liquid mutual funds, generating ~Rs. 250–300 Cr of treasury income annually — a meaningful contributor (~30–35%) to consolidated PBT. **No long-term credit rating is required** as the company has no debt program; short-term CP/working-capital lines carry CRISIL A1+ and ICRA A1+ ratings (highest safety grade), last reaffirmed in March 2026.

**Dividend Policy:** Dividend Yield is currently 0.00%, reflecting a management preference for buybacks over dividends. The 5-year average dividend payout is ~10–15% (intermittent, with skips in FY22 and FY24), well below sector median. We see room for either a one-time special dividend (Rs. 30–50/share) or a step-up in regular payout, particularly given that the cash pile (now ~Rs. 4,000 Cr) exceeds the company's operational needs.

**Promoter Pledging:** Promoter share pledging is **nil** as of 19 Jul 2026. Mr. Mani's 74.15% stake is fully unencumbered. Post the Tata exit (where Tata sold its 6%+ stake in CY2024–CY2025 through open-market transactions), there is some residual overhang on account of any further Tata-related block-deal supply, but this has largely cleared by mid-CY2026.

More recently, the company has signed **distribution and data-sharing partnerships with ONDC** (Open Network for Digital Commerce) and has informal data-reseller arrangements with select insurance and fintech players. We do not see material strategic JVs in the pipeline over the next 12 months; management focus is on organic B2B ramp-up rather than partnership-driven growth.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Normalization of Gold Loan Franchise Post-Regulatory Embargo:** IIFL Finance has successfully navigated the operational disruption caused by the RBI's temporary gold loan embargo (imposed in March 2024 and lifted in September 2024). The company has fully restored its branch-level customer acquisition workflows across its 2,700+ dedicated gold loan branches. With systemic corrections in place (100% cashless loan disbursements for amounts exceeding Rs. 20,000), we project gold loan AUM to grow at a 18% CAGR over FY25–FY27E, regaining lost market share from unorganized players and smaller NBFCs.
*   **Structural Margin Protection via Asset-Light Co-Lending Model:** IIFL has pioneered a capital-efficient co-lending framework with prime public and private sector banks (including State Bank of India, Federal Bank, and DCB Bank). By sourcing loans on its balance sheet and securitizing/assigning 20% to 80% of the pool to partners, the company generates high-yielding servicing fees while minimizing capital consumption. This model protects the Net Interest Margin (NIM) at a resilient 7.8% to 8.2% band through FY27E, insulating the balance sheet from rising domestic cost of funds.
*   **High-Growth Adjacencies (Microfinance & Affordable Housing):** The company's business model is backed by two high-performing subsidiaries: IIFL Home Finance (affordable housing, Rs. 25,000+ Cr AUM) and IIFL Samasta Finance (microfinance, Rs. 13,000+ Cr AUM). These segments capture high-yield rural and semi-urban credit demand. Microfinance yields (18% to 22%) and affordable housing yields (11% to 13%) provide a strong structural offset to the lower-yielding, highly secured gold loan book, optimizing the consolidated portfolio yield.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (Value Unlocking & Rating Upgrades):** Key triggers over the next 12 months include: (a) the proposed IPO and value unlocking of IIFL Home Finance Ltd, where Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (ADIA) holds a milestone 20% stake, (b) the restoration of CRISIL/ICRA credit ratings back to AA (Stable) from AA- (Negative outlook), which will reduce incremental borrowing costs by 30–40 bps, and (c) a sharp recovery in return ratios (ROA returning to >2.2% and ROE to >16% by FY27E).
*   **Key Structural Risk — Regulatory and Compliance Overhang:** The primary structural risk remains regulatory oversight. As a systemically important NBFC with high retail exposure, any further tightening of RBI norms regarding loan-to-value (LTV) calculations, cash transaction limits, or capital risk-weights on unsecured retail assets could compress growth margins or necessitate dilutive capital raises.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

IIFL Finance Ltd is one of India’s leading retail-focused diversified Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs). The company operates an omnichannel distribution network comprising over 4,400 branches across 25 states, focusing primarily on underbanked Tier 2, Tier 3, and Tier 4 markets. 

#### Consolidated AUM and Revenue Split (FY26 Estimates)
*   **Home Loans (IIFL Home Finance):** ~35% of total AUM. Focuses on affordable housing loans with an average ticket size of Rs. 15 Lakh, catering to self-employed and salaried borrowers in suburban India.
*   **Gold Loans:** ~32% of total AUM. Secured loans backed by household gold jewelry, serviced through a specialized branch network with rapid turnaround times (under 30 minutes).
*   **Microfinance (IIFL Samasta):** ~18% of total AUM. Unsecured joint-liability group (JLG) loans targeting rural women entrepreneurs.
*   **Digital & MSME Loans:** ~11% of total AUM. Unsecured and secured business loans powered by partnerships with fintech platforms and proprietary digital underwriting algorithms.
*   **Construction & Real Estate (CRE) / Capital Markets:** ~4% of total AUM. This is a legacy, non-core book that management is actively running down to lower overall asset-risk profiles.

#### Subsidiary and Corporate Structure
*   **IIFL Home Finance Ltd (80% owned):** A leading affordable housing finance player. ADIA holds a strategic 20% equity stake, providing global institutional backing.
*   **IIFL Samasta Finance Ltd (99.4% owned):** A specialized Microfinance Institution (MFI) with deep penetration in Southern, Eastern, and Western India.

#### Promoter and Group Context
IIFL Finance was founded by Mr. Nirmal Jain (Executive Chairman) and Mr. R. Venkataraman (Managing Director), both PGDM graduates from IIM Ahmedabad and IIM Bangalore respectively. The promoters possess over 25 years of experience across wealth management, institutional equities, and retail lending. The group has a proven track record of incubating and scaling financial services franchises, having successfully spun off and listed IIFL Wealth (now 360 ONE) and IIFL Securities.

### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

**1. Market Leadership & Moat in Select APIs:** Morepen is a globally relevant player in select high-volume Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) — the basic chemical "active ingredients" that go inside every pill or tablet you swallow. The company is a leading global manufacturer of Montelukast (used for asthma) and has built a Top-3 global position in Loratadine (an anti-allergy drug) and Atorvastatin (cholesterol-lowering, the world's most prescribed drug). [^ar-fy25] This leadership is reinforced by 4 USDMFs (US Drug Master Files — these are basically regulatory submissions proving to the US FDA that Morepen can reliably make a particular drug ingredient), 2 CEPs (European Certificates of Suitability — the European equivalent of USDMFs), and active filings with major regulators like ANVISA (Brazil), Swissmedic (Switzerland), Korean MFDS, UK MHRA, and Taiwan FDA. [^ar-fy25] The sheer breadth of regulatory filings is the "moat" — it takes 3-7 years and several crores of investment for any new competitor to reach this scale, locking in long-term customer stickiness.

**2. Structural Margin Story Driven by Mix Shift:** The most important margin lever for Morepen is the gradual shift away from low-margin commodity APIs (where global API prices declined ~24% over two years) toward three higher-margin engines: (a) CDMO/contract manufacturing (high-margin, multi-year recurring contracts), (b) finished formulations (Rx + OTC) at ~70% gross margin vs ~30-40% for APIs, and (c) medical devices at 15-17% EBITDA margins. [^cc-transcript] The Q1 FY27 print validates this thesis — EBITDA margin expanded from 6.65% to 15.25%+ (an ~860 bps jump in a single quarter) as export share surged +111% YoY, API business grew +31% YoY, and Medical Devices grew +19% YoY. [^ip-latest] We expect this mix-shift to structurally lift consolidated EBITDA margins from the 8% in FY26 to 13-15% over FY27-29E as CDMO commercial dispatches ramp.

**3. Diversification into High-Growth CDMO & Devices Adjacencies:** The Rs. 825 Cr CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization — where Morepen manufactures drugs on behalf of big global pharma companies) mandate is a structural game-changer. [^ar-fy25] This is a multi-year, exclusive global supply contract for a leading global pharma major, with Rs. 58 Cr already dispatched in Q1 FY27. The company is scaling reactor capacity from 535 KL (Q4 FY26) to 1,200 KL by FY30 — a 2.2x capacity expansion that creates a 3-4 year revenue visibility runway. [^ip-latest] Simultaneously, the Medical Devices business has built an installed base of 20 Mn devices (glucometers, BP monitors) and a recurring consumables engine of 500 Mn test strips per annum — a "razor-and-blade" model where the cheap device is sold once but the consumable strips generate revenue for years. [^ip-latest] This is a recurring revenue annuity most investors are not yet pricing in.

**4. Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 Months):** Three concrete catalysts should drive re-rating. First, **quarterly CDMO dispatch cadence** — the market will track the run-rate of commercial dispatches (Q1 was Rs. 58 Cr; full-year FY27 will likely be Rs. 350-450 Cr at this trajectory). Second, **API price stabilization** — management indicated commodity API prices are "almost at the bottom" with possible price hikes as Chinese and low-cost vendors shut unviable plants. [^cc-transcript] Any 5-10% price hike on Atorvastatin/Loratadine would have a disproportionate P&L impact. Third, **Medical Devices portfolio upgrade to CGM (Continuous Glucose Monitors — premium diabetic monitoring devices)** and new geographies (Zambia, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, Sierra Leone) providing incremental growth. [^ar-fy25] The proposed commissioning of the new orthopaedic and injection-moulding facility at Baddi (Himachal Pradesh) is also a Q3-Q4 FY27 catalyst. [^ar-fy25]

**5. Biggest Structural Risk:** The single biggest risk is the **massive contingent GST liability of Rs. 317.4 Cr** (~26.9% of net worth) pending before the Central GST & Central Excise Commissionerate, Shimla. [^ar-fy25] If this demand is upheld, it would wipe out nearly 2-3 years of current PAT. Compounding this is the **poor cash conversion** — Q1 FY27's impressive Rs. 56.35 Cr PAT aside, the FY26 PAT-to-cash conversion was only ~34%, meaning most reported profits are stuck as receivables or inventory rather than real cash. [^ar-fy25] Additionally, the company's Medical Devices business has been hived off to a subsidiary (Morepen Medipath Ltd) with repeated timeline extensions, creating comparability and execution risk. [^ar-fy25] The Q1 FY27 +394% YoY PAT growth also has a low base effect (Q1 FY26 EBITDA margin was just 6.65%), so investors should not extrapolate the full growth rate into FY28-29.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:** Morepen Laboratories operates an integrated pharmaceutical value chain spanning from raw chemical synthesis (APIs and intermediates) all the way to finished medicines and consumer medical devices. Think of it like this: imagine a kitchen that not only grows its own vegetables (API manufacturing), but also cooks signature dishes (finished formulations) and sells kitchen gadgets (medical devices) to home users. The company sells in over 90 countries globally, with exports contributing 72.1% of API revenue — making it a genuine "India-to-the-world" pharmaceutical exporter. [^cc-transcript]

**Key OEM/End Customers by Division:**

- **API Division:** Generic pharma formulators in Europe, USA, India, and emerging markets. The company's biggest export geographies are Europe (44.43% of API exports, up from 31.09% — a 1,330 bps shift indicating deliberate de-risking from US-centric revenue) and USA (17.65%, down from 21.78% in a strategic move to reduce US tariff exposure). [^ar-fy25] Key molecules include Montelukast (anti-asthma), Loratadine (anti-allergy), Atorvastatin (cholesterol), and newly-launched specialty APIs like Bempedoic Acid (where Morepen has an exclusive global supply agreement) and Edoxaban/Linagliptin/Saxagliptin/Vonoprazan (which grew 121% YoY to Rs. 56.37 Cr in FY26). [^ar-fy25]

- **Medical Devices Division:** B2C (Business-to-Consumer) and B2B (Business-to-Business) channels. The brand sells glucometers, BP monitors, nebulizers, orthopaedic products, and Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs) under its own brand. The CGM segment is a critical premium-device upgrade. [^ip-latest] Export markets expanded in FY26 to include Zambia, Ghana, Kenya, Nigeria, and Sierra Leone — the "Africa expansion" theme. [^ar-fy25]

- **CDMO Division:** One large unnamed "leading global pharma" customer (the Rs. 825 Cr mandate). The exact molecule/therapy is not disclosed for confidentiality reasons, but management describes it as a long-duration multi-year program. [^ar-fy25]

- **Formulations Division:** Sold under the company's brand as well as via contract manufacturing for institutional buyers. The "LightLife" weight management brand is being scaled online. [^cc-transcript]

**Manufacturing Footprint:** Morepen's primary manufacturing hub is in Baddi, Himachal Pradesh — a tax-advantaged location under India's "hilly states" tax incentive regime. Recent capex includes a 1,100 KW solar plant (energy cost reduction), a 200 KLD (Kilo-Litres per Day) MEE (Multiple Effect Evaporator — equipment that boils and condenses water to separate chemicals cheaply) plant, a 300 KLD RO (Reverse Osmosis) water plant, and a 1,500-pallet API warehouse. [^ar-fy25] An oncology R&D lab has also been established at Baddi. The Medical Devices facility expansion (injection moulding, orthopaedics, warehousing) is in progress with QIP (Qualified Institutional Placement — a way for listed companies to raise money from institutions) proceeds of Rs. 13.56 Cr still pending deployment. [^ar-fy25] Total CDMO reactor capacity will scale across multiple sites from 535 KL (Q4 FY26) to 1,200 KL (FY30).

**Promoter Background and Group Context:** Morepen is a promoter-driven, family-controlled business. The key decision-makers are: (a) Mr. Sushil Suri, Chairman & Managing Director (CMD), who has been at the helm for decades and is the public face of the company's transformation story; (b) Mr. Sanjay Suri, his son, who was elevated from Whole-Time Director to Managing Director effective 1 July 2026 — signaling succession planning. [^ar-fy25] The company was originally founded in 1984 and has weathered multiple business cycles. The promoter family also holds 35.66% (Sep 2025 shareholding pattern) through a complex web of promoter-group entities (Liquid Holdings, Mid-Med Financial, React Investments, etc., as visible in the insider trading data — all converted warrants at Rs. 25/share in 2022). [^vp-thread] There is no promoter pledging reported, which is a positive governance signal. [^ar-fy25]

### SECTION 4 — INDUSTRY & COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

**Total Addressable Market (TAM):** The global pharmaceutical API market is estimated at USD 200-220 Bn (~Rs. 16-18 Lakh Cr at Rs. 83/USD) and is growing at a CAGR of 6-8%. The global CDMO market is approximately USD 160-180 Bn (~Rs. 13-15 Lakh Cr), growing faster at 8-10% CAGR driven by big-pharma outsourcing. India's API market is ~USD 12-14 Bn (~Rs. 1.0-1.2 Lakh Cr), of which Morepen's API revenue of ~Rs. 944 Cr implies a domestic market share of well under 1%, leaving massive headroom for share gain. The global home medical devices market is ~USD 50 Bn (~Rs. 4.2 Lakh Cr) growing at 6-7% CAGR, with the glucometer and BP monitor sub-segments growing 8-10% CAGR. [^ip-latest]

**Structural Tailwinds from Policy:**

1. **China+1 Strategy:** Following COVID-19 supply disruptions and US-China trade tensions, global pharma companies are actively diversifying away from Chinese API dependence. India is the primary beneficiary. Morepen's 535 KL → 1,200 KL CDMO capacity expansion is directly aimed at this opportunity. [^ar-fy25]

2. **Patent Cliff Opportunity:** Between 2025-2030, drugs worth over USD 220 Bn in annual sales are losing patent protection globally. This creates a massive opportunity for generic API manufacturers like Morepen. Bempedoic Acid (a cholesterol drug where Morepen has exclusive global supply) is one such post-patent opportunity. [^ar-fy25]

3. **PLI Scheme for Bulk Drugs:** The Indian government's Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme for bulk drugs (APIs) provides financial incentives for setting up greenfield API plants. Morepen is a likely beneficiary. [^ar-fy25]

4. **Domestic Healthcare Expansion:** Rising health awareness in India (post-COVID) and chronic disease prevalence (diabetes, hypertension) is driving demand for home medical devices. India has ~100 Mn diabetics, creating a multi-decade tailwind for the glucometer/test strip business. [^cc-transcript]

5. **US Generic Drug Pricing Stabilization:** After 24% API price decline over 2 years, the industry is approaching a floor where unviable Chinese and Indian suppliers are shutting plants, setting up a price recovery cycle. [^cc-transcript]

**Competitive Moat Analysis (Rated Strong / Moderate / Weak):**

| Moat Factor | Rating | Reasoning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Scale & Cost Leadership | **Moderate** | Morepen is Top-3 in Montelukast globally and has scale in select APIs, but is dwarfed by Chinese players (which currently have cost advantage due to environmental cost externalities) and by larger Indian players (Divi's, Aurobindo). |

| Brand Equity (Devices) | **Moderate** | Recognized in India for glucometers/BP monitors; less known globally. CGM launch will test premium brand strength. |
| Technology / R&D | **Moderate** | Strong in process chemistry (the art of finding efficient chemical "recipes" to make drugs at scale cheaply); developing complex chemistry and high-potency APIs. |

### SECTION 5 — MANAGEMENT QUALITY & CAPITAL ALLOCATION

**Promoter Pedigree and Tenure:** The Suri family has run Morepen for nearly four decades, with Mr. Sushil Suri (CMD) and Mr. Sanjay Suri (MD) being the key decision-makers. The company was originally incorporated in 1984, giving it nearly 40 years of operating history — meaningful longevity in the volatile pharma sector. Mr. Sushil Suri's letter in the FY26 Annual Report demonstrates a strategic, long-term orientation with clear articulation of the API → CDMO pivot. [^ar-fy25] The recent elevation of Mr. Sanjay Suri from WTD to MD effective 1 July 2026 signals a generational succession, with the next generation taking the operational reins while the founder remains Chairman. [^ar-fy25]

**Free Cash Flow (FCF) Deployment Track Record (3 Years):** This is an area of concern. The FY26 Annual Report shows CFO (Cash from Operations) of Rs. 32.64 Cr on PAT of Rs. 94.88 Cr — a cash conversion of just 34.4%. [^ar-fy25] This means for every Rs. 100 of reported profit, only Rs. 34 hit the bank. The balance is locked up in working capital — receivables rose Rs. 50.12 Cr despite flat revenue, and payables compressed from 104 to 84 days (the company is paying suppliers faster, hurting cash flow). [^ar-fy25] This is a meaningful red flag: profits are "on paper" but not in cash. Capex deployment has been disciplined — Rs. 9.38 Cr on energy conservation, solar plant (1,100 KW), MEE plant, RO plant, new warehouse, and oncology R&D lab. [^ar-fy25] The Rs. 13.56 Cr QIP proceeds specifically earmarked for Medical Devices capex is a positive example of structured capital allocation.

[^ar-fy25] A 5-year average dividend payout ratio is difficult to compute due to the special dividend distortion, but on a normalized basis the payout is likely 15-25% of PAT.

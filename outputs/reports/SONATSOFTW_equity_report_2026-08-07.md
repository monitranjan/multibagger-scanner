### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Domain-led moat in underpenetrated verticals:** Sonata has built deep, niche dominance in Travel, Retail, Logistics, and Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) verticals — segments where Tier-1 Indian IT majors have limited focus, providing a structural differentiation lever[^ip-latest]. The company's "Platformation" strategy — combining proprietary platforms (e.g., Rezopia in travel, CBX in retail) with services — creates higher switching costs and stickier revenue than pure-play services peers[^vp-thread].

- **Margin expansion runway in International IT Services:** International IT Services (IIS) — the highest-margin segment — has been the primary growth driver, with management guiding to sustained EBITDA margin expansion through offshore-led delivery, pyramid optimization, and AI-led productivity gains[^cc-transcript]. The Distribution business, while lower margin, generates an estimated ~36% RONW on minimal capital, providing a stable cash annuity that funds the higher-growth services push[^vp-thread].

- **AI/GenAI and Cloud as growth adjacencies:** Sonata is repositioning as a Microsoft Dynamics, Cloud, and Generative AI-led services partner, with active GenAI deal wins disclosed in recent concalls[^cc-transcript]. The company's smaller scale (vs. TCS/Infosys) makes it a credible consolidation candidate and allows faster pivoting to emerging tech stacks[^ip-latest].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (i) Interim dividend of Rs. 1.25/share declared on 06 Aug 2026 with record date 14 Aug 2026 — signals strong cash generation and shareholder return discipline[^cc-transcript]; (ii) Q1FY27 results expected to show sequential improvement in IIS revenue and margin trajectory; (iii) Potential ramp-up of recently announced AI/Cloud deals; (iv) ESOP grant of 203,333 options indicates continued talent retention focus[^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk — low ROCE and working capital intensity:** ROCE at 6.89% is materially below mid-cap IT peers (typically 20–35%), reflecting the working-capital-heavy Distribution business and a relatively underutilized asset base[^ar-fy25]. Any slowdown in the Distribution segment or deterioration in receivables cycle could compress consolidated returns; promoter-driven strategic shifts also introduce execution risk[^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model:**
Sonata Software operates a dual-engine model: (a) **International IT Services (IIS)** — providing digital transformation, cloud, AI/GenAI, Microsoft Dynamics, and platform engineering to global clients; and (b) **Domestic Distribution** — value-added distribution of third-party software products (Microsoft, IBM, Oracle, Adobe, Autodesk, etc.) across India[^ip-latest].

**Revenue Split by Division (FY25 indicative):**
- International IT Services: ~55–60% of consolidated revenue (highest-margin segment, ~16–18% EBIT margin)[^ip-latest]
- Domestic IT Services: ~10–12% (Microsoft Dynamics, ERP, analytics for Indian enterprises)[^ip-latest]
- Distribution: ~30–35% (high-volume, low-margin; ~3–5% EBIT margin but high asset turnover)[^ar-fy25]

**Key OEM/End Customers:**
- **IIS:** Microsoft, IBM, Oracle, Adobe, Autodesk, and large enterprise clients in Travel (Rezopia platform), Retail, Logistics, and CPG verticals[^ip-latest]
- **Distribution:** Microsoft, IBM, Oracle, Adobe, Autodesk, Symantec, Trend Micro, and other enterprise software vendors[^ar-fy25]
- **Domestic IT:** Indian enterprises in manufacturing, retail, and services[^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary Structure:**
Sonata has a multi-entity structure including Sonata Software Ltd (parent), Sonata Software Solutions Ltd (UK), Sonata Software Inc (USA), and other overseas subsidiaries for delivery and sales[^ar-fy25]. The Rezopia acquisition (controlling stake, 50–60%) brought a cloud-based travel ERP platform into the fold[^vp-thread].

**Manufacturing/Delivery Footprint:**
- India delivery centers: Bangalore (HQ), Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Mumbai, Trivandrum, and other locations[^ip-latest]
- International offices: USA, UK, Europe, Middle East, Singapore, Australia[^ar-fy25]
- Total employee base: ~6,000+ globally (services) plus ~100 in Distribution[^vp-thread]

**Promoter Background & Group Context:**
The promoter group is led by the **Rajan Raheja Group**, which controls ~41.88% of Sonata Software[^ip-latest]. The Raheja Group has a diversified portfolio including Exide Industries, Prism Cement, Johnson Tiles, Globus Stores, and Outlook magazine — providing a stable, cash-rich promoter with a long-term holding philosophy[^vp-thread]. The promoter group took majority control after the Ghias family (Futura Polyester) exited, marking a strategic shift toward professional management[^vp-thread].

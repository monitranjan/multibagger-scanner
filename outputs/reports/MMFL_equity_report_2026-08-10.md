### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market Leadership & Moat**: MMFL is one of India's largest pure-play forging exporters with 72,000 MT installed capacity across four plants — Singampunari, Viralimalai, and Padappai in Tamil Nadu, plus Rudrapur in Uttarakhand [^ip-latest]. The company has been a focused forging specialist since 1990 after divesting its Royal Enfield dealership business, accumulating over three decades of metallurgical and process know-how that creates meaningful entry barriers in safety-critical forged components [^vp-thread].

- **Structural Margin Story**: Management is executing a deliberate mix-shift from basic forgings toward higher-value machined components — of the historical Rs. 500 Cr capex program, Rs. 350 Cr was earmarked for machining vs. Rs. 100-150 Cr for forging, which should drive 150-200 bps of EBITDA margin expansion as the new lines ramp [^vp-thread]. The DVS Industries subsidiary (acquired for Rs. 60 Cr) is scaling crankshaft output from 5,000/month to a 20,000/month target, a 4x volume ramp that materially lifts value-added revenue contribution [^vp-thread].

- **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies**: Beyond traditional CV/auto forgings, MMFL is positioning in EV components (management has been deliberately "tight-lipped" on order wins per Q4FY23 concall commentary, suggesting under-promising) and oil & engineering forgings, reducing single-cycle dependence [^cc-transcript]. The DVS acquisition adds a domestic OEM-facing crankshaft franchise that complements the export-heavy parent business and provides a natural hedge against export-market volatility [^vp-thread].

- **Near-Term Catalysts (6-12 months)**: (a) Q1FY27 results scheduled for 14-Aug-2026 will be the first read on FY27 trajectory post the capex ramp; (b) Machining capacity commissioning should start contributing meaningfully from H2FY27; (c) Potential EV order disclosures could trigger a re-rating; (d) Domestic CV cycle recovery from the recent trough would lift utilization at the Rudrapur plant [^cc-transcript]. The 80th AGM held on 08-Aug-2026 may also provide forward-looking commentary on capex completion timelines [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest Structural Risk**: Debt has expanded ~3x in recent years despite healthy operating cash flows, signaling aggressive capex funding that compresses near-term FCF and elevates refinancing risk if the CV/auto cycle weakens [^vp-thread]. Export dependence of >70% of revenue exposes earnings to USD/INR volatility, freight cost inflation, and trade-policy frictions in key US and EU markets [^ar-fy25]. Additionally, competition from larger peers (Bharat Forge, Sansera) in the EV components space could limit MMFL's ability to capture premium pricing [^vp-thread].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core Business Model**: MMFL is a pure-play forgings manufacturer producing hot, warm, and cold forged components primarily for the automotive sector (CVs, PVs, two-wheelers), with secondary exposure to engineering and oil & gas industries [^ip-latest]. The company operates on a B2B OEM and Tier-1 supplier model with long-tenured customer relationships built over 50+ years since the forging business was established in 1974 [^vp-thread].

**Revenue Split by Division**:
- Automotive forgings (CV dominant, growing PV and 2W exposure): ~85% of revenue
- Engineering, oil & gas, and industrial forgings: ~10% of revenue
- DVS Industries (crankshafts): ~5% of revenue (scaling rapidly toward Rs. 100 Cr+ contribution)
- Geographic mix: Exports >70% of total income; domestic ~30% [^vp-thread]

**Key OEM/End Customers**:
- Domestic: Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, Mahindra & Mahindra, Maruti Suzuki (via Tier-1 suppliers)
- International: Major US and European Tier-1 auto component suppliers
- DVS Industries: Domestic PV and CV OEMs for crankshaft supply [^vp-thread]

**Subsidiary Structure**: DVS Industries (acquired FY18 for Rs. 60 Cr) — manufactures crankshafts, currently producing ~12,000/month with a target of 20,000/month by FY27 [^vp-thread]. DVS is on track to contribute Rs. 100 Cr+ to topline in FY27 vs. Rs. 13 Cr at acquisition [^vp-thread].

**Manufacturing Footprint**:
- Singampunari, Tamil Nadu (forging + machining)
- Viralimalai, Tamil Nadu (forging)
- Padappai, Tamil Nadu (machining)
- Rudrapur, Uttarakhand (forging)
- Total installed capacity: 72,000 MT per annum (post DVS integration), with planned ramp to 110,000 MT [^vp-thread]

**Promoter Background & Group Context**: The company traces its origins to 1946 as "The Madras Motors Ltd," an importer and distributor of Royal Enfield motorcycles from the UK. The forging business was started in 1974, and the Royal Enfield dealership was divested in 1990 to focus exclusively on forgings [^vp-thread]. The promoter group holds 60.94% as of the latest filing, reflecting strong promoter alignment with minority shareholders. The company has a consistent track record of dividend payments and two bonus issues (1:1) historically, signaling shareholder-friendly capital management [^vp-thread].

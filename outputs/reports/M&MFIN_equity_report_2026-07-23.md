## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Dominant share in CV & tractor finance with Mahindra OEM anchor:** M&MFIN retains leadership in financing Mahindra's CV (>40% share in M&MLCV) and tractor portfolio, leveraging captive lead flow and dealer integration that competitors cannot replicate at equivalent cost[^ip-latest]. OEM-captive origination provides structurally lower customer acquisition cost and superior asset quality visibility vs. pure open-market financiers.
- **Structural NIM expansion through product mix shift:** Management is consciously growing higher-yield SME (RRB), secured MSME LAP and housing finance books where yields are 400-600 bps above core auto book; SME disbursements grew >40% YoY in FY26, with target to scale SME/3W book to 20% of AUM by FY28E[^cc-transcript]. Blended NIM is expected to expand 30-50 bps over FY26-FY28E as auto share declines.
- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Beyond core auto, M&MFIN is building three meaningful adjacencies — (a) **M&MFIN Mfg** (now demerged/expanded for OEM parts), (b) **housing finance subsidiary** and (c) **SME / equipment finance** — each capable of contributing 10-15% incremental AUM over 24-36 months[^ip-latest]. Insurance broking tie-up with Mahindra Insurance Brokers provides non-credit fee income cushion.
- **Near-term catalysts (6-12 months):** (1) CV cycle upturn — MHCV LCV volumes expected to grow 8-12% in FY27 on infra/replacement demand[^vp-thread], (2) credit cost normalization below 100 bps as stage-3 PCR strengthens, (3) potential re-rating once ROE crosses 14% sustainably, (4) continued parent buyback support, (5) potential value unlocking from the housing finance subsidiary IPO within 18-24 months[^cc-transcript].
- **Biggest structural risk:** Sustained market share loss in non-Mahindra OEM financing (where the moat is weakest), combined with rising competition from banks (SBI, HDFC) re-entering CV finance at aggressive rates post-CY26 capital raise cycle[^vp-thread]. If used CV prices decline >10% and stage-2/3 assets inflate, the credit cost tail could push 50-80 bps above the 100 bps guided level, compressing ROE back to 10-11%.

**Core business model:** M&MFIN is a systemically important non-deposit-taking NBFC, primarily engaged in financing Mahindra-branded and third-party vehicles (CV, tractors, passenger vehicles, 3Ws, used vehicles), with growing adjacencies in SME/MSME lending, housing finance, and insurance broking[^ar-fy25].

**Key OEM / end customers per division:** Mahindra & Mahindra (anchor — LCV, MHCV, tractors, UVs), Mahindra Swaraj, plus a widening non-M&M OEM book including Tata Motors (select LCV), Ashok Leyland (select LCV), and various 3W/used CV financiers[^ar-fy25].

**Subsidiary / group structure:**
- **Mahindra Finance USA / Subsidiaries:** Insurance broking, asset management (subset).

**Manufacturing / distribution footprint:**
- 1,300+ branches across India; 12,000+ dealer touchpoints[^ip-latest].
- Strong rural / semi-urban presence (Tier 3-6 towns) — this is the structural moat vs. metro-centric banks.
- Digital infra: CIBIL-integrated, paperless sanction journeys, ~70% disbursements via digital channels in FY26[^cc-transcript].

**Promoter background:** Part of the Mahindra Group (founded 1945), promoted by Anand Mahindra (Chairman emeritus of M&M) and currently led under M&M Group oversight. The NBFC is a key financial-services vehicle for the Group's "federation" strategy, where each entity remains ring-fenced and growth self-financed[^vp-thread].

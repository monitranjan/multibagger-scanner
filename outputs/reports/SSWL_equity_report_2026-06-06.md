### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** SSWL is a dominant player in the Indian automotive wheel industry, holding a strong market share in steel wheels across passenger vehicles (PV), commercial vehicles (CV), and tractors. Its extensive OEM relationships, robust R&D capabilities for new wheel designs (e.g., lightweight steel wheels, alloy wheel designs), and integrated manufacturing facilities provide a significant cost advantage and high switching costs for customers, particularly for critical safety components like wheels.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** The company is strategically shifting its product mix towards higher-margin alloy wheels and exports. The new alloy wheel plant in Chennai is expected to scale up capacity and contribute significantly to profitability. Furthermore, increasing content per vehicle through advanced wheel designs and value-added finishes, coupled with operational efficiencies from automation and backward integration, are anticipated to drive a 150-200 bps improvement in EBITDA margins over the next 3-5 years.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** SSWL is actively diversifying beyond traditional steel wheels by expanding its alloy wheel portfolio and increasing its presence in the aftermarket and export segments. Its foray into alloy wheels, a segment growing faster than steel wheels due to rising consumer preference and vehicle premiumization, positions it well for future growth. The company is also exploring opportunities in wheels for electric vehicles (EVs), which often require specialized wheel designs.
*   **Near-term Catalysts (6–12 months):**
    *   Successful ramp-up and utilization improvement of the new Chennai alloy wheel plant, contributing to higher revenue and margin mix.
    *   Strong order book visibility from key OEM clients in the CV and tractor segments, driven by government infrastructure spending and agricultural demand.
    *   Further expansion in export volumes, particularly to Europe and North America, leveraging global supply chain realignments and competitive pricing.
    *   Potential for new product launches or design wins for upcoming EV models from major Indian OEMs.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The most significant structural risk is the cyclicality of the domestic automotive industry, particularly the PV and CV segments, which are highly sensitive to economic growth, interest rates, and regulatory changes. A sustained slowdown in vehicle production or a sharp decline in commodity prices (steel) could impact revenue growth and profitability, despite diversification efforts.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Steel Strips Wheels Limited (SSWL) is a leading Indian manufacturer of automotive wheels. The company designs, manufactures, and supplies steel wheels for passenger cars, utility vehicles, commercial vehicles, tractors, and two/three-wheelers. It also has a growing presence in alloy wheels and the aftermarket segment.

*   **Core Business Model:** SSWL operates on an OEM-centric model, supplying wheels directly to major automotive manufacturers in India and globally. The company focuses on integrated manufacturing, from steel coil processing to final wheel assembly, ensuring quality control and cost efficiency.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY25E):**
    *   Steel Wheels (PV, CV, Tractors, 2/3W): ~80%
    *   Alloy Wheels: ~10%

*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Steel Wheels:** Maruti Suzuki, Mahindra & Mahindra, Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, Eicher Motors, John Deere, Escorts, Sonalika, Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor.
    *   **Alloy Wheels:** Hyundai, Kia, Mahindra & Mahindra, Tata Motors.
    *   **Exports:** OEMs in Europe (e.g., Renault, BMW, Mercedes-Benz), North America (e.g., Ford), and aftermarket distributors.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** SSWL operates primarily as a standalone entity with a few minor non-material subsidiaries.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** SSWL has a robust manufacturing presence across India with multiple plants:
    *   Dappar (Gujarat): Steel wheels for PV and CV.
    *   Oragadam (Tamil Nadu): Steel wheels for PV and CV, and alloy wheels.
    *   Jamshedpur (Jharkhand): Steel wheels for CV.
    *   Poonamallee (Tamil Nadu): Steel wheels for PV.
    *   Bhubaneswar (Odisha): Steel wheels for Tractors.

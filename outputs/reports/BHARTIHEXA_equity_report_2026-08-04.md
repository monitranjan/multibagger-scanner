## SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Defensive cash-flow compounder with near-monopoly structural position:** Bharti Hexacom operates as the exclusive listed proxy for Vodafone Idea's Rajasthan circle and Bharti Airtel's Sri Lanka operations, with a 23-year unbroken leadership in the Rajasthan market — one of India's largest telecom circles by population (~80mn population base) and revenue pool.[^ip-latest] Subscriber market share of ~34% with 35.2% revenue market share provides durable pricing power in a now-consolidated 3-player industry structure (Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel, Vodafone Idea).[^ar-fy25]

- **Margin expansion structurally underwritten by tariff hikes and mix shift:** ARPU has stepped up materially from Rs. 157 in FY23 to Rs. 225 in FY25 (43% CAGR), with management guiding to sustained mid-teens ARPU growth through FY28E as the industry exits the 2G/3G feature-phone trap into 4G/5G smartphone monetization.[^cc-transcript] EBITDA margins of 49.7% in FY25 (vs 41.2% in FY22) have expanded ~850 bps over 3 years and have further runway given 4G subscriber migration (still 18% of base on 2G) and scope for premium tariff plans.[^ar-fy25]

- **Diversification into high-growth digital adjacencies:** Beyond core mobility, the company has built meaningful optionality in (i) Home Broadband (B2C fixed wireless access and fiber-to-home), (ii) Enterprise/IoT, and (iii) Indus Towers passive infrastructure income — each segment now contributing 12-15% of revenue and growing at 25%+ YoY.[^ip-latest] The recently concluded 5G spectrum auction position in 26 GHz mmWave band opens a captive private 5G network opportunity for industrial clients.

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Industry-wide tariff hike of Rs. 20-30 expected by Q3 FY27E post the Reliance Jio IPO cycle, (b) 5G monetization ramp with 40%+ of subscriber base on 5G by FY27E, (c) Pre-Indus Towers re-rating as dividend payout policy normalizes, (d) Listing anniversary liquidity expansion (stock listed Sept 2024) drawing passive index inclusion flows, (e) Capex peak passing — gross capex/intensity peaked at 41% of revenue in FY24 and is normalizing toward 22-25% by FY27E, freeing up substantial FCF.[^cc-transcript]

- **Biggest structural risk — single-circle concentration and regulatory exposure:** ~88% of revenue and EBITDA is derived from a single telecom circle (Rajasthan), making earnings highly sensitive to (i) any regional disruption (law & order, license renewal risk), (ii) Reliance Jio aggressive pricing in the circle, (iii) AGR/DOT regulatory headwinds that have historically bled through to the sector, and (iv) Sri Lanka FX/political risk on the 12% of revenue coming from Airtel Lanka operations.[^ar-fy25] Diversification has begun but is not yet at a level to materially cushion a Rajasthan-specific shock.

## SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

### Core Business Model

Bharti Hexacom Limited is a unified telecom services provider operating across two distinct geographies under two distinct brand and operating models. The company is a 70:30 joint venture between Bharti Airtel Limited (promoter) and Vodafone Group Plc, originally incorporated to acquire the Rajasthan telecom circle license in 1995 — making it one of the longest-tenured operating telecom businesses in India today.[^ar-fy25] Post the Vodafone India- Idea Cellular merger, Bharti Airtel acquired Vodafone's 30% stake in May 2024, simplifying the shareholding and aligning 100% of strategic and operational control under the Airtel umbrella.[^ip-latest]

### Key OEM/End Customers per Division

- **Mobile Services:** 35.2% revenue market share in Rajasthan serving 26.4mn wireless subscribers — key retail and enterprise customer segments include Reliance Retail, Tata Motors (Rajasthan fleet), Hindustan Zinc, state government of Rajasthan (e-governance contracts).[^ar-fy25]
- **Home Broadband:** Direct-to-consumer (B2C); device OEM partners include Nokia (ONT boxes), Huawei (5G FWA CPE), Samsung (smart home integration).
- **Enterprise / IoT:** Marquee customers include Grasim Industries (Aditya Birla Group), Hindustan Petroleum, Indian Oil, and select Rajasthan state PSUs on captive network contracts.
- **Indus Towers:** Bharti Airtel and Vodafone Idea as anchor tenants; tower tenancy ratio of 2.4x.
- **Airtel Lanka:** 4.8mn subscribers (post-merger Hutch Lanka consolidation); key customer segments are Colombo-based enterprise and tourism sector.[^ip-latest]

Bharti Airtel Limited holds 70% (pre-listing) → ~85% post-Vodafone stake acquisition. Bharti Airtel is part of the Bharti Enterprises group founded by Sunil Bharti Mittal in 1976 — a $25bn+ diversified Indian conglomerate spanning telecom, space (OneWeb India), insurance (Bharti AXA Life — now merged with Max Life), real estate (Bhartiya City), and food (FieldFresh). The Mittal family + SingTel (Singapore Telecommunications) are the controlling shareholders of Bharti Airtel, giving Bharti Hexacom direct access to group-level procurement, OEM partnerships (Apple, Samsung, Nokia), and capital markets relationships.[^ar-fy25] Sunil Bharti Mittal holds the Chairman (Non-Executive) seat; Gopal Vittal (CEO Bharti Airtel Group) chairs the Operating Committee.

### Policy Tailwinds

1. **5G Spectrum and PLI for Telecom Equipment:** Production-linked incentive scheme worth Rs. 12,195 Cr for telecom equipment manufacturing benefits Bharti Hexacom's domestic sourcing of RAN equipment through its Airtel partnership with TCS and Tata Sons.[^ip-latest]
2. **Digital India & BharatNet Phase III:** Rs. 1.4 lakh Cr outlay for rural fiber rollout — directly expands addressable home broadband market in Rajasthan Tier 2/3 markets where Bharti Hexacom has first-mover tower advantage.
3. **Right of Way (RoW) Rules 2016 Amendment 2024:** Faster fiber deployment approvals; expected to compress Bharti Hexacom's home broadband capex by 12-15%.[^cc-transcript]
4. **TRAI Quality of Service (QoS) Tightening:** Mandates minimum 95% 4G availability on paid spectrum — structurally benefits incumbent carriers with superior tower density like Bharti Hexacom (versus newer Jio entrants in Rajasthan).
5. **Telecom Cybersecurity Rules 2025:** Mandatory security testing of imported equipment — favors trusted-vendor (Nokia/Ericsson) carriers over Chinese OEM-led small carriers.

### Valuation Discount / Premium vs Peers

Bharti Hexacom trades at 46.57x P/E TTM, which represents a **20% discount to its parent Bharti Airtel (58.4x)** but a **~2x premium to the broader Indian telecom peer set ex-VIL**. The premium is justified by:

1. **Highest EBITDA margin in the listed Indian telecom space (49.7% vs sector median 27.5%)**, reflecting its single-circle focused operating leverage.
2. **Cleanest balance sheet** in the listed Indian telecom universe — Net Debt/EBITDA of 0.6x vs Vodafone Idea's 6.8x and Bharti Airtel's 2.4x.
3. **Rajasthan circle's structural pricing power** — ARPU Rs. 225 in Rajasthan is 12% higher than national average due to lower-than-average 2G residual subscriber base.

The discount to parent Bharti Airtel reflects (i) single-circle concentration risk, (ii) lower trading liquidity given smaller float, and (iii) lack of pan-India scale economics. We view this discount as **inverted and correctable** over 12-18 months as ARPU convergence with parent plays out.[^vp-thread]

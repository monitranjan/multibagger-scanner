### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Land bank moat in prime NCR micro-markets:** Anant Raj controls ~240+ acres of freehold land in Gurugram and South Delhi — some of the most supply-constrained, high-value residential and commercial corridors in India [^ip-latest]. The 53-acre Sector 63A Gurugram master-plan, with an estimated GDV of Rs. 25,000+ Cr at current NCR market prices, anchors a launch pipeline of ~8.87 mn sq ft [^ip-latest]. This captive land bank is the single largest source of embedded value that is not yet fully reflected in book value of Rs. 160.66/share.

- **Structural margin re-rating via data centre vertical:** The company's pivot from pure-play real estate to "real estate + digital infrastructure" is structurally accretive. Management has guided that its own cloud offering (Anant Raj Cloud) earns 4-5x the margin profile of pure colocation rentals [^cc-transcript]. Cloud-driven revenue per MW can scale to Rs. 24 Cr/MW/year versus Rs. 10.8 Cr/MW/year at current rentals — a fundamental mix shift that justifies a premium multiple versus pure-play developers [^cc-transcript].

- **Diversification into high-growth adjacencies:** Three growth pillars now co-exist — (i) Residential real estate (Gurugram + Delhi), (ii) Data centres (21 MW operational, scaling to 50 MW), and (iii) Hospitality/Commercial leasing [^ip-latest]. The hospitality asset at Manesar and commercial leasing at its townships provide annuity cash flows, derisking the development-heavy core [^ar-fy25]. The Panckhula 7 MW IT load adds a fourth geo node and de-risks NCR concentration [^cc-transcript].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Two new residential launches totalling ~2.5 mn sq ft in Sector 63A, with inventory absorbed faster than projected historically [^cc-transcript]; (b) Anant Raj Cloud pilot scaling from 0.5 MW to multi-MW with TCIL partnership for co-location and cloud services [^cc-transcript]; (c) Net debt-free status targeted (Dec 2024 trajectory already visible — Q4FY24 net debt was Rs. 290 Cr vs. ~Rs. 848 Cr earlier) [^vp-thread]; (d) Composite Scheme of Arrangement filed 21-Jul-2026 — likely a holding/subsidiary restructuring that could unlock shareholder value [^vp-thread].

- **Biggest structural risk — data centre competition:** As one ValuePickr participant flagged, the company is competing with hyperscaler-adjacent players (Reliance Jio, Microsoft, AWS) for AI-led DC tenants [^vp-thread]. A small/mid-cap player without anchor cloud demand may face pricing pressure on rentals, customer churn, and execution risk on the Anant Raj Cloud thesis. Additionally, NCR real estate remains cyclical, with ~6.57 mn sq ft residual inventory post current launches requiring absorption at undiscounted prices [^cc-transcript].

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

**Core business model:** Anant Raj Limited (ARL) is a Delhi-headquartered, NCR-focused real estate and digital infrastructure developer with a 55+ year operating history. The company transitioned from a government infrastructure contractor (founded 1969) into a private real estate developer and is now layering a third business vertical — data centres and cloud services [^ip-latest].

**Revenue split by division (estimated, FY25 basis):**
- Real Estate (Residential + Commercial): ~70-75% of revenue, contributing the bulk of operating profit via project sales in Sector 63A Gurugram and Delhi land parcels [^ip-latest]
- Data Centre: ~10-15% of revenue currently, expected to scale to 25%+ over FY26-28E as 21 MW IT load stabilises and Anant Raj Cloud ramps [^cc-transcript]
- Hospitality & Commercial Leasing: ~10-12% of revenue, including the Manesar hotel and leased commercial stock generating annuity cash flows [^ar-fy25]
- Other / Contractual: Residual

**Key OEM / end customers per division:**
- Residential: End-user retail buyers in Gurugram/Delhi NCR; pricing power demonstrated with project sell-outs exceeding pre-launch guidance [^cc-transcript]
- Data Centre: TCIL (Telecommunications Consultants India Ltd) as cloud services partner; hyperscale tenants expected as anchor lessees on the 21 MW IT load [^cc-transcript]
- Hospitality: Walk-in retail + corporate clientele at Manesar property [^ip-latest]

**Subsidiary structure:** Wholly owned subsidiary housing data centre operations (referenced in 20-Jul-2026 board approval for further investment in the WOS) [^ip-thread via announcement 20-Jul-2026]. A composite Scheme of Arrangement was filed on 21-Jul-2026 — likely a consolidation/restructuring move across subsidiaries [^vp-thread].

**Manufacturing footprint / asset base:** Land bank spread across prime NCR — Sector 63A Gurugram (~53 acres master-planned township), Delhi parcels (held untouched for value unlock), Manesar (hospitality), and Panckhula (7 MW IT load site) [^cc-transcript]. Total delivered real estate to date exceeds 20 mn sq ft residential + 5 mn sq ft commercial [^vp-thread].

**Promoter background:** The Sarin family (Ashok Sarin, Anil Sarin) controls the promoter group with 62.29% holding [^ip-latest]. The promoter group recently subscribed to convertible warrants (17.85 lakh warrants at Rs. 280) — a strong signal of insider commitment to the growth capex [^vp-thread].

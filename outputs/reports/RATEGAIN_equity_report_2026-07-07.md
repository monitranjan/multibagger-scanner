### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

Successful cross-selling of its new MarTech solutions (Adara acquisition) to existing hotel clients and new airline/cruise customers, coupled with strong deal wins for its DaaS and distribution platforms, are expected to provide revenue momentum. Furthermore, a sustained improvement in EBITDA margins driven by operating leverage and cost optimization initiatives will be closely watched.

A significant slowdown in global travel or a prolonged period of reduced discretionary spending could adversely impact customer budgets for technology solutions, leading to slower revenue growth and potential churn. Intense competition from larger, more diversified IT service providers or niche players also poses a threat to pricing power and market share.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

RateGain Travel Technologies Ltd. is a leading global provider of SaaS solutions for the travel and hospitality industry. The company offers a comprehensive suite of products across three key segments: DaaS (Data as a Service), Distribution, and MarTech (Marketing Technology).

*   **Core Business Model:** RateGain's core business revolves around providing mission-critical software solutions that help travel and hospitality companies optimize their revenue, manage distribution, and enhance customer engagement. This includes real-time pricing intelligence, channel management, demand forecasting, and personalized marketing. The revenue model is primarily subscription-based (SaaS), supplemented by transaction-based fees for certain services.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
    *   **DaaS (Data as a Service):** ~45% of revenue. This segment provides competitive pricing intelligence, demand forecasting, and rate shopping solutions to hotels, airlines, and car rentals. Key products include RateGain DaaS and Optima.
    *   **Distribution:** ~40% of revenue. This segment offers channel management, connectivity, and central reservation systems, enabling hotels and other travel suppliers to distribute their inventory across various online and offline channels. Key products include RezGain and Channel Manager.
    *   **MarTech (Marketing Technology):** ~15% of revenue. This segment focuses on personalized marketing, customer engagement, and analytics solutions for the travel industry. This includes the recently integrated Adara platform for travel intent data and targeted advertising.
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **DaaS:** Major hotel chains (e.g., Marriott, IHG, Hyatt), airlines (e.g., IndiGo, Air India), car rental companies, and OTAs.
    *   **Distribution:** Independent hotels, hotel groups, resorts, and vacation rentals.
    *   **MarTech:** Airlines, OTAs, destination marketing organizations, and hotel groups.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** RateGain operates through several wholly-owned subsidiaries globally, including RateGain Technologies Inc. (USA), RateGain UK Ltd. (UK), RateGain Travel Technologies (Singapore) Pte. Ltd., and recently acquired entities like Ricco and Adara. These subsidiaries facilitate global sales, support, and R&D activities.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** As an IT product company, RateGain does not have a traditional manufacturing footprint. Its operations are centered around software development, data centers, and customer support. It has development centers in Noida (Uttar Pradesh) and Gurgaon (Haryana) in India, along with sales and support offices in various global locations, including the US, UK, Spain, UAE, and Singapore.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** The company was founded by Mr. Bhanu Chopra, who serves as the Chairman and Managing Director. Mr. Chopra has over two decades of experience in technology and entrepreneurship, with a strong vision for leveraging data and AI in the travel sector. RateGain is not part of a larger diversified business group but is an independent, professionally managed entity focused solely on travel technology. The promoter group has a strong track record of innovation and strategic acquisitions to expand market reach and product capabilities.

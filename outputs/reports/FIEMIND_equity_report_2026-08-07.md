### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

- **Market leadership in 2W automotive lighting with deep OEM entrenchment:** Fiem is the dominant lighting supplier to India's top two-wheeler OEMs — Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India (HMSI), Hero MotoCorp, Suzuki Motorcycles, TVS Motor Company and Yamaha — with an estimated 55–60% share in the organised 2W OE lighting market [^ip-latest]. The company's nine strategically located plants near OEM assembly hubs (Haryana, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Rajasthan, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand) create a structural logistics moat that competitors find difficult to replicate [^ar-fy25].

- **Structural margin expansion via LED mix shift:** LED headlamps carry a 4–5x ASP premium over conventional halogen units, and Fiem has already developed ~100 LED SKUs across automotive and non-automotive verticals [^vp-thread]. Management commentary on the Q4FY26 concall indicated that LED contribution to lighting revenue has risen from ~7% in FY15 to ~22–25% by FY25, driving a ~250–300 bps blended margin uplift over the cycle [^cc-transcript]. We model further 150–200 bps of margin expansion as LED penetration in 2W scales from current ~15% to ~35% by FY28E.

- **Diversification into high-growth non-auto adjacencies:** Fiem has built three non-automotive growth engines — (i) LED luminaires for EESL/street-lighting (Rs. 11.53 Cr EESL order win in Jan 2015 was the seed contract) [^vp-thread], (ii) Integrated Passenger Information System (IPIS) with LED displays for Indian Railways and state transport buses [^vp-thread], and (iii) solar LED products. These adjacencies are at an inflection point and could contribute 12–15% of revenue by FY28E vs. ~7% currently [^ip-latest].

- **Near-term catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Q1FY27 results scheduled for 13 Aug 2026 — we expect 18–22% YoY revenue growth and 80–100 bps EBITDA margin expansion on the back of premiumisation and operating leverage [^cc-transcript]; (b) HMSI and Hero MotoCorp are in the middle of new model launches (Activa e:, Hero Xtreme 160R refresh) which carry higher LED content per vehicle [^vp-thread]; (c) Potential new order wins in the railway IPIS vertical post successful pilots; (d) Capacity expansion at the new Mysore plant expected to commission in H2FY27, adding ~Rs. 350–400 Cr of incremental annual revenue potential [^ip-latest].

- **Biggest structural risk — 2W OEM concentration and China LED threat:** Top-3 customers (HMSI, Hero, Suzuki) contribute ~55–60% of automotive revenue, creating concentration risk if any major OEM in-sources lighting or shifts sourcing to a Tier-1 global player [^ar-fy25]. Additionally, Chinese LED component suppliers (MLS, Mulinsen) have been aggressively pricing into India, which could compress LED module gross margins by 100–150 bps if Fiem is unable to pass through input cost inflation [^vp-thread]. Working capital intensity (debtor days of 55–65) also remains a sensitivity in a rising rate environment.

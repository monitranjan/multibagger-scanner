### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** OCCL Ltd. holds a dominant position in the insoluble sulphur (IS) market in India, with a significant market share and a strong brand recall among tire manufacturers. The company's long-standing relationships with global and domestic tire majors further solidify its leadership.

Furthermore, OCCL benefits from operational efficiencies derived from its integrated manufacturing process and continuous R&D efforts to optimize production. The shift towards higher-grade IS and premium applications, coupled with stable raw material sourcing, is expected to drive a structural improvement in EBITDA margins by 150-200 bps over the next 2-3 years, reaching ~18-19% by FY27E.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** While its core remains IS, OCCL is actively exploring and investing in new specialty chemical derivatives and rubber chemicals, leveraging its existing chemical expertise and infrastructure. This diversification into new product lines, potentially targeting non-tire rubber applications and other industrial segments, will reduce dependence on the cyclical tire industry and open up new avenues for growth, contributing an estimated 10-15% of revenue from new products by FY28E.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include the commissioning of the recently announced capacity expansion for insoluble sulphur, which will allow OCCL to cater to increasing demand from domestic and export markets, particularly as global tire production normalizes. Strong demand from radialization in the Indian tire industry and a pick-up in global automotive production will directly benefit OCCL. Furthermore, any favorable movement in crude oil prices (a key input for some raw materials) could further boost profitability.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The most significant structural risk for OCCL is its high dependence on the global tire industry. Any prolonged downturn in automotive production, trade protectionist measures affecting tire exports, or a significant shift in tire manufacturing technology away from sulphur-based vulcanization could severely impact demand for its core product. While diversification efforts are underway, the current revenue concentration makes it vulnerable to this cyclicality.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

OCCL Ltd. (formerly Oriental Carbon & Chemicals Ltd.) is a leading manufacturer of Insoluble Sulphur (IS), a critical performance additive used primarily in the tire and other rubber industries for vulcanization. The company operates two main business segments:
*   **Insoluble Sulphur (IS):** This is the core business, accounting for approximately 85-90% of total revenue. IS is used as a vulcanizing agent in rubber, particularly in radial tires, to prevent blooming and improve the overall quality and performance of the rubber compound. OCCL manufactures various grades of IS, including conventional and high-stability variants, marketed under the "Diamond Sulphur" brand.
*   **Chemicals (Sulphuric Acid & Oleum):** This segment contributes the remaining 10-15% of revenue. The company produces Sulphuric Acid and Oleum, which are essential raw materials for its IS production, and also sells surplus quantities to external customers in various industries.

**Revenue Split by Division (FY24A):**
*   Insoluble Sulphur: ~87%
*   Sulphuric Acid & Oleum: ~13%

**Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
*   **Insoluble Sulphur:** Major global and domestic tire manufacturers including Apollo Tyres, MRF, CEAT, JK Tyre, Michelin, Bridgestone, Goodyear, Continental, and Pirelli. The company has long-standing relationships and is an approved supplier to most leading tire OEMs worldwide.
*   **Sulphuric Acid & Oleum:** Primarily captive consumption for IS production. External sales are to various chemical and industrial customers.

**Subsidiary Structure:** OCCL has a wholly-owned subsidiary, OCCL North America Inc., established to strengthen its presence and service capabilities in the North American market.

**Manufacturing Footprint:**
*   **India:** OCCL operates two manufacturing facilities in India:
    *   Dharuhera, Haryana: Focuses on Insoluble Sulphur production.
    *   Mundra, Gujarat: Newer, integrated facility for Insoluble Sulphur and Sulphuric Acid. This plant benefits from proximity to port for raw material imports and finished goods exports.
*   Total Plant Count: 2

**Promoter Background and Group Context:** OCCL is promoted by the Kolkata-based Duncan Goenka Group, a diversified conglomerate with interests in tea, chemicals, and other sectors. The Group has a long history in India's industrial landscape. Mr. R.P. Goenka is the Chairman Emeritus, and Mr. Dipankar Chatterji is the Chairman of OCCL. The promoters have a deep understanding of the chemical industry and have steered OCCL's growth and market leadership in insoluble sulphur over several decades.

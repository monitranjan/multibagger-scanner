### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1.  **Dominant Market Leadership with Robust Moat:** IndiGo maintains an undisputed leadership position in the Indian aviation market with a domestic market share consistently above 60% (as of May 2026). This is driven by its strong brand, extensive network (over 100 domestic and international destinations), unparalleled operational efficiency (high aircraft utilization, quick turnarounds), and a cost-effective lean operating model. Its scale provides significant bargaining power with suppliers (aircraft manufacturers, fuel providers) and distribution channels, creating a sustainable competitive moat that is difficult for new entrants to replicate.
2.  **Structural Margin Improvement & Network Optimization:** The company is poised for structural margin improvement driven by several factors: a) increasing share of international traffic (higher yields, less competitive), b) full realization of cost benefits from new-generation A320neo and A321neo aircraft (better fuel efficiency, lower maintenance costs), c) enhanced ancillary revenue streams (baggage, seat selection, in-flight meals), and d) ongoing network rationalization for optimal capacity deployment. We expect EBITDA margins to stabilize in the 18-20% range over FY27E-FY29E, compared to the volatility seen in previous cycles.
3.  **Strategic Expansion into High-Growth Adjuncts:** IndiGo is diversifying beyond its core domestic passenger business into high-growth adjacencies. This includes a significant push into international routes, which currently account for ~25% of its capacity and are expected to grow to ~35% by FY29E, offering higher yields and less intense competition. Furthermore, its growing cargo operations (IndiGo CarGo) leverage existing belly space and dedicated freighters, tapping into India's expanding logistics market. Strategic partnerships, such as the codeshare with Turkish Airlines, enhance global connectivity without significant capital outlay.
4.  **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key catalysts include: a) continued strong passenger traffic growth (expected 15-18% YoY for FY27E) driven by India's rising disposable incomes and improving air travel penetration, b) stable crude oil prices (Brent in the $75-85/bbl range) which will support fuel cost management, c) resolution of supply chain issues for Pratt & Whitney engines, improving aircraft availability and reducing grounding costs, and d) potential for further tariff rationalization post-elections, allowing for more dynamic pricing strategies.
5.  **Biggest Structural Risk: Fuel Price Volatility & Geopolitical Events:** The most significant structural risk remains the inherent volatility of aviation turbine fuel (ATF) prices, which typically constitute 35-40% of operating costs. Any sharp, sustained increase in crude oil prices, exacerbated by geopolitical tensions (e.g., Middle East conflicts, Russia-Ukraine war), could severely impact profitability. While the company hedges a portion of its fuel exposure, a prolonged spike would significantly erode margins and could necessitate fare hikes, potentially impacting demand.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Interglobe Aviation Limited, operating under the brand name IndiGo, is India's largest passenger airline by market share and fleet size. Its core business model revolves around providing low-cost, on-time, and hassle-free air travel primarily in the domestic market, with a growing international presence.

*   **Core Business Model:** IndiGo operates on a low-cost carrier (LCC) model, characterized by a single aircraft type (Airbus A320 family for narrow-body, A321 for longer routes, and ATRs for regional connectivity), high aircraft utilization, quick turnarounds, direct point-to-point flights, and unbundled services (charging for ancillaries like baggage, meals, seat selection). This model focuses on efficiency and cost control to offer competitive fares.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (% of Revenue, FY26A):**
    *   Passenger Revenue: ~88-90%
    *   Ancillary Revenue (Baggage, Seat Selection, Meals, etc.): ~10-12%
    *   Cargo Revenue: <1% (Growing segment)
    *   Other Operating Revenue (Charter services, maintenance, etc.): <1%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Passenger:** Individual travelers, corporate clients, travel agencies. IndiGo serves a broad demographic, from first-time flyers to frequent business travelers.
    *   **Cargo:** Logistics companies, e-commerce players, individual shippers. Leveraging belly capacity of passenger flights and dedicated freighters.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Interglobe Aviation Limited is the primary operating entity. There are no significant listed subsidiaries that materially alter the core business profile.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** As an airline, IndiGo does not have a manufacturing footprint in the traditional sense. Its operational footprint includes:
    *   **Hubs:** Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM), Bengaluru (BLR), Hyderabad (HYD), Kolkata (CCU) – these are major operational bases for flight scheduling, maintenance, and crew.
    *   **Maintenance Facilities:** Strategically located across major airports.
    *   **Crew Bases:** Across major cities to optimize crew utilization.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** IndiGo was co-founded by Rahul Bhatia (InterGlobe Enterprises) and Rakesh Gangwal. InterGlobe Enterprises, a diversified Indian conglomerate, holds the promoter stake. The group has a strong track record in the travel and hospitality sector, including hotels, travel distribution, and aviation services. Rahul Bhatia, as the Managing Director, plays a pivotal role in strategy and execution. The promoter group's deep industry knowledge and long-term vision have been instrumental in IndiGo's growth and market dominance.

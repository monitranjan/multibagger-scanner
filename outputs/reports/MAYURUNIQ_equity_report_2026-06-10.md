### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS (5 bullet points)

*   **Market Leadership & Moat:** Mayur Uniquoters is a leading manufacturer of Artificial Leather (PVC and PU based) in India, holding a dominant market share in the automotive OEM segment. Its long-standing relationships with key automotive OEMs (Maruti Suzuki, Mahindra & Mahindra, Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland) and stringent quality approvals create high switching costs and a strong competitive moat. The company's diverse product portfolio catering to various applications (automotive, footwear, furnishing, accessories) further strengthens its market position.
*   **Structural Margin Story:** The company is strategically focused on increasing the share of value-added products, particularly in the PU segment, which commands higher margins compared to PVC. Furthermore, ongoing operational efficiencies, backward integration initiatives (e.g., in-house printing, lamination), and prudent raw material sourcing are expected to drive a structural improvement in EBITDA margins by 150-200 bps over the next 2-3 years, targeting 18-19% by FY28E.
*   **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies:** Mayur Uniquoters is actively diversifying into newer, high-growth adjacencies. Its foray into the medical textiles segment with products like blood pressure cuffs and medical gowns offers significant growth potential, leveraging its existing manufacturing expertise and global quality standards. Additionally, the increasing adoption of artificial leather in non-automotive segments like contract furnishing and fashion accessories provides avenues for sustained revenue growth beyond its core automotive business.
*   **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** Key near-term catalysts include the successful ramp-up of new capacities at its Jaipur plants, particularly for PU leather, which will cater to increased demand from domestic automotive OEMs and export markets. Furthermore, a strong order book from the footwear segment, driven by new product development and increased penetration in organised retail, is expected to support revenue growth in FY27E. Any new OEM approvals or entry into new export geographies will also act as positive triggers.
*   **Biggest Structural Risk:** The biggest structural risk for Mayur Uniquoters is its significant dependence on crude oil derivatives for raw materials (PVC resins, plasticizers, solvents). Any sharp and sustained increase in crude oil prices, without the ability to fully pass on costs to customers, could significantly impact profitability and margins. While the company has hedging mechanisms and long-term contracts, prolonged volatility remains a concern.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business Model:** Mayur Uniquoters manufactures and supplies a wide range of Artificial Leather (both PVC and PU-based) primarily for the automotive, footwear, furnishing, and accessories industries. The company operates on a B2B model, supplying directly to OEMs and large manufacturers. Its expertise lies in customizing products to meet specific client requirements regarding texture, color, thickness, and performance attributes.
*   **Revenue Split by Division (% of revenue) (FY25A Est.):**
    *   Automotive OEM: ~50%
    *   Footwear: ~30%
    *   Furnishing & Others (including exports): ~20%
*   **Key OEM/End Customers per Division:**
    *   **Automotive:** Maruti Suzuki, Mahindra & Mahindra, Tata Motors, Ashok Leyland, Hyundai, Honda, Toyota.
    *   **Footwear:** Relaxo Footwears, Bata India, Liberty Shoes, Paragon Footwear.
    *   **Furnishing & Others:** Various furniture manufacturers, bag manufacturers, and export clients.
*   **Subsidiary Structure:** Mayur Uniquoters Ltd operates primarily as a standalone entity with no significant domestic or international subsidiaries impacting its core operations.
*   **Manufacturing Footprint:** The company has a robust manufacturing footprint primarily concentrated in Rajasthan. It operates multiple state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities in:
    *   Jaipur, Rajasthan (multiple units)
    *   Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh (PVC film manufacturing)
    *   Total plant count: 3 large integrated manufacturing complexes.
*   **Promoter Background and Group Context:** Mayur Uniquoters was founded by Mr. Suresh Kumar Poddar, a visionary entrepreneur with extensive experience in the artificial leather industry. The Poddar family continues to be actively involved in the management and strategic direction of the company. The group has a strong reputation for ethical business practices, quality products, and long-term stakeholder value creation. While Mayur Uniquoters is the flagship entity, the promoter group has interests in other related businesses, but the focus remains on the core artificial leather operations.

3.  **Competition from Imports (M × H):** Increased import penetration, especially from China, can lead to pricing pressure and market share loss. Monitoring: Import duties, landed cost of imported artificial leather, company's realization per sq. meter.

5.  **Regulatory Changes (L × M):** Stricter environmental norms or changes in trade policies could impact operations or costs. Monitoring: New environmental regulations, trade agreements impacting synthetic leather.
6.  **Capacity Utilization Constraints (L × L):** Inability to scale up production efficiently to meet demand could limit growth. Monitoring: Quarterly capacity utilization rates, capex deployment timelines.

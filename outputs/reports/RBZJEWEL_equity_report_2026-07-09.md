### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

*   **Niche Antique Gold Jewelry Leadership:** RBZ Jewellers commands a dominant position in the "Antique Gold" segment, a high-margin, labor-intensive category. The company’s design-led moat allows it to maintain superior margins compared to mass-market retail jewelers.
*   **Structural Margin Upside:** With a shift toward a B2C retail model from a wholesale-centric legacy, the company is capturing the "retail premium." We expect EBITDA margins to expand by 150-200 bps by FY27E as the revenue mix shifts toward higher-margin retail sales.
*   **Expansion into High-Growth Geographies:** The company is aggressively scaling its retail footprint in Gujarat and expanding into untapped Tier-II/III cities. This provides a clear runway for revenue growth independent of volatile bullion prices.
*   **Near-term Catalysts:** Sustained wedding season demand and the stabilization of gold prices are expected to drive volume growth in H2 FY27. Further, the commissioning of new retail showrooms will serve as a primary revenue lever in the next 6–12 months.
*   **Structural Risk:** The primary risk remains the high concentration of business in the Gujarat market (geographic risk) and the inherent volatility in gold prices impacting inventory carrying costs and working capital cycles.

### SECTION 3 — BUSINESS OVERVIEW

*   **Core Business:** RBZ Jewellers operates as a vertically integrated player, specializing in the design, manufacturing, and sale of antique gold jewelry.
*   **Revenue Split:** Approximately 70% of revenue is derived from wholesale operations (supplying to national/regional retailers), while 30% is generated through direct-to-consumer retail, with the retail mix trending upward.
*   **Manufacturing:** The company operates a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, which serves as the hub for its design and production activities.
*   **Promoter Context:** The company is led by Rajendrakumar Zaveri, who brings decades of industry experience. The group emphasizes a family-run professionalized structure with a deep focus on design intellectual property.

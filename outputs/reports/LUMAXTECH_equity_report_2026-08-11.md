### SECTION 2 — INVESTMENT THESIS

1. **Dominant 2W Auto-Ancillary Franchise with Tier-1 OEM Status:** Lumax Auto Technologies is a top-3 supplier of sheet metal components, fabricated assemblies and tubular parts to Indian 2W OEMs with marquee customers including Bajaj Auto, Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India (HMSI), Hero MotoCorp and TVS Motor[^ip-latest]. Long-tenured supplier relationships (typically 10+ years on platform programs) and multi-product single-vendor status create meaningful switching costs and protect wallet share even during OEM volume cycles[^ar-fy25].

2. **Structural Margin Expansion Story:** Consolidated EBITDA margin has structurally expanded from sub-10% in FY20 to ~13–14% in FY25-26, driven by (i) product mix shift toward value-added fabricated assemblies and head/tail lamps, (ii) backward integration into critical tooling, and (iii) operating leverage on a higher revenue base[^ip-latest]. Management has guided for sustained 50–100 bps annual margin expansion through scale, capex absorption and the high-margin IAC ramp-up[^cc-transcript].

3. **Diversification into High-Growth Adjacencies (IAC Entry):** Board has approved a new Intelligent Ambient Comfort (IAC) Division manufacturing plant at Chakan, Maharashtra (announced 10 Aug 2026), marking entry into HVAC/cabin comfort modules — a Rs. 15,000+ Cr TAM growing at 18–20% CAGR[^ar-fy25]. This complements the JV with FAE Technologies (Spain) for advanced lighting and opens adjacent wallet with the same OEM set without greenfield customer acquisition cost[^ip-latest].

4. **Near-Term Catalysts (6–12 months):** (a) Commissioning of Chakan IAC plant by Q3FY27E, expected to contribute Rs. 200–300 Cr revenue at peak run-rate; (b) new order wins tied to Maruti Suzuki's upcoming SUV launches; (c) margin tailwind from steel/aluminum cost normalization; (d) sustained 2W export demand recovery in Africa and Latin America; (e) potential re-rating as the IAC segment scales and the consolidated mix shifts toward higher-multiple business[^cc-transcript].

5. **Biggest Structural Risk — Capital Intensity and Governance Overhang:** ROCE at just 8.27% (FY25A) sits materially below the peer median of ~14%, indicating that incremental capex is value-dilutive unless asset turns improve and the IAC segment lifts blended ROCE[^ar-fy25]. Additionally, related party transactions (~15–20% of consolidated sales historically with sister-listed Lumax Industries) remain a persistent governance overhang raised repeatedly on investor forums, even though management maintains arm's-length pricing and customer segregation[^vp-thread].
